CC-3

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2023
  1. 1 Answer any five questions :
  2. 1(a) Find closure of the sets \(\mathbb N,\mathbb R-\mathbb Q,\mathbb Q-\mathbb Z,\) and \(\{1/n:n=1,2,\ldots\}\).
  3. 1(b) Find the derived set of the set \(\displaystyle\bigcup_{i=0}^\infty[(i,i+1)-\{i+\tfrac13,i+\tfrac23\}]\).
  4. 1(c) Give examples of countable, uncountable and finite open covers of \(\mathbb R\).
  5. 1(d) Show that \(\mathbb N\) is a closed set but \(\mathbb Q\) is neither open nor closed.
  6. 1(e) Find total length of the curve \(x^2+y^2+2x=0\).
  7. 1(f) Find the convergent subsequences of the sequence \(\{\sin\frac{n\pi}{2}\}\).
  8. 1(g) Find asymptote of the curve \(y^3+x^3=3xy\).
  9. 2 State and prove Taylor's theorem with Cauchy's form of remainder. [5]
  10. 3 Find the area inside the two cardioids \(r=5(1+\cos\theta)\) and \(r=5(1-\cos\theta)\). [5]
  11. 4 Expand \((1+x)^m,\ m\in\mathbb R\sim\mathbb N,\) in power series with proper justification [5]
  12. 5 Define compact sets. State and prove Heine-Borel covering theorem. [5]
  13. 6(a) Show that a point \(x\) is limit point of \(S\) if and only if there is a sequence \(x_n\in S\sim\{x\}\) which converges to \(x\). [3]
  14. 6(b) What is nested intervals. State Nested interval theorem [2]
  15. 7(a) Find the intrinsic equation of the cycloid \(x=a(\theta+\sin\theta),\ y=a(1-\cos\theta)\), the fixed point being the origin. [3]
  16. 7(b) Find envelope of the straight lines \(\frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}=1\) where the parameters are connected by the relation \(a+b=1\) [2]
  17. 8 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions.\((2\times5=10)\)
  18. 8(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Show that no integer in the following sequence is a perfect square: \(11,111,1111,11111,\ldots\). \item For \(n\ge1\), and positive integers \(a,b\) if \(a^n\mid b^n\) find \(\mathrm{GCD}(a,b)\). \end{enumerate} [3]
  19. 8(b) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item For a composite number \(n>4\), prove or disprove \(n\mid(n-1)!\) \item How many primes are there atleast less than \(2^{1024}\)? \item State the \textquoteleft Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic\textquoteright. \end{enumerate} [1]
  20. 8(c) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Find the last two digit of \(9^{9^9}\). \item Establish the congruence \(2222^{5555}+5555^{2222}\equiv0\) (modulo 7). \end{enumerate} [3]
  21. 9 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions.\((2\times10=20)\)
  22. 9(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Describe explicitly the sets \(K=\{a^2\mid a\in D_4\}\) and \(L=\{a\in D_4\mid a^2=e\}\). Are \(K\) and \(L\) subgroups of \(D_4\)? If so, which of them are commutative as a group. \item Let \(G\) be a finite group of even order. Show that \(G\) contains exactly odd number of elements of order 2. \item Show that a semigroup \(S\) is a group if and only if for all \(a,b\in S\), the equations \(ax=b\) and \(ya=b\) have solutions in \(S\). \end{enumerate} [3]
  23. 9(b) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item If \(H\) and \(K\) are two nontrivial subgroups of \(\mathbb Q\), then show that \(H\cap K\) is also nontrivial. Does this hold if \(\mathbb Q\) is replaced by \(\mathbb R\)? Justify. \item Let \(H\) be a subgroup of \(G\), and \(a\in G\) such that \(a^n=e\) and \(a^m\in H\). If \(\gcd(m,n)=1\), then show that \(a\in H\). \item Let \(H\) and \(K\) be two subgroups of a group \(G\). Prove that \(HK\) is a subgroup if and only if \(HK=KH\). Consider the subgroups \(H=\{e,(12)\}\) and \(K=\{e,(123),(132)\}\) of \(S_3\). Is \(HK\) a subgroup of \(S_3\)? \end{enumerate}
  24. 9(c) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Show that every finitely generated subgroup of \(\mathbb Q\) is cyclic. Find two generators of the subgroup \(H=\langle\tfrac23,\tfrac45\rangle\) of \(\mathbb Q\). Is there any other generators of \(H\)? \item Let \(G\) be a finite cyclic group of order \(n\). If \(d\) is a positive integer such that \(d\mid n\), show that \(G\) has a unique subgroup of order \(d\). \item Let \(G\) be a cyclic group of order 450. Find total number of distinct subgroups and generators of \(G\). How many elements of \(G\) are of order 50? \end{enumerate} [3]
