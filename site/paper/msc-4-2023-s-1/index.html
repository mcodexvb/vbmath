MMC-41

Differential Geometry and Manifold Theory
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2023
  1. 1 Define smooth manifold of dimension $n$, $n\in\mathbb N$. What do you mean by local coordinates for a manifold? Find an atlas with appropriate charts for $S^1=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2=1\}\subset\mathbb R^2$ which provides a smooth manifold structure on $S^1$ mentioned above of some dimension to be determined by you. [[4+5+1]]
  2. 2 Introduce the concept of the tangent space $T_p\mathcal M$ at a point $p$ on a manifold $\mathcal M$. Derive a basis for $T_p\mathcal M$ in terms of the local coordinates $(x^1,x^2,\ldots,x^m)$ of $\mathcal M$. Define the Lie bracket (commutator) of two tangent vectors in $T_p\mathcal M$ and verify whether the tangent space $T_p\mathcal M$ forms a Lie algebra with the bilinear map as the Lie bracket. Define vector fields on $\mathcal M$ and its action on smooth functions $C^\infty(U)$, where $U$ is some neighbourhood of $\mathcal M$. Prove that each vector field $\mathbf X\in\mathcal X(U)$ is a linear derivation on $C^\infty(U)$. [[2+2+3+2+1]]
  3. 3 Define differential forms and wedge product of forms on the manifold $\mathbb R^3$. Introduce the notion of exterior derivative of differential forms in $\Lambda_p(\mathbb R^3)$. Prove the formula $d(\alpha\wedge\beta)=d\alpha\wedge\beta+(-1)^p\alpha\wedge d\beta$ $\forall\alpha\in\Lambda_p(\mathbb R^3),\beta\in\Lambda_q(\mathbb R^3)$. When a form is said to be exact? Prove that every exact form is closed in $\mathbb R^3$. [[2+2+3+1+2]]
  4. 4 What do you mean by the first (I) and the second (II) fundamental forms for a smooth surface $\mathcal S\subset\mathbb R^3$? Give their geometrical significance. Obtain I and II for the (sphere) surface $\vec r(u,v)=(a\sin u\cos v,a\sin u\sin v,a\cos u)$. [[2+2+3+3]]
  5. 5 Define normal and geodesic curvatures of curves on a smooth surface $\mathcal S\in\mathbb R^3$. Obtain the expression for normal curvature in terms of the coefficients of fundamental forms. Obtain formulas for Gaussian and mean curvatures from the expression just obtained by you. Compute the Gaussian and mean curvatures for the sphere $\vec r(u,v)=(\sin u\cos v,\sin u\sin v,\cos v)$. [[2+3+3+2]]
  6. 6 State the definition of covariant derivative $\vec\nabla_{\mathbf X}(\mathbf Y)$. Introduce Riemann-Christoffel curvature tensor $R^l_{ijk}$. State and prove Gauss's Theorema Egregium in terms of $R^l_{ijk}$. State Bianchi's identity and define Ricci tensor. When a Riemannian space is said to be flat? [[1+2+4+1+1+1]]
