SECMA-2

Analysis-II and Tensor Calculus
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2024
  1. 1(a) What do you mean by the limit of a function \(f(z)\) when \(z\) approaches \(z_0\)? Explain with examples. Show that such a limit, if exists, is unique.
  2. 1(b) Prove, using \(\epsilon\)-\(\delta\) notations, that \(f(z)=\dfrac{1}{\overline z}\) is continuous at \(z=i\).
  3. 1(c) Find the stereographic projection of the straight line \(19x+8y=24\) on the Riemann sphere \(\xi^2+\eta^2+(\zeta-\frac12)^2=\frac14\). \hfill
  4. 2(a) Use \(\epsilon\)-\(\delta\) notations to prove \(\displaystyle\lim_{z\to-1}\dfrac{z^2+7z+9}{2z+5}=1\).
  5. 2(b) When does a complex sequence \(\{z_n\}\) converge to a limit \(z\)? Explain with examples. Find the limit points of the sequence \(\{i^n+\tfrac1n\}\).
  6. 2(c) Describe, in detail, how a point \(z=x+iy\) on the complex plane is stereographically projected onto a point \((\xi,\eta,\zeta)\) on a suitable sphere. \hfill
  7. 3(a) Examine whether the limit \(\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\frac{\overline z^2}{z}\) exists. Hence find the value of \(\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\frac{\overline z^2e^{\frac{i}{|z|}}}{z}\).
  8. 3(b) If \(f(z)\) and \(g(z)\) are continuous at \(z_0\) and \(c\in\mathbb C\), show that \(cf(z)+g(z)\) is continuous at \(z_0\).
  9. 3(c) Prove that a convergent sequence is bounded. \hfill
  10. 1(a) Define dummy index and free index in an indexed expression. Using summation convention, expand \(\delta^i_j a^{jk}\) and prove that it equals \(a^{ik}\).
  11. 1(b) Define \(e\)-systems of order 2 and prove that \(e_{ij}e^{ik}=\delta_k^j\).
  12. 1(c) If \(a_{pq}x^px^q=0\) for all values of the independent variables \(x^1,\ldots,x^n\) and \(a_{pq}\)'s are constants, then prove that \(a_{ij}+a_{ji}=0\). \hfill
  13. 2(a) If \(x^i=a^i_py^p\) and \(z^i=b^i_qx^q\), then prove that \(z^i=b^i_pa^p_qy^q\).
  14. 2(b) Using summation convention, compute \(\delta^i_k\delta^k_\ell\delta^\ell_i\) and \(\delta^i_j\delta^j_\ell\delta^\ell_k\delta^k_i\).
  15. 2(c) Prove that \(e_{imn}e^{irs}a^{mn}=a^{rs}-a^{sr}\). \hfill
  16. 3(a) If \(A^i\) and \(B_i\) are respectively contravariant and covariant vectors, then prove that \(A^iB_i\) is an invariant.
  17. 3(b) If \(A^i\) and \(B^j\) are two contravariant vectors, then prove that the \(n^2\) quantities \(A^iB^j\) are the components of a contravariant tensor of rank 2.
  18. 3(c) Define mixed tensors of rank 3 with first order of contravariance and second order of covariance and vice versa. \hfill
  19. 4(a) If the co-ordinates of a tensor are all zero in one co-ordinate system, then prove that they are all zero in every other co-ordinate system.
  20. 4(b) Prove that the outer multiplication of two mixed tensors of types \((p,q)\) and \((r,s)\) is a mixed tensor of type \((p+r,q+s)\).
  21. 4(c) Let \(p\ge6\) and \(q\ge3\) be two integers. Can you obtain a tensor of type \((p-5,q-2)\) from a tensor of type \((p,q)\) via finite number of contractions? Justify your answer. \hfill
  22. 5(a) Let \(A_i\) be a covariant vector, determine, with proof, whether \(\dfrac{\partial A_i}{\partial x^j}\) is a tensor or not.
  23. 5(b) If \(a_{ij}\) is a skew-symmetric tensor, then prove that \((\delta^i_j\delta^k_\ell+\delta^i_\ell\delta^k_j)a_{ik}=0\).
  24. 5(c) If a tensor \(T_{ijk}\) is symmetric in the first two indices and skew-symmetric in the last two indices, then prove that \(T_{ijk}=0\). \hfill
  25. 6(a) Prove that the scalar product of a relative covariant vector of weight \(\omega\) and a relative contravariant vector of weight \(\omega'\) is a relative scalar of weight \((\omega+\omega')\).
  26. 6(b) Prove that the determinant of a tensor of type \((1,1)\) is an invariant.
  27. 6(c) Determine, with proof, the number of distinct components of a completely symmetric tensor of type \((0,3)\) where the indices assume values from 1 to \(n\). \hfill
