CC-1

Analysis-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2020
  1. 1(a) State Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem. [[2]]
  2. 1(b) Let $S$ be a bounded subset of $\mathbb R$ with $SupS=M$ and $InfS=m$. Prove that the set $T=\{x-y:x\in S,y\in S\}$ is a bounded set with $SupT=M-m$ and $InfT=m-M$. [[5]]
  3. 1(c) Prove that $\mathbb N$ is unbounded above. [[3]]
  4. 2(a) Let $S=\{(-1)^n+\frac1n:n\in\mathbb N\}$. Find $S'$. [[5]]
  5. 2(b) Is $\mathbb Q$ dense in $\mathbb R$? Justify your answer. [[3]]
  6. 2(c) Applying L'Hospital rule, find $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{x-\tan x}{x^3}$. [[2]]
  7. 3(a) For any $A,B\subset\mathbb R$, prove that $(A\cap B)'\subset A'\cap B'$. Is the converse true? Justify . [[3]]
  8. 3(b) State and prove Rolle's theorem. [[5]]
  9. 3(c) Applying LMVT prove that, $0<\log_e\left(\frac{e^x-1}{x}\right)<x$, $x>0$. [[2]]
  10. 4(a) If $f'$ exist in $[0,1]$, show that the equation $2x(f(1)-f(0))=f'(x)$ has atleast one solution in $(0,1)$. [[3]]
  11. 4(b) Find $a,b,c$ such that $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{ae^x-b\cos x+ce^{-x}}{x\sin x}=2$. [[5]]
  12. 4(c) If $y=\sin pt,x=\sin t$, prove that $(1-x^2)y_2-xy_1+p^2y=0$. [[2]]
  13. 5(a) If $y=\sin(m\sin^{-1}x)$, prove that\\(i) $(1-x^2)y_2-xy_1+m^2y=0$\\(ii) $(1-x^2)y_{n+2}-(2n+1)xy_{n+1}+(m^2-n^2)y_n=0$ [[3+3]]
  14. 5(b) If $f'(x)\ge0$ on $[1,2]$, then prove that $f\left(\frac{x+y}2\right)\le\frac{f(x)+f(y)}2$, $\forall x,y\in[1,2]$ . [[4]]
  15. 6(a) By using $(\epsilon-\delta)$ definition, evaluate $\lim_{x\to2}\frac{2x^2-8}{x-2}$ and find $\delta$ if $\epsilon=0.1$. [[3]]
  16. 6(b) If $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=l$ and $\lim_{x\to a}g(x)=m\ne0$ then prove that $\lim_{x\to a}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac lm$. [[5]]
  17. 6(c) Verify whether $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{2x+|x|}{2x-|x|}$ exists or not. [[2]]
  18. 7(a) Examine the continuity of the function $f(x)=\begin{cases}\frac{x^4+4x^3+2x}{\sin x}&\text{when }x\ne0\\0&\text{when }x=0\end{cases}$ at $x=0$. [[2]]
  19. 7(b) Let $f:[a,b]\to[c,d]$ and $g:[c,d]\to[e,f]$ be two continuous mappings. Prove that $g\circ f:[a,b]\to[e,f]$ is continuous. [[4]]
  20. 7(c) A function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies the condition $f(x+y)=f(x)f(y)$ $\forall x,y\in\mathbb R$. If $f$ is continuous at $x=0$, prove that $f$ is continuous on $\mathbb R$. [[4]]
  21. 8(a) State and prove nested intervals theorem. [[5]]
  22. 8(b) Find the limit of the sequence $\{x_n\}$ where $x_n=(2^n+3^n)^{1/n}$. [[3]]
  23. 8(c) Evaluate $\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]n$. [[2]]
  24. 9(a) Prove that a sequence is convergent iff it is a Cauchy sequence. [[6]]
  25. 9(b) Give an example of a sequence of rational numbers that converges to an irrational number. [[1]]
  26. 9(c) Evaluate $\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\{(2n+1)(2n+2)\cdots(2n+n)\}^{1/n}$. [[3]]
