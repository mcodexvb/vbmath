MMC-11

Real Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2016
  1. 1(a) Show that the following sets are measurable. Also find their measure:\\(i) $\mathbb{Q}\cap[-1,1]$ \quad (ii) set of irrationals in $[0,1]$, \quad (iii) $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}(n,n+1)$. [3]
  2. 1(b) Write down the domain and range of $\mu^{*}$ and $\mu$. Show that $\mu^{*}[0,1]=1$. [1+3]
  3. 1(c) Suppose that $\mu(E)>1$. Show that there is a $F_\sigma$-set $F\subset E$ such that $\mu(F)=\mu(E)-1$. [3]
  4. 1(d) Show that every subset of a set of measure zero is measurable. [2]
  5. 2(a) Construct a nonmeasurable set. Show that every measurable subset of that set has measure zero. [4+2]
  6. 2(b) If $f$ and $g$ are measurable then show that $f+g$ and $f^2$ are also measurable. [3]
  7. 2(c) If $f:E\to\mathbb{R}$ is continuous almost everywhere on $E$, then show that $f$ is measurable. [3]
  8. 3(a) State and prove Egoroff theorem. [1+4]
  9. 3(b) If $\{f_n\}$ converges to $f$ a.e on $E$ and $\mu(E)<\infty$ then show that $\{f_n\}$ converges in measure to $f$ on $E$. Is the condition $\mu(E)<\infty$, necessary? Justify your answer. [4+1]
  10. 3(c) Show that if $f$ is of bounded variation then $f$ is measurable. [2]
  11. 4(a) Show that if $f$ is bounded and measurable on $E$ then $f$ is Lebesgue integrable. [4]
  12. 4(b) If $f$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$ then show that $f$ is Lebesgue integrable on $[a,b]$ and the two integrals are equal. Give an example to show that the converse is not true in general. [3+1]
  13. 4(c) Let $\{E_n\}$ be a sequence of disjoint measurable sets with $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}E_n=E$. If $f$ is Lebesgue integrable on $E$ then show that $\displaystyle\int_{\cup E_n}f\,d\mu=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{E_n}f\,d\mu$. [4]
  14. 5(a) If a function $f$ is summable on a measurable set $E$ then prove that for every $\epsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that $\left|\displaystyle\int_{e}f\,d\mu\right|<\epsilon$ whenever $\mu(e)<\delta$, $e\subset E$. [4]
  15. 5(b) State and prove dominated convergence theorem. [4+1]
  16. 5(c) If $f$ is summable on $E$ and $c$ is a constant then show that $cf$ is also summable on $E$, and $\displaystyle\int_{E}(cf)\,d\mu=c\int_{E}f\,d\mu$. [3]
  17. 6 Answer \textit{any two}: [2+2]
  18. 6(a) Let $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$, if $x$ is rational\\\hspace*{3.2em}$=1$, \hspace*{3em} if $x$ is irrational.\\Show that $f$ is Lebesgue integrable in $[0,1]$ and also find $\displaystyle\int_0^1 f\,dx$.
  19. 6(b) Examine if $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}\sin\dfrac1x$, $x\neq0$ is summable on $[0,1]$\\\hspace*{3.2em}$=0$, \hspace*{3em} $x=0$
  20. 6(c) If $f$ and $g$ are measurable show that the set $\{x:f(x)>g(x)\}$ is also measurable.
