MMC-15

Partial Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2021
  1. 1(a) If $u(x,y)$ is the solution of the Cauchy problem $xu_x+u_y=2021$, $u(x,2021)=-\sin(x^2)$, $x>0$, then find the value of $u(\sqrt{\frac\pi2},2021)$.
  2. 1(b) Consider the second-order partial differential equation (PDE) \[ u_{xx}+2u_{xy}+(x^2+4y^2)u_{yy}=\log_e(x^2+4y^2). \] Characterize the PDE on, inside, and outside the ellipse $x^2+4y^2=1$.
  3. 1(c) Using Cauchy's method of characteristics, find an integral surface of the PDE $xu_x+yu_y-u_xu_y=0$ passing through the curve $u=2x^2$, $y=0$. [3+2+5]
  4. 2(a) Find the complete integral of the PDE \[ u_{xx}-2u_{xy}+u_{yy}-5u_x+5u_y+6u=e^{2x+3y}+\cos(2x+3y). \]
  5. 2(b) Solve the following PDE by Monge's method: \[ y^2u_{xx}+2xyu_{xy}+x^2u_{yy}+xu_x+yu_y=0. \] [5+5]
  6. 3(a) Define Monge cone of a first order nonlinear PDE $f(x,y,u,u_x,u_y)=0$ at any point $(x_0,y_0,u_0)$. Find the expression of the Monge cone of the PDE $(u_x-1)(u_y-1)=1$ at $(0,0,0)$.
  7. 3(b) Let $\Omega$ denote the unit disk given by $\{(x,y):x^2+y^2\leq1\}$ and let $\Omega^c$ be its complement in the plane. Find the number of real characteristic curves $\forall(x,y)\in\Omega^c$ of the PDE \[ (1-y^2)u_{xx}-2xu_{xy}+u_{yy}=0. \]
  8. 3(c) Reduce the PDE $u_{xx}+u_{xy}+u_{yy}=0$ to canonical form.
  9. 4(a) Solve the homogeneous heat equation with the help of integral transform method \[ u_t=4u_{xx},\quad x\in(0,\pi),\ t>0, \] BC's: $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}(0,t)=\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}(\pi,t)=0$, $t>0$, IC: $u(x,0)=e^{2x}$, $0\leq x\leq\pi$.
  10. 4(b) Using appropriate integral transform method, solve the initial boundary value problem (IBVP) described as \[ u_{tt}=u_{xx}+\sin(\pi x),\quad x\in(0,1),\ t>0, \] BC's: $u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0$, $t>0$, IC's: $u(x,0)=0$, $u_t(x,0)=0$, $0\leq x\leq1$. [5+5]
  11. 5(a) What are the limitations of the separation of variables method related to the solution of PDE?
  12. 5(b) Consider the homogeneous Dirichlet diffusion problem \[ u_t-u_{xx}=0,\quad x\in(0,2),\ t>0, \] BC's: $u(0,t)=u(2,t)=0$, $t\geq0$ IC: $u(x,0)=x(2-x)$, $x\in[0,2]$. Show that (i) $0<u(x,t)<1$ for every $t>0$ and $0<x<2$ and (ii) $\int_0^2u^2dx\leq\frac{16}{15}$ for $t\geq0$.
  13. 5(c) With the help of separation of variables method, obtain the solution of the following initial boundary value problem (IBVP) for the wave equation: \[ u_{tt}-9u_{xx},\quad x\in(0,1),\ t>0, \] \textit{[No right-hand side is printed.]} BC's: $u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0$, $t>0$, IC's: $u(x,0)=0$, $u_t(x,0)=x(1-x)$, $0\leq x\leq1$. [1+4+5]
  14. 6(a) Define Dirichlet boundary condition for the Laplace equation in some function domain $\mathbb R$ and in some boundary of the domain $\partial\mathbb R$. If the Dirichlet problem has a solution $u(x,y)$ which is continuous in a bounded region $\overline{\mathbb R}$ and satisfies the Laplace equation $\nabla^2u=0$ in the interior of $\mathbb R$, then it is unique - prove it.
  15. 6(b) Solve the following interior Neumann problem for a circle: PDE: $\nabla^2u=0,\ 0\leq r<a,\ 0\leq\theta<2\pi$ BC: $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial r}=g(\theta)$ at $r=a$, $0\leq\theta<2\pi$ where $g(\theta)$ is a continuous function on $\partial\mathbb R$.
