MMC-24

Partial Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2017
  1. 1(a) Apply $\sqrt u=v$ and $v(x,y)=f(x)+g(y)$ to solve the PDE $x^4u_x^2+y^2u_y^2=4u$. Hence, find the singular integral of the PDE, if it exists. [4+1]
  2. 1(b) Reduce $u_{xx}+y^2u_{yy}=y$ ($y>0$) to canonical form. [5]
  3. 2(a) Find a surface passing through the parabolas $u=0$, $y^2=4ax$ and $u=1$, $y^2=-4ax$ satisfying $xu_{xx}+2u_x=0$. [3]
  4. 2(b) Determine the type of the PDE \[ u_{xx}+2u_{yz}+\cos x\,u_z-e^{y^2}u=\cosh z,\quad u=u(x,y,z). \] [2]
  5. 2(c) Solve the following PDE by using Monge's method: $x^2u_{xx}-y^2u_{yy}-xu_x+yu_y=xy$. [5]
  6. 3(a) Find the expression of the Monge cone of the PDE $x^2u_x^2+y^2u_y^2=u^2$ at $(1,2,3)$. [2]
  7. 3(b) Determine the characteristic strips of the PDE $u=u_x^2-3u_y^2$ and obtain the integral surface which passes through the curve $x=s$, $y=0$, $u=s^2$, $s$ is the parameter. [4]
  8. 3(c) Obtain the solution of the free vibrations of a semi-infinite string governed by \begin{align*} \text{PDE:}\quad &u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx},\quad 0<x<\infty,\ t>0\\ \text{IC's:}\quad &u(x,0)=f(x)\\ &u_t(x,0)=g(x)\\ &u,u_x\to0\text{ as }x\to\infty. \end{align*} [4]
  9. 4(a) What do you mean by Dirichlet-type and Neumann-type boundary conditions for a PDE? Are the solutions corresponding to above types boundary conditions unique? Explain. [1+2]
  10. 4(b) Separate \[ u_x+2u_{tx}-10u_{tt}=0,\qquad u(0,t)=0,\ u_x(l,t)=0 \] into an equation of $x$ and an equation of $t$. [2]
  11. 4(c) Obtain the solution by separation of variables method of the following IBVP for the Laplace equation: \[ u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0\text{ for }0<x<\pi\text{ and }0<y<\pi \] where $u(x,0)=x^2$, $u(x,\pi)=0$, $u_x=0$ at $(0,y)$ and $(\pi,y)$. \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [5]
  12. 5(a) Use Laplace transform to solve: \begin{align*} \text{PDE:}\quad &u_{tt}=u_{xx},\quad 0<x<2017,\ t>0\\ \text{BC's:}\quad &u(0,t)=u(2017,t)=0,\quad t>0\\ \text{IC's:}\quad &u(x,0)=\sin(\pi x),\quad \left.u_t\right]_{(x,0)}=-\sin(\pi x),\quad 0<x<2017. \end{align*} [5]
  13. 5(b) Use the method of integral transform to show that \[ u(x,t)=\frac1{2\sqrt{\pi t}}\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(v)e^{-\frac{(x-v)^2}{4t}}dv \] is the solution of the heat equation $u_t=u_{xx}$, $-\infty<x<\infty$, $t>0$ with $u=f(x)$ when $t=0$, $u(x,t)$ is bounded. [5]
  14. 6(a) Write the Hadamard's conditions for a well-posed PDE. [2]
  15. 6(b) Solve the following interior Dirichlet problem for a circle: PDE: $\nabla^2u=0$, $0\leq r<a$, $0\leq\theta<2\pi$ BC: $u(a,\theta)=f(\theta)$, $0\leq\theta<2\pi$ where $f(\theta)$ is a continuous function on $\partial\mathbb R$. Hence deduce Poisson's integral formula of the form \[ u(r,\theta)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\varphi=0}^{2\pi}\frac{(a^2-r^2)f(\theta)}{a^2-2ar\cos(\varphi-\theta)+r^2}d\varphi. \] \endgroup \begingroup\renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of value as indicated in the margin.\par (Notations and symbols have their usual meaning unless otherwise stated)} [6+2]
