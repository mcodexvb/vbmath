MMO-31(P5)

Rings and Modules-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1(a) If $\{M_\alpha\}_{\triangleleft}$ is a family of submodules of $M$, then\par \hspace*{1em}i) Define $\sum_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha$\par \hspace*{1em}ii) Show that $\sum_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha$ is the intersection of all the submodules of $M$ and contain the set $\bigcup_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha$.
  2. 1(b) Let $N$ be a submodule of an $R$-module $M$. Prove that $M/N$ is simple if and only if $N$ is a maximal submodule of $M$.
  3. 1(c) Let $M$ be a $\mathbb Z$-module. Denote $N=\{x\in M\mid nx=0\}$. Show that $Hom_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z_n,M)\simeq N$. \hfill [3+4+3]
  4. 2(a) Let $\{M_\alpha\}_{\triangleleft}$ be a family of $R$-modules. Define $\prod_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha$. State and prove the universal mapping property of $\prod_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha$.
  5. 2(b) Let $\{M_\alpha\}_{\triangleleft}$ be a family of $R$-modules and suppose that $\triangleleft=\Gamma\cup\Lambda$. If $\Gamma\cap\triangleleft=\varphi$, then show that $\prod_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha\simeq\prod_\Gamma M_\alpha\oplus\prod_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha$.
  6. 2(c) Let $\{M_\alpha\}_{\triangleleft}$ be a family of submodules of an $R$-modules $M$. Such that $M=\sum_{\triangleleft}N_\alpha$. Show that the sum $\sum_{\triangleleft}N_\alpha$ is direct if and only if every $x\in M$ can be written uniquely as a sum $x=\sum_{\triangleleft}x_\alpha$ where $x_\alpha\in M_\alpha$ for every $\alpha\in\triangleleft$. \hfill [3+4+3]
  7. 3(a) Prove that an $R$-module $F$ has a basis if and only if it satisfies UMP.
  8. 3(b) Let $V$ be an infinite dimensional vector space. Show that every subspace of $V$ is a direct summand.
  9. 3(c) Let $F$ be an $R$-module. Show that $F$ is free if and only if $F\simeq\bigoplus_{\triangleleft}R$. \hfill [4+2+4]
  10. 4(a) Define tensor product of two $R$-modules $M_R$ and ${}_RN$. Show that tensor product of two $R$-modules is unique upto isomorphism.
  11. 4(b) Show that tensor product of two free $R$-modules is free.
  12. 4(c) Find $\mathbb Q\otimes\mathbb Z_{12}$. \hfill \par\hfill P.T.O. [4+4+2]
  13. 5(a) Define category. Show that pushout exists in $Mod_R$.
  14. 5(b) Give an example with justifications of a covariant functor.
  15. 5(c) Prove that $Hom_R\!\left(\bigoplus_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha,N\right)\simeq\prod_{\triangleleft}Hom_R(M_\alpha,N)$. \hfill [4+3+3]
  16. 6(a) If $M_1\xrightarrow{f}M$ is a split monomorphism with splitting map $f':M\to M_1$, then show that $M=imf\oplus\ker f'$.
  17. 6(b) Prove that a short exact sequence splits on the left if and only if it splits on the right.
  18. 6(c) Let $O\to M_1\xrightarrow{f}M\xrightarrow{g}M_2\to O$ be a split short exact sequence. Prove that $M\simeq M_1\oplus M_2$. \hfill [3+4+3]
