Paper

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2021
  1. 1(a) Define convex set. Test whether the set $X=\{(x,y)\subset\mathbb R^2:y\le|x|\}$ is convex or not. [1+2]
  2. 1(b) Verify whether optimum solution of the LPP\\ Maximize $Z=x_1+x_2$\\ subject to \[\begin{aligned}2x_1-x_2&\ge-1\\x_1&\le2\\x_1+x_2&\le3\\x_1,x_2&\ge0\end{aligned}\] exists or not. Is that unique, if exists? [4]
  3. 1(c) Consider the system of equations \[\begin{aligned}2x_1+x_2-5x_3&=20\\4x_1-3x_2-10x_3&=15.\end{aligned}\] Find, if possible, the basic solution with $x_1$ as non-basic variable. [3]
  4. 2(a) Check whether $(2,3,1)$ is a basic feasible solution of the system of equations \[\begin{aligned}2x_1+x_2+4x_3&=11\\3x_1+x_2+5x_3&=14.\end{aligned}\] Find other basic feasible solutions, if exists. [5]
  5. 2(b) Solve the LPP\\ Minimize $Z=-4x_1-10x_2$\\ subject to \[\begin{aligned}2x_1+x_2&\le50\\2x_1+5x_2&\le100\\2x_1+3x_2&\le90\\x_1,x_2&\ge0\end{aligned}\] by the simplex method, if possible. [5]
  6. 3(a) Obtain the dual of the LPP\\ Maximize $Z=x_1+x_2+x_3$\\ subject to \[\begin{aligned}x_1-3x_2+4x_3&=5\\x_1-2x_2&\ge3\\2x_2-x_3&\le4\end{aligned}\] $x_1,x_2\ge0$ and $x_3$ is unrestricted in sign. [4]
  7. 3(b) Solve the following LPP by two-phase method.\\ Maximize $Z=5x_1+x_2-2x_3+x_4$\\ subject to \[\begin{aligned}x_1+5x_2-8x_3+3x_4&=6\\3x_1-x_2+x_3+x_4&=2\\x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4&\ge0.\end{aligned}\] [6]
  8. 4(a) Find the optimal solution and the minimum cost of the transportation problem \[\begin{array}{c|rrrr|r} &D_1&D_2&D_3&D_4&a_i\\\hline O_1&5&3&6&4&30\\O_2&3&4&7&8&15\\O_3&9&6&5&8&15\\\hline b_j&10&25&18&7& \end{array}.\] [5]
  9. 4(b) Obtain the optimal assignment and minimum cost for the assignment problem \[\begin{array}{c|rrrrr} &I&II&III&IV&V\\\hline A&11&17&8&16&20\\B&9&7&12&6&15\\C&13&16&15&12&16\\D&21&24&17&28&26\\E&14&10&12&11&15 \end{array}.\] [5]
  10. 5(a) Define pure strategy and mixed strategy of a game. [2]
  11. 5(b) Determine value of $a$ so that the game with the following pay off matrix is strictly determinable. \textit{[The prompt uses \(a\), and the matrix prints \(\lambda\).]} \[\begin{array}{|r|r|r|}\hline\lambda&7&3\\\hline -2&\lambda&-8\\\hline -3&4&\lambda\\\hline\end{array}\] [3]
  12. 5(c) Solve the game problem graphically \[\begin{array}{c|rr}&B_1&B_2\\\hline A_1&2&-1\\A_2&3&2\\A_3&-1&5\\A_4&-2&1\end{array}.\] [5]
  13. 1(a) Define sampling distribution of a statistic. Find the sampling distribution of the mean for the gamma population. [1+2]
  14. 1(b) Find the variance of the sample variance $S^2$ for a random sample drawn from a normal $(m,\sigma)$ population. Show that $S^2$ is a consistent but biased estimate of $\sigma^2$. [3+2+2]
  15. 2(a) Find the distribution of the smallest sample value in a sample of size $n$ from the population whose distribution is given by \[dF=e^{-x}\,dx,\quad 0\le x\le\infty.\] Also, find the mean and variance of the distribution. [4+1+1]
  16. 2(b) Prove that the maximum likelihood estimate of the parameter $\lambda$ of a population having density function $2(\lambda-x)/\lambda^2$, $0<x<\lambda$, for a sample of unit size is $2x$, $x$ being the sample value. Hence show that the estimate is biased. [3+1]
  17. 3(a) Let $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ be a random sample of size $n$ drawn from a $N(0,\sigma)$ population. Show that the statistic $\sum_{i=1}^n \frac{x_i^2}{n}$ is an unbiased estimate of $\sigma^2$. Examine whether this estimate is consistent. [2+4]
  18. 3(b) Using the Neyman-Pearson theorem construct a test of the null hypothesis $H_0:m=m_0$ against an alternative $H_1:m=m_1$ for a $N(m,\sigma)$ population where $\sigma$ is known and $m_0>m_1$. [4]
  19. 4(a) In the framework of hypothesis test, define Type I and Type II errors. Hence define the level of significance and power of the test.
  20. 4(b) Let $p$ denote the probability of getting a head when a given coin is tossed once. Suppose that the hypothesis $H_0:5=0.5$ is rejected in favor of $H_1:p=0.6$ if 10 trials result in 7 or more heads. Calculate the probabilities of Type I and Type II errors. \textit{[The null hypothesis prints \(5=0.5\).]} [2+2]
  21. 5(a) The weights in gram of a sample of 12 items are 7, 13, 22, 15, 12, 14, 18, 8, 21, 23, 10, 17 taken at random from its population which is normal having standard deviation 5. Find 95\% confidence limits for the mean of the population. Given that \[\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{1.96}^\infty e^{-x^2/2}\,dx=0.025.\] [4]
  22. 5(b) Find by the method of likelihood ratio testing a test for the null hypothesis $H_0:m=m_0$ for a $N(m,\sigma)$ population when $\sigma$ is known. [6]
