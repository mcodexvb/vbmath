CC-9

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2022
  1. 1 Define simple harmonic motion. A particle moves from rest in a straight line under an attractive force $\mu\times(\text{distance})^{-2}$ per unit mass to a fixed point on the line. Show that if the initial distance from the center of force be \lq$2a$\rq, then the distance will be \lq$a$\rq{} after a time $(\frac{\pi}{2}+1)\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}$. [2+8]
  2. 2 Obtain the expressions for tangential and normal accelerations of a particle moving in a plane curve. [10]
  3. 3(a) A particle describes an equiangular spiral $r=ae^{m\theta}$ with constant velocity. Find the components of velocity and acceleration along the radius vector and perpendicular to it. [5]
  4. 3(b) A particle describes a path with an acceleraton $\frac{\mu}{y^3}$ which is always parallel to the axis of $y$ and directed toward the $x$-axis. If the particle be projected from a point $(0,a)$ with the velocity $\frac{\sqrt\mu}{a}$ parallel to $x$-axis, then show that the path described by is a circle. [5]
  5. 4(a) If the central orbit be an ellipse under a force towards the centre, then find the law of force. Find also the velocity at any point of the orbit. [5]
  6. 4(b) A particle moves with a central acceleration $\mu(r+\frac{a^4}{r^3})$ being projected from an apse at a distance $a$ with a velocity $2\sqrt\mu a$. Prove that it describes the curve $r^2(2+\cos\sqrt3\theta)=3a^2$. [5]
  7. 5 Find the equation of a trajectory when a particle is projected with a velocity $u$ at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon in a medium whose resistance is $mk\times(\text{velocity})$. [10]
  8. 1 Define astatic equilibrium. Find the condition for astatic equilibrium of a system of coplanar forces acting at different points of a rigid body. [2+8]
  9. 2 Three forces $P,Q,R$ act along the sides of a triangle formed by the lines $x+y=3$, $2x+y=1$ and $x-y=-1$. Find the equation of the line of action of the resultant. [10]
  10. 3 Define \lq forces of constraints\rq. Use the principle of virtual work to find the conditions of equilibrium of a system of coplanar forces acting on a rigid body. [2+8]
  11. 4(a) Obtain the expression for virtual work done by the tension in a string. [5]
  12. 4(b) A solid hemisphere is supported by a string fixed to a point on its rim and to a point on a smooth vertical wall with which the curved surface of the hemisphere is in contact. If $\theta,\phi$ are the inclinations of the string and the plane base of the hemisphere to the vertical, prove that $\tan\phi=\frac38+\tan\theta$. [5]
  13. 5(a) Find the conditions of equilibrium of a system of non-coplanar forces acting at different points of a rigid body. [5]
  14. 5(b) Six forces, each equal to $P$, act along the edge of a cube, taken in order, which do not meet a given diagonal. Show that their resultant is a couple of moment $2\sqrt3.Pa$, where $a$ is the edge of the cube. \renewcommand{\meaningplural}{} \renewcommand{\paperplural}{} [5]
