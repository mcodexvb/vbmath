MMC-15

Classical Mechanics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2017
  1. 1(a) Discuss the existence and uniqueness of the solution of the PDE $2p+3q+8u=0$ subject to the initial conditions (i) $u(x,x)=x^4$, (ii) $u(x,\frac{3x-1}2)=x^2$, and (iii) $u(x,\frac{3x-1}2)=e^{-4x}$. [3]
  2. 1(b) Using $v=\log_e u$ and $v=f(x)+g(y)$, show that the solution of the Cauchy problem $y^2u_x^2+x^2u_y^2=(xyu)^2$, $u(x,0)=\exp(x^2)$ is $u(x,y)=\exp\bigl(x^2+i\frac{\sqrt3}2 y^2\bigr)$. [4]
  3. 1(c) Define Monge cone of a first order nonlinear PDE $f(x,y,u,p,q)=0$ at any point $(x_0,y_0,u_0)$. Hence find the Monge cone of the equation \(x^2p^2-q^2=1\) at \((2,1,3)\).\par
  4. 2(a) Solve: \[ (x^2D^2-xyDD'-2y^2D'^2+xD-2yD')u=\log_e\left(\frac yx\right)-\frac1{2017}. \] \[ \left[D^n\equiv\frac{\partial^n}{\partial x^n},\ D'^n\equiv\frac{\partial^n}{\partial y^n},\ u=u(x,y)\right] \] [4]
  5. 2(b) Reduce $y^2\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+x^2\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=0$, $x>0$, $y>0$, to canonical form. [6]
  6. 3(a) Solve the PDE $u(qs-pt)=pq^2$ by Monge's method. \[ \left[r=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2},\ s=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y},\ t=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}\right] \] [5]
  7. 3(b) Find by the method of characteristics, the integral surface of $pq=xy$ which passes through the curve $u=x$, $y=0$. [5]
  8. 4(a) Define the domain in which the following PDEs can be classified as either elliptic or parabolic or hyperbolic: \begin{romanparts} \item $t-xs+py+qx=0$, \item $r+2xs+(1-y^2)t=0$. \end{romanparts} [3]
  9. 4(b) A uniform string of length $L$ is stretched tightly between two fixed points at $x=0$ and $x=L$. If it is displaced a small distance $\epsilon$ at a point $x=b$, $0<b<L$, and released from rest at time $t=0$, find an expression for the displacement at subsequent times, satisfying the PDE $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=c^2\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$. $[u(x,t)$ denotes the displacement of the string at a position $x$ and at any time $t]$ \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [7]
  10. 5(a) Using the Laplace transform method, solve the following IBVP: \begin{align*} \text{PDE:}\quad &\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=\frac1{c^2}\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}-\cos(\omega t),\quad 0\leq x<\infty,\quad 0\leq t<\infty,\\ \text{BC's:}\quad &u(0,t)=0,\ u(x,t)\text{ is bounded as }x\to\infty,\\ \text{IC's:}\quad &u(x,0)=\left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\right]_{(x,0)}=0. \end{align*} [5]
  11. 5(b) Use finite Fourier cosine transform to solve: \[ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=k^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2},\quad 0<x<\pi,\ t>0, \] with the boundary condition $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}=0$ when $x=0$ and $x=\pi$, $t>0$, and the initial condition $u=f(x)$ when $t=0$, $0<x<\pi$. [5]
  12. 6(a) If the Dirichlet problem for a bounded region has a solution, then it is unique whereas if the Neumann problem for a bounded region has a solution, then it is either unique or it differs from the one another by a constant only -- prove it. [3]
  13. 6(b) Solve the following PDE having steady state temperature distribution in a semi-circular plate of radius $a$: \begin{align*} \text{PDE:}\quad &\nabla^2u(r,\theta)=u_{rr}+\frac1r u_r+\frac1{r^2}u_{\theta\theta}=0,\\ \text{BC's:}\quad &u(a,\theta)=U_0,\ u(r,0)=0,\ u(r,\pi)=0. \end{align*} [7]
