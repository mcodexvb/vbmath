MMO-41

Magneto-hydrodynamics and Magnetized Plasmas-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1 Obtain the dispersion relation for stimulated Raman scattering in the nonlinear interaction of circularly polarized $EM$ waves with low-frequency electron plasma oscillations. [10]
  2. 2(a) Explain the concepts of `phase velocity' and `group velocity' of a wave propagating in a conducting medium. [3+3]
  3. 2(b) For the motion of an electron in an oscillating $EM$ field derive an expression for the ponderomotive force. [4]
  4. 3 Consider the propagation of nonlinear wave packets in a dispersive plasma medium. Assuming a plane wave form of a perturbation, derive the nonlinear Schr\"odinger equation. Hence discuss the linear stability of its plane wave solution. [5+5]
  5. 4 Write short notes on\par (i) Whistler waves, (ii) Helicons. [5+5]
  6. 5 Starting from the Vlasov-Poisson system of equations obtain the dispersion relation for electron plasma oscillations. Hence explain the concept of two-stream instability in the wave-particle interaction. [5+5]
  7. 6 Explain the concept of a kinetic Alfv\'en wave in plasmas. How is it different from the usual $MHD$ Alfv\'en wave? Show that the dispersion relation for kinetic Alfv\'en waves can be obtained as $\omega^2=\mathrm{k}_z^2\mathrm{V}_A^2\left(1+\mathrm{k}_x^2\rho_i^2\right),$\par where $\omega$ is the wave frequency, $k_{x,z}$ are the components of the wave vector along the x- and z- axes, and $\rho_i$ is the ion gyroradius. [2+2+6]
