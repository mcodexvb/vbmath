BMC-42

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) Peter wants to keep some hens with him and has Rs. 20000. The old hens can be bought for Rs. 300 but young ones cost Rs. 400. The old hens lay 20 eggs per week and the young ones 60 eggs per week, each worth being Rs. 5. The feed for young and old hens costs Rs. 100 and Rs. 60 per hen per week. He can keep 60 hens with him. How many of each kind of hens Peter should buy to get a profit of more than Rs. 5000. Formulate as an LPP. [3]
  2. 1(b) Solve graphically the LPP \[\begin{aligned} \text{Maximize }z&=x_1+x_2\\ \text{subject to }x_1+x_2&\le1,\\-3x_1+x_2&\ge3,\\x_1,x_2&\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [3]
  3. 1(c) Use simplex method to obtain the inverse of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}3&2\\4&-1\end{pmatrix}$. [4]
  4. 2(a) The two linearly independent equations with three variables are given below: \[\begin{aligned}2x_1-3x_2+5x_3&=10,\\4x_1+x_2+10x_3&=20.\end{aligned}\] Find, if possible, a basic solution with $x_2$, a non-basic variable. [3]
  5. 2(b) Justify or correct the following statement: The objective function of an LPP assumes its optimal value at an extreme point of the convex set of feasible solutions. [2]
  6. 2(c) Prove that the set $X=\{(x_1,x_2):x_1x_2\le2018,\ x_1\ge0,\ x_2\ge0\}$ is not a convex set. [3]
  7. 2(d) State fundamental duality theorem of LPP. Why is it called fundamental?
  8. 3(a) Solve by Charne's Big M-method and prove that the following LPP has no feasible solution: \[\begin{aligned} \text{Maximize }z&=2x_1-x_2+5x_3\\ \text{subject to }x_1+2x_2+2x_3&\le2,\\ \tfrac52x_1+3x_2+4x_3&=12,\\4x_1+3x_2+2x_3&\ge24,\\ x_1,x_2,x_3&\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [4]
  9. 3(b) In a primal problem, the $5^{\mathrm{th}}$ constraint is an equation and the $4^{\mathrm{th}}$ variable is unrestricted in sign. What will be nature of the $5^{\mathrm{th}}$ dual variable and the $4^{\mathrm{th}}$ dual constraint? By solving the dual of the LPP \[\begin{aligned} \text{Minimize }z&=2x_1+2x_2\\ \text{subject to }2x_1+4x_2&\ge1,\\x_1+2x_2&\ge1,\\2x_1+x_2&\ge1,\\x_1,x_2&\ge0, \end{aligned}\] show that the optimal value of the primal as also of the dual is $\frac43$.
  10. 4(a) Solve the following LPP by two-phase method: \[\begin{aligned} \text{Minimize }z&=4x_1+x_2\\ \text{subject to }x_1+2x_2&\le3,\\4x_1+3x_2&\ge6,\\3x_1+x_2&=3,\\x_1,x_2&\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [5]
  11. 4(b) Use dual simplex method to solve the LPP: \[\begin{aligned} \text{Maximize }z&=-2x_1-3x_2-x_3\\ \text{subject to }2x_1+x_2+2x_3&\ge3,\\3x_1+2x_2+x_3&\ge4,\\x_1,x_2,x_3&\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [5]
  12. 5(a) Formulate the following transportation problem as an LPP: \[\begin{array}{r|ccc|l} &D_1&D_2&D_3&a_i\ (\text{supply})\\\hline O_1&c_{11}&c_{12}&c_{13}&a_1\\O_2&c_{21}&c_{22}&c_{23}&a_2\\\hline b_j\ (\text{Demand})&b_1&b_2&b_3& \end{array}\] where $c_{ij}$ is the cost coefficient. [2]
  13. 5(b) In a transportation problem \[\begin{array}{r|rrr} &D_1&D_2&D_3\\\hline O_1&10&9&8\\O_2&10&7&10\\O_3&11&9&7\\O_4&12&14&10 \end{array}\] $x_{11}=6$, $x_{12}=2$, $x_{22}=7$, $x_{32}=1$, $x_{33}=8$, $x_{41}=4$ is an initial basic feasible solution (IBFS). Is the solution optimal? Find the minimum cost of the problem. [4]
  14. 5(c) Find the optimal assignment and the corresponding optimal cost from the following cost matrix: \[\begin{array}{rr|rrrrr} &&\multicolumn{5}{c}{\text{Men}}\\ &&\mathrm I&\mathrm{II}&\mathrm{III}&\mathrm{IV}&\mathrm V\\\hline &A&1&3&2&3&6\\&B&2&4&3&1&5\\ \text{Jobs}&C&5&6&3&4&6\\&D&3&1&4&2&2\\&E&1&5&6&5&4 \end{array}\] [4]
  15. 6 Attempt \emph{any two} questions: [$5\times2=10$]
  16. 6(a) Define pure and mixed strategies in connection with game problem. What is a two-person-zero-sum game? Find the range of values of $p$ and $q$ so that 2 is the value of the following game: \[\begin{array}{rr|rrr} &&\multicolumn{3}{c}{\text{Player B}}\\&&B_1&B_2&B_3\\\hline &A_1&0&2&3\\\text{Player A}&A_2&8&5&q\\&A_3&2&p&4 \end{array}\]
  17. 6(b) In a rectangular game the pay-off matrix is given by: \[\begin{array}{rr|rrrrr} &&\multicolumn{5}{c}{\text{Player B}}\\&&B_1&B_2&B_3&B_4&B_5\\\hline &A_1&10&5&5&20&4\\\text{Player A}&A_2&11&15&10&17&25\\&A_3&7&12&8&9&8\\&A_4&5&13&9&10&5 \end{array}\] Explain whether the players will use pure or mixed strategies. What is the value of the game?
  18. 6(c) Solve the following $4\times2$ game geometrically: \[\begin{array}{rr|rr} &&\multicolumn{2}{c}{\text{Player B}}\\&&B_1&B_2\\\hline &A_1&2&-1\\\text{Player A}&A_2&3&2\\&A_3&-1&5\\&A_4&-2&1 \end{array}\] Interpret the game result in the context of biasedness.
