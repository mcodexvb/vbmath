CC-11

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2019
  1. 1(a) Prove that the integral $\int_0^\infty x^{m-1}e^{-x}dx$ is convergent if and only if $m>0$. [3]
  2. 1(b) A function $f$ is defined on $[0,1]$ by $f(0)=0$ and $f(x)=(-1)^{n+1}(n+1)$, for $\frac1{n+1}<x\le\frac1n$ $(n=1,2,3,\dots)$. Examine convergence of $(i)\int_0^1f(x)dx$ $(ii)\int_0^1|f|(x)dx$. [4]
  3. 1(c) Show that the improper integral $\int_1^\infty\frac x{1+x^2}\sin x\,dx$ is convergent. [3]
  4. 2(a) State and prove Abel's test for convergence of improper integrals. [4]
  5. 2(b) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of continuous functions defined on a subset $D$ of \unreadable{missing glyph (printed as an empty box; presumably $\mathbb R$)} and $\{f_n\}$ be uniformly convergent on $D$ to a function $f$. Show that $f$ is continuous on $D$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Define the sequence of functions $\{f_n\}$ on $[0,1]$ by $f_n(x)=nxe^{-nx^2}$, $x\in[0,1]$. Show that $\{f_n\}$ is not uniformly convergent on $[0,1]$. [3]
  7. 3(a) Prove that the sequence $\{f_n\}$ defined by $f_n(x)=\frac{nx}{1+nx}$, $x\ge0$ is not uniformly convergent on $[0,\infty)$, but the convergence is uniform on $[a,\infty)$ if $a>0$.
  8. 3(b) The sequence of functions $\{f_n\}$ is defined on $[0,1]$ by $f_n(x)=x^{n-1}-x^n$, $x\in[0,1]$. Examine the uniform convergence of $\{f_n\}$ on $[0,1]$. [3]
  9. 3(c) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of $R$-integrable functions defined on $[a,b]$ which is uniformly convergent to a function $f$ on $[a,b]$. Show that $f$ is $R$-integrable on $[a,b]$. Also prove that $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^bf_n(x)dx=\int_a^bf(x)dx$. [4]
  10. 4(a) Define radius of convergence of a power series. Determine the radius of convergence of the power series $\frac13-x+\frac{x^2}{3^2}-x^3+\frac{x^4}{3^4}-x^5+\dots$.
  11. 4(b) Let $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ be a power series with radius of convergence $R(>0)$. Let $f(x)$ be the sum of the series on $(-R,R)$. Show that $f$ is continuous on $(-R,R)$. [3]
  12. 4(c) Let $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$, $x\in[0,1]$ be a power series with radius of convergence 1. If $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n$ be convergent, then prove that $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ is uniformly convergent on $[0,1]$. [4]
  13. 5(a) Find the sum of the series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty(2^n+3^n)x^n$, with its range of validity. [3]
  14. 5(b) Is the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty\sin nx$ a Fourier series? Justify your answer. [2]
  15. 5(c) Expand in a series of sines and cosines of multiples in $x$ of the function given by $f(x)=\begin{cases}x-\pi,&\text{when }-\pi<x<0,\\\pi-x,&\text{when }0<x<\pi.\end{cases}$ What is the sum of the series for $x=\pm\pi$ and $x=0$?
  16. 7 Answer \textit{any five} questions. [$5\times2=10$]
  17. 7(a) Find the partial differential equation (PDE) of all planes which have equal $x$ and $y$ intercepts, and hence classify the PDE.
  18. 7(b) For an arbitrary continuously differentiable function $\phi$, show that $x^2+y^2+z^2=\phi(xy)$, $z=z(x,y)$ is a general solution of $z(px-qy)=y^2-x^2$, $p=\frac{\partial z}{\partial x},q=\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$.
  19. 7(c) Show that the Cauchy problem $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=0$, $z(x,y)=x$ on $x^2+y^2=1$ has a unique solution in $\{(x,y)\in\unreadable{missing glyph (printed as an empty box; presumably $\mathbb R^2$)}:(x,y)\ne(0,0)\}$.
  20. 7(d) Show that the PDEs $f(x,y,p,q)=0$, $g(x,y,p,q)=0$ are compatible if $\frac{\partial(f,g)}{\partial(x,p)}+\frac{\partial(f,g)}{\partial(y,q)}=0$.
  21. 7(e) Let $z=z(x,y)$ be a solution of $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=1$, $z(x,y)=0$ on $x+y=0$. Then, find the value of $z(0,1)$.
  22. 7(f) Verify that $z=ax+by+a+b-ab$ is a complete integral of the PDE $z=px+qy+p+q-pq$, where $a$ and $b$ are arbitrary constants.
  23. 7(g) Let $x=x(t,s)$, $y=y(t,s)$, $z=z(t,s)$, $s\in\unreadable{missing glyph (printed as an empty box; presumably $\mathbb R$)}$ be the characteristics curve of the PDE $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}-4z=0$, passing through a given curve $x_0=0$, $y_0=s$, $z_0=s^2$. Then, find the initial strip.
  24. 7(h) Find the value of $P(2019,2020)$, where $P(x,y)$ be a particular integral of the PDE $\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x^2}-\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=2y-x^2$.
  25. 8(a) Find the integral surface of the PDE $\frac{y-z}{yz}p+\frac{z-x}{zx}q=\frac{x-y}{xy}$, which passes through the parabola $z=1$, $x^2=y$.
  26. 8(b) Let $z(x,y)=2f(y)\cos(x-2y)$, $(x,y)\in\unreadable{missing glyph (printed as an empty box; presumably $\mathbb R^2$)}$, be a solution of the initial value problem (IVP) $2\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=z$, $z(x,0)=\cos(x)$. Then, find the value of $f(1)$. [5+5]
  27. 9(a) Show that the PDEs $xpq+yq^2=1$ and $p=2019q$ are compatible and find a one-parameter family of common solutions.
  28. 9(b) Apply Charpit's method to solve the PDE $p=(qy+z)^2$, passing through $y=x^2$, $xz=3$. [5+5]
  29. 10(a) Find by the method of characteristics, the integral surface of $p^2x+qy=z$, $z(s,1)=-s$.
  30. 10(b) Solve: $(D^2-2DD'+D'^2-3D+3D'+2)z=\sin(2x+3y)$. $\left[D^n\equiv\frac{\partial^n}{\partial x^n},D'^n\equiv\frac{\partial^n}{\partial y^n},z=z(x,y)\right]$ [5+5]
