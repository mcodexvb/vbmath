TIFR Graduate School · GS 2024 · Mathematics

  1. 1 What is the number of even positive integers $n$ such that every group of order $n$ is abelian? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item Greater than $2$, but finite \item Infinite \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $S = \{g \in \mathbb{R}[x] \mid g \text{ is a polynomial of degree at most } n\}$. For $g \in S$ let $A_g = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid e^x = g(x)\} \subset \mathbb{R}$. Let $m = \min\{\#A_g \mid g \in S\}$, and $M = \max\{\#A_g \mid g \in S\}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $m = 0$, $M = n$ \item $m = 0$, $M = n + 1$ \item $m = 1$, $M = n$ \item $m = 1$, $M = n + 1$ \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $V, W$ be nonzero finite dimensional vector spaces over $\mathbb{C}$. Let $m$ be the dimension of the space of $\mathbb{C}$-linear transformations $V \to W$ viewed as a real vector space. Let $n$ be the dimension of the space of $\mathbb{R}$-linear transformations $V \to W$ viewed as a real vector space. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $n = m$ \item $2n = m$ \item $n = 2m$ \item $4n = m$ \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Consider the real vector space of infinite sequences of real numbers \[ S = \{(a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots) \mid a_k \in \mathbb{R}, \, k = 0, 1, 2, \dots\}. \] Let $W$ be the subspace of $S$ consisting of all sequences $(a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots)$ which satisfy the relation $a_{k+2} = 2a_{k+1} + a_k$, $k = 0, 1, 2, \dots$. What is the dimension of $W$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $3$ \item $\infty$ \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $f: [0,\infty) \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. If $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^1 f(x + n)\,dx = 2$, then which of the following statements about the limit $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^1 f(nx)\,dx$ is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The limit exists and equals $0$ \item The limit exists and equals $\frac{1}{2}$ \item The limit exists and equals $2$ \item None of the remaining three options is correct \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to [0,\infty)$ be a function such that for any finite set $E \subset \mathbb{R}$ we have $\sum_{x \in E} f(x) \le 1$. Let $C_f = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid f(x) > 0\} \subset \mathbb{R}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $C_f$ is finite \item $C_f$ is a bounded subset of $\mathbb{R}$ \item $C_f$ has at most one limit point \item $C_f$ is a countable set \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 Let $p$ be a prime. Which of the following statements is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exists a noncommutative ring with exactly $p$ elements \item There exists a noncommutative ring with exactly $p^2$ elements \item There exists a noncommutative ring with exactly $p^3$ elements \item None of the remaining three statements is correct \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Consider the sequence $\{a_n\}$ for $n \ge 1$ defined by $a_n = \lim_{N \to \infty} \sum_{k=n}^N \frac{1}{k^2}$. Which of the following statements about this sequence is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\lim_{n \to \infty} n a_n$ does not exist \item $\lim_{n \to \infty} n a_n$ exists and equals $2$ \item $\lim_{n \to \infty} n a_n$ exists and equals $1$ \item $\lim_{n \to \infty} n^2 a_n$ exists and equals $1$ \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function that is a solution to the ordinary differential equation \[ f'(t) = \sin^2(f(t)) \quad (\forall t \in \mathbb{R}), \qquad f(0) = 1. \] Which of the following statements is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ is neither bounded nor periodic \item $f$ is bounded and periodic \item $f$ is bounded, but not periodic \item None of the remaining three statements is correct \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Let $B$ denote the set of invertible upper triangular $2 \times 2$ matrices with entries in $\mathbb{C}$, viewed as a group under matrix multiplication. Which of the following subgroups of $B$ is the normalizer of itself in $B$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & 0 \\ 0 & b \end{pmatrix} \;\middle|\; a, b \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\} \right\}$ \item $\left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & 0 \\ 0 & a \end{pmatrix} \;\middle|\; a \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\} \right\}$ \item $\left\{ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & c \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \;\middle|\; c \in \mathbb{C} \right\}$ \item $\left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & c \\ 0 & a \end{pmatrix} \;\middle|\; a \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}, \, c \in \mathbb{C} \right\}$ \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 What is the least positive integer $n > 1$ such that $x^n$ and $x$ are conjugate, for every $x \in S_{11}$? Here, $S_{11}$ denotes the symmetric group on $11$ letters. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $10$ \item $11$ \item $12$ \item $13$ \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Consider the following statements: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item Let $G$ be a group and let $H \subset G$ be a subgroup of index $2$. Then $[G, G] \subseteq H$. \item Let $G$ be a group and let $H \subset G$ be a subgroup that contains the commutator subgroup $[G, G]$ of $G$. Then $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. \end{enumerate} Which of the following statements is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (A) and (B) are both true \item (A) and (B) are both false \item (A) is true and (B) is false \item (A) is false and (B) is true \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 For any symmetric real matrix $A$, let $\lambda(A)$ denote the largest eigenvalue of $A$. Let $S$ be the set of positive definite symmetric $3 \times 3$ real matrices. Which of the following assertions is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exist $A, B \in S$ such that $\lambda(A + B) < \max(\lambda(A), \lambda(B))$ \item For all $A, B \in S$, $\lambda(A + B) > \max(\lambda(A), \lambda(B))$ \item There exist $A, B \in S$ such that $\lambda(A + B) = \max(\lambda(A), \lambda(B))$ \item None of the remaining three assertions is correct \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 Let $\theta \in (0, \pi/2)$. Let $T: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ be the linear map which sends a vector $v$ to its reflection with respect to the line through $(0,0)$ and $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$. Then the matrix of $T$ with respect to the standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$ is given by \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\begin{pmatrix} \cos 2\theta & \sin 2\theta \\ \sin 2\theta & -\cos 2\theta \end{pmatrix}$ \item $\begin{pmatrix} \cos 2\theta & \sin 2\theta \\ -\sin 2\theta & \cos 2\theta \end{pmatrix}$ \item $\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & -\cos\theta \end{pmatrix}$ \item $\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}$ \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 For a polynomial $f(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}[x, y]$ let $X_f = \{(a, b) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid f(a, b) = 1\} \subset \mathbb{R}^2$. Which of the following statements is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $f(x, y) = x^2 + 4xy + 3y^2$, then $X_f$ is compact \item If $f(x, y) = x^2 - 3xy + 3y^2$, then $X_f$ is compact \item If $f(x, y) = x^2 - 4xy - y^2$, then $X_f$ is compact \item None of the remaining three statements is correct \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 What is the number of distinct subfields of $\mathbb{C}$ isomorphic to $\mathbb{Q}[\sqrt[3]{2}]$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $3$ \item Infinite \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $\mathbb{F}_3$ denote the finite field with $3$ elements. What is the number of one dimensional vector subspaces of the vector space $\mathbb{F}_3^5$ over $\mathbb{F}_3$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $5$ \item $121$ \item $81$ \item None of the remaining three options \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 For a positive integer $n$, let $a_n, b_n, c_n, d_n$ be the real numbers such that \[ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}^n = \begin{pmatrix} a_n & b_n \\ c_n & d_n \end{pmatrix}. \] Which of the following numbers equals $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n / b_n$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $e$ \item $3/2$ \item None of the remaining three options \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 Consider the complex vector space \[ V = \{f \in \mathbb{C}[x] \mid f \text{ has degree at most } 50, \text{ and } f(ix) = -f(x) \text{ for all } x \in \mathbb{C}\}. \] Then the dimension of $V$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $50$ \item $25$ \item $13$ \item $47$ \end{enumerate}
  20. 20 Let $S$ denote the set of sequences $a = (a_1, a_2, \dots)$ of real numbers such that $a_k$ equals $0$ or $1$ for each $k$. Then the function $f: S \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by \[ f((a_1, a_2, \dots)) = \frac{a_1}{10} + \frac{a_2}{10^2} + \dots \] is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item injective but not surjective \item surjective but not injective \item bijective \item neither injective nor surjective \end{enumerate}
  21. 1 If $G$ is a group of order $361$, then $G$ has a normal subgroup $H$ such that $H \cong G/H$.
  22. 2 There exists a metric space $X$ such that the number of open subsets of $X$ is exactly $2024$.
  23. 3 The function $d: \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ given by $d(x, y) = |e^x - e^y|$ defines a metric on $\mathbb{R}$, and $(\mathbb{R}, d)$ is a complete metric space.
  24. 4 Let $n$ be a positive integer, and $A$ an $n \times n$ matrix over $\mathbb{R}$ such that $A^3 = \operatorname{Id}$. Then $A$ is diagonalizable in $M_n(\mathbb{R})$, i.e., there exists $P \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ such that $P$ is invertible and $PAP^{-1}$ is a diagonal matrix.
  25. 5 If $A \in M_n(\mathbb{Q})$ is such that the characteristic polynomial of $A$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, then $A$ is diagonalizable in $M_n(\mathbb{C})$, i.e., there exists $P \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ such that $P$ is invertible and $PAP^{-1}$ is a diagonal matrix.
  26. 6 The complement of any countable union of lines in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is path connected.
  27. 7 The subsets $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid (y^2 - x)(y^2 - x - 1) = 0\}$ and $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid y^2 - x^2 = 1\}$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ (with the induced metric) are homeomorphic.
  28. 8 $\mathbb{Q} \cap [0,1]$ is a compact subset of $\mathbb{Q}$.
  29. 9 Suppose $f: X \to Y$ is a function between metric spaces, such that whenever a sequence $\{x_n\}$ converges to $x$ in $X$, the sequence $\{f(x_n)\}$ converges in $Y$ (but it is not given that the limit of $\{f(x_n)\}$ is $f(x)$). Then $f$ is continuous.
  30. 10 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, and assume that $|f'(x)| \ge 1$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Then for each compact set $C \subset \mathbb{R}$, the set $f^{-1}(C)$ is compact.
  31. 11 There exists a function $f: [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ which is not Riemann integrable and satisfies \[ \sum_{i=1}^n |f(t_i) - f(t_{i-1})|^2 < 1 \] for every choice of a positive integer $n$ and of $0 \le t_0 < t_1 < t_2 < \dots < t_n \le 1$.
  32. 12 Let $E \subset [0,1]$ be the subset consisting of numbers that have a decimal expansion which does not contain the digit $8$. Then $E$ is dense in $[0,1]$.
  33. 13 Let $G$ be a proper subgroup of $(\mathbb{R}, +)$ which is closed as a subset of $\mathbb{R}$. Then $G$ is generated by a single element.
  34. 14 There exists a unique function $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $f$ is continuous at $x = 0$, and such that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $f(x) + f\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) = x$.
  35. 15 A map $f: V \to W$ between finite dimensional vector spaces over $\mathbb{R}$ is a linear transformation if and only if $f(x) = f(x - a) + f(x - b) - f(x - a - b)$ for all $x, a, b \in V$.
  36. 16 Let $R$ be the ring $\mathbb{C}[x]/(x^2)$ obtained as the quotient of the polynomial ring $\mathbb{C}[x]$ by its ideal generated by $x^2$. Let $R^\times$ be the multiplicative group of units of this ring. Then there is an injective group homomorphism from $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}) \times (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})$ into $R^\times$.
  37. 17 Let $A \in M_2(\mathbb{Z})$ be such that $|A_{ij}(n)| \le 50$ for all $1 \le n \le 10^{50}$ and all $1 \le i, j \le 2$, where $A_{ij}(n)$ denotes the $(i, j)$-th entry of the $2 \times 2$ matrix $A^n$. Then $|A_{ij}(n)| \le 50$ for all positive integers $n$.
  38. 18 Let $A, B$ be subsets of $\{0, \dots, 9\}$. It is given that, on choosing elements $a \in A$ and $b \in B$ at random, $a + b$ takes each of the values $0, \dots, 9$ with equal probability. Then one of $A$ or $B$ is singleton.
  39. 19 If $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is uniformly continuous, then there exists $M > 0$ such that for all $x \in \mathbb{R} \setminus [-M, M]$, we have $f(x) < x^{100}$.
  40. 20 If a sequence $\{f_n\}$ of continuous functions from $[0,1]$ to $\mathbb{R}$ converges uniformly on $(0,1)$ to a continuous function $f: [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$, then $\{f_n\}$ converges uniformly on $[0,1]$ to $f$.
