MMC-16

Integral Transforms and Integral Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2020
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{five} questions from the following: [$5\times2=10$]
  2. 1(a) Solve the Fredholm integro-differential equation \[ u'(x)=-1+\cos x+\int_0^{\pi/2}tu(t)dt,\quad u(0)=0. \] [2]
  3. 1(b) Find the Laplace transform of $f(t)$, where $f(t)=e^{-2t}t^3+e^{-7t}\sin5t-t\cos3t$. [2]
  4. 1(c) Find the value of $u(0)+u(\frac\pi4)+u(\frac\pi2)$, where $u(x)$ satisfies the equation \[ \frac{d^2u}{dx^2}=-x+\int_0^x(x-t)u(t)dt,\quad u(0)=0,\ u'(0)=1. \] [2]
  5. 1(d) Using modified Adomian decomposition method, solve the integral equation \[ y(x)=\cos x-x(1-e^{\sin x})-\int_0^x xe^{\sin t}y(t)dt. \] [2]
  6. 1(e) Convert the integral equation $y(x)=5x^2+\int_0^1k(x,t)y(t)dt$ to an equivalent differential equation, where $k(x,t)=t(1-x)$ when $0\leq t\leq x$ and $k(x,t)=x(1-t)$ when $x\leq t\leq1$. [2]
  7. 1(f) Find the inverse Laplace transform of $F(s)$, where $F(s)=\dfrac{s}{s^2+4s+7}+\dfrac1{(s+3)^4}$. [2]
  8. 1(g) Find the eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the integral equation \[ y(x)=\lambda\int_0^5 e^x t y(t)dt. \] [2]
  9. 2(a) Find the resolvent kernel of the Fredholm integral equation \[ y(x)=x^{11}+\lambda\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(x+t)y(t)dt. \] [4]
  10. 2(b) Convert the differential equation $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+4xy=2$, $y(0)=0$, $y'(1)=1$ to an equivalent integral equation. [3]
  11. 2(c) Find the inverse Fourier sine transform of $\dfrac{e^{-as}}s$. Hence find the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty \dfrac{\sin2x}{x}dx$. [2+1]
  12. 3(a) Using Hilbert-Schmidt theorem, solve the integral equation $y(x)=x+\int_0^1(1-3xt)y(t)dt$. [4]
  13. 3(b) If $f$ and $g$ are piecewise continuous on $[0,\infty)$ and of exponential order, then prove that $L\{f*g\}=L\{f\}.L\{g\}$. Using the above result prove that $\beta(m,n)=\dfrac{\Gamma(m).\Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(m+n)}$. [2+1]
  14. 3(c) If $L\{f(t)\}=F(s)$, then prove that $L\{\frac{f(t)}t\}=\displaystyle\int_s^\infty F(x)dx$. Hence evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty \dfrac{e^{-4t}\sin t}{t}dt$. Does the Laplace transform of $\dfrac{\cos at}{t}$ exist? [1+1+1]
  15. 4(a) State Fredholm alternative theorem. Find the conditions for which the integral equation $y(x)=f(x)+\int_0^1k(x,t)y(t)dt$ has infinitely many solutions, where $k(x,t)=(t+1)x$ when $0\leq t\leq x$ and $k(x,t)=(x+1)t$ when $x\leq t\leq1$. [1+3]
  16. 4(b) Using Laplace transform, solve the differential equation \[ \frac{d^2y}{dt^2}+3\frac{dy}{dt}+4y=h(t),\quad y(0)=0,\ y'(0)=1, \] where $h(t)=3$ when $0\leq t\leq\frac\pi2$ and $h(t)=0$ when $t>\frac\pi2$. [3]
  17. 4(c) Solve the differential equation $t\dfrac{dx}{dt}-x=1$ by Laplace transform method. [2]
  18. 4(d) Give an example of a function which is neither piecewise continuous nor of exponential order but whose Laplace transform exists. [1]
  19. 5 Solve the Volterra integral equation $y(x)=x-\int_0^x (x-t)y(t)dt$ by
  20. 5(a) Laplace transform method. [2]
  21. 5(b) series solution method. [2]
  22. 5(c) Adomian decomposition method. [2]
  23. 5(d) successive approximations method. [2]
  24. 5(e) successive substitutions method. [2]
  25. 6(a) Solve the Volterra integral equation of first kind $e^{x^2/2}-1=\int_0^x\sin(x-t)u(t)dt$. [2]
  26. 6(b) Find the Fourier transform of $f(x)$, where $f(x)=1-x^2$ if $|x|<1$ and $f(x)=0$ if $|x|>1$. Hence using Persaval's identity, evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\left(\dfrac{\sin x-x\cos x}{x^3}\right)^2dx$. [3+1]
  27. 6(c) Prove that $L\{f(t)\}=\dfrac{\int_0^T e^{-st}f(t)dt}{1-e^{-Ts}}$, where $f(t)$ is a periodic function with period $T$.
