MMO-41(P2)

Advanced Topology-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2017
  1. 1(a) Prove that every regular space having countable locally finite base is normal. [5]
  2. 1(b) State and prove Nagata-Smirnov metrization theorem. [7]
  3. 2(a) Let $(X,U)$ be a uniform space and $D$ be the family of all pseudometrics on $X$ which are uniformly continuous from $(X\times X,\,U\times U)$ to $(\mathbb R,\,U_{\mathbb R})$ ($U_{\mathbb R}$ is the usual uniformity on $\mathbb R$). Prove that $D$ generates $U$. [4]
  4. 2(b) Let $U$ be the uniformity generated by a family $D$ of pseudometrics on a set $X$. For each $d\in D$, let $U_d$ be the uniformity induced by the pseudometric $d$ and $Y=\prod_{d\in D}(X_d,U_d),\ X_d=X,\ \forall d\in D$. Prove that the evaluation mapping $f:X\to Y$ defined by $(f(x))(d)=x,\ \forall d\in D,\ x\in X$, is a uniform embedding. [4]
  5. 2(c) Prove that a topological space is uniformizable iff it is completely regular. [4]
  6. 3(a) Prove that a convergent net in a uniform space is Cauchy. Is the converse true? Answer with reasons. Prove that a Cauchy net is convergent iff it has a cluster point. [5]
  7. 3(b) Prove that a uniform space is compact iff it is complete and totally bounded. [4]
  8. 3(c) Prove that every continuous function from a compact uniform space to a uniform space is uniformly continuous. [3]
  9. 4(a) Give an example of a topological group. [3]
  10. 4(b) If $H$ is an algebraic subgroup of a topological group $G$ then show that $\bar H$ is also a subgroup of $G$. Also show that every open subgroup is a clopen subgroup. [3]
  11. 4(c) Prove that in a topological group every component containing the identity element is a closed normal subgroup. [3]
  12. 4(d) For any open \textit{nbd} $U$ of $e$ of a connected topological group $G$, show that $G=\displaystyle\bigcup_{n\ge1}U^n$ \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}[ 2 ]\end{center} [3]
  13. 5(a) Let $(G,\tau)$ be a topological group and $H$ be a normal subgroup of $G$. Then prove that $\left(G/H,\tau'\right)$ is a topological group where $\tau'$ is the quotient topology induced by the canonical mapping $\phi:G\to G/H,\ \phi(x)=xH,\ x\in G$. Also show that $\phi$ is an open homomorphism from $(G,\tau)$ to $\left(G/H,\tau'\right)$. [4]
  14. 5(b) Let $\alpha$ be an algebraic homomorphism from a topological group $(G_1,\tau_1)$ into a topological group $(G_2,\tau_2)$ and let $H$ be the kernel of $\alpha$. Further let $\alpha=\alpha_0\circ\phi$ for some $\alpha_0:G/H\to G_2$, where $\phi:G_1\to G_1/H$ is the canonical mapping. Prove that $\alpha:(G_1,\tau_1)\to(G_2,\tau_2)$ is continuous or open iff $\alpha_0:\left(G_1/H,\tau'\right)\to(G_2,\tau_2)$ is continuous or open ($\tau'$ is the quotient topology on $G_1/H$). Hence show that if $\alpha:(G_1,\tau_1)\to(G_2,\tau_2)$ is open homomorphism then the topological group $\left(G/H,\tau'\right)$ is isomorphic to $(G_2,\tau_2)$. [4]
  15. 5(c) Prove that every locally compact Hausdorff topological group is normal. [4]
  16. 6 Answer \underline{any two} : \par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace Give an example of a $2^{\text{nd}}$ countable space which is not metrizable.\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace Show that if a topological group is $T_0$ then it is $T_3$.\par \hspace*{1em}(iii)\enspace Prove that a topological group is homogeneous.
