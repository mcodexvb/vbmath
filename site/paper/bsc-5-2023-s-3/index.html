DSEMA-1

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2023
  1. 1(a) Obtain the moment of inertia (M.I.) of a solid sphere about a diameter. [5]
  2. 1(b) Show that the M.I. of an elliptic area of mass $M$ and semi-axes $a$ and $b$ about a diameter of length $r$ is $M\frac{a^2b^2}{4r^2}$. [5]
  3. 2(a) Find the condition for a straight line, at any point of its length, to be a principal axis of a material system. [5]
  4. 2(b) A uniform lamina is bounded by a parabolic arc of latus rectum $4a$ and a double ordinate at a distance $b$ from the vertex. If $b=\frac a3(7+4\sqrt7)$, then show that two of the principal axes at the end of the latus rectum are the tangent and the normal there. [5]
  5. 3(a) Obtain the equation of motion of a rigid body relative to the centre of inertia. [5]
  6. 3(b) A rough uniform board of mass $M$ and length $2l$, rests on a smooth horizontal plane, and a man of mass $m$ walks on it from one end to the other. Show that the distance through which the board moves is \[\frac{2ml}{m+M}.\] [5]
  7. 4(a) Obtain kinetic energy of a rigid body rotating about an axis. [3]
  8. 4(b) A fine string has two masses $M$ and $M'$ tied to its ends and passes over a rough pulley, of mass $m$ and radius $a$, whose centre is fixed. If the string does not slip over the pulley, then show that $M$ will descend with an acceleration equal to \[\frac{(M-M')a^2}{(M+M')a^2+mk^2}g,\] where $k$ is the radius of gyration of the pulley. [7]
  9. 5(a) Define compound pendulum. Prove that the centres of oscillation and suspension are interchangeable. [1+4]
  10. 5(b) An elliptic lamina is such that when it swings about one latus rectum as a horizontal axis, the other latus rectum passes through the centre of oscillation. Prove that the eccentricity of the ellipse is $\frac12$. [5]
  11. 6(a) Obtain the kinetic energy of a body moving in two dimensions. [5]
  12. 6(b) A circular cylinder of radius $a$ and radius of gyration $k$ rolls without slipping inside a fixed hollow cylinder of radius $b$. Show that the plane through their axes moves like a circular pendulum of length \[(a-b)\left(\frac{\text{[obscured]}+k^2}{a^2}\right).\] [5]
  13. 1(a) What do you mean by a ‘homogeneous fluid’? Show that in a homogeneous fluid at rest under gravity, the difference of pressures between any two points is proportional to the difference of their depths. [1+4]
  14. 1(b) A fine glass tube in the form of an equilateral triangle is filled with equal volumes of three liquids which do not mix and whose densities are in arithmetic progression. The tube is held in a vertical plane, and the side that contains portions of the heaviest and lightest liquids make an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical. Show that the surface separation divides the side in the ratio $\cos(30^\circ-\alpha):\cos(30^\circ+\alpha)$. [5]
  15. 2(a) A homogeneous liquid of density $\rho$ is in equilibrium under the forces $\frac{\mu}{x}$, $\frac{\mu y}{y^2+z^2}$, $\frac{\mu z}{y^2+z^2}$ ($\mu$ is a constant) at $(x,y,z)$. Find the equation of the free surface. Also, show that pressure at the point $(x,y,z)$ is $\Pi+\rho\mu\log\sqrt{x^2(y^2+z^2)}$, where $\Pi$ is the atmospheric pressure. [4+1]
  16. 2(b) A hollow weightless cone of semi-vertical angle $\alpha$ and of height $h$ is filled with liquid of density $\rho$ and freely hung from a point on the rim of its base. Show that the thrust on the base is \[\frac{4\pi\rho gh^3\tan^3\alpha\sin\alpha}{\sqrt{1+15\sin^2\alpha}}.\] [5]
  17. 3(a) A cylindrical tumbler, half filled with a liquid of density $\rho'$, is filled up with a liquid of density $\rho$, which does not mix with the former one. Show that the ratio of the thrust on the base of the tumbler to the whole pressure on its curved surface is $2r(\rho+\rho'):h(\rho'+3\rho)$, where $h$ is the height of the tumbler and $r$ the radius of its base. [5]
  18. 3(b) Define centre of pressure of a given plane area in a fluid in equilibrium. Show that the depth of centre of pressure (C.P.) of a plane area immersed in a homogeneous liquid is greater than the depth of its centroid or centre of gravity (C.G.). [1+4]
