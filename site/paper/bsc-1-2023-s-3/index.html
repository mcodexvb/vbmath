SECMA-1

Algebra II and Analysis II
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2023
  1. 1(a) Define a consistent system of linear equations. What is the nature of the solution(s) of a consistent linear system of three equations in four variables. [[1+2]]
  2. 1(b) Let the linear system \begin{align*}x+3y&=f\\cx+dy&=g\end{align*} be consistent for every $(f,g)\in\mathbb R^2$. What can you say about the coefficients $c$ and $d$? If $f=g=0$, find the nature of the solutions. [[2+1]]
  3. 1(c) Suppose that for the linear system $AX=b$, the coefficient matrix $A$ has a pivot position in every row. Show that the system is consistent? What happens if $A$ has a pivot position in every column? [[2+2]]
  4. 2(a) Is $b=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\\6\end{pmatrix}$ a linear combination of $\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\0\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\2\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}5\\-6\\8\end{pmatrix}$? Justify. [[3]]
  5. 2(b) Find the value of $h$ so that $b\in span\{a_1,a_2\}$, where $a_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\4\\-2\end{pmatrix},a_2=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\\7\end{pmatrix},b=\begin{pmatrix}4\\1\\h\end{pmatrix}$. [[2]]
  6. 2(c) Find the $span\left\{\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\3\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\\4\end{pmatrix}\right\}$. [[2]]
  7. 2(d) Find a condition so that $b=\begin{pmatrix}h\\k\\t\end{pmatrix}$ is in $span\left\{\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}0\\2\\3\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\1\end{pmatrix}\right\}$. [[3]]
  8. 3(a) Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix. Show that the columns of $A$ spans $\mathbb R^n$ if and only if for each $b\in\mathbb R^n$, the equation $AX=b$ has a solution. [[4]]
  9. 3(b) Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&3&0&3\\-1&-1&-1&1\\0&-2&1&-4\\2&0&3&1\end{pmatrix}$. Does the equation $AX=b$ have a solution for each $b\in\mathbb R^4$? [[3]]
  10. 3(c) Let $A$ is a $3\times3$ matrix. If there is a vector $b\in\mathbb R^3$ such that the equation $AX=b$ has a unique solution, then show that the columns of $A$ spans $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  11. 4(a) Let $A$ be a $7\times5$ matrix. If the columns of $A$ are linearly independent, find the number of pivot columns of $A$. Does $AX=0$ have unique solution? [[3]]
  12. 4(b) Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix. Show that the columns of $A$ are linearly independent if and only if for each $b\in\mathbb R^m$, the equation $AX=b$ has at most one solution. [[4]]
  13. 4(c) Show that three vectors $a_1,a_2,a_3\in\mathbb R^3$ are linearly independent if and only if they can not be on the same plane. [[3]]
  14. 5(a) Define subspace and a basis of a subspace. Show that the null space of a matrix is a subspace. [[3]]
  15. 5(b) Let $U$ be a subspace of $\mathbb R^n$. If $U$ has a basis of $k$ elements, then show that a linearly independent subset of $U$ can have at most $k$ vectors. [[4]]
  16. 5(c) Find a basis and the dimension of the subspace $V=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid2x-y+3z=0\}$. [[3]]
  17. 6(a) If possible, construct a $3\times4$ matrix $A$ such that the $\dim N(A)=2$ and $\dim C(A)=2$. Otherwise, explain why it is not possible. [[3]]
  18. 6(b) Let $U$ be the reduced row echelon form of a matrix $A$. Show that the nonzero rows of $U$ form a basis of the row space $R(A)$ of $A$. [[4]]
  19. 6(c) Let $A$ be a $3\times3$ matrix whose row space is the $span\{(3,0,-2),(1,2,0)\}$. Find the null space of $A$. [[3]]
  20. 1(a) Find $y_n$ where\\(i) $y=e^x\cos x$.\quad(ii) $y=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac xa\right)$. [[4]]
  21. 1(b) If $y=\sin(m\sin^{-1}x)$, prove that\\(i) $(1-x^2)y_2=xy_1-m^2y$,\\(ii) $(1-x^2)y_{n+2}-(2n+1)xy_{n+1}+(m^2-n^2)y_n=0$. [[2+4]]
  22. 2(a) State and prove Lagrange's Mean Value theorem. [[6]]
  23. 2(b) In the Mean Value Theorem $f(x+h)=f(x)+hf'(x+\theta h)$, $0<\theta<1$, show that the limiting value of $\theta$ as $h\to0^+$ is $\frac12$ where $f(x)=\sin x$. [[4]]
  24. 3(a) Evaluate: $\lim_{x\to0}(\cos x)^{\cot^2x}$. [[4]]
  25. 3(b) If $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{a\sin x+\sin2x}{x^3}$ is finite then find the value of $a$ and the limit. [[6]]
