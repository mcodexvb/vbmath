CC-1

Analysis-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2017
  1. 1(a) If $\underset{x\to a}{Lt}f(x)=\ell(\ne0)$ then prove that in a deleted neighbourhood of $a$, $f$ keeps the same sign as that of $\ell$. [5]
  2. 1(b) Using $(\varepsilon-\delta)$ definition find $\underset{x\to0}{Lt}\frac{\sin x}x$. [5]
  3. 2(a) Prove that every monotone decreasing sequence which is bounded below is convergent and converges to is greatest lower bound. [6]
  4. 2(b) Test the convergence of $\left(\frac1{\sqrt{n^2+1}}+\frac1{\sqrt{n^2+2}}+\text{---}+\frac1{\sqrt{n^2+n}}\right)$. [4]
  5. 3(a) Prove that a sequence is convergent iff it is a Cauchy sequence. [6]
  6. 3(b) Examine if the sequence $x_n=1-\frac12+\frac13+\text{----}+\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}n$ is a Cauchy sequence. [4]
  7. 4(a) Prove that every continuous function defined over a closed bounded interval $[a,b]$ is bounded there in and attains its bounds. Does the result hold if $[a,b]$ is replaced by an open interval $(a,b)$? [4+2]
  8. 4(b) Verify the differentiability of the function $f(x)=\begin{cases}x\sin\frac1x&\text{for }x\ne0\\0&\text{for }x=0\end{cases}$ At $x=0$. [4]
  9. 5(a) If $y=x^{2x}$, $n$ is a positive integer, then show that $y_n=2^n\{1.3.5.\text{---}(2n-1)\}x^n$. [4]
  10. 5(b) Find $a,b,c$ such that $\underset{x\to0}{Lt}\frac{ae^x-b\cos x+ce^{-x}}{x\sin x}=2$. [4]
  11. 5(c) Show that $\cosh x\ge1$ for all real $x$. [2]
  12. 6(a) State and prove Libnitz's theorem for successive differentiation. [5]
  13. 6(b) If $y=e^{a\sin^{-1}x}$ then show that $(1-x^2)y_{n+2}-(2n+1)xy_{n+1}-(n^2+a^2)y_n=0$ and hence find $(y_n)_0$. [5]
  14. 7(a) Prove that a point $\xi$ is a limit point of a set $A$ iff every neighbourhood of $\xi$ contains infinitely many points of $A$. [3]
  15. 7(b) Define a derived set. Show that derived set of a bounded set is bounded. [3]
  16. 7(c) Let $S$ be a bounded subset of $\mathbb R$ and $T$ be a set given by $T=\{x-y:x,y\in S\}$.\par Prove that (i) $Sup\,T=Sup\,S-Inf\,S$.\par (ii) $Inf\,T=Inf\,S-Sup\,S$. [4]
  17. 8(a) State and prove Rolle's theorem. [5]
  18. 8(b) From the relation $f(x+h)=f(x)+hf'(x+\theta h)$, show that $\theta$ is independent on $x$ where $f(x)=a+bx+cm^x$, where $a,b,c$ are constants. [3]
  19. 8(c) Show that $2x^3+2x^2-10x+6$ is positive for $x>1$. [2]
  20. 9(a) If $n$ is an even positive integer then show that $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^nx\,dx=\frac{n-1}n\cdot\frac{n-3}{n-2}\cdot\frac{n-5}{n-4}\ldots\frac34\cdot\frac12\cdot\frac\pi2$ [5]
  21. 9(b) If $I_n=\int x^n\sqrt{a-x}\,dx$ then show that $(2n+3)I_n=2anI_{n-1}-2x^n(a-x)^{3/2}$ and hence evaluate $\int_0^ax^3\sqrt{ax-x^2}\,dx$ [5]
