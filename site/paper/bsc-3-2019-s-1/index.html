CC-5

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2019
  1. 1(a) If \((L,R)\) denotes the section of rational numbers where \(L=\{x:x\le0\}\cup\{x>0\text{ and }x^2<2\}\) and \(R=\{x:x>0\text{ and }x^2>2\}\), then show that \(L\) has no greatest member and \(R\) has no least member. \hfill 4
  2. 1(b) If \((L_1,R_1)\) and \((L_2,R_2)\) denote the section of real number \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) then find the section of \(\alpha+\beta\) with proper justifications. \hfill 3
  3. 1(c) Show that if a function \(f\) is continuous on a closed and bounded interval \(I\) then \(f\) is uniformly continuous on \(I\). \hfill 3
  4. 2(a) State and prove Jordan decomposition theorem. \hfill 5
  5. 2(b) Prove that a function of bounded variation need not be continuous and a continuous function need not be of bounded variation. \hfill 5
  6. 3(a) Show that if a function \(f\) is continuous on \([a,b]\), then it is bounded and attains its least upper bound and greatest lower bound in \([a,b]\). \hfill 5
  7. 3(b) Define uniformly continuous function on a subset of \(\missingglyph\). If \(f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)\ \forall x,y\in\missingglyph\) and \(f\) is continuous at a point of \(\missingglyph\), prove that \(f\) is uniformly continuous on \(\missingglyph\). \hfill 5
  8. 4(a) What do you mean by a countable set? Prove that the set \(\missingglyph\) of all real numbers is uncountable. \hfill 5
  9. 4(b) If a function \(f:[a,b]\to\missingglyph\) is continuous and injective, then prove that \(f\) is strictly monotone on \([a,b]\). \hfill 3
  10. 4(c) Given a real valued function \(f\) is continuous on \([0,2]\) and \(f(0)=f(2)\). Prove that there exists at least a point \(c\in[0,1]\) such that \(f(c)=f(c+1)\). \hfill 2
  11. 5(a) If \(f\) is continuous and RS-integrable with respect to a non-decreasing function \(g\) over \([a,b]\), then prove that \(\exists\xi\in[a,b]\) such that \((RS)\int_a^b f\,dg=f(\xi)[g(b)-g(a)]\). \hfill 3
  12. 5(b) Evaluate \((RS)\int_a^b f\,dg\) where \(f(x)=x(3-x)\) and \[ g(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{lll} x & \text{when} & 0\le x<1\\ 1 & \text{when} & 1\le x<2\\ 3-x & \text{when} & 2\le x\le3 \end{array}\right\} \]
  13. 5(c) Let \(I\subset\missingglyph\) be an interval and \(f:I\to\missingglyph\) be a function and is differentiable at \(c\in I\). If \(f'(c)>0\) then show that \(f\) is increasing at \(c\). \hfill 3
  14. 6(a) State and prove Raabe's test. \hfill \(1+4\)
  15. 6(b) Test the convergence of the series \(\displaystyle\sum\frac{1}{x^n+x^{-n}}\) for \(x>0\). \hfill 3
  16. 6(c) If \(\sum u_n\) is a convergent series of positive real numbers, prove that \(\displaystyle\sum\frac{u_n}{1+u_n}\) is convergent. \hfill 2
  17. 7(a) Test the convergence of the series \(1+\frac{x}{1!}+\frac{2^2x^2}{2!}+\frac{3^3x^3}{3!}+\cdots,\ x>0\). \hfill 3
  18. 7(b) State Kummer's test. Show that D'Alembert ratio test is the consequence of Kummer's test. \hfill \(1+2\)
  19. 7(c) State and prove Leibnitz's test. \hfill \(1+3\)
  20. 8(a) If \(f\) is continuous on \([a,b]\), then show that \(f\) is \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\). \hfill 3
  21. 8(b) Define a function \(f\) on \([0,1]\) by \(f(0)=0\) and \(f(x)=(-1)^{r-1},\ \frac{1}{r+1}<x\le\frac1r\) for \(r=1,2,3,\ldots\). Examine the \(R\)-integrability of \(f\) on \([0,1]\). \hfill 3
  22. 8(c) If \(f\) is \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\), then show that \(cf\) is \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\) where \(c\in\missingglyph\) with \(c<0\). Also show that \(\int_a^b cf(x)\,dx=c\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\). \hfill \(3+1\)
  23. 9(a) Let \(f\) be non-negative integrable function defined on \([a,b]\) which is continuous at \(c\in(a,b)\) and \(f(c)>0\). Show that \(\int_a^b f(x)\,dx>0\). \hfill 3
  24. 9(b) State and prove fundamental theorem of integral calculus. \hfill \(1+3\)
  25. 9(c) Show that \(\displaystyle\int_0^1\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4-x^2+x^3}}>\frac12\). \hfill 3
