Differential Geometry and Manifold Theory
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2025
  1. 1 Introduce the Hausdorff second countable topological space. Define chart, atlas, maximal atlas and manifold on a second countable Hausdorff topological space. Illustrate chart atlas, maximal atlas for $S^1$, the collection of points $(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2=1$. [[3]]
  2. 2 Define tangent vector at a point $p$ in a manifold $\mathcal M$. What do you mean by tangent space $T_p\mathcal M$ at a point $p\in\mathcal M$, tangent bundle $T\mathcal M$ of $\mathcal M$. Derive a basis for $T_p\mathcal M$ in terms of the local coordinates $y^1,y^2,\ldots,y^n$ of a $n$ dimensional manifold $\mathcal M$. Define vector fields on $\mathcal M$ and its action on smooth functions $C^\infty(U)$, where $U$ is some open set of $\mathcal M$. Prove that each vector field $\mathbf X\in\mathcal X(U)$ is a linear derivation on $C^\infty(U)$. [[3]]
  3. 3 Introduce differential forms and wedge products on a manifold $\mathcal M$. Define exterior derivative of differential forms belonging in $\Lambda_p(\mathcal M)$. Prove the formula $d(\alpha\wedge\beta)=d\alpha\wedge\beta+(-1)^r\alpha\wedge d\beta$ for $\alpha\in\bigwedge^r$. When a form is said to be exact. Prove that every exact form is closed in $\mathcal M$. Is the converse true? Prove it, if yes. [[3]]
  4. 4 Define the inner product in the tangent space? Introduce metric tensor with the aid of the inner product just introduced. Prove that $g_{ij}$ may be regarded as mapping $g:T\mathcal M\times T\mathcal M\to\mathbb R$ and $g:T^*\mathcal M\to T\mathcal M$. Derive expressions for $g_{ij}$ in the spherical polar coordinate system $(r\sin u\cos v,r\sin u\sin v,r\cos u)$ and verify whether is it orthogonal? [[3]]
  5. 5 Define Hodge $*$ map between the space of forms. Give the image of the Hodge map on the differential forms $dx^3$ and $dx^2\wedge dx^3$ in $\mathbb R^4$. Derive expressions for $\mathrm{grad}(\vec\nabla)$, $\mathrm{div}(\vec\nabla\cdot)$, $\mathrm{curl}(\vec\nabla\times)$ and Laplacian $(\nabla^2$ or $\triangle)$ operators as the combination of $d$ operator and $*$-map in a three dimensional manifold $\mathcal M$. [[3]]
  6. 6 Discuss the process of ``pull back'' and ``push forward'' of map $f:\mathcal M\to\mathcal N$. If $\alpha=3xy\,dx+2y^2dy+xz\,dz$ be a 1-form in $\mathbb R^3$ and $\sigma:I\to\mathbb R$ be a parametrized curve given by $t\to(t^2,t,t^5)$, find $(\sigma^*\alpha)(t)$. [[3]]
  7. 7 Define Lie group and Lie group of transformations with an illustration. Examine whether the transformation $(x,y)\to(x^*,y^*)=e^{\epsilon_1}x+\epsilon_2,e^{2\epsilon_1}y+\epsilon_3$ is a Lie group of transformation. Find the infinitesimal generators, if yes. [[3]]
  8. 8 Define Lie algebra of a set of vector fields. When a Lie algebra is called solvable? Examine whether the vector fields corresponding to the infinitesimal generators of problem 7 is a solvable Lie algebra. Find a two dimensional subalgebra, if available. [[3]]
