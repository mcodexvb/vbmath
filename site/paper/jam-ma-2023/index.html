  1. 1 Let $v_1, \ldots, v_9$ be the column vectors of a non-zero $9 \times 9$ real matrix $A$. Let $a_1, \ldots, a_9 \in \mathbb{R}$, not all zero, be such that $\sum_{i=1}^9 a_i v_i = 0$. Then the system $A x = \sum_{i=1}^9 v_i$ has \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item more than one but only finitely many solutions \item no solution \item infinitely many solutions \item a unique solution \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $a_n = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n$ and $b_n = n \cos \left( \frac{n! \pi}{2^{10}} \right)$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$.   4. 4 Let $p(x) = x^{57} + 3x^{10} - 21x^3 + x^2 + 21$ and $q(x) = p(x) + \sum_{j=1}^{57} p^{(j)}(x)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $p^{(j)}(x)$ denotes the $j^{\text{th}}$ derivative of $p(x)$.
