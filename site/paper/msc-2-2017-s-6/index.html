MMC-26

Numerical Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2017
  1. 1(a) Obtain the cubic spline interpolation for the function $f(x)$ whose tabular values are known at $(n+1)$ distinct equispaced points by using piecewise cubic Hermite interpolation formula under natural spline conditions. [6]
  2. 1(b) Define $f(x_0,x_0,\ldots,x_0)$ for coincident values of $(n+1)$ arguments and hence deduce Taylor's formula with Lagrange's form of remainder. [4]
  3. 2(a) Obtain the best uniform polynomial approximation $P_n(x)$ of degree $n$ in the minimax sense to the function $f(x)$ which is continuous on $[a,b]$. [6]
  4. 2(b) Use Lanczos economization to approximate $f(x)=(2x-1)^3$ by a straight line on the interval $[0,1]$, so that the maximum norm of the error function is minimized. [4]
  5. 3(a) Explain briefly the Romberg's quadrature rule for integration of $f(x)$ over the interval $[a,b]$. Point out the stopping criterion based on either a row or a column convergence test. Comment also on the accuracy of the method. [6]
  6. 3(b) Determine the coefficients in the formula \[ \int_0^{2h}x^{-1/2}f(x)dx=(2h)^{1/2}[a_0f(0)+a_1f(h)+a_2f(2h)]+R \] and calculate the remainder $R$, when $f'''(x)$ is constant. [4]
  7. 4(a) Explain briefly the power method for the largest eigen pair of a real numerical matrix including its stopping criterion. State how the least eigen pair can be obtained for both the cases of singular and non-singular matrices and also how other remaining eigen pairs are obtained. [6]
  8. 4(b) Transform the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&2&2\\2&1&2\\2&2&1\end{pmatrix}$ to tridiagonal form by Givens' method. [4]
  9. 5(a) Briefly describe Adams' method for solving numerically the well-posed initial value problem $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=f(x,y)$, $y(a)=y_0$ in a finite interval $[a,b]$. [6]
  10. 5(b) Prepare a scheme corresponding to the single step method for the differential equation $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=f(x,y)$ which produces exact result for $y(x)=a+be^{-x}$; $a,b$ being constants. \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [4]
  11. 6(a) Briefly describe an explicit finite difference scheme for solving the problem \[ \frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=c^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2},\quad c>0,\ t>0,\ 0<x<l \] with \[ u(0,t)=0=u(l,t),\ t>0 \] \[ u(x,0)=F(x)\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,0)=G(x),\ 0\leq x\leq l \] in which $F(x)$ and $G(x)$ are known functions of $x$. [6]
  12. 6(b) Discuss the shooting method for solving boundary value problem \[ -u''+p(x)u'+q(x)u=r(x),\quad a<x<b \] subject to the given conditions $u'(a)=\gamma_1$, $u'(b)=\gamma_2$ where $p(x)$, $q(x)$ and $r(x)$ are assumed to be continuous on $[a,b]$ and $\gamma_1,\gamma_2$ are non-zero real constants. \endgroup [4]
