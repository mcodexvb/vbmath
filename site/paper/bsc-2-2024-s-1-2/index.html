MJMA-4

Differential Equations-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2024
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{ten} questions from the following. [\(10\times2=20\)]
  2. 1(a) Find the non-zero value of \(n\) for which the differential equation \((3xy^2+n^2x^2y)\,dx+(nx^3+3x^2y)\,dy=0\) is an exact differential equation. [2]
  3. 1(b) Solve the differential equation \(3x(xy-2)\,dx+(x^3+2y)\,dy=0\). [2]
  4. 1(c) Find an integrating factor of the differential equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{2xy^2+y}{x-2y^3}\). [2]
  5. 1(d) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of parabolas \(y=cx^2\). [2]
  6. 1(e) Solve the nonlinear differential equation \(\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2+(e^x+e^{-x})\dfrac{dy}{dx}+1=0\). [2]
  7. 1(f) Solve: \((D^2+9)^3(D^2-16)^3y=0\). [2]
  8. 1(g) Solve the differential equation \(\dfrac{d^3y}{dx^3}=xe^x\). [2]
  9. 1(h) Find a second order linear homogeneous differential equation whose linearly independent solutions are \(x\), and \(xe^x\). [2]
  10. 1(i) Solve the boundary value problem \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=0,\ y(0)=0,\ y(\frac{\pi}{2})=1\). [2]
  11. 1(j) Find two different solutions of the differential equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=y^{1/3},\ y(0)=0\). [2]
  12. 1(k) Solve: \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}=8y^3\). [2]
  13. 1(l) Find the singular solution of the differential equation \(y=px+p^2\). [2]
  14. 1(m) Solve: \(\dfrac{d^4y}{dx^4}-\cot x\,\dfrac{d^3y}{dx^3}=0\). [2]
  15. 1(n) Solve the differential equation \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2+1=0\). [2]
  16. 1(o) Solve: \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(x\dfrac{dy}{dx})=x;\ y(1)=0,\ \left.\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=1}=0\). [2]
  17. 2 Obtain the differential equation of family of circles \(x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0\), where \(g,\ f\) and \(c\) are parameters. [6]
  18. 3 Solve the homogeneous equation \((x^2-xy+y^2)\,dx-xy\,dy=0\). [6]
  19. 4 If \(e^{\int\phi(x)\,dx}\) is an integrating factor of the differential equation \(M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0\), then find the expression of \(\phi(x)\). Hence solve the equation \(2y\,dx+x\,dy=0\).
  20. 5 Show that if \(f\) is any solution of the differential equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=A(x)y^2+B(x)y+C(x)\), then the transformation \(y=f+\frac1v\) reduces the above differential equation to a linear equation in \(v\). Hence solve the equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=(1-x)y^2+(2x-1)y-x\); given solution \(f(x)=1\).
  21. 6 Prove that the transformation \(v=y^{1-n}\ (n\ne0\text{ or }1)\) reduces the Bernoulli equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=P(x)y+Q(x)y^n\) to a linear equation in \(v\). Hence solve the equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}+y=xy^3\).
  22. 7 Solve the differential equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}+y=f(x)\) with initial condition \(y(0)=0\), where \(f(x)=2\), when \(0\le x<1\) and \(f(x)=0\), when \(x\ge1\). Hence find the value of \(y(5)\).
  23. 8 Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves \(r^n\sin(n\theta)=c^n\), \(c\) being the parameter. [6]
  24. 9 Find a family of oblique trajectories that intersect the family of straight lines \(y=cx\) at angle \(45^\circ\). [6]
  25. 10 Solve the nonlinear differential equation \(y^2\log(y)=xyp+p^2\). [6]
  26. 11 Reduce the equation \((px-y)(x-py)=2p\) to Clairaut's form by the substitution \(x^2=u\) and \(y^2=v\). Hence solve the equation.
  27. 12 Solve the differential equation \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+3\dfrac{dy}{dx}+2y=e^{e^x}\). [6]
  28. 13 Solve: \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2\dfrac{dy}{dx}=e^{2x}\sin x\). [6]
  29. 14 Solve the Cauchy-Euler equation \(x^2y''-4xy'+6y=\log(x^2),\ x>0\). [6]
  30. 15 Using the method of variation of parameters, solve the equation \((D^2+1)y=\sec x\tan x\). [6]
  31. 16 Test for exactness and solve the differential equation \((1+x^2)y''+4xy'+2y=\sec^2x\); given that \(y(0)=0\) and \(y'(0)=1\).
