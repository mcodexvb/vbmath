MMC-15

Partial Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2022
  1. 1(a) If $u(x,y)$ is the solution of the Cauchy problem $yu_x+xu_y=0$, $u(0,y)=e^{-y^2}$, then find the value of $u(1,2)$. State the region of $xy$-plane in which the solution can be determined uniquely.
  2. 1(b) Solve the following PDE by Monge's method: \[ 2xu_{xx}-(x+2y)u_{xy}+yu_{yy}=\frac{x+2y}{x-2y}(2u_x-u_y). \]
  3. 1(c) Find the nature of the PDE $\frac14u_{xx}+\sqrt{2022}\,u_{xy}+\{2022-\operatorname{sgn}(y)\}u_{yy}=0$, for $y>0$ and $y<0$.
  4. 2(a) Show that a linear PDE of the type \[ \sum_{r,s}C_{rs}x^ry^s\frac{\partial^{r+s}u}{\partial x^r\partial y^s}=f(x,y) \] may be reduced to one with constant coefficients by the substitutions $\xi=\ln x$, $\eta=\ln y$. Hence, find the complete integral of the PDE \[ x^2u_{xx}-xyu_{xy}-2y^2u_{yy}+xu_x-2yu_y=\ln\frac{y}{x}-\frac1{2023}. \]
  5. 2(b) Find a surface satisfying $u_{xx}-2u_{xy}+u_{yy}=6$ and touching the hyperbolic paraboloid $u=xy$ along its section by the plane $x=y$.
  6. 3(a) Using Cauchy's method of characteristics, find an integral surface of the PDE \[ u_y+uu_x=-x,\quad y\geq0,\qquad u(x,0)=f(x),\quad -\infty<x<\infty. \]
  7. 3(b) Convert the following PDE to the canonical form, and hence solve it: \[ x^2(y-1)u_{xx}-x(y^2-1)u_{xy}+y(y-1)u_{yy}+xyu_x-u_y=0. \] [5+5]
  8. 4(a) Solve the following homogeneous wave equation by the method of Laplace transform: \[ 9u_{tt}=u_{xx}, \] $u(0,t)=f(t)$, $\lim_{x\to\infty}u(x,t)=0$, $x,t\geq0$ where $u$ is the deflection of a string, released from rest on the $x$-axis $[u(x,0)=0=u_t(x,0)]$.
  9. 4(b) Use the complex form of the Fourier transformation to show that \[ u=\frac1{2\sqrt{\pi t}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(\xi)e^{-\frac{(x-\xi)^2}{4t}}d\xi \] is the solution of the PDE $u_t=u_{xx}$, $-\infty<x<\infty$, $t>0$ with $u=f(x)$ when $t=0$ and $u(x,t)$ is bounded. [5+5]
  10. 5(a) State maximum-minimum principle for a function $u(x,y)$ which is continuous in a closed region $\overline{\mathbb R}$ ($=\mathbb R\cup\partial\mathbb R$) and satisfies the Laplace equation $\nabla^2u=0$ in the interior of $\mathbb R$.
  11. 5(b) Let $\Omega=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2\mid x^2+y^2<1\}$ be the open unit disc in $\mathbb R^2$ with boundary $\partial\Omega$. If $u(x,y)$ is the solution of the Dirichlet problem $u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0$ in $\Omega$ and $u(x,y)=2x^2-1$ on $\partial\Omega$. Find the value of $u(\frac12,\frac12)$.
  12. 5(c) By using the method of separation of variables, obtain the solution of the following initial boundary value problem (IBVP) for the wave equation: \[ u_{tt}=u_{xx},\quad x\in(0,\pi),\ t>0, \] BC's: $u(0,t)=u(\pi,t)=0$, $t>0$, IC's: $u(x,0)=\sin x-\sin2x$, $u_t(x,0)=0$, $0\leq x\leq\pi$. [2+3+5]
  13. 6(a) If $\phi$ is a harmonic function in $\mathbb R$ and $\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial n}=0$ on $\partial\mathbb R$, then $\phi$ is a constant in $\overline{\mathbb R}$ ($=\mathbb R\cup\partial\mathbb R$) - prove it.
  14. 6(b) Solve the following exterior Dirichlet problem for a circle: PDE: $\nabla^2u=0$, $r>a$, $0\leq\theta<2\pi$ BC: $u(a,\theta)=f(\theta)$, $0\leq\theta<2\pi$ where $f(\theta)$ is a continuous function on $\partial\mathbb R$ and $u(r,\theta)$ must be bounded as $r\to\infty$. Hence deduce Poisson's integral formula of the form \[ u(r,\theta)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi} \frac{(r^2-a^2)f(\phi)}{r^2-2ra\cos(\phi-\theta)+a^2}\,d\phi. \] \endgroup
