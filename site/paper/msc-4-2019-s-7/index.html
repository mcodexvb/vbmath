MMO-41(A9)

Lie Group of Transformations and Partial Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1(a) If $y=e^x$ is a solution of $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=e^{2x}-ye^x+e^{-x}y^2$, find another solution of the ODE using the transformation $y=e^x+\dfrac1v$.
  2. 1(b) Using tanh-coth method solve Burger's equation. [5+5]
  3. 2 Find the solution of \textit{KdV} equation and using the Conservation laws of \textit{KdV} equation, find the analytical solution of damped forced \textit{KdV} equation. [3+7]
  4. 3 Using Hirota Direct method, find one soliton solution of the \textit{KdV} equation. [10]
  5. 4 Derive Lax compatibility equation. Hence find the Lax pair of \textit{KdV} and \textit{MKdV} equation. [3+7]
  6. 5 Let $y=y_1(x)$ be a particular solution of the \textit{Strum-Liouville} problem\par $y''+(\lambda-u)y=0,$\hfill(1)\par where the value of the spectral parameter $\lambda=\lambda_1$. Consider the \textit{Darboux} transformation $y\to y[1]=\left(\dfrac d{dx}-l\right)y$ of an arbitrary solution of (1) where $l=\dfrac d{dx}(\log y_1)$. Show that $y[1]$ obeys the following equation\par $y''[1]+(\lambda-u[1])\,y[1]=0$, where $u[1]$ is given by $u[1]=u-\dfrac d{dx}(l)=u-\dfrac{d^2}{dx^2}\log y_1$. [10]
  7. 6 Find one soliton solution of \textit{Sine-Gordon} and \textit{Liouville's} equation by using Backlund transformation. [5+5]
