CC-7

Differential Equations-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2020
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{ten} questions from the following: \hfill [\(10\times2=20\)]
  2. 1(a) Solve the initial value problem \((xy^2-\cos x\sin x)\,dx+y(x^2-1)\,dy=0,\ y(0)=2\). [2]
  3. 1(b) Solve the first order differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=(y-5x)^2+6,\ y(0)=0\). [2]
  4. 1(c) Solve: \(4x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=0\). [2]
  5. 1(d) Find a third order linear homogeneous differential equation whose linearly independent solutions are \(x,\ x^2,\) and \(x^3\). [2]
  6. 1(e) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of parabolas \(y=cx^2\), where \(c\) is a parameter. [2]
  7. 1(f) Solve the nonlinear differential equation \(\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2+(x+y)\frac{dy}{dx}+xy=0\). [2]
  8. 1(g) Consider the differential equation \(x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}-4y=0\). If \(W(1)=1\), then find the value of \(W(5)-W(2)\). [2]
  9. 1(h) Find four different solutions of the differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=y^{1/3},\ y(0)=0\). [2]
  10. 1(i) Solve: \((D^2+16)^2(D-4)^2(D^2-16)^2y=0\). [2]
  11. 1(j) Find an integrating factor of the differential equation \(y(y\,dx-x\,dy)+x^2(2y\,dx+2x\,dy)=0\). [2]
  12. 1(k) Find the differential equations of all circles passing through the origin and having their centers on the axis of \(x\). [2]
  13. 1(l) Let \(x(t)\) be the solution of the differential equation \(\frac{dx}{dt}=(x-3)(x-5)(x-11)(x-15)\) satisfying the initial condition \(x(0)=6\). Find the value of \(x(t)\) when \(t\to\infty\). [2]
  14. 1(m) Radium disappears at a rate proportional to the amount present. If 10\% of the original amount disappears in 100 years, how much will remain present at the end of 200 years. [2]
  15. 1(n) Explain why it is always possible to express any homogeneous differential equation \(M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0\) in the form \(\frac{dy}{dx}=F(\frac{x}{y})\). [2]
  16. 2(a) Prove that the transformation \(v=y^{1-n}(n\ne0\text{ or }1)\) reduces the Bernoulli equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=P(x)y+Q(x)y^n\) to a linear equation in \(v\). Hence solve the equation \(x\frac{dy}{dx}+y=y^2x^3\cos x\).
  17. 2(b) Solve the second order linear differential equation \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-y=x\sin x+(1+x^2)e^x\). [4]
  18. 2(c) Solve: \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2+9=0\). [2]
  19. 3(a) Solve the nonlinear differential equation \(y=2x\frac{dy}{dx}+x^2\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^4\). [3]
  20. 3(b) Solve the equation \(x(1+x)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+(2-x^2)\frac{dy}{dx}-(2+x)y=x(1+x)^2\), where \(y=e^x\) and \(y=\frac1x\) are two linearly independent solutions of the corresponding homogeneous equation. [4]
  21. 3(c) If \(u\) and \(v\) are any two solutions of the equation \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0\) on an interval \([a,b]\), then prove that their Wronskian \(W(u,v)\) is either identically zero or never zero on \([a,b]\). [3]
  22. 4(a) Solve the homogeneous differential equation \((x^2-y^2)\,dx+2xy\,dy=0\). [3]
  23. 4(b) Let \(x^m\) is an integrating factor of the differential equation \(2x^2(x+1)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+x(7x+3)\frac{dy}{dx}-3y=x^2\). Find the possible values of \(m\). [3]
  24. 4(c) Find \(v(x)\) such that \(y(x)=e^{4x}v\) is a particular solution of the differential equation \((D^2-8D+16)y=(2x+9x^8+13x^{12})e^{4x}\). Hence find the general solution of it. [4]
  25. 5(a) Using the method of undetermined coefficients, solve the equation \((D^2+D-6)y=10e^{2x}-18e^{3x}-6x-11\). [3]
  26. 5(b) Find the singular solution and extraneous loci of the differential equation \(4p^2x=(3x-1)^2\). [4]
  27. 5(c) Solve the equation \((1+x^2)^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2x(1+x^2)\frac{dy}{dx}+4y\) by changing the independent variable. [3]
  28. 6(a) Reduce the equation \((x^2+y^2)(1+p)^2-2(x+y)(1+p)(x+py)+(x+py)^2=0\) to Clairaut's form by the substitution \(x^2+y^2=v\) and \(x+y=u\). Hence solve the equation. [4]
  29. 6(b) Solve the differential equation \((x+2)\frac{dy}{dx}+y=f(x)\) with initial condition \(y(0)=4\), where \(f(x)=2x\), when \(0\le x<2\) and \(f(x)=4\), when \(x\ge2\). Hence find the value of \(y(5)\). [5]
  30. 6(c) Use the method of isoclines to sketch some of the solution curves of the equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{-x^2}\). [1]
  31. 7(a) If \(e^{\int\phi(\frac yx)\,d(\frac yx)}\) is an integrating factor of the differential equation \(M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0\), then find the expression of \(\phi(\frac yx)\). [3]
  32. 7(b) Solve the differential equation \((xy^2-e^{\frac1{x^3}})\,dx-(x^2y)\,dy=0\). [4]
  33. 7(c) Find the equation of the family of curves which cut the members of the family of hyperbolas \(y^2+2xy-x^2=c\) at angle of \(45^\circ\). \def\paperfull{25} [3]
