MMC-23

Abstract Algebra
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1(a) Can the group $D_4$ be expressed as an internal direct product of two of its proper subgroups? Justify your answer. [3]
  2. 1(b) Find the number of elements of order 5 in $\mathbb Z_5\times\mathbb Z_{25}$. [3]
  3. 1(c) For two relatively prime positive integers $m,n$ show that $U(mn)\simeq U(m)\times U(n)$. [4]
  4. 2(a) Let $G$ be a group of order $p^n$ and $S$ be a $G$-set, where $p$ is a prime number. Show that $|S|\equiv|\{a\in S:ga=a\text{ for all }g\in G\}|(\bmod\ p)$. [3]
  5. 2(b) Find the class equation of $Q_8$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Find all 2-subgroups of $A_4$. [2]
  7. 2(d) Show that every group of order $2p$, where $p$ is an odd prime is simple. [2]
  8. 3(a) State and prove Sylow's second theorem on finite groups. [1+4]
  9. 3(b) Show that there does not exits any simple group of order $p^2q$ for two distinct primes $p$ and $q$. [4]
  10. 3(c) Classify all finite Abelian groups of order 60. [1]
  11. 4(a) Show that every prime element in an integral domain is irreducible. Give an example with proper justifications that the converse may not hold. [2+3]
  12. 4(b) How many units are there in the ring $\mathbb Z_8[x]$? Justify your answer. [2]
  13. 4(c) Check whether the ideal $<2,x>$ principal or not in the polynomial ring $\mathbb Z[x]$? Does there exist an Euclidean valuation in $\mathbb Z[x]$? Justify your answer. [1+1]
  14. 5(a) Show that every principal ideal domain is a unique factorization domain. [5]
  15. 5(b) Find the GCD of 2 and $1+i\sqrt5$ in $\mathbb Z[i,\sqrt5]$ [3]
  16. 5(c) Show that $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]$ has no unitm in between 1 and $1+\sqrt2$. [1+1]
  17. 6(a) State Eisenstein's criterion of irreducibility of a polynomial over a unique factorization domain. Hence, prove or disprove that $\frac{\mathbb Q[x,y]}{<y^2-x>}$ is a field. [1+3]
  18. 6(b) Find all values of $n$ for which $x^3+nx+2$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Z$. [3]
  19. 6(c) Factorize (if possible) the polynomial $x^4+1$ in $\mathbb Z_5[x]$. [3]
