MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2021
  1. 1(a) State and prove Morera's theorem. [3]
  2. 1(b) State maximum modulus theorem. Using this theorem show that if $|f(z)|>k$ on $|z|=R$, $f(z)$ is regular for $|z|\leq R$ and $|f(0)|<k$, then $f(z)$ has at least one zero in $|z|<R$. [3]
  3. 1(c) State and prove Taylor's theorem. [4]
  4. 2(a) Expand $f(z)=\frac1{(z+1)(z+3)}$ in Laurent series in the region $|z|>3$. [3]
  5. 2(b) Show that if $f(z)=\frac{\phi(z)}{(z-\alpha)^n}$, where $\phi(z)$ is analytic at $\alpha$ and $\phi(\alpha)\ne0$ then $\alpha$ is a pole of $f(z)$ of order $n$. [3]
  6. 2(c) State open mapping theorem. [1]
  7. 2(d) If $\beta$ be a pole of $f(z)$ of order $m$ and a pole of $g(z)$ of order $n(>m)$ then what kind of a point $\beta$ relative to the function $f(z)+g(z)$. [3]
  8. 3(a) If a function $f(z)$ is regular in a domain $D$ and if $z_1,z_2,\ldots,z_n,\ldots$ is a sequence of zeros of $f(z)$ having as limit point an interior point $\alpha$ of $D$, then show that $f(z)\equiv0$. [3]
  9. 3(b) If $\alpha$ be a limit point of the zeros of $f(z)$ and if $f(z)$ is analytic in some deleted neighbourhood $0<|z-\alpha|<\rho$ of $\alpha$, then show that $\alpha$ is an isolated essential singularity of $f(z)$. [3]
  10. 3(c) Find out the zeros and determine the nature of the singularities of the function $f(z)=\frac{z+2}{z^2}\sin\left(\frac1{z+1}\right)$ and also find the residue at $z=0$. [4]
  11. 4(a) State and prove Casorati-Weierstrass theorem. [3]
  12. 4(b) Assume that $f(z)$ is analytic in $|z|<1$, $f(0)=0$ and $|f(z)|\leq1$. If for a point $\alpha$, $0<|\alpha|<1$ satisfying $|f(\alpha)|=|\alpha|$ then show that $f(z)=e^{i\theta}z$ ($\theta=constant$). [4]
  13. 4(c) State Cauchy's residue theorem. Using this theorem evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=\frac32}\frac{z-1}{z^2+2z}dz$. [3]
  14. 5(a) Let $f(z)$ be analytic within and on a positively oriented simple closed curve $C$ except for a finite number of poles within $C$ and let $f(z)\ne0$ any where on $C$, then show that \[ \frac1{2\pi}\Lambda_C\arg f(z)=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}dz=Z-P, \] where $Z$ = the number of zeros of $f(z)$ within $C$, a zero of order $m$ being counted $m$ times, and $P$ = the number of poles of $f(z)$ within $C$, a pole of order $n$ being counted $n$ times. [7]
  15. 5(b) Let $f(z)$ be analytic in $|z|\leq1$ and $|f(z)|<1$ on $|z|=1$. Show that there exists just one point $\beta$ within the circle $|z|=1$ such that $f(\beta)=\beta$. [3]
  16. 6(a) If a function $f(z)$ has no singularity in the finite z-plane and has a pole of order $m$ at infinity then $f(z)$ must be a rational integral function of degree $m$. [3]
  17. 6(b) Find $\mathit{Res}[f(z),\infty]$, where $f(z)=\frac{z}{z^2-1}$. [2]
  18. 6(c) Evaluate any one of the following by the method of contour integration: (i) $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2}{(x^2+1)(x^2+4)}dx$; (ii) $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\sin x^2dx$. \begingroup\renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of values as indicated in the margin.} [5]
