CCMA-2

Algebra I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2020
  1. 1(a) Define zero of a polynomial. Find the multiple root(s) of the polynomial $x^4-9x^2+4x+12=0$ and solve the equation. [[1+4]]
  2. 1(b) State and prove the factor theorem. [[3]]
  3. 1(c) If $x^2+px+1$ is a factor of the polynomial $ax^3+bx+c$, then show that $a^2-c^2=ab$. [[2]]
  4. 2(a) Let $a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4$ be the four roots of the equation $x^4+p_1x^3+p_2x^2+p_3x+p_4=0$. Find the value of $(1+a_1^2)(1+a_2^2)(1+a_3^2)(1+a_4^2)$. [[3]]
  5. 2(b) State the Descarte's rule of signs. Using the Descarte's rule of signs, show that the equation $x^7+5x^4-3x+k=0$ can have at most three real roots for every real number $k$. [[1+4]]
  6. 2(c) Form a biquadratic equation with real coefficients two of whose roots are given by $3i\pm5$. [[2]]
  7. 3(a) Let $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be three roots of the equation $x^3+qx+r=0$. Find the equation whose roots are $\frac1\alpha+\frac1\beta+1,\frac1\beta+\frac1\gamma+1,\frac1\gamma+\frac1\alpha+1$. Hence or otherwise find the value of $(\alpha+\beta+\alpha\beta)(\beta+\gamma+\beta\gamma)(\gamma+\alpha+\gamma\alpha)$. [[4+1]]
  8. 3(b) Reduce the cubic equation $8x^3-36x^2+42x-5=0$ to its standard form and then solve by the Cardan's method. [[5]]
  9. 4(a) Show that every matrix is row equivalent to a unique matrix in reduced row echelon form. [[5]]
  10. 4(b) Find an equation involving $g,h$ and $k$ that makes the augmented matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&-4&7&g\\0&3&-5&h\\-2&5&-9&k\end{pmatrix}$ correspond to a consistent system. Hence or otherwise find $span((1,0,-2),(-4,3,5),(7,-5,-9))$. [[3+2]]
  11. 5(a) Show that the system \begin{align*}y+2z-3w&=0\\x-y+z-2w&=0\\-x-3z+5w&=0\\x+y+5z-8w&=0\end{align*} has infinitely many solutions. Find a basis and dimension of the solution space. [[2+3]]
  12. 5(b) Suppose the coefficient matrix of a system of linear equations has a pivot position in every row. Explain why the system is consistent. Find with justification if the system has unique solution. [[3+2]]
  13. 6(a) Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix. Prove that the system $Ax=b$ has a solution for every $b\in\mathbb R^m$ if and only if $r(A)=m$. [[4]]
  14. 6(b) Let $v_1=(3,0,2)$ and $v_2=(2,0,3)$. Give a geometric description of $span(v_1,v_2)$ without finding it explicitly. [[3]]
  15. 6(c) Let $v_1,v_2,\cdots,v_m$ be $m$ vectors in $\mathbb R^n$. Prove that they are linearly dependent if and only if either of them is a linear combination of the others. [[3]]
  16. 7(a) Let $A$ be a $3\times3$ matrix with the property that the system $AX=b$ has unique solution for some $b\in\mathbb R^3$. Explain why the columns of $A$ span $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  17. 7(b) Show that every subspace of $\mathbb R^n$ has a basis. [[4]]
  18. 7(c) Find the row space of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2&3\\5&-4&3\\9&-3&-3\end{pmatrix}$. Show that the solution space of $AX=0$ is orthogonal to the row space. [[3]]
  19. 8(a) Prove that the pivot columns of $A$ form a basis of the column space $C(A)$. [[4]]
  20. 8(b) Suppose that $\mathbb R^3=span(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$. Explain why $\{\alpha,\beta,\gamma\}$ is a basis of $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  21. 8(c) Show that a non null proper subspace of $\mathbb R^3$ is either a line through the origin or a plane through the origin. [[3]]
  22. 9(a) Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix. Prove that the columns of $A$ form a basis of $\mathbb R^n$ if and only if $A$ is invertible. [[4]]
  23. 9(b) Find the eigen values of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&-2&-3\\-1&1&-1\\2&2&5\end{pmatrix}$ and the eigen vectors belonging to some one of the eigen values. [[4]]
  24. 9(c) Let $A$ be a square matrix such that $A^2=0$. Show that $0$ is the only eigen value of $A$. [[2]]
