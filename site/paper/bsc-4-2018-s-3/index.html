BMC-43

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) Prove, analytically, that any system of coplanar forces acting on a rigid body will be in astatic equilibrium if $V=\sum_i(x_iX_i+y_iY_i)=0$ where $X_i,Y_i$ are the resolved parts of the forces $P_i$ acting at $(x_i,y_i)$ parallel to the coordinates axes $(i=1,2,\ldots,n)$. [5]
  2. 1(b) A system of forces in one plane reduces to a couple whose moment is $G$. When each force is turned about its point of application through a right angle it reduces to a couple of moment $H$. Show that when each force is turned through an angle $\alpha$, the system is equivalent to a couple whose moment is $G\cos\alpha+H\sin\alpha$. [5]
  3. 2(a) Use the principle of virtual work to find the conditions of equilibrium of a system of coplanar forces acting on a rigid body. [5]
  4. 2(b) A square framework formed of uniform heavy rods of equal weight $W$ joined together is hung up by a corner. A weight $W$ is suspended from which of the three lower corners and the shape of the square is preserved by a light rod along the horizontal diagonal. Find the thrust of the light rod. [5]
  5. 3(a) Define field of force, conservative field and potential energy of a system. [3]
  6. 3(b) A body rests in equilibrium on another fixed body, being rough enough to prevent sliding. The portions of the two bodies in contact are spherical and the line joining their centres in position of equilibrium is vertical. To find the condition for stable and unstable equilibrium. [Assume that the surface of the lower body, which is fixed, is convex upwards]. [7]
  7. 4(a) Find the conditions of equilibrium of a system of non-coplanar forces acting at different points of a rigid body. [5]
  8. 4(b) Prove that, if equal forces act along the edges $BC,CA,AB,DA,DB,DC$ of a regular tetrahedron, the central axis is perpendicular from $D$ to the plane $ABC$ and the pitch of the equivalent wrench is $\frac{a}{2\sqrt2}$, where \textquoteleft a\textquoteright{} is an edge of the tetrahedron. [5]
  9. 5(a) Prove that, whatever origin and axes are chosen, the quantities $LX+MY+NZ$ and $X^2+Y^2+Z^2$ are invariant for a given system of forces acting on a rigid body. [5]
  10. 5(b) Forces $X,Y,Z$ act along the three straight lines $y=b,z=-c$; $z=c,x=-a$; $x=a,y=-b$ respectively; show that they will have a single resultant $\frac aX+\frac bY+\frac cZ=0$. [5]
  11. 6(a) An open railway truck of mass $M$ is running on smooth horizontal rail under rain falling vertically which it catches and retains. Show that, if $u$ be the initial velocity of the truck, then the distance travelled in time $t$ is $\frac{Mu}{\mu}\log(1+\frac{\mu t}{M})$, where $\mu$ is the mass of rain water falling into the truck per unit of time. [5]
  12. 6(b) A particle is allowed to slide down a smooth vertical circle of radius $27a$ from rest at the highest point. Show that, on leaving the circle, it moves in a parabola whose latus rectum is $16a$. [5]
