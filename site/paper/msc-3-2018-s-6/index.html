MMC-16

Methods of Applied Mathematics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1 Answer \textit{any five} questions: \hfill\par \hspace*{1em}i) Find the Laplace transform of $f(t)$,\par \hspace*{2.5em}where $f(t)=\begin{cases}3t&0<t<2\\6&2<t<4\end{cases}$\par \hspace*{2.5em}and $f(t+4)=f(f)$.\par \hspace*{1em}ii) Find the inverse Laplace transform of $F(P)$,\par \hspace*{2.5em}where $F(P)=\dfrac{P^2-7P+11}{(P-1)(P-2)(P-4)}$.\par \hspace*{1em}iii) Find the value of $\lambda$ for which the integral equation\par \hspace*{2.5em}$y(x)=e^x+\lambda\displaystyle\int_0^1 5e^xe^ty(t)\,dt$ has infinitely many solutions.\par \hspace*{1em}iv) Find the value of $u(5)+u(7)$, where $u(x)$ is the solution of the Volterra integral equation \quad $1+x-e^x=\displaystyle\int_0^x(t-x)u(t)\,dt$.\par \hspace*{1em}v) Solve the Fredholm integro-differential equation\par \hspace*{2.5em}$\dfrac{du}{dx}=\sec^2x-\ell n(2)+\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/3}u(t)\,dt,\ u(0)=0.$\par \hspace*{1em}vi) Starting with initial approximation $y_0(x)=x^2$, find the third approximation for the integral equation \quad $y(x)=\dfrac{x^3}2-2x-\displaystyle\int_0^xy(t)\,dt$.\par \hspace*{1em}vii) Find the finite sine transform of $\sin ax$.\par \hspace*{1em}viii) If $L\{f(t)\}=F(s)$,\par \hspace*{2.5em}then prove that $L\left\{\dfrac{f(t)}t\right\}=\displaystyle\int_s^{\infty}F(x)\,dx$.
  2. 2(i) With the help of Hilbert-Schmidt theorem, solve the integral equation\par \[y(x)=1+\frac2\pi\int_0^{\pi}\cos(x+t)\,y(t)\,dt.\] \par\hfill P.T.O. [5]
  3. 2(ii) Find the resolvent kernel of the Volterra integral equation\par \[y(x)=e^x\sin x+\int_0^x\frac{2+\cos x}{2+\cos t}\,y(t)\,dt.\] Hence find the value of $y(\pi/2)$. [5]
  4. 3(i) Use Laplace transforms to solve the initial valve problem\par \[\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}+4\frac{dy}{dt}+5y=h(t),\ y(0)=0,\ y'(0)=1\] where $h(t)=\begin{cases}1,&0<t<\pi/2\\0,&t>\pi/2\end{cases}$. [5]
  5. 3(ii) Apply convolution theorem to prove that $\beta(m,n)=\dfrac{\Gamma m\,\Gamma n}{\Gamma(m+n)}$,\quad $m,n>0$. [3]
  6. 3(iii) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}e^{-t}\frac{\sin t}t\,dt$. [2]
  7. 4(i) State Fredholm alternative theorem. Using the theorem find the conditions for which the integral equation\par \[y(x)=f(x)+\lambda\int_0^1(1-3xt)\,y(t)\,dt\ \text{ has infinitely many solution.}\] [1+4]
  8. 4(ii) Solve the integral equation\par \[y(x)=1-x\cos x+x+x^2+\sin x-\int_0^xy(t)\,dt\ \text{ by using modified Adomian decomposition}\] \unreadable{stray line ``method.\ 2'' printed at the foot of page 3; the end of this item and its marks appear detached from the text}
  9. 4(iii) Solve the first kind Volterra integral equation $\displaystyle\int_0^x3^{x-t}y(t)\,dt=x$. [2]
  10. 4(iv) Give an example of a function which is not of exponential order but whose Laplace transform exits. [1]
  11. 5(i) Find $f(x)$, where $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}f(x)\cos(\lambda x)\,dx=\begin{cases}1-\lambda,&0\le\lambda<1,\\0,&\lambda>1.\end{cases}$ [2]
  12. 5(ii) Find the Fourier transform of $f(x)=\begin{cases}1-x^2,&|x|<1,\\0,&|x|>1.\end{cases}$\par Hence evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}\left(\frac{x\cos x-\sin x}{x^3}\right)\cos(x/2)\,dx$. [5]
  13. 5(iii) Using Parseval's identity, Prove that $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}\frac{t^2}{(t^2+1)^2}\,dt=\pi/4$. [2]
  14. 5(iv) If $f(t)=\begin{cases}e^{-xt}\varphi(t),&t>0\\0,&t<0\end{cases}$, then\par Prove that $F\{f(t)\}=L\{\varphi(t)\}$. [1]
  15. 6(i) Find the necessary condition for the existence of the extremum of the functional\par \[I[y(x)]=\int_{x_0}^{x_1}f(x,y,y',y'')\,dx.\] [4]
  16. 6(ii) Find the shortest distance between the parabola $y=x^2$ and the straight line $y=x-5$. [4]
  17. 6(iii) Find the extremum of the functional\par \[I[y(x)]=\int_0^e\left(xy'^2+yy'\right)dx.\] satisfying $y(1)=0$ and $y(e)=1$. [1]
