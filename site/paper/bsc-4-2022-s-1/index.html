CCMA-8

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2022
  1. 1(a) Prove that limit of a function of two variables if exists, is unique. [3]
  2. 1(b) Using $\epsilon-\delta$ definition, show that $\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{x^2y^2}{x^2+y^2}=0$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Prove that if $f(x,y)$ is continuous at a point $(a,b)$ and $f(a,b)\ne0$ then $f(x,y)$ has the sign of $f(a,b)$ in some neighbourhood of the point $(a,b)$. [4]
  4. 2(a) State and prove a sufficient condition for continuity of a function $f(x,y)$ at a point $(a,b)$. [4]
  5. 2(b) For the function $f(x,y)=\begin{cases}xy &\text{for }|x|\ge|y|\\-xy&\text{for }|x|<|y|\end{cases}$, show that $\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}(0,0)\ne\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\partial x}(0,0)$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Using Euler's theorem, if $u=\tan^{-1}\frac{x^3+y^3}{x^2+y^2}$ then show that $x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac12\sin 2u$. [3]
  7. 3(a) State and prove a sufficient condition for differentiability of a function $f(x,y)$ at a point $(a,b)$. [5]
  8. 3(b) State Young's theorem for equality of two mixed second order partial derivatives of a function $f(x,y)$ at a point $(a,b)$. Show that for the function $f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\frac{x^2y^2}{x^2+y^2}&\text{for }(x,y)\ne(0,0)\\0&\text{for }(x,y)=(0,0)\end{cases}$, $\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}(0,0)=\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\partial x}(0,0)$ but the conditions of Young's theorem are not satisfied. [5]
  9. 4(a) State Implicit function theorem. Show that $F(x,y)=x^2+2xe^y+y=0$ determines a unique function $y=f(x)$ about $(0,0)$. Also find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ at $(0,0)$. [4]
  10. 4(b) State and prove mean value theorem for a function of two variables. [3]
  11. 4(c) Using Taylor's theorem, expand $f(x,y)=xy^2+3x-2$ in powers of $(x+1)$ and $(y-2)$. [3]
  12. 5(a) Show that a necessary condition for $f(x,y)$ to have an extreme value at $(a,b)$ is that $f_x(a,b)=0$ and $f_y(a,b)=0$, provided these partial derivatives exist. [3]
  13. 5(b) Show that $f(x,y)=y^2+x^2y+x^4$ has a minimum at $(0,0)$. [4]
  14. 5(c) Evaluate $\iint_R(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy$ over $R$ bounded by $y=x^2$, $x=2$, $y=1$. [3]
  15. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  16. 1(a) If a transformation $y=uv$ transforms the given ordinary differential equation (ODE) \[\phi(x)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-4044\frac{d\phi}{dx}\frac{dy}{dx}+\psi(x)y=0\] into the normal form $\frac{d^2v}{dx^2}+\xi(x)v=0$, then evaluate $u$. Share your observation how $v$ can be obtained.
  17. 1(b) Given the solution $y_1(x)=\frac1{x-1}$, find the second linearly independent solution of the ODE $(x^2-x)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+(3x-1)\frac{dy}{dx}+y(x)=0$, $x\ne0,1$.
  18. 1(c) Show that the equation \[\left(x^2+2y^2\frac{dy}{dx}\right)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2y\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^3+2(x+y\cos x)\frac{dy}{dx}-(\sin x)y^2=0\] is exact and find its first integral.
  19. 1(d) What do the solutions of the differential equation \[\frac{dx}{3}=\frac{dy}{4}=\frac{dz}{5}\] represent geometrically? What are the significance of the numbers in the denominators?
  20. 1(e) Consider the following ordinary simultaneous differential equations: \[\begin{aligned} (D+1)x+(D-1)y&=e^t,\\ (D^2+D+1)x+(D^2-D+1)y&=t^2,\quad D^n\equiv\frac{d^n}{dt^n}. \end{aligned}\] The general solution of the system contains no arbitrary constant - Justify the statement with reason.
  21. 1(f) Find an integrating factor of the total differential equation \[(2xz-yz)\,dx+(2yz-zx)\,dy-(x^2-xy+y^2)\,dz=0.\] Also, carry out your ideas if $z(x^2+y^2-xy)=0$ holds.
  22. 1(g) Convert the following ODE into two first-order equations: \[a_0(t)\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}+a_1(t)\frac{dy}{dt}+a_2(t)y=a_3(t);\quad a_0(t)\ne0\] with $y=c_1$ and $\frac{dy}{dt}=c_2$ at $t=0$.
  23. 1(h) How many solutions exist for the boundary value problem \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=0,\quad 0<x<\pi\] subject to $y(0)+y(\pi)=0=y'(0)+y'(\pi)$? Justify your answer. [$5\times2=10$]
  24. 2(a) Transform the linear ODE \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0\] by the substitution $y(x)=z(x)\exp[-\frac12\int_0^x P(t)\,dt]$ into its normal form $\frac{d^2z}{dx^2}+I(x)z=0$ where $I(x)=Q(x)-\frac12\frac{dP}{dx}-\frac{P^2}{4}$.
  25. 2(b) Solve: \[\frac{dx}{x(2y^4-z^4)}=\frac{dy}{y(z^4-2x^4)}=\frac{dz}{z(x^4-y^4)}.\] [5+5]
  26. 3(a) Factorise the operator on the left hand side of $[xD^2+(x-2)D-2]y=x^3e^{2022x}$, $D^n\equiv\frac{d^n}{dx^n}$ and hence solve it.
  27. 3(b) Solve the simultaneous differential equations: \[\begin{aligned} 4\frac{dx}{dt}+9\frac{dy}{dt}+11x+3y&=e^t,\\ 3\frac{dx}{dt}+7\frac{dy}{dt}+8x+24y&=e^{2t}. \end{aligned}\] [5+5]
  28. 4(a) Verify that the following total differential equation is integrable and hence solve the equation: \[3x^2(y+z)\,dx+(z^2-x^3)\,dy+(y^2-x^3)\,dz=0.\]
  29. 4(b) Solve the following system of two linear homogeneous differential equations with constant coefficients using eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the matrix: \[\begin{aligned} \frac{dx}{dt}&=7x+y.\\ \frac{dy}{dt}&=7y,\\ x(t=0)&=7,\quad y(t=0)=1. \end{aligned}\] [5+5]
