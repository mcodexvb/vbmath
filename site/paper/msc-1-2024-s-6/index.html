MMC-16

Integral Transforms and Integral Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2024
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{five} questions from the following: [$5\times2=10$]
  2. 1(a) Find the value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty te^{-5t}\cos3t\,dt$. [2]
  3. 1(b) Find the inverse Fourier transform of $F(s)$, where $F(s)=\frac1{7+8is-s^2}$. [2]
  4. 1(c) Find the value of $\lambda$ for which the integral equation $y(x)=\lambda\int_0^1x^3t^3y(t)dt$ has infinitely many solutions. [2]
  5. 1(d) Solve the Fredholm integro-differential equation \[ u'(x)=9x^2+\int_0^1xu(t)dt,\quad u(0)=1. \] [2]
  6. 1(e) Find the Laplace transform of $f(t)$, where $f(t)=e^{2t}$ when $0<t<5$ and $f(t)=t-2$ when $t>5$. [2]
  7. 1(f) Using modified Adomian decomposition method, solve the integral equation \[ y(x)=\cos x-(1-e^{\sin x})x-x\int_0^x e^{\sin t}y(t)dt. \] [2]
  8. 1(g) Find the value of $u(2)+u(5)$, where $u(x)$ satisfies the equation $xe^{-x}=\int_0^x e^{t-x}u(t)dt$. [2]
  9. 2(a) Find the resolvent kernel of the integral equation \[ y(x)=(1+x^2)^7+7\int_0^x\frac{1+x^2}{1+t^2}y(t)dt. \] [4]
  10. 2(b) Convert the integral equation $y(x)=\int_0^x(x-t)y(t)dt-x\int_0^1(1-t)y(t)dt$ into an equivalent differential equation. [2]
  11. 2(c) Solve Volterra integral equation of first kind $\int_0^x(1-x^2+t^2)y(t)dt=\frac{x^2}{2}$. [2]
  12. 2(d) Solve the Volterra integro-differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=x+\int_0^x y(x-t)\cos t\,dt$, $y(0)=4$. [2]
  13. 3(a) State Fredholm alternative theorem. Show that the integral equation \[ y(x)=f(x)+\frac1\pi\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(x+t)y(t)dt \] possesses no solution for $f(x)=x$, but it possesses infinitely many solutions when $f(x)=1$. [1+4]
  14. 3(b) Using Laplace transform, solve the initial value problem $\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}+y=e^{-t}$; $y(0)=5$, $y'(0)=7$. [2]
  15. 3(c) If $f$ and $g$ are piecewise continuous on $[0,\infty)$ and of exponential order then prove that $L\{f*g\}=L\{f\}.L\{g\}$. Using the above result, find the inverse Laplace transform of the function $\frac1{s^2(s-1)}$. [2+1]
  16. 4(a) Transform the differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+xy=5$, $y(0)=0$, $y(1)=1$ into an equivalent integral equation. [4]
  17. 4(b) Using Hilbert-Schmidt theorem, solve the integral equation $y(x)=x+\frac1\pi\int_0^1(1-3xt)y(t)dt$. [4]
  18. 4(c) Prove that $L\{t^nf(t)\}=(-1)^n\frac{d^n}{ds^n}[F(s)]$, where $L\{f(t)\}=F(s)$. [2]
  19. 5 Solve the Volterra integral equation $y(x)=x^2-\displaystyle\int_0^x(x-t)y(t)dt$ by
  20. 5(a) Laplace transform method. [2]
  21. 5(b) series solution method. [2]
  22. 5(c) Adomian decomposition method. [2]
  23. 5(d) successive approximations method. [2]
  24. 5(e) successive substitutions method. [2]
  25. 6(a) Find the inverse Fourier transform of $F(s)=e^{-s|y|}$. [3]
  26. 6(b) Find the Fourier sine transform of $f(x)=e^{-ax}$. Hence using Persaval's identity, evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\frac{x^2}{(a^2+x^2)(b^2+x^2)}dx$. [1.5+1.5]
  27. 6(c) Find the Fourier cosine transform of $f(x)$, where $f(x)=\cos2x$ when $0\leq x\leq3$, and $f(x)=0$ when $x>3$. [2]
  28. 6(d) Find the Fourier sine transform of $\frac{e^{-ax}}x$. [2]
