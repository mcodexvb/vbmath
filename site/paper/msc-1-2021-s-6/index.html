MMC-16

Integral Transforms and Integral Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2021
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{five} questions from the following: [$5\times2=10$]
  2. 1(a) Solve the Fredholm integro-differential equation \[ u'(x)=-10x+\int_{-1}^1(x-t)u(t)dt,\quad u(0)=1. \] [2]
  3. 1(b) Find the Laplace transform of $f(t)$, where $f(t)=\cos t$ when $0<t<\pi$ and $f(t)=\sin t$ when $t>\pi$. [2]
  4. 1(c) Solve the homogeneous integral equation \[ y(x)=\lambda\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos x\sin t\,y(t)dt. \] [2]
  5. 1(d) Using modified Adomian decomposition method, solve the integral equation \[ y(x)=e^{2x}-\frac{(e^2+1)x}{4}+\int_0^1xt\,y(t)dt. \] [2]
  6. 1(e) Convert the integral equation $y(x)=2x^2+3+\int_0^1k(x,t)y(t)dt$ to an equivalent differential equation, where $k(x,t)=4t$ when $0\leq t\leq x$ and $k(x,t)=4x$ when $x\leq t\leq1$. [2]
  7. 1(f) Find the inverse Laplace transform of $F(s)$, where \[ F(s)=\frac{s}{s^2+3s+2}+\frac1{(s-2)^4}+\frac{e^{-\pi s}}{s^2+1}. \] [2]
  8. 1(g) With the help of an example, show that there exist functions which are neither piecewise continuous nor of exponential order but whose Laplace transform exist. [2]
  9. 1(h) Find the inverse Fourier transform of $\dfrac1{(2+is)(6+is)}$. [2]
  10. 2(a) Find the resolvent kernel of the Fredholm integral equation \[ y(x)=e^x+\int_{-1}^1x^2t^2y(t)dt. \] [3]
  11. 2(b) Convert the differential equation $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+4y=x$, $y(0)=1$, $y'(1)=0$ to an equivalent integral equation. [3]
  12. 2(c) Find the Fourier cosine transform of $f(x)$, where $f(x)=\sin5x$ when $0\leq x\leq7$, and $f(x)=0$ when $x>7$. [2]
  13. 2(d) Find the resolvent kernel of the Volterra integral equation $y(x)=\sin x+\displaystyle\int_0^x y(t)dt$. [2]
  14. 3(a) Using Hilbert-Schmidt theorem, solve the integral equation $y(x)=1+\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(x+t)y(t)dt$. [4]
  15. 3(b) If $f$ and $g$ are piecewise continuous on $[0,\infty)$ and of exponential order, then prove that $L\{f*g\}=L\{f\}.L\{g\}$. Using the above result prove that $\beta(m,n)=\dfrac{\Gamma(m).\Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(m+n)}$ where $m,n>0$. [2+1]
  16. 3(c) If $L\{f(t)\}=F(s)$, then prove that $L\{\int_0^t f(u)du\}=\dfrac{F(s)}s$. Hence find the Laplace transform of the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^t \frac{\sin x}x\,dx$. [2+1]
  17. 4(a) State Fredholm alternative theorem. Find the conditions for which the integral equation $y(x)=f(x)+\lambda\int_{-1}^1(x^2t^3-t^2x^3)y(t)dt$ has infinitely many solutions. [1+3]
  18. 4(b) Using Laplace transform, solve the differential equation $\dfrac{d^2y}{dt^2}+9y=\cos2t$, $y(0)=1$, $y(\pi/2)=-1$. [3]
  19. 4(c) Solve, by using Laplace transform, the simultaneous differential equation \[ \frac{dx}{dt}-2x+3y=0,\qquad 2x+\frac{dy}{dt}-y=0, \] where $x(0)=8$ and $y(0)=3$. [3]
  20. 5 Solve the Volterra integral equation $y(x)=x+\int_0^x(x-t)y(t)dt$ by
  21. 5(a) Laplace transform method. [2]
  22. 5(b) series solution method. [2]
  23. 5(c) Adomian decomposition method. [2]
  24. 5(d) successive approximations method. [2]
  25. 5(e) successive substitutions method. [2]
  26. 6(a) Solve the Volterra integro-differential equation of first kind \[ 2xe^x+e^x-x-1=\int_0^x(x-t+1)u''(t)dt,\quad u(0)=0,\ u'(0)=1. \] [3]
  27. 6(b) Find the Fourier sine transform of $e^{-ax}$. Hence using Persaval's identity, evaluate \[\int_0^\infty\frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^2}dx.\] [2+1]
  28. 6(c) Prove that $L\{f(t)\}=\dfrac{\int_0^T e^{-st}f(t)dt}{1-e^{-Ts}}$, where $f(t)$ is a periodic function with period $T$. Using the above result, find the Laplace transform of the periodic function $\sin t$. [2+1]
  29. 6(d) Give an example of a kernel in an integral equation that is not separable. [1]
