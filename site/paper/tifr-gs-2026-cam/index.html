TIFR Graduate School · GS 2026 · Mathematics PhD at CAM

  1. 1 Consider the following subsets of $\mathbb{R}^2$. \begin{itemize} \item $X=\{(t,\sin(1/t)):t\in(0,1)\}$ \item $Y=\{(t,\cos(1/t)):t\in(0,1)\}$ \item $Z=\{(t,\exp(1/t)):t\in(0,1)\}$ \end{itemize} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Only one of the sets $X,Y,Z$ is connected. \item Exactly two of the sets $X,Y,Z$ are connected. \item All of the three sets $X,Y,Z$ are connected. \item None of the sets $X,Y,Z$ are connected. \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 \textbf{Theorem:} Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space. Suppose $E$ and $F$ are disjoint subsets of $X$, and that $E$ is unbounded. If \underline{\hspace{3cm}}, then there exists a $\delta>0$ such that $d(e,f)\ge\delta$ for all $e\in E$ and $f\in F$. Choose the statement below that when used in the blank above makes the theorem true. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $E$ is open and $F$ is open. \item $E$ is open and $F$ is compact. \item $E$ is closed and $F$ is closed. \item $E$ is closed and $F$ is compact. \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $X=(0,1)$ and $f_n,f:X\to\mathbb{R}$ be continuous functions. Consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $f_n$ converges uniformly to $f$ on $X$. \item For any convergent sequence $(x_n)$ in $X$, that is $\lim x_n\in X$, we have $\lim f_n(x_n)=f(\lim x_n)$. \end{enumerate} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) and (ii) $\Rightarrow$ (i). \item (i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) but (ii) $\not\Rightarrow$ (i). \item (i) $\not\Rightarrow$ (ii) but (ii) $\Rightarrow$ (i). \item (i) $\not\Rightarrow$ (ii) and (ii) $\not\Rightarrow$ (i). \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 For the two dimensional Lebesgue measure on the set $X=\{(t^2,t^3)\in\mathbb{R}^2:t\in\mathbb{R}\}$, consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item If $Y\subseteq X$, then $Y$ is measurable. \item $\mathbb{R}^2\setminus X$ is measurable. \item $X$ has infinite measure. \end{enumerate} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i), (ii), and (iii) are true. \item (i) and (ii) are true, but (iii) is false. \item (ii) and (iii) are true, but (i) is false. \item (i), (ii), and (iii) are false. \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be continuous such that $f(0)=1$ and $f(x)=0$ for $|x|>1$. Define $f_n(x)=f(x+n)$ for $n\in\mathbb{N}$ and $x\in\mathbb{R}$. Consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $(f_n)$ converges in $L^\infty(\mathbb{R})$. \item $(f_n)$ converges in $L^p(\mathbb{R})$ for some $1\le p<\infty$. \item $(f_n)$ converges in $L^p(\mathbb{R})$ for all $1\le p<\infty$. \end{enumerate} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i), (ii), and (iii) are true. \item (i) and (ii) are true, but (iii) is false. \item (i) and (iii) are true, but (ii) is false. \item (i), (ii), and (iii) are false. \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Let $M=\left\{f\in L^2(-1,1):\int_{-1}^1 f(x)\,\mathrm{d} x=0\right\}$, and $g(x)=x^2$. What is $\inf\{\|g-f\|_2: f\in M\}$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{3}$. \item $\dfrac13$. \item $\dfrac23$. \item $1$. \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 Let $T:\bigl(C^1([0,1]),\|\cdot\|_\infty\bigr)\to\bigl(C([0,1]),\|\cdot\|_\infty\bigr)$ be defined by $T(f)=f'$. Consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $T$ is a closed operator. \item $\bigl(C^1([0,1]),\|\cdot\|_\infty\bigr)$ is a Banach space. \end{enumerate} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i) is true but (ii) is false. \item (i) and (ii) are both true. \item (i) and (ii) are both false. \item (i) is false but (ii) is true. \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Consider the holomorphic functions \[ f(z)=\frac{\sin z}{z^3}\quad\text{and}\quad g(w)=f\!\left(\frac1w\right),\qquad z,w\in\mathbb{C}\setminus\{0\}. \] Choose the correct statement. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(z)$ has a pole of order $3$ at $z=0$ and $g(w)$ has a pole of order $3$ at $w=0$. \item $f$ has a pole of order $3$ at $z=0$ and $g(w)$ has an essential singularity at $w=0$. \item $f$ has a pole of order $2$ at $z=0$ and $g(w)$ has a pole of order $3$ at $w=0$. \item $f$ has a pole of order $2$ at $z=0$ and $g(w)$ has an essential singularity at $w=0$. \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 Consider the following domains in $\mathbb{C}$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\Re z>0\text{ and }\Im z>0\}$. \item A bounded domain. \item $\mathbb{C}\setminus\{0\}$. \item $\mathbb{C}\setminus\overline{U}$ where $U$ is an open subset of $\mathbb{C}$ with $U\neq\mathbb{C}$. \end{enumerate} From which of the above domains does there exist an injective holomorphic function into the unit disc $\mathbb{D}$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i), (ii), and (iii). \item (i), (ii), and (iv). \item (i), (iii), and (iv). \item (i) and (ii). \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Let $X$ be a Hausdorff topological space such that for all closed sets $A\subseteq X$ and continuous functions $f:A\to[0,1]$ there exists a continuous function $\tilde f:X\to[0,1]$ such that $\tilde f|_A=f$. Which of the following statements is necessarily true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $C_1$ and $C_2$ are disjoint closed sets, then there are disjoint open sets $O_1$ and $O_2$ such that $C_1\subseteq O_1$ and $C_2\subseteq O_2$. \item $X$ is compact. \item $X$ is connected. \item All singleton subsets of $X$ are open. \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 Let $X$ and $Y$ be metric spaces, and $f:X\to Y$ be a bijective continuous function. Choose the correct statement. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $X$ is compact, then $f$ is a homeomorphism. \item If $Y$ is compact, then $X$ is compact. \item If $Y$ is connected, then $X$ is connected. \item If $X=\mathbb{R}$ and $Y\subseteq\mathbb{R}^2$, then $Y$ is a closed set. \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Let $E$ be a closed convex subset of a Hilbert space $H$. For each $x\in H$, there exists a unique $y_0\in E$ minimizing $\{\|x-y\|:y\in E\}$. Which of the following does this $y_0$ satisfy? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\langle x-y_0,z-y_0\rangle=0,\ \forall z\in E$. \item $\langle x-y_0,z-y_0\rangle\le0,\ \forall z\in E$. \item $\langle x-y_0,z-y_0\rangle\ge0,\ \forall z\in E$. \item $y_0\in x+E^\perp$. \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 Consider the following functional equation for $f$ in $C([0,1])$: \[ 2f(x)-\int_0^1\cos(f(y))\sqrt{1+(x-y)^2}\,\mathrm{d} y+\sin(e^x)=0,\qquad x\in[0,1]. \] Choose the correct statement. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exists no solution in $C([0,1])$. \item There exists a unique solution $f\in C([0,1])$. \item There exist exactly two solutions in $C([0,1])$. \item There exist infinitely many solutions in $C([0,1])$. \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 Consider the following functions on $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $f(t)=t+2t^2\sin(1/t)$. \item $g(t)=t+2t^3\sin(1/t)$. \item $h(t)=t^3\cos(t)$. \end{enumerate} Which among the above functions has an inverse in $(-\epsilon,\epsilon)\setminus\{0\}$ for some $\epsilon>0$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Only (iii). \item Only (ii) and (iii). \item Only (ii). \item All the functions (i), (ii), and (iii). \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 Let $f_n$ and $f$ be real-valued Lebesgue measurable functions on $[0,1]$. Suppose that $(f_n)$ converges to $f$ pointwise. Consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item For all $\epsilon>0$ there exists a compact set $K\subseteq[0,1]$ such that $(f_n)$ converges uniformly to $f$ on $K$ and the Lebesgue measure of $K$ is greater than $1-\epsilon$. \item There exists a dense set $U\subseteq[0,1]$ such that $(f_n)$ converges uniformly to $f$ on $U$. \end{enumerate} Which of the above statements are necessarily true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i) and (ii) are both true. \item (i) is true but (ii) is false. \item (ii) is true but (i) is false. \item (i) and (ii) are both false. \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exists a function $f\in C(\mathbb{R})$ such that $\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\to0}\int_0^1\bigl(f(x+\epsilon)-f(x)\bigr)\,\mathrm{d} x\ne0$. \item There exists a function $f\in L^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ such that $\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\to0}\int_0^1\bigl(f(x+\epsilon)-f(x)\bigr)\,\mathrm{d} x\ne0$. \item There exists a function $f\in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ such that $\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\to0}\int_0^1\bigl(f(x+\epsilon)-f(x)\bigr)\,\mathrm{d} x\ne0$. \item For all functions $f\in L^1(\mathbb{R})$, $\displaystyle\lim_{\epsilon\to0}\int_0^1\bigl(f(x+\epsilon)-f(x)\bigr)\,\mathrm{d} x=0$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $f_n(x)=\sin(n\pi x)$. Choose the correct statement. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(f_n)$ converges in $L^2(0,2)$. \item $(f_n)$ does not have any convergent subsequence in $L^2(0,2)$. \item There exists $E\subseteq(0,2)$ such that $E$ has Lebesgue measure $2$ and $f_n|_E$ converges pointwise. \item There exists an interval $F\subset(0,2)$ such that $f_n|_F$ converges in $L^2(F)$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 Let $X$ denote $\mathbb{R}^{2025}$ equipped with the norm $\|(x_1,\ldots,x_{2025})\|=\sum_{i=1}^{2025}|x_i|$ and $T:X\to X$ be defined by \[ T(u)=\begin{cases}u,&\text{if }\|u\|\le1,\\ u/\|u\|,&\text{if }\|u\|>1.\end{cases} \] Consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $\|T(u)-T(v)\|\le\|u-v\|$ for all $u,v\in X$. \item $\|T(u)-T(v)\|\le2\|u-v\|$ for all $u,v\in X$. \item $\|T(u)-T(v)\|\le3\|u-v\|$ for all $u,v\in X$. \end{enumerate} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (i), (ii) and (iii) are true. \item Only (ii) and (iii) are true. \item Only (iii) is true. \item (i), (ii) and (iii) are false. \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 Let $\mathcal F=\{f:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{D}:\ f \text{ is holomorphic and } f(\tfrac12)=0\}$ and $\alpha=\sup\{\Im f(0):f\in\mathcal F\}$. Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\alpha=1$. \item $\alpha=\dfrac13$. \item $\alpha=\dfrac12$. \item $\alpha=\dfrac23$. \end{enumerate}
  20. 20 Let $f:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic such that $|f(z)|\ge\sqrt{|z|}$ for all $z\in\mathbb{D}$. Consider the following statements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item There exists $z_0\in\mathbb{D}$ such that $|f(z_0)|<1$. \item There exists $z_0\in\mathbb{D}$ such that $|f(z_0)|\ge1$. \item The function $f$ is bounded. \end{enumerate} Choose the correct option. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Only (i) is true. \item Only (ii) is true. \item Only (i) and (ii) are true. \item Only (ii) and (iii) are true. \end{enumerate}
