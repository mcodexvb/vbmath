MMO-41

Advanced Complex Analysis-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2016
  1. 1(a) Define a meromorphic function. Prove that a function $f$ is meromorphic in the extended complex plane if and only if it is a rational function. [3]
  2. 1(b) Show that $\mathrm{cosec}\,z=\dfrac1z+\displaystyle\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}{}'(-1)^n\left(\dfrac1{z-n\pi}+\dfrac1{n\pi}\right)$ where $(')$ indicates that the term corresponding to $n=0$ is omitted from the sum. [4]
  3. 1(c) Let $n(x)$ denote the number of zeros of $f$ in $|z|\le x$, (zeros are counted with multiplicities). Suppose that $f$ is analytic in $|z|<R_1$ and $f(0)\ne0$. Let $r_1,r_2,\ldots$ be the moduli of zeros of $f$ in $|z|<R_1$, arranged in the non-decreasing order. If $r_m\le R<r_{m+1}$, then show that $\displaystyle\int_0^R\frac{n(x)}xdx=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log\left|f\left(Re^{i\theta}\right)\right|d\theta-\log|f(0)|.$ [3]
  4. 2(a) For a meromorphic function $f$, define $m(r,f),\ N(r,f).$ [2]
  5. 2(b) If $f_1,f_2,\ldots,f_p$ be p meromorphic functions in $|z|<r$, then show that\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace $m\left(r,\displaystyle\prod_{\gamma=1}^{p}f_\gamma\right)\le\sum_{\gamma=1}^{p}m(r,f_\gamma);$\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace $N\left(r,\displaystyle\prod_{\gamma=1}^{p}f_\gamma\right)\le\sum_{\gamma=1}^{p}N(r,f_\gamma).$ [3]
  6. 2(c) Establish the formula $T(r,f)=T\left(r,\dfrac1f\right)+\log|f(0)|$, where $f(0)\ne0,\infty$.\par Hence find $T(r,f)$ when $f(z)=z-2.$ [3]
  7. 2(d) Define the order of a meromorphic function $f(z)$. Find the order of $f(z)=e^{2z}$. [2]
  8. 3(a) For $|k|>1$, show that $|T(r,kf)-T(r,f)|\le\log|k|.$ [2]
  9. 3(b) Show that if $f(z)$ is meromorphic in $|z|<R$ and $h(z)=\dfrac{af(z)+b}{cf(z)+d}$, where a, b, c, d are constants such that $ad-bc\ne0$ and if $f(0)\ne\infty,\ h(0)\ne\infty$, then $T(r,h)=T(r,f)+O(1)\quad(0<r<R).$ [3]
  10. 3(c) State and prove Cartan's identity. [3]
  11. 3(d) Prove that for a meromorphic function $f,\ T(r,f)$ is an increasing convex function of $\log r$. \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}[ 2 ]\end{center} [2]
  12. 4(a) If a real valued non-decreasing function $S(r)$ of non-negative real variable is of order $\rho\,(0<\rho<\infty)$ and has convergence class, then show that $S(r)$ has minimal type of order $\rho$. [3]
  13. 4(b) If $f$ be a non-constant entire function then show that $T(r,f)\le\log M(r,f)\le3T(2r,f)$ for large $r$. [3]
  14. 4(c) Show that for the function $f(z)=e^{e^z},\ \dfrac{T(r,f)}{\log M(r,f)}\to0$ as $r\to\infty$. [4]
  15. 5(a) Suppose $f(z)$ is a non-constant meromorphic function in the complex plane and $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_q\,(\ge3)$ are distinct values in the extended complex plane. Then show that \[(q-2)T(r,f)<\sum_{j=1}^{q}N\left(r,\frac1{f-a_j}\right)-N_1(r)+S(r,f),\] where $N_1(r)$ and $S(r,f)$ are defined by second fundamental theorem. [3]
  16. 5(b) State and prove Nevanlinna's theorem on deficient values. [5]
  17. 5(c) Let $f$ and $g$ be non-constant polynomials and share 0 and 1 IM, then show that $f=g$. [2]
  18. 6(a) State and prove Nevanlinna's five-point uniqueness theorem. [4]
  19. 6(b) State Milloux's theorem. [1]
  20. 6(c) If $f$ is a non-constant meromorphic function, then prove that $T(r,f')\le\{2+o(1)\}T(r,f).$ [3]
  21. 6(d) If $\rho_1,\rho_2$ be the orders of the meromorphic functions $f_1(z)$ and $f_2(z)$ respectively, then show that order of $f_1+f_2\le\max\{\rho_1,\rho_2\}.$ [2]
