BMC-23

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2017
  1. 1 Answer \textit{any ten} questions from the following: \par
  2. 1(a) Find the conditions for which the general solution of the differential equation with constant coefficients $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+a\frac{dy}{dx}+by=0$ approaches zero as $x\to\infty$.
  3. 1(b) Find $y(4)$, where $y(x)$ is the solution of the differential equation $\frac d{dx}\left(x\frac{dy}{dx}\right)=x;\ y(1)=0,\ y'(1)=0$.
  4. 1(c) Solve the following nonlinear differential equation: $\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2+5\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)+6=0$.
  5. 1(d) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of straight lines $y=kx$, $k$ is some parameter.
  6. 1(e) Solve the following differential equation by inspection method: $xdy+ydx+\frac{xdy-ydx}{x^2+y^2}=0$.
  7. 1(f) Find the differential equation, whose solution is of the form $y(x)=a+bx+cx^2+de^{-11x}$, where $a,b,c$ and $d$ are arbitrary constants.
  8. 1(g) Solve the following differential equation by method of separation of variables: $\log\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)=ax+by$.
  9. 1(h) Find the value of $n$ for which the differential equation $\left(e^x+\frac{nx}y\right)dx-\frac{x^2}{y^2}dy=0$ is exact and hence solve the resulting equation.
  10. 1(i) Find the function $v(x)$, where $v(x)$ satisfies the differential equation $(D^2+1)^5(D-5)^2v(x)=0;\ D\equiv\frac d{dx}$.
  11. 1(j) Find the function $f(x)$, where $\Delta^{-1}(x^3+x^2)=f(x)$.
  12. 1(k) Find the Wronskian of linearly independent solutions of the differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+5\frac{dy}{dx}+6y=0$.
  13. 1(l) If an integral curve of the differential equation $(y)\frac{dy}{dx}=1$ passes through $(0,0)$ and $(\alpha,1)$, then find the value of $\alpha$.
  14. 1(m) Find the value of $u_5$, where $u_x$ is the solution of the difference equation $u_{x+2}-u_{x+1}-6u_x=0$; where $u_1=1,u_2=13$.
  15. 2(a) Show that the solution of the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}+P(x)y=Q(x)$ is $y(x)=e^{-\int Pdx}\int Qe^{\int Pdx}dx+Ce^{-\int Pdx}$, where $C$ is an arbitrary constant. Hence solve the equation $\frac{dy}{dx}+P(x)y=Q(x)$ with $y(0)=3$, where $P(x)=\begin{cases}2&0\le x\le1\\-\frac2x&x>1\end{cases}$ and $Q(x)=4x$. [2+4]
  16. 2(b) Solve the second order linear differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2\frac{dy}{dx}+y=xe^x\sin x$. [3]
  17. 2(c) Use the method of isoclines to sketch some of the solution curves of the equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=x^2+y^2$. [1]
  18. 3(a) Find a family of oblique trajectories that intersect the family of hyperbolas $xy=c$ at an angle $45^o$. [4]
  19. 3(b) Solve the nonlinear differential equation $y=2px+p^3y^2$. [3]
  20. 3(c) Solve the differential equation $x\frac{dy}{dx}-y=(x-1)\left(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-x+1\right)$, where $x$ and $e^x$ are two linearly independent solutions of the corresponding homogeneous equation. [3]
  21. 4(a) Transform the differential equation $x^2p^2+2pxy+py^2+y^2=0$ to Clairaut's form by the substitutions $y=u$ and $xy=v$ and hence solve the equation. [3]
  22. 4(b) Find the singular solution of the differential equation $xp^2=(x-a)^2$. Also find the tac-locus, cusp-locus and node-locus, if there by any. [4]
  23. 4(c) Reduce the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=1-x(y-x)-x^5(y-x)^5$ to a linear form. [1]
  24. 4(d) Radium disappears at a rate proportional to the amount present. If 5\% of the original amount disappears in 50 years, how much will remain after the end of 100 years. [2]
  25. 5(a) Consider the first order differential equation $M(x,y)dx+N(x,y)dy=0$. If $\frac1{\psi(M,N)}\left(\frac{\partial M}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial N}{\partial x}\right)=\varphi(x+y)$ is a function of $(x+y)$alone and $e^{\int\varphi(x+y)d(x+y)}$is an integrating factor of the above differential equation, then find the function $\psi(M,N)$.\par Hence solve the equation $(2xy-y^2-y)dx+(2xy-x^2-x)dy=0$. [5]
  26. 5(b) Solve the differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-4\frac{dy}{dx}+4y=x^3e^{2x}+xe^{2x}$ by the method of undetermined coefficients. [3]
  27. 5(c) Find the differential equation of all circles whose centres are on the $y$-axis. [2]
  28. 6(a) Solve the difference equation $u_{x+2}-7u_{x+1}-8u_x=x^{(2)}2^x$. [5]
  29. 6(b) Find an integrating factor of the differential equation $(-3x^{-1}-2y^4)dx+(-3y^{-1}+xy^3)dy=0$. [3]
  30. 6(c) Show that in a first order first degree homogeneous equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{f(x,y)}{g(x,y)}$, the variables can be separated by the substitution $y=vx$. [2]
