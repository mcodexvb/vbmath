SECMA-1

Algebra II and Analysis II
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2024
  1. 1(a) Define reduced row echelon matrix. Reduce the augmented matrix of the system \begin{align*}2x+y-3z&=5\\x-7z&=8\\5x-2y+3z&=2\end{align*} to its reduced row echelon form. [[2+2]]
  2. 1(b) Solve the following system. \begin{align*}x-2y+3z&=1\\2x-5y+10z&=0\\4x-7y+8z&=2.\end{align*} [[3]]
  3. 1(c) Find the values of $k$ so that the following system has unique solution. \begin{align*}x+y+z&=1\\x+2y+4z&=k\\x+4y+10z&=k^2.\end{align*} [[3]]
  4. 2(a) Draw the column picture for the system $2x+y=8$ and $x+2y=7$. [[3]]
  5. 2(b) Determine if $(2,-1,6)$ is a linear combination of the vectors $(1,-1,2),(5,-4,4)$ and $(3,-2,8)$. [[3]]
  6. 2(c) Express the vector $(2,5,7)$ as a linear combination of the three vectors $(1,2,3),(1,0,1)$ and $(1,3,4)$. [[4]]
  7. 3(a) Find the value of $h$ so that the vector $(1,h,1)$ is in the plane spanned by the vectors $(3,5,4)$ and $(2,4,3)$. [[3]]
  8. 3(b) Show that two vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ can not span the whole $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  9. 3(c) Define linear independence of vectors. Check linear independence of the vectors $\{(1,0,-1,2),(-3,2,-5,4),(5,7,-4,-3)\}$. [[1+3]]
  10. 4(a) Find the values of $h$ so that $\{(1,-2,-1,3),(-3,0,5,4),(-5,6,-4,h)\}$ is linearly independent. [[3]]
  11. 4(b) Let $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb R^3$. Show that $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are linearly independent if and only if they span a plane through the origin. [[4]]
  12. 4(c) Define subspaces of $\mathbb R^n$. Show that $V=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid2x+3y-z=0\}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  13. 5(a) Let $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\alpha_4\in\mathbb R^4$ and $A=(\alpha_1|\alpha_2|\alpha_3|\alpha_4)$. Show that $\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\alpha_4\}$ is a basis of $\mathbb R^4$ if and only if $A$ is invertible. [[4]]
  14. 5(b) Find the null space of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&2&4\\0&4&1\\3&2&11\end{pmatrix}$ and a spanning set of the null space. [[3]]
  15. 5(c) Let $A\in M_3(\mathbb R)$ and $b\in\mathbb R^3$. If the system $AX=b$ has a unique solution, then show that $r(A)=3$. Hence or otherwise show that the columns of $A$ span $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  16. 6(a) Show that every subspace of $\mathbb R^n$ has a basis. [[4]]
  17. 6(b) Find a basis and the dimension of the subspace $V=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid2x+3y-z=0\}$. [[3]]
  18. 6(c) Find a basis of the solution space of the homogeneous system: \begin{align*}x-2y+z&=0\\2x+3y-4z&=0\\4x-y-2z&=0.\end{align*} [[3]]
  19. 1(a) Find $y_n$ where $y=e^x\sin^2x$. [[3]]
  20. 1(b) If $y=\sin^{-1}x$ then show that\\(i) $(1-x^2)y_2-xy_1=0$.\\(ii) $(1-x^2)y_{n+2}-(2n+1)xy_{n+1}-n^2y_n=0$.\\Find also the value of $(y_n)_0$. [[7]]
  21. 2(a) Evaluate $\lim_{x\to0}\left(\frac1{x^2}\right)^{\tan x}$. [[4]]
  22. 2(b) Find the values of $a$ and $b$ in order that $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{x(1+a\cos x)-b\sin x}{x^3}=1$. [[6]]
  23. 3(a) State and prove Rolle's theorem. [[5]]
  24. 3(b) Verify Rolle's theorem for the function $f$ defined in $[1,2]$ by $f(x)=x(x-1)(x-2)$. [[2]]
  25. 3(c) In the Mean Value Theorem, $f(x+h)=f(x)+hf'(x+\theta h)$, if $f(x)=a+bx+cm^x$ where $a,b,c$ and $m$ are constants, then show that $\theta$ is independent on $x$. [[3]]
