MMC-42

Operations Research
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1(a) When an LPP has unbounded solution? Give the answer in connection with the revised simplex method (RSM). [2]
  2. 1(b) Find an optimum solution to the following LPP by applying RSM: \[\begin{aligned}\text{Maximize}\quad&f(x_1,x_2)=x_1+x_2\\\text{subject to}\quad&2x_1+5x_2\ge6,\\&x_1+x_2\ge2,\\&x_1,x_2\ge0.\end{aligned}\] [8]
  3. 2(a) Briefly discuss the utility of sensitivity analysis. [2]
  4. 2(b) Consider the following LPP: \[\begin{aligned}\text{Maximize}\quad&f(x_1,x_2)=4x_1+5x_2\\\text{subject to}\quad&3x_1+4x_2\le14,\\&2x_1+x_2\le6,\\&4x_1+2x_2\le8,\\&x_1,x_2\ge0.\end{aligned}\] Show that the optimality of the solution is not violated if the right hand side of the first constraint varies between 6 and 16. Show further that the range of $c_2$ is $\left(2,\frac{16}3\right)$ in order that the optimal solution obtained remains optimal. [4+4]
  5. 3(a) State the relative advantages and disadvantages of the Gomory's cutting plane method over the Branch and Bound method to solve an integer linear programming problem (ILPP). [2]
  6. 3(b) Solve the following ILPP: \[\begin{aligned}\text{Maximize}\quad&f(x_1,x_2)=6x_1+2x_2\\\text{subject to}\quad&3x_1+2x_2=5,\\&x_1,x_2\ge0\text{ and are integers.}\end{aligned}\] [8]
  7. 4(a) Use the Kuhn-Tucker necessary and sufficient conditions to solve the following nonlinear programming problem (NLPP): \[\begin{aligned}\text{Minimize}\quad&f(x_1,x_2)=(x_1-1)^2+(x_2-5)^2\\\text{subject to}\quad&-x_1^2+x_2\le4,\\&x_1-(x_2-2)^2\le3,\\&x_1,x_2\ge0.\end{aligned}\] \par\textbf{P.T.O.} [5]
  8. 4(b) Solve the following NLPP graphically: \[\begin{aligned}\text{Maximize}\quad&f(x_1,x_2)=x_1+2x_2\\\text{subject to}\quad&x_1^2+x_2^2\le1,\\&2x_1+x_2\le2,\\&x_1,x_2\ge0.\end{aligned}\] [5]
  9. 5(a) In a deterministic inventory model where supply is instantaneous and shortages are permitted in the sense of back orders, prove that the optimal value of total inventory cost is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2DC_sC_1C_2}{C_1+C_2}}$. [6]
  10. 5(b) The demand for an item is 18,000 units per year. The holding cost per unit time is Rs. 1.20, the cost of shortage is Rs. 5.00 and the production cost is Rs. 400.00. Assuming that replenishment rate is instantaneous, determine the optimal order quantity. [4]
  11. 6(a) For the queuing model system $(M/M/1:N/FCFS)$, find the probability of $n$ customers in the system in the form $p_n=\dfrac{(1-\rho)\rho^n}{1-\rho^{N+1}}$, $\rho\ne1$, $n=0,1,2,\ldots,N$. [6]
  12. 6(b) A car park contains 5 cars. The arrival of car is Poisson at a mean rate of 10 per hour. The length of time each car spends in the car park has negative exponential distribution with mean of 5 hours. How many cars are in the car park and what is the probability of a newly arriving customer finding the car park full and having to park elsewhere? \begin{center}Use separate answer Script for each Unit\\M.A./M.Sc. Examination 2019\\Semester - IV\\Mathematics\\Elective Course: MME-41(Applied Stream)\\( Electromagnetic Theory and Programming in Matlab )\end{center} \textbf{Time: Three Hours}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par \begin{center}Questions are of value as indicated in the margin.\\Notations and symbols have their usual meanings.\end{center} [2+2]
  13. 1(a) State and explain the Coulomb's law in electrostatics for two point charges. [1+2]
  14. 1(b) Define electric field and potential at a point for a charged particle of charge $q$. Find the electric field near a long uniformly charged cylindrical rod of radius $a$.
  15. 1(c) State Gauss' law in integral form and hence derive its differential form. [2]
  16. 2(a) Define electrostatic energy of a point charge $q$ in an electric field $\boldsymbol E$. Show that for a continuous charge distribution the energy density of $\boldsymbol E$ is given by $u_e=\frac12\varepsilon_0|\boldsymbol E|^2$. [1+3]
  17. 2(b) Derive the equation of continuity for a steady state current of electric charges. What is Ohm's law? Hence show that in the case of a steady state current, the continuity equation reduces to the Laplace equation for the electrostatic potential $\phi$. [2+1+1]
  18. 2(c) What is Lorentz force? Define the magnetic scalar potential $\phi_m$. [1+1]
  19. 3(a) Explain the phenomenon of electromagnetic induction and hence establish the differential form of Faraday's law. [2+2]
  20. 3(b) Write down the Maxwell's equations (in SI units) of electromagnetic theory. [4]
  21. 3(c) When the fields are time dependent, express the electric field $\boldsymbol E$ in terms of the scalar $(\phi)$ and vector $(\boldsymbol A)$ potentials. [2]
  22. 1 Attempt \textit{any five} questions (choose the correct alternative): [5x1=5]
  23. 1(a) What does MATLAB stand for? (i) Math Library (ii) Matrix Laboratory (iii) Matrix Library (iv) Matric Library.
  24. 1(b) Which command is used to clear a command window? (i) clear (ii) clear all (iii) clc (iv) clcr.
  25. 1(c) What would be the output of the following code in editor or command window? $A=[0\ 1;\ 1\ 0];\ B=2;\ C=A+B$ \quad (i) $C=\begin{smallmatrix}2&3\\3&2\end{smallmatrix}$ \quad (ii) $C=\begin{smallmatrix}2&1\\3&0\end{smallmatrix}$ \quad (iii) $C=\begin{smallmatrix}0&3\\1&2\end{smallmatrix}$ \quad (iv) $C=\begin{smallmatrix}2&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}$.
  26. 1(d) Which function is preferable to find the magnitude of a complex number: (i) abs() (ii) mod() (iii) absolute() (iv) MATLAB does not support complex arguments.
  27. 1(e) All MATLAB computations are done in (i) Single precision (ii) Double precision (iii) Linear accuracy (iv) Multi-level precision. \par\textbf{P.T.O.}
  28. 1(f) Which symbol is used to initialize a variable? (i) = (ii) $\to$ (iii) $==$ (iv) init.
  29. 1(g) What is the difference between the expressions $(9*1-8)$ and $(9-1*8)$? (i) Computational difference (ii) No difference (iii) Final results are different (iv) Cannot be determined.
  30. 1(h) Which function is used to generate a vector $x$ of 10 evenly spaced points in between 0 and 1? (i) linspace() (ii) zeros() (iii) ones() (iv) eye().
  31. 2(a) Write programs in MATLAB to (i) plot the function $\sin x$, $0\le x\le2\pi$ (ii) compute the sum of successive pairs: $b_i=a_i+a_{i+1}$, $i=1,2,\ldots,n-1$. [2+2]
  32. 2(b) Write the output of $A=[[1\ 2\ 3]'[4\ 5\ 6]']$ in command window. [1]
  33. 3(a) Use MATLAB code to find the roots of the polynomial $z^4+3Z^2+Z-4=0$. [1]
  34. 3(b) What will be displayed when you run the following code in MATLAB window? \par \texttt{a = 0; while a < 10\ \ a = a+3;\ \ end\ \ disp(a)} [2]
  35. 3(c) Use FFT to find the frequency spectrum of a signal buried in noise. You can form a signal containing a 100 Hz sinusoid of amplitude 1. [2]
  36. 4 Write a program in MATLAB to solve the following Lotka-Volterra equations: $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=ax-bxy,\ \dfrac{dy}{dt}=-cx+dxy$ with the initial conditions $x(0)=y(0)=2$. Here, the constants are $a=1.5$, $b=1$, $c=3$ and $d=1$. [5]
  37. 5 Write a program in MATLAB to solve the following coupled nonlinear equations: $x=y+\cos(x+y)$, $y=x+\sin(xy)$. [5]
