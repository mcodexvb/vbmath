MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2017
  1. 1(a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_\Gamma(z-\alpha)^{-m}dz$, where $\Gamma$ is any circle about $\alpha$ and $m$ is any integer. [3]
  2. 1(b) If $f(z)$ is analytic within and on a positively oriented simple closed contour $\Gamma$ and if $\alpha$ is any point within $\Gamma$, prove that \[ f'(\alpha)=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_\Gamma\frac{f(z)}{(z-\alpha)^2}dz. \] [5]
  3. 1(c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\oint_\Gamma\frac{z-1}{z^2-2z}dz$, where $\Gamma$ is a positively oriented simple closed contour containing 0 and 2 in its interior. [2]
  4. 2(a) State and prove fundamental theorem of algebra. [5]
  5. 2(b) Show that $f(z)=z^{10}+z^5+1$ vanishes at least for one point in $|z|<2$. [3]
  6. 2(c) If $z=\alpha$ is a pole of $f(z)$ of order $m$ then show that $\dfrac1{f(z)}$ has a zero of order $m$ at $z=\alpha$. [2]
  7. 3(a) State and prove Taylor's theorem. [5]
  8. 3(b) Expand $f(z)=\dfrac1{1-z}$ in its power series about $z=0$. [2]
  9. 3(c) If $f(z)=\dfrac{P(z)}{Q(z)}$, where $P(z)$ and $Q(z)$ are analytic at $\alpha$ and $P(\alpha)\ne0$, while $\alpha$ is a simple zero of $Q(z)$, then prove that $\alpha$ is a simple pole of $f(z)$ with residue $\dfrac{P(\alpha)}{Q'(\alpha)}$. [3]
  10. 4(a) Prove that a function $f$ has a zero at $\alpha$ of order $m$ if and only if $f$ can be expressed in the form $f(z)=(z-\alpha)^m\psi(z)$, where $\psi(z)$ is analytic at $\alpha$ and $\psi(\alpha)\ne0$. [4]
  11. 4(b) Show that $\sec\frac1z$ has simple poles at the points $\alpha=\dfrac1{(n+\frac12)\pi}$, where $n$ is an integer and find the residue of the function at $\alpha$. [4]
  12. 4(c) Prove that the zeros of a non-constant analytic function are isolated points. \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [2]
  13. 5(a) State and prove Cauchy's residue theorem. [4]
  14. 5(b) Show that $\displaystyle\oint_\Gamma\frac{e^{2z}}{\cosh\pi z}dz=4i\sin1$, where $\Gamma$ denotes the unit circle. [3]
  15. 5(c) If $k>1$, show that the equation $z^ne^{k-z}=1$ has $n$ roots within $|z|=1$, where $n$ is a positive integer. [3]
  16. 6(a) Find the essential singularities of the function $f(z)=\sin\dfrac1{z-1}+\sin\dfrac1{z+1}$. [3]
  17. 6(b) A function which is analytic everywhere including the point at infinity is a mere constant -- prove it. [2]
  18. 6(c) Evaluate \textbf{\textit{any one}} of the following by the method of contour integration: \begin{romanparts} \item $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\cos x^2dx;$ \item $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x\sin x}{a^2x^2+b^2}dx,\quad a,b>0.$ \end{romanparts} [5]
