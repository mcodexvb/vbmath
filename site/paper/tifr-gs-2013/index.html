TIFR Graduate School · GS 2013 · Mathematics

  1. 1 Every countable group $G$ has only countably many distinct subgroups.
  2. 2 Any automorphism of the group $(\mathbb{Q}, +)$ under addition is of the form $x \mapsto qx$ for some $q \in \mathbb{Q}$.
  3. 3 The equation $x^3 + 3x - 4 = 0$ has exactly one real root.
  4. 4 The equation $x^3 + 10x^2 - 100x + 1729 = 0$ has at least one complex root $\alpha$ such that $|\alpha| > 12$.
  5. 5 All non-trivial proper subgroups of $(\mathbb{R}, +)$ are cyclic.
  6. 6 Every infinite abelian group has at least one element of infinite order.
  7. 7 If $A$ and $B$ are similar matrices then every eigenvector of $A$ is an eigenvector of $B$.
  8. 8 If a real square matrix $A$ is similar to a diagonal matrix and satisfies $A^n = 0$ for some $n$, then $A$ must be the zero matrix.
  9. 9 There is an element of order $51$ in the multiplicative group $(\mathbb{Z}/103\mathbb{Z})^*$.
  10. 10 Any normal subgroup of order $2$ is contained in the center of the group.
  11. 11 Consider the sequences $x_n = \sum_{j=1}^n \frac{1}{j}$ and $y_n = \sum_{j=1}^n \frac{1}{j^2}$. Then $\{x_n\}$ is Cauchy but $\{y_n\}$ is not.
  12. 12 $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x^2)}{\text{[obscured]}} = 1$.
  13. 13 Let $f: [a, b] \to [c, d]$ and $g: [c, d] \to \mathbb{R}$ be Riemann integrable functions defined on the closed intervals $[a, b]$ and $[c, d]$ respectively. Then the composite $g \circ f$ is also Riemann integrable.
  14. 14 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be defined by $f(x) = \sin x^3$. Then $f$ is continuous but not uniformly continuous.
  15. 15 Let $x_1 \in (0, 1)$ be a real number between $0$ and $1$. For $n \ge 1$, define $x_{n+1} = x_n - x_n^{n+1}$. Then $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n$ exists.
  16. 16 Suppose $\{a_i\}$ is a sequence in $\mathbb{R}$ such that $\sum |a_i||x_i| < \infty$ whenever $\sum |x_i| < \infty$. Then $\{a_i\}$ is a bounded sequence.
  17. 17 The integral $\displaystyle \int_0^\infty e^{-x^5}\,dx$ is convergent.
  18. 18 Let $P(x) = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^3}{3!} + \dots + \frac{x^n}{n!}$ where $n$ is a large positive integer. Then $\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{e^x}{P(x)} = 1$.
  19. 19 Every differentiable function $f: (0, 1) \to [0, 1]$ is uniformly continuous.
  20. 20 Consider the function $f(x) = ax + b$ with $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the iteration $x_{n+1} = f(x_n)$, $n \ge 0$, for a given $x_0$ converges to $b/(1 - a)$ whenever $0 < a < 1$.
  21. 21 Every homeomorphism of the $2$-sphere to itself has a fixed point.
  22. 22 The intervals $[0, 1)$ and $(0, 1)$ are homeomorphic.
  23. 23 Let $X$ be a complete metric space such that distance between any two points is less than $1$. Then $X$ is compact.
  24. 24 There exists a continuous surjective function from $S^1$ onto $\mathbb{R}$.
  25. 25 There exists a complete metric on the open interval $(0, 1)$ inducing the usual topology.
  26. 26 There exists a continuous surjective map from the complex plane onto the non-zero reals.
  27. 27 If every differentiable function on a subset $X \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ (i.e., restriction of a differentiable function on a neighbourhood of $X$) is bounded, then $X$ is compact.
  28. 28 Let $f: X \to Y$ be a continuous map between metric spaces. If $f$ is a bijection, then its inverse is also continuous.
  29. 29 Let $f$ be a function on the closed interval $[0, 1]$ defined by \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} x & \text{if } x \text{ is rational,} \\ x^2 & \text{if } x \text{ is irrational.} \end{cases} \] Then $f$ is continuous at $0$ and $1$.
  30. 30 There exists an infinite subset $S \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ such that any three vectors in $S$ are linearly independent.
  31. 31 The inequality $\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n} < \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}$ is false for all $n$ such that $101 \le n \le 2000$.
  32. 32 $\displaystyle \lim_{n \to \infty} \left((n + 1)^{1/3} - n^{1/3}\right) = \infty$.
  33. 33 There exists a bijection between $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the open interval $(0, 1)$.
  34. 34 Let $S$ be the set of all sequences $\{a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n, \dots\}$ where each entry $a_i$ is either $0$ or $1$. Then $S$ is countable.
  35. 35 Let $\{a_n\}$ be any non-constant sequence in $\mathbb{R}$ such that $a_{n+1} = \frac{a_n + a_{n+2}}{2}$ for all $n \ge 1$. Then $\{a_n\}$ is unbounded.
  36. 36 The function $f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by $f(n) = n^3 - 3n$ is injective.
  37. 37 The polynomial $x^3 + 3x - 2\pi$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{R}$.
  38. 38 Let $V$ be the vector space consisting of polynomials with real coefficients in variable $t$ of degree $< 9$. Let $D: V \to V$ be the linear operator defined by $D(f) := \frac{df}{dt}$. Then $0$ is an eigenvalue of $D$.
  39. 39 If $A$ is a complex $n \times n$ matrix with $A^2 = A$ then $\operatorname{rank} A = \operatorname{trace} A$.
  40. 40 The series $\displaystyle 1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{4}} + \dots$ is divergent.
