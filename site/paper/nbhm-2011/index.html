NBHM · Research Scholarships Screening Test 2011

  1. 1.1 Solve: $x^4 - 3x^3 + 4x^2 - 3x + 1 = 0$.
  2. 1.2 Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Let $H$ and $K$ be subgroups of a group $G$. For $g \in G$, define the double coset $HgK = \{hgk \mid h \in H,\ k \in K\}$. Then, if $H$ is normal, we have $HgH = gH$ for all $g \in G$. \item Let $GL(n; \C)$ be the group of all $n \times n$ invertible matrices with complex entries. The set of all $n \times n$ invertible upper triangular matrices is a normal subgroup. \item Let $\M(n; \R)$ denote the set of all $n \times n$ matrices with real entries (identified with $\R^{n^2}$ and endowed with its usual topology) and let $GL(n; \R)$ denote the group of invertible matrices. Let $G$ be a subgroup of $GL(n; \R)$. Define \[ H = \left\{A \in G \;\middle|\; \begin{array}{l}\text{there exists } \varphi : [0, 1] \to G \text{ continuous,}\\ \text{such that } \varphi(0) = A,\ \varphi(1) = I\end{array}\right\}. \] Then, $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. \end{enumerate}
  3. 1.3 How many (non-isomorphic) groups of order $15$ are there?
  4. 1.4 Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity. Let $M$ be an ideal such that every element of $R$ not in $M$ is a unit. Then $R/M$ is a field. \item Let $R$ be as above and let $M$ be an ideal such that $R/M$ is an integral domain. Then $M$ is a prime ideal. \item Let $R = \mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ be the ring of real-valued continuous functions on $[0, 1]$ with respect to pointwise addition and pointwise multiplication. Let $M = \{f \in R \mid f(0) = f(1) = 0\}$. Then $M$ is a maximal ideal. \end{enumerate}
  5. 1.5 Write down all the possible values for the degree of an irreducible polynomial in $\R[x]$.
  6. 1.6 Let $V$ be the real vector space of all polynomials in $\R[x]$ with degree less than, or equal to $4$. Consider the linear transformation which maps $p \in V$ to its derivative $p'$. If the matrix of this transformation with respect to the basis $\{1, x, x^2, x^3, x^4\}$ is $A$, write down the matrix $A^3$.
  7. 1.7 Let $\T(n; \R) \subset \M(n; \R)$ denote the set of all matrices whose trace is zero. Write down a basis for $\T(2; \R)$.
  8. 1.8 What is the quotient space $\M(n; \R)/\T(n; \R)$ isomorphic to?
  9. 1.9 Construct a $2 \times 2$ matrix $A (\neq I)$ with real entries such that $A^3 = I$.
  10. 1.10 If $A \in \M(n; \R)$, let ${}^t A$ denote its transpose. A matrix $S \in \M(n; \R)$ is said to be \emph{skew-symmetric} if ${}^t S = -S$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $S \in \M(n; \R)$ is skew-symmetric and non-singular, then $n$ is even. \item Let $G = \{T \in GL(n; \R) \mid {}^t T S T = S,\ \text{for all skew-symmetric } S \in \M(n; \R)\}$. Then $G$ is a subgroup of $GL(n; \R)$. \item Let $I_n$ and $O_n$ denote the $n \times n$ identity and null matrices respectively. Let $S$ be the $2n \times 2n$ matrix given in block form by \[ \begin{bmatrix} O_n & I_n \\ -I_n & O_n \end{bmatrix}. \] If $X$ is a $2n \times 2n$ matrix such that ${}^t X S + SX = 0$, then the trace of $X$ is zero. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 Let $\{a_n\}$ be a sequence of positive terms. Pick out the cases which imply that $\sum a_n$ is convergent. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} n^{\frac{3}{2}} a_n = \frac{3}{2}$. \item $\displaystyle\sum n^2 a_n^2 < \infty$. \item $\displaystyle\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} < \left(\frac{n}{n+1}\right)^2$, for all $n$. \end{enumerate}
  12. 2.2 Evaluate: \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \left\{\frac{1}{1+n^3} + \frac{4}{8+n^3} + \dots + \frac{n^2}{n^3+n^3}\right\}. \]
  13. 2.3 Find the points in $\R$ where the following function is differentiable: \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} \tan^{-1} x, & \text{if } |x| \le 1 \\ \frac{\pi}{4}\sgn(x) + \frac{|x|-1}{2}, & \text{if } |x| > 1, \end{cases} \] where $\sgn(x)$ equals $+1$ if $x > 0$, $-1$ if $x < 0$ and is equal to zero if $x = 0$ and $\tan^{-1}(x)$ takes its values in the range $]-\pi/2, \pi/2[$ for real numbers $x$.
  14. 2.4 Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $P$ is a polynomial in one variable with real coefficients which has all its roots real, then its derivative $P'$ has all its roots real as well. \item The equation $\cos(\sin x) = x$ has exactly one solution in the interval $\left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$. \item $\cos x > 1 - \frac{x^2}{2}$ for all $x > 0$. \end{enumerate}
  15. 2.5 Let $f, f_n : [0, 1] \to \R$ be continuous functions. Complete the following sentence such that both statements (a) and (b) below are true: ``Let $f_n \to f$ \underline{\hspace{2cm}}'' \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^1 f_n(x)\,dx = \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$. \item $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty}\lim_{x \to 0} f_n(x) = \lim_{x \to 0}\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x)$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Let $f : ]0, 1[ \to \R$ be continuous. Pick out the statements which imply that $f$ is uniformly continuous. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $|f(x) - f(y)| \le \sqrt{|x - y|}$, for all $x, y \in ]0, 1[$. \item $f(1/n) \to 1/2$ and $f(1/n^2) \to 1/4$. \item $f(x) = x^{\frac{1}{2}} \sin\dfrac{1}{x^3}$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 2.7 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\iint_{[0,1]\times[0,1]} \max\{x, y\}\,dxdy$.
  18. 2.8 Let $f : \C \to \C$ be analytic. Pick out the cases when $f$ is \textbf{not} necessarily a constant. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\ImPart(f'(z)) > 0$ for all $z \in \C$. \item $f(n) = 3$ for all $n \in \Z$. \item $f'(0) = 0$ and $|f'(z)| \le 3$ for all $z \in \C$. \end{enumerate}
  19. 2.9 Let $f : \C \to \C$ be analytic. Write $z = x + iy$ and $f = u + iv$, where $u$ and $v$ are real valued functions of $x$ and $y$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f'(z) = \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x} + i\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}$. \item $\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} = 0$. \item $f''(0) = \dfrac{1}{2\pi i}\displaystyle\int_{|z|=1} \frac{f(z)}{z^3}\,dz$. \end{enumerate}
  20. 2.10 Find the square roots of $1 + i\sqrt{3}$.
  21. 3.1 Which of the following define a metric? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $d((x, y), (x', y')) = \min\{|x - x'|, |y - y'|\}$ on $\R^2$. \item $d((x, y), (x', y')) = |x| + |y| + |x'| + |y'|$ on $\R^2$. \item $D((x, y), (x', y')) = d(x, x') + d(y, y')$ on $X \times X$, where $(X, d)$ is a metric space. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space and let $A \subset X$. For $x \in X$ define $d(x, A) = \inf\{d(x, y) \mid y \in A\}$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $x \mapsto d(x, A)$ is a uniformly continuous function. \item If $\partial A = \{x \in X \mid d(x, A) = 0\} \cap \{x \in X \mid d(x, X \setminus A) = 0\}$, then $\partial A$ is closed for any $A \subset X$. \item Let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of $X$ and define $d(A, B) = \inf\{d(a, B) \mid a \in A\}$. Then $d(A, B) = d(B, A)$. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Let $X$ be a topological space and for $A \subset X$, denote by $\overline{A}$ and $A^\circ$, the closure and interior of $A$ respectively. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\overline{A \cup B} = \overline{A} \cup \overline{B}$. \item $\overline{A \cap B} = \overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$. \item Consider $\R$ as the $x$-axis in $\R^2$. Then $\R^\circ = \emptyset$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 3.4 Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Let $\{X_i\}_{i \in \mathcal{I}}$ be topological spaces. Then, the product topology is the smallest topology on $X = \prod_{i \in \mathcal{I}} X_i$ such that each of the canonical projections $p_i : X \to X_i$ is continuous. \item Let $X$ be a topological space and $W \subset X$. Then, the induced subspace topology on $W$ is the smallest topology such that $id|_W : W \to X$, where $id$ is the identity map, is continuous. \item Let $X = \R^n$ with the usual topology. This is the smallest topology such that all linear functionals on $X$ are continuous. \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Which of the following subsets are dense in the given spaces? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The set of trigonometric polynomials in the space of continuous functions on $[-\pi, \pi]$ which are $2\pi$-periodic (with the sup-norm topology). \item The subset of $\mathcal{C}^\infty$ functions with compact support in $\R$ in the space of bounded real-valued continuous functions on $\R$ (with the sup-norm topology). \item $GL(n; \R)$ in $\M(n; \R)$ (with its usual topology after identification with $\R^{n^2}$). \end{enumerate}
  26. 3.6 Pick out the compact sets. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\{(x, y) \mid x^2 - y^2 = 1\} \subset \R^2$. \item $\{\operatorname{Tr}(A) \mid A \in \M(n; \R),\ A \text{ orthogonal}\} \subset \R$, where $\operatorname{Tr}(A)$ denotes the trace of the matrix $A$. \item The set of all matrices in $\M(n; \R)$ all of whose eigenvalues satisfy the condition $|\lambda| \le 2$. \end{enumerate}
  27. 3.7 Pick out the connected sets. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\{(x, y) \mid xy = 1\} \subset \R^2$. \item The set of all upper triangular matrices in $\M(n; \R)$. \item The set of all invertible diagonal matrices in $\M(n; \R)$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Let $f : \Z \to \Z^2$ be a bijection. There exists a continuous function from $\R$ to $\R^2$ which extends $f$. \item Let $D$ denote the closed unit disc in $\R^2$. There exists a continuous mapping $f : D \setminus \{(0, 0)\} \to \{x \in \R \mid |x| \le 1\}$ which is onto. \item Let $D$ denote the closed unit disc in $\R^2$. There exists a continuous mapping $f : D \setminus \{(0, 0)\} \to \{x \in \R \mid |x| > 1\}$ which is onto. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 A Hausdorff topological space is said to be normal if given any two disjoint closed sets $A$ and $B$, there exist disjoint open sets $U$ and $V$ such that $A \subset U$ and $B \subset V$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Every metric space is normal. \item If $X$ is a normal space with at least two distinct points, then there exist non-constant real-valued continuous functions on $X$. \item If $X$ is normal and $Y \subset X$ is closed, then $Y$ is normal for the induced topology. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Which of the following pairs of sets are homeomorphic? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $A = \{(x, y) \mid x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 5 = 0\}$ and $B = \{(x, y) \mid 5x^2 + 3y^2 = 1\}$. \item $A = \{(x, y) \mid x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 5 = 0\}$ and $B = \{(x, y) \mid 5x^2 - 3y^2 = 1\}$. \item $A = \{(x, y) \mid x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 5 \le 0\}$ and $B = \{(x, y) \mid 5x^2 + 3y^2 \ge 1\}$. \end{enumerate}
  31. 4.1 Simpson's rule is used to approximate the integral $\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$. If $f$ is a polynomial, what is the maximum possible degree it can have so that Simpson's rule gives the exact value of this integral?
  32. 4.2 A right circular cylinder of fixed volume has maximum total surface area. What is the relationship between its height $h$ and radius $r$?
  33. 4.3 In the equations governing the flow of an incompressible fluid of uniform density, if $\mathbf{u}$ is the velocity vector and $p$ is the pressure, write down the equation which expresses the law of conservation of mass.
  34. 4.4 A particle of mass $M$ is attached to a fixed wall by a spring. The spring exerts no force when the particle is at its equilibrium position at $x = 0$ and exerts a restoring force proportional to the displacement when it is displaced to a distance $x$. In addition, there is a damping force due to the medium in which the displacement takes place, which is a force opposing the motion and is proportional to the velocity of the particle. If the particle is pulled to a position $x_0$ at time $t = 0$ and is released without any velocity, write down the initial value problem governing the motion of the particle.
  35. 4.5 Solve the following linear programming problem: \begin{align*} \max z &= 5x + 7y \\ x - y &\le 1 \\ 2x + y &\ge 2 \\ x + 2y &\le 4 \\ x, y &\ge 0. \end{align*}
  36. 4.6 Write down the dual of the above problem.
  37. 4.7 Find the general solution of the system: \begin{align*} \frac{dx}{dt} &= x + y \\ \frac{dy}{dt} &= 4x - 2y. \end{align*}
  38. 4.8 Let $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$ be vectors in $\R^3$. Express $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ as a linear combination of $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$.
  39. 4.9 Solve: $\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = 6xy;\quad u(0, y) = y;\quad \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}(1, y) = 0$.
  40. 4.10 Let $\Omega$ be a smooth plane domain of unit area. Let $u(x, y) = 3x^2 + y^2$. If $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ denotes its outer normal derivative on $\partial\Omega$, the boundary of $\Omega$, compute $\int_{\partial\Omega} \frac{\partial u}{\partial n}\,ds$.
  41. 5.1 Let $V$ be a real vector space of real-valued functions on a given set. Assume that constant functions are in $V$ and that if $f \in V$, then $f^2 \in V$ and that $|f| \in V$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $f, g \in V$, then $fg \in V$. \item If $f, g \in V$, then $\max\{f, g\} \in V$. \item If $f \in V$ and $p$ is any polynomial in one variable, with real coefficients, then $p(f) \in V$. \end{enumerate}
  42. 5.2 A fair coin is tossed $10$ times, the tosses being independent of each other. Find the probability that the results of the third, fourth and fifth tosses are identical.
  43. 5.3 Determine if the following collections are countable or uncountable. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The collection of all finite subsets of $\N$. \item The collection of all infinite sequences of positive integers. \item The collection of all roots of all polynomials in one variable, with integer coefficients. \end{enumerate}
  44. 5.4 Find the maximum value of $x + 2y + 3z$ subject to the constraint $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$.
  45. 5.5 Let $A_n$ be the $n \times n$ matrix whose $(i, j)$-th entry is given by $2\delta_{ij} - \delta_{i+1,j} - \delta_{i,j+1}$, where $\delta_{ij}$ equals $1$ if $i = j$ and zero otherwise. Compute the determinant of $A_n$.
  46. 5.6 How many real roots does the following equation have? $3^x + 4^x = 5^x$.
  47. 5.7 Let $N > 1$ be a positive integer. Let $\phi(N)$ denote the number of positive integers less than $N$ and prime to it (unity being included in this count). Express the sum of all the integers less than $N$ and prime to it in terms of $\phi(N)$.
  48. 5.8 Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The sum of $r$ consecutive positive integers is divisible by $r$. \item The product of $r$ consecutive positive integers is divisible by $r!$. \item For each positive integer $r$, there exist $r$ consecutive positive integers which are all composite. \end{enumerate}
  49. 5.9 Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer and let $0 \le k \le n$. We denote by $C_k$, the number of ways of choosing $k$ objects from $n$ distinct objects. Sum to $n$ terms: \[ 3C_1 + 7C_2 + 11C_3 + \cdots \]
  50. 5.10 Find the sum of the following infinite series: \[ \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1\cdot 4}{5\cdot 10} + \frac{1\cdot 4\cdot 7}{5\cdot 10\cdot 15} - \cdots \]
