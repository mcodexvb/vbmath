MMC-14

Ordinary Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2016
  1. 1(a) Using Picard's method of successive approximations, find first two approximations for the differential equation $y'=y-x$, with $y(0)=2$. [2]
  2. 1(b) Show that $f(x,y)=xy^2$ satisfies a Lipschitz condition on the rectangle $|x|\leq1$, $|y|\leq2$ but does not satisfy Lipschitz condition on the strip $|x|\leq1$, $|y|\leq\infty$. [2]
  3. 1(c) Give an example of a function $f(x,y)$, so that $f(x,y)$ satisfies a Lipschitz condition but $\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial y}$ is not continuous in a domain $D$. [1]
  4. 1(d) State the uniqueness theorem of $1^{st}$ order initial value problem. Hence find the largest interval of existence of a unique solution of the differential equation $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\cos^2x+y^2$, $y(0)=0$. [1+4]
  5. 2(a) State initial and boundary value problems. [2]
  6. 2(b) Using Green's function solve the initial value problem $y''+4y=f(x)$, $y(0)=1$, $y'(0)=-2$, where the forcing function $f$ is defined as\\$f(x)=\begin{cases}0, & x<0\\ \sin2x, & 0\leq x\leq2\pi\\ 0, & x>2\pi.\end{cases}$ [5]
  7. 2(c) Find the Green's function for the boundary value problem $x^2y''-3xy'+3y=24x^5$ with $y(1)=0$, $y(2)=0$. [3]
  8. 3(a) Consider the second order linear differential equation $a_0(t)\dfrac{d^2x}{dt^2}+a_1(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}+a_2(t)x=0$ where $a_0$ has a continuous second derivative, $a_1$ has a continuous first derivative, $a_2$ is continuous.\\Prove that the above differential equation is self-adjoint iff $\dfrac{d}{dt}\left[a_0(t)\right]=a_1(t)$.\\Hence transform the equation $f(t)\dfrac{d^2x}{dt^2}+g(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}=0$, into an equivalent self-adjoint equation. [4]
  9. 3(b) Let $f$ and $g$ be any two solutions of $\dfrac{d}{dt}\left[p(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}\right]+q(t)x=0$, on the interval $[a,b]$.\\Then prove that $p(t)\left[f(t)g'(t)-f'(t)g(t)\right]=k$, where $k$ is a constant. [3]
  10. 3(c) Let $\{f,g\}$ and $\{F,G\}$ be two fundamental sets of the differential equation $a_0(t)\dfrac{d^2x}{dt^2}+a_1(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}+a_2(t)x=0$, on $[a,b]$.\\Prove that $W(f,g)=cW(F,G)$ for all $t\in[a,b]$, where $c$ is a constant. [3]
  11. 4(a) Find the characteristic values and characteristic functions of the Sturm-Liouville problem\\$\dfrac{d}{dx}\left[x\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right]+\dfrac{\lambda}{x}y=0$, with $y'(1)=0$ and $y'\!\left(e^{2\pi}\right)=0$, $\lambda$ is a non-negative parameter. [5]
  12. 4(b) Solve the nonhomogeneous linear system\\$\dfrac{dX}{dt}=\begin{pmatrix}6&-3\\2&1\end{pmatrix}X+\begin{pmatrix}e^{2t}\\-e^{2t}\end{pmatrix}.$ [5]
  13. 5(a) Determine the nature of the equilibrium point for the system\\$x'=2x+4y,$\\$y'=-2x+6y,$\\Examine the stability of the equilibrium point. [2]
  14. 5(b) Find the general solution of the linear system\\$x'=10x-y,$\\$y'=25x+2y,$\\by eigenvalue-eigenvector method. [6]
  15. 5(c) Let $u$ and $v$ be two linearly independent solutions of the linear equation\\$\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\dfrac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0.$\\Prove that the Wronskian $W(u,v)=Aw^{-\int P\,dx}$, $A$ is a constant. [2]
  16. 6(a) Find the general solution of the differential equation\\$x^2y''+xy'+(x^2-n^2)y=0$, where $n(n>0)$ is not an integer. [4]
  17. 6(b) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int J_3(x)\,dx$ and express the result in terms of $J_0$ and $J_1$. [2]
  18. 6(c) Prove that $P_n(x)=\dfrac{1}{2^nn!}\dfrac{d^n}{dx^n}(x^2-1)^n$. Using the above formula find the values of $p_2(x)$. [3+1]
