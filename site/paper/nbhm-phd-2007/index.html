NBHM · 2007 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Let $G$ be a group of order $n$. Which of the following conditions imply that $G$ is abelian? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $n = 15$. \item $n = 21$. \item $n = 36$. \end{enumerate}
  2. 1.2 Which of the following subgroups are necessarily normal subgroups? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The kernel of a group homomorphism. \item The center of a group. \item The subgroup consisting of all matrices with positive determinant in the group of all invertible $n \times n$ matrices with real entries (under matrix multiplication). \end{enumerate}
  3. 1.3 List all the units in the ring of Gaussian integers.
  4. 1.4 List all possible values occurring as $\deg f$ (degree of $f$) where $f$ is an irreducible polynomial in $\R[x]$.
  5. 1.5 Write down an irreducible polynomial of degree $3$ over the field $\F_3$ of three elements.
  6. 1.6 Let $A = (a_{ij})$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with real entries. Let $A_{ij}$ be the cofactor of the entry $a_{ij}$ of $A$. Let $\widetilde{A} = (A_{ij})$ be the matrix of cofactors. What is the rank of $\widetilde{A}$ under the following conditions: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item the rank of $A$ is $n$? \item the rank of $A$ is $\le n - 2$? \end{enumerate}
  7. 1.7 Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries which is not a diagonal matrix. Pick out the cases when $A$ is diagonalizable. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $A$ is idempotent. \item $A$ is nilpotent. \item $A$ is unitary. \end{enumerate}
  8. 1.8 For $n \ge 2$, let $\mathcal{M}(n)$ denote the ring of all $n \times n$ matrices with real entries. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $A \in \mathcal{M}(2)$ is nilpotent and non-zero, then there exists a matrix $B \in \mathcal{M}(2)$ such that $B^2 = A$. \item If $A \in \mathcal{M}(n)$, $n \ge 2$, is symmetric and positive definite, then there exists a symmetric matrix $B \in \mathcal{M}(n)$ such that $B^2 = A$. \item If $A \in \mathcal{M}(n)$, $n \ge 2$, is symmetric, then there exists a symmetric matrix $B \in \mathcal{M}(n)$ such that $B^3 = A$. \end{enumerate}
  9. 1.9 Which of the following matrices are non-singular? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $I + A$ where $A \neq 0$ is a skew-symmetric real $n \times n$ matrix, $n \ge 2$. \item Every skew-symmetric non-zero real $5 \times 5$ matrix. \item Every skew-symmetric non-zero real $2 \times 2$ matrix. \end{enumerate}
  10. 1.10 Let $V$ be a real finite-dimensional vector space and $f$ and $g$ non-zero linear functionals on $V$. Assume that $\ker(f) \subset \ker(g)$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\ker(f) = \ker(g)$. \item $\ker(g)/\ker(f) \cong \R^k$ for some $k$ such that $1 \le k < n$. \item There exists a constant $c \neq 0$ such that $g = cf$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 In each of the following cases, state whether the series is absolutely convergent, conditionally convergent (\emph{i.e.}\ convergent but not absolutely convergent) or divergent. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{1}{2n+3}$. \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{n}{n+2}$. \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{n\log n}{e^n}$. \end{enumerate}
  12. 2.2 Determine the interval of convergence of the series: \[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{n-1} \frac{(x-1)^n}{n}. \]
  13. 2.3 What is the cardinality of the following set? \[ A = \{f \in \mathcal{C}^1[0, 1] : f(0) = 0,\ f(1) = 1,\ |f'(t)| \le 1 \text{ for all } t \in [0, 1]\}. \]
  14. 2.4 Evaluate: \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^{\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]} \cos\left(\frac{k\pi}{n}\right) \] where $\left[\frac{n}{2}\right]$ denotes the largest integer not exceeding $\frac{n}{2}$.
  15. 2.5 Which of the following improper integrals are convergent? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^3 + 2x + 2}}$. \item $\displaystyle\int_0^5 \frac{dx}{x^2 - 5x + 6}$. \item $\displaystyle\int_0^5 \frac{dx}{\sqrt[3]{7x + 2x^4}}$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Which of the following series converge uniformly? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (a_n \cos nt + b_n \sin nt)$ over the interval $[-\pi, \pi]$ where $\sum_n |a_n| < \infty$ and $\sum_n |b_n| < \infty$. \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} e^{-nx} \cos nx$ over the interval $]0, \infty[$. \item $\displaystyle x^2 + \frac{x^2}{1+x^2} + \frac{x^2}{(1+x^2)^2} + \cdots$ over the interval $[-1, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 2.7 Let $f \in \mathcal{C}[0, \pi]$. Determine the cases where the given condition implies that $f \equiv 0$. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} x^n f(x)\,dx = 0$ for all integers $n \ge 0$. \item $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} f(x) \cos nx\,dx = 0$ for all integers $n \ge 0$. \item $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} f(x) \sin nx\,dx = 0$ for all integers $n \ge 1$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 2.8 Let $C$ be the circle defined by $|z| = 3$ in the complex plane, described in the anticlockwise direction. Evaluate: \[ \int_C \frac{2z^2 - z - 2}{z - 2}\,dz. \]
  19. 2.9 Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Let $f$ and $g$ be analytic in the disc $|z| < 2$ and let $f = g$ on the interval $[-1, 1]$. Then $f \equiv g$. \item If $f$ is a non-constant polynomial with complex coefficients, then it can be factorized into (not necessarily distinct) linear factors. \item There exists a non-constant analytic function in the disc $|z| < 1$ which assumes only real values. \end{enumerate}
  20. 2.10 Let $\Omega \subset \C$ be an open and connected set and let $f : \Omega \to \C$ be an analytic function. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f$ is bounded if $\Omega$ is bounded. \item $f$ is bounded only if $\Omega$ is bounded. \item $f$ is bounded if, and only if, $\Omega$ is bounded. \end{enumerate}
  21. 3.1 In each of the following, $f$ is assumed to be continuous. Pick out the cases when $f$ \emph{cannot} be onto. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f : [-1, 1] \to \R$. \item $f : [-1, 1] \to \Q \cap [-1, 1]$. \item $f : \R \to [-1, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Consider the set of all $n \times n$ matrices with real entries identified with $\R^{n^2}$, endowed with its usual topology. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The subset of all invertible matrices is connected. \item The subset of all invertible matrices is dense. \item The subset of all orthogonal matrices is compact. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Pick out the functions that are uniformly continuous on the given domain. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ on the interval $]0, 1[$. \item $f(x) = x^2$ on $\R$. \item $f(x) = \sin^2 x$ on $\R$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 3.4 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space and let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of $X$. Define \[ d(A, B) = \inf\{d(a, b) : a \in A,\ b \in B\}. \] Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $A$ and $B$ are disjoint, then $d(A, B) > 0$. \item If $A$ and $B$ are closed and disjoint, then $d(A, B) > 0$. \item If $A$ and $B$ are compact and disjoint, then $d(A, B) > 0$. \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Pick out the sets that are homeomorphic to the set $\{(x, y) \in \R^2 : xy = 1\}$. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\{(x, y) \in \R^2 : xy = 0\}$. \item $\{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 - y^2 = 1\}$. \item $\{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 = 1\}$. \end{enumerate}
  26. 3.6 Let $(X_i, d_i)$, $i = 1, 2, 3$, be the metric spaces where $X_1 = X_2 = X_3 = \mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ and \begin{align*} d_1(f, g) &= \sup_{t \in [0, 1]} |f(x) - g(x)| \\ d_2(f, g) &= \int_0^1 |f(x) - g(x)|\,dx \\ d_3(f, g) &= \left(\int_0^1 |f(x) - g(x)|^2\,dx\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}. \end{align*} Let $id$ be the identity map of $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ onto itself. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $id : X_1 \to X_2$ is continuous. \item $id : X_2 \to X_1$ is continuous. \item $id : X_3 \to X_2$ is continuous. \end{enumerate}
  27. 3.7 Pick out the compact sets. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\{(z_1, z_2) \in \C \times \C : z_1^2 + z_2^2 = 1\}$. \item The unit sphere in $\ell_2$, the space of all square summable real sequences, with its usual metric \[ d(\{x_i\}, \{y_i\}) = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} |x_i - y_i|^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}. \] \item The closure of the unit ball of $\mathcal{C}^1[0, 1]$ in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Let $f : S^1 \to \R$ be any continuous map, where $S^1$ is the unit circle in the plane. Let \[ A = \{(x, y) \in S^1 \times S^1 : x \neq y,\ f(x) = f(y)\}. \] Is $A$ non-empty? If the answer is `yes', is it finite, countable or uncountable?
  29. 3.9 Let $f : S^1 \to \R$ be any continuous map, where $S^1$ is the unit circle in the plane. Let \[ A = \{(x, y) \in S^1 \times S^1 : x = -y,\ f(x) = f(y)\}. \] Is $A$ non-empty?
  30. 3.10 Let $f \in \mathcal{C}^1[-1, 1]$ such that $|f(t)| \le 1$ and $|f'(t)| \le \frac{1}{2}$ for all $t \in [-1, 1]$. Let \[ A = \{t \in [-1, 1] : f(t) = t\}. \] Is $A$ non-empty? If the answer is `yes', what is its cardinality?
  31. 4.1 Let $u$ be a smooth function defined on the ball centered at the origin and of radius $a > 0$ in $\R^3$. Assume that \[ \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial z^2} = 1 \] throughout the ball. Compute: \[ \int_S \frac{\partial u}{\partial n}\,dS \] where $S$ is the sphere with centre at the origin and radius $a$ and $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ denotes the outer normal derivative of $u$ on $S$.
  32. 4.2 Consider a homogeneous fluid moving with velocity $u$ in space. Write down the equation which expresses the principle of conservation of mass.
  33. 4.3 Let $C$ be the equatorial circle on the unit sphere in $\R^3$ and let $\tau$ be the unit tangent vector to $C$ taken in the anticlockwise sense. Compute: \[ \int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot \tau\,ds \] where $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = x\mathbf{i} - y\mathbf{j} - z\mathbf{k}$.
  34. 4.4 Determine the value of the least possible positive number $\lambda$ such that the following problem has a non-trivial solution: \begin{align*} u''(x) + \lambda u(x) &= 0, \quad 0 < x < 1 \\ u'(0) = u'(1) &= 0. \end{align*}
  35. 4.5 A pendulum of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is pulled to an angle $\alpha$ from the vertical and released from rest. Write down the differential equation satisfied by the angle $\theta(t)$ made by the pendulum with the vertical at time $t$, using the principle of conservation of energy. (If $s$ is the arc length measured from the vertical position, then the velocity $v$ is given by $v = \frac{ds}{dt}$.)
  36. 4.6 Find d'Alembert's solution to the problem: \begin{align*} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} &= \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}, \quad x \in \R,\ t > 0 \\ u(x, 0) &= x^2, \quad x \in \R \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x, 0) &= 0, \quad x \in \R. \end{align*}
  37. 4.7 Solve: Minimize $z = 2x_1 + 3x_2$, such that \begin{align*} x_1 + x_2 &\le 4 \\ 3x_1 + x_2 &\ge 4 \\ x_1 + 5x_2 &\ge 4 \end{align*} and such that $0 \le x_1 \le 3$, and $0 \le x_2 \le 3$.
  38. 4.8 Consider the iterative scheme $x_{n+1} = B x_n + c$ for $n \ge 0$, where $B$ is a real $N \times N$ matrix and $c \in \R^N$. The scheme is said to be convergent if the sequence $\{x_n\}$ of iterates converges for \emph{every} choice of initial vector $x_0$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The scheme is convergent if, and only if, the spectral radius of $B$ is $< 1$. \item The scheme is convergent if, and only if, for some matrix norm $\|\cdot\|$, we have $\|B\| < 1$. \item The scheme is convergent if, and only if, $B$ has an eigenvalue $\lambda$ such that $0 < \lambda < 1$. \end{enumerate}
  39. 4.9 Write down the Laplace transform $L[f](p)$ of the function $f(x) = \sin ax$, where $a > 0$.
  40. 4.10 What is the necessary and sufficient condition for the following problem to admit a solution? \begin{align*} -\Delta u &= f \quad \text{in } \Omega \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial n} &= g \quad \text{on } \partial\Omega \end{align*} where $\Omega \subset \R^n$ is a bounded domain with boundary $\partial\Omega$, $\Delta$ is the Laplace operator, $f$ and $g$ are given smooth functions and $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ denotes the outer normal derivative of $u$.
  41. 5.1 Find the area of the polygon whose vertices are the $n$-th roots of unity in the complex plane, when $n \ge 3$.
  42. 5.2 Define $p_n(t) = \cos(n \cos^{-1} t)$ for $t \in [-1, 1]$. Express $p_4(t)$ as a polynomial in $t$.
  43. 5.3 What is the probability that a point $(x, y)$, chosen at random in the rectangle $[-1, 1] \times [0, 1]$ is such that $y > x^2$?
  44. 5.4 An urn contains four white balls and two red balls. A ball is drawn at random and is replaced in the urn each time. What is the probability that after two successive draws, both balls drawn are white?
  45. 5.5 Let $ABC$ be a triangle in the plane such that $BC$ is perpendicular to $AC$. Let $a, b, c$ be the lengths of $BC$, $AC$ and $AB$ respectively. Suppose that $a, b, c$ are integers and have no common divisor other than $1$. Which of the following statements are necessarily true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Either $a$ or $b$ is an even integer. \item The area of the triangle $ABC$ is an even integer. \item Either $a$ or $b$ is divisible by $3$. \end{enumerate}
  46. 5.6 What are the last two digits in the usual decimal representation of $3^{400}$?
  47. 5.7 Find the number of integers less than $3600$ and prime to it.
  48. 5.8 Let $n$ be a positive integer. Give an example of a sequence of $n$ consecutive composite numbers.
  49. 5.9 For a point $P = (x, y)$ in the plane, write $f(P) = ax + by$, where $a$ and $b$ are given real numbers. Let $f(A) = f(B) = 10$. Let $C$ be a point not on the line joining $A$ and $B$ and let $C'$ be the reflection of $C$ with respect to this line. If $f(C) = 15$, find $f(C')$.
  50. 5.10 Let $V$ be a four dimensional vector space over the field $\F_3$ of three elements. Find the number of distinct one-dimensional subspaces of $V$.
