DSEMA-1

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2022
  1. 1(a) What is meant by equimomental bodies? Show that a uniform plane triangular lamina is equimomental with a system of three particles placed at the middle points of the sides, each equal to one-third the mass of the lamina. [1+4]
  2. 1(b) Show that the equation of the momental ellipsoid at the centre of a solid ellipsoid is \[(b^2+c^2)x^2+(c^2+a^2)y^2+(a^2+b^2)z^2=\textit{constant}.\] [5]
  3. 2(a) If $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be the distances of the vertices of a triangle from a straight line in its plane, then show that the moment of inertia of the triangle about this line is \[\frac M6(\alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2+\alpha\beta+\beta\gamma+\gamma\alpha),\] where $M$ denotes its mass. [5]
  4. 2(b) Show that at the centre of the quadrant of an ellipse, the principal axes in its plane are inclined at an angle $\frac12\tan^{-1}\left(\frac4\pi\frac{ab}{a^2-b^2}\right)$ to the axes. [5]
  5. 3(a) State D'Alembert's principle. Obtain the general equations of motion of a rigid body from D'Alembert's principle. [1+4]
  6. 3(b) A plank of mass $M$ is initially at rest along a straight line of greatest slope of a smooth plane inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon and a man of mass $M'$ starting from the upper end walks down the plank so that it does not move. Show that he gets to the other end in time \[\sqrt{\frac{2M'a}{(M+M')g\sin\alpha}}\] where $a$ is the length of the plank. [5]
  7. 4(a) Obtain the equation of motion of a rigid body about the axis of rotation. [5]
  8. 4(b) A uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $2a$ can turn freely about one fixed end. It is started with an angular velocity $\omega$ from the position in which it hangs vertically. Find its angular velocity at any instant. [5]
  9. 5(a) Find the moment of momentum of a body moving in two dimensions about the origin. [5]
  10. 5(b) Show that the length of the simple equivalent pendulum of an elliptic lamina of semi-major axis $a$ and eccentricity $e$ when the axis of oscillation is a latus rectum is $\frac{a}{2e}(1+2e^2)$. [5]
  11. 6(a) An imperfectly rough sphere moves from rest down a plane inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon. Discuss the motion. [5]
  12. 6(b) A uniform solid cylinder is placed with its axis horizontal on a plane, whose inclination to the horizon is $\alpha$. Show that the least coefficient of friction between it and the plane is $\frac13\tan\alpha$, when it may roll but not slide. [5]
  13. 1(a) Explain the terms “perfect fluid” and pressure at a point in the fluid. [1+1]
  14. 1(b) Prove that if the fluid is in equilibrium, the pressure at a point is the same in every direction. [4]
  15. 1(c) A fine circular tube in a vertical plane contains a column of liquid of density $\sigma_1$, which subtends a right angle at the centre and a column of density $\sigma_2$, subtending an angle $\phi$. Prove that the radius through the common surface makes with the vertical an angle $\tan^{-1}\frac{\sigma_1-\sigma_2+\sigma_2\cos\phi}{\sigma_1+\sigma_2\sin\phi}$. [4]
  16. 2(a) Define the surface of equi-pressure. Show that for a conservative field of forces, the surfaces of equi-pressure, equi-density and equi-potential energy coincide. [1+4]
  17. 2(b) The side $AB$ of a triangle $ABC$ is in the surface of a heavy homogeneous liquid and the points $D,E$ are taken on $AC$ such that the thrusts on the triangles $BAD$, $BDE$ and $BEC$ are equal. Show that $AD:DE:EC=1:(\sqrt2-1):(\sqrt3-\sqrt2)$. [5]
  18. 3(a) A uniform solid sphere, of radius $r_1$ and density $\sigma$ is surrounded by a concentric shell of liquid of outer radius $r_2$ and density $\rho$. The liquid is subject to an attractive force $\frac{g}{r_2\rho}\left[\frac{(\sigma-\rho)r_1^3}{r^2}+r\rho\right]$ per unit mass towards the centre, where $r$ is the distance from the centre. Assuming the outer boundary of the liquid to be free from pressure, calculate the pressure at any point of the surface of the sphere. [5]
  19. 3(b) Define centre of pressure of a given area in a fluid in equilibrium. An ellipse of eccentricity $\frac14$ is just immersed in water with its major axis vertical. Show that the centre of pressure coincides with a focus. [1+4]
