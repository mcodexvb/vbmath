CC-8

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1(a) Prove that the function $f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{xy}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}&\text{if }(x,y)\ne(0,0),\\0&\text{if }(x,y)=(0,0),\end{cases}$ is continuous at $(0,0)$. [3]
  2. 1(b) Find the directional derivative of $f(x,y)=2x^2-xy+5$ at $(1,1)$ in the direction of unit vector $\bar\beta=(\frac35,-\frac45)$. [3]
  3. 1(c) State and prove sufficient conditions for the differentiability for a function of two variables. [4]
  4. 2(a) If $f(u,v)$ is a differentiable function of two independent variables $u$ and $v$, which are also differentiable function of one independent variable $x$, then show that $f$ is a differentiable function of $x$ and $\frac{df}{dx}=\frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\frac{du}{dx}+\frac{\partial f}{\partial v}\frac{dv}{dx}$. [3]
  5. 2(b) State and prove Schwarz theorem. [1+4]
  6. 2(c) Approximate the change in the hypotenuse of a right angled triangle whose sides are 6 cm and 8 cm, when the shorter side is lengthened by $\frac14$ cm and the longer side is shortened by $\frac18$ cm. [2]
  7. 3(a) Let $z$ be a differentiable function in rectangular Cartesian coordinates $x$ and $y$. If $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y-r\sin\theta$, then prove that \[\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial r^2}+\frac1r\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}+\frac1{r^2}\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial\theta^2}=\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}.\] [4]
  8. 3(b) Let $f(0)=0$ and $f'(x)=\frac1{1+x^2}$, without using the method of integration, prove that $f(x)+f(y)=f(\frac{x+y}{1-xy})$. [3]
  9. 3(c) State and prove Euler's theorem for a function of three variables. [3]
  10. 4(a) Let $f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\frac12,&\text{if }y\text{ is rational}\\x,&\text{if }y\text{ is irrational}\end{cases}$ and $R$ is the square $[0,1;0,1]$. Evaluate $\int_0^1dy\int_0^1f(x,y)dx$ and $\int_0^1dx\int_0^1 f(x,y)dy$ if exist. [3]
  11. 4(b) Evaluate $\int_0^1dy\int_y^1e^{x^2}dx$. [3]
  12. 4(c) Using the transformation formula $x+y=u$, $y=uv$, show that $\int_0^1 dx\int_0^{1-x}e^{\frac y{x+y}}dy$ transform into $\iint_E e^{\frac y{x+y}}dxdy$, where $E$ is the triangle bounded by $x=0$, $y=0$, $x+y=1$ and whose value is $\frac{e-1}{2}$. [4]
  13. 5(a) Evaluate $\iint_E\frac{dxdy}{(1+x^2+y^2)^2}$ over $E$, which is one loop of the lemniscate $(x^2+y^2)^2-(x^2-y^2)=0$. [3]
  14. 5(b) Evaluate $\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-x^2}dx$. [4]
  15. 5(c) Evaluate $\iint_R[x+y]dxdy$ where $R$ is the rectangle $[0,2;0,1]$, $[a]$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $a$. [3]
  16. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions. [$2\times5=10$]
  17. 1(a) Let $y(x)=v_1(x)\cos(3x)+v_2(x)\sin(3x)$ be a solution to $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+9y=\frac1{\sin(3x)}$. Find the value of $v_2(x)$.
  18. 1(b) Consider the boundary value problem (BVP) $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\alpha y(x)=0$, $\alpha\in\mathbb R$, with the boundary conditions $y(0)=0$, $y(\pi)=k$ ($k$ is a non-zero real number). For $\alpha=-1$, $k>0$, the BVP has a solution $y(x)$ such that $y(x)>0$ for all $x\in(0,\pi)$ -- Justify the statement.
  19. 1(c) Given the solution $y_1(x)=(x-1)^{-1}$, find the second solution of the differential equation (DE) $(x^2-x)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+(3x-1)\frac{dy}{dx}+y=0$, $x\ne0,1$.
  20. 1(d) Use the substitution $x=\frac1t$ to show that for the DE $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\frac12(\frac1{x^2}+\frac1x)\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac1{2x^3}y=0$, the point $x=\infty$ ($t=0$) is a regular singular point.
  21. 1(e) Give geometrical interpretation of the integrable equation $P\,dx+Q\,dy+R\,dz=0$.
  22. 1(f) Consider the system of linear differential equations (Des) \[\begin{aligned}\frac{dx_1}{dt}&=5x_1-2x_2,\\\frac{dx_2}{dt}&=4x_1-x_2,\end{aligned}\] with the initial conditions $x_1(0)=0$, $x_2(0)=1$. Find the value of $\log_e|x_2(2)-x_1(2)|$.
  23. 1(g) Convert the initial value problem (IVP) \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+3\frac{dy}{dx}+2y=0,\quad y(x=0)=1,\quad\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right]_{x=0}=3\] into an IVP for a system in normal form.
  24. 1(h) Consider the ordinary differential equation (ODE) $4x\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2\frac{dy}{dx}+y=0$. Find the sum of the roots of the indicial equation of the Frobenius series solution for the above ODE in a neighbourhood of $x=0$.
  25. 2(a) Find at least the first four non-zero terms in a power series expansion about $x=0$ of a general solution to the given ODE $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-x\frac{dy}{dx}+2y=\cos x$. [6]
  26. 2(b) Solve the simultaneous equations: $\frac{dx}{\cos(x+y)}=\frac{dy}{\sin(x+y)}=\frac{dz}{z}$. [4]
  27. 3(a) Let the function $p_1(x)$ be differentiable in some interval I of $\mathbb R$. Show that the substitution $y(x)=z(x)\exp(-\frac12\int^x p_2(t)dt)$ transforms the equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+p_1(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+p_2(x)y=r(x)$ to \[\frac{d^2z}{dx^2}+\left[p_2(x)-\frac12\frac{dp_1}{dx}-\frac14p_1^2(x)\right]z=r(x)\exp\left(\frac12\int^xp_1(t)dt\right).\] [5]
  28. 3(b) Verify that the condition of integrability is satisfied by the equation $(1+yz)dx+x(z-x)dy-(1+xy)dz=0$ and hence solve the equations.
  29. 4(a) State the Picard-Lindel\"of's existence and uniqueness theorem on the solution of the IVP $\frac{dy}{dx}=f(x,y),\ y(x=x_0)=y_0$. Hence, by constructing the approximate solutions, show that $y(x)=\frac52e^{2x}-\frac32$ is a solution of the IVP $\frac{dy}{dx}=2y+3,\ y(x=0)=1$.
  30. 4(b) By matrix / vector method, find the general solution of the homogeneous system: \[\frac{dX}{dt}=\begin{bmatrix}5&-2\\4&-1\end{bmatrix}X,\quad \text{where }X=\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}.\] Comment on the particular solution if $X(t=0)=\begin{bmatrix}2\\3\end{bmatrix}$.
