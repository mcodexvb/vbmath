MMO-31(A14)

Critical Point Theory in Fluid Topology-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1 Answer any five questions:
  2. 1(a) Describe physical interpretation of a plane autonomous system.
  3. 1(b) State continuum hypothesis.
  4. 1(c) What do you mean by unsteady non-uniform flow? Comment on the existence of temporal and convective acceleration of such flow.
  5. 1(d) List the key differences between displacement thickness and momentum thickness.
  6. 1(e) Show that streamlines and potential lines are orthogonal to each other.
  7. 1(f) Define skin-friction lines. What does it physically signify?
  8. 1(g) What do you mean by node-saddle point of attachment? How does it form in the flow field?
  9. 1(h) Sketch the flow patterns on a surface of a simply connected body in case of separation with one saddle point and three nodes.
  10. 2(a) Let $\vec x=\vec x(t)$ be the response of the following linear dynamical system\par \[\dot x=\alpha x-\beta y\] \[\dot y=\beta x+\alpha y,\] that satisfies the initial condition $\vec x(0)=\vec x_0$. Determine the conditions on the real constants $\alpha$ and $\beta$ that will ensure $\lim_{t\to\infty}\vec x(t)=(0,0)$. Can $(0,0)$ be a node or saddle point? [5]
  11. 2(b) Classify (if possible) each critical point of the following plane autonomous system.\par \[x^\prime=-3x+y^2+2,\ y^\prime=x^2-y^2.\] [5]
  12. 3(a) State Reynolds transport theorem. Using this theorem, derive continuity equation for fluid motion. [2+3]
  13. 3(b) Describe the role of pressure gradient in the context of boundary layer separation on a solid wall with no slip boundary condition for a laminar flow. [5]
  14. 4(a) Show that the behaviour of the skin-friction lines in the vicinity of the critical points at $P_0(x_0,z_0)$ is governed by the nature of the solutions of a system of linear algebraic equations of the form $F\Psi=O$ where,\par \[F=\begin{pmatrix}\tau_{xx}|_{P_0}-S&\tau_{xz}|_{P_0}\\\tau_{zx}|_{P_0}&\tau_{zz}|_{P_0}-S\end{pmatrix},\ \Psi=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda\\\mu\end{pmatrix}\ \text{and}\ O=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.\] Also, classify different critical points of the skin-friction line patterns. [4+3]
  15. 4(b) The velocity components in a two-dimensional incompressible fluid are given by $u=e^x\cosh y$ and $v=-e^x\sinh x$. Determine the equation of the streamline for this flow. [3]
  16. 5(a) Describe flow topology of separation surface of detachment type and separation surface of attachment type. [5]
  17. 5(b) Derive non-dimensional form of the streamfunction-vorticity ($\psi$-$\omega$) formulation of the two-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes equations. [5]
  18. 6(a) Discuss flow topology associated with a separation with one saddle point and two foci. [7]
  19. 6(b) State Poincar\'e-Bendixson formula when flow domain with surface complexity one contains node, focus, saddle point, half-saddle and half-node as critical points. [3]
