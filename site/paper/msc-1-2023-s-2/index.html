MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2023
  1. 1(a) State and prove Morera's theorem. [1+3]
  2. 1(b) State and prove minimum modulus theorem. [1+2]
  3. 1(c) State Taylor's theorem. Using this theorem expand $f(z)=\frac1{z^2}$ in power series about $z=-1$. [1+2]
  4. 2(a) State Laurent's theorem. Apply Laurent's theorem to show that $e^{z+\frac1z}=\sum_{n=-\infty}^\infty b_nz^n$ valid in $0<|z|<\infty$, where $\pi b_n=\int_0^\pi e^{2\cos\theta}\cos n\theta\,d\theta$ and $b_{n-1}-b_{n+1}=nb_n$. [1+3]
  5. 2(b) Define zero of an analytic function. If $f(z)$ be a non-constant analytic function regular in a region $R$, then prove that $f(z)$ have at most a finite number of zeros in every closed sub region of $R$. [1+2]
  6. 2(c) State and prove Schwarz's lemma. [3]
  7. 3(a) State and prove Casorati-Weierstrass theorem. [1+3]
  8. 3(b) Show that if $f(z)=\frac{\phi(z)}{(z-\alpha)^n}$, where $\phi(z)$ is analytic at $\alpha$ and $\phi(\alpha)\ne0$ then $\alpha$ is a pole of $f(z)$ of order $n$ and conversely. [2+2]
  9. 3(c) If $z=\alpha$ is a pole of $f(z)$ of order $n$ then show that $\frac1{f(z)}$ has a zero of order $n$ at $z=\alpha$. [2]
  10. 4(a) Find the singularities of the function $f(z)=\cot(\frac1z)$ and determine the nature of the singularities. [3]
  11. 4(b) Establish the formula \[ Z-P=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_C\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}dz \] where the symbols used have their usual meanings. [4]
  12. 4(c) Prove that limit point of zeros of a non-constant analytic function is an essential singularity. [3]
  13. 5(a) Find the singularities of the function $f(z)=\sin\frac1{1-z}+\sin\frac1{1+z}$. [3]
  14. 5(b) State Cauchy's residues theorem. Using this theorem evaluate $\displaystyle\int_C\frac{e^{2z}}{(z+2)^3}dz$, where $C$ is the circle $|z-1|=2$. [1+2]
  15. 5(c) State Rouche's theorem. Show that the roots of the equation $16z^5-z+8=0$ all lie in the annulus between $|z|=\frac12$ and $|z|=1$. [1+3]
  16. 6(a) Define residue of a function at the point at infinity. Find $\mathit{Res}[f(z),\infty]$, where $f(z)=\frac{z}{z^2+1}$. [1+2]
  17. 6(b) If $z=\infty$ is a pole of order $m$ for an analytic function $f(z)$ then prove that $f(z)$ is a polynomial of degree $m$. [2]
  18. 6(c) Evaluate any one of the following by the method of contour integration: \begin{romanparts} \item $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\frac{dx}{x^2a^2+1}$, $a<0$; \item $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{x\sin x\,dx}{x^2+b^2}$, $b>0$. \end{romanparts} \begingroup\renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of values as indicated in the margin.} [5]
