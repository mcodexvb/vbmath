BMC-54

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2017
  1. 1(a) State and prove the Parallel Axis Theorem. Use this result to find the moment of inertia of a circular arc about a tangent line. [5+1]
  2. 1(b) Find the moment of inertia of an elliptic area about a line $CP$ inclined at an angle $\theta$ to the major axis, and about a tangent parallel to $CP$. [4]
  3. 2(a) Define the principal axes of a rigid body. Show that, for a plane lamina, the principal axes at a point $O$ are a normal $Oz$ to its plane and two lines $Ox',Oy'$ incline at an angle $\theta$ respectively to mutually perpendicular axes $Ox$ and $Oy$ given by $\tan2\theta=\frac{2F}{B-A}$. [1+4]
  4. 2(b) If $A$ and $B$ be the moments of inertia of a uniform lamina about two perpendicular axes $Ox$ and $Oy$, lying in its plane, and $F$ be the product of inertia of the lamina about these lines, show that the principal moments at $O$ are equal to $\frac12\left[(A+B)\pm\sqrt{(A-B)^2+4F^2}\right]$. [5]
  5. 3(a) Prove that the centre of mass of a rigid body moves as if the total mass and the resultant external forces were applied at this point. [5]
  6. 3(b) A plank, of Mass $M$, is initially at rest along a line of greatest slope of a smooth plane inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon, and a man, of weight $M'$, starting from the upper end walks down the plank so that it does not move; show that he gets to the other end in time $\sqrt{\frac{2M'a}{(M+M')g\sin\alpha}}$, where $a$ is the length of the plank. [5]
  7. 4(a) Show that, for motion about a fixed axis $AB$, the angular momentum of rotation of a rigid body is given by $\vec\Omega=I_{AB}\vec\omega$, and the total external moment of the force on the body is, $\vec\Lambda=I_{AB}\frac{d\vec\omega}{dt}$, where $\vec\omega$ is the angular velocity and $I_{AB}$ is the moment of inertia of the body about the axis $AB$. [5]
  8. 4(b) A uniform rod, of mass $m$ and length $2a$, can turn freely about one end which is fixed; it is started with angular velocity $\vec\omega$ from the position in which it hangs vertically; find the motion. [5]
  9. 5(a) Define a compound pendulum. Deduce its equation of motion for small vibrations and hence obtain an expression for the length of the simple equivalent pendulum. [6]
  10. 5(b) Find the time of oscillation of a compound pendulum consisting of a rod, of mass $m$ and length $a$, carrying at one end a sphere of mass $m_1$ and radius $b$, the other end being fixed. [4]
  11. 6(a) A uniform solid circular cylinder is placed with its axis horizontal on a plane, whose inclination to the horizon is $\alpha$. Show that the least coefficient of friction $\mu$ between it and the plane, so that it may roil and not slide, is $\frac13\tan\alpha$. [7]
  12. 6(b) Find an expression for the total kinetic energy of motion for the above problem and show that it equals the work done by gravity. [3]
