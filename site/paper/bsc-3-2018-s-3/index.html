CC-7

Differential Equations-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1 Answer \textbf{\textit{any ten}} questions: \hfill \(10\times2=20\) \begin{romanparts} \item Let \(y(x)\) be the solution of the differential equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=(y-1)(y-5)(y-7)\) satisfying the initial condition \(y(0)=6\). Find the value of \(y(x)\) when \(x\to\alpha\). \item If an integral curve of the differential equation \((y-x)\dfrac{dy}{dx}=1\) passes through the points \((0,0)\) and \((\alpha,1)\), then find the value of \(\alpha\). \item Find the differential equation of all parabolas having their axes parallel to the \(y\)-axis. \item Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves \(y=cx^3\), \(c\) being a parameter. \item Let \(y(x)\) be the solution of the differential equation \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(x\dfrac{dy}{dx})=x,\ y(1)=0,\ y'(1)=0\). What is the value of \(y(5)\)? \item Let \(g(x,y)\,dx+(x+y)\,dy=0\) is an exact differential equation where \(g(x,0)=x^2\). Find the general solution of the above differential equation. \item Consider the differential equation \(x^2y''-x(x+2)y'+(x+2)y=x^3\). If \(y=x\) is a linearly independent solution of the above differential equation, then find a second linearly independent solution. \item If \(y(t)\) is a solution of the differential equation \(y''+4y=2e^t\), then evaluate \(\lim_{t\to\infty}e^{-t}y(t)\). \item Find an integrating factor of the differential equation \(\sqrt{x}\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2x\dfrac{dy}{dx}+3y=0\). \item Solve: \(\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^3-(x^2+xy+y^2)\dfrac{dy}{dx}+x^2y+xy^2=0\). \item A body whose temperature is initially \(100^\circ c\) is allowed to cool in air, whose temperature remains at a constant temperature \(20^\circ c\). It is given that after 10 minutes, the body has cooled to \(80^\circ c\). Find the temperature of the body after half an hour. \item Let \(y_1(x)\) and \(y_2(x)\) be two solutions of \((1-x^2)y''-2xy'+(\sec x)y=0\) with Wronskian \(W(x)\). If \(y_1(0)=1,\ y_1'(0)=0\) and \(W(\frac12)=\frac15\), then find the value of \(y_2'(0)\). \item Find four different solutions of the initial value problem. \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=xy^{1/2},\ y(0)=0\). \end{romanparts}
  2. 2(a) Solve the differential equation \[ \frac{dy}{dx}+y=g(x),\quad y(0)=2,\quad 0\le x<\infty\quad \text{where }g(x)=\begin{cases}3,&0\le x<\pi/2\\\cos x,&x\ge\pi/2.\end{cases} \] \hfill 5
  3. 2(b) Prove that the transformation \(y=f+\frac1v\) reduces the differential equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=A(x)y^2+B(x)y+C(x)\) to a linear equation in \(v\), where \(f\) is any solution of the above differential equation. Hence solve the equation \[ \frac{dy}{dx}=-8xy^2+4x(4x+1)y-(8x^3+4x^2-1); \quad\text{given that }f(x)=x. \] \hfill \(2+3\)
  4. 3(a) Find a family of oblique trajectories that intersect the family of parabolas \(y^2=cx\) at angle \(60^\circ\). \hfill 4
  5. 3(b) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves \(r^n=a^n\cos n\theta\), \(a\) being a parameter. \hfill 2
  6. 3(c) Solve: \(\left(y+\dfrac{y^3}{3}+\dfrac{x^2}{2}\right)dx+\dfrac14(x+xy^2)\,dy=0\). \hfill 4
  7. 4(a) Solve: \(\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^3-4xy\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)+8y^2=0\). \hfill 4
  8. 4(b) If \(x^my^n\) is an integrating factor of the differential equation \((x^7y^2+3y)\,dx+(3x^8y-x)\,dy=0\), then find the value of \(m\) and \(n\). \hfill 2
  9. 4(c) Solve, by the method of variation of parameters: \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2\dfrac{dy}{dx}+y=\dfrac{1}{x^2e^x}\). \hfill 4
  10. 5(a) Examine for singular solution and extraneous loci, if any, of the differential equation \(\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2(2-3y)^2=4(1-y)\). \hfill 5
  11. 5(b) Solve: \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}-y=x\sin x+(1+x^2)e^x\). \hfill 4
  12. 5(c) What is difference between the function \(y=\frac1x\) and solution of the differential equation \(xy'+y=0,\ y(1)=1\). \hfill 1
  13. 6(a) Reduce the differential equation \[ \left(\frac{dy}{dx}x^2+y^2\right)\left(x\frac{dy}{dx}+y\right) =\left(\frac{dy}{dx}+1\right)^2 \] to Clairaut's form by using the substitutions \(u=xy\) and \(v=x+y\) and hence find its complete primitive. \hfill 5
  14. 6(b) Solve the equation \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\dfrac{dy}{dx}-6y=10e^{2x}-18e^{3x}-6x-11,\) by the method of undetermined coefficients. \hfill 5
  15. 7(a) Verify that the differential equation \((1+x+x^2)\dfrac{d^3y}{dx^3}+(3+6x)\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+6\dfrac{dy}{dx}=0\) is exact. \hfill 2
  16. 7(b) Solve: \(y^3\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}=5\). \hfill 3
  17. 7(c) Solve: \(x^3\dfrac{d^3y}{dx^3}-4x^2\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+8x\dfrac{dy}{dx}-8y=0\). \hfill 2
  18. 7(d) If \(e^{\int\phi(x/y)\,d(x/y)}\) is an integrating factor of the differential equation \[M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0,\] then find the function \(\phi\left(x/y\right)\). \hfill 3 \def\paperfull{20}
