MME-31

Pure Stream
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1(a) Show that every field $F$ is either an extension of $\mathbb Q$ or an extension of $\mathbb Z_p$, for some prime $p$. [4]
  2. 1(b) Let $F/K$ be a finite field extension. Show that every element of $F$ is algebraic over $K$. Does the converse hold in general? Justify your answer. [3+1]
  3. 1(c) Show that an extension $F/K$ is algebraic if and only if every subring $R$ with $K\subset R\subset F$ is a field. [2]
  4. 2(a) Prove that every nonconstant polynomial $f(x)$ over a field $K$ has a splitting field over $K$. [4]
  5. 2(b) Find a necessary and sufficient condition on $a$ and $b$ so that the splitting field of the cubic $x^3+ax+b$ has degree 3 over $\mathbb Q$. [4]
  6. 2(c) Show by an example that product of two normal extensions is not normal in general. [2]
  7. 3(a) Show that every polynomial over a field of characteristic zero is separable. Give an example of a polynomial, which is not separable. [3+2]
  8. 3(b) Suppose $F/K$ be a finite extension and $F$ is a perfect field. Then show that $K$ is perfect. Does the statement hold for an infinite extension? Justify. [3+2]
  9. 4(a) If $F$ is a finite field of $p^n$ elements, where $p$ is a prime and $n\in\mathbb N$, then show that $[F:\mathbb Z_p]=n$. [3]
  10. 4(b) Let $F$ be a field such that $F\setminus\{0\}$ is cyclic. Show that $F$ is finite. [3]
  11. 4(c) Find the number of proper subfields of the field of order $2^{100}$. [2]
  12. 4(d) Consider the field $\mathbb F=\mathbb Z[i]/\langle2+i\rangle$. Does there exist an element $c\in\mathbb F$ such that $\mathbb F=\mathbb Z_p(c)$? [2]
  13. 5(a) Describe the Galois group of the field extension $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ of $\mathbb Q$. Hence find the fixed fields of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$. [3+2]
  14. 5(b) Let $F/K$ be a Galois extension, and $L$ be a intermediate field of $F/K$. Then show that $L$ is a normal extension of $K$ if and only if $G(F/L)$ is a normal subgroup of $G(F/K)$. \par\hfill P.T.O. [5]
  15. 6(a) Suppose that $a$ is a root of the polynomial $x^3+x+1$ in some extension of $\mathbb Z_2$. Express $a^{100}$ in the form $c_2a^2+c_1a+c_0$. [2]
  16. 6(b) Give an example of a polynomial of degree $n$ over a field $F$ having a splitting field of degree $n!$. [2]
  17. 6(c) Find a polynomial $P(x)\in\mathbb Q[x]$ such that $\mathbb Q\left(\sqrt{1+\sqrt5}\right)$ is ring isomorphic to $\mathbb Q[x]/\langle P(x)\rangle$. [2]
  18. 6(d) Let $K$ be a field. Then prove that there exist an algebraic field extension $F/K$ such that $F$ is algebraically closed. [4]
