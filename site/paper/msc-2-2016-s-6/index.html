MMC-26

Numerical Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2016
  1. 1(a) Define a spline function. When does it call a natural spline? Carry out an error analysis in cubic spline interpolation for the function $f(x)$ whose tabular values are known at $(n+1)$ distinct equispaced points. [6]
  2. 1(b) Given a set of $(n+1)$ data points $(x_i,y_i)$, $i=0(1)n$ with $x_i\neq x_j$ for $i\neq j$, show that the Newton's divided difference interpolation polynomial $p_n(x)$ may be expressed in the form\\$p_n(x)=p_{n-1}(x)+(x-x_0)(x-x_1)\ldots\ldots(x-x_{n-1})f(x_0,x_1,\ldots\ldots,x_n)$\\where $p_{n-1}(x)$ interpolates at $(x_i,y_i)$, $i=0(1)n-1$. [4]
  3. 2(a) Using Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process, find the least squares polynomial approximation of degree $n$ for the function $f(x)$ having $(n+1)$ tabular values corresponding to distinct argument values with a non-negative weight function $w(x)$. [6]
  4. 2(b) Approximate $e^x$ on $[0,1]$ by taking first two terms of the Taylor's series expansion of $e^x$ about $x=a$. How should `a' be chosen to minimize the error in maximum norm? [4]
  5. 3(a) Derive an expression for the error in Newton-Cotes quadrature formula to find $\displaystyle\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$ by taking odd number of ordinates. [6]
  6. 3(b) Define the degree of precision of a numerical integration formula. State and prove the necessary and sufficient condition for the degree of precision of Gauss quadrature formula to be $(2n+1)$. [4]
  7. 4(a) Briefly describe Jacobi's method for computing all the eigen pairs of a real symmetric numerical matrix of order $n$. [6]
  8. 4(b) Does the LU decomposition method fail to solve the following system of equations?\\$x+y-z=2$\\$2x+2y+5z=-3$\\$3x+2y-3z=6$\\If yes, then how do you obtain its LU decomposition? [4]
  9. 5(a) Carry out an error analysis to discuss the convergence of a single step method used to solve an well-posed initial value problem $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=f(x,y)$, $y(a)=y_0$ in a finite interval $[a,b]$. [6]
  10. 5(b) Make a comparative statement regarding the criterion of accuracy of both the Adams' and Milne's predictor-corrector method right from their respective formulae. Comment also on the stability of the methods. [4]
  11. 6(a) Briefly describe an explicit finite difference scheme for solving the problem\\$k\dfrac{\partial^2f}{\partial x^2}=\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial t}$, $t>0$, $0<x<l$, $k>0$\\subject to the boundary conditions $f(0,t)=0=f(l,t)$, $t>0$ and the initial condition $f(x,0)=F(x)$, $0\leq x\leq l\ (<\infty)$. [6]
  12. 6(b) Discuss the shooting method for solving the boundary value problem\\$-u''+p(x)u'+q(x)u(x)=r(x),\ a<x<b$\\subject to the conditions $u(a)=\gamma$, $u(b)=\gamma_2$ where $p(x)$, $q(x)$ and $r(x)$ are assumed to be continuous in $[a,b]$ and $\gamma_1,\gamma_2$ are non-zero constants. [4]
