MME-41

Pure Stream
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2023
  1. 1(a) Show that every nonconstant polynomial over a field of characteristic 0 is separable. [[3]]
  2. 1(b) Give an example of a inseparable polynomial over a field with finite characteristic. Give reasons to justify your answer. [[2]]
  3. 1(c) Let $F/K$ be a finite field extension of characteristic $p>0$ such that $p\nmid[F:K]$. Prove that $F/K$ is not separable. Give an example of such extension with proper justification. [[3+2]]
  4. 2(a) Find the Galois group of the polynomial $(x^2-2)(x^2-3)$ over $\mathbb Q$. Which group is isomorphic to this group upto isomorphism? Give reasons to justify your answer. [[4+2]]
  5. 2(b) Find the generator of the Galois group $G(F_{p^3}/F_p)$. [[4]]
  6. 3(a) Prove that a finite extension $F/K$ is Galois extension if and only if $G(F/K)=[F:K]$. [[4]]
  7. 3(b) Let $K$ be a field and $n$ be a positive integer. Suppose that characteristic $K\nmid n$. Let $R$ be set of all $n$-th roots of unity in the splitting Field $F$ of $x^n-1$ over $K$. Then show that $\xi\in F$ is a primitive $n$-th root of unity if and only if $R=\langle\xi\rangle$. [[3+3]]
  8. 4(a) Let $X$ be a topological space and let $f,g,h$ be three paths in $X$ from $x_1$ to $x_2$, from $x_2$ to $x_3$, from $x_3$ to $x_4$ respectively. Prove that $[f]*([g]*[h])=([f]*[g])*[h]$. [[5]]
  9. 4(b) What is a contractible space? Explain with examples. Examine if the disc $\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2\le4\}$ is a contractible space. Give an example of a non-contractible space. [[1+1+2+1]]
  10. 5(a) What do you mean by a simply connected space? Show that a path connected space $X$ is simply connected if and only if any two paths in $X$ with the same initial and final points are path homotopic. [[1+5]]
  11. 5(b) Let $X=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:(x-1)^2+(y-2)^2=4\}$ and let $Y=X\setminus\{1,4\}$. Compute $\pi_1(Y,(3,2))$ and $\pi_1(Y,(-1,2))$. [[2+2]]
  12. 6(a) Let $X$ and $Y$ be two topological spaces. Show that a covering map $p:X\longrightarrow Y$ is an open map. Is the converse true? Justify your answer. [[3+2]]
  13. 6(b) Explain the path lifting property for a covering space. Prove that the fundamental group of $S^1$ is $(\mathbb Z,+)$. [[1+4]]
