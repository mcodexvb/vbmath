BMC-32

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2016
  1. 1 Answer \textit{any three} questions: [3x5=15]
  2. 1(a) (i) Let $G$ be a semigroup such that for every $x\in G$ there exists unique $y$ in $G$ for which $xyx=x$. Show that $G$ is a group. [3]
  3. 1(ii) Give an example to show that order of product of two elements is infinite where orders of the elements are finite. [2]
  4. 1(b) (i) Let $G$ be a group. Show that if $G$ has finitely many subgroups, then $G$ is finite. [3]
  5. 1(ii) Give an example of a group $G$ and a non empty subset $H$ such that $HH\subseteq H$, but $H$ is not a subgroup of $G$. [2]
  6. 1(c) (i) Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group of order $n$. Then show that there is unique subgroup of order $m$, for each divisor $m$ of $n$. [3]
  7. 1(ii) Find number of elements of order 5 in $\mathbb Z_{1000}$. [2]
  8. 1(d) (i) Let $G$ be a group of order 15 and A and B be subgroups of $G$ of order 5 and 3, respectively. Show that $G=AB$. [2]
  9. 1(ii) In the multiplicative group $C^*$, describe geometrically the coset $(2+3i)H$ of the subgroup $H=\{z\in c^*:|z|=1\}$. [2]
  10. 1(iii) Let $G=\langle a\rangle$ be a cyclic group of order 35. Find $[G:\langle a^7\rangle]$. [1]
  11. 1(e) (i) Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$ such that for all $x,y\in G\setminus H$, $xy\in H$. Show that $G$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. [2]
  12. 1(ii) State and prove Lagrange's theorem. [1+2]
  13. 2 Answer \textit{any two} questions: [2x5=10]
  14. 2(a) Prove that a ring $\mathfrak R$ is commutative if an only if $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$ for all $a,b\in\mathfrak R$. Give an example of ring that contains elements $a,b$ such that $(a-b)(a+b)\neq a^2-b^2$. [3+2]
  15. 2(b) For any positive integer $n$, show that $(\mathbb Z_n,+_n,\bullet_n)$ is an integral domain if and only if $n$ is prime. Give an example of a ring that contains neither zero divisor nor unit. [4+1]
  16. 2(c) Show that the non-zero elements of a skewfield forms a group with respect to multiplication. Give an example of a skewfield which is not integral domain. [4+1]
  17. 3 Answer \textit{any three} questions: [3x5=15]
  18. 3(a) (i) Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space. Suppose that $S$ is a linearly independent set of vectors in $V$. Prove that $S$ can be extended to a basis of $V$. [3]
  19. 3(ii) Let $\mathbb R_3[x]$ be the set of real polynomials of degree atmost 2. Show that $\{(x-1)(x-2),(x-2)(x-3),(x-3)(x-1)\}$ is a basis of $\mathbb R_3[x]$. [2]
  20. 3(b) (i) Let M be a $i\times j$ matrix. Prove that Row rank M + nullity M $=j$. [3]
  21. 3(ii) Find the dimension of the space $\{(x_1,x_2,---,x_n)\in\mathbb R^n:x_1+2x_2+3x_2+---+nx_n=0\}$ [2]
  22. 3(c) (i) State and prove the replacement theorem for a finite dimensional vector space. [3]
  23. 3(ii) Let A be an $n\times n$ matrix with zero determinant. Prove that, the row vectors of A are linearly independent. [2]
  24. 3(d) (i) Find the dimension of the smallest subspace of $\mathbb R^4$, containing the vectors $(-3,2,1,4),(4,1,0,2)$ \& $(-10,3,2,6)$. [3]
  25. 3(ii) Let $\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$ be a basis of $\mathbb R^3$. Prove that no nonzero vector is perpendicular to each $v_1,v_2,v_3$. [2]
  26. 3(e) Let $A=(a_{ij})_{n\times n}$ be an $n\times n$ matrix. Define trace. $(A)=\sum_{i=1}^na_{ij}$. \\Let $H=\{A\in M_n(\mathbb R):\mathrm{trace}(A)=0\}$. Prove that $H$ is a subspace of $M_n(\mathbb R)$ and find the dimension of $H$. [3]
