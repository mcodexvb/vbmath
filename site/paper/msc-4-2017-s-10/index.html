MMO-41

Advanced Functional Analysis-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2017
  1. 1(a) Prove that every closed convex subset of a Hilbert space $H$ has a unique member of smallest norm. [4]
  2. 1(b) Show that a Hilbert space $H$ contains a complete orthonormal sequence iff $H$ is separable. [4]
  3. 1(c) Let $M\subset H$ where $H$ is a Hilbert space. Prove that $M^{\perp}$ (orthogonal complement of $M$) is a closed subspace of $H$. [2]
  4. 2(a) Let $f$ be a sesquilinear form defined on a Hilbert space and $\hat f$ be the associated quadratic form of $f$. Prove that $f$ is bounded iff $\hat f$ is bounded and moreover $\|\hat f\|\le\|f\|\le2\|\hat f\|$. [3]
  5. 2(b) Prove that for every bounded linear operator $T:H\to H$ where $H$ is a Hilbert space its adjoint operator $T^*$ exists satisfying $\|T\|=\|T^*\|$. [4]
  6. 2(c) Show that the collection of all self-adjoint operators defined on a real Hilbert space $H$ form a closed subspace of $B(H,H)$ where $B(H,H)$ is the set of all bounded linear operators defined on $H$. [3]
  7. 3(a) If $N_1$ and $N_2$ are normal operators defined on a Hilbert space $H$ such that either commutes with the adjoint of the other. Prove that $N_1+N_2$ and $N_1N_2$ are normal operators on $H$. [4]
  8. 3(b) Let $T:H\to H$ be a self-adjoint operator where $H$ is a Hilbert space. Show that \[\|T\|=\sup\{|\langle Tx,x\rangle|:\|x\|=1\}.\] [4]
  9. 3(c) Prove that every self-adjoint operator is normal but converse may not be true. [2]
  10. 4(a) Show that the spectrum $\sigma(T)$ of a bounded linear operator $T$ on a complex Banach space $X$ is compact (assume $\sigma(T)\neq\phi$). [5]
  11. 4(b) Prove that residual spectrum $\sigma_r(T)$ of a bounded self-adjoint linear operator $T:H\to H$ (where $H$ is a complex Hilbert space) is empty. [3]
  12. 4(c) Let $T\in B(H,H)$ where $H$ is a Hilbert space. Then prove that $\sigma(T^*)=\{k\in C:\bar k\in\sigma(T)\}$, symbols bear their usual meaning. [2]
  13. 5(a) Let $A$ be a complex Banach algebra with identity. Show that for any $x\in A$, the spectrum $\sigma(x)\neq\varphi$. [6]
  14. 5(b) Define a total orthonormal set in a Hilbert space. Establish the Bessel's inequality for an orthonormal set of vectors in a Hilbert space. \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}( 2 )\end{center} [1+3]
  15. 6(a) Prove that spectral radius $r_\sigma(T)$ of a bounded linear operator $T$ defined on a complex Banach space can be expressed as \[r_\sigma(T)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{\|T^n\|}.\] [5]
  16. 6(b) Let $P_1$ and $P_2$ be two projection operators on a Hilbert space $H$. Prove that $P_1+P_2$ is a projection operator on $H$ iff $P_1(H)$ and $P_2(H)$ are orthogonal. [3]
  17. 6(c) Show that every unitary operator on a Hilbert space is isometric but not conversely. [2]
