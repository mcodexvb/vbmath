MJMA-9

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2026
  1. 1 Answer \textit{any five} questions. [$5\times2=10$]
  2. 1(a) Form the partial differential equation (PDE) by eliminating arbitrary function $\phi$ from the following equation: $\alpha x+\beta y-\gamma u=\phi(x^2+y^2-u^2)$.
  3. 1(b) Classify linear and semilinear types of first-order PDEs of the form $f\left(x,y,u,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)=0,\ u=u(x,y)$.
  4. 1(c) Find the general integral of the following PDE: $2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+3\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=5$.
  5. 1(d) Explain briefly the relationship between the surface represented by $P(x,y,u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+Q(x,y,u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=R(x,y,u)$ and $P(x,y,u)dx+Q(x,y,u)dy+R(x,y,u)du=0$.
  6. 1(e) Find the family of surfaces orthogonal to the family of surfaces whose PDE is $y\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+x\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=2u$.
  7. 1(f) What do you mean by a singular integral as applied to the solution of a first-order PDE $f\left(x,y,u,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)=0,\ u=u(x,y)$? Explain in your own way.
  8. 1(g) Find the domain of definition where the PDEs $u^2=xy\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$ and $x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$ are compatible.
  9. 1(h) Find the particular integral of the PDE $\frac{\partial^2y}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}-4\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=\sin(2x+y)$.
  10. 2(a) Solve the following PDE by Lagrange's method: $x^2(y-u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y^2(u-x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=u^2(x-y)$. [5]
  11. 2(b) Find the value of $u(2025,2026)$, where $u(x,y)$, $(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2$, be a solution of the Cauchy value problem $2025\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+2026\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=u,\ u(x,0)=\cos x$. [4]
  12. 2(c) Find the complete integral of the following PDE using Charpit's method: $u\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$.
  13. 3(a) Prove that the PDEs $x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$ and $u\left(x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)=2xy$ are compatible and hence solve them. [2+4]
  14. 3(b) Prove that $F(x,y,u,c_1,c_2)\equiv c_1x+c_2y+c_1c_2-u=0$, is the complete integral of the PDE $f\left(x,y,u,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)\equiv x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}-u=0.$ Hence, find the followings: (i) Singular integral, if any, (ii) General integral for $c_1=c_2$, and (iii) Particular integral subject to the conditions $u(1,1)=5$ and $u(1,2)=8$. [2+3+2+2]
  15. 4(a) What value of $c$ makes the following linear PDE with constant coefficients factorizable or reducible? $2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}-3\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}+17\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-7\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+cu=0$ [3]
  16. 4(b) Solve: $4\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}-2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=\sin(x+y)$. [7]
  17. 4(c) Find an interal surface satisfying $\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=6y$ containing the two lines, $y=0=u$, $y=2=u$. [5]
  18. 1 State the theorem of perpendicular axes. Use it to find the M.I. of a circular disc about a line perpendicular to its plane and passing through the centre. [3+7]
  19. 2(a) Find the M.I. of a body about any line having d.cs. $(l,m,n)$. [5]
  20. 2(b) A uniform lamina is bounded by a parabolic arc of latus rectum $4a$ and a double ordinate at a distance $b$ from the vertex. If $b=\frac a3(7+4\sqrt7)$, then show that two of the principal axes at the end of the latus rectum are the tangent and the normal there. [5]
  21. 3(a) Derive the general equations of motion from D'Alembert's principle. [5]
  22. 3(b) A thin rod of length $2a$ revolves with uniform angular velocity $\omega$ about a vertical axis through a small joint at one extremity of the rod so that it describes a cone of semi-vertical angle $\alpha$. Prove that $\omega^2=\frac{3g}{4a\cos\alpha}$. [5]
  23. 4(a) Obtain the equation of motion of a rigid body rotating about a fixed axis. [5]
  24. 4(b) Two unequal masses $M$ and $M'$ rest on two rough planes and the planes are inclined at angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ to the horizon. They are connected by a fine string passing over a small pulley, of mass $m$ and radius $a$. The pulley is placed at the common vertex of the two planes. Show that the acceleration of either mass is $g[M(\sin\alpha-\mu\cos\alpha)-M'(\sin\beta+\mu'\cos\beta)]/(M+M'+m\frac{k^2}{a^2})$, where $\mu,\mu'$ are the coefficients of friction and $k$ is the radius of gyration of the pulley about its axis, and $M$ is the mass which moves downwards. [5]
  25. 5(a) Define compound pendulum. Find the length of simple equivalent pendulum. [2+3]
  26. 5(b) A solid homogeneous cone, of height $h$ and vertical angle $2\alpha$, oscillates about a horizontal axis through its vertex. Show that the length of the simple equivalent pendulum is $\frac h5(4+\tan^2\alpha)$. [5]
  27. 6(a) Prove that the moment of momentum of a rigid body about the origin is $Mvp+Mk^2\dot\theta$. [5]
  28. 6(b) A uniform sphere of radius $a$ rolls down an inclined plane, rough enough to prevent any sliding. Discuss the motion and show that for pure rolling, $\mu>\frac27\tan\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is the inclination of the plane to the horizon and $\mu$ is the coefficient of friction. \fbox{\parbox{3.2cm}{\small Use separate answer script for each unit}} [5]
