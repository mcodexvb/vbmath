MMC-22

Topology
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1(a) Prove Schr\"oder-Bernstein theorem for cardinalities. [4]
  2. 1(b) For cardinal numbers $u$ and $v$ show that $2^{u+v}=2^u.2^v$. [3]
  3. 1(c) State and prove Axiom of choice using Zorn's lemma. [3]
  4. 2(a) State and prove the necessary and sufficient conditions for a collection $\mathcal B$ to be a basis for a topology. [4]
  5. 2(b) Give an example of a topology on a 3-element set that is neither discrete nor indiscrete, and justify. [3]
  6. 2(c) Define $T_2$ and $T_1$ spaces. Prove that every $T_2$ space is $T_1$, but converse is not true. [3]
  7. 3(a) Give an example with justification of an open map that is not continuous. [3]
  8. 3(b) Prove that a map $f$ is closed if and only if $\overline{f(A)}\subseteq f(\overline A)$ for any $A$. [4]
  9. 3(c) Show that $A^o\cup B^o\subset(A\cup B)^o$ and give an example to show that the inclusion can be strict. [3]
  10. 4(a) Show that every compact subspace of a $T_2$ space is closed and show, by example, that this may fail if the space is not $T_2$. [3+3]
  11. 4(b) Show that a space is regular if and only if $\forall x\in X$ and $\forall$ open set $V\subset X$ containing $x$, $\exists$ an open set $V_x$ containing $x$ such that $\overline{V_x}\subset V$. [4]
  12. 5(a) When is a topological space called metrizable ? Give an example, with justification, of a topological space that is not metrizable. [1+3]
  13. 5(b) Show that every subspace of a $T_3$ space is $T_3$. [3]
  14. 5(c) Show that every metric space is normal. [3]
  15. 6(a) Show that the union of any family of connected sets with nonempty intersection is connected. [3]
  16. 6(b) Define component of a topological space, prove that every component of a topological space is closed. [3]
  17. 6(c) Examine, with justification, whether the following spaces are connected:\par 1. $\mathbf R$ with the usual topology,\par 2. $\mathbf R$ with the lower limit topology,\par 3. $\mathbf R$ with the co-countable topology,\par 4. $\mathbf R$ with the discrete topology. [4]
