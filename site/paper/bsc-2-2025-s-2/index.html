MJMA-4

Differential Equations-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{ten} questions from the following. [$10\times2=20$]
  2. 1(a) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of circles $x^2+y^2=c^2$. [2]
  3. 1(b) Find the non-zero value of $n$ for which the differential equation $\left(\frac{ny}{x^3}+\frac y{x^2}\right)dx+\left(\frac1{x^2}-\frac1x\right)dy=0$ is an exact differential equation. [2]
  4. 1(c) Solve the differential equation $(x^3+y^3)dx+3xy^2dy=0$. [2]
  5. 1(d) Solve the nonlinear differential equation $\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2+\frac{dy}{dx}-12=0$. [2]
  6. 1(e) Solve: $(D+1)(D+2)(D^2+4)^4(D^2+1)^2y=0$. [2]
  7. 1(f) Solve the differential equation $\frac{d^4y}{dx^4}=x^2+e^{2x}$. [2]
  8. 1(g) Find a second order linear homogeneous differential equation whose linearly independent solutions are $x$ and $x^2$. [2]
  9. 1(h) Solve the linear equation $x\frac{dy}{dx}-4y=x^5e^x$. [2]
  10. 1(i) Solve the boundary value problem $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+4y=0,\ y(0)=0,y(\frac\pi8)=1$. [2]
  11. 1(j) Find two different solutions of the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}+|y|=2$. [2]
  12. 1(k) Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=e^{2y}$. [2]
  13. 1(l) Show that, in an equation of the form $yf(xy)dx+xg(xy)dy=0$, the variables can be separated by the substitution $xy=v$. [2]
  14. 1(m) Find the Wronskian of the functions $e^x$, $e^{2x}$ and $e^{3x}$. [2]
  15. 1(n) Solve the differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2-1=0$. [2]
  16. 1(o) Solve: $x\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\frac{dy}{dx}=7$. [2]
  17. 2 Solve the homogeneous equation $(x^2-y^2)dx+2xydy=0$. [6]
  18. 3 If $e^{\int\phi(y)dy}$ is an integrating factor of the differential equation $M(x,y)dx+N(x,y)dy=0$, then find the expression of $\phi(y)$. Hence solve the equation $ydx+2xdy=0$. [4+2]
  19. 4 Find the differential equation of the family of circles whose centers lie on the $y$-axis. [6]
  20. 5 Solve the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}+y=f(x)$ with initial condition $y(0)=6$, where $f(x)=5$, when $0\le x<10$ and $f(x)=1$, when $x\ge10$. Hence find the value of $y(15)$. [5+1]
  21. 6 Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves $r(1+\cos\theta)=2c$, $c$ being the parameter. [6]
  22. 7 Find a family of oblique trajectories that intersect the family of hyperbolas $xy=c$ at angle $45^\circ$, $c$ being the parameter. [6]
  23. 8 Solve the nonlinear differential equation $y+px=x^4p^2$. [6]
  24. 9 Reduce the equation $x^2p^2+y(2x+y)p+y^2=0$ to Clairaut's form by the substitution $y=u$ and $xy=v$. Hence solve the equation. [5+1]
  25. 10 Solve the differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+3\frac{dy}{dx}+2y=e^{e^x}$. [6]
  26. 11 Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-6\frac{dy}{dx}+8y=e^{2x}+\sin3x$. [6]
  27. 12 Solve the Cauchy-Euler equation $x^2y''+3xy'+y=x+\frac1x,\ x>0$. [6]
  28. 13 Find an integrating factor and solve the differential equation $(2ydx+3xdy)+2xy(3ydx+4xdy)=0$. [3+3]
  29. 14 Using the method of variation of parameters, solve the equation $x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-x\frac{dy}{dx}-3y=x^2\log x$, where $x^3$ and $\frac1x$ are two linearly independent solutions of the corresponding homogeneous equation. [6]
  30. 15 Suppose $n\ne0,1$. Show that the transformation $v=y^{1-n}$ reduces the Bernoulli equation to a linear equation in $v$. Hence solve the equation $\frac{dy}{dx}+y=xy^3$. [3+3]
  31. 16 Show that if $f$ is any solution of the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=A(x)y^2+B(x)y+C(x)$, then the transformation $y=f+\frac1v$ reduces the above differential equation to a linear equation in $v$. Hence solve the equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=-8xy^2+4x(4x+1)y-(8x^3+4x^2-1)$; given solution $f(x)=x$. [4+2]
