CC-4

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2020
  1. 1 Answer any five questions: \hfill \(5\times2=10\)
  2. 1(a) Find the transformed equation of the straight line \(\frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}=2\) when the origin is transferred to the point \((a,b)\).
  3. 1(b) Find the eccentricity and foci of the ellipse \(5x^2+9y^2-40x-36y=9\).
  4. 1(c) Find the points of intersection of the two conics \(\frac1r=1+\cos\theta\) and \(\frac3r=1-\cos\theta\).
  5. 1(d) Find the radius of the circle \(3x^2+3y^2+3z^2+x-5y-2=0,\ x+y=2\).
  6. 1(e) Find the equation of the tangent plane to the hyperbolic paraboloid \(5x^2-2y^2=2z\) parallel to \(10x-6y-z=7\).
  7. 1(f) Show that the angle between the lines of section of plane \(3x+y+5z=0\) and the cone \(6yz-2zx+5xy=0\) is \(\cos^{-1}\frac16\).
  8. 1(g) On the conic \(r=\frac{6}{1-\cos\theta}\), find the point with least radius vector.
  9. 2(a) When the axes are turned through an angle, the expression \(ax+by\) becomes \(a'x'+b'y'\) referred to new axes; show that \(a^2+b^2=a'^2+b'^2\). [5]
  10. 2(b) Reduce the equation \(x^2-5xy+y^2+8x-20y+15=0\) to its canonical form and determine the nature of the conic. [5]
  11. 3(a) The normals at the ends of the locus rectum of the parabola \(y^2=4ax\) meet the carve again in \(Q\) and \(Q'\). Prove that \(QQ'=12a\). [5]
  12. 3(b) Prove that the straight line \(r\cos(\theta-\alpha)=p\) touches the conic \(\frac{l}{r}=1+e\cos\theta\) if \((l\cos\alpha-ep)^2+l^2\sin^2\alpha=p^2\). [5]
  13. 4(a) Find the equation of the sphere passing through the point \((1,0,-3)\) and through the circle represented by \(x^2+y^2+z^2-4x-6y+2z=16,\quad 3x+y+3z-4=0\). [4]
  14. 4(b) If the straight line \(\frac{x}{1}=\frac{y}{2}=\frac{z}{3}\) represents one of a set of three mutually perpendicular generators of the cone \(5yz-8zx-3xy=0\), the find the equation of the other two. [6]
  15. 5(a) If \(P,Q,R\) and \(P',Q',R'\) be the feet of the six normals drawn from a point to the ellipsoid \(\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1\) and the plane \(PQR\) is given by \(lx+my+nz=p\) then show that the equation of the plane \(P'Q'R'\) is given by \(\frac{x}{a^2l}+\frac{y}{b^2m}+\frac{z}{c^2n}+\frac{1}{p}=0\). [6]
  16. 5(b) If the normal at a point \(P\) of then hyperboloid \(x^2+y^2=4z\) meets the plane \(YOZ\) and \(ZOX\) at the points \(Q\) and \(R\) respectively, then show that \(PQ=PR\). [4]
  17. 1(a) Show that if \(\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c\) be any three non-coplanar vectors then \(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c,\mathbf c\times\mathbf a,\mathbf a\times\mathbf b\) are also non-coplanar. Express \(\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c\) in terms of \(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c,\mathbf c\times\mathbf a\) and \(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b\).
  18. 1(b) Find the shortest distance between the lines \(\frac{x-1}{2}=\frac{y-2}{3}=\frac{z-3}{4}\) and \(\frac{x-2}{3}=\frac{y-3}{4}=\frac{z-4}{5}\) by vector method. [2]
  19. 2(a) If \(\mathbf a\) and \(\mathbf b\) are two non-collinear vectors such that \(\mathbf a=\mathbf c+\mathbf d\), where \(\mathbf c\) is a vector parallel to \(\mathbf b\) and \(\mathbf d\) is a vector perpendicular to \(\mathbf b\), obtain expressions for \(\mathbf c\) and \(\mathbf d\) in terms of \(\mathbf a\) and \(\mathbf b\). [3]
  20. 2(b) Find \(\mathbf r\) and \(\lambda\), where \(\mathbf a\times\mathbf r=\mathbf b\times\lambda\mathbf a\); \(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf r=2\). Given that \(\mathbf a=\mathbf i+2\mathbf j-\mathbf k\) and \(\mathbf b=2\mathbf i-\mathbf j+\mathbf k\). [3]
  21. 3(a) In the triangle \(\triangle ABC\) let \(BC=a,\ CA=b,\ AB=c\). Prove that \(\cos C=\frac{a^2+b^2-c^2}{2ab}\) by vector method, where \(a,b,c\) denote the lengths of sides of the triangle. [4]
  22. 3(b) Find the equation of a plane containing the line \(\mathbf r=\mathbf a+t\mathbf b\) and parallel to the vector \(\mathbf c\). [2]
  23. 4(a) If \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are perpendicular to each other, then prove that \(\alpha\times(\beta\times\gamma)\) and \((\alpha\times\beta)\times\gamma\) are also perpendicular to each other. [3]
  24. 4(b) Show that a necessary and sufficient condition that a proper vector \(\mathbf u\) always remains parallel to a fixed line is that \(\mathbf u\times\frac{d\mathbf u}{dt}=0\). [3]
  25. 5(a) Find the curvature of the curve \(\mathbf r=\mathbf f(t)\) by vector method. [3]
  26. 5(b) Prove that \(\nabla\left(\frac{f}{g}\right)=\frac{g\nabla f-f\nabla g}{g^2}\). [3]
  27. 6(a) Prove that \(\int_\Gamma\mathbf t\cdot d\mathbf r=2\pi\), where \(\mathbf t\) is the unit tangent vector and \(\Gamma\) is the unit circle in the \(xy\)-plane about the origin as center. [3]
  28. 6(b) Determine whether the force field \(\mathbf F=2xz\mathbf i+(x^2-y)\mathbf j+(2z-x^2)\mathbf k\) is conservative or not. [3]
  29. 7 Verify Green's theorem in the plane for \(\int_\Gamma(x^2\,dx+xy\,dy)\), where \(\Gamma\) is the square in the \(xy\)-plane given by \(x=0,\ y=0,\ x=a,\ y=a\) \((a>0)\) described in the positive sence (anti-clockwise manner). [6]
  30. 8 Verify the Divergence Theorem for the vector function \(\mathbf F=(x^2-yz)\mathbf i+(y^2-zx)\mathbf j+(z^2-xy)\mathbf k\), taken over the rectangular parallelopiped \(0\le x\le a,\ 0\le y\le b,\ 0\le z\le c\). [6]
