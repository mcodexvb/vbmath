NBHM · 2018 · M.Sc. scholarship

  1. 1.1 The polynomial $x^3 - 11x^2 + ax - 36$ has a positive root which is the product of the other two roots. Find the value of $a$.
  2. 1.2 How many group homomorphisms are there from $\mathbb{Z}_8$ into $\mathbb{Z}_{10}$?
  3. 1.3 In which of the following examples is $H$ a normal subgroup of $G$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $G = \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ and $H$ is the subgroup of all upper triangular matrices in $G$. \item $G = \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{R})$ and $H$ is the subgroup of all matrices in $G$ with positive determinant. \item $G = \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ and $H$ is the subgroup of all matrices in $G$ with determinant equal to unity. \end{enumerate}
  4. 1.4 In each of the cases in the preceding exercise, where $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$, identify the quotient group $G/H$.
  5. 1.5 Which of the following statements are true?\\ (a) Every group of order $11$ is cyclic. \quad (b) Every group of order $111$ is cyclic. \quad (c) Every group of order $1111$ is cyclic.
  6. 1.6 Let $\sigma \in S_7$ be the permutation given by \[ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\ 2 & 1 & 5 & 4 & 7 & 6 & 3 \end{pmatrix}. \] Which of the following permutations are conjugate to $\sigma$?\\ (a) $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\ 1 & 3 & 5 & 6 & 2 & 7 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$. \qquad (b) $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\ 1 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 3 & 2 & 7 \end{pmatrix}$.
  7. 1.7 Let $A : \mathbb{R}^m \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a linear transformation. If $W$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$, define $W^{\perp} = \{y \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid (x, y) = 0 \text{ for all } x \in W\}$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ (a) $R(A) \subset (\ker(A^T))^{\perp}$. \quad (b) $R(A) = (\ker(A^T))^{\perp}$. \quad (c) Neither of the above statements need be necessarily true.
  8. 1.8 Let $n \ge 3$. Let $\{w_j\}_{j=1}^{n}$ be a basis for $\mathbb{R}^n$ such that the inner-product $(w_j, w_{\ell}) = 0$ whenever $j \neq \ell$. Let $W$ be an arbitrary subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$. Let $1 < k < n$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exists $x \in W$ such that $x \neq 0$ and such that $(x, w_j) = 0$ for all $1 \le j \le k - 1$. \item If the dimension of $W$ is equal to $k$, then there exists $x \in W$ such that $x \neq 0$ and such that $(x, w_j) = 0$ for all $1 \le j \le k - 1$. \item If the dimension of $W$ is strictly greater than $k$, then there exists $x \in W$ such that $x \neq 0$ and such that $(x, w_j) = 0$ for all $1 \le j \le k - 1$. \end{enumerate}
  9. 1.9 Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be a non-zero singular matrix. Consider the following problem: find $X \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ such that \[ \text{(i) } AXA = A, \qquad \text{(ii) } XAX = X \quad \text{and} \quad \text{(iii) } AX = XA. \] Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If a solution to the above problem exists, then $A$ is not nilpotent. \item If $A$ represents a projection, then the above problem admits a solution. \item If $n = 2$ and if a solution to the above problem exists, then $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb{R}$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 1.10 Let $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be a non-zero singular matrix such that $\operatorname{tr}(A) \neq 0$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item For every such matrix $A$, the problem stated in the preceding exercise need not have a solution. \item For every such matrix $A$, the problem stated in the preceding exercise has a solution given by $X = \dfrac{1}{\operatorname{tr}(A)}A$. \item For every such matrix $A$, the problem stated in the preceding exercise has a solution given by $X = \dfrac{1}{(\operatorname{tr}(A))^2}A$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 Let $\{a_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of real numbers. Which of the following conditions imply that the sequence is Cauchy?\\ (a) $|a_n - a_{n+1}| \le \dfrac{1}{n}$. \qquad (b) $|a_n - a_{n+1}| \le \dfrac{(n + 1)^n}{n^{\frac{3}{2} + n}}$. \qquad (c) $|a_n - a_{n+1}| \le \dfrac{n\log n}{e^n}$.
  12. 2.2 Let $\{a_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ and $\{b_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be two real sequences. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n$ are convergent, then the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n b_n$ is convergent. \item If the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ is convergent and if the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n$ is absolutely convergent, then the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n b_n$ is absolutely convergent. \item If $a_n \ge 0$ for all $n$ and is such that the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ is convergent, and if the sequence $\{b_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is bounded, then the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n b_n$ is absolutely convergent. \end{enumerate}
  13. 2.3 Find the largest interval in $\mathbb{R}$ in which the following series converges: \[ (x + 1) - \frac{(x + 1)^2}{4} + \frac{(x + 1)^3}{9} - \frac{(x + 1)^4}{16} + \cdots. \]
  14. 2.4 Which of the following functions are continuous at every point of their respective domains of definition?\\ (a) $f(x) = \displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x^2 e^{nx} + x}{e^{nx} + 1},\ x \in \mathbb{R}$. \quad (b) $f(x) = \displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x^2 e^{nx} + \cos x}{e^{nx} + 1},\ x \in \mathbb{R}$. \quad (c) $f(x) = \displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n}\log(e^n + x^n),\ x \ge 0$.
  15. 2.5 Which of the following functions are uniformly continuous over their respective domains of definition? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{g(x - n)}{2^n},\ x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a bounded and uniformly continuous function. \item $f(x) = \cos x \cos\dfrac{\pi}{x},\ x \in ]0, 1[$. \item $f(x) = \sin x \sin\dfrac{\pi}{x},\ x \in ]0, 1[$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Which of the following functions are differentiable at $x = 0$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(x) = \sin(|x|x)$. \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} \sin(x^2), & \text{if } x \in \mathbb{Q}, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} \sin(|x|), & \text{if } x \in \mathbb{Q}, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ \end{enumerate}
  17. 2.7 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $x \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$. Find a closed form expression (in terms of $n$ and $x$ only) for the following sum: $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} k e^{kx}$.
  18. 2.8 Fill in the blank below with the correct symbol ($<, \le, >$ or $\ge$): \[ \tan^{-1} x \;\cdots\; \frac{x}{1 + x^2}, \quad \text{for all } x > 0. \]
  19. 2.9 What is the coefficient of $x^7$ in the Taylor series expansion, around $x = 0$, of the function $f(x) = \sin^{-1} x$?
  20. 2.10 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin^2 x}{\sin x + \cos x}\,dx$.
  21. 3.1 Let $X$ be a non-empty set and let $A \subset X$. Define $\chi_A(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } x \in A, \\ 0, & \text{if } x \notin A. \end{cases}$ If $E$ and $F$ are subsets of $X$, find a subset $G$ of $X$ such that $|\chi_E - \chi_F| = \chi_G$.
  22. 3.2 If $P = (x_1, y_1)$ and $Q = (x_2, y_2)$ are arbitrary points in the plane, define the metric $d(P, Q) = \max\{|x_1 - x_2|, |y_1 - y_2|\}$. Let $P = \left(2, \frac{1}{2}\right)$. Let $S = [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There does not exist any point $Q \in S$ such that $d(P, Q) = \min\{d(P, X) \mid X \in S\}$. \item There exists a unique point $Q \in S$ such that $d(P, Q) = \min\{d(P, X) \mid X \in S\}$. \item There exist infinitely many points $Q \in S$ such that $d(P, Q) = \min\{d(P, X) \mid X \in S\}$. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ be fixed. For $0 \le k \le n$, let $C_k = \binom{n}{k}$. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\sum_{\substack{0 \le k \le n \\ k \text{ even}}} \frac{C_k}{k + 1}$.
  24. 3.4 Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of real-valued functions defined on $\mathbb{R}$ and let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. Let $\varepsilon > 0$. Define $E_n(\varepsilon) = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid |f_n(x) - f(x)| \ge \varepsilon\}$, and $D = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid \text{the sequence } \{f_n(x)\}_{n=1}^{\infty} \text{ does not converge to } f(x)\}$. Express $D$ in terms of the sets of the form $E_n(\varepsilon)$.
  25. 3.5 Evaluate: $(\sqrt{3} + i)^{14} + (\sqrt{3} - i)^{14}$.
  26. 3.6 Let $0 < \theta < 2\pi$. Let $T = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}$. If $B$ is the closed unit disc in the plane, what is the area of $T(B)$?
  27. 3.7 Let $A$ be the point $(0, 4)$ in the plane. Let $B$ be an arbitrary point on the $x$-axis. Let $L$ be the mid-point of the line segment $AB$. Let the perpendicular bisector of this line segment meet the $y$-axis at $M$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of the line segment $LM$. Find the locus of $N$ (as $B$ moves on the $x$-axis).
  28. 3.8 A plane in $\mathbb{R}^3$ meets the coordinate axes at points $A, B$ and $C$ respectively. If the centroid of the triangle $\triangle ABC$ is the point $\left(1, \frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{3}\right)$, find the equation of the plane.
  29. 3.9 What is the highest power of $3$ dividing $1000!$?
  30. 3.10 A sports contingent consists of $m = k + \ell$ members, of which $k$ members form the team and $\ell$ members are reserves. If $n > m$ candidates are available for selection, we can first choose the $m$ members and then choose the team members amongst them, or, we can first choose the team and then choose the reserves. Write down the combinatorial identity which states that both ways give the same number of ways of forming the contingent.
