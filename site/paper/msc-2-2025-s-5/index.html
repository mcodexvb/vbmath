MMC-25

New Syllabus
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1(a) Define strain vector. Obtain the relation between strain vector and strain tensor. [2+3]
  2. 1(b) The displacement in an elastic solid is given as follows\par \hspace*{2em}$u_1=\epsilon(X_1+2X_2+3X_3)$\par \hspace*{2em}$u_2=\epsilon(-2X_1+X_2)$\par \hspace*{2em}$u_3=\epsilon(X_1+4X_2+2X_3)$\par where $\epsilon$ is a small quantity. Calculate dilatation, rotation, shear and principal strains. [5]
  3. 2(a) Prove that the stress vector at a point on any arbitrary plane surface is a linear function of three stress vectors acting on any three mutually perpendicular planes through that point. [5]
  4. 2(b) The state of stress at a point is given by\par \[\tau_{ij}=\begin{bmatrix}a&2&1\\2&0&2\\1&2&0\end{bmatrix}\] where $a$ is a constant. Determine $a$ such that there is at least one plane through the point in such a way that resultant stress on that plane is zero. Determine the d.c.s. of the normal to that plane. [5]
  5. 3(a) State the principles of conservation of mass in Lagrangian and Eulerian methods. Write down their corresponding equations of continuity and prove their equivalence. [2+1+2]
  6. 3(b) Write down the stress equation of equilibrium. Given the following stress distribution $\tau_{ij}=$\par \[\begin{bmatrix}x_2&-x_3&0\\-x_3&0&-x_2\\0&-x_2&T\end{bmatrix}\] find $T$ such that stress distribution is in equilibrium with body force $\vec F=-g\vec e_3$. [1+4]
  7. 1(a) Find all fixed points and their stabilities of $f(x)=x^2-1,x\in\mathbb R$. [2]
  8. 1(b) For $f(x)=-\frac32x^2+\frac52+1,x\in\mathbb R$, show that $\{0,1,2\}$ is an unstable 3-cycle . [3]
  9. 1(c) State Poincar\'e-Bendixson theorem. Show that the system given by $\dot x=x-y-x(x^2+2y^2),\ \dot y=x+y-y(x^2+2y^2)$, admits at least one periodic orbit in a region $R$. Specify the region $R$. [1+4]
  10. 2(a) Define a logistic map. Prove that the logistic map $f(x)=rx(1-x)$ has a 2-cycle for $r>3$. [1+4]
  11. 2(b) Define Schwarzian Derivative and find the Schwarzian derivative of a function $f(x)=\sin x$ at $x=0$. [2]
  12. 2(c) Suppose $f(x)$ is a polynomial such that all the roots of $f^\prime(x)$ are real and distinct. Then show that $Sf(x)<0$. [3]
  13. 3(a) Define the Tent map $T(x)$. Also find the fixed points of $T^2(x)$. Show that the set of points $\{\frac25,\frac45\}$ forms a periodic 2-cycle of the tent map $T(x)$. [1+2+2]
  14. 3(b) State and Prove Dulac's Criteria. [1+2]
  15. 3(c) Find the potential function of the dynamical system\par \[\dot x=2xy+y^3,\ \dot y=x^2+3xy^2-2y.\] [2]
