MJMA-3

Algebra III
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2024
  1. 1(a) For \(n\ge3\), let \(H=\{\beta\in S_n\mid\beta(1)=1\text{ or }2\text{ and }\beta(2)=1\text{ or }2\}\). Prove that \(H\) is a subgroup of \(S_n\). Determine \(|H|\) for \(n=5\).
  2. 1(b) Explain why \(S_8\) contains subgroups isomorphic to \(\mathbb Z_{15}\) and \(U(16)\)?
  3. 1(c) Let \(\beta\in S_7\) and suppose \(\beta^4=(2143567)\). Find \(\beta\). [2]
  4. 2(a) State and Prove Lagrange's Theorem on finite groups.
  5. 2(b) Let \(H\) be a subgroup of \(\mathbb R^*\), the group of nonzero real numbers under multiplication. If \(\mathbb R^+\subseteq H\subseteq\mathbb R^*\), Then prove that \(H=\mathbb R^+\) or \(H=\mathbb R^*\). [3]
  6. 2(c) Let \(|G|=33\). What are the possible orders for the elements of \(G\)? Show that \(G\) must have an element of order 3.
  7. 3(a) Let \(G\) be a group of order 100 that has a subgroup \(H\) of order 25. Show that every element of \(G\) of order 5 is in \(H\). [4]
  8. 3(b) Prove that an Abelian group of order 15 is cyclic. [3]
  9. 3(c) Let \(H\) and \(K\) be subgroups of a finite group \(G\) with \(H\subseteq K\subseteq G\) and \([G:H]\) a prime number. Show that either \(H=K\) or \(K=G\). [3]
  10. 4(a) Does the subgroup \(H=\{(1),(12)(34)\}\) normal in \(A_4\)? Justify your answer. [2]
  11. 4(b) What is the order of the element \(14+\langle8\rangle\) in the factor group \(\mathbb Z_{24}/\langle8\rangle\)? Give an example of non commutative group that have a cyclic quotient group. Justify your answer.
  12. 4(c) If a factor group \(G/Z(G)\) has order \(p^2\) for some prime \(p\), to what familiar group is \(G/Z(G)\) isomorphic? Explain why there is no group \(G\) with the property that \(G/Z(G)\) is isomorphic to the group of integers under addition.
  13. 5(a) Show that subgroup of a group with index 2 is normal. Give an example of a normal subgroup in group with index greater than 2. Justify your answer.
  14. 5(b) Show that every subgroup of an Abelian group is normal. Give an example with proper justification of a non commutative group every subgroup of which is normal.
  15. 6(a) State and prove First Isomorphism Theorem on groups.
  16. 6(b) Suppose that \(\phi\) is a homomorphism from \(S_4\) onto \(\mathbb Z_2\). Determine \(\operatorname{Ker}\phi\). [2]
  17. 6(c) Find all homomorphisms from \(\mathbb Z_6\) to \(S_3\). [3]
  18. 7(a) Describe the units of \(M_2(\mathbb Z)\). [3]
  19. 7(b) Let \(R\) be a ring. If \((R,+)\) is a cyclic group, show that \(R\) is a commutative ring. [3]
  20. 7(c) Let \(R\) be a commutative ring with unity. If \(a_0\) is a unit and \(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n\) are nilpotents in \(R\), show that \(a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2+\cdots+a_nx^n\) is a unit in \(R[x]\). [4]
  21. 8(a) Give an example of an infinite Boolean ring with justification. [3]
  22. 8(b) Prove that \(\mathbb Z_n\) is a field if and only if \(p\) is a prime number. [4]
  23. 8(c) Show that every Boolean ring is commutative. [3]
  24. 9(a) Let \(R\) be a finite commutative ring with unity. Show that every nonzero element of \(R\) is a unit or a zero-divisor. [4]
  25. 9(b) Show that the set of all nilpotent elements of a commutative ring form an ideal. [3]
  26. 9(c) List all the nilpotent elements of \(\mathbb Z_{100}\). Find the total number of nilpotent elements of \(\mathbb Z_{p^mq^n}\) where \(p,q\) are prime numbers and \(m,n\) are positive integers. [3]
  27. 10(a) Give an example with justification of an infinite integral domain that has characteristic 3. [3]
  28. 10(b) Does there exist any subring of \(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z\) that is not an ideal of \(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z\)? Show that every subring of \(\mathbb Z\) is an ideal of \(\mathbb Z\). [4]
  29. 10(c) Let \(R\) be a ring with \(1\) and has no divisors of zero. Show that for every \(a,b\in R,\ ab=1\) if and only if \(ba=1\). [3]
  30. 11(a) Show that the factor ring \(\mathbb R[x]/\langle x^2+1\rangle\) is a field. [3]
  31. 11(b) Determine all ideals of \(\mathbb R\times\mathbb R\). [3]
  32. 11(c) Let \(m\) and \(n\) be two positive integers and \(d=\gcd(m,n)\). Show that \(\langle m,n\rangle=\langle d\rangle\). [4]
  33. 12(a) Prove that the ideal \(I=\{(3x,y)\mid x,y\in\mathbb Z\}\) is not properly contained in any proper ideal of \(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z\). [4]
  34. 12(b) Give an example of a ring that has exactly two proper nontrivial ideals. Justify. [3]
  35. 12(c) Prove that the ring \(M_n(\mathbb R)\) of all \(n\times n\) real matrices has no nontrivial proper ideals. [3]
