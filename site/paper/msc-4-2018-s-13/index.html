MMO-41(P5)

Rings and Modules-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) Define exact covariant functors. Show that the contravariant functor $Hom_R(-,M):Mod_R\to Ab$ is left exact. [1+3]
  2. 1(b) Let $M$ be a right $R$-module. Show that the functor $Hom_R(-,M):Mod_R\to Ab$ is right exact if and only if $M$ is injective. [2]
  3. 1(c) Let $M$ be a right $R$-module. Prove that $M$ is injective if and only if for every essential right ideal $A$ of $R$ and $R$-linear map $f:A\to M$, there is $m\in M$ such that $f(a)=ma$ for every $a\in A$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Let $\{M_\alpha\}_\Delta$ be a family of right $R$-modules. Prove that $\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Delta}M_\alpha$ is projective if and only if $M_\alpha$ is projective for every $\alpha\in\Delta$. [4]
  5. 2(b) Show that every $R$-module is a homomorphic image of a projective $R$-module. [2]
  6. 2(c) Prove that an $R$-module is projective if and only if for every $R$-linear maps $f:N_1\to N_2$ and $g:M\to N_2$ where $N_1$ is injective, there is an $R$-linear map $\varphi:M\to N_1$ such that $\varphi\circ f=g$. [4]
  7. 3(a) Define finitely cogenerated $R$-modules. Prove that a right $R$-module $M$ is finitely generated if and only if for every family $\{M_\alpha\}_\Delta$ of $R$-modules and epimorphism $f:\displaystyle\bigoplus_\Delta M_\alpha\to M$, there is a finite subset $F\subseteq\Delta$ and an epimorphism $\tilde f:\displaystyle\bigoplus_FM_\alpha\to M$. [1+3]
  8. 3(b) Let $N$ be a submodule of $M$. Prove that $M$ is Noetherian if and only if both $N$ and $\dfrac MN$ are Noetherian. [3]
  9. 3(c) Define composition series. Let $0\to M_1\to M\to M_2\to0$ be an exact sequence of $R$-modules and $R$-linear maps. Prove that $M$ has a composition series if and only if both $M_I$ and $M_2$ have a composition series. [3]
  10. 4(a) Let $A$ be a right ideal of $R$. Prove that $A\subseteq J(R)$ if and only if for every right $R$-module $N\subseteq M$ with $\dfrac MN$ finitely generated, $N+MA=M$ implies that $M=N$. [4]
  11. 4(b) Prove that a commutative ring $R$ is primitive if and only if it is a field. [2]
  12. 4(c) Define right primitive rings. Let $P$ be an ideal of a ring $R$. Prove that $P$ is a right primitive ideal if and only if $P=ann_R(M)$ for some simple right $R$-module $M$. [4]
  13. 5(a) Let $S$ be an $m$-system in $R$. Prove that there is a prime ideal $P$ in $R$ such that $P\cap S=\phi$. [3]
  14. 5(b) Let $R$ be a ring. Prove that $P(R)=\{a\in R\mid a\text{ is strongly nilpotent}\}$. [3]
  15. 5(c) Define subdirect product of rings. Let $R$ be a ring and $\{R_\alpha\}_\Delta$ be a family of rings. State and prove a necessary and sufficient condition for $R$ to be a subdirect product of $\{R_\alpha\}_\Delta$. \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}[ 2 ]\end{center} [4]
  16. 6(a) Define radical $\sqrt I$ of an ideal $I$. Show that $\sqrt I$ is the intersection of all prime ideals containing $I$. [3]
  17. 6(b) Show that an ideal $Q$ is semiprime if and only if $Q=\sqrt Q$. [3]
  18. 6(c) Prove that a ring $R$ has a composition series if and only if it is both noetherian and artinian. [4]
