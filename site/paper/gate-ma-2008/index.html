  1. 1 Consider the subspace $W = \{[a_{ij}] : a_{ij} = 0 \text{ if } i + j \text{ is even}\}$ of all $10 \times 10$ real matrices. Then the dimension of $W$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $25$ \item $50$ \item $75$ \item $100$ \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $S$ be the open unit disk and $f : S \to \mathbb{C}$ be a real-valued analytic function with $f(0) = 1$. Then the set $\{z \in S : f(z) \ne 1\}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item empty \item nonempty finite \item countably infinite \item uncountable \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $E = \{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : 0 \le x \le 1,\ 0 \le y \le x\}$. Then $\displaystyle\iint_E (x + y)\,dx\,dy$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $-1$ \item $0$ \item $\dfrac{1}{2}$ \item $1$ \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 For $(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2$, let $f(x,y) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{2xy}{x^2 + y^2}, & \text{if } (x,y) \ne (0,0), \\ 0, & \text{if } (x,y) = (0,0). \end{cases}$ Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $f_x$ and $f_y$ exist at $(0,0)$, and $f$ is continuous at $(0,0)$ \item $f_x$ and $f_y$ exist at $(0,0)$, and $f$ is discontinuous at $(0,0)$ \item $f_x$ and $f_y$ do not exist at $(0,0)$, and $f$ is continuous at $(0,0)$ \item $f_x$ and $f_y$ do not exist at $(0,0)$, and $f$ is discontinuous at $(0,0)$ \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $y$ be a solution of $y' = e^{-y^2} - 1$ on $[0, 1]$ which satisfies $y(0) = 0$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $y(x) > 0$ for $x > 0$ \item $y(x) < 0$ for $x > 0$ \item $y$ changes sign in $[0, 1]$ \item $y \equiv 0$ for $x > 0$ \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 For the equation $x(x - 1)y'' + \sin(x)y' + 2x(x - 1)y = 0$, consider the following statements:\\ $P$: $x = 0$ is a regular singular point.\\ $Q$: $x = 1$ is a regular singular point.\\ Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item both $P$ and $Q$ are true \item $P$ is false but $Q$ is true \item $P$ is true but $Q$ is false \item both $P$ and $Q$ are false \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 Let $G = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$ and $H = \{-1, 1\}$ be groups under multiplication. Then the map $\varphi : G \to H$ defined by $\varphi(x) = \dfrac{x}{|x|}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item not a homomorphism \item a one-one homomorphism, which is not onto \item an onto homomorphism, which is not one-one \item an isomorphism \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 The number of maximal ideals in $\mathbb{Z}_{27}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $0$ \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $3$ \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 For $1 \le p \le \infty$, let $\|\cdot\|_p$ denote the $p$-norm on $\mathbb{R}^2$. If $\|\cdot\|_p$ satisfies the parallelogram law, then $p$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $3$ \item $\infty$ \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Consider the initial value problem $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$, $y(x_0) = y_0$. The aim is to compute the value of $y_1 = y(x_1)$ where $x_1 = x_0 + h$ ($h > 0$). At $x = x_1$, if the value of $y_1$ is equated to the corresponding value of the straight line passing through $(x_0, y_0)$ and having the slope equal to the slope of the curve $y(x)$ at $x = x_0$, then the method is called \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item Euler's method \item Improved Euler's method \item Backward Euler's method \item Taylor series method of order $2$ \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 The solution of $x u_x + y u_y = 0$ is of the form \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $f(y/x)$ \item $f(x + y)$ \item $f(x - y)$ \item $f(xy)$ \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 If the partial differential equation $(x - 1)^2 u_{xx} - (y - 2)^2 u_{yy} + 2x u_x + 2y u_y + 2xy u = 0$ is parabolic in $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ but not in $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus S$, then $S$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x = 1 \text{ or } y = 2\}$ \item $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x = 1 \text{ and } y = 2\}$ \item $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x = 1\}$ \item $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : y = 2\}$ \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 Let $E$ be a connected subset of $\mathbb{R}$ with at least two elements. Then the number of elements in $E$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item exactly two \item more than two but finite \item countably infinite \item uncountable \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 Let $X$ be a non-empty set. Let $\mathcal{T}_1$ and $\mathcal{T}_2$ be two topologies on $X$ such that $\mathcal{T}_1$ is strictly contained in $\mathcal{T}_2$. If $I : (X, \mathcal{T}_1) \to (X, \mathcal{T}_2)$ is the identity map, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item both $I$ and $I^{-1}$ are continuous \item both $I$ and $I^{-1}$ are not continuous \item $I$ is continuous but $I^{-1}$ is not continuous \item $I$ is not continuous but $I^{-1}$ is continuous \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 Let $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_{10}$ be a random sample from $N(80, 3^2)$ distribution. Define $S = \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10} U_i$ and $T = \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10} \left(U_i - \frac{S}{10}\right)^2$, where $U_i = \dfrac{X_i - 80}{3}$, $i = 1, 2, \dots, 10$. Then the value of $E(ST)$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $0$ \item $1$ \item $10$ \item $\dfrac{80}{3}$ \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 Two (distinguishable) fair coins are tossed simultaneously. Given that ONE of them lands up head, the probability of the OTHER to land up tail is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $\dfrac{1}{3}$ \item $\dfrac{1}{2}$ \item $\dfrac{2}{3}$ \item $\dfrac{3}{4}$ \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $c_{ij} \ge 2$ be the cost of the $(i, j)$-th cell of an assignment problem. If a new cost matrix is generated by the elements $c_{ij}^* = \dfrac{1}{2}c_{ij} + 1$, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item optimal assignment plan remains unchanged and cost of assignment decreases \item optimal assignment plan changes and cost of assignment decreases \item optimal assignment plan remains unchanged and cost of assignment increases \item optimal assignment plan changes and cost of assignment increases \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 Let a primal linear programming problem admit an optimal solution. Then the corresponding dual problem \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item does not have a feasible solution \item has a feasible solution but does not have any optimal solution \item does not have a convex feasible region \item has an optimal solution \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 In any system of particles, suppose we do not assume that the internal forces come in pairs. Then the fact that the sum of internal forces is zero follows from \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item Newton's second law \item conservation of angular momentum \item conservation of energy \item principle of virtual displacement \end{enumerate}
  20. 20 Let $q_1, q_2, \dots, q_n$ be the generalized coordinates and $\dot{q}_1, \dot{q}_2, \dots, \dot{q}_n$ be the generalized velocities in a conservative force field. If under a transformation, the new coordinate system has the generalized coordinates $Q_1, Q_2, \dots, Q_n$ and velocities $\dot{Q}_1, \dot{Q}_2, \dots, \dot{Q}_n$, then the equation $\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial q_k} = \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{q}_k}\right)$ takes the form \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial Q_k} = \varphi\dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{Q}_k}\right)$ \item $\varphi\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial Q_k} = \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{Q}_k}\right)$ \item $\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial Q_k} = -\varphi\dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{Q}_k}\right)$ \item $\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial Q_k} = \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{Q}_k}\right)$ \end{enumerate}
