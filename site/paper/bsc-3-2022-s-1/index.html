CCMA-5

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2022
  1. 1(a) What is the sequence of partial sums of a series \(\sum a_n\)? Show that a series \(\sum a_n\) of non-negative terms is convergent if and only if the sequence of its partial sums is a bounded sequence.
  2. 1(b) Examine the convergence of the series \(\sum\{(n^3+1)^{\frac13}-n\}\) and \(\sum\log(1+\frac1n)\). \par\hfill
  3. 2(a) If \(\sum a_n\) is a series of positive terms and \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\log\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}>1\), show that \(\sum a_n\) must converge.
  4. 2(b) Find the values of \(\alpha\) for which the series \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^\infty\frac1{(\log n)^\alpha}\) converges.
  5. 2(c) Discuss the convergence of the series \(\displaystyle\sum\frac1{3n+2}\). \hfill
  6. 3(a) What do you mean by absolutely and conditionally convergent series? Show that the series \(\displaystyle\sum\frac{(-1)^{n-1}n}{n^2+1}\) is conditionally convergent.
  7. 3(b) Let \(\sum a_n\) be an absolutely convergent series and \(\{b_n\}\) be a bounded sequence. Show that \(\sum a_nb_n\) is absolutely convergent. \hfill
  8. 4(a) Using \(\epsilon\)-\(\delta\) definition, explain when a function \(f\) is not continuous at a point \(c\).
  9. 4(b) Show that the function \(f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R\) defined by \(f(x)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }x\in\mathbb Q\\0&\text{if }x\notin\mathbb Q\end{cases}\) is not continuous at any point of \(\mathbb R\).
  10. 4(c) Let \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) be continuous on \([a,b]\). Prove that \(f\) is bounded on \([a,b]\). \hfill
  11. 5(a) Let \(f,g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R\) be continuous on \(\mathbb R\). Prove that the set \(S=\{x\in\mathbb R:f(x)=g(x)\}\) is closed in \(\mathbb R\).
  12. 5(b) Prove that the set of points of discontinuities of a function on a closed and bounded interval is a countable set. \hfill \par\textit{[The word “monotone” is inserted by hand before “function.”]}
  13. 6(a) When is a function said to be uniformly continuous on an interval? Show that a continuous function \(f:(a,b)\to\mathbb R\) becomes uniformly continuous on \((a,b)\) if and only if \(f\) admits of a continuous extension to \(\mathbb R\).
  14. 6(b) Check the continuity and uniform continuity of the function \(f:\mathbb Q\to\mathbb R\) given by \(f(x)=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }x<\sqrt5\\1&\text{if }x>\sqrt5.\end{cases}\) \hfill
  15. 7(a) Let \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) be continuous on \([a,b]\) and let \(f'\) be bounded on \((a,b)\). Prove that \(f\) is function of bounded variation on \([a,b]\). Is the boundedness of \(f'\) necessary here? Justify your answer.
  16. 7(b) Prove or disprove: A function \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) of bounded variation can be expressed as the difference of two monotone increasing functions on \([a,b]\). \hfill
  17. 8(a) For a bounded function \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\), prove that the lower Riemann integral cannot exceed the upper Riemann integral. Explain when they become equal.
  18. 8(b) Find the lower and upper Riemann integrals of the function \(f(x)=e^x\) on \([a,b]\) and determine if \(f\) is Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\). \hfill
  19. 9(a) If \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) is bounded on \([a,b]\), prove that \(f\) is Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\) if and only if for each \(\epsilon>0\), there exists a \(\delta>0\) such that \(U(P,f)-L(P,f)<\epsilon\) for every partition \(P\) of \([a,b]\) with \(\lVert P\rVert<\delta\).
  20. 9(b) Show by an example that a bounded function which is continuous on \([a,b]\) except for an infinite set \(S\subset[a,b]\) having infinite number of limit points may be Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\). \hfill \renewcommand{\paperfull}{40}
