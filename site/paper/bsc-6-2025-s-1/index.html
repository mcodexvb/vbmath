CCMA-13

Analysis VI
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2025
  1. 1(a) Prove that a necessary and sufficient condition that a function $f(z)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$ tends to a complex number $l=\alpha+i\beta$ as $z=x+iy$ tends to $z_0=a+ib$ is that $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}u(x,y)=\alpha$ and $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}v(x,y)=\beta$. [4]
  2. 1(b) Let $f$ be an entire function such that $f(z)=u(y)+iv(x)$. Show that $f$ can be expressed as $f(z)=az+b$, for some $a,b\in\mathbb C$ with $\operatorname{Re}(a)=0$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Verify Cauchy-Riemann equations for the function \[ f(z)=\begin{cases} \dfrac{x^3-y^3}{x^2+y^2}+i\dfrac{x^3+y^3}{x^2+y^2}&\text{for }z\ne0,\\ 0&\text{for }z=0 \end{cases} \quad\text{at }z=0. \] Is $f$ differentiable at $z=0$? Justify your answer. [3]
  4. 2(a) Let $f(z)=u+iv$ be an entire function such that $v$ is bounded. Prove or disprove: $f$ is constant. [3]
  5. 2(b) Let $f(z)=u+iv$ be an analytic function on a domain $G$. Find $f(z)$ explicitly if $2u+3v=13(x^2-y^2)+2x+3y$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Describe stereographic projection of the complex plane $\mathbb C$ and obtain the formulae for the point $(\xi,\eta,\zeta)$ on the Riemann sphere corresponding to a given point $z=x+iy$ in $\mathbb C$. [4]
  7. 3(a) Find all the M\"obius transformations of the unit disc $|z|\leq 1$ onto the upper half-plane $\operatorname{Im}(w)\geq0$. [4]
  8. 3(b) Show that the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{i^n}{n}$ is conditionally convergent. [3]
  9. 3(c) Prove that a M\"obius transformation maps a circle onto a circle and inverse points onto inverse points. [3]
  10. 4(a) Let $f$ be analytic in a domain $D$ and $f'(a)\ne0$ for some $a\in D$. Prove or disprove: $f$ is one-one in some neighbourhood of $a$. [3]
  11. 4(b) State orientation principle and use to find the image of the interior of thr circle $|z+3|=3$ under the M\"obius transformation $w(z)=\dfrac{z}{2z+6}$. [4]
  12. 4(c) Find the integral of the function $f(z)=x+y-3ix^3$ along the curve $y=x$ from the point $z=0$ to the point $z=1+i$. [3]
  13. 5(a) State and prove Cauchy's Integral Formula for derivative. [4]
  14. 5(b) Let $f$ be analytic in a domain $G$. Prove that all the derivatives of $f$ exist and are analytic in $G$. [3]
  15. 5(c) Evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_C\frac{e^{iz}}{z(z-\pi)}\,dz$, where $C$ is the (positively oriented) circle $|z|=3$. [3]
  16. 6(a) Define a `Discrete metric space'. [1]
  17. 6(b) Prove that for any three points $x,y,z$ in a metric space $(X,\rho)$, $|\rho(x,y)-\rho(z,x)|\leq\rho(y,z)$. [2]
  18. 6(c) Let $(X,\rho)$ be a metric space and $d(x,y)=\frac{3\rho(x,y)}{1+3\rho(x,y)}$. Examine if $(X,d)$ is a metric space. [3]
  19. 6(d) Prove that $C[a,b]$, the set of all real valued continuous functions on $[a,b]$, is a metric space with respect to a metric defined by you. [4]
  20. 7(a) Show that arbitrary union of open sets is open in a metric space. [3]
  21. 7(b) Is the arbitrary intersection of open sets open? Justify your answer. [1+2]
  22. 7(c) Define the closure $\bar A$ of a set $A$ in a metric space $(X,\rho)$. Prove that if $\alpha\in\bar A$ then $S\cap A\ne\phi$ for every neighbourhood $S$ of $\alpha$. [1+3]
  23. 8(a) Define a convergent sequence in a metric space $(X,\rho)$. If $x_n\to\alpha$ and $y_n\to\beta$ as $n\to\infty$, then show that $\rho(x_n,y_n)\to\rho(\alpha,\beta)$ as $n\to\infty$. [1+2]
  24. 8(b) Prove that a Cauchy sequence in a metric space, which has a convergent subsequence, is itself convergent. [3]
  25. 8(c) Show that $l_p(p\geq2)$ is a complete metric space. [4]
  26. 9(a) Let $X$ be a complete metric space and $Y$ be a subset of $X$. Then prove that $Y$, considered as a metric space is closed if and only if it is complete. [2+2]
  27. 9(b) Define a set of first category and a set of second category in a metric space. [2]
  28. 9(c) Define a fixed point of a linear operator $T$ in a metric space $(X,\rho)$. Let $X=(0,\frac19)$ be a metric space with the usual metric of $R$. Let $T:X\to X$ be given by $Tx=x^2$. Show that $T$ is a contraction mapping having no fixed point. [1+3]
  29. 10(a) State and prove Banach fixed point theorem. [1+4]
  30. 10(b) Let $(X,\rho)$ and $(Y,\rho_1)$ be two metric spaces and $f:X\to Y$ be a function. Then $f(x)$ is continuous at $x\in X$ if and only if for every sequence $\{x_n\}$, $x_n\in X$ converging to $x\in X$ implies $f(x_n)\to f(x)$ as $n\to\infty$. [5]
