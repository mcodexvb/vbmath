IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2014

  1. Let $f(x)=\left|x^{2}-25\right|$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The total number of points of $\mathbb{R}$ at which $f$ attains a local extremum (minimum or maximum) is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 1 \item 2 \item 3 \item 4 \end{enumerate}
  2. The coefficient of $(x-1)^{2}$ in the Taylor series expansion of $f(x)=x e^{x}(x \in \mathbb{R})$ about the point $x=1$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{e}{2}$ \item $2 e$ \item $\frac{3 e}{2}$ \item $3 e$ \end{enumerate}
  3. Let $f(x, y)=\sum_{k=1}^{10}\left(x^{2}-y^{2}\right)^{k}$ for all $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$. Then for all $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$, \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $x \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x, y)-y \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x, y)=0$ \item $x \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x, y)+y \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x, y)=0$ \item $y \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x, y)-x \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x, y)=0$ \item $y \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x, y)+x \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x, y)=0$ \end{enumerate}
  4. For $a, b, c \in \mathbb{R}$, if the differential equation $$ \left(a x^{2}+b x y+y^{2}\right) d x+\left(2 x^{2}+c x y+y^{2}\right) d y=0 $$ is exact, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $b=2, c=2 a$ \item $b=4, c=2$ \item $b=2, c=4$ \item $b=2, a=2 c$ \end{enumerate}
  5. If $f(x, y, z)=x^{2} y+y^{2} z+z^{2} x$ for all $(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}$ and $\nabla=\frac{\partial}{\partial x} \mathbf{i}+\frac{\partial}{\partial y} \mathbf{j}+\frac{\partial}{\partial z} \mathbf{k}$, then the value of $\nabla \cdot(\nabla \times \nabla f)+\nabla \cdot(\nabla f)$ at $(1,1,1)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item 3 \item 6 \item 9 \end{enumerate}
  6. The radius of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} 2^{2 n} x^{n^{2}}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{4}$ \item 1 \item 2 \item 4 \end{enumerate}
  7. Let $G$ be a group of order 17. The total number of non-isomorphic subgroups of $G$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 1 \item 2 \item 3 \item 17 \end{enumerate}
  8. Which one of the following is a subspace of the vector space $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\left\{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}: x+2 y=0,2 x+3 z=0\right\}$ \item $\left\{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}: 2 x+3 y+4 z-3=0, z=0\right\}$ \item $\left\{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}: x \geq 0, y \geq 0\right\}$ \item $\left\{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}: x-1=0, y=0\right\}$ \end{enumerate}
  9. Let $T: \mathbb{R}^{3} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{3}$ be the linear transformation defined by $T(x, y, z)=(x+y, y+z, z+x)$ for all $(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\operatorname{rank}(T)=0, \operatorname{nullity}(T)=3$ \item $\operatorname{rank}(T)=2, \operatorname{nullity}(T)=1$ \item $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{nullity}(T)=2$ \item $\operatorname{rank}(T)=3, \operatorname{nullity}(T)=0$ \end{enumerate}
  10. Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function satisfying $x+\int_{0}^{x} f(t) d t=e^{x}-1$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the set $\{x \in \mathbb{R}: 1 \leq f(x) \leq 2\}$ is the interval \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $[\log 2, \log 3]$ \item $[2 \log 2,3 \log 3]$ \item $\left[e-1, e^{2}-1\right]$ \item $\left[0, e^{2}\right]$ \end{enumerate}
  11. The system of linear equations $$ \begin{aligned} x-y+2 z &=b_{1} \\ x+2 y-z &=b_{2} \\ 2 y-2 z &=b_{3} \end{aligned} $$ is inconsistent when $\left(b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}\right)$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(2,2,0)$ \item $(0,3,2)$ \item $\quad(2,2,1)$ \item $(2,-1,-2)$ \end{enumerate}
  12. Let $A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}a & -1 & 4 \\ 0 & b & 7 \\ 0 & 0 & 3\end{array}\right]$ be a matrix with real entries. If the sum and the product of all the eigenvalues of $A$ are 10 and 30 respectively, then $a^{2}+b^{2}$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 29 \item 40 \item 58 \item 65 \end{enumerate}
  13. Consider the subspace $W=\left\{\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{10}\right) \in \mathbb{R}^{10}: x_{n}=x_{n-1}+x_{n-2}\right.$ for $\left.3 \leq n \leq 10\right\}$ of the vector space $\mathbb{R}^{10}$. The dimension of $W$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 2 \item 3 \item 9 \item 10 \end{enumerate}
  14. Let $y_{1}(x)$ and $y_{2}(x)$ be two linearly independent solutions of the differential equation $$ x^{2} y^{\prime \prime}(x)-2 x y^{\prime}(x)-4 y(x)=0 \text { for } x \in[1,10] \text {. } $$ Consider the Wronskian $W(x)=y_{1}(x) y_{2}^{\prime}(x)-y_{2}(x) y_{1}^{\prime}(x)$. If $W(1)=1$, then $W(3)-W(2)$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 1 \item 2 \item 3 \item 5 \end{enumerate}
  15. The equation of the curve passing through the point $\left(\frac{\pi}{2}, 1\right)$ and having slope $\frac{\sin (x)}{x^{2}}-\frac{2 y}{x}$ at each point $(x, y)$ with $x \neq 0$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-x^{2} y+\cos (x)=\frac{-\pi^{2}}{4}$ \item $\quad x^{2} y+\cos (x)=\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}$ \item $x^{2} y-\sin (x)=\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}-1$ \item $x^{2} y+\sin (x)=\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}+1$ \end{enumerate}
  16. The value of $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the curves $x^{2}+\alpha y^{2}=1$ and $y=x^{2}$ intersect orthogonally is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-2$ \item $\frac{-1}{2}$ \item $\frac{1}{2}$ \item 2 \end{enumerate}
  17. Let $x_{n}=2^{2 n}\left(1-\cos \left(\frac{1}{2^{n}}\right)\right)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item does NOT converge \item converges to 0 \item converges to $\frac{1}{2}$ \item converges to $\frac{1}{4}$ \end{enumerate}
  18. Let $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right)=c$, where $c$ is a positive real number. Then the sequence $\left\{\frac{x_{n}}{n}\right\}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item is NOT bounded \item is bounded but NOT convergent \item converges to $c$ \item converges to 0 \end{enumerate}
  19. Let $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}$ be two series, where $a_{n}=\frac{(-1)^{n} n}{2^{n}}, b_{n}=\frac{(-1)^{n}}{\log (n+1)}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item both $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}$ are absolutely convergent \item $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ is absolutely convergent but $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}$ is conditionally convergent \item $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ is conditionally convergent but $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}$ is absolutely convergent \item both $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{n}$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}$ are conditionally convergent \end{enumerate}
  20. The set $\left\{\frac{x^{2}}{1+x^{2}}: x \in \mathbb{R}\right\}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item connected but NOT compact in $\mathbb{R}$ \item compact and connected in $\mathbb{R}$ \item compact but NOT connected in $\mathbb{R}$ \item neither compact nor connected in $\mathbb{R}$ \end{enumerate}
  21. The set of all limit points of the set $\left\{\frac{2}{x+1}: x \in(-1,1)\right\}$ in $\mathbb{R}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $[1, \infty)$ \item $(1, \infty)$ \item $[-1,1]$ \item $[-1, \infty)$ \end{enumerate}
  22. Let $S=[0,1] \cup[2,3)$ and let $f: S \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be defined by $f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{lll}2 x & \text { if } & x \in[0,1] \text {, } \\ 8-2 x & \text { if } & x \in[2,3) .\end{array}\right.$ If $T=\{f(x): x \in S\}$, then the inverse function $f^{-1}: T \rightarrow S$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item does NOT exist \item exists and is continuous \item exists and is NOT continuous \item exists and is monotonic \end{enumerate}
  23. Let $f(x)=x^{3}+x$ and $g(x)=x^{3}-x$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. If $f^{-1}$ denotes the inverse function of $f$, then the derivative of the composite function $g \circ f^{-1}$ at the point 2 is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{2}{13}$ \item $\frac{1}{2}$ \item $\frac{11}{13}$ \item $\frac{11}{4}$ \end{enumerate}
  24. For all $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$, let $f(x, y)= \begin{cases}x & \text { if } y=0, \\ x-y^{3} \sin (1 / y) & \text { if } y \neq 0 .\end{cases}$ Then at the point $(0,0)$, \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ is NOT continuous \item $f$ is continuous but NOT differentiable \item $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$ exists but $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}$ does NOT exist \item $f$ is differentiable \end{enumerate}
  25. For all $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$, let $f(x, y)= \begin{cases}\frac{x}{|x|} \sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}} & \text { if } x \neq 0, \\ 0 & \text { if } x=0 .\end{cases}$ Then $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(0,0)+\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(0,0)$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-1$ \item 0 \item 1 \item 2 \end{enumerate}
  26. Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a function with continuous derivative such that $f(\sqrt{2})=2$ and $f(x)=\lim _{t \rightarrow 0} \frac{1}{2 t} \int_{x-t}^{x+t} s f^{\prime}(s) d s$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $f(3)$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\sqrt{3}$ \item $\quad 3 \sqrt{2}$ \item $3 \sqrt{3}$ \item 9 \end{enumerate}
  27. The value of $\int_{x=0}^{1} \int_{y=0}^{x^{2}} \int_{z=0}^{y}(y+2 z) d z d y d x$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{53}$ \item $\frac{2}{21}$ \item $\frac{1}{6}$ \item $\frac{5}{3}$ \end{enumerate}
  28. If $C$ is a smooth curve in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ from $(-1,0,1)$ to $(1,1,-1)$, then the value of $\int_{C}\left(2 x y+z^{2}\right) d x+\left(x^{2}+z\right) d y+(y+2 x z) d z$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item 1 \item 2 \item 3 \end{enumerate}
  29. Let $C$ be the boundary of the region $R=\left\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}:-1 \leq y \leq 1,0 \leq x \leq 1-y^{2}\right\}$ oriented in the counterclockwise direction. Then the value of $\oint_{C} y d x+2 x d y$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{-4}{3}$ \item $\frac{-2}{3}$ \item $\frac{2}{3}$ \item $\frac{4}{3}$ \end{enumerate}
  30. Let $G$ be a cyclic group of order 24 . The total number of group isomorphisms of $G$ onto itself is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 7 \item 8 \item 17 \item 24 \end{enumerate}
  31. Let $S_{n}$ be the group of all permutations on the set $\{1,2, \ldots, n\}$ under the composition of mappings. For $n>2$, if $H$ is the smallest subgroup of $S_{n}$ containing the transposition $(1,2)$ and the cycle $(1,2, \ldots, n)$, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $H=S_{n}$ \item $H$ is abelian \item the index of $H$ in $S_{n}$ is 2 \item $\mathrm{H}$ is cyclic \end{enumerate}
  32. Let $S$ be the oriented surface $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=1$ with the unit normal $\mathbf{n}$ pointing outward. For the vector field $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z)=x \mathbf{i}+y \mathbf{j}+z \mathbf{k}$, the value of $\iint_{S} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} d S$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{\pi}{3}$ \item $2 \pi$ \item $\frac{4 \pi}{3}$ \item $4 \pi$ \end{enumerate}
  33. Let $f:(0, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function such that $f^{\prime}\left(x^{2}\right)=1-x^{3}$ for all $x>0$ and $f(1)=0$. Then $f(4)$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{-47}{5}$ \item $\frac{-47}{10}$ \item $\frac{-16}{5}$ \item $\frac{-8}{5}$ \end{enumerate}
  34. Which one of the following conditions on a group $G$ implies that $G$ is abelian? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The order of $G$ is $p^{3}$ for some prime $p$ \item Every proper subgroup of $G$ is cyclic \item Every subgroup of $G$ is normal in $G$ \item The function $f: G \rightarrow G$, defined by $f(x)=x^{-1}$ for all $x \in G$, is a homomorphism \end{enumerate}
  35. Let $S=\left\{x \in \mathbb{R}: x^{6}-x^{5} \leq 100\right\}$ and $T=\left\{x^{2}-2 x: x \in(0, \infty)\right\}$. The set $S \cap T$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item closed and bounded in $\mathbb{R}$ \item closed but NOT bounded in $\mathbb{R}$ \item bounded but NOT closed in $\mathbb{R}$ \item neither closed nor bounded in $\mathbb{R}$ \end{enumerate}
  36. Find all the critical points of the function $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ defined by $f(x, y)=x^{3}+x y+y^{3}$ for all $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$. Also, examine whether the function $f$ attains a local maximum or a local minimum at each of these critical points.
  37. Given that there is a common solution to the following equations: $$ \begin{aligned} &\mathbf{P}: y^{\prime}+2 y=e^{x} y^{2}, \quad y(0)=1, &\mathbf{Q}: y^{\prime \prime}-2 y^{\prime}+\alpha y=0 \end{aligned} $$ find the value of $\alpha$ and hence find the general solution of $\mathbf{Q}$.
  38. Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a twice differentiable function such that $f\left(\frac{1}{2^{n}}\right)=0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Show that $f^{\prime}(0)=0=f^{\prime \prime}(0)$.
  39. Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with real entries such that $A^{2}=A$. If I denotes the $n \times n$ identity matrix, then show that $\operatorname{rank}(A-I)=\operatorname{nullity}(A)$.
  40. Evaluate $\iint_{S} \frac{x y}{\sqrt{1+2 x^{2}}} d S$, where the surface $S=\left\{\left(x, y, x^{2}+y\right) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}: 0 \leq x \leq y, x+y \leq 1\right\}$.
  41. Let $f:(0,1) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function such that $\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right| \leq 5$ for all $x \in(0,1)$. Show that the sequence $\left\{f\left(\frac{1}{n+1}\right)\right\}$ converges in $\mathbb{R}$.
  42. Let $H$ be a subgroup of the group $(\mathbb{R},+)$ such that $H \cap[-1,1]$ is a finite set containing a nonzero element. Show that $H$ is cyclic.
  43. If $K$ is a nonempty closed subset of $\mathbb{R}$, then show that the set $\{x+y: x \in K, y \in[1,2]\}$ is closed in $\mathbb{R}$.
