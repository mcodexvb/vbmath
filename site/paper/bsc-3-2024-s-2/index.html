MJMA-6

Differential Equations II and Dynamics of a Particle
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2024
  1. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  2. 1(a) Solve the system of ordinary differential equations (ODEs) \[\frac{dx}{dt}=-wy,\qquad\frac{dy}{dt}=wx\] and show that the point $(x,y)$ lies on a circle.
  3. 1(b) Given the solution $y_1(x)=e^x$, find the second linearly independent solution $y_2(x)$ of the ODE \[(x+2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-(4x+9)\frac{dy}{dx}+(3x+7)y=0,\quad x\in\mathbb R-\{2\}.\]
  4. 1(c) Construct two first-order differential equations from the following ODE: \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2\frac{dy}{dx}+2025y=0\] with $y=2023$ and $\frac{dy}{dx}=2024$ at $x=0$.
  5. 1(d) If $y=u(x)$ is a solution of $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0$, then show that another independent solution $y=v(x)$ is given by $v(x)=u(x)\int\frac{W(u,v)}{[u(x)]^2}\,dx$, where the Wronskian $W(u,v)$ satisfies the ODE $\frac{dW}{dx}+PW=0$.
  6. 1(e) Define ordinary and singular points of a second order linear ODE \[a_0(x)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+a_1(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+a_2(x)y=a_3(x).\] about the point $x=x_0$. Give examples in support of your answer.
  7. 1(f) Find the roots of the indicial equation of \[2x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-(x+x^2)\frac{dy}{dx}+(x^2-2)y=0\] about the point $x=0$.
  8. 1(g) State the condition for integrability of the Pfaffian differential equation \[P(x,y,z)\,dx+Q(x,y,z)\,dy+R(x,y,z)\,dz=0.\] What is the number of independent conditions of integrability in a Pfaffian differential equation with four variables?
  9. 1(h) Solve the following total differential equation by inspection method: \[(yz+xyz)\,dx+(zx+xyz)\,dy+(xy+xyz)\,dz=0.\] [$5\times2=10$]
  10. 2(a) State and prove Abel-Liouville's formula for the $n^{\mathrm{th}}$ order linear ODE.
  11. 2(b) Let $y_1(x)$ and $y_2(x)$ be two solutions of the ODE \[(1-x^2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}+(\sec x)y=0,\quad -1<x<1\] with Wronskian $W(x)$. If $y_1|_{x=\frac12}=2$, $\frac{dy_1}{dx}|_{x=\frac12}=\frac13$, $y_2|_{x=\frac12}=5$, $\frac{dy_2}{dx}|_{x=\frac12}=1$, find the value of $W(\frac{\sqrt3}{2})$.
  12. 2(c) Factorize the operator on the left-hand side of $[(x+3)D^2+(2x+7)D+2]y=(x+3)^2e^x$ ($D^n\equiv\frac{d^n}{dx^n}$) to represent the ODE in right order. [7+5+3]
  13. 3(a) Solve the ODE \[(1-x^2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-x\frac{dy}{dx}+4y=0\] in series near the ordinary point $x=0$. What will be the particular solution for $y|_{x=0}=1$ and $\frac{dy}{dx}|_{x=0}=2$?
  14. 3(b) Solve the following system of two linear homogeneous differential equations with constant coefficients using eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the matrix: \[\begin{aligned} \frac{dx}{dt}&=-x-6y,\\ \frac{dy}{dt}&=3x+5y,\\ x(t=0)&=0,\quad y(t=0)=2. \end{aligned}\] [9+6]
  15. 4(a) Identify the number of independent arbitrary constants within the general solution of the following simultaneous equations: \[\begin{aligned} \phi_1(D)x+\phi_2(D)y&=\phi(t),\\ \psi_1(D)x+\psi_2(D)y&=\psi(t),\quad D\equiv\frac{d}{dt}. \end{aligned}\]
  16. 4(b) What do the solutions of the differential equation \[\frac{dx}{P(x,y,z)}=\frac{dy}{Q(x,y,z)}=\frac{dz}{R(x,y,z)}\] represent geometrically? Hence solve the following: \[\frac{dx}{x^2+y^2+yz}=\frac{dy}{x^2+y^2-xz}=\frac{dz}{z(x+y)}.\]
  17. 4(c) Solve the total differential equation after satisfying the conditions of integrability \[(yz-1)\,dx+(z-x)x\,dy+(1-xy)\,dz=0.\]
  18. 1(a) Define central force and the centre of force. [3]
  19. 1(b) A particle moves from rest in a straight line under an attractive force $\mu\times(\text{distance})^{-2}$ per unit mass. Show that if the initial distance of the particle from the centre of force be $2a$, then the distance will be $a$ after a time $(\frac{\pi}{2}+1)(\frac{a^3}{\mu})^{1/2}$. [7]
  20. 2(a) Prove that the resultant of two simple harmonic motions having slightly different periods is simple harmonic, of period equal to either of the component motion with varying amplitude and epoch. [3]
  21. 2(b) A mass is suspended from a ceiling by a light elastic string of natural length $l$. When the mass hangs in equilibrium, the length of the string is $(l+c)$. The mass is started off from the position of equilibrium with downward vertical velocity $v$. If, in the subsequent motion, the string never becomes slack, then show that $v^2<cg$. [7]
  22. 3(a) Establish the relation between angular velocity and linear velocity. [3]
  23. 3(b) Derive the expressions for the tangential and normal acceleration. [7]
  24. 4(a) A particle describes a catenary under a force which acts parallel to the axis. Find the law of force and the velocity at any point of the path. [5]
  25. 4(b) Show that a rectangular hyperbola can be described by a particle under a force parallel to an asymptote which varies as the cube of the distance from the other asymptote. [5]
  26. 5(a) Prove that the central orbit is a plane curve. [3]
  27. 5(b) A boat, which is rowed with constant velocity $u$, starts from a point A on the bank of a river which flows with a constant velocity $v$ and it points always towards a point B on the other bank exactly opposite to A. Find the equation of the path of the boat. If $u=v$, then show that the path is a parabola whose focus is B. [7]
  28. 6(a) Define areal velocity. [3]
  29. 6(b) If a particle describes a circle of radius $a$ under a force from a point at distance $c(<a)$ from the centre, then show that the force varies as \[r\,(r^2+a^2-c^2)^{-3}.\] \renewcommand{\paperfull}{60} [7]
