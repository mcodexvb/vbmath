BMC-41

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2017
  1. 1 Answer \emph{any ten} questions: [10x1=10]
  2. 1(a) Evaluate $\int_0^\infty x^4e^{-x}dx$, if it converges.
  3. 1(b) Find the area in the first quadrant included between the parabola $x^2=4y$, the $y$-axis and the line $y=25$.
  4. 1(c) Find the value of $\lambda(2)-\mu(2,1)$, where the tripple integral over the region bounded by the planes $2x+y+z=4$, $x=0$, $y=0$ and $z=0$ is given by $\int_0^2\int_0^{\lambda(x)}\int_0^{\mu(x,y)}dz\,dy\,dx$.
  5. 1(d) Change the order of the integration in the integral $\int_0^{\pi/2}dx\int_0^{\cos x}x^2dy$.
  6. 1(e) Find the condition for which the improper integral $\int_5^\infty x^\mu dx$ is convergent.
  7. 1(f) Find the value of the integration $\int_0^1 x^4(1-x)^3dx$.
  8. 1(g) Find the value of $y(\pi)$, where $y(x)=\frac{d}{dx}\int_{\sin^2 x}^{2\sin x}e^{t^2}dt$.
  9. 1(h) Is the function $f(x)=\begin{cases}x\sin\frac{\pi}{x};&x\in(0,1]\\0;&x=0\end{cases}$, a function of bounded variation on $[0,1]$.
  10. 1(i) Give an example of a bounded function for which lower and upper integral for a partition may not be equal. Justify your answer.
  11. 1(j) Use Second mean value theorem to show that $\left|\int_a^b\frac{\sin x}{x}dx\right|<\frac4a$.
  12. 1(k) For $K^2>1$, show that $\int_0^1\frac{dx}{\sqrt{(1-x^2)(1-K^2x^2)}}\ge\frac\pi6$.
  13. 1(l) Construct a function $\phi(x)$ such that $\int_1^3[x]dx=\phi(3)-\phi(1)$.
  14. 1(m) Define $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ by $f(x)\begin{cases}1/x&\text{if }x>0\\1&\text{if }x=0.\end{cases}$ Examine if $(R)\int_0^1 f\,dx$ exists.
  15. 1(n) Give an example to show that the Riemann integral of a discontinuous function need not exist. Justify your answer.
  16. 2(a) By using the transformation $x=r\cos\theta$, $y=r\sin\theta$, show that \[\iint_E\frac{dx\,dy}{(1+x^2+y^2)^2}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\tan^{-1}\left(\frac12\right),\] where $E$ is the triangle with vertices at $(0,0)$, $(2,0)$ and $(1,\sqrt3)$. [4]
  17. 2(b) If $f(x,\alpha)$ and $\frac{\partial f}{\partial\alpha}(x,\alpha)$ are continuous functions of $x$ and $\alpha$ for $a\le x\le b$, $c\le\alpha\le d$, $a,b$ being independent of $\alpha$, then prove that \[\frac{d}{d\alpha}\int_a^b f(x,\alpha)dx=\int_a^b\frac{\partial f}{\partial\alpha}(x,\alpha)dx.\] Using the above result, evaluate $\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{(a^2\cos^2x+b^2\sin^2x)^2}$. [2+4]
  18. 3(a) Evaluate $\iint_R\sqrt{|x^2-2y|}\,dx\,dy$, where $R:[-2,2;0,2]$. [5]
  19. 3(b) Prove that $\iiint_V(lx+my+nz)^2dx\,dy\,dz=\frac{4\pi}{15}(l^2+m^2+n^2)$, where $V$ is taken throughout the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2=1$. [5]
  20. 4(a) Applying Dirichlet's test, show that $\int_0^\infty\cos(x^2)dx$ is convergent. [3]
  21. 4(b) Express $\int_0^1 x^m(1-x^n)^pdx$ as a Beta function and $\int_0^1\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^n}}$ as a Gamma function. [2]
  22. 4(c) Let $\alpha$ be a monotonic increasing function on $[a,b]$ and is continuous at $c$ where $a\le c\le b$. Let $f$ be a function be such that $f(c)=1$, and $f(x)=0$ for $x\ne c$. Prove that $f$ is Riemann-Stieltjes integrable and (RS) $\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=0$. [3]
  23. 4(d) Show that $\int_\pi^{2\pi}\sin x\,d(\cos x)=-\frac{\pi}{2}$. [2]
  24. 5(a) A function $f$ is bounded and integrable over $[a,b]$. If $\int_a^b[f(x)]^2dx=0$, then prove that $f(x)=0$ at all points of continuity of $f$. [4]
  25. 5(b) If $f$ is Riemann integrable over $[a,b]$ and continuous at $c$, $a<c<b$, then prove that $\int_a^x f(t)dt$; $a\le x\le b$, is differentiable at $x=c$. [4]
  26. 5(c) Show that the function $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}x^2\cos\frac1x;&\text{if }x\ne0\\0;&\text{if }x=0,\end{cases}$ is a function of bounded variation on $[0,1]$. [2]
  27. 6(a) Show that if $f(x)$ be monotone on $[a,b]$, $\int_a^b fdx$ lies in between $f(a)(b-a)$ and $f(b)(b-a)$. [3]
  28. 6(b) Let $f(x)$ be Riemann integrable and nonnegative on $[a,b]$. If $f(x)$ be continuous at $c$ on $[a,b]$ and $f(c)>0$, then prove that $\int_a^b f(x)dx>0$. [3]
  29. 6(c) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\int_0^x\sin t\,dt}{x^2}$, it it exists. [2]
  30. 6(d) Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\ne\frac1n\\1&\text{if }x=\frac1n;\quad\text{for }n=1,2,\ldots.\end{cases}$ Is $f$ Riemann integrable on $[0,1]$? Give reasons. [2]
