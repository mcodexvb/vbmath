  1. 1 The dimension of the vector space $V = \{A = (a_{ij})_{n \times n} : a_{ij} \in \mathbb{C},\ a_{ij} = -a_{ji}\}$ over the field $\mathbb{R}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $n^2$ \item $n^2 - 1$ \item $n^2 - n$ \item $\dfrac{n^2}{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 The minimal polynomial associated with the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $x^3 - x^2 - 2x - 3$ \item $x^3 - x^2 + 2x - 3$ \item $x^3 - x^2 - 3x - 3$ \item $x^3 - x^2 + 3x - 3$ \end{enumerate}

  5. 5 For what values of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ is the quadrature formula $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx \approx \alpha f(-1) + f(\beta)$ exact for all polynomials of degree $\le 1$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item $\alpha = 1,\ \beta = 1$ \item $\alpha = -1,\ \beta = 1$ \item $\alpha = 1,\ \beta = -1$ \item $\alpha = -1,\ \beta = -1$ \end{enumerate}

  10. 10 Consider the metrics $d_2(f, g) = \left(\displaystyle\int_a^b |f(t) - g(t)|^2\,dt\right)^{1/2}$ and $d_\infty(f, g) = \sup_{t \in [a, b]} |f(t) - g(t)|$ on the space $X = C[a, b]$ of all real-valued continuous functions on $[a, b]$.
