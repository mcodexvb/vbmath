MMC-41

Differential Geometry
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2016
  1. 1(a) Interpret the meaning of covariant derivative of components of vectors in a vector field in terms of partial derivatives of the vector field. [5]
  2. 1(b) Using the Serret-Frenet's formulas find the curvature and torsion of the curve whose equations in cylindrical coordinates are $x^1=a,x^2=\theta(s),x^3=0.$ [5]
  3. 2(a) Define parallelism of a vector field along a curve in $E_3$ and find a necessary and sufficient condition for its parallelism. Deduce equation of a straight line in curvilinear coordinate system. [1+3+2]
  4. 2(b) For a space $V_2$, prove that $gR_{ij}=-g_{ij}R_{1212}$ and $gR=-2R_{1212}$.\par Hence deduce that $V_2$ is an Einstein space. [4]
  5. 3(a) State and prove Bianchi's identity. Also deduce the tensor equation $G^i_{j,i}=0$, where $G^i_j$ is the Einstein tensor. [3+2]
  6. 3(b) Calculate the Gaussian curvature of the surface whose quadratic form is \[ds^2=c^2\sin^2u^1\left(du^2\right)^2+c^2\left(du^1\right)^2\] where $c$ is a constant and $u^1,u^2$ are surface coordinates. [5]
  7. 4(a) What is geodesic curvature? Prove that a curve on a surface is geodesic iff its goedesic curvature is zero. [2+2]
  8. 4(b) Prove that the parametric curves on a surface are geodesic iff $\left\{\begin{matrix}2\\11\end{matrix}\right\}=0$ and $\left\{\begin{matrix}1\\22\end{matrix}\right\}=0.$ [6]
  9. 5(a) Deduce equations of Gauss and Codazzi.
  10. 5(b) For a space curve C on a surface S, deduce the formula $\kappa_g=\kappa\sin\theta$, where $\kappa_g$ is the geodesic curvature, $\kappa$ is the curvature of C and $\theta$ is the angle between the normal to the surface and the principal normal. [4]
  11. 6(a) Show that if $\kappa_{(1)}$ and $\kappa_{(2)}$ be two principal curvatures then \[\kappa_{(1)}+\kappa_{(2)}=2H\] and \quad $\kappa_{(1)}\kappa_{(2)}=K$\par Also deduce that principal directions are orthogonal to each other. [5+2]
  12. 6(b) Show that the normal curvatures in the directions of coordinate curves are $\dfrac{b_{11}}{a_{11}}$ and $\dfrac{b_{22}}{a_{22}}$. [3]
