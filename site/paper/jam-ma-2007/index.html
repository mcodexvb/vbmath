IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2007

  1. Let $A(t)$ denote the area bounded by the curve $y=e^{-|x|}$, the $x$-axis and the straight lines $x=-t$ and $x=t$. Then $\lim _{t \rightarrow \infty} A(t)$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 2 \item 1 \item $1 / 2$ \item 0 \end{enumerate}
  2. If $k$ is a constant such that $x y+k=e^{(x-1)^{2} / 2}$ satisfies the differential equation $x \frac{d y}{d x}=\left(x^{2}-x-1\right) y+(x-1)$, then $k$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 1 \item 0 \item $-1$ \item $-2$ \end{enumerate}
  3. Which of the following functions is uniformly continuous on the domain as stated? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(x)=x^{2}, \quad x \in \mathbf{R}$ \item $f(x)=\frac{1}{x}, \quad x \in[1, \infty)$ \item $f(x)=\tan x, \quad x \in(-\pi / 2, \pi / 2)$ \item $f(x)=[x], \quad x \in[0,1]$ \end{enumerate} ( $[x]$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$ )
  4. Let $R$ be the ring of polynomials over $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$ and let $I$ be the ideal of $R$ generated by the polynomial $x^{3}+x+1$. Then the number of elements in the quotient ring $R / I$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 2 \item 4 \item 8 \item 16 \end{enumerate}
  5. Which of the following sets is a basis for the subspace $W=\left\{\left[\begin{array}{ll}x & y \\ 0 & t\end{array}\right]: x+2 y+t=0, y+t=0\right\}$ of the vector space of all real $2 \times 2$ matrices? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\left\{\left[\begin{array}{ll}1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{ll}0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{ll}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{array}\right]\right\}$ \item $\left\{\left[\begin{array}{cc}2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{cc}1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1\end{array}\right]\right\}$ \item $\left\{\left[\begin{array}{cc}-1 & 1 \\ 2 & -1\end{array}\right]\right\}$ \item $\left\{\left[\begin{array}{cc}1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1\end{array}\right]\right\}$ \end{enumerate}
  6. Let $G$ be an Abelian group of order $10$. Let $S=\left\{g \in G: g^{-1}=g\right\}$. Then the number of non-identity elements in $S$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 5 \item 2 \item 1 \item 0 \end{enumerate}
  7. Let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ be an increasing sequence of positive real numbers such that the series $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_{k}$ is divergent. Let $s_{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{k}$ for $n=1,2, \ldots$ and $t_{n}=\sum_{k=2}^{n} \frac{a_{k}}{s_{k-1} s_{k}}$ for $n=2,3, \ldots$. Then $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} t_{n}$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1 / a_{1}$ \item 0 \item $1 /\left(a_{1}+a_{2}\right)$ \item $a_{1}+a_{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  8. For every function $f:[0,1] \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ which is twice differentiable and satisfies $f^{\prime}(x) \geq 1$ for all $x \in[0,1]$, we must have \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f^{\prime \prime}(x) \geq 0$ for all $x \in[0,1]$ \item $f(x) \geq x$ for all $x \in[0,1]$ \item $f\left(x_{2}\right)-x_{2} \leq f\left(x_{1}\right)-x_{1}$ for all $x_{1}, x_{2} \in[0,1]$ with $x_{2} \geq x_{1}$ \item $f\left(x_{2}\right)-x_{2} \geq f\left(x_{1}\right)-x_{1}$ for all $x_{1}, x_{2} \in[0,1]$ with $x_{2} \geq x_{1}$ \end{enumerate}
  9. Let $f: \mathbf{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ be defined by $$ f(x, y)=\left\{\begin{array}{cc} \frac{x^{2} y}{x^{4}+y^{2}} & \text { if }(x, y) \neq(0,0) \\ 0 & \text { if }(x, y)=(0,0) \end{array}\right. $$ Which of the following statements holds regarding the continuity and the existence of partial derivatives of $f$ at $(0,0)$ ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Both partial derivatives of $f$ exist at $(0,0)$ and $f$ is continuous at $(0,0)$ \item Both partial derivatives of $f$ exist at $(0,0)$ and $f$ is NOT continuous at $(0,0)$ \item One partial derivative of $f$ does NOT exist at $(0,0)$ and $f$ is continuous at $(0,0)$ \item One partial derivative of $f$ does NOT exist at $(0,0)$ and $f$ is NOT continuous at $(0,0)$ \end{enumerate}
  10. Suppose $\left(c_{n}\right)$ is a sequence of real numbers such that $\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left|c_{n}\right|^{1 / n}$ exists and is non-zero. If the radius of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_{n} x^{n}$ is equal to $r$, then the radius of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^{2} c_{n} x^{n}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item less than $r$ \item greater than $r$ \item equal to $r$ \item equal to 0 \end{enumerate}
  11. The rank of the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1 & 4 & 8 \\ 2 & 10 & 22 \\ 0 & 4 & 12\end{array}\right]$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 3 \item 2 \item 1 \item 0 \end{enumerate}
  12. Let $f: \mathbf{R} \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ be a continuous function. If $\int_{0}^{x} f(2 t) d t=\frac{x}{\pi} \sin (\pi x)$ for all $x \in \mathbf{R}$, then $f(2)$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-1$ \item 0 \item 1 \item 2 \end{enumerate}
  13. Let $\vec{u}=\left(a e^{x} \sin y-4 x\right) \hat{\mathrm{i}}+\left(2 y+e^{x} \cos y\right) \hat{\mathrm{j}}+\alpha z \hat{\mathrm{k}}$, where $a$ is a constant. If the line integral $\oint_{C} \vec{u} \cdot d \vec{r}$ over every closed curve $C$ is zero, then $a$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-2$ \item $-1$ \item $0$ \item $1$ \end{enumerate}
  14. One of the integrating factors of the differential equation $\left(y^{2}-3 x y\right) d x+\left(x^{2}-x y\right) d y=0$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1 /\left(x^{2} y^{2}\right)$ \item $1 /\left(x^{2} y\right)$ \item $1 /\left(x y^{2}\right)$ \item $1 /(x y)$ \end{enumerate}
  15. Let $C$ denote the boundary of the semi-circular disk $D=\left\{(x, y) \in \mathbf{R}^{2}: x^{2}+y^{2} \leq 1, y \geq 0\right\}$ and let $\varphi(x, y)=x^{2}+y$ for $(x, y) \in D$. If $\hat{n}$ is the outward unit normal to $C$, then the integral $\oint_{C}(\vec{\nabla} \varphi) \cdot \hat{n} d s$, evaluated counter-clockwise over $C$, is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item $\pi-2$ \item $\pi$ \item $\pi+2$ \end{enumerate}
  16. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $M=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1+i & 2 i & i+3 \\ 0 & 1-i & 3 i \\ 0 & 0 & i\end{array}\right]$. Determine the eigenvalues of the matrix $B=M^{2}-2 M+I$ \item Let $N$ be a square matrix of order 2 . If the determinant of $N$ is equal to 9 and the sum of the diagonal entries of $N$ is equal to $10$ , then determine the eigenvalues of $N$. \end{enumerate}
  17. \begin{enumerate} \item Using the method of variation of parameters, solve the differential equation $$ x^{2} \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}+x \frac{d y}{d x}-y=x^{2}, $$ given that $x$ and $\frac{1}{x}$ are two solutions of the corresponding homogeneous equation. \item Find the real number $\alpha$ such that the differential equation $$ \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}+2(\alpha-1)(\alpha-3) \frac{d y}{d x}+(\alpha-2) y=0 $$ has a solution $y(x)=a \cos (\beta x)+b \sin (\beta x)$ for some non-zero real numbers $a, b, \beta$. \end{enumerate}
  18. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $a, b, c$ be non-zero real numbers such that $(a-b)^{2}=4 a c$. Solve the differential equation $a(x+\sqrt{2})^{2} \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}+b(x+\sqrt{2}) \frac{d y}{d x}+c y=0$. \item Solve the differential equation $$ d x+\left(e^{y \sin y}-x\right)(y \cos y+\sin y) d y=0 . $$ \end{enumerate}
  19. Let $f(x, y)=x\left(x-2 y^{2}\right)$ for $(x, y) \in \mathbf{R}^{2}$. Show that $f$ has a local minimum at $(0,0)$ on every straight line through $(0,0)$. Is $(0,0)$ a critical point of $f$ ? Find the discriminant of $f$ at $(0,0)$. Does $f$ have a local minimum at $(0,0)$ ? Justify your answers.
  20. \begin{enumerate} \item Find the finite volume enclosed by the paraboloids $z=2-x^{2}-y^{2}$ and $z=x^{2}+y^{2}$. \item Let $f:[0,3] \rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ be a continuous function with $\int_{0}^{3} f(x) d x=3$. Evaluate $$ \int_{0}^{3}\left[x f(x)+\int_{0}^{x} f(t) d t\right] d x $$ \end{enumerate}
  21. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $S$ be the surface $\left\{(x, y, z) \in \mathbf{R}^{3}: x^{2}+y^{2}+2 z=2, z \geq 0\right\}$, and let $\hat{n}$ be the outward unit normal to $S$. If $\vec{F}=y \hat{\mathrm{i}}+x z \hat{\mathrm{j}}+\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right) \hat{\mathrm{k}}$, then evaluate the integral $\iint_{S} \vec{F} \cdot \hat{n} d S$. \item Let $\vec{r}=x \hat{i}+y \hat{j}+z \hat{k}$ and $r=|\vec{r}|$. If a scalar field $\varphi$ and a vector field $\vec{u}$ satisfy $\vec{\nabla} \varphi=\vec{\nabla} \times \vec{u}+f(r) \vec{r}$, where $f$ is an arbitrary differentiable function, then show that $\nabla^{2} \varphi=r f^{\prime}(r)+3 f(r)$ \end{enumerate}
  22. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $D$ be the region bounded by the concentric spheres $S_{1}: x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=a^{2}$ and $S_{2}: x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=b^{2}$, where $a<b$. Let $\hat{n}$ be the unit normal to $S_{1}$ directed away from the origin. If $\nabla^{2} \varphi=0$ in $D$ and $\varphi=0$ on $S_{2}$, then show that $\iiint_{D}|\vec{\nabla} \varphi|^{2} d V+\iint_{S_{1}} \varphi(\vec{\nabla} \varphi) \cdot \hat{n} d S=0$ \item Let $C$ be the curve in $\mathbf{R}^{3}$ given by $x^{2}+y^{2}=a^{2}, z=0$ traced counter-clockwise, and let $\vec{F}=x^{2} y^{3} \hat{\mathrm{i}}+\hat{\mathrm{j}}+z \hat{\mathrm{k}}$. Using Stokes' theorem, evaluate $\oint_{C} \vec{F} \cdot d \vec{r}$. \end{enumerate}
  23. Let $V$ be the subspace of $\mathbf{R}^{4}$ spanned by the vectors $(1,0,1,2),(2,1,3,4)$ and $(3,1,4,6)$. Let $T: V \rightarrow \mathbf{R}^{2}$ be a linear transformation given by $T(x, y, z, t)=(x-y, z-t)$ for all $(x, y, z, t) \in V$. Find a basis for the null space of $T$ and also a basis for the range space of $T$
  24. \begin{enumerate} \item Compute the double integral $\iint_{D}(x+2 y) d x d y$, where $D$ is the region in the $x y$-plane bounded by the straight lines $y=x+3, y=x-3, y=-2 x+4$ and $y=-2 x-2$. \item Evaluate $\int_{0}^{\pi / 2}\left[\int_{\pi / 2}^{\pi} \frac{\sin x}{x} d x\right] d y+\int_{\pi / 2}^{\pi}\left[\int_{y}^{\pi} \frac{\sin x}{x} d x\right] d y$. \end{enumerate}
  25. \begin{enumerate} \item Does the series $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{k} k+x^{k}}{k^{2}}$ converge uniformly for $x \in[-1,1]$ ? Justify. \item Suppose $\left(f_{n}\right)$ is a sequence of real-valued functions defined on $\mathbf{R}$ and $f$ is a real-valued function defined on $\mathbf{R}$ such that $\left|f_{n}(x)-f(x)\right| \leq\left|a_{n}\right|$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and $a_{n} \rightarrow 0$ as $n \rightarrow \infty$. Must the sequence $\left(f_{n}\right)$ be uniformly convergent on $\mathbf{R}$ ? Justify. (6) \end{enumerate}
  26. \begin{enumerate} \item Suppose $f$ is a real-valued thrice differentiable function defined on $\mathbf{R}$ such that $f^{\prime \prime \prime}(x)>0$ for all $x \in \mathbf{R}$. Using Taylor's formula, show that $f\left(x_{2}\right)-f\left(x_{1}\right)>\left(x_{2}-x_{1}\right) f^{\prime}\left(\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}\right)$ for all $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ in $\mathbf{R}$ with $x_{2}>x_{1}$. \item Let $\left(a_{n}\right)$ and $\left(b_{n}\right)$ be sequences of real numbers such that $a_{n} \leq a_{n+1} \leq b_{n+1} \leq b_{n}$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$. Must there exist a real number $x$ such that $a_{n} \leq x \leq b_{n}$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$ ? Justify your answer. \end{enumerate}
  27. Let $G$ be the group of all $2 \times 2$ matrices with real entries with respect to matrix multiplication. Let $G_{1}$ be the smallest subgroup of $G$ containing $\left[\begin{array}{ll}0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{cc}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1\end{array}\right]$, and $G_{2}$ be the smallest subgroup of $G$ containing $\left[\begin{array}{cc}0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right]$ and $\left[\begin{array}{cc}0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0\end{array}\right]$. Determine all elements of $G_{1}$ and find their orders. Determine all elements of $G_{2}$ and find their orders. Does there exist a one-to-one homomorphism from $G_{1}$ onto $G_{2}$ ? Justify.
  28. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $p$ be a prime number and let $\mathbf{Z}$ be the ring of integers. If an ideal $J$ of $\mathbf{Z}$ contains the set $p \mathbf{Z}$ properly, then show that $J=\mathbf{Z}$. (Here $p \mathbf{Z}=\{p x: x \in \mathbf{Z}\}$.) \item Consider the ring $R=\{a+i b: a, b \in \mathbf{Z}\}$ with usual addition and multiplication. Find all invertible elements of $R$. \end{enumerate}
  29. \begin{enumerate} \item Suppose $E$ is a non-empty subset of $\mathbf{R}$ which is bounded above, and let $\alpha=\sup E$. If $E$ is closed, then show that $\alpha \in E$. If $E$ is open, then show that $\alpha \notin E$. \item Find all limit points of the set $E=\left\{n+\frac{1}{2 m}: n, m \in \mathbf{N}\right\}$. \end{enumerate}
