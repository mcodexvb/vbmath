NBHM · 2022 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1 Let $n$ be the smallest positive integer that has $2022$ as a factor and has exactly $2022$ factors (counting both $1$ and $n$ as factors of $n$). Let $d$ be the largest positive integer such that $2^d$ divides $n$. What is $d$?
  2. 2 A box contains $b$ blue balls and $y$ yellow balls. One ball is drawn at random from the box, but is then put back into the box along with $a$ additional balls of the same colour. Another ball is now drawn at random from the box. What is the probability that the first ball drawn was blue, given that the second ball drawn is yellow (in terms of $b, y,$ and $a$)?
  3. 3 The inner surface of a radially symmetric coffee mug is described as follows: with the $x$-, $y$-, and $z$-axes being oriented as usual, consider the portion of the curve $z = x^4$ in the $x$-$z$ plane that is below the horizontal line $z = 1$; then rotate it about the $z$-axis to obtain the surface. Find the volume of the coffee mug.
  4. 4 Consider the surface $S$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$ defined by the equation $z^2 - xy = 0$. What is the number of connected components of its complement (in $\mathbb{R}^3$)?
  5. 5 The complex numbers $a_n$, $n \ge 0$, are defined by the condition that \[ \sum_{n \ge 0} a_n(z - 2i)^n = 1 + 2z + 3z^2 + \dots = \sum_{n \ge 0} (n + 1)z^n \] holds for each $z$ in $\mathbb{C}$ such that $|z| < 1$ and $\Im(z) > 0$. What is $\limsup \sqrt[n]{|a_n|}$?
  6. 6 Find the largest positive real number $r$ such that $|\Im\sin(z)| \le 1$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|z| \le r$. If there is no such largest $r$, write ``infinity''.
  7. 7 Given two subsets $C$ and $S$ of a vector space $V$, their sum is $C + S := \{c + s \mid c \in C,\ s \in S\}$. Take $V = \mathbb{R}^3$, $C := \{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid |x| \le 1,\ |y| \le 1,\ |z| \le 1\}$ and $S := \{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \le 1\}$. Find the volume of their sum $C + S$.
  8. 8 Compute $\displaystyle\iint_A (3x^2 y - y^3)\,dxdy$ where $A$ is the region $\{(x, y) \mid x^2 + (y - 1)^2 \le 1\}$.
  9. 9 An element of the alternating group $A_5$ is picked at random (each element is equally likely to be picked). What is the expected value of its order?
  10. 10 On the set $\mathcal{M}$ of all $m \times n$ real matrices, two matrices $A$ and $B$ are equivalent if there exist a real invertible $m \times m$ matrix $P$ and a real invertible $n \times n$ matrix $Q$ such that $A = PBQ$. Into how many disjoint equivalence classes is $\mathcal{M}$ partitioned by this equivalence relation?
  11. 11 What is the area of the region $\left\{z + \frac{z^2}{2} \;\middle|\; z \in \mathbb{C},\ |z| \le 1\right\}$?
  12. 12 Let $\Gamma := C[-1, 1]$ with the supremum norm. Let $K$ be the kernel of the linear functional $I : f \mapsto \int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx$ on $\Gamma$. Consider the function $f(x) = x^2$ in $\Gamma$. What is its distance from the subspace $K$?
  13. 13 Let $X = \{1, 2, \dots, n\}$ ($n \ge 3$). Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the group of bijections of $X$, acting on $X \times X \times X$ by $\varphi\cdot(i, j, k) := (\varphi(i), \varphi(j), \varphi(k))$. How many orbits does this induced action of $\mathcal{S}$ on $X \times X \times X$ have?
  14. 14 Let $k$ be a field with $8$ elements and $K$ a field with $64$ elements. How many ring homomorphisms are there from $k$ to $K$?
  15. 15 Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the subset of real valued continuous functions $f(x)$ on $[-1, 1]$ such that $f(x) \ge 0$ (for all $x \in [-1, 1]$) and $\limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_{-1}^{1} f(x)^n\,dx < \infty$. What is the image of $\mathcal{C}$ in $\mathbb{R}$ under the map that takes $f(x)$ in $\mathcal{C}$ to $\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx$?
  16. 16 Let $\mathcal{M}$ be the real vector space of all $n \times n$ real matrices with inner product $(A, B) := \operatorname{trace}(AB^t)$. Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the subspace of symmetric $n \times n$ matrices, and $P : \mathcal{M} \to \mathcal{S}$ the orthogonal projection to $\mathcal{S}$. For $X \in \mathcal{M}$, write a formula (in terms of $X$) for $P(X)$.
  17. 17 Let $\mathcal{H} := \{a + bi \in \mathbb{C} \mid b > 0\}$ be the open upper half plane. Let $\varphi$ be an analytic function on $\mathcal{H}$ such that $\varphi(\mathcal{H}) \subseteq \mathcal{H}$, $\varphi(i) = i$, and $\varphi(2i) = i/2$. What is $|\varphi(1 + i)|$? (Write ``indeterminable'' if not enough information.)
  18. 18 Let $A$ be the abelian group $\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{7\mathbb{Z}} \oplus \frac{\mathbb{Z}}{49\mathbb{Z}}$. How many subgroups of order $7$ does $A$ have?
  19. 19 Let $\mathcal{M}$ be the algebra of all $3 \times 3$ complex matrices. For $A \in \mathcal{M}$, let $\Lambda_A : \mathcal{M} \to \mathcal{M}$ be defined by $X \mapsto AX - XA$. What are the possible values of the rank of $\Lambda_A$, as $A$ varies over all of $\mathcal{M}$?
  20. 20 Consider the topology on $\mathbb{C}$ where a subset $Z$ of $\mathbb{C}$ is closed iff $Z = \{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid f(z) = 0\}$ for some polynomial $f(x) \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$. Write respectively the cardinalities of the closures of the three singleton sets: $\{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}\}$, $\{e^{2\pi i/15}\}$, $\{\pi\}$.
  21. 21 What is the number of conjugacy classes of cyclic subgroups of order $15$ in the symmetric group $S_{15}$?
  22. 22 Let $f(x) := \sin(\pi x/2)$ on $\mathbb{R}$. For $y \in \mathbb{R}$, define $x_0(y) := y$ and $x_{n+1}(y) = f(x_n(y))$ for $n \ge 1$, and $g(y) := \lim_{n \to \infty} x_n(y)$. Find $\int_0^3 g(y)\,dy$.
  23. 23 Let $y(t)$ be a smooth real function on an open interval containing $[0, 1]$ satisfying $y''(t) + w(t)y(t) = \lambda y(t)$, with $y(t) > 0$ on $[0, 1]$ and $y'(0) = y'(1) = 0$. Given that $\int_0^1 \left(\frac{y'}{y}\right)^2 dt = 10$ and $\int_0^1 w(t)\,dt = 20$, what is $\lambda$?
  24. 24 Let $u(x)$ be a real valued continuous function on $[0, 1]$ such that $u(\frac{1}{2}) = 1$, $u(x) \ge 0$ and $u(x) \le \int_0^x u(t)\,dt$ for all $x \in [0, 1]$. What is $u(1)$? (Write ``indeterminable'' or ``no such $u$'' if applicable.)
  25. 25 A smooth positive function $y = f(x)$ on $x > 0$ has the property that for any point $(x_0, y_0)$ on its graph $C$, $(x_0, y_0)$ is the mid-point of the segment $L(x_0, y_0)$ of the tangent line to $C$ at $(x_0, y_0)$ that lies in the first quadrant. Given that $f(1) = 2$, what is $f(2022)$?
  26. 26 Suppose $A$ is an $n \times n$ complex matrix ($n \ge 2$) such that every matrix that commutes with $A$ is a polynomial in $A$.\\ (a) Such a matrix $A$ is necessarily diagonalizable.\\ (b) Such a matrix $A$ cannot be nilpotent.\\ (c) The characteristic and minimal polynomials coincide for every such $A$.
  27. 27 Let $V = \{v_1, \dots, v_7\}$ be a set with $7$ elements. Which of the following tuples can occur as valency tuples $(p_1, \dots, p_7)$ of a simple graph structure on $V$? (True/False)\\ (a) $(2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 3, 3)$ \qquad (b) $(2, 2, 4, 6, 4, 2, 2)$ \qquad (c) $(2, 6, 4, 6, 2, 6, 4)$
  28. 28 Let $f(z)$ be analytic on an open set containing the closed unit disk $D := \{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid |z| \le 1\}$. Let $m = \min_{z \in D} |f(z)|$ and $M = \min_{z \in C} |f(z)|$, where $C = \{|z| = 1\}$. Assume $m < M$. Then:\\ (a) $f(z)$ admits a zero on $D$. \qquad (b) $f(z)$ attains on $D$ every complex value $w$ such that $|w| < M$.
  29. 29 Let $S = \mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, y) \mid y \neq 0\}$ with subspace topology $\tau_1$. Let $\pi : \mathbb{R}^2 \to S$ be defined by $(x, y) \mapsto (x, y)$ for $x \neq 0$ and $(0, y) \mapsto (0, 0)$ for all $y$, and let $\tau_2$ be the quotient topology on $S$. Then: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The topologies $\tau_1$ and $\tau_2$ are not comparable. \item $\tau_2$ is regular. \item $\tau_2$ admits a countable basis. \item $\tau_2$ is metrizable. \item Any subset of $S$ that is compact with respect to $\tau_2$ is also compact with respect to $\tau_1$. \end{enumerate}
  30. 30 Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a smooth, bounded function such that $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x)$ and $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(-x)$ exist. Then:\\ (a) The function $f$ is uniformly continuous. \qquad (b) The derivative $f'$ is uniformly continuous.
  31. 31 Let $A$ be an arbitrary special orthogonal $3 \times 3$ real matrix ($AA^t = A^t A = I$, $\det A = 1$). Then: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $A$ admits $1$ as an eigenvalue. \item If all the eigenvalues of $A$ are real, then $A$ is the identity $3 \times 3$ matrix. \item There is a complex invertible matrix $P$ such that $PAP^{-1}$ is diagonal. \end{enumerate}
  32. 32 Consider a sequence of i.i.d.\ random variables $\{X_n\}_{n \ge 1}$ taking values in $\{1, -1\}$ and satisfying $\lim_{n \to \infty} X_n = X$ almost surely, for some random variable $X$. Then:\\ (a) $X$ is a constant, almost surely. \qquad (b) $X_1$ is a constant, almost surely. \qquad (c) $X$ is distributed as $X_1$.
  33. 33 Let $\mathbb{R}'$ be $\mathbb{R}$ with the topology having $\{[a, \infty) \mid a \in \mathbb{R}\}$ as a basis. Consider $\mathbb{R}' \times \mathbb{R}'$ with product topology, $S = \{(x, \sin(x)) \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\}$ with subspace topology, and $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to S$ defined by $\varphi(x) := (x, \sin x)$. Then:\\ (a) The image of $(\pi/2, \pi)$ under $\varphi$ is closed in $S$. \qquad (b) The image of $(0, \pi/2)$ under $\varphi$ is closed in $S$.
  34. 34 Let $d, e$ be positive integers and $T : \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}^e$ a linear transformation. Then: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $T$ is an open map if and only if $T$ is surjective. \item $T$ is a closed map if and only if either $T = 0$ or $T$ is injective. \end{enumerate}
