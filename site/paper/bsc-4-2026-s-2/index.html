MJMA-8

Analysis-IV
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2026
  1. 1(a) Define pointwise convergence and uniform convergence of a sequence of functions $f_n$ on a set $E$. [[3]]
  2. 1(b) Let $f_n(x)=\frac{nx}{1+n^2x^2}$, $0\le x\le1$. Find the pointwise limit of $\{f_n\}$ on $[0,1]$. Show that the convergence is not uniform on $[0,1]$, but is uniform on $[a,1]$, where $0<a<1$ [[4]]
  3. 1(c) Give an example of a sequence of continuous functions on $[0,1]$ which converges pointwise to a discontinuous function. Justify your answer. [[3]]
  4. 2(a) Suppose $\{f_n\}$ is a sequence of Riemann-integrable functions on $[a,b]$ and $f_n\to f$ uniformly on $[a,b]$. Prove that $f$ is Riemann-integrable on $[a,b]$ and \[\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^b f_n(x)dx=\int_a^b f(x)dx.\] [[6]]
  5. 2(b) Let $\{r_i\}$ be the listing of rational numbers in $[0,1]$. Consider the sequence $\{f_n\}$, $f_n(x)=1$, if $x=r_1,r_2,\ldots,r_n$; $=0$, otherwise. Show that each $f_n$ is Riemann-integrable but the limit function is not. Is the convergence of $\{f_n\}$ uniform? [[4]]
  6. 3(a) State and prove the Weierstrass M-test for uniform convergence of a series of functions. [[2+4]]
  7. 3(b) Discuss the pointwise convergence and uniform convergence of the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}e^{nx}\sin(nx)$ on $(-\infty,0]$. Also examine its uniform convergence on $(-\infty,-a]$, where $a>0$. [[4]]
  8. 4(a) Define the radius of convergence and the interval of convergence of a power series. [[2]]
  9. 4(b) State the Cauchy--Hadamard theorem for the radius of convergence of a power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nx^n$. [[2]]
  10. 4(c) Find the interval of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}\frac{(x-3)^n}{n5^n}$, including the end-points. [[4]]
  11. 4(d) If a power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nx^n$ has radius of convergence $R>0$, show that it converges uniformly on $[-r,r]$ for every $0<r<R$. [[2]]
  12. 5(a) For $n\in\mathbb N$, define $f_n(x)=nx(1-x)^n$, $0\le x\le1$. Find the pointwise limit of $\{f_n\}$ on $[0,1]$ and determine whether the convergence is uniform. [[5]]
  13. 5(b) If $\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}f(x,y)=l$ and $\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}g(x,y)=m$, then prove that $\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}[f(x,y)g(x,y)]=lm$. [[5]]
  14. 6(a) Prove that limit of a function of two variables if exists, is unique. [[3]]
  15. 6(b) If $\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}f(x,y)=\lambda<0$, then show that there is a deleted neighbourhood of $(a,b)$ in which $f(x,y)<0$. [[3]]
  16. 6(c) Using $\epsilon-\delta$ definition, show that $\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{xy}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}=0$. [[4]]
  17. 7(a) Prove that if $f(x,y)$ is continuous at a point $(a,b)$ then so is $|f(x,y)|$. Show by an example the converse may not be true. [[2+2]]
  18. 7(b) If $f(x,y)$ is continuous at a point $(\alpha,\beta)$ then show that the function $f(\alpha,y)$ of the single variable $y$ is continuous at $y=\beta$. [[2]]
  19. 7(c) State and prove a sufficient condition for continuity of a function $f(x,y)$ at a point $(a,b)$. [[4]]
  20. 8(a) Give an example of a function of two variables for which $f_x(a,b)$ and $f_y(a,b)$ both exist but $f(x,y)$ is not continuous at $(a,b)$. [[3]]
  21. 8(b) If $f(x,y)=\begin{cases}xy&\text{for }|x|\ge|y|\\-xy&\text{for }|x|<|y|,\end{cases}$ then show that $f_{xy}(0,0)\ne f_{yx}(0,0)$. [[5]]
  22. 8(c) When a function $f(x,y)$ is said to be differentiable at a point $(a,b)$? [[2]]
  23. 9(a) State Euler's theorem for a homogeneous function of degree $n$. If $f(x,y)$ is homogeneous function of degree $n$ and if $f(x,y)$ possesses continuous partial derivatives upto second order then show that \[x^2\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x^2}+2xy\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x\partial y}+y^2\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y^2}=n(n-1)f.\] [[1+4]]
  24. 9(b) State and prove Schwarz's theorem for equality of two mixed second order partial derivatives of a function $f(x,y)$ at a point $(a,b)$. [[1+4]]
  25. 10(a) Let $z_1$ and $z_2$ be differentiable functions of $y_1$ and $y_2$, which are themselves differentiable functions of $x_1$ and $x_2$. Then show that $\frac{\delta(z_1,z_2)}{\delta(x_1,x_2)}=\frac{\delta(z_1,z_2)}{\delta(y_1,y_2)}\cdot\frac{\delta(y_1,y_2)}{\delta(x_1,x_2)}$. [[4]]
  26. 10(b) Apply Mean Value Theorem to $f(x,y)=\sin\pi x+\cos\pi y$ and express $f(\frac12,0)-f(-\frac12,0)$ in terms of first order partial derivatives of $f$ and deduce that there exists $\theta$, where $0<\theta<1$, such that $\frac4\pi=\cos\frac\pi2\theta+\sin\frac\pi2(1-\theta)$. [[6]]
  27. 11(a) State Implicit function theorem. Show that $F(x,y)=x^2+2xe^y+y=0$ determines a unique function $y=f(x)$ about $(0,0)$. [[2+2]]
  28. 11(b) State and prove Taylor's theorem for a function of two variables. [[2+4]]
  29. 12(a) Show that for the function $f(x,y)=x^2-2xy+y^2+x^3-y^3+x^5$, $f(0,0)$ is neither a maximum value nor a minimum value. [[3]]
  30. 12(b) Using Lagrange method find the rectangle of perimeter $s$ which has maximum area. [[4]]
  31. 12(c) Evaluate $\iint_E xy(x+y)dxdy$ over $E$ bounded by $y=x$ and $y=x^2$. [[3]]
