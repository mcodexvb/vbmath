CC-6

Algebra-III
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2022
  1. 1 Answer any two questions.\((10\times2=20)\)
  2. 1(a) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item How many distinct \(r\)-cycles are there in a symmetric group \(S_n\)? \item Find the order of the permutation \((1\ 2\ 3\ 4)\circ(5\ 6)\) in the symmetric group \(S_6\). \item Find a conjugate element of \((2\ 3\ 6\ 8)\) other than itself in \(S_8\). \item How many elements of order 2 are there in \(S_4\)? \end{enumerate} [2]
  3. 1(b) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Let \(H\) be a subgroup of a finite group \(G\). Prove that \([G:H]=\frac{|G|}{|H|}\). \item Let \(\mathbb C^*\) be the group of nonzero complex numbers under multiplication and \(H=\{a+bi\in\mathbb C^*:a^2+b^2=1\}\) be a subgroup of \(\mathbb C^*\). Give a geometric description of the coset \((3+4i)H\). \item State Cauchy's Theorem on finite groups. Let \(H\) and \(K\) be two subgroups of an Abelian group. If \(|H|=12\) and \(|K|=18\) then prove that \(H\cap K\) is cyclic. \end{enumerate}
  4. 1(c) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Write down the Caley table for the quotient group \(\frac{\mathbb Z_{18}}{\langle[6]\rangle}\). Hence find the order of \([13]+\langle[6]\rangle\) in \(\frac{\mathbb Z_{18}}{\langle[6]\rangle}\). \item Let \(H\) be a proper subgroup of a group \(G\) such that for all \(x,y\in G\setminus H,\ xy\in H\). Show that \(H\) is normal in \(G\). \item Show that \(A_4\) is the only subgroup of \(S_4\) of order 12. \end{enumerate} [3]
  5. 2 Answer any two questions.\((10\times2=20)\)
  6. 2(a) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Prove that a finite ring without zero divisor contains an unity. \item Give an example of a non commutative ring having exactly 81 elements. Justify your answer. \item Find all units (if exists) of the ring \(S=\{\begin{pmatrix}a&a\\a&a\end{pmatrix}\mid a\in\mathbb Q\}\). \end{enumerate} [3]
  7. 2(b) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Prove that \(\mathbb Z_n\) is a field if and only if \(n\) is prime. \item Give an example of an infinite ring with characteristic 2. Justify your answer. \item Let \((F,+,\cdot)\) be a field of characteristic \(p\), where \(p\) is prime. Show that \(|F|=p^m\) for some \(m\in\mathbb N\). \end{enumerate}
  8. 2(c) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Show that a commutative ring with unity is a field if and only if it has no proper ideals. \item Give an example of subring \(S\) of a ring \(R\) such that \(S\) contains an unity but \(R\) does not contains an unity. Justify your answer. \item Let \(R\) is a Boolean ring. Then find the value of \((a+b)ab\) for \(a,b\in R\). \end{enumerate} [3]
  9. 3 Answer any two questions.\((10\times2=20)\)
  10. 3(a) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Let \(V=\{(x,y):x,y\in F\}\), where \(F\) is a field. Define addition of elements of \(V\) coordinatewise, and for \(c\in F\) and \((x,y)\in V\), define \(c(x,y)=(x,0)\). Is \(V\) a vector space over \(F\) with these operations? Justify your answer. \item Determine whether \(u=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1\\2&2&2\end{pmatrix}\) and \(v=\begin{pmatrix}2&2&2\\3&3&3\end{pmatrix}\) are linearly dependent or not. Find a subset \(S\) of \(\mathbb R\) which is linear dependent in the vector space \(\mathbb R_{\mathbb R}\) but linear independent in the vector space \(\mathbb R_{\mathbb Q}\). \item Find two subspaces \(X\) and \(Y\) of \(\mathbb R^3\) such that \(\mathbb R^3=X+Y\) but \(\mathbb R^3\ne X\oplus Y\). \end{enumerate} [3]
  11. 3(b) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Show that every finitely generated vector space has a basis. \item Prove that \(\mathbb R_{\mathbb Q}\) is not a finite dimensional vector space \item Extend the set \(\{(-2,1,1,0),(-1,2,0,1)\}\) to a basis of \(\mathbb R^4\). \end{enumerate} [3]
  12. 3(c) \begin{enumerate}\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Show that for each subspace \(W\) of a vector space \(V_F\) there is a subspace \(W_1\) of \(V_F\) such that \(V=W\oplus W_1\). \item Find the dimension of the quotient space \(p_{10}(t)/p_5(t)\), where \(p_n(t)\) denotes the vector space of all polynomials of degree less or equal to \(n\) over \(\mathbb R\). \item Find \(\left\|\begin{pmatrix}i&1\\2+i&i\end{pmatrix}\right\|\) in the inner product space \(M_{2\times2}(C)\), with the inner product defined by \(\langle A,B\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(B^*A)\) for \(A,B\in M_{2\times2}(C)\). \end{enumerate} \renewcommand{\paperfull}{60} [3]
