MMO-31(A5)

Dynamics of Ecological System-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1 A population of fish follows logistic growth given by\par \[\frac{dN}{dt}=rN\left(1-\frac NK\right),\] \[N(t=0)=N_0>0,\] where $r$ is the intrinsic growth rate and $K$ is the carrying capacity. Also, given $N(t_1)=K_1$, $N(t_1+T)=K_2$ and $N(t_1+2T)=K_3$, show that the carrying capacity is given by $K=\dfrac{\frac1{K_1}-\frac2{K_2}+\frac1{K_3}}{\frac1{K_1K_3}-\frac1{K_2^2}}$. What is your prediction on the model dynamics in view of the carrying capacity $K$? [8+2]
  2. 2 What do you mean by functional response in ecological sense? Write down the mathematical expressions with ecological examples of Holling type-I, type-II, type-III, and type-IV functional responses. Explain the derivation for Holling type-II functional response. [2+4+4]
  3. 3 Consider the following Leslie-Gower interacting species model with Holling type II scheme:\par \[\frac{du}{dt}=u(1-u)-\frac{uv}{u+\alpha},\] \[\frac{dv}{dt}=\beta v\left(1-\frac{\gamma v}{u+\delta}\right),\] \[u(t=0)\ge0,\ v(t=0)\ge0,\] where all the system parameters are positive. Construct a Lyapunov function for which the co-existence equilibrium point is globally asymptotically stable (GAS) in $\mathbf R=\{(u,v):0\le u\le1,0\le v\le\frac{1+\delta}\gamma\}$. [10]
  4. 4(a) What do you mean by prey refuge in an interacting species system?\par
  5. 4(b) Comment on the dissipativeness property of the system and influence of the refuge parameter $m$ about the co-existence equilibrium point of the following predator-prey model:\par \[\frac{dx}{dt}=rx\left(1-\frac xK\right)-\frac{p(1-m)xy}{(1-m)x+\alpha},\] \[\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{ep(1-m)xy}{(1-m)x+\alpha}-\beta y,\ 0<e<1,0\le m<1,\] \[x(t=0)\ge0,\ y(t=0)\ge0,\] where all the system parameters are positive. [2+8]
  6. 5(a) Explain Kermack-McKendrick SIR model for the spread of infectious disease.\par
  7. 5(b) Consider the SIR model with vital rates, and assume that recovered individuals loose their immunity at rate $\eta$. The system of ODEs is given by\par \[\frac{dS}{dt}=N\mu-\mu S-\beta SI,\] \[\frac{dI}{dt}=\beta SI-\nu I-\mu I,\] \[\frac{dR}{dt}=\nu I-\mu R-\eta R,\] \[S(t=0)>0,\ I(t=0)>0,\ R(t=0)>0.\] Find an expression for $R_0$ (basic reproductive number) and interpret it biologically. Write down your specific observation when $R_0<1$, $R_0=1$ and $R_0>1$.
  8. 6 Consider the following SIS epidemic model with disease-induced mortality $\gamma$:\par \[\frac{dS}{dt}=\Lambda-\mu S-\beta SI+\alpha I,\] \[\frac{dI}{dt}=\beta SI-(\alpha+\gamma+\mu)I,\] \[S(t=0)>0,\ I(t=0)>0,\] where $S$ is the number of susceptibles, $I$ is the number of infected, $\beta$ is the transmission factor, $\alpha$ is the recovery rate, $\Lambda$ is the birth factor, and $\mu$ is the natural death rate.\par (a) Calculate the Jacobian of each equilibrium and determine the stability.\par (b) Use the Dulac criterion to rule out periodic solutions.\par (c) Use the Poincar\'e-Bendixson theorem to show convergence to equilibrium. [4+3+3]
