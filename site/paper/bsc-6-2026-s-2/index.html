MJMA-15

Analysis-VI
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2026
  1. 1(a) If $f$ is an analytic function on a domain $D$ such that the modulus of $f(z)$ is constant for all $z\in D$, then show that $f$ must be constant. [[4]]
  2. 1(b) Find an analytic function $f(z)=u+iv$ on $\mathbb C$ such that $f(i)=1$ and $u(x,y)=x^4+y^4-6x^2y^2-4xy$. [[6]]
  3. 2(a) Examine the validity of Cauchy-Riemann equations at $z=0$ for the function $f(z)=\sqrt{xy}$ and hence check if $f$ is analytic there. [[3+2]]
  4. 2(b) What is orientation principle for Möbius map? Using it, find the image of the interior of the circle $|z+2|=2$ under the map $w(z)=\frac{z}{2z+8}$. [[5]]
  5. 3(a) Evaluate $\int_\Gamma(\bar z\,dz+z\,d\bar z)$ along the curve $\Gamma$ defined by $z^2+2z\bar z+(\bar z)^2=(3+i)z+(3-i)\bar z$ from the point $z=1+i$ to the point $z=2-2i$. [[5]]
  6. 3(b) Find all the Möbius transformations of the unit disc $|z|\le1$ onto the lower half-plane $\mathrm{Im}(w)\le0$. [[5]]
  7. 4(a) Define the inverse points with respect to a circle. Show that a Möbius map takes a circle into a circle and inverse points to inverse points. [[1+4]]
  8. 4(b) Compute the integral of the function $f(z)=\bar z^2$ along the parabola $x=y^2$ from the point $z=0$ to the point $z=1+i$. [[5]]
  9. 5(a) State and prove Liouville's Theorem. [[1+4]]
  10. 5(b) Evaluate $\oint_C\frac{z\,dz}{(z^2+36)(z-3i)}$, where $C$ is the (positively oriented) circle $|z|=5$. [[5]]
  11. 6(a) When is a series $\sum_{z=1}^\infty z_n$ conditionally and absolutely convergent? Examine the series $\sum_{z=1}^\infty\frac{n^22^n}{(1+i)^n}$ for convergence. [[2+3]]
  12. 6(b) State Cauchy-Hadamard Theorem. Find the radius and region of convergence of the power series $\sum_{z=0}^\infty\frac{z^{3n}}{(3+i)^n}$. [[1+4]]
  13. 1(a) Define a metric space. For any four points $x,y,z,w$ in a metric space $(X,\rho)$, prove that $|\rho(x,y)-\rho(z,w)|\le\rho(x,w)+\rho(y,z)$. [[2+3]]
  14. 1(b) Prove that $l_3$ is a metric space. [[5]]
  15. 2(a) Define an open set in a metric space. Let $(X,\rho)$ be a metric space and $G\subset X$. Then show that if $G$ is open then it is the union of open spheres. [[2+3]]
  16. 2(b) Define the closure $\bar F$ of a set $F\subset X$ in a metric space $(X,\rho)$. Prove that the closure of a set $F$ consisting of a single point is the set $F$. [[2+3]]
  17. 3(a) Define a Cauchy sequence in a metric space. Give an example of a Cauchy sequence in a metric space with proper justification. [[2+2]]
  18. 3(b) Show that if a sequence $\{x_n\}$ converges, then it is a Cauchy sequence. Is converse of this true? Justify your answer. [[3+1+2]]
  19. 4(a) Define a complete metric space. Prove that $C[a,b]$ is a complete metric space. [[2+4]]
  20. 4(b) Let $X$ be a complete metric space and let $Y$ be a subset of $X$. Then show that $Y$, considered as a metric space is complete if and only if it is closed. [[4]]
  21. 5(a) State and prove Baire's category theorem. [[1+4]]
  22. 5(b) Define a contraction mapping over a metric space. Give an example of a contraction mapping having no fixed point. [[2+3]]
  23. 6(a) Let $(X,\rho)$ and $(Y,\rho_1)$ be two metric spaces and $f:X\to Y$ be a function. Then $f(x)$ is continuous at $x\in X$ if and only if for every sequence $\{x_n\},x_n\in X$ converging to $x\in X$ implies $f(x_n)\to f(x)$ as $n\to\infty$. [[4]]
  24. 6(b) Define a compact a metric space. Prove that every compact metric space is complete. [[2+4]]
