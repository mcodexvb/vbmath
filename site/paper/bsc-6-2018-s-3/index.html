BMC-63

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2018
  1. 1 Assuming points in space are described by general curvilinear coordinates $(u_1,u_2,u_3)$, show that $\left\{\frac{\partial\vec r}{\partial u_1},\frac{\partial\vec r}{\partial u_2},\frac{\partial\vec r}{\partial u_3}\right\}$ and $\{\vec\nabla u_1,\vec\nabla u_2,\vec\nabla u_3\}$ are (bi-orthogonal) reciprocal systems of vectors. Write down the expression for infinitesimal arc length (line element) of a space curve in terms of general curvilinear coordinates $(u_1,u_2,u_3)$. Interpret symbols involved there. [3+1+1]
  2. 2 What do you mean by an invariant or a scalar field? If $\Phi$ be an invariant, determine which of $\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial x^p},\frac{\partial^2\Phi}{\partial x^p\partial x^q}$, is a tensor. Construct symmetric and skew-symmetric tensors from the tensor $A^{pq}_{rs}$. [1+3+1]
  3. 3 What do you mean by metric tensors? Is it symmetric? Justify your answer. Verify whether its partial derivatives are tensor or not. Write down Christoffel symbols of first and second kind in terms of metric tensor. [1+1+1+1+1]
  4. 4 Define covariant derivative. Show that covariant derivative of a tensor is again a tensor. Find covariant derivative of a mixed tensor of rank 5, contravariant of order 2 and covariant of order 3. [2+2+1]
  5. 5 Verify whether $A_{p,qr}-A_{p,rq}$ for any covariant tensor $A_p$ can be expressed as linear combination of $A_p$ with a suitable coefficient to be determined by you. State quotient law. Hence or otherwise verify whether coefficients of linear combination mentioned above are tensor or not. Find their rank and order, if yes. [2+1+1+1]
  6. 6 What do you mean by geodesic in a Riemannian space? Derive its equation in general curvilinear coordinates $(u_1,u_2,u_3)$. Hence find the equation for geodesics in rectangular Cartesian coordinate system. [1+3+1]
  7. 1(a) What is meant by sampling distribution? Show that the co-efficient of excess of the sampling distribution of the mean is $\gamma_2/n$, $n$ is the size of the sample and $\gamma_2$ is the co-efficient of excess of the population. If $X$ be Normal $(10,5)$ and $X_1,X_2,\dots,X_{501}$ be a random sample of size 501 from the population of $X$, what is the expected value of sample variance $S^2$?
  8. 1(b) Define confidence interval for a parameter. Compute the confidence interval for the parameter `$m$' of a Normal $(m,\sigma)$ population. Let $X_1,X_2,\dots,X_{11}$ be a random sample of size 11 from a Normal distribution with unknown mean `$m$' and variance $\sigma^2=9.9$. If $\sum_{i=1}^{11}x_i=132$ then for what value of the constant $k$ is $(12-k\sqrt{0.9},12+k\sqrt{0.9})$ 90\% confidence interval for `$m$'? (Given that $Z_{0.05}=1.64$)
  9. 2(a) If $T$ be a consistent estimator for $\theta$, then show that $T^2$ is a consistent estimator for $\theta^2$. [2]
  10. 2(b) Define i) statistical hypothesis, ii) critical region of a test. iii) test of a hypothesis iv) power of a test.
  11. 2(c) State and prove Neyman-Pearson theorem for finding a best critical region for testing a simple hypothesis against a simple alternative.
  12. 3(a) Suppose that $X$ is a random variable about which the hypothesis $H_0:X\sim UNIF(0,1)$ against $H_a:X\sim N(0,1)$ is to be tested. What is the form of the likelihood ratio test critical region based on one observation of $X$? [2]
  13. 3(b) Let $X$ be a single observation from a population with probability density $f(x;\theta)=\begin{cases}\frac{\theta^xe^{-\theta}}{x!}&\text{for }x=0,1,2,\dots,\infty\\0,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$ where $\theta\ge0$. Find the likelihood ratio test critical region for testing the null hypothesis $H_0:\theta=2$ against the alternative $H_a:\theta\ne2$. [3]
  14. 3(c) Let $X_1,X_2,\dots,X_{20}$ be a random sample from a distribution with probability density function $f(x;p)=\begin{cases}p^x(1-p)^{1-x}&\text{if }x=0,1\\0,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$ where $0<p\le\frac12$ is a parameter. The hypothesis $H_0:p=\frac12$ is to be tested against $H_a:p<\frac12$. If $H_0$ be rejected when $\sum_{i=1}^{20}x_i\le6$, then what is the probability of type-I error? [3]
  15. 3(d) A random sample of size 4 is taken from a Normal distribution with unknown mean $m$ and variance $\sigma^2>0$. To test $H_0:m=0$ against $H_a:m<0$ the following test is used: ``Reject $H_0$ if and only if $X_1+X_2+X_3+X_4<-20$''. Find the value of $\sigma$ so that the significance level of this test will be close to 0.14. \fbox{\parbox{3.4cm}{\small Use separate answer scripts for each unit}} [2]
