CCMA-11

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2020
  1. 1(a) If $f:[a,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ is such that $f$ is bounded and integrable on $[a,X]$ for all $X>a$ and if $\int_a^\infty f$ is absolutely convergent. Then show that $\int_a^\infty f$ is convergent. Moreover, show that the converse is not true. [5]
  2. 1(b) State and prove the Dirichlet's test for convergence of improper integral and then show that $\int_0^\infty(\sin x/x)\,dx$ is convergent. [5]
  3. 2(a) Prove that for $n>0$, $\sqrt\pi[\Gamma(2n)]=2^{2n-1}\Gamma(2n)\Gamma(n+(1/2))$. [4]
  4. 2(b) Prove that for $p>-1$, $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^p x\,dx\times\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^{p+1}x\,dx=\pi/[2(p+1)]$. [3]
  5. 2(c) If $(f_n)$ is a sequence of functions on $[0,1]$ such that \[f_n(x)=\begin{cases} 0&\text{if }0\le x<\frac1{n+1}\\ \sin^2(\frac{\pi}{x})&\text{if }\frac1{n+1}\le x\le\frac1n\\ 0&\text{if }\frac1n<x\le1. \end{cases}\] Then show that $(f_n)$ converges to a continuous function, but not uniformly. [3]
  6. 3(a) Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of Riemann integrable functions converging uniformly to $f$ on a closed and bounded interval $[a,b]$. Then show that $f$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$ and $\int_a^b f_n\to\int_a^b f$ as $n\to\infty$. [5]
  7. 3(b) Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of functions converging uniformly to $f$ on $[0,\infty)$. Also assume that $\int_0^\infty f$ and $\int_0^\infty f_n$ exist, $n\in\mathbb N$. Is it true that $\int_0^\infty f_n\to\int_0^\infty f$ as $n\to\infty$? [2]
  8. 3(c) Assume that $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable and $f'$ is uniformly continuous on $\mathbb R$. Verify that $n[f(x+(1/n))-f(x)]\to f'(x)$ uniformly on $\mathbb R$. [3]
  9. 4(a) Let $R$ be the radius of convergence of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_nx^n$ and let $[a,b]$ be a closed and bounded subset of $(-R,R)$. Then prove that $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_nx^n$ converges uniformly on $[a,b]$. [5]
  10. 4(b) Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of real-valued and uniformly continuous functions, converging uniformly to $f$ on $(a,b)$. Then show that $f:(a,b)\to\mathbb R$ is uniformly continuous. [3]
  11. 4(c) Find the radius of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty n^nx^{n^3}$ and then determine its interval of convergence. [2]
  12. 5(a) Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be periodic function with period $2\pi$ such that \[f(x)=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }-\pi<x\le0\\x&\text{if }0<x\le\pi.\end{cases}\] Find the Fourier series of $f$ and then prove that $(\pi^2/8)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(1/(2n-1)^2)$. [5]
  13. 5(b) Assuming the validity of differentiating under the integral sign, show that \[\int_0^{\pi/2}\log(\alpha\cos^2\theta+\beta\sin^2\theta)\,d\theta=\pi\log((\sqrt\alpha+\sqrt\beta)/2).\] [5]
  14. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  15. 1(a) Find a partial differential equation (PDE) by eliminating arbitrary function $\phi$ from the equation $xz=\phi(x^4+x^2y^2)$.
  16. 1(b) Distinguish between linear, semilinear and quasilinear types of first order PDE in two independent variables.
  17. 1(c) Solve the PDE $py+qx=0$, together with the auxiliary condition that $z(0,y)=e^{-y^2}$.
  18. 1(d) Under which conditions Charpit's method can be applied to solve first order nonlinear PDE?
  19. 1(e) Show that there is no singular integral for PDE of the type $f(p,q)=0$.
  20. 1(f) Let $z=z(x,y)$ be the complete integral of the PDE $pq=xy$ passing through the points $(0,0,1)$ and $(0,1,\frac12)$ in the $xyz$-space. Find the value of $z(x,y)$ evaluated at $(2,-2)$.
  21. 1(g) Find the particular integral of the PDE \[(2D-3D')^2=\tan(3x+2y);\quad D\equiv\frac\partial{\partial x},\ D'\equiv\frac\partial{\partial y}.\] \textit{[The operator is printed without an operand.]}
  22. 1(h) What do you mean by a well-posed problem for a PDE? [$5\times2=10$]
  23. 2(a) For the PDE \[px(z-2y^2)=(z-qy)(z-y^2-2x^2),\] find the general integral and the integral surface through the curve $x^2=y^6=z^3$.
  24. 2(b) Find the complete integral of the following PDE by Charpit's method: \[p^2+q^2-2px-2qy+1=0.\] [5+5]
  25. 3(a) Show that the PDEs $2(z+xp+yq)=yp^2$ and $p=\frac{2020}{y^2}$ are compatible on a specified domain $D$ and solve them.
  26. 3(b) Find the singular and general integrals of the following PDE: \[z=px+qy+\sqrt{1+p^2+q^2}.\] [5+5]
  27. 4(a) Using Cauchy's method of characteristics, find an integral surface of the PDE $pq=1$ passing through the straight line $x_0=2s$, $y_0=2s$, $z_0=5s$.
  28. 4(b) Solve: \[\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x\partial y}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=z+e^{-x}.\] [5+5]
