MMC-15

Classical Mechanics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2018
  1. 1 Derive relations among velocities and accelerations of a particle observed with reference to two frames, one is rotating relative to other having common origin. [5]
  2. 2 Obtain the equation of motion of a particle moving under the action of gravity near the Earth surface (take the rotation of the Earth about its axis into account). [5]
  3. 3 Derive expressions of kinetic energy and angular momentum involved in the rotational motion of a rigid body. [3+2]
  4. 4 Explain Euler's angle. Hence derive expression for angular velocity of rotational motion of rigid body in terms of Euler's angle and their time derivative. [3+2]
  5. 5 Derive Euler's equation of motion of rigid body and find its solution when the body is symmetric. [5]
  6. 6 Define generalized coordinate of mechanical system? State D'Alembert's principle. Derive Lagrange's equation of motion of second kind. [1+1+3]
  7. 7 State Legendre's theorem in its general form and use result of this theorem \unreadable{handwritten insertion over ``to''} derive Hamilton's equation of motion of a holonomic mechanical system. [2+3]
  8. 8 Define generalized coordinate and obtain kinetic energy, potential energy, Lagrangian, Legrange's equation of motion and its solution for the motion of a particle along a line under the influence of a force directed towards a fixed point (center) on the line with a magnitude proportional to the distance of the particle from the center. [5]
  9. 9 Show that Lagrangian of a system with holonomic constraints is not unique. Hence verify whether $L(x,\dot x)=\dfrac12\dot x^2$ and $L(x,\dot x)=\log(\dot x)$ may be treated a inequivalent Lagrangian for the free motion of a particle along a line. [2+1+2]
  10. 10 Define Hamilton's principal function. Derive equation for it. Show that Hamilton's principal function may be regarded as generating function of a special type of canonical transformations. Discuss the significance of the transformation. \par\hfill P.T.O. [1+1+2+1]
  11. 11 Define Poisson bracket. Show that Poisson bracket follows Jacobi identity. [1+4]
  12. 12 What do you mean by a constant of the motion of a mechanical system? Prove that Poisson bracket of two constant of motion is also a constant of the motion. [1+4]
  13. 13 Study the small oscillation around the stable equilibrium point of a mechanical system moving under the influence of a force described by the potential\par \[V(x,y)=\frac12\left(x^2+y^2\right)-xy.\] [1+4]
  14. 14 Write down the correspondence among generalized coordinate, momenta and associated operators on Hilbert space for the quantum mechanical description of motion of a particle on a line. Hence derive the Schr\"odinger equation for this system. [2+3]
