CC-7

Differential Equations-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2022
  1. 1 Answer any \emph{ten} questions from the following: [$10\times 2=20$]
  2. 1(a) Solve: $(D^2+2D+5)^2(D^2+5D+4)^3(D+1)^2y=0$. [2]
  3. 1(b) Find a third order linear homogeneous differential equation whose linearly independent solutions are $e^x$, $e^{-2x}$, and $e^{3x}$. [2]
  4. 1(c) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of parabolas $y^5=cx^7$, $c$ being the parameter. [2]
  5. 1(d) Find three different solutions of the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=xy^{1/2}$, $y(0)=0$. [2]
  6. 1(e) Find an integrating factor of the differential equation $(xy^2-e^{1/x^3})\,dx-(x^2y)\,dy=0$. [2]
  7. 1(f) Find the differential equation of the circles $(x-\alpha)^2+(y-\beta)^2=r^2$, where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are arbitrary constants, and $r$ is a fixed constant. [2]
  8. 1(g) Solve the differential equation $x^2\bigl(\frac{dy}{dx}\bigr)^2+5xy\frac{dy}{dx}+4y^2=0$. [2]
  9. 1(h) Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y}{x}+\cot(\frac{y}{x})$. [2]
  10. 1(i) Consider the differential equation $x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}-4y=0$. If $W(1)=9$, then find the value of $W(4)-W(2)$. [2]
  11. 1(j) Find an integrating factor of the second order ordinary differential equation \(2x^2(x+1)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+x(7x+3)\frac{dy}{dx}-3y=x^2\). [2]
  12. 1(k) Let $x(t)$ be the solution of the differential equation $\frac{dx}{dt}=(x-5)(x-7)$ satisfying the initial condition $x(0)=6$. Find the value of $x(t)$ when $t\to-\infty$. [2]
  13. 1(l) Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=f(y)$. [2]
  14. 1(m) Find the condition under which the general solution of the differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+b\frac{dy}{dx}+cy=0$ tends to zero as \textit{[printed variable obscured by ink, handwritten \(x\) appears]} $\to\infty$. [2]
  15. 1(n) Solve the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=5x(1-\frac{x}{2})$, $y(0)=1$, and find $y(x)$ when $x\to\infty$. [2]
  16. 2(a) Solve the second order linear differential equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\frac{dy}{dx}-2y=\sin(2x)+e^{2x}$. [4]
  17. 2(b) A certain radioactive material is known to decay at a rate proportional to the amount present. If initially there is 50 miligrams of the material present and after two hours it is observed that the material has lost 10\% of its original mass, then find the mass of the material after four hours. [2]
  18. 2(c) Construct a direction field and sketch a few solution curves of the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=2y-x$. [2]
  19. 2(d) Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}=y^{1/3}(\sin 2x)$, $y(0)=0$. [2]
  20. 3(a) Find the singular solution and extraneous loci of the differential equation $x^3p^2+x^2yp+27=0$. [4]
  21. 3(b) Using the method of undetermined coefficients, solve the equation $(D^2+D-6)y=-18e^{3x}-6x-11$. [3]
  22. 3(c) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves $r^n\cos(n\theta)=c^n$, $c$ being the parameter. [3]
  23. 4(a) Using the method of variation of parameters, solve the equation \(x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-x\frac{dy}{dx}-3y=x^2\log(x)\), given that $y=x^3$, and $y=\frac{1}{x}$ are two linearly independent solutions of the corresponding homogeneous equation. [3]
  24. 4(b) If $u$ and $v$ are any two solutions of the equation $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0$ on an interval $[a,b]$, then prove that their Wronskian $W(u,v)$ is either identically zero or never zero on $[a,b]$. [3]
  25. 4(c) Solve: $y=2x\frac{dy}{dx}+x^4(\frac{dy}{dx})^8$. [3]
  26. 4(d) Solve the boundary value problem $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=0$, $y(0)=0$, $y(\frac{\pi}{2})=1$. [1]
  27. 5(a) Solve the Cauchy-Euler equation $x^2y''-4xy'+6y=\log(x^2)$, $x>0$. [3]
  28. 5(b) Prove that the transformation $v=y^{1-n}$ ($n\ne 0$ or $1$) reduces the Bernoulli equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=P(x)y+Q(x)y^n$ to a linear equation in $v$. Hence solve the equation $x\frac{dy}{dx}+y=x^2y^2$. [4]
  29. 5(c) If $e^{\int\phi(\frac{x}{y})\,d(\frac{x}{y})}$ is an integrating factor of the differential equation $M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0$, then find the expression of $\phi(\frac{x}{y})$. [3]
  30. 6(a) Solve the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}+y=f(x)$ with initial condition $y(0)=6$, where $f(x)=5$, when $0\le x<10$ and $f(x)=1$, when $x\ge10$. Hence find the value of $y(12)$. [3]
  31. 6(b) Reduce the equation $xy(y-px)=x+py$ to Clairaut's form by the substitution $x^2=u$ and $y^2=v$. Hence solve the equation. [3]
  32. 6(c) Show that in a first order and first degree homogeneous differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{f(x,y)}{g(x,y)}$, the variables can be separated by the substitution $y=vx$. Hence solve the initial value problem $(y+\sqrt{x^2+y^2})\,dx-x\,dy=0$, $y(2)=0$. [4]
  33. 7(a) Let $y(x)$ be the solution of the differential equation $(xy+y+e^{-x})\,dx+(x+e^{-x})\,dy=0$ satisfying the condition $y(0)=1$. Then find the value of $y(-1)$. [3]
  34. 7(b) Prove that the second order linear differential equation $p_0(x)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+p_1(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+p_2(x)y=0$ is exact iff $p_0''(x)-p_1'(x)+p_2(x)=0$. [4]
  35. 7(c) Solve: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+y=x^2$. [2]
  36. 7(d) Convert the differential equation $y=xf(p)+\phi(p)$ to a linear equation in $x$. \renewcommand{\paperfull}{25} \renewcommand{\paperplural}{} [1]
