MMO-41(P2)

Advanced Topology-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) State and prove Urysohn's metrization theorem. Give an example of a topological space which is not metrizable. [1+6+2]
  2. 1(b) In a normal space $X$ if every closed set is an $G_\delta$-set then prove that for any open set $V,\exists$ a continuous function $f:X\to[0,1]$ such that $f(x)>0\ \forall x\in V$ and $f(x)=0,\ \forall x\in X\setminus V.$ [3]
  3. 2(a) Define uniform topology $\tau_u$ in a uniform space $(X,\mathcal U)$. Prove that $\{U[x]\}_{U\in\mathcal U}$ is the \textit{nbd} system in $(x,\tau_u)$.\par Show that for $A\subseteq X$,\par \hspace*{2em}$\tau_u$ - closure $A=\bigcap_{U\in\mathcal U}AU$\par Prove that $(X,\tau_u)$ is regular. [2+2+2+1]
  4. 2(b) Prove that a uniform space $(X,\mathcal U)$ is Hausdorff iff $\tau_u$ is a Hausdorff topology on $X$. [3]
  5. 2(c) Prove that every uniformly continuous function is continuous. [2]
  6. 3(a) Prove that a uniform space having a countable base has a normalised countable base. [4]
  7. 3(b) Prove that a uniform space having a countable base is pseudometrizable. [8]
  8. 4(a) Prove that a topology $\tau$ on a group $(\mathrm G,\cdot)$ forms a topological group iff the function $(x,y)\to x^{-1}y:(\mathrm G\times\mathrm G,\tau\times\tau)\to(\mathrm G,\tau)$ is continuous.\par Prove that the additive group of real numbers with usual topology is a topological group. [3+2]
  9. 4(b) If $C$ is compact and $F$ is closed in a topological group $(\mathrm G,\tau)$ and if $C\cap F=\phi$ then show that this is a symmetric \textit{nbd} $V$ of $e$ such that $CV\cap FV=\phi.$\par Also show that $CF$ is closed. [3+2]
  10. 4(c) Let $(\mathrm G,\tau)$ be topological group and $\beta$ be the \textit{nbd} system of the identity element $e$. Then show that $\{L(U);U\in\beta\}$ is a base for a uniformity on $G$, where $L(U)=\{(x,y);x^{-1}y\in U\}.$ \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}[ 2 ]\end{center} [2]
  11. 5(a) Let $(G,\tau)$ be a topological group. Then show that the following statements are equivalent:\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace $(G,\tau)$ is $T_0$\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace $(G,\tau)$ is $T_1$\par \hspace*{1em}(iii)\enspace $(G,\tau)$ is $T_2$\par \hspace*{1em}(iv)\enspace $\{e\}=\cap\{U;U\text{ is a }nbd\text{ of }e\}.$ [3]
  12. 5(b) Let $(G,\tau)$ be a topological group and $H$ be a subgroup of $G$. Show that $H$ is closed iff there is a closed \textit{nbd} $U$ of `$e$' such that $H\cap U$ is closed. [3]
  13. 5(c) Prove that the centre of the Hausdorff topological group is a closed normal subgroup. [3]
  14. 5(d) Prove that in a Hausdorff connected topological group every normal discrete subgroup is a closed normal subgroup of the centre. [3]
  15. 6 Answer \underline{any two} : \par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace If $\mathcal U$ is a uniformity generated by a family of pseudometrics $D$ having at least one member is metric then prove that $\mathcal U$ is Hausdorff.\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace Prove that the uniform topology is completely regular.\par \hspace*{1em}(iii)\enspace Show that for a compact normal subgroup $H$ of a topological group $(G,\tau)$, the canonical mapping $\varphi:(G,\tau)\to\left(G/H,\bar\tau\right)$ is closed ($\bar\tau$ is the quotient topology).
