IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2025

  1. 1 The sum of the infinite series \[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{n+1} \frac{\pi^{2n+1}}{2^{2n+1} (2n)!} \] is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-\pi$ \item $\frac{\pi}{4}$ \item $\frac{\pi}{2}$ \item $-\frac{\pi}{4}$ \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 For which one of the following choices of $N(x, y)$, is the equation \[ (e^x \sin y - 2y \sin x) \, dx + N(x, y) \, dy = 0 \] an exact differential equation? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $N(x, y) = e^x \sin y + 2 \cos x$ \item $N(x, y) = e^x \cos y + 2 \cos x$ \item $N(x, y) = e^x \cos y + 2 \sin x$ \item $N(x, y) = e^x \sin y + 2 \sin x$ \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $f, g: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be two functions defined by \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} x |x| \left| \sin \frac{1}{x} \right| & \text{if } x \neq 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } x = 0 \end{cases} \] and \[ g(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 \sin \frac{1}{x} + x \cos \frac{1}{x} & \text{if } x \neq 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } x = 0 \end{cases} \] Then, which one of the following is TRUE? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ is differentiable at $x=0$, and $g$ is NOT differentiable at $x=0$ \item $f$ is NOT differentiable at $x=0$, and $g$ is differentiable at $x=0$ \item $f$ is differentiable at $x=0$, and $g$ is differentiable at $x=0$ \item $f$ is NOT differentiable at $x=0$, and $g$ is NOT differentiable at $x=0$ \end{enumerate}
