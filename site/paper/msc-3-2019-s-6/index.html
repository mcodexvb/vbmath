MMO-31(A3)

Computational Fluid Dynamics-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2019
  1. 1(a) Classify the partial differential equation\par \[y^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-2xy\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}+x^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=\frac{y^2}x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{x^2}y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\] into parabolic or hyperbolic or elliptic equation. Hence reduce the equation to its canonical form and solve it. [1+2+2]
  2. 1(b) Find the second order accurate discretized form of third order partial derivative with respect to one independent variable using the values of the dependent variable at the $(i,j),(i-1,j),(i-2,j),(i-3,j),(i-4,j)$ points. [5]
  3. 2(a) Consider a general two level difference method using three points on each time level, in the form\par \[a_2u_{m-1}^{n+1}+a_1u_m^{n+1}+a_0u_{m+1}^{n+1}-b_2u_{m-1}^n-b_1u_m^n-b_0u_{m+1}^n=0,\] where coefficients $a_i$'s and $b_i$'s are arbitrary. Find the truncation error in approximating the differential equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$ by the above difference equation. Determine the parameters $a_i$'s and $b_i$'s imposing necessary conditions so that the difference equation is consistent with the differential equation. Find the Schmidt, Laasonen and Crank-Nicholson method from this general scheme. [2+2+2]
  4. 2(b) Using the finite difference scheme\par \[u_m^{n+1}=\frac43u_m^n-\frac13u_m^{n-1}+\frac{2\lambda}3\delta_x^2u_m^{n+1}\] where $\lambda=k/h^2$, solve the following initial-boundary value problem:\par \hspace*{2em}$\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2},\ t>0$\par \hspace*{2em}$u(x,0)=x\qquad0\le x\le\frac12$\par \hspace*{4.7em}$=1-x\quad\frac12\le x\le1$\par \hspace*{2em}$u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0$\par when $h=\frac14$ and $\lambda=\frac12$. Integrate upto two time steps. \par\hfill P.T.O. [4]
  5. 3(a) For the solution of the differential equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}+cy$, $c$ is constant, the following difference schemes were written:\par \hspace*{1em}(i) $u_{lm}^{n+1}=\left[1+\lambda\left(\delta_x^2+\delta_y^2\right)+c\lambda h^2\right]u_{lm}^n$\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) $\left[1-c\lambda h^2-\lambda\left(\delta_x^2+\delta_y^2\right)\right]u_{lm}^{n+1}=u_{lm}^n$\par where $\lambda=k/h^2$. Show that (i) is stable for $0<\lambda\le1/(4-ch^2/2)$, $c<0$ and (ii) is unconditionally stable for $c\le0$. [3+3]
  6. 3(b) Find the truncation error of Peaceman-Rachford ADI method for the solution of the two dimensional heat equation\par \[\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=a(x,y)\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+b(x,y)\frac{\partial^2y}{\partial y^2}\] under suitable initial and boundary conditions where $a>0,\ b>0$. [4]
  7. 4(a) Show that the partial differential equation\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}\] with $u=f(x)$ and $\partial u/\partial t=g(x)$ at $t=0$, may be replaced by the difference scheme\par \[u_m^{n+1}=-u_m^{n-1}+2\left(1-r^2\right)u_m^n+r^2\left(u_{m+1}^n+u_{m-1}^n\right)\] \[u_m^0=f_m\] \[u_m^1=kg_m+\left(1-r^2\right)f_m+\tfrac12r^2\left(f_{m+1}+f_{m-1}\right)\] where $r=k/h$. Find an expression for the local truncation error of each of the finite difference expressions and find the order of the method.\par Using\quad$f(x)=x\qquad0\le x\le\frac12$\par \hspace*{3.8em}$=1-x\quad\frac12\le x\le1$\par \hspace*{3.8em}$g(x)=0,\qquad0\le x\le1$\par and $u(0,t)=0=u(1,t)$, find the solution upto two time steps with $h=0.2,\ r=0.5$. [2+2+2]
  8. 4(b) If the finite difference approximation\par \[\delta_t^2u_m^n=r^2\delta_x^2u_m^n-crh\,\delta_{2t}u_m^n\] is used for the initial-boundary value problem\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-2c\frac{\partial u}{\partial t},\ 0<x<1,\ t>0,\] \hspace*{2em}$u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0,\ u(x,0)=f(x),\ \dfrac{\partial u(x,0)}{\partial t}=g(x)$,\par where $r=k/h$, show that the difference scheme is stable if $0<r\le1$. [4]
  9. 5(a) Considering the existence of the solution of the linear partial differential equation\par \[A\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+C\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}+D\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+E\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+Fu=G\] in a region $R$ with boundary $\partial R$ where $A,B,C,\ldots,G$ are continuous functions of $x$ and $y$ in $R+\partial R$, find the finite difference approximation when the mesh lengths of all four neighbouring sides of a nodal point are different. Hence deduce five-point and nine-point formulae for the Poisson equation $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=G(x,y)$ at a node whose all four neighbouring sides are equal. [6]
  10. 5(b) Find the solution of $\nabla^2u=0$ in $R$ subject to the Dirichlet condition $u(x,y)=x-y$ on $\partial R$, where $R$ is the region inside the triangle with vertices $(0,0),(7,0),(0,7)$ and $\partial R$ is its boundary. Assume the step length $h=2.0$. [4]
  11. 6 Find the solution of the mixed boundary value problem\par \hspace*{2em}$\nabla^2u=0,\quad0\le x^2+y^2\le1,\ x\ge0,\ y\ge0$\par \hspace*{2em}$u=0$, on $x=0$\par \hspace*{2em}$u=x^3/3$ on $y=0$\par \hspace*{2em}$\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial n}=x^3-3xy^2$ on $x^2+y^2=1$\par using the five point formula and suitable approximation to $\partial u/\partial n$ on the boundary. [10]
