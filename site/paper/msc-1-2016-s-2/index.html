MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2016
  1. 1(a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_\Gamma \mathrm{Re}\,z\,dz$, where $\Gamma$ is the contour joining $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$ by a straight line. [2]
  2. 1(b) State Cauchy's theorem. By constructing an example show that certain condition (to be specified by you) for the validity of Cauchy's theorem is sufficient but not necessary. [1+1]
  3. 1(c) State and prove Cauchy's integral formula. [1+3]
  4. 1(d) Evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_\Gamma\frac{e^{2z}}{(z+1)^4}\,dz$, where $\Gamma$ is the circle $|z|=3$. [2]
  5. 2(a) State and prove Liouville's theorem. [1+2]
  6. 2(b) If $f(z)$ is analytic for all finite values of $z$ and if $|f(z)|\geq K$, a fixed positive number, then prove that $f(z)$ is a constant. [2]
  7. 2(c) Let $f(z)$ be a continuous function in a simply connected domain $R$ and $z_0$ be any arbitrary but fixed point in $R$. If the integral $F(z)=\displaystyle\int_{z_0}^{z}f(w)\,dw$ is independent of the path so long as the path lies in $R$, then show that $F'(z)=f(z)$ for every $z$ in $R$. [3]
  8. 2(d) Expand $f(z)=\dfrac{1}{z-4}$ in powers of $(z-3)$. Mention the region of validity of the series. [2]
  9. 3(a) State and prove Maximum Modulus theorem. [1+4]
  10. 3(b) State Minimum Modulus theorem. [1]
  11. 3(c) If $|f(z)|>m$ on $|z|=r$, $f(z)$ is regular for $|z|\leq r$ and $|f(0)|<m$, then show that $f(z)$ has at least one zero in $|z|<r$. Hence show that $f(z)=z^9+z^5-1$ vanishes at least for one point in $|z|<2$. [2+2]
  12. 4(a) State and prove Laurent's theorem. [1+4]
  13. 4(b) Prove that $e^{z+\frac1z}=\displaystyle\sum_{-\infty}^{\infty}a_nz^n$ where $\pi a_n=\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}e^{2\cos\theta}\cos n\theta\,d\theta$ and $a_{n-1}-a_{n+1}=na_n$. [3]
  14. 4(c) Expand the function $f(z)=\dfrac{1}{z^2(z-1)(z+2)}$ in $1<|z|<2$. [2]
  15. 5(a) Prove that a function $f$ has a pole of order $m$ at $\alpha$ if and only if $f$ can be expressed in the form $f(z)=\dfrac{\psi(z)}{(z-\alpha)^m}$ in some neighbourhood of $\alpha$, where $\psi(z)$ is analytic at $z=\alpha$ and $\psi(\alpha)\neq0$. [3]
  16. 5(b) If $f(z)$ has a pole at $\alpha$, then show that $|f(z)|\to\infty$ a $z\to\alpha$ in any manner. [2]
  17. 5(c) Prove that the limit point of the zeros of an analytic function is an essential singularity of the function, unless the function is identically zero. [3]
  18. 5(d) Show that the sum of the residues of $\dfrac{e^{-z}}{z^2-a^2}$ at its pole is $-\dfrac1a\sinh a$. [2]
  19. 6(a) Let $f(z)$ be analytic within and on a simple closed curve $C$ except for a finite number of poles within $C$ and let $f(z)\neq0$ anywhere on $C$. Then prove that\\$\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\Delta_C\arg f(z)=\dfrac{1}{2\pi i}\displaystyle\oint_C\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz=Z-P$, where $Z$ and $P$ respectively denote the number of zeros and poles of $f(z)$ within $C$, a zero or pole of order $m$ being counted $m$ times. [5]
  20. 6(b) Evaluate \textit{any one} of the following by the method of contour integration: \\(i) $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\cos x\,dx}{(x^2+a^2)^2}$, $a>0$;\\(ii) $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{1+a\cos\theta}$, $|a|<1$. [5]
