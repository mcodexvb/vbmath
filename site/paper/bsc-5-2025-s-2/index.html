MJMA-12

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2025
  1. 1(a) Solve graphically the following LPP by the moving hyperplane method. \[\text{Minimize }z=2x-y,\quad\text{subject to }x+y\le5,\ x+2y\le8,\ 4x+3y\ge12,\ x,y\ge0.\] [4]
  2. 1(b) Show that $x_1=5,x_2=0,x_3=-1$ is a basic solution of the following system of equations. \[x_1+2x_2+x_3=4,\qquad2x_1+x_2+5x_3=5.\] Find other sets of basic solutions, if there be any. [2+4]
  3. 2(a) Prove that the vectors $(1,1,1),(1,1,0)$, and $(1,0,0)$ form a basis in $E^3$. Prove also that the vector $(1,3,1)$ can replace any one of the three vectors of the basis to form a new basis. [2+2]
  4. 2(b) Define a convex set. Check whether the set $X=\{(x_1,x_2):x_1,x_2\le1,\ x_1x_2\ge0\}$ is a convex set or not. [1+2]
  5. 2(c) Give examples of (i) convex hulls in $E^2$ and $E^3$ and (ii) convex polyhedron in $E^2$. [2+1]
  6. 3(a) Solve by simplex method the following LPP. \[\text{Maximize }z=x_1+x_2+3x_3,\quad\text{subject to }3x_1+2x_2+x_3\le3,\ 2x_1+x_2+2x_3\le2,\ x_1,x_2,x_3\ge0.\] [6]
  7. 3(b) Find the dual problem of the following LPP. \[\text{Maximize }z=6x_1+5x_2+10x_3,\] \[\text{subject to }4x_1+5x_2+7x_3\le5,\ 3x_1+7x_3\le10,\ 2x_1+x_2+8x_3=20,\ 2x_2+9x_3\ge5,\] \[x_1,x_2\ge0\text{ and }x_3\text{ is unrestricted in sign.}\] [4]
  8. 4 Solve the following transportation problem. \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{l|ccc|c}From $\backslash$ To&$D_1$&$D_2$&$D_3$&Supply\\\hline$O_1$&8&7&3&60\\$O_2$&3&8&9&70\\$O_3$&11&3&5&80\\\hline Demand&50&80&80&\end{tabular}\end{center} [10]
  9. 5 A company has four machines $M_1,M_2,M_3$, and $M_4$ on which to do three jobs $J_1,J_2$, and $J_3$. Each job can be assigned to one and only one machine. The cost of each job on each machine is given in the following tableau. What are the job assignments that will minimize the cost? \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{c|cccc}&$M_1$&$M_2$&$M_3$&$M_4$\\\hline$J_1$&18&24&28&32\\$J_2$&8&13&17&19\\$J_3$&10&15&19&22\end{tabular}\end{center} [10]
  10. 6 Use dual simplex method to solve the LPP. \[\text{Minimize }z=10x_1+6x_2+2x_3,\quad\text{subject to }-x_1+x_2+x_3\ge1,\ 3x_1+x_2-x_3\ge2,\ x_1,x_2,x_3\ge0.\] \begin{center}``OR''\end{center} [10]
  11. 7 Solve the traveling salesman problem with the following cost matrix $[c_{ij}]_{4\times4}$, where $c_{ij}$ is the cost of traveling from city $B_i$ to the city $C_j$: \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{c|cccc}&$C_1$&$C_2$&$C_3$&$C_4$\\\hline$B_1$&$\infty$&15&30&4\\$B_2$&6&$\infty$&4&1\\$B_3$&10&15&$\infty$&16\\$B_4$&7&18&13&$\infty$\end{tabular}\end{center} [10]
  12. 1(a) Explain what is meant by mathematical modelling? Describe the main steps involved in the mathematical modelling process. [2+2]
  13. 1(b) Differentiate between: (i) Linear and nonlinear models. (ii) Static and dynamic models. (iii) Deterministic and stochastic models. [2+2+2]
  14. 2(a) Model the exponential (Malthusian) growth of a population using a differential equation. A population of 1000 grows at 4\% annually. Find the population after 5 years. How long will it take to double at 6\% growth? [2+3]
  15. 2(b) Derive the logistic growth model and explain the meaning of each parameter. A population grows logistically with $K=1000$, $P_0=100$, $r=0.5$. Find the time when $P(t)$ reaches 500. [2+3]
  16. 3(a) Derive the differential equation for a damped harmonic oscillator by defining the damping ratio and natural frequency of the system. Explain underdamped, critically damped and overdamped motions mathematically. Explain the phenomena if an external periodic perturbative force ($f_0\cos(pt)$) is applied to this system. [4+3+3]
  17. 4(a) Model a series LR circuit with a voltage source $V(t)$ using a differential equation. Explain all the physical features of the model. For $L=1$ H, $R=2\,\Omega$ and $V(t)=10$ V DC, find the expression of current $I(t)$ with $I(0)=0$. [2+1+2]
  18. 4(b) Draw the schematic diagram of the $LRC$ circuit and find the related differential equation. Compare the dynamics of the circuit with SHM having damping or forcing. [5]
  19. 5(a) Define the SIR epidemic model and explain the significance of each parameter within it. [3]
  20. 5(b) From the SIR model, explain: (i) Early exponential growth. (ii) Maximum number of infected people. (iii) Reproduction number. (iv) Vaccination. [1+1+1+1]
  21. 5(c) Model a predator-prey system using differential equations by explaining the meaning of each terms. [3]
  22. 6(a) Derive the nonlinear differential equation for a simple pendulum of length $L$ under gravity $g$. Solve the linearized model. [3+2]
  23. 6(b) Use perturbation technique to solve the Van der Pol equation $y''+\epsilon(1-y^2)y'+y=0$ with $y(0)=A$, $y'(0)=0$ (up to order of $\epsilon$) for very small $\epsilon$. Hence, show the effect of the nonlinear term. [5]
