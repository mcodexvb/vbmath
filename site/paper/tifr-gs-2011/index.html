TIFR Graduate School · GS 2011 · Mathematics

  1. 1 Consider the sequence $\{x_n\}$ defined by $x_n = \frac{[nx]}{n}$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$ where $[\,]$ denotes the integer part. Then $\{x_n\}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item converges to $x$. \item converges but not to $x$. \item does not converge. \item oscillates. \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0} x \sin(1/x^2)$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$. \item $0$. \item $\infty$. \item oscillates. \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $A$ be a $5 \times 5$ matrix with real entries, then $A$ has \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item an eigenvalue which is purely imaginary. \item at least one real eigenvalue. \item at least two eigenvalues which are not real. \item at least $2$ distinct real eigenvalues. \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 The groups $\mathbb{Z}_9$ and $\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_3$ are \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item isomorphic \item abelian \item non abelian \item cyclic \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 The differential equation $\displaystyle \frac{dy}{dx} = y^{1/3}$, $y(0) = 0$ has \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item a unique solution. \item no nontrivial solution. \item finite number of solutions. \item infinite number of solutions. \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 The function $f_n(x) = n \sin(x/n)$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item does not converge for any $x$ as $n \to \infty$. \item converges to the constant function $1$ as $n \to \infty$. \item converges to the function $x$ as $n \to \infty$. \item does not converge for all $x$ as $n \to \infty$. \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 The equation $x^{22} \equiv 2 \pmod{23}$ has \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item no solutions. \item $23$ solutions. \item exactly one solution. \item $22$ solutions. \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 The sum of the squares of the roots of the cubic equation $x^3 - 4x^2 + 6x + 1 = 0$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $0$. \item $4$. \item $16$. \item none of the above. \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 The function $f(x)$ defined by \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} ax + b, & x \ge 1, \\ x^2 + 3x + 3, & x \le 1 \end{cases} \] is {[obscured in source]} \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item for a unique value of $a$ and infinitely many values of $b$. \item for a unique value of $b$ and infinitely many values of $a$. \item for infinitely many values of $a$ and $b$. \item none of the above. \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Let $m \le n$ be natural numbers. The number of injective maps from a set of cardinality $m$ to a set of cardinality $n$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $m!$ \item $n!$ \item $(n - m)!$ \item none of the above. \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 \emph{(True/False)} For any real number $c$, the polynomial $x^3 + x + c$ has exactly one real root.
  12. 12 \emph{(True/False)} $e^{\sqrt{2}} > 3$.
  13. 13 \emph{(True/False)} $A$ is a $3 \times 4$-matrix of rank $3$. Then the system of equations $Ax = b$ has exactly one solution.
  14. 14 \emph{(True/False)} $\log x$ is uniformly continuous on $(\text{[obscured]}, \infty)$.
  15. 15 \emph{(True/False)} If $A, B$ are closed subsets of $[0, \infty)$, then $A + B = \{x + y \mid x \in A, \, y \in B\}$ is closed in $[0, \infty)$.
  16. 16 \emph{(True/False)} The polynomial $x^4 + 7x^3 - 13x^2 + 11x$ has exactly one real root.
  17. 17 \emph{(True/False)} The value of the infinite product $\displaystyle \prod_{n=2}^\infty \left(1 - \frac{1}{n^2}\right)$ is $1$.
  18. 18 \emph{(True/False)} Consider the map $T$ from the vector space of polynomials of degree at most $5$ over the reals to $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$, given by sending a polynomial $P$ to the pair $(P(3), P'(3))$ where $P'$ is the derivative of $P$. Then the dimension of the kernel is $3$.
  19. 19 \emph{(True/False)} The derivative of the function $\displaystyle \int_0^{\sqrt{x}} e^{-t^2}\,dt$ at $x = 1$ is $e^{-1}$.
  20. 20 \emph{(True/False)} The equation $63x + 70y + 15z = 2010$ has an integral solution.
  21. 21 \emph{(True/False)} Any continuous function from the open unit interval $(0, 1)$ to itself has a fixed point.
  22. 22 \emph{(True/False)} There exists a group with a proper subgroup isomorphic to itself.
  23. 23 \emph{(True/False)} The space of solutions of infinitely differentiable functions satisfying the equation $y'' + y = 0$ is infinite dimensional.
  24. 24 \emph{(True/False)} The series $\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}}{n}$ diverges.
  25. 25 \emph{(True/False)} The function \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } x \text{ is rational,} \\ x & \text{if } x \text{ is irrational} \end{cases} \] is not continuous anywhere on the real line.
  26. 26 Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$-matrix with complex entries. The number of $2 \times 2$-matrices $A$ with complex entries satisfying the equation $A^3 = A$ is infinite.
  27. 27 In the ring $\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ the equation $x^2 = 1$ has exactly $2$ solutions.
  28. 28 There are $n$ homomorphisms from the group $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ to the additive group of rationals.
  29. 29 A bounded continuous function on $\mathbb{R}$ is uniformly continuous.
  30. 30 The symmetric group $S_5$ consisting of permutations on $5$ symbols has an element of order $6$.
  31. 31 Suppose $f_n(x)$ is a sequence of {[obscured in source]} on the closed interval $[0, 1]$ converging to $0$ pointwise. {[obscured in source]} converges to $0$.
  32. 32 {[obscured in source]} from $S_3$ to $\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$.
  33. 33 If $A$ and $B$ are $3 \times 3$ matrices and $A$ is invertible, then there exists an integer $n$ such that $A + nB$ is invertible.
  34. 34 Let $P$ be a degree $3$ polynomial with complex coefficients such that the constant term is $2010$. Then $P$ has a root $\alpha$ with $|\alpha| > 10$.
  35. 35 Suppose a box contains three cards, one with both sides white, one with both sides black, and one with one side white and the other side black. If you pick a card at random, and the side facing you is white, then the probability that the other side is white is $1/2$.
  36. 36 There exists a set $A \subset \{1, 2, \dots, 100\}$ with $65$ elements, such that $65$ cannot be expressed as a sum of two elements in $A$.
  37. 37 Let $S$ be a finite subset of $\mathbb{R}^3$ such that any three elements in $S$ span a two dimensional subspace. Then $S$ spans a two dimensional space.
  38. 38 Any non-singular $k \times k$-matrix with real entries can be made singular by changing exactly one entry.
  39. 39 Let $f$ be a continuous integrable function of $\mathbb{R}$ such that either $f(x) > 0$ or $f(x) + f(x + 1) > 0$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x)\,dx > 0$.
  40. 40 A gardener throws $18$ seeds onto an {[obscured in source]} two seeds are within a distance of $25$ centimetres.
