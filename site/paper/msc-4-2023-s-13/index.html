MMO-41(P1)

Advanced Complex Analysis-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2023
  1. 1(a) State and prove Mittag-Leffler's Theorem. [[1+3]]
  2. 1(b) Let $f(z)$ be regular in $|z|<R$ and $f(0)\ne0$. If it has zeros at $a_1,a_2,\ldots a_m$ with moduli not exceeding $r(0<r<R)$ then show that \[\log\left\{\left|\frac{r^m}{a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_m}f(0)\right|\right\}=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|f(re^{i\theta})|d\theta.\] [[3]]
  3. 1(c) State Poisson-Jensen formula. Prove the result for the function $f(z)=\frac1{z-a}$, $(|a|<R)$ in $|z|<R$. [[1+2]]
  4. 2(a) Define Nevanlinna's characteristic function $T(r,f)$. Find $T(r,f)$ when $f(z)=e^{z^2}$. [[1+2]]
  5. 2(b) Show that for any $a$, $|T(r,f+a)-T(r,f)|\le\log^+|a|+\log2$. [[1+2]]
  6. 2(c) State Cartan's identity. Using this prove that $\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}m(r,e^{i\theta})d\theta\le\log2$. [[1+2]]
  7. 2(d) Prove that $T(r,f)$ is a convex function of $\log r$. [[2]]
  8. 3(a) Prove that $T(R,f)=T(R,\frac1f)+\log|f(0)|$, $f(0)\ne0,\infty$. [[2]]
  9. 3(b) State and prove Nevanlinna's first fundamental theorem. [[1+3]]
  10. 3(c) Find the order of the meromorphic function $f(z)=\frac{z+1}{z^2-4}$. [[2]]
  11. 3(d) If $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$ are the orders of the meromorphic functions $f_1$ and $f_2$ respectively, then show that the order of $f_1\pm f_2\le\max\{\rho_1,\rho_2\}$. [[2]]
  12. 4(a) Define $\delta(a,f)$ and find $\delta(a,f)$ when $f(z)=2e^z$ and $a=0$. [[1+2]]
  13. 4(b) State and prove Nevanlinna's theorem on deficient values. [[1+4]]
  14. 4(c) Show that there can have at most two distinct values $a$ for which the equation $f(z)=a$ has no roots. [[2]]
  15. 5(a) When two meromorphic functions $f(z)$ and $g(z)$ share a value 'a' CM and IM? [[2]]
  16. 5(b) Let $f$ and $g$ be two non-constant polynomials and 'a' be a finite complex number. If $f$ and $g$ share 'a' CM then show that there exists a non-zero constant $k$ such that $(f-a)\equiv k(g-a)$. [[2]]
  17. 5(c) Let $f$ and $g$ be two non-constant rational functions. If $f$ and $g$ share two distinct values 'a' and 'b' CM, then show that $\frac{f-a}{f-b}\equiv k\frac{g-a}{g-b}$, $k\ne0$. [[4]]
  18. 5(d) Give an example with proper justification to show that there exist functions $f$ and $g$ share four values IM but $f\ne g$. [[2]]
  19. 6(a) State and prove Milloux theorem. [[1+5]]
  20. 6(b) If $f(z)$ is a non-constant meromorphic function then prove that $\rho_{f'}\le\rho_f$. [[2]]
  21. 6(c) Prove that derivative of an elliptic function is elliptic. [[2]]
