CCMA-14

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2025
  1. 1(a) Let $\phi:G_1\to G_2$ be a group homomorphism. Let $H_1$ be a subgroup of $G_1$ and let $H_2$ be a subgroup of $G_2$. Prove that $\phi(H_1)$ is a subgroup of $G_2$ and $\phi^{-1}(H_2)$ is a subgroup of $G_1$.
  2. 1(b) State and prove that first isomorphism theorem of groups.
  3. 1(c) Prove that there exist exactly two groups of order 4. [3+4+3]
  4. 2(a) Let $G$ be a finite abelian group of odd order. Prove that $\phi:G\to G$ defined by $\phi(x)=x^2$ is a group homomorphism. Justify whether $\phi$ is an isomorphism.
  5. 2(b) Let $\phi:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ be defined by $\phi((x,y))=x$. Prove that $\phi$ is a group homomorphism and determine $\ker(\phi)$.
  6. 2(c) Prove that $\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z$ is not a homomorphic image of $\mathbb Z/16\mathbb Z$.
  7. 2(d) Let $G$ be an infinite group such that $G$ is a homomorphic image of $\mathbb Z$. Prove that $G\simeq\mathbb Z$. [3+3+2+2]
  8. 3(a) Let $G$ be a group and let $\operatorname{Aut}(G)=\{\phi:G\to G:\phi\text{ is an isomorphism}\}$. Prove that $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is a group under the binary operation of composition of maps.
  9. 3(b) Let $G$ be a group such that $Z(G)=\{e\}$. Prove that $Z(\operatorname{Aut}(G))=\{\operatorname{Id}_G\}$.
  10. 3(c) Prove that the characteristic subgroups of $(\mathbb Q,+)$ are precisely $\{0\}$ and $\mathbb Q$. [3+4+3]
  11. 4(a) Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity and let $I$ and $J$ be two ideals in $R$ such that $I+J=R$. Prove that $R/I\cap J\simeq R/I\times R/J$. Hence or otherwise find the smallest positive integer $n$ that leaves a remainder of 3 when divided by 7 and a remainder of 4 when divided by 11.
  12. 4(b) Prove that $\mathbb Z[i]/\langle 2+i\rangle\simeq\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z$. [6+4]
  13. 5(a) Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity and let $P$ be a non-zero prime ideal in $R$. Is $P[X]=\left\{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{m}a_iX^i:a_i\in P\text{ and }m\in\mathbb N\right\}$ necessarily a prime ideal in $R[X]$? Justify your answer.
  14. 5(b) Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity and let $x\in R$ be a nilpotent element. Prove that $x$ belongs to every prime ideal of $R$.
  15. 5(c) Let $R_1$ and $R_2$ be two commutative rings with unity and let $\phi:R_1\to R_2$ be a ring homomorphism. Let $q$ be a prime ideal in $R_2$. Prove that $\phi^{-1}(q)$ is a prime ideal in $R_1$.
  16. 5(d) Let $R_1$ and $R_2$ be two commutative rings with unity and let $\phi:R_1\to R_2$ be a ring homomorphism. Prove that $\phi(\operatorname{Nil}(R_1))\subseteq\operatorname{Nil}(R_2)$. [4+2+2+2]
  17. 6(a) Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces over a field $F$ and let $T:V\to W$ be a linear transformation. Prove that $\dim(V)=\operatorname{rank}(T)+\operatorname{nullity}(T)$.
  18. 6(b) Let $V$ be a vector space of dimension $n\geq 2$ over a field $F$ and let $T:V\to V$ be a linear transformation. If $T^2\equiv0$, then prove that $\operatorname{rank}(T)\leq\frac n2$.
  19. 6(c) Let $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ and $U:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3$ be linear transformations. Can $U\circ T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ be invertible? Justify your answer. [4+3+3]
  20. 7(a) Let $S$ be a non-empty subset of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$. Prove that $S^0$ is a subspace of $V^*$. Moreover, if $S_1\subseteq S_2\subseteq V$, then prove that $S_2^0\subseteq S_1^0\subseteq V^*$.
  21. 7(b) Let $W$ be a subspace of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$. Prove that \[ \dim(W)+\dim(W^0)=\dim(V). \]
  22. 7(c) For two subspaces $W_1$ and $W_2$ of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$, prove that \[ (W_1+W_2)^0=W_1^0\cap W_2^0\quad\text{and}\quad(W_1\cap W_2)^0=W_1^0+W_2^0. \] [3+3+4]
  23. 8(a) Prove that two similar matrices have the same eigenvalues.
  24. 8(b) Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $F$ and let $T:V\to V$ be a linear transformation. Let $\lambda\in F$ be an eigenvalue of $T$ and let $E_\lambda=\{\alpha\in V:T(\alpha)=\lambda\alpha\}$. Prove that $E_\lambda$ is a subspace of $V$.
  25. 8(c) Let $\lambda\in F$ and $\mu\in F$ be two distinct eigenvalues of a linear transformation $T:V\to V$. Prove that $E_\lambda\cap E_\mu=\{0\}$.
  26. 8(d) Prove that the matrix $\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$ is not diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$ but is diagonalizable over $\mathbb C$. [2+2+2+4]
  27. 9(a) Prove that $A=\begin{bmatrix}3&1&-1\\2&2&-1\\2&2&0\end{bmatrix}$ is not diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$ but $B=\begin{bmatrix}5&-6&-6\\-1&4&2\\3&-6&-4\end{bmatrix}$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$.
  28. 9(b) Let $A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb R)$ be such that $A^k=0$ for some integer $k\geq 1$. Prove that $A^n=0$.
  29. 9(c) Let $n>2$ be an integer and let $A$ be an $n\times n$ real matrix such that $A^n=0$ but $A^{n-1}\ne0$. Prove that there does not exist any $n\times n$ real matrix $B$ such that $B^2=A$. [5+3+2]
