MMC-24

Classical Mechanics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2026
  1. 1 Derive the equation of motion of a particle relative to rotating earth as $\frac{\partial^2\vec r}{\partial t^2}+2\vec\omega\times\frac{\partial\vec r}{\partial t}=\frac{\vec F}m+\vec g$. Hence obtain the equation of motion of a free particle in three-dimensional Cartesian coordinate system. [8+2]
  2. 2 State Ferrel's law. A particle is projected vertically upward with a velocity $v$ from a point $O$ on the earth's surface. Prove that when it returns to the horizontal plane through $O$, it will have a westerly deviation $\frac{4v^3\omega\cos\lambda}{3g^2}$, where $\lambda$ is the latitude and $\omega$ is the angular velocity of the earth. [2+8]
  3. 3 Discuss small oscillation of a simple pendulum when the motion of the bob is not in a fixed vertical plane (earth's rotation is neglected). Hence show that the path described by the bob on the horizontal plane is an ellipse. [8+2]
  4. 4 Starting from D'Alembert's principle, obtain the Lagrange's equation of motion for a conservative field of force. Derive Hamilton's canonical equation of motion from Lagrange' s equation. [5+5]
  5. 5(a) Define the action integral. State Hamilton's principle of least action. Derive Euler's Lagrange formula from Hamilton's principle. [1+1+3]
  6. 5(b) Define Poisson bracket. Show that\par \hspace*{1em}(a) $\{q_i,q_j\}=0$\par \hspace*{1em}(b) $\{p_i,p_j\}=0$\par \hspace*{1em}(c) $\{p_i,q_j\}=\delta_{ij}$. [2+3]
  7. 6(a) Derive Euler's dynamical equation for the motion of a rigid body about a fixed point. [5]
  8. 6(b) A body moves under no forces about a fixed point $O$. The principal moments of inertia at $O$ be $6A,3A,A$. Initially, the angular velocity of the body has components $n,0,3n$ about the principal axes. Show that at any later time, $\omega_y=-\sqrt5n\tanh(\sqrt5nt)$. [5]
