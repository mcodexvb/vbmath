CC-3

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2022
  1. 1 Answer any five questions :
  2. 1(a) Find the derived sets of the sets \(\mathbb N,\ \mathbb R-\mathbb Q,\ \mathbb Q-\mathbb Z,\) and \(\{1/n:n=1,2,\ldots\}\).
  3. 1(b) Find the closure of the set \(\displaystyle\bigcup_{i=0}^{\infty}[(i,i+1)-\{i+\tfrac13,i+\tfrac23\}]\).
  4. 1(c) Give examples with justification of an open set which is not an interval and a closed set which is also not an interval.
  5. 1(d) Show that arbitrary union of closed sets may not be an closed set.
  6. 1(e) Find envelope of the family of lines \(\frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}=1\), where \(ab=c\).
  7. 1(f) Give examples with justification of two sets which are both open and closed.
  8. 1(g) Find area of the loop of the curve \(y^2=x(x-2)^2\)
  9. 2 State and prove Taylor's theorem with Lagranges's form of remainder. [5]
  10. 3 Find total length of perimeter of the astroid \(x^{\frac23}+y^{\frac23}=1\). [5]
  11. 4 Expand \(\log(1+x)\) in power series with proper justtification [5]
  12. 5 State and prove Lindelöf covering theorem. [5]
  13. 6 Show that every sequence of reals has a monotone subsequence. [5]
  14. 7 If \(\rho_1\) and \(\rho_2\) are the radii of curvature at the ends P and D of conjugate diameters CP and CD respectively of the ellipse \(\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1\), then show that \(\rho_1^{\frac23}+\rho_2^{\frac23}=\frac{a^2+b^2}{ab^{2/3}}\), where C is centre of the ellipse. [5]
  15. 1 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions. \((2\times5=10)\)
  16. 1(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item If \(a\) is odd, then show that \(32\mid(a^2+3)(a^2+7)\). \item State and proof Division Algorithm of integers. \end{enumerate}
  17. 1(b) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Show that there are infinite number of primes. \item If \(\gcd(a,b)=1\) then find \(\gcd(a^2+b^2,a^2b^2)\). \end{enumerate} [2]
  18. 1(c) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Find the general solution in integers of the equation \(5x+12y=80\). \item Examine if the equation \(7x+56y=90\) is Diophentine equation. \end{enumerate} [2]
  19. 2 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions. \((2\times10=20)\)
  20. 2(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Give an example of a mapping which is neither one-one nor onto? Justify your answer. \item Show that composition of two bijective mappings is a bijective mapping. \item In a group \(G\), show that \((ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}\). Find a condition on \(G\) such that \((ab)^{-1}=a^{-1}b^{-1}\). \end{enumerate}
  21. 2(b) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item In the group \(GL_2(\mathbb R)\) find two elements other than identity one of which is finite and other is of infinite order. \item Can a group be expressed as a union of its two proper subgroups? Support your answer. \item Find all subgroups of the group \(U_{50}=\{[a]\in\mathbb Z_{50}\mid\gcd(a,50)=1\}\). \end{enumerate} [6]
  22. 2(c) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi}.} \item Let \(G\) be a finite cyclic group of order \(n\). Then show that there is an element \(b\in G\) of order \(n\). Hence show that \(D_4\) is noncyclic. \item Let \(G\) be a finite cyclic group of order \(n\). Then show that there is a subgroup of order \(m\) in \(G\) for each \(m\mid n\). \end{enumerate} [5]
