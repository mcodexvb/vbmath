MJMA-9

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2025
  1. 1 Prove that the M.I. of a rigid body about a straight line having d.cs.\ $(l,m,n)$ is $Al^2+Bm^2+Cn^2-2Dmn-2Enl-2Flm$.
  2. 2 Find the principal axes of a right circular cone at a point on the circumference of the base and show that one of them will pass through its C.G., if the vertical angle of the cone be $2\tan^{-1}\frac12$.
  3. 3 Obtain the equation of motion relative to the centre of inertia.
  4. 4 A rough uniform board of mass $M$ and length $2l$, rests on a smooth horizontal plane, and a man of mass $m$ walks on it from one end to the other. Show that the distance through which the board moves is $\frac{2ml}{m+M}$.
  5. 5 Define compound pendulum. Show that the length of the simple equivalent pendulum of an elliptic lamina of semi-major axis $a$ and eccentricity $e$ when the axis of oscillation is a latus rectum, is $\frac a{2e}(1+2e^2)$.
  6. 6 Prove that the K.E. of a rigid body moving in two-dimensions is $\frac12Mv^2+\frac12Mk^2\dot\theta^2$.
  7. 7 A sphere of radious $a$ is projected with underhand spin $\Omega$ up a slope of angle $\alpha$. Show that if the velocity $V$ of projection be large, then the sphere will turn back after a time $\frac{5V-2a\Omega}{5g\sin\alpha}$. \par\textit{[Time and marks absent in the scan.]}
