CCMA-13

Analysis VI
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2021
  1. 1(a) Examine the continuity and analyticity of the function $f(z)=z^{\frac12}$ in $\mathbb C$. [4]
  2. 1(b) Explain the difference between the differentiability and analyticity of a complex function. Justify your answer with examples. [4]
  3. 1(c) Prove or disprove: If a function $f:\mathbb C\longrightarrow\mathbb C$ is continuous, then $f'(z)$ exists at least at some $z\in\mathbb C$. [2]
  4. 2(a) Let $f:\mathbb C\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ be differentiable. Prove that $f$ is constant. [4]
  5. 2(b) If $f(z)$ is continuous at a point $z_0$, prove that $\overline{f(z)}$ is also continuous at $z_0$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Determine the convergence of the sequence $\left\{\sin\frac{n\pi}4\right\}$. [3]
  7. 3(a) Prove or disprove: If $\{|z_n|\}$ converges, then $\{z_n\}$ also converges. [2]
  8. 3(b) Show that the Cauchy-Riemann equations are satisfied for the function \[f(z)=\begin{cases} \frac{xy}{x^2+y^2}&\text{for }z\ne0\\ 0&\text{for }z=0 \end{cases}\] at $z=0$ but still $f'(0)$ does not exist. [4]
  9. 3(c) Find all the analytic functions whose imaginary part is $e^x\cos y$. [4]
  10. 4(a) Determine all the bilinear transformations of the half-plane $Re(z)\ge0$ into the unit disc $|w|\le1$. [6]
  11. 4(b) Prove or disprove: $|\sin z|\le1$, for all $z\in\mathbb C$. [4]
  12. 5(a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{x\sin 3x}{x^2+9}\,dx$ using the method of contour integration. [6]
  13. 5(b) Determine the image of the exterior of the circle $|z+2|=2$ under the mapping $f(z)=\frac{z}{2z+8}$. [4]
  14. 6(a) Let $X$ be a non empty set and $\rho$ be a real valued function of ordered pairs of elements of $X$ which satisfies the following two conditions: \par $\rho(x,y)=0$ if and only if $x=y$ and $\rho(x,y)\le \rho(x,z)+\rho(y,z)$. Show that $\rho(x,y)$ is a metric on $X$. [3]
  15. 6(b) Show that the space of all convergent sequences of real numbers with a suitable metric defined by you form a metric space. [4]
  16. 6(c) Let $(X,\rho)$ be a metric space and $V\subset X$. Then $V$ is an open set if and only if it is the union of open spheres. [3]
  17. 7(a) Show by an example the union of an infinite number of closed sets need not be closed. [2]
  18. 7(b) Prove that a Cauchy sequence which has a convergent subsequence is itself convergent. [3]
  19. 7(c) Show that the Euclidean space $R^n$ is a complete metric space. [5]
  20. 8(a) State and prove Cantor's intersection theorem. [4]
  21. 8(b) Define a set of second category. Give an example of it. [2]
  22. 8(c) State and prove Baire's category theorem. [4]
  23. 9(a) Define a contraction mapping. Give an example of it. [3]
  24. 9(b) Show that any contraction mapping is uniformly continuous. [2]
  25. 9(c) State and prove Banach's contraction principle theorem. [5]
  26. 10(a) A function $f:X\to Y$ is continuous if and only if $f^{-1}(B)$ is an open subset of $X$ whenever $B$ is an open subset of $Y$-prove it. [4]
  27. 10(b) Define a connected metric space. \par Prove that a metric space $(X,\rho)$ is connected if and only if $X$ and $\phi$ are the only sets which are both open and closed in $(X,\rho)$. [6]
