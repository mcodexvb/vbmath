MMC-14

Ordinary Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Use Picard's method of successive approximations and find the first and second approximate solutions of\par \[\frac{dy}{dx}=z;\ \frac{dz}{dx}=x^3(y+z),\ \text{where, }y=1;\ z=\tfrac12\text{ when }x=0.\] [4]
  2. 1(b) State the existence and uniqueness theorem of first order initial value problem. Hence determine the interval of existence of the \textit{IVP}: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=y^2$, $y(1)=-1$, where the rectangle $R$ is given by\par \[R:\{(x,y):|x-1|\le a,\ |y+1|\le b\}\] [1+3]
  3. 1(c) Consider $f(x,y)=x^3|y|$. Prove that $f$ satisfies a Lipschitz condition on $R:|x|\le2,\ |y|\le2$ but $\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial y}$ does not exist at $(x,0)$ if $x\ne0$. [2]
  4. 2(a) What is a fundamental matrix of vector differential equation? Show that if $\phi(t)$ is a fundamental matrix of $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=A(t)X$, then the solution of $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=A(t)X+F(t)$ can be assumed as\par \[X(t)=\phi(t)\int_{t_0}^{t}\phi^{-1}(u)F(u)\,du.\] [4]
  5. 2(b) Find the unique solution of the differential equation $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=\begin{pmatrix}6&-3\\2&1\end{pmatrix}X+\begin{pmatrix}e^{5t}\\4\end{pmatrix}$ that satisfies the initial condition $\phi(0)=\begin{pmatrix}9\\4\end{pmatrix}$. [4]
  6. 2(c) Is $\phi(t)=\begin{pmatrix}2e^t&-e^{-3t}\\-4e^t&2e^{-3t}\end{pmatrix}$ a fundamental matrix for the system $\dot x=Ax$? [2]
  7. 3(a) If $P^{-1}AP=D$, $D$ being a diagonal matrix, then show that\par \[e^{At}=Pe^{Dt}P^{-1}=P\begin{bmatrix}e^{\lambda_1t}&0\\-&e^{\lambda_2t}\end{bmatrix}P^{-1},\text{ where }D=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda_1&0\\0&\lambda_2\end{bmatrix}.\] [3]
  8. 3(b) Show that, $e^{A+B}=e^Ae^B$, provided $AB=BA$. \par\hfill P.T.O. [3]
  9. 3(c) Write down the theorem for solving the homogeneous system of first order ODE,\par \[\frac{dx}{dt}=AX,\] where the matrix $A$ has repeated eigen values.\par Hence solve\par \hspace*{2em}$\dot x=5x+4y$\par \hspace*{2em}$\dot y=-x+y$ [4]
  10. 4(a) Find all the eigen values and eigen functions of the sturm-Liouville problem,\par \hspace*{2em}$y''+\lambda y=0$, with $y(0)+y'(0)=0$ and $y(1)+y'(1)=0$. [5]
  11. 4(b) Find the Green's function of the ODE:\par \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-y=0\text{ with }y(0)=0=y(1).\text{ Hence solve }\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-y=x.\] [5]
  12. 5(a) If $f$ be the solution of $\dfrac d{dt}\left[P(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}\right]+\varphi(t)x=0$ having first derivative $f'$ on $a\le t\le b$ and has a infinite number of zeros on $a\le t\le b$, then show that $f(t)=0$ for all $t$ on $a\le t\le b$. [3]
  13. 5(b) If $f$ and $g$ be any two solution of $\dfrac d{dt}\left[P(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}\right]+\varphi(t)x=0$ on $a\le t\le b$, then show that\par \[P(t)\left[f(t)g'(t)-f'(t)g(t)\right]\text{ is constant for all }t\in[a,b].\] [3]
  14. 5(c) If $f$ and $g$ be the two solutions of\par \[\frac d{dt}\left[P(t)\frac{dx}{dt}\right]+\varphi(t)x=0\] Such $f$ and $g$ have a common zero, then show that $f$ and $g$ are linearly independent on $a\le t\le b$. [4]
  15. 6(a) Find the condition under which the fixed point of the system:\par \hspace*{2em}$\dot x=ax+by$\par \hspace*{2em}$\dot y=cx+dy$ \hfill is a centre. [3]
  16. 6(b) Show that the linearized system corresponding to\par \hspace*{2em}$\dot x=-y+px\left(x^2+y^2\right),$\par \hspace*{2em}$\dot y=x+py\left(x^2+y^2\right)$\par ($P$ is a parameter), predicts the fixed point as a centre $\forall p$.. Show further that the fixed point is a stable spiral if $p<0$ and unstable spiral if $p>0$ for the original system. [4]
  17. 6(c) Linearize the system $\dot x=e^{-x-3y}-1$, $\dot y=-x\left(1-y^2\right)$ about the fixed point $(0,0)$. Also classify the fixed point. [2+1]
