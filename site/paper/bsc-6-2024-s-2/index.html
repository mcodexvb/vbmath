CCMA-14

Algebra IV
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2024
  1. 1(a) Let $\phi:G_1\to G_2$ be a group homomorphism and let $x_1\in G_1$. If $o(x_1)$ is finite, then prove that $o(\phi(x_1))$ divides $o(x_1)$.
  2. 1(b) Determine, with justification, the number of group homomorphisms from $\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z$ to $\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z$.
  3. 1(c) Let $\phi:G_1\to G_2$ be a group homomorphism. Prove that $\phi$ is injective if and only if $\ker(\phi)=\{e_1\}$. [3+3+4]
  4. 2(a) Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. When do you say that “$H$ is a characteristic subgroup of $G$”?
  5. 2(b) If $H\operatorname{char}G$, then prove that $H\trianglelefteq G$.
  6. 2(c) Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$ and let $g\in G$. Prove that $H\simeq gHg^{-1}$, where $gHg^{-1}=\{ghg^{-1}:h\in H\}$. [2+4+4]
  7. 3(a) Let $G'$ be the commutator subgroup of $G$. Prove that $G'\trianglelefteq G$ and $G/G'$ is abelian.
  8. 3(b) Let $N\trianglelefteq G$ be such that $N\cap G'=\{e\}$. Prove that $N\subseteq Z(G)$. [6+4]
  9. 4(a) Let $R$ be a ring of characteristic $p$, where $p$ is a prime number. Prove that the map $\phi:R\to R$ defined by $\phi(r)=r^p$ is a ring homomorphism.
  10. 4(b) Prove that the fields $\mathbb R$ and $\mathbb C$ are not isomorphic.
  11. 4(c) Let $\phi:\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q$ be a ring homomorphism. Prove that $\phi(x)=x$ for all $x\in\mathbb Q$. [4+3+3]
  12. 5(a) Let $R$ be a ring and let $I_1$ and $I_2$ be two ideals of $R$ with $I_1\subseteq I_2$. Prove that $\left.R/I_1\middle/I_2/I_1\right.\simeq R/I_2$.
  13. 5(b) Prove that the ring $C[0,1]=\{f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R:f\text{ is continuous}\}$ is not an integral domain.
  14. 5(c) Let $R$ be an integral domain with fraction field $K$. If $S$ is an integral domain with $R\subseteq S\subseteq K$, then prove that $\mathbb Q(S)=K$. [4+3+3]
  15. 6(a) Prove that every non-zero prime ideal in a Boolean ring is a maximal ideal.
  16. 6(b) Prove that $I=\{f\in C[0,1]:f(\frac12)=0\}$ is a maximal ideal in $C[0,1]$.
  17. 6(c) Give an example, with justification, of an ideal $J$ of $C[0,1]$ that is not a prime ideal. [4+4+2]
  18. 7(a) Let $F$ be a field and let $V$ be a vector space of dimension $n$ over $F$. Let $B$ be a fixed $n\times n$ matrix over $F$. Prove that $T:V\to V$ defined by $T(A)=AB-BA$ is a linear transformation.
  19. 7(b) Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over a field $F$ and let $T:V\to V$ be a linear operator such that $\operatorname{Im}(T)=\ker(T)$. Prove that $n$ is an even integer. Moreover, find an example of such a linear operator for some vector space $V$.
  20. 7(c) Let $T:V\to V$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ over a field $F$. Prove that the following two statements are equivalent. \par (i) $\ker(T)\cap\operatorname{Im}(T)=\{0\}$. \par (ii) If $T(T(\alpha))=0$ for some $\alpha\in V$, then $T(\alpha)=0$. [3+3+4]
  21. 8(a) Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces over a field $F$ and let $T:V\to W$ be a linear transformation. Prove that $\ker(T^t)=(\operatorname{Im}(T))^0$.
  22. 8(b) Let $A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb R)$ be such that $\operatorname{trace}(A^tA)=0$. Prove that $A=0$.
  23. 8(c) Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb R$ and let $f,g\in V^*$. Assume that $h:V\to\mathbb R$ defined by $h(\alpha)=f(\alpha)g(\alpha)$ is also an element of $V^*$. Prove that either $f\equiv0$ or $g\equiv0$. [3+3+4]
  24. 9(a) Let $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ be a linear operator such that $T^2\equiv0$. Prove that either $T\equiv0$ or there exists a basis of $\mathbb R^2$ relative to which the matrix of $T$ is $\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$.
  25. 9(b) Let $A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{bmatrix}\in M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)$. Prove that $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$.
  26. 9(c) Let $A=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&0&0\\a&0&0&0\\0&b&0&0\\0&0&c&0\end{bmatrix}\in M_{4\times4}(\mathbb R)$. Find, with justification, conditions on $a,b$ and $c$ such that $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$. [3+4+3]
