CC-1

Analysis-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2022
  1. 1(a) Evaluate $\lim_{x\to a}\cos x$ by using $(\epsilon-\delta)$ definition of the limit of a function. [[2]]
  2. 1(b) If $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=l$ and $\lim_{x\to a}g(x)=m(\ne0)$ then prove that $\lim_{x\to a}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac lm$. [[6]]
  3. 1(c) Examine if $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{2x+|x|}{2x-|x|}$ exists. [[2]]
  4. 2(a) Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous such that for $c\in(a,b)$, $f(c)\ne0$. Show that $f(x)$ has the same sign as that of $f(c)$ in some neighbourhood of $c$. [[4]]
  5. 2(b) A function $f$ is defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}\cos\frac1x&\text{for }x\ne0\\0&\text{for }x=0\end{cases}$ Is $f$ continuous at $x=0$? Support your answer. [[3]]
  6. 2(c) A function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies the condition $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)$ for all $x,y\in\mathbb R$. If $f$ is continuous at one point $c\in\mathbb R$, then prove that $f$ is continuous at every point in $\mathbb R$. [[3]]
  7. 3(a) State and prove nested intervals theorem. [[5]]
  8. 3(b) Prove that the sequence $\{x_n\}$ defined by $x_1=\sqrt2$ and $x_{n+1}=\sqrt{2x_n}$ for all $n\ge1$ converges to 2. [[3]]
  9. 3(c) Evaluate $\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1.3.5.\dots.(2n-1)}{2.4.6.\dots.(2n)}$. [[2]]
  10. 4(a) Prove that a sequence of real numbers is convergent iff it is a Cauchy sequence. [[6]]
  11. 4(b) Give an example of a sequence of rational numbers that converges to an irrational number. [[1]]
  12. 4(c) Evaluate $\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\{(2n+1)(2n+2)\cdots(2n+n)\}^{\frac1n}$. [[3]]
  13. 5(a) Prove that a point $x$ is a limit point of a set $A$ in $\mathbb R$ iff every neighbourhood of $x$ contains infinitely many points of $A$. [[3]]
  14. 5(b) Let $S$ be a non-empty subset of $\mathbb R$ and $T=\{-x:x\in S\}$. Prove that $T$ is bounded and $\sup T=-\inf S$ and $\inf T=-\sup S$. [[4]]
  15. 5(c) State Archimedean property of $\mathbb R$. Prove that if $x\in\mathbb R$ and $x>0$ then there exists a natural number $n$ such that $0<\frac1n<x$. [[3]]
  16. 6(a) If $y=x^{n-1}\log x$ then find $y_n$. [[3]]
  17. 6(b) If $y=(x^2-1)^n$ then show that $(x^2-1)y_{n+2}+2xy_{n+1}-n(n+1)y_n=0$. [[4]]
  18. 6(c) If $y=x^{2n}$ where $n$ is a positive integer, then show that $y_n=2^n\{1.3.5.\cdots.(2n-1)\}x^n$. [[3]]
  19. 7(a) State and prove Rolle's theorem and give its geometrical interpretation. [[5]]
  20. 7(b) Prove that $\frac x{x+1}<\log(1+x)<x$, $\forall x>0$. [[3]]
  21. 7(c) In the Mean Value Theorem, $f(x+h)=f(x)+hf'(x+\theta h)$, show that $\theta$ is independent of $x$ where $f(x)$ is given by $f(x)=a+bx+cm^x$. [[2]]
  22. 8(a) State L. Hospital's theorem and hence evaluate $\lim_{x\to0}\left(\frac{\tan x}x\right)^{\frac1x}$. [[5]]
  23. 8(b) Find the constants $a$ and $b$ so that $\lim_{\theta\to0}\frac{\theta(1+a\cos\theta)-b\sin\theta}{\theta^3}=1$. [[5]]
  24. 9(a) If $I_n=\int x^n\sqrt{a-x}\,dx$ then prove that $I_n=\frac{2na}{2n+3}I_{n-1}-\frac{2x^n}{2n+3}(a-x)^{\frac32}$ and hence evaluate $\int_0^ax^3\sqrt{ax-x^2}\,dx$. [[6]]
  25. 9(b) State and prove Cauchy's Mean Value theorem and deduce Lagrange's Mean Value theorem from this theorem. [[4]]
