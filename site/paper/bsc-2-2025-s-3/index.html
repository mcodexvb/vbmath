SECMA-2

Analysis-II and Tensor Calculus
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1(a) What do you mean by a bounded sequence? Show that a convergent sequence is bounded.
  2. 1(b) Prove, using $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations, that $\lim_{z\to\infty}\left(7+\frac1{z+i}\right)=7$.
  3. 1(c) If $(\xi,\eta,\zeta)$ and $(\xi',\eta',\zeta')$ are the stereographic projections of the points $z$ and $\frac1{\bar z}$ respectively, prove that $\xi=\xi',\ \eta+\eta'=0,\ \zeta+\zeta'=1$.
  4. 2(a) Use $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations to prove that the function $f(z)=z^4$ is continuous at $z=a(\ne0)$.
  5. 2(b) Show that a bounded infinite set has at least one limit point.
  6. 2(c) Find the stereographic projection of the circle $x^2+y^2+4x-6y+7=0$ in the complex plane $\mathbb C$ on the Riemann sphere $\xi^2+\eta^2+\left(\zeta-\frac12\right)^2=\frac14$. [[3+4+3]]
  7. 3(a) When is a function $f$ said to be continuous at a point $z_0$? Explain an example by $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations.
  8. 3(b) Define limit points of a sequence $\{z_n\}$. Find the limit points of $\left\{(-i)^n\left(1+\frac{3i}n\right)\right\}$.
  9. 3(c) Prove that a complex function continuous on a closed and bounded region is bounded.
  10. 1 Define scalar, vector, and tensor fields. Explain covariant, contravariant, and mixed tensors and their order and rank. A field $A(p,q,r,s)(\bar x)$ of coordinates $\bar x=(x^1,x^2,x^3,\cdots,x^n)$ is such that their relations in two coordinate systems $\bar x$ and $\bar{\bar x}(=(\bar x^1,\bar x^2,\cdots,\bar x^n))$ are\par $\bar A(i,j,k,l)(\bar{\bar x})=\frac{\partial\bar x^k}{\partial x^s}\frac{\partial\bar x^i}{\partial x^r}\frac{\partial x^q}{\partial\bar x^j}\frac{\partial x^p}{\partial\bar x^l}A(p,q,r,s)(\bar x),\quad p,q,r,s,i,j,k,l=1,2,\cdots,n.$\par Examine whether the field is a tensor field. Find its order and rank, if yes. [5]
  11. 2 Transform the tensor field $(2x+y,xyz,(x+y)^2z)$ in Cartesian coordinates to the contravariant and covariant components in spherical polar coordinates. [5]
  12. 3 Define inner multiplication, contraction, and quotient law of tensor fields. If $A^{pqr}_i$ and $B^{lm}_{st}$ are two tensor fields, examine which one among their sum, difference $A^{pqr}_i\pm B^{lm}_{st}$, and the product $A^{pqr}_iB^{lm}_{st}$ is a tensor field. Justify the answer. Prove that the contraction of $B^j_i$ is a scalar field. [5]
  13. 4 Define symmetric and skew-symmetric tensors. Given $A^{ijk}$ and $B_{lmn}$ are two skew-symmetric tensors. Verify the (symmetric/skew-symmetric) nature of $C^{ij}_{lm}=A^{ijk}B_{lmk}$. If $\Phi=a_{pq}A^pA^q$, show that $\Phi$ can be expressed as $\Phi=b_{ij}A^iA^j$, where $b_{ij}$ is a symmetric tensor field. [5]
  14. 5 Let $A(i,j,k)$ be such that $A(i,j,k)B^{jl}_i=C^l_k$, where $B^{jl}_i$ is an arbitrary tensor field and $C^l_k$ is a mixed tensor of rank $(1,1)$. Prove that $A(i,j,k)$ is a tensor field. Determine its rank. [5]
  15. 6 Prove that the partial derivative of a scalar field is a vector field, while the partial derivative of a vector or a tensor field is not a tensor field. Define covariant derivative. Write down the formula for the covariant derivative of the tensor field $A^{ij}_{klm}(\bar x)$ with respect to the variable $x^r$. [5]
  16. 7 Define the line element of a curve in space. Show that the square of the line element can be expressed as a quadratic form? Do they behave like a tensor? Examine whether it is symmetric or anti-symmetric, if so. Provide a geometric interpretation of the coefficients. [5]
  17. 8 Find metric tensors $g_{ij}$ and its associated tensors $g^{ij}$ in the cylindrical polar coordinates $(\rho,\phi,z)$ and spherical polar coordinates $(r,\theta,\phi)$. [5]
  18. 9 Define Christoffel's symbols of the first and second kind. Evaluate the expressions for Christoffel's first- and second-kind symbols in orthogonal curvilinear coordinates in terms of partial derivatives of metric tensors. Hence, obtain their expressions for Cartesian coordinate systems. [5]
  19. 10 Derive the equation for a geodesic on a curved surface. Hence, find two geodesics on a cylindrical surface of radius $a$, passing through a given point on it. Interpret those geodesics. [5]
  20. 11 Write the formula for the second-order covariant derivatives of a vector field $B_i(\bar x)$ with respect to two variables $x^l,x^m$ (say). Is it commutative under the interchange of derivatives with respect to $x^l,x^m$? If no, find the difference $(B_{i,lm}-B_{i,ml})$, and examine whether this difference can be written as a sum of $B_l(\bar x)$. Hence or otherwise define the Riemann-Christoffel tensor. [5]
  21. 12 Prove the formulae a) $\mathrm{div}A^p=\frac1{\sqrt g}\frac\partial{\partial x^k}(\sqrt gA^k)$, b) $\Gamma^i_{ij}=\frac\partial{\partial x^j}\sqrt g$. [5]
