TIFR Graduate School · GS 2022 · Mathematics

  1. 1 Consider the following properties of a metric space $(X, d)$: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Roman*)] \item $(X, d)$ is complete as a metric space. \item For any sequence $\{Z_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of closed nonempty subsets of $X$, such that $Z_1 \supseteq Z_2 \supseteq \cdots$ and $\lim_{n \to \infty} (\sup_{x, y \in Z_n} d(x, y)) = 0$, $\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty Z_n$ is a singleton set. \end{enumerate} Which of the following sentences is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item (I) implies (II) and (II) implies (I). \item (I) implies (II) but (II) does not imply (I). \item (I) does not imply (II) but (II) implies (I). \item (I) does not imply (II) and (II) does not imply (I). \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Consider the following assertions: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Roman*)] \item $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid xy = 1\}$ is connected. \item $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{C}^2 \mid xy = 1\}$ is connected. \end{enumerate} Which of the following sentences is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Both (I) and (II) are true. \item (I) is true but (II) is false. \item (I) is false but (II) is true. \item Both (I) and (II) are false. \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 What is the number of solutions of $x = \frac{x^2}{50} - \cos\frac{x}{2} + 2$ in $[0, 10]$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $0$ \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $\infty$ \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Let $A$ be an element of $M_4(\mathbb{R})$ with characteristic polynomial $t^4 - t$. What is the characteristic polynomial of $A^2$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $t^4 - t$ \item $t^4 - 2t^3 + t^2$ \item $t^4 - t^2$ \item None of the other three options \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $V = \{f \in \mathbb{R}[x] \mid \deg f \le n\}$ be the real vector space of real polynomials of degree at most $n$. Let $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{R}}(V)$ denote the real vector space of linear transformations from $V$ to itself. For $m \in \mathbb{Z}$, let $T_m \in \operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{R}}(V)$ be such that $(T_m f)(x) = f(x + m)$ for all $f \in V$. Then the dimension of the vector subspace of $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{R}}(V)$ given by $\operatorname{Span}(\{T_m \mid m \in \mathbb{Z}\})$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $n$ \item $n + 1$ \item $n^2$ \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Let $T$ be the linear transformation from the real vector space $\mathbb{R}[x]$ to itself, given by $T(f) = f'$, where $f'$ is the derivative of $f$. Consider the following statements about $T$: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Roman*)] \item $T$ is nilpotent. \item The only eigenvalue of $T$ is $0$. \end{enumerate} Which of the following sentences is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Both (I) and (II) are true. \item (I) is true but (II) is false. \item (I) is false but (II) is true. \item Both (I) and (II) are false. \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 What is the cardinality of the set of $\theta \in [0, 2\pi)$ such that the linear map $\mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ given by the matrix \[ \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \] has an eigenvector in $\mathbb{R}^2$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $4$ \item $\infty$ \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Let $p$ be a prime number, and let $A$ equal $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ viewed as a $2 \times 2$ matrix with integer entries. What is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that the matrix $A^n$ is congruent to the $2 \times 2$ identity matrix modulo $p$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $p^2 - 1$ \item $p - 1$ \item $p$ \item $p + 1$ \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 What is the largest value of $n$ for which there exists a set $\{A_1, \dots, A_n\}$ of (distinct) nonzero matrices in $M_2(\mathbb{C})$ such that $A_i^* A_j$ has trace zero for all $1 \le i < j \le n$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item Greater than $1$ but at most $4$ \item Greater than $4$ but finite \item $\infty$ \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Let $p$ be a prime number. What is the number of elements in the group $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ that have order exactly $p$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $0$ \item $p - 1$ \item $p$ \item $\infty$ \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 Consider the real polynomial $f(x) = x^{11} - x^7 + x^2 - 1$. Which of the following sentences is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(x)$ has exactly one positive root. \item $f(x)$ has exactly two positive roots. \item $f(x)$ has at least three positive roots. \item None of the other three options. \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Consider polynomials $f_1(x, y) = \sum_{i,j=0}^\infty a_{ij} x^i y^j$ and $f_2(x, y) = \sum_{i,j=0}^\infty b_{ij} x^i y^j \in \mathbb{R}[x, y]$ (where $a_{ij} = b_{ij} = 0$ for all but finitely many $(i, j) \in \mathbb{N}^2$), such that $f_1(p, q) = f_2(p, q)$ for all $(p, q) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ satisfying $p^2 = q^2$. Which of the following sentences is true for all such $f_1$ and $f_2$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $a_{00} = b_{00}$, but we may not have $a_{ij} = b_{ij}$ for all $(i, j)$ with $i + j = 1$. \item $a_{ij} = b_{ij}$ if $i + j \le 1$, but we may not have $a_{ij} = b_{ij}$ for all $(i, j)$ with $i + j = 2$. \item $a_{ij} = b_{ij}$ if $i + j \le 2$, but we may not have $a_{ij} = b_{ij}$ for all $(i, j)$ with $i + j = 3$. \item $a_{ij} = b_{ij}$ if $i + j \le 3$, but we may not have $f_1 = f_2$. \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 What is the number of bijections $f: \{1, 2, \dots, 9\} \to \{1, 2, \dots, 9\}$ such that, for all distinct $i, j \in \{1, \dots, 9\}$, whenever the squares labelled $i$ and $j$ in the diagram below share an edge, the squares labelled $f(i)$ and $f(j)$ share an edge too? \begin{center} \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|} \hline 1 & 2 & 3 \\ \hline 4 & 5 & 6 \\ \hline 7 & 8 & 9 \\ \hline \end{tabular} \end{center} \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $8$ \item $9$ \item $4$ \item $24$ \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 Let $m$ be the number of positive integers $n$ such that $1 \le n \le 2022$ and such that $n$ has an odd number of (positive integer) divisors. Then $m$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $22$ \item $33$ \item $44$ \item $55$ \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 Let $S$ be the set of nonnegative continuous functions $f$ on $[0,1]$ satisfying \[ \int_0^1 \sin^2(x) f(x)\,dx = \int_0^1 \sin(x)\cos(x) f(x)\,dx = \int_0^1 \cos^2(x) f(x)\,dx = 1. \] Then $S$ is: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item an uncountable set \item a countably infinite set \item a finite and nonempty set \item the empty set \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 Let $f(x) = 1 - \sin x$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Define \[ a_n = \sqrt[n]{f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) f\left(\frac{2}{n}\right) \cdots f\left(\frac{n}{n}\right)}. \] Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\{a_n\}_n$ converges to $0$ \item $\{a_n\}_n$ diverges to $\infty$ \item $\{a_n\}_n$ converges and $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n > 0$ \item none of the other three options is correct \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Which of the following is true for every function $u: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ which is continuously differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ (i.e., $u$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ and its derivative $u'$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$), and satisfies $u(y) \ge u(x) + u'(x)(y - x)$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $u'$ is nonnegative. \item $u$ attains a minimum at some $x \in \mathbb{R}$. \item $u'$ is nondecreasing. \item $u'$ is nonincreasing. \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 Let $\{x_n\}$ be a sequence of positive numbers such that $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = x$. Define \[ z_n = \frac{1}{n}\left[x_1 \left(1 + \frac{x}{n}\right)^n + x_2 \left(1 + \frac{x}{n-1}\right)^{n-1} + \dots + x_n (1 + x)\right]. \] Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\{z_n\}$ converges to $xe$ \item $\{z_n\}$ converges to $e^x$ \item $\{z_n\}$ does not have a limit \item $\{z_n\}$ converges to $x e^x$ \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 The value of $\displaystyle \lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^1 \frac{n e^x}{1 + n^2 x^2}\,dx$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\pi e$ \item $\frac{\pi}{2}$ \item $\frac{\pi}{2} e$ \item $\pi$ \end{enumerate}
  20. 20 What is the number of real solutions of the equation $e^{\sin x} = \pi^x$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $0$ \item $1$ \item Countably infinite \item Uncountable \end{enumerate}
  21. 1 $\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \mathbb{Q}^2$ is connected but not path-connected.
  22. 2 If $X$ is a connected metric space, and $F$ is a subring of $C(X, \mathbb{R})$ that is a field, then every element of $C(X, \mathbb{R})$ that belongs to $F$ is a constant function.
  23. 3 Let $K \subseteq [0,1]$ be the Cantor set. Then there exists no injective ring homomorphism $C([0,1], \mathbb{R}) \to C(K, \mathbb{R})$.
  24. 4 There exists a metric space $(X, d)$ such that the group of isometries of $X$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$.
  25. 5 Let $A \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be a nonempty subset such that any continuous function $f: A \to \mathbb{R}$ is constant. Then $A$ is a singleton set.
  26. 6 For a nilpotent matrix $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$, let \[ \exp(A) := \sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{A^k}{k!} = \operatorname{Id} + \frac{A}{1!} + \frac{A^2}{2!} + \dots \in M_n(\mathbb{R}). \] If $A$ is a nilpotent matrix such that $\exp(A) = \operatorname{Id}$, then $A$ is the zero matrix.
  27. 7 There exists $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$, with $A^2 = A \ne 0$, such that $|a| + |b| < 1$ and $|c| + |d| < 1$.
  28. 8 If $A \in M_3(\mathbb{C})$ is such that $A^i$ has trace zero for all positive integers $i$, then $A$ is nilpotent.
  29. 9 For any finite cyclic group $G$, there exists a prime power $q$ such that $G$ is a subgroup of $\mathbb{F}_q^\times$.
  30. 10 There are only finitely many isomorphism classes of finite nonabelian groups, all of whose proper subgroups are abelian.
  31. 11 Every subring of a unique factorization domain is a unique factorization domain.
  32. 12 Let $f_1, f_2, f_3, f_4 \in \mathbb{R}[x]$ be monic polynomials each of degree exactly two. Then there exist a real polynomial $p \in \mathbb{R}[x]$ and a subset $\{i, j\} \subset \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ with $i \ne j$, such that $f_i \circ p = c f_j$ for some $c \in \mathbb{R}$.
  33. 13 There exists a finite abelian group $G$ such that the group $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ of automorphisms of $G$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z}$.
  34. 14 There exists an integral domain $R$ and a surjective homomorphism $R \to R$ of rings that is not injective.
  35. 15 There exists $f \in C([0,1], \mathbb{R})$ satisfying the following two conditions: \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item $\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx = 1$, and \item $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^1 f(x)^n\,dx = 0$. \end{enumerate}
  36. 16 Let $a_n \ge 0$ for each positive integer $n$. If the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \sqrt{a_n}$ converges, then so does the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{a_n}{n^{1/4}}$.
  37. 17 There exists a differentiable function $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = 2$ and $\lim_{x \to \infty} f'(x) = 1$.
  38. 18 Let $f: [0,1] \to [0,\infty)$ be continuous on $[0,1]$ and twice differentiable in $(0,1)$. If $f''(x) = 7f(x)$ for all $x \in (0,1)$, then $f(x) \le \max\{f(0), f(1)\}$ for all $x \in [0,1]$.
  39. 19 There are $N$ balls in a box, out of which $n$ are blue ($1 < n < N$) and the rest are red. Balls are drawn from the box one by one at random, and discarded. Then the probability of picking all the blue balls in the first $n$ draws is the same as the probability of picking all the red balls in the first $(N - n)$ draws.
  40. 20 The set $\{f(x) \in \mathbb{R}[x] \mid f(n) \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ for all } n \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ is uncountable.
