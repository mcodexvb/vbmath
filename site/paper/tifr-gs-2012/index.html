TIFR Graduate School · GS 2012 · Mathematics

  1. 1 If $H_1$ \& $H_2$ are subgroups of a group $G$ then $H_1 \cdot H_2 = \{h_1 h_2 \in G \mid h_1 \in H_1, \, h_2 \in H_2\}$ is a subgroup of $G$.
  2. 2 There exist polynomials $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, with complex coefficients, such that $\left(\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right)^2 = x$.
  3. 3 Let $f$ be real valued, differentiable on $(a, b)$ and $f'(x) \ne 0$ for all $x \in (a, b)$. Then $f$ is $1$--$1$.
  4. 4 The inequality $\displaystyle \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{(\log \log 2)^n}{n!} > \frac{3}{5}$ holds.
  5. 5 Every subgroup of order $74$ in a group of order $148$ is normal.
  6. 6 Let $u_1, u_2, u_3, u_4$ be vectors in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $u = \sum_{j=1}^4 t_j u_j$; $t_j > 0$ and $\sum_{j=1}^4 t_j = 1$. Then three vectors $v_1, v_2, v_3 \in \mathbb{R}^2$ may be chosen from $\{u_1, u_2, u_3, u_4\}$ such that $u = \sum_{j=1}^3 s_j v_j$, $s_j \ge 0$, $\sum_{j=1}^3 s_j = 1$.
  7. 7 The inequality $\sqrt{1 + x} < 1 + x/2$ for $x \in (-1, 10)$ is true.
  8. 8 If $n$ is not a multiple of $23$ then the remainder when $n^{11}$ is divided by $23$ is $\pm 1 \pmod{23}$.
  9. 9 Suppose $A$ is a nilpotent matrix and $I$ is the identity matrix. Then $(I + A)$ is invertible.
  10. 10 The equations \begin{align*} x_1 + \frac{1}{2} x_2 + \frac{1}{3} x_3 &= 1 \\ x_1 + \frac{1}{4} x_2 + \frac{1}{9} x_3 &= 1 \\ x_1 + \frac{1}{8} x_2 + \frac{1}{27} x_3 &= 1 \end{align*} have no solution.
  11. 11 The automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2)$ is abelian.
  12. 12 Let $V$ be the vector space consisting of polynomials of $\mathbb{R}[t]$ of $\deg \le 2$. The map $T: V \to V$ sending $f(t)$ to $f(t) + f'(t)$ is invertible.
  13. 13 The polynomials $(t - 1)(t - 2)$, $(t - 2)(t - 3)$, $(t - 3)(t - 4)$, $(t - 4)(t - 6) \in \mathbb{R}[t]$ are linearly independent.
  14. 14 $A \in M_2(\mathbb{C})$ and $A$ is nilpotent then $A^2 = 0$.
  15. 15 Let $P$ be an $n \times n$ matrix whose row sums equal $1$. Then for any positive integer $m$ the row sums of the matrix $P^m$ equal $1$.
  16. 16 There is a non trivial group homomorphism from $\mathbb{C}$ to $\mathbb{R}$.
  17. 17 If the equation {[obscured in source]} holds in a {[obscured in source]} $yzx = 1$.
  18. 18 Any $3 \times 3$ and $5 \times 5$ skew-symmetric matrices have always zero determinants.
  19. 19 The rank of the matrix \[ \begin{pmatrix} 11 & 12 & 13 & 14 \\ 21 & 22 & 23 & 24 \\ 31 & 32 & 33 & 34 \\ 41 & 42 & 43 & 44 \end{pmatrix} \] is $2$.
  20. 20 The number $2$ is a prime in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$.
  21. 21 Let $f: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Then the derivative $\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y}$ can exist without $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$ existing.
  22. 22 If $f$ is continuous on $[0, 1]$ and if $\int_0^1 f(x) x^n\,dx = 0$ for $n = 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots$, then $\int_0^1 f^2(x)\,dx = 0$.
  23. 23 Suppose that $f \in \mathfrak{L}^2(\mathbb{R})$. Then $f \in \mathfrak{L}^1(\mathbb{R})$.
  24. 24 The integral $\displaystyle \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \frac{e^{-x}}{1 + x^2}\,dx$ is convergent.
  25. 25 If $A \subset \mathbb{R}$ and open then the interior of the closure $\overline{A}^\circ$ is $A$.
  26. 26 If $f \in C^\infty$ and $f^{(k)}(0) = 0$ for all integer $k \ge 0$, then $f \equiv 0$.
  27. 27 Let $f: [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ be continuous then $f$ assumes the value $\int_0^1 f^2(t)\,dt$ somewhere in $[0, 1]$.
  28. 28 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ {[obscured in source]} that $\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x - h)}{h}$ exists for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $f$ is differentiable in $\mathbb{R}$.
  29. 29 The functions $f(x) = x|x|$ and $x|\sin x|$ are not differentiable at $x = 0$.
  30. 30 The composition of two uniformly continuous functions need not always be uniformly continuous.
  31. 31 $f: [0, \infty] \to [0, \infty]$ is continuous and bounded then $f$ has a fixed point.
  32. 32 The polynomial $X^8 + 1$ is irreducible in $\mathbb{R}[X]$.
  33. 33 The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & \pi & 3 \\ 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ is diagonalisable.
  34. 34 If a rectangle $R := \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid A \le x \le B, \, C \le y \le D\}$ can be covered (allowing overlaps) by $25$ discs of radius $1$ then it can also be covered by $101$ discs of radius $\frac{1}{2}$.
  35. 35 Given any integer $n \ge 2$, we can always find an integer $m$ such that each of the $n - 1$ consecutive integers $m + 2, m + 3, \dots, m + n$ are composite.
  36. 36 The $10 \times 10$ matrix {[obscured in source; last column $v_1 w_{10}, v_2 w_{10}, \dots, v_{10} w_{10}$]} has rank $2$, where $v_i, w_i \in \mathbb{C}$.
  37. 37 If every continuous function on $X \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ is bounded, then $X$ is compact.
  38. 38 The graph of $xy = 1$ in $\mathbb{C}^2$ is connected.
  39. 39 If $z_1, z_2, z_3, z_4 \in \mathbb{C}$ satisfy $z_1 + z_2 + z_3 + z_4 = 0$ and $|z_1|^2 + |z_2|^2 + |z_3|^2 + |z_4|^2 = 1$, then the least value of $|z_1 - z_2|^2 + |z_1 - z_4|^2 + |z_2 - z_3|^2 + |z_3 - z_4|^2$ is $2$.
  40. 40 Consider the differential equations (with $y$ a function of $x$): \begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)] \item $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = y$, $y(0) = 0$, \item $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = |y|^{1/3}$, $y(0) = 0$. \end{enumerate} Then (1) has infinitely many solutions but (2) has finite number of solutions.
