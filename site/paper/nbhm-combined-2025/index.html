NBHM · 2025 · Master’s and doctoral scholarship

  1. 1 Let $X$ be the set of all continuous real-valued functions on the interval $I = [0, 1]$. Equip $X$ with the metric $d : X \times X \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by \[ d(f, g) = \int_0^1 |f(x) - g(x)|\,dx. \] Consider the sequence $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ in $X$ where \[ f_n(x) = \begin{cases} n, & \text{if } 0 \le x \le \frac{1}{n^2}, \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}, & \text{if } \frac{1}{n^2} \le x \le 1. \end{cases} \] On the metric space $(X, d)$, which of the following is/are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The sequence $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ is not Cauchy. \item The sequence $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ is Cauchy. \item The sequence $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ is Cauchy but does not converge in $X$. \item The sequence $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ converges in $X$. \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Does $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_0^1 \frac{n x^n}{1 + x}\,dx$ exist? If it does not exist, then write ``limit does not exist''; if the limit exists, then compute it.
  3. 3 For what non-zero reals $k$ does the series \[ \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{1 + ((\log \log n)/(k \log n))}} \] converge?
  4. 4 The prime elements of the ring $\mathbb{Z}[i] = \{a + ib \mid a, b \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ are called Gaussian primes. Which of the following are Gaussian primes?\\ (a) $5 + 0i$ \qquad (b) $0 + 7i$ \qquad (c) $3 + 5i$ \qquad (d) $4 + 5i$
  5. 5 Given a metric $d$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$, the closed unit ball around $(0, 0)$ with respect to $d$ is the set $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid d((x, y), (0, 0)) \le 1\}$. If $d$ is induced by a norm, then which of the following shapes can the closed unit ball around $(0, 0)$ with respect to $d$ take? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item A rectangle \item An ellipse \item An equilateral triangle \item A parallelogram that is not a rectangle \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Let $S_{11}$ be the symmetric group on $11$ letters. How many subgroups of order $11$ are there in $S_{11}$?
  7. 7 For $n \ge 1$, let $S_n$ denote the set of all permutations of $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$. Let $p_n$ denote the probability of the event that a randomly chosen permutation does not fix any integer in its original position. Find $\lim_{n \to \infty} p_n$.
  8. 8 Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with real entries and having rank $1$, where $n \ge 2$. Find $\det(A + I)$ in terms of $A$.
  9. 9 On $\mathscr{C}[-1, 1]$, the set of all continuous real-valued functions on the interval $I = [-1, 1]$, consider the following two metrics $d_1$ and $d_2$ given by \[ d_1(f, g) = \max_{t \in I} |f(t) - g(t)| \quad \text{and} \quad d_2(f, g) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{-1}^{1} |f(s) - g(s)|\,ds. \] Let $A \subset \mathscr{C}[-1, 1]$ be the set of all continuous functions $f : I \to I$. Which of the following is/are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $A$ is a bounded subset of both $(\mathscr{C}[-1, 1], d_1)$ and $(\mathscr{C}[-1, 1], d_2)$. \item $\sup_{f, g \in A} d_2(f, g) < \sup_{F, G \in A} d_1(F, G)$. \item $A$ is a closed subset of $(\mathscr{C}[-1, 1], d_1)$. \item $A$ is a closed subset of $(\mathscr{C}[-1, 1], d_2)$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 How many $4$-digit numbers divisible by $15$ can you form using only the digits $2, 3, 5$, and $7$, with repetition of digits allowed?
  11. 11 Let $\mathscr{P}(4)$ be the vector space of real polynomials of degree less than or equal to $4$. Given a basis $B = \{p_0, \dots, p_4\}$ of $\mathscr{P}(4)$, let $S_B$ be the set of degrees of $p_i$. What are the possible cardinalities of $S_B$?
  12. 12 What is the value of the triple $(a, b, c)$ if $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ defined as \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} x^3 - 2x^2 + 3, & \text{if } x \ge 1, \\ ax^2 + bx + c, & \text{if } x < 1, \end{cases} \] is twice differentiable?
  13. 13 Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be an entire function with $f(x + iy) = u(x, y) + iv(x, y)$ (where $u$ and $v$ are real-valued). Further, let \[ \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = x^2 - y^2, \qquad \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -2xy, \] and $f(0) = -1$. Evaluate $f(2)$.
  14. 14 Which of the following subsets of $\mathbb{N}$, where $\mathbb{N} := \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$, are closed under multiplication? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\{a^2 + b^2 \mid a, b \in \mathbb{N}\}$ \item $\{a^3 + b^3 \mid a, b \in \mathbb{N}\}$ \item $\{a^2 + b^2 + c^2 \mid a, b, c \in \mathbb{N}\}$ \item $\{a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + d^2 \mid a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{N}\}$ \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 What is the number of homomorphisms from the symmetric group $S_7$ to the alternating group $A_8$ (i.e., the subgroup of all even permutations in the symmetric group $S_8$)?
  16. 16 Consider the finite sequence $A_1 = (2, 2^2, 2^3, \dots, 2^n)$ with $n$ terms, $n \ge 3$. We form the new finite sequence $A_2$ with $(n - 1)$ terms by taking the averages of consecutive terms in $A_1$, i.e., \[ A_2 = \left(\frac{2 + 2^2}{2},\ \frac{2^2 + 2^3}{2},\ \dots,\ \frac{2^{n-1} + 2^n}{2}\right). \] Similarly, define $A_{i+1}$ by taking averages of consecutive elements of the sequence $A_i$ for $i \ge 2$. Repeat this process until you have only one term. What is its value?
  17. 17 Find the number of $2 \times 2$ real invertible matrices whose entries are $1, 2, 3$, or $4$, allowing repetition of these numbers for different entries.
  18. 18 Let $x, y, z$ be integers and let $1 < x < y < z$. You are given that $(x - 1)(y - 1)(z - 1) \mid (xyz - 1)$. You are also given that there is more than one integer value of $(xyz - 1)/((x - 1)(y - 1)(z - 1))$ for $x, y, z$ as above. Find all integer values of $(xyz - 1)/((x - 1)(y - 1)(z - 1))$ for $x, y, z \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $1 < x < y < z$.
  19. 19 Let $V = M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be the real vector space of $n \times n$ matrices with real entries. Let $A \in V$ and consider the linear map $T(X) = AX$, for all $X \in V$. What is the trace of $T$ in terms of $A$?
  20. 20 Let $a_n$ be a sequence defined recursively as $a_0 = 2$, $a_1 = 5$, and $a_{n+2} = 5a_{n+1} - 4a_n$ for all $n \ge 0$. Find a formula (i.e., a closed-form expression in terms of $n$) for $a_n$.
  21. 21 Let $x$ be a non-zero solution to the ODE $\ddot{x} - \sin^2(t)x^3 = 0$ with initial condition $x(0) = x_0$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item For $x_0 = 0$, there are infinitely many solutions in any neighbourhood of $t = 0$. \item Any nonzero solution can vanish at most once in $[0, \infty)$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 22 It is known that in an arithmetic progression $\{a, a + d, a + 2d, \dots\}$, where $a, d \in \mathbb{Z}_+$, there are infinitely many primes, provided that $\gcd(a, d) = 1$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item For a prime number $p$, let $m_p = \max\{m \in \mathbb{Z}_+ \setminus \{1\} : \text{each of the numbers } p - 1,\ p + 1,\ \text{and } p + 2 \text{ has at least } m \text{ distinct prime factors}\}$. Then, there exists an $N$ such that $m_p < N$ for all primes $p$. \item Let $\gcd(a, d) = 1$. Then, there are infinitely many numbers in the arithmetic progression $\{a, a + d, a + 2d, \dots\}$ having exactly two prime factors. \end{enumerate}
  23. 23 Let $i := \sqrt{-1}$ denote a square root of $-1$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item All subrings of $\mathbb{Q}[i]$ are unique factorization domains. \item All subrings of $\mathbb{Q}$ are exactly of the form $\mathbb{Z}\left[\frac{1}{n}\right]$ for some non-zero integer $n$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 24 In what follows, $V$ will denote a subset of $\mathbb{R}$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $V$ can be endowed with the structure of a non-zero vector space over $\mathbb{R}$, then $V$ must necessarily be an unbounded set. \item Let $V = (0, +\infty)$ and define $x \boxplus y := xy$ (i.e., the usual multiplication in $\mathbb{R}$) for each $x, y \in V$. There does not exist any scalar multiplication between the reals and the elements of $V$ that will make $V$ a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ with vector addition given by $\boxplus$. \end{enumerate}
  25. 25 Let $A$ be a non-zero $2 \times 2$ complex matrix such that $\exp(A) = I_2$, where $I_2$ is the $2 \times 2$ identity matrix. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $A$ is diagonalizable. \item $\operatorname{Trace}(A)$ is zero. \end{enumerate}
  26. 26 Let $(x(t), y(t)) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ be a non-constant solution to the ODE \[ \dot{x} = \frac{x^3}{1 + y^2}, \qquad \dot{y} = \frac{y^3}{1 + x^2}, \qquad t > 0, \] and assume that the limits $\lim_{t \to 0^+} x(t) =: x(0)$ and $\lim_{t \to 0^+} y(t) =: y(0)$ exist. Let $T = \{t \ge 0 : (x(t), y(t)) = (x(0), y(0))\}$. What is the cardinality of $T$?
  27. 27 Suppose $40$ identical empty boxes are arranged in a line. You are given $30$ balls of identical size and of $5$ different colours. In how many ways can you put these balls into the given boxes so that each box contains at most one ball and all balls of the same colour are consecutively placed in line?
  28. 28 It is known that \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(\sum_{j=1}^{n} \frac{1}{j} - \log n\right) = \gamma, \] where $\gamma$ is the Euler--Mascheroni constant. For a real number $x$, suppose $\{x\}$ denotes the fractional part of $x$, i.e., $\{x\} \in [0, 1)$ and $x - \{x\} \in \mathbb{Z}$. In terms of $\gamma$, evaluate \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \left\{\frac{n}{j}\right\}. \]
  29. 29 Let $R = \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ be the commutative ring of integers modulo $n$ and consider $p(x) = x^2 + x + 1$ and $q(x) = x^4 + 2x^3 + x^2 + 2025x + 2024$ from $R[x]$. The number of integers $n$, where $n \ge 10$, such that $p(x)$ divides $q(x)$ in $R[x]$ is equal to what?
  30. 30 Let $D$ be a domain in the complex plane, let $a \in D$, and let $f : D \to \mathbb{C}$ be a holomorphic function. Suppose there exists a number $r > 0$ such that the disc $D(a, r) := \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z - a| < r\}$ is contained in $D$ and such that $|f(a)| \le |f(z)|$ for every $z \in D(a, r)$. What more can you say about $f$? Give the most specific answer possible from the information given.
  31. 31 Let $X$ be a path connected, locally path connected, and semi-locally simply connected topological space. If the fundamental group of $X$ is $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$, then how many equivalence classes of path connected covering spaces does $X$ have?
  32. 32 Let $\prod (x - \lambda_i)^{n_i}$, $n_i \in \mathbb{Z}_+$, be the minimal polynomial of an $n \times n$ matrix $A$ with real entries, $n \ge 2$. Find the minimal polynomial of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} A & I \\ 0 & A \end{pmatrix}$.
  33. 33 Consider the real space $L^2[-1, 1]$ with the standard inner product and the subspace $M = \operatorname{span}\{t^{2k} : k = 0, 1, 2, \dots\}$. Find $M^{\perp}$, i.e., the orthogonal complement to $M$ in $L^2[-1, 1]$.
  34. 34 Let $\mathbb{D}^*$ denote the set $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1,\ z \neq 0\}$. Let $f : \mathbb{D}^* \to \mathbb{C} \setminus \{\pm 10\}$ be a holomorphic map. Which of the following is/are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ always has an essential singularity at $z = 0$. \item $f$ either has an essential singularity or a pole at $z = 0$. \item $f$ can never have an essential singularity at $z = 0$. \item $f$ has neither an essential singularity nor a pole at $z = 0$. \end{enumerate}
  35. 35 Identify explicitly the set $\{\det(A - A^T) \in \mathbb{R} : A \in M_4(\mathbb{R})\}$, where $M_4(\mathbb{R})$ is the real vector space of $4 \times 4$ matrices with real entries and $A^T$ denotes the transpose of the matrix $A$.
  36. 36 Let $I$ denote the interval $[0, 1]$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Let $f : I \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous a.e.\ Then there exists a continuous function $g : I \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $f = g$ a.e. \item Let $g : I \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. If a function $f : I \to \mathbb{R}$ is such that $f = g$ a.e., then $f$ is continuous a.e. \end{enumerate}
  37. 37 Let $p$ be a fixed prime and $\mathbb{F}_p$ be the finite field with $p$ elements. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Suppose $L \supset K \supset \mathbb{F}_p$ be field extensions such that $\mathrm{Gal}(L/K) = \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{F}_p) = \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$. Then, $\mathrm{Gal}(L/\mathbb{F}_p) = \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$. \item There exist infinitely many Galois extensions of $\mathbb{Q}$ with Galois group isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$. \end{enumerate}
  38. 38 Let $X$ be a Banach space. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Given a sequence of unit vectors $(u_n)_{n \ge 1}$ in $X$ and $(\lambda_n)_{n \ge 1} \subset \mathbb{R}$, $\sum_n \lambda_n u_n$ converges in $X$ if and only if $\sum_n |\lambda_n| < \infty$. \item $X$ is finite dimensional if and only if each of its subspaces is closed. \end{enumerate}
  39. 39 By a rational map $f$ we mean a complex-valued function $f$ of the form $f = p/q$, where $p, q \in \mathbb{C}[z]$ and have no common zeros, defined at all complex numbers other than the zeros of $q$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Let $\mathscr{D}$ denote the intersection of two fixed, distinct, intersecting open discs in $\mathbb{C}$. There exists no rational function that maps $\mathscr{D}$ conformally and injectively onto the sector \[ S_a = \{re^{i\theta} \in \mathbb{C} : r > 0 \text{ and } -a < \theta < a\} \] for some $a \in (0, \pi)$. \item Given some (fixed) open disc in $\mathbb{C}$, there exists some rational map $p/q$ with $q$ \textbf{non-constant} that maps this disc onto a bounded image. \end{enumerate}
  40. 40 Let $S^1 \subset \mathbb{C}$ denote the unit circle, which forms a group under the operation $e^{i\theta} \cdot e^{i\gamma} = e^{i(\theta + \gamma)}$ with identity element $1 \in \mathbb{C}$. Define $G := \{a + ib \in S^1 : a, b \in \mathbb{Q}\}$. Note that $G$ itself forms a subgroup of $S^1$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The group $G$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$. \item For a fixed prime $p$, the subset $H := \{(a, b) \in G : a = r/p^s \text{ for some } r, s \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ forms a subgroup of $G$. \end{enumerate}
