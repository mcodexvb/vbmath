CCMA-12

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2022
  1. 1(a) What do you mean by significant figure of an approximate number? State the rules of significant figures with suitable examples. [1+4]
  2. 1(b) Find the decimal equivalent to the following floating point machine number: \[\texttt{1 10000100 10100001111000000000000}\] [3]
  3. 1(c) Calculate $\sqrt{25.11}-\sqrt{25.1001}$ correct to three significant figures, providing necessary steps. [2]
  4. 2(a) If the 3rd order differences of $f(x)$ be constant and $f(-1)=-1$, $f(0)=0$, $f(1)=1$, $f(2)=8$ and $f(3)=27$, find $f(4)$ using difference table. [3]
  5. 2(b) Prove that $\delta(E^{\frac12}+E^{-\frac12})\equiv\Delta E^{-1}+\Delta$. [3]
  6. 2(c) What do you mean by `round-off errors' in numerical data? Show how these errors propagated in a difference table. List your observations from the difference table. [4]
  7. 3(a) Establish Lagrange's interpolation formula when the functional values $y=f(x)$ are known at $(n+1)$ points. Also derive the error involved in this formula [6]
  8. 3(b) The population of a city are given below: \[\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline \text{Year}&1971&1981&1991&2001&2011&2021\\\hline \text{Population (in lakh)}&46.52&66.23&81.01&93.70&101.58&120.92\\\hline \end{array}\] Using suitable interpolation formula estimate the population of the city for the year 2015. [4]
  9. 4(a) Why we need to study numerical differentiation? Deduce numerical differentiation formula from Newton's forward interpolation formula at an interpolating point. Also derive the error involved in this formula. [1+3+2]
  10. 4(b) Show that $\nabla y_{n+1}=h\left[1+\frac12\nabla+\frac{5}{12}\nabla^2+\cdots\right]Dy_n$, where $D$ is the differential operator. [4]
  11. 5(a) Establish Trapezoidal rule from Newton-Cotes quadrature formula (closed type). Estimate the error involved in this rule. [6]
  12. 5(b) The following table gives the values of acceleration $(f)$ of a particle in cm/sec$^2$ at equal interval of time $(t)$ in sec. Find the velocity of the particle at 2 secs. \[\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline t&0.0&0.5&1.0&1.5&2.0\\\hline f&0.3989&0.3521&0.2420&0.1295&0.0540\\\hline \end{array}\] [4]
  13. 6(a) Explain the Regula-Falsi method to determine approximately one real root of an equation $f(x)=0$. Give its geometric interpretation and discuss its convergence. [2+2+4]
  14. 6(b) The equation $f(x)=3x^3+4x^2+4x+1=0$ has a root in $(-1,0)$. Determine an iteration function $\phi(x)$ such that the sequence $x_{k+1}=\phi(x_k)$, $x_0=-\frac12$, $k=0,1,2,\ldots$, converges to the root. [2]
  15. 7(a) Can bisection method be used to find the roots of the equation $f(x)=\sin x+1=0$? Why or why not? [2]
  16. 7(b) What do you mean by degree of precision of a quadrature formula? Find the degree of precision of the following quadrature formula \[\int_{-1}^1 f(x)\,dx=f\left(-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\right)+f\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\right).\] [1+3]
  17. 7(c) Construct the quadratic polynomial that interpolates the function $f(x)=\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}$ at $x=-1,0,1$. Also show that $|f(x)-P(x)|\le\frac{\pi^3}{72\sqrt3}$ for $|x|\le1$. [1+3]
  18. 8(a) Show that the estimate of error of the linear iteration method in the approximate value $x_n$ of one real root of the equation $f(x)=0$ is given by \[|\xi-x_n|\le\frac{m}{1-m}|x_n-x_{n-1}|,\] where $\xi$ is a root of the equation and $m$ is positive proper fraction. [4]
  19. 8(b) Solve the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{x^2}{1+y^2}$ with the condition $y(0)=0$ by Picard's method to find $y(0.25)$ correct to 3 decimal places. [3]
  20. 8(c) Find an expression for the iteration matrix $Q_{GS}$ by Gauss-Seidel iteration method $\vec x^{(k)}=Q_{GS}\vec x^{(k-1)}+\vec c$, $k\ge1$ for solving the linear algebraic system of equations given by the single matrix equation $A\vec x=\vec b$. You must mention all the matrices involved in the process. [3]
  21. 9(a) What do you mean by partial pivoting? Carry out the total operational count required for solving a system of $n$ linear equations in $n$ unknowns by Gaussian elimination method. [5]
  22. 9(b) Solve the following system of equations \[\begin{aligned} 5.09x_1+3.46x_2+1.09x_3&=1.28\\ 2.82x_1+6.46x_2-4.27x_3&=4.65\\ 1.27x_1-3.09x_2+7.54x_3&=2.19 \end{aligned}\] using Gauss-Jordon elimination method. [5]
  23. 10(a) Derive the second order Runge-Kutta method for solving the initial value problem $\frac{dy}{dx}=f(x,y)$, $y(x_0)=y_0$. [5]
  24. 10(b) Compute $y(1.2)$, from the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=0.05x^{-2}-y^2$, $y(1)=1$, taking $h=0.05$, by Modified Euler's method, correct to four decimal places. [5]
