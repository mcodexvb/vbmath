MMO-41(A5)

Dynamics of Ecological System-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1(a) Solve the nonlinear differential system $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-x$, $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=-y+x^2$, $\dfrac{dz}{dt}=z+x^2$ and determine the stable and unstable manifolds. [4]
  2. 1(b) Sketch a phase portrait for the system $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=x+y-x(x^2+y^2)$, $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=-x+y-y(x^2+y^2)$, $\dfrac{dz}{dt}=-z$. [4]
  3. 1(c) Prove that the origin of the system $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-2y+yz$, $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=x(1-z)$, $\dfrac{dz}{dt}=xy$ is stable but not asymptotically stable by using the Lyapunov function $L(x,y,z)=ax^2+by^2+cz^2$. [2]
  4. 2 Analyse the model and hence note down the effects of fear and additional food in the following delayed predator-prey model: \[\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{rx}{1+ky}-d_1x-a_1x^2-\frac{a_2xy}{b_1+\alpha\mu A+x},\quad\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{ca_2[x(t-\tau)+\mu A]y}{b_1+\alpha\mu A+x(t-\tau)}-d_2y,\quad x(t=0)\ge0,\ y(t=0)\ge0.\] [5+5]
  5. 3 Consider the following modified Hastings and Powell model: \[\frac{dX}{dT}=rX\left(1-\frac XK\right)-\frac{M_1(1-m_1)XY}{A_1+(1-m_1)X},\] \[\frac{dY}{dT}=\frac{E_1M_1(1-m_1)XY}{A_1+(1-m_1)X}-D_1Y-\frac{M_2(1-m_2)YZ}{A_2+(1-m_2)Y}-HY^2,\] \[\frac{dZ}{dT}=\frac{E_2M_2(1-m_2)YZ}{A_2+(1-m_2)Y}-D_2Z,\] $X(0)\ge0$, $Y(0)\ge0$, $Z(0)\ge0$, $0<E_1,E_2<1$; $m_1,m_2\in[0,1)$.\par \par\textbf{P.T.O.}\par Write down your observations around co-existence equilibrium point on the subsequent issues:
  6. 3(a) Boundedness,
  7. 3(b) Linear stability analysis,
  8. 3(c) Hopf-bifurcation,
  9. 3(d) Effect of refuge. [2+3+2+3]
  10. 4 Carry out an analysis to obtain the necessary and sufficient conditions of Turing bifurcation for two species interaction with diffusion. [10]
  11. 5 A reaction -- diffusion model is given by \[\frac{\partial M_1}{\partial t}=r\left(a-M_1+M_1^2M_2\right)+\frac{\partial^2M_1}{\partial x^2},\qquad\frac{\partial M_2}{\partial t}=r\left(b-M_1^2M_2\right)+D\frac{\partial^2M_2}{\partial x^2},\] where $M_1$ and $M_2$ are morphogen concentrations. All the parameters $r,a,b,c,D$ are positive constants.
  12. 5(a) Explain the model and find the non-zero homogeneous steady state.
  13. 5(b) Show the condition of stability (without diffusion) is $b-a-(a+b)^3<0$.
  14. 5(c) Linearize the system about the non-zero steady state and find the condition for diffusive instability. [2+3+5]
  15. 6 Explicate the travelling wavefronts of pursuit and evasion in a predator-prey system: \[\frac{\partial U}{\partial t}=AU\left(1-\frac UK\right)-BUV+D_1\nabla^2U,\qquad\frac{\partial V}{\partial t}=CUV-DV+D_2\nabla^2V,\] $U(0,x)>0$, $V(0,x)>0$, $\nabla^2\equiv\dfrac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}$. [10]
