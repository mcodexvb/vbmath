MMC-35

Galois Theory-I and Multivariable Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Find the degree of the extension $\mathbb Q\left(\sqrt[3]2,\sqrt3\right)$ over $\mathbb Q$. [2]
  2. 1(b) Show that a finite extension of prime degree is a simple extension. [2]
  3. 1(c) Let $K/F$ be an extension and let $\alpha,\beta\in K$ are algebraic over $F$ with degrees $m,n$ respectively. If $(m,n)=1$ then show that $[F(\alpha,\beta):F]=mn$. [2]
  4. 1(d) Show that an extension $F/K$ is algebraic if and only if, every ring $R$ with $K\subset R\subset F$ is a field. [4]
  5. 2(a) Show that the splitting field of a polynomial of degree $n$ over $K$ has degree utmost $n!$. Give an example of it. [3+1]
  6. 2(b) Let $f(x),g(x)\in K[x]$, a polynomial ring over $K$. Suppose that $g(x)=f(ax+b)$, where $0\ne a,b\in K$. Prove that $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ have same splitting field over $K$. [2]
  7. 2(c) Examine if $x^4+x+1\in\mathbb Q[x]$ is a separable polynomial. [2]
  8. 2(d) Let $F$ be a field of characteristic $p$ ($p$ being prime) and $K/F$ be a finite irreparable extension. Show that $p\,|\,[K:F]$. [2]
  9. 3(a) Construct a polynomial $f(x)$ over $\mathbb Z_p$ ($p$ is prime) such that $g(x)\,|\,f(x)$, for every irreducible polynomial $g(x)\in\mathbb Z_p[x]$. [4]
  10. 3(b) Let $F$ be a field such that the multiplicative group $(F^*,\cdot)$ is cyclic. Show that $F$ is finite. Find a generator of the cyclic group of nonzero elements of a finite field of order 29. [3+1]
  11. 3(c) Does there exist a finite field of order 70? Justify your answer. \par\hfill P.T.O. [2]
  12. 1 Suppose $f:A\to\mathbb R^m$ be a function and $A\subset\mathbb R^n$ be an open set.\par
  13. 1(a) Define total derivative of $f$ at $x_0\in A$. [1]
  14. 1(b) If $f$ is differentiable at $x_0\in A$ then show that $f$ is continuous at $x_0$. [3]
  15. 1(c) Suppose $f=(f_1,f_2,\ldots,f_m)$. If all partial derivatives $\dfrac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}$ where $i=1,2,\ldots,m$, $j=1,2,\ldots n$ exist and continuous in a neighbourhood of a point $x_0\in A$, then show that $f$ is differentiable at $x_0$. [4]
  16. 1(d) Let $f:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ be defined by $f(x,y)=\left(\dfrac{\sin x+e^y}{x^2+y^2},\dfrac1{x^2+y^2-1}\right)$. Show that $f$ is differentiable at every point $(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2$ such that $x^2+y^2\ne0$ or 1. [2]
  17. 2(a) Suppose $f:A\to\mathbb R^n$ and $g:B\to\mathbb R^p$, where $A\subset\mathbb R^m$ and $B\subset\mathbb R^n$ are open sets. Suppose further that $f(A)\subset B$. If $f$ is differentiable at $x_0\in A$ and $g$ is differentiable at $f(x_0)\in B$ then show that $g\circ f$ is differentiable at $x_0$ and $D(g\circ f)(x_0)=(Dg)(f(x_0))Df(x_0)$. [4]
  18. 2(b) State and prove implicit function theorem for the function $F:\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$. [6]
  19. 3(a) State and prove Taylor's theorem for a function $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$. [4]
  20. 3(b) Let $f:A\to\mathbb R$, $A\subset\mathbb R^n$ be an open set. If $x_0\in A$ be a local minima of $f$ then show that the Hessian matrix is $+ve$ semi definite. [3]
  21. 3(c) Find the volume of largest rectangular box with edges parallel to the co-ordinate axes and inscribed in the ellipsoid\par \[\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1.\]
