TIFR Graduate School · GS 2016 · Mathematics

  1. 1 The value of the product $\left(1 + \frac{1}{1!} + \frac{1}{2!} + \dots\right)\left(1 - \frac{1}{1!} + \frac{1}{2!} - \frac{1}{3!} + \dots\right)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $1$ \item $e^2$ \item $0$ \item $\log_e 2$. \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Which of the following is false? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \sin\frac{1}{n}$ diverges \item $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \sin\frac{1}{n^2}$ converges \item $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \cos\frac{1}{n}$ diverges \item $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \cos\frac{1}{n^2}$ converges. \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 The value of the series $\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n}{2^n}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $3$ \item $4$. \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be the function defined by $f(x) = \frac{\sin x}{|x| + \cos x}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f$ is differentiable at all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. \item $f$ is not differentiable at $x = 0$. \item $f$ is differentiable at $x = 0$ but $f'$ is not continuous at $x = 0$. \item $f$ is not differentiable at $x = \frac{\pi}{2}$. \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Which of the following continuous functions $f: (0, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$ can be extended to a continuous function on $[0, \infty)$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f(x) = \sin\frac{1}{x}$ \item $f(x) = \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^2}$ \item $f(x) = \cos\frac{1}{x}$ \item $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$. \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Let $V$ be the vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ consisting of polynomials $p(t)$ over $\mathbb{R}$ of degree less than or equal to $4$. Let $D: V \to V$ be the linear operator that takes any polynomial $p(t)$ to its derivative $p'(t)$. Then the characteristic polynomial $f(x)$ of $D$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $x^4$ \item $x^5$ \item $x^3(x - 1)$ \item $x^4(x - 1)$. \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 Let $A = \{\text{[obscured]} : a_i = 0, 1, 2, 3 \text{ or } 4\} \subset \mathbb{R}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $A$ is {[obscured in source]} \item {[obscured in source]} \item $A$ is uncountable but does not contain an open interval. \item $A$ contains an open interval. \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 The number of group homomorphisms from $\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z}$ to $\mathbb{Z}/29\mathbb{Z}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $1$ \item $20$ \item $29$ \item $580$. \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $3$ with real coefficients. Which of the following is possible? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $p(x)$ has no real roots. \item $p(x)$ has exactly $2$ real roots. \item $p(1) = -1$, $p(2) = 1$, $p(3) = 11$ and $p(4) = 35$. \item $i - 1$ and $i + 1$ are roots of $p(x)$, where $i$ is the square root of $-1$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Let $\{a_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ and $\{b_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be two sequences of real numbers such that the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n^2$ and $\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n^2$ converge. Then the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n b_n$ \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item is absolutely convergent. \item may not converge. \item is always convergent, but may not converge absolutely. \item converges to $0$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 Let $v_i = (v_i^{(1)}, v_i^{(2)}, v_i^{(3)}, v_i^{(4)})$, for $i = 1, 2, 3, 4$, be four vectors in $\mathbb{R}^4$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^4 v_i^{(j)} = 0$, for each $j = 1, 2, 3, 4$. Let $W$ be the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^4$ spanned by $\{v_1, v_2, v_3, v_4\}$. Then the dimension of $W$ over $\mathbb{R}$ is always \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item either equal to $1$ or equal to $4$. \item less than or equal to $3$. \item greater than or equal to $2$. \item either equal to $0$ or equal to $4$. \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Let $A$ be a subset of $[0, 1]$ with non-empty interior, and let $\mathbb{Q} + A = \{q + a : q \in \mathbb{Q}, \, a \in A\}$. Which of the following is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\mathbb{Q} + A = \mathbb{R}$. \item $\mathbb{Q} + A$ can be a proper subset of $\mathbb{R}$. \item $\mathbb{Q} + A$ need not be closed in $\mathbb{R}$. \item $\mathbb{Q} + A$ need not be open in $\mathbb{R}$. \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuously differentiable function such that $|f(x) - f(y)| \ge |x - y|$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the equation $f'(x) = \frac{1}{2}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item has exactly one solution. \item has no solution. \item has a countably infinite number of solutions. \item has uncountably many solutions. \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to [0, \infty)$ be a continuous function such that $g(x) = (f(x))^2$ is uniformly continuous. Which of the following statements is always true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f$ is bounded. \item $f$ may not be uniformly continuous. \item $f$ is uniformly continuous. \item $f$ is unbounded. \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 Which of the following sequences of functions $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges uniformly? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f_n(x) = x^n$ on $[0, 1]$ \item $f_n(x) = 1 - x^n$ on $[0, 1]$ \item $f_n(x) = \frac{1}{1 + n x^2}$ on $[0, 1]$ \item $f_n(x) = \frac{x}{1 + n x^2}$ on $[0, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 Let $S$ be a collection of subsets of $\{1, 2, \dots, 100\}$ such that the intersection of any two sets in $S$ is non-empty. What is the maximum possible cardinality $|S|$ of $S$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $100$ \item $2^{100}$ \item $2^{99}$ \item $2^{98}$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $S$ be the set of all $3 \times 3$ matrices $A$ with integer entries such that the product $AA^t$ is the identity matrix. Here $A^t$ denotes the transpose of $A$. Then $|S| =$ \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $12$ \item $24$ \item $48$ \item $60$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 Let $A$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix with integer entries such that $\det(A) = 1$. What is the maximum possible number of entries of $A$ that are even? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $2$ \item $3$ \item $6$ \item $8$. \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 The limit \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(\frac{1}{n} + \frac{1}{n + 1} + \dots + \frac{1}{2n}\right) = \] \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $e$ \item $2$ \item $\log_e 2$ \item $e^2$. \end{enumerate}
  20. 20 Let $G = \mathbb{Z}/100\mathbb{Z}$ and let $S = \{h \in G : \operatorname{Order}(h) = 50\}$. Then $|S|$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $20$ \item $25$ \item $30$ \item $50$. \end{enumerate}
  21. 21 Let $A_1 \supset A_2 \supset \cdots \supset A_n \supset A_{n+1} \supset \cdots$ be an infinite sequence of non-empty subsets of $\mathbb{R}^3$. Which of the following conditions ensures that their intersection is non-empty? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item Each $A_i$ is uncountable. \item Each $A_i$ is open. \item Each $A_i$ is connected. \item Each $A_i$ is compact. \end{enumerate}
  22. 22 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space. Which of the following is possible? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $X$ has exactly $3$ dense subsets. \item $X$ has exactly $4$ dense subsets. \item $X$ has exactly $5$ dense subsets. \item $X$ has exactly $6$ dense subsets. \end{enumerate}
  23. 23 Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be the sequence of functions on $\mathbb{R}$ defined by $f_n(x) = n^2 x^n$. Let $A$ be the set of all points $a$ in $\mathbb{R}$ such that the sequence $\{f_n(a)\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $A = \{0\}$ \item $A = [0, 1)$ \item $A = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-1, 1\}$ \item $A = (-1, 1)$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 24 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function such that $f(i) = 0$ for all $i \in \mathbb{Z}$. Which of the following statements is always true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\operatorname{Image}(f)$ is closed in $\mathbb{R}$. \item $\operatorname{Image}(f)$ is open in $\mathbb{R}$. \item $f$ is uniformly continuous. \item None of the above. \end{enumerate}
  25. 25 Let $S^1 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| = 1\}$ be the unit circle. Which of the following is false? Any continuous function from $S^1$ to $\mathbb{R}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item is bounded. \item is uniformly continuous. \item has image containing a non-empty open subset of $\mathbb{R}$. \item has a point $z \in S^1$ such that $f(z) = f(-z)$. \end{enumerate}
  26. 26 Which of the following is false? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item Any continuous function from $[0, 1]$ to $[0, 1]$ has a fixed point. \item Any homeomorphism from $[0, 1)$ to $[0, 1)$ has a fixed point. \item Any bounded continuous function from $[0, \infty)$ to $[0, \infty)$ has a fixed point. \item Any continuous function from $(0, 1)$ to $(0, 1)$ has a fixed point. \end{enumerate}
  27. 27 For $n \ge 1$, let $S_n$ denote the group of all permutations on $n$ symbols. Which of the following statements is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $S_3$ has an element of order $4$. \item $S_4$ has an element of order $6$. \item $S_4$ has an element of order $5$. \item $S_5$ has an element of order $6$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 28 Which of the following rings is an integral domain? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^2 + x + 1)$ \item $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^2 + 5x + 6)$ \item $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^3 - 2)$ \item $\mathbb{R}[x]/(x^7 + 1)$. \end{enumerate}
  29. 29 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to (0, \infty)$ be a twice differentiable function such that $f(0) = 1$ and $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \int_a^b f'(x)\,dx$ for all $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ with $a \le b$. Which of the following statements is false? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f$ is one to one. \item The image of $f$ is compact. \item $f$ is unbounded. \item There is only one such function. \end{enumerate}
  30. 30 For $X \subset \mathbb{R}^n$, consider $X$ as a metric space with metric induced by the usual Euclidean metric on $\mathbb{R}^n$. Which of the following metric spaces $X$ is complete? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $X = \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} \subset \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ \item $X = \mathbb{Q} \times \mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$ \item $X = (-\pi, \pi) \cap \mathbb{Q} \subset \mathbb{R}$ \item $X = [-\pi, \pi] \cap (\mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}) \subset \mathbb{R}$. \end{enumerate}
