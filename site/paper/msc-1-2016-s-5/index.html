MMC-16

Methods of Applied Mathematics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2016
  1. 1 Answer \textit{any five} questions: [5x2=10]
  2. 1(a) Solve the Fredholm integro-differential equation $u'(x)=36x^2+\displaystyle\int_0^1u(t)\,dt$, $u(0)=1$.
  3. 1(b) Find $f(x)$, where $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}f(x)\cos(\lambda x)\,dx=\begin{cases}1-\lambda, & 0\leq\lambda<1\\ 0, & \lambda>1\end{cases}$.
  4. 1(c) Find the value of $y(2)$, where $y(x)$ satisfies the integral equation $y(x)=1+\displaystyle\int_0^xe^{-x+t}y(t)\,dt$.
  5. 1(d) Find the extremum of the functional $I[y(x)]=\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\left(2xy-y^2+y'^2\right)dx$, where $y(0)=0$ and $y(\pi/2)=0$.
  6. 1(e) Find the differential equation corresponding to the integral equation $f(x)=x+\displaystyle\int_0^x\sin(x-t)f(t)\,dt$.
  7. 1(f) Find the inverse Laplace transform of $F(s)$, where $F(s)=\dfrac{3s-2}{s^2-4s+20}$.
  8. 1(g) What is the value of $\phi(1)+\phi(2)$, where $\phi(x)=\dfrac{5x}{6}+\dfrac12\displaystyle\int_0^1xt\phi(t)\,dt$.
  9. 2(a) Find $L\{f(t)\}$, where $f(t)=\begin{cases}\sin t+\cos t, & 0<t<2,\\ t-e^{7-t}, & 2<t<3,\\ 9t, & t>3.\end{cases}$ [3]
  10. 2(b) Using the Laplace transform method, solve the differential equation,\\$\dfrac{d^2y}{dt^2}-3\dfrac{dy}{dt}+2y=f(t)$, where $f(t)=\begin{cases}2, & 0\leq t<4\\ 0, & t>4\end{cases}$, $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=0$. [6]
  11. 2(c) Find the value of $\lambda$ for which the integral equation $y(t)=\lambda\displaystyle\int_0^1tsy(s)\,ds$ has a non-trivial solution. [1]
  12. 3(a) Find necessary conditions for the existence of the extremum of the functional\\$I[y(x),z(x)]=\displaystyle\int_a^bf(x,y,z,y',z')\,dx$. [4]
  13. 3(b) With the help of resolvent kernel solve the Volterra integral equation\\$y(x)=x3^x-\displaystyle\int_0^x3^{x-t}y(t)\,dt$. [4]
  14. 3(c) Find the first approximate extremum of the functional\\$I[y(x)]=\displaystyle\int_0^1\left(y'^2+y^2\right)dx$, $y(0)=0$, $y(1)=1$ by using the Rayleigh-Ritz method. [2]
  15. 4(a) State Fredholm alternative theorem. Using Fredholm alternative theorem, find the conditions for which the integral equation $y(x)=f(x)+\lambda\displaystyle\int_{-a}^{a}(x+t)y(t)\,dt$, where $f$ is continuous and $a$ is a positive constant, has infinite number of solutions. [1+5]
  16. 4(b) Find the extremal of the functional $I[y(x)]=\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}\left(y'^2-y^2\right)dx$, under the conditions $y(0)=0$, $y(\pi)=1$ and subject to the constraint $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}y\,dx=1$. [4]
  17. 5(a) Solve the first kind Volterra integral equation $\displaystyle\int_0^x\left(1-x^2+t^2\right)y(t)\,dt=\dfrac{x^2}{2}$. [2]
  18. 5(b) With the help of Hilbert-Schmidt theorem, solve the integral equation\\$y(x)=1-\displaystyle\int_0^1(1-3xt)y(t)\,dt$. [5]
  19. 5(c) Solve the integro-differential equation\\$u''(x)=\cosh x+\displaystyle\int_0^xe^{-(x-t)}u(t)\,dt$, $u(0)=1$, $u'(0)=1$. [3]
  20. 6(a) Find the Fourier sine transform of $f(x)$, where $f(x)=\begin{cases}x, & 0<x<1,\\ 2-x, & 1<x<2,\\ 0, & x>2.\end{cases}$ [3]
  21. 6(b) Using Parseval's identity, evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}\frac{1}{(a^2+t^2)(b^2+t^2)}\,dt$. [2]
  22. 6(c) Convert the boundary value problem $y''(x)+xy(x)=1$ with boundary conditions $y(0)=y(1)=0$ into an integral equation. [3]
  23. 6(d) Using modified Adomian decomposition method, solve the integral equation\\$u(x)=\left(1-e^{\tan x}\right)x+\sec^2x+\displaystyle\int_0^{x}xe^{\tan t}u(t)\,dt$, $0<x<\pi/2$. [2]
