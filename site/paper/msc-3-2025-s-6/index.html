MMO-31(P13)

M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1(a) Let $R$ be an integral domain and let $M$ be an $R$-module. Define Tor$(M)$ and prove that it is a submodule of $M$. Determine Tor$(M)$ for $M=\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z$.
  2. 1(b) Let $M$ be an $R$-module and let $N$ be a submodule of $M$. If both $N$ and $M/N$ are finitely generated, then prove that $M$ is also finitely generated.
  3. 1(c) Determine, with proof, the annihilator of the $\mathbb Z$-module $\mathbb Z/12\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/15\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/18\mathbb Z$. [5+3+2]
  4. 2(a) Prove that the set of all algebraic numbers is countable.
  5. 2(b) Find, with proof, an element $\theta\in\mathbb C$ such that $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt[3]3)=\mathbb Q(\theta)$.
  6. 2(c) Let $K$ be a number field and let $\alpha\in K$. Prove that the field polynomial of $\alpha$ is a power of the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $\mathbb Q$. [3+4+3]
  7. 3(a) Let $K$ be a number field and let $\alpha\in K$. Prove that $\mathbb Q(\alpha)=\mathbb Q(\theta)$ if and only if all the $K$-conjugates of $\alpha$ are distinct.
  8. 3(b) Prove that the discriminant of any basis of a number field is a non-zero rational number.
  9. 3(c) Prove that $\mathbb B\cap\mathbb Q=\mathbb Z$. [3+4+3]
  10. 4(a) Determine the ring of integers of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]5)$.
  11. 4(b) Let $p$ be an odd prime number. Determine the discriminant of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)$. [6+4]
  12. 5(a) Prove that $\mathcal O_K$ is noetherian.
  13. 5(b) Let $\mathfrak a$ be a non-zero ideal in $\mathcal O_K$. Prove that $\mathfrak a$ contains a product of finite number of prime ideals.
  14. 5(c) Prove that any non-zero rational integer belongs to at most finitely many ideals of $\mathcal O_K$. [3+4+3]
  15. 6(a) Prove that $\mathcal O_K$ is a PID if and only if it is a UFD.
  16. 6(b) Define a relation $\sim$ on the set of all fractional ideals of $K$ by declaring $A\sim B$ if and only if $\langle\alpha\rangle A=\langle\beta\rangle B$ for some $\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal O_K\setminus\{0\}$. Prove that $\sim$ is an equivalence relation.
  17. 6(c) Prove that the equivalence relation $\sim$ partitions the set of fractional ideals of $K$ into finite number of equivalence classes. \begin{center}Internal Exam of Number Theory - I\end{center} \begin{itemize}\item Answer all the questions; total marks is 12. All questions carry equal marks.\end{itemize} [3+3+4]
  18. (1) Let $\theta\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$ be such that $\theta^3-\theta^2+\theta=0$. Find $[\mathbb Q(\theta):\mathbb Q]$.
  19. (2) Let $F$ and $K$ be two fields with $F\subseteq K$. If $\alpha\in K$ is algebraic over $F$, then prove that $F[\alpha]=F(\alpha)$.
  20. (3) Let $\theta\in\mathbb C$ be an algebraic number. Prove that $n\theta$ is an algebraic integer for some $n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$.
  21. (4) Prove that every integral basis of a number field $K$ is also a $\mathbb Q$-basis of $K$. \begin{center}Internal Exam of Number Theory - I\end{center} \begin{itemize}\item Answer all the questions; total marks is 12. All questions carry equal marks.\end{itemize}
  22. (1) Let $d\not\equiv1\pmod4$ be a square-free integer. Prove that the ring of integers of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ is $\mathbb Z[\sqrt d]$.
  23. (2) For any number field $K$, prove that $\mathcal O_K$ is Noetherian. Hence prove that factorization exists in $\mathcal O_K$.
  24. (3) Prove that any non-zero ideal in $\mathcal O_K$ contains a finite product of prime ideals.
