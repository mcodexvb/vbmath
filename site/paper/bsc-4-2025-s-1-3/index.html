MJMA-9

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2025
  1. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  2. 1(a) When is a partial differential equation (PDE) considered well-posed in Hadamard's sense?
  3. 1(b) Form a PDE by eliminating the arbitrary function $\phi$ from \[u=x^2+y^2+\phi\left(\frac1x+\log_e y\right).\]
  4. 1(c) Distinguish between linear, semilinear and quasilinear types of first order PDE in two independent variables.
  5. 1(d) Show that the PDE \[\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2=u^2\] has solutions of the form $u=c_1e^x$ and $u=c_2e^y$, where $c_1$ and $c_2$ are two independent arbitrary constants, but their sum $c_1e^x+c_2e^y$ is not a solution of the equation. Justify your answer.
  6. 1(e) Find the integral surface of the Cauchy problem \[\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-3\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=0,\quad u(x,0)=-x^3.\]
  7. 1(f) Define the compatibility of two first-order PDEs in two independent variables.
  8. 1(g) Find a singular solution of the following non-linear PDE, if any: \[\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)\left(u-x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)=1.\]
  9. 1(h) Find the solution of \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=0,\] such that $u$ is the product of a function of $x$ and a function of $y$. [$5\times2=10$]
  10. 2(a) Find the general solution of the following first order quasi-linear PDE: \[P(x,y,u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+Q(x,y,u)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=R(x,y,u).\] Hence, comment on the uniqueness of the obtained integral surface with given initial curve $\Gamma:\{x_0(s),y_0(s),u_0(s)\}$.
  11. 2(b) Show that the orthogonal surface passing through the hyperbola $x^2-y^2=9,u=0$ to the one-parameter family of surface represented by $u=Cxy(x^2+y^2)$ is \[(x^2-y^2)^2(x^2+y^2+4u^2)=81(x^2+y^2),\] where $C$ is a constant.
  12. 3(a) Let $u(x,y)=2f(y)\cos(x-2y)$, $(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2$, be a solution of the initial value problem (IVP) \[2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=u,\quad u(x,0)=\cos x.\] Find the value of $f(1)$.
  13. 3(b) Prove that the PDEs \[\left[\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2\right]y=u\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\quad\text{and}\quad\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2=2025,\] are compatible and hence find the one-parameter family of solutions. Furthermore, identify the domain in which the one-parameter family of solutions is applicable.
  14. 3(c) Does any singular integral exist for a first-order PDE of the form $f(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y})=0$? Justify your answer.
  15. 4(a) Check whether $u=c_1y+c_2(x^2-c_1)$ is a complete integral or not of the PDE \[2xu-x^2\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)-2xy\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)=0,\] where $c_1$ and $c_2$ are two independent arbitrary constants.
  16. 4(b) Find the complete integral of the following PDE using Charpit's method: \[x^2\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2+y^2\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2=u^2.\]
  17. 4(c) Solve: \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-6\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}+9\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}-4\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+12\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+4u=e^{2x}\tan(3x+y).\] [2+7+6]
  18. 1 State the principle of parallel axes. Using this principle, obtain the M.I. of a circular disc about a tangent parallel to the diameter. [3+7]
  19. 2(a) Show that the momental ellipsoid at a point on the edge of the circular base of a thin hemispherical shell is given by the equation $2x^2+5(y^2+z^2)-3xz=\text{constant}$. [5]
  20. 2(b) To find whether a given straight line is, at any point of its length, a principle axis of a material system. [5]
  21. 3(a) Using D'Alembert's principle, obtain the equation of motion of the centre of inertia. [3]
  22. 3(b) A rough uniform board of mass $M$ and length $2l$, rests on a smooth horizontal plane, and a man of mass $m$ walks on it from one end to the other. Show that the distance through which the board moves is $\frac{2ml}{m+M}$. [7]
  23. 4(a) Obtain the kinetic energy of a rigid body rotating about an axis. [3]
  24. 4(b) A vertical thread upwinds itself from a reel, the upper end of the thread being fixed. Prove that the downward acceleration of the reel is $\frac{a^2}{a^2+k^2}\times g$ and that the tension of the thread is $\frac{k^2}{a^2+k^2}\times Mg$, where $M$ is the mass of the reel, $a$ is its radius and $k$ is the radius of gyration about that axis. [7]
  25. 5(a) Prove that the centres of oscillation and suspension are interchangeable. [3]
  26. 5(b) A uniform elliptic board swings about a horizontal axis at right angle to the board and passing through one focus. If the centre of oscillation be at the other focus, then prove that the eccentricity of the ellipse is $\sqrt{\frac25}$. [7]
  27. 6(a) Prove that the kinetic energy of a rigid body moving in two dimensions is $\frac12 Mv^2+\frac12 Mk^2\dot\theta^2$. [5]
  28. 6(b) A circular cylinder of radius $a$ and radius of gyration $k$ rolls without slipping inside a fixed hollow cylinder of radius $b$. Show that the plane through their axes moves like a circular pendulum of length \[(a-b)\left(1+\frac{k^2}{a^2}\right).\] [5]
