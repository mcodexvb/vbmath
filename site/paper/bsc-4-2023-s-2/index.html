CC-9

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2023
  1. 1(a) Obtain the resultant of two simple harmonic motions in the same straight line having slightly different periods. [5]
  2. 1(b) A particle of mass $m$ is attached to a light wire which is stretched tightly between two fixed points with a tension $T$. If $a$ and $b$ be the distances of the particle from the two ends, then prove that the period of small transverse oscillation of $m$ is $2\pi\sqrt{\frac{mab}{T(a+b)}}$. [5]
  3. 2(a) Define harmonic oscillator with an illustration. Discuss the motion of a particle of mass $m$ moving in a simple harmonic motion when it is disturbed by a periodic disturbing force of the form $mk\cos pt$. [1+4]
  4. 2(b) A particle is describing a circle of radius $a$ in such a way that its tangential acceleration is $k$ times the normal acceleration, where $k$ is a constant. If the speed of the particle at any point be $u$, then prove that it will return to the same point after a time $\frac{a}{ku}(1-e^{-2\pi k})$. [5]
  5. 3 Deduce the expressions for radial and cross-radial components of velocity and acceleration in plane polar coordinates. [10]
  6. 4(a) An insect crawls at a constant rate $u$ along the spoke of a cart wheel, of radius $a$. The cart is moving with a constant velocity $v$. Find the acceleration along and perpendicular to the spoke. [5]
  7. 4(b) If the radial and transverse velocities of a particle be always proportional to each other, then show that the path is an equiangular spiral. If, in addition to the above, the radial and the transverse accelerations too be proportional to each other, then show that the velocity of the particle varies as some power of radius vector. [2+3]
  8. 5(a) Obtain the polar equation of a central orbit. Prove that, in a central orbit, the angular momentum is constant. [5]
  9. 5(b) A particle of unit mass is projected with velocity $u$ at an inclination $\alpha$ above the horizon in a medium, the resistance of which is $k$ times the velocity. Show that the time to the greatest height is less than half the time of flight. [5]
  10. 1 If $P_1,P_2,\ldots,P_n$ be a given system of coplanar forces acting, respectively, at the points $A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_n$ of a body, then show that the system can be reduced to a single force $R$ at any base point $O$ and a single couple $G$. Show further that they can be reduced to a single force $R$ in which the point of application of $R$ is displaced. [7+3]
  11. 2 A system of coplanar forces has the total moments $H,2H$ and $3H$ about the points $(0,0)$, $(0,1)$ and $(2,4)$ respectively. Find the magnitude and the equation of the line of action of the resultant. [10]
  12. 3 State the principle of virtual work for a particle and hence deduce the conditions for equilibrium. [3+7]
  13. 4 A square framework formed of heavy uniform rods of equal weight $W$ jointed together is hung up by one corner. A weight $W$ is suspended from each of the three lower corners and the shape of the square is preserved by a light rod along the horizontal diagonal. Find the thrust of the light rod. [10]
  14. 5(a) What is meant by Poinsot's central axis? [5]
  15. 5(b) Two forces act, one along the line $y=0,z=0$ and the other along the line $x=0,z=c$. Show that the locus of the central axis is $(x^2+y^2)z=cy^2$. [5]
