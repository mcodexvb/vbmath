CCMA-4

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2023
  1. 1 Answer any five questions: [\(5\times2=10\)]
  2. 1(a) Determine the transformed equation of \(11x^2+16xy-y^2=0\) when the axes are rotated through an angle \(\tan^{-1}\tfrac12\)?
  3. 1(b) Determine the value of \(\lambda\) and \(\mu\) so that \(\lambda x^2+3xy-2y^2-5x+\mu=0\) represents a pair of perpendicular lines.
  4. 1(c) Find the values of \(a\) and \(f\) for which \(ax^2-6xy+9y^2+4x+2fy+1=0\) may represent a conic with infinitely many centres.
  5. 1(d) Find the equation of the pair of straight lines joining the origin to the points of intersection of the ellipse \(2x^2+4y^2=8\) and the line \(2x+y=3\).
  6. 1(e) What do you mean by \textquoteleft invariant\textquoteright? Give an example.
  7. 1(f) Find the length of the common chord of the circles \(x^2+y^2-4x+2y+1=0\) and \(x^2+y^2+2x-4y+1=0\).
  8. 1(g) If \(px^2+5y^2+qz^2-axy+2px-4qy-2az+d=0\) represents a sphere of radius 2, then find the value of \(d\).
  9. 1(h) Find the equation of the cone whose vertex is the origin and base is the circle \(x=a,\ y^2+z^2=b^2\).
  10. 2 If by rotation of transformation of rectangular axes, the expression \(\frac{x^2}{\lambda}+\frac{y^2}{\mu}\) transforms to \(px'^2+2rx'y'+qy'^2\), then show that the expression \(\frac{x^2}{a-k}+\frac{y^2}{b-k}\) transforms to \[ \frac{px'^2+2rx'y'+qy'^2+k(r^2-pq)(x'^2+y'^2)}{1-(p+q)k-(r^2-pq)k^2}. \] \hfill [5]
  11. 3 Show that one of the bisectors of the angles between the pair of lines \(ax^2+2hxy+by^2=0\) will pass through the point of intersection of the two lines \(ax^2+2hxy+by^2+2fx+2gy+c=0\), if \(h(f^2-g^2)=fg(a-b)\). [5]
  12. 4 Reduce the equation \(x^2-2xy+y^2+6x-14y+29=0\), to its canonical form and determine the nature of the conic. Also find the equation of its axes. [5]
  13. 5 Determine the limiting points of the coaxial system of circles given by \(S=x^2+y^2+2x-4y+5=0\) and \(S'=x^2+y^2-4x+6y+13=0\). [5]
  14. 6 A sphere of radius \(r\) passes through the origin \(O\) and cuts the axes in \(P,Q,R\) respectively. Find the locus of the centroid of the triangle \(PQR\). [5]
  15. 7 Find the equation of the right circular cone whose vertex is at \((-1,2,3)\), the semi-vertical angle is \(60^\circ\) and the axis is parallel to \(\frac x3=\frac y4=\frac z5\). [5]
  16. 1 Answer any five questions: [\(5\times2=10\)]
  17. 1(a) If \(\vec\rho_1\) and \(\vec\rho_2\) are the unit vectors inclined at an angle \(\theta\), then show that \(2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}=|\vec\rho_1-\vec\rho_2|\).
  18. 1(b) If \(\vec a=\hat i-2\hat j,\ \vec b=\hat j+\hat k,\) then find the component of \(\vec a\) along \(\vec b\).
  19. 1(c) Find the volume of the tetrahedron, the co-ordinates of whose vertices are \((0,1,2)\), \((3,0,1)\), \((1,1,1)\) and \((4,3,2)\).
  20. 1(d) Find the work done by the forces \(4\hat i+\hat j-3\hat k\), \(3\hat i+\hat j-\hat k\) and \(\hat i-3\hat k\) which together work on a particle and displace it from the point \((1,2,3)\) to the point \((5,4,1)\).
  21. 1(e) Find the maximum value of the directional derivative of \(\phi=x^2+z^2-y^2\) at the point \((1,3,2)\).
  22. 1(f) Define curl and divergence of a vector quantity.
  23. 1(g) If \(\vec r=a\cos t\,\hat i+a\sin t\,\hat j+bt\,\hat k\), then find \(\left[\dfrac{d\vec r}{dt},\dfrac{d^2\vec r}{dt^2},\dfrac{d^3\vec r}{dt^3}\right]\).
  24. 1(h) State Serret-Frenet formulae.
  25. 2(a) Use vector method to show that \(\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}\) in a triangle \(ABC\). [3]
  26. 2(b) Show that \(|\vec a\times\vec b|^2|\vec a\times\vec c|^2-\{(\vec a\times\vec b)\cdot(\vec a\times\vec c)\}^2=|\vec a|^2[\vec a\,\vec b\,\vec c]^2\). [2]
  27. 3 A space curve is given by \(x=t,\ y=t^2,\ z=t^3\). Find the curvature \((\kappa)\) and torsion \((\tau)\) at \(t=0\). [5]
  28. 4(a) State and prove a necessary and sufficient condition for a vector function \(\vec r=\vec g(t)\) to have a constant magnitude. [3]
  29. 4(b) If \(\dfrac{d\vec a}{dt}=\vec r\times\vec a\) and \(\dfrac{d\vec b}{dt}=\vec r\times\vec b\), then show that \(\dfrac{d}{dt}(\vec a\times\vec b)=\vec r\times(\vec a\times\vec b)\), where \(\vec r\) is a constant vector and \(\vec a,\vec b\) are vector functions of a scalar variable \(t\). [2]
  30. 5 Show that the vector \(\vec F=(6xy+z^3)\hat i+(3x^2-z)\hat j+(3xz^2-y)\hat k\) is irrotational. For \(\vec F\), find a scalar function \(\phi\) such that \(\vec F=\vec\nabla\phi\).
  31. 6 If \(\vec\nabla\cdot\vec E=0\), \(\vec\nabla\cdot\vec H=0\), \(\vec\nabla\times\vec E=-\dfrac{\partial\vec H}{\partial t}\) and \(\vec\nabla\times\vec H=\dfrac{\partial\vec E}{\partial t}\), then show that \(\nabla^2\vec H=\dfrac{\partial^2\vec H}{\partial t^2}\) and \(\nabla^2\vec E=\dfrac{\partial^2\vec E}{\partial t^2}\). [5]
  32. 7(a) Let \(\vec a\) and \(\vec b\) two vector functions. Prove that \(\operatorname{div}(\vec a\times\vec b)=\vec b\cdot(\operatorname{curl}\vec a)-\vec a\cdot(\operatorname{curl}\vec b)\). [3]
  33. 7(b) Find the angle between the surfaces \(x^2+y^2+z^2=9\) and \(z=x^2+y^2-3\) at the point \((2,-1,2)\). [2]
