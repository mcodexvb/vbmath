CCMA-11

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2021
  1. 1(a) A function $f$ is bounded and integrable on $[a,b]$ for every $b>a$ such that the improper integral $\int_a^b f\,dx$ is convergent. Does $\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)$ exist? Justify. Find the value of $\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)$ if/whenever it exists. [2+5]
  2. 1(b) If $f$ and $g$ are two positive functions on $[a,b]$ such that $\lim_{x\to a+}f(x)/g(x)=0$, then show that $\int_a^b f\,dx$ converges, provided $\int_a^b g\,dx$ converges. [3]
  3. 2(a) Let $f_n$ be uniformly continuous on an interval $I\subseteq\mathbb R$, for $n=1,2,\ldots$ such that $f_n\longrightarrow f$ uniformly on $I$. Is $f$ uniformly continuous on $I$? Justify. [5]
  4. 2(b) For each $n\ge1$, suppose that the function $f_n$ is continuous on an interval $I\subseteq\mathbb R$ and $f_n\longrightarrow f$ uniformly on $I$. Let $\{x_n\}$ be a sequence in $I$ which converges to $x\in I$. Discuss the convergence of the sequence $\{f_n(x_n)\}$. [5]
  5. 3(a) Given a convergent sequence $\{a_n\}$, determine whether the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n\cos nx$ is uniformly convergent on $\mathbb R$. [4]
  6. 3(b) Prove that the series $\sum(-1)^nx^n(1-x)$ converges uniformly on $[0,1]$. Discuss the uniform convergence of $\sum x^n(1-x)$. [3+3]
  7. 4(a) Define the radius of convergence and the interval of convergence of a power series $\sum a_nx^n$. Determine the interval of convergence for the power series \[\sum a_nx^n=(x-1)+\frac{(2!)^2}{4!}(x-1)^2+\frac{(3!)^2}{6!}(x-1)^3+\cdots.\] [2+4]
  8. 4(b) Let $f$ be the sum of a power series $\sum a_nx^n$. Prove or disprove: The function $f$ is differentiable on the interval of convergence of $\sum a_nx^n$. [4]
  9. 5(a) For which functions, the Fourier series are defined in general? Define the Fourier series and Fourier coefficients for such a function $f$. Does the Fourier series of $f$ converge to the function $f$? [4]
  10. 5(b) Find the Fourier series of the periodic function $f:[-\pi,\pi]\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ (with period $2\pi$) defined by \[f(x)=\begin{cases}1&\text{for }-\pi<x\le0\\2x+1&\text{for }0\le x\le\pi.\end{cases}\] Also find the sum of the series at $x=0,\pm\pi,\pm2\pi$. [6]
  11. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  12. 1(a) What is the difference between a linear and nonlinear partial differential equation (PDE)?
  13. 1(b) Find a partial differential equation by eliminating arbitrary function $\phi$ from the equation $x^2+y^2=\phi(yz-y^2)$; $z=z(x,y)$.
  14. 1(c) Define the singular integral of the first-order PDE in two independent variables.
  15. 1(d) Solve the PDE $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=2xy$, together with the auxiliary condition that $z(x,x^2)=2$.
  16. 1(e) Classify compatible systems of first-order PDEs in two independent variables.
  17. 1(f) Show that the complete integral of the PDE $z=(x-7)\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+(y-9)\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$ represents all possible planes passing through the point $(7,9,0)$.
  18. 1(g) Find the particular integral (PI) of the PDE \[2\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+3\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=\log_e(2y-3x).\]
  19. 1(h) Find two initial strips of the following PDE, which passes through the $x$-axis in the context of Cauchy's method of characteristic: \[2z=\left[\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2\right]+\left(2\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}-x\right)\left(2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}-y\right).\] [$5\times2=10$]
  20. 2(a) If $z(x,y)$ is the solution of the Cauchy problem $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=2021$, $z(x,0)=-x^2$, $x>0$, then find the value of $z(1,2021)$. Is the solution unique? Justify your answer.
  21. 2(b) Show that the PDEs $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}+y(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y})^2-1=0$ and $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}-9\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=0$ are compatible on a specified domain and hence find the one-parameter family of common solutions.
  22. 3(a) For the PDE \[(z^2-2yz-y^2)\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+x(y+z)\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=x(y-z),\] find the general integral and the integral surface through the curve $x=2y=3z$.
  23. 3(b) Find the singular integral of the following PDE: \[z=x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}+3\sqrt[3]{\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}}.\]
  24. 4(a) Using Cauchy's method of characteristics, find an integral surface of the PDE $x(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x})^2+y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=z$ passing through the straight line $x_0=s$, $y_0=1$, $z_0=-s$, $0<s<1$.
  25. 4(b) Solve: \[2\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x\partial y}+\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial y^2}-3\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=3\cos(3x-2y).\] [5+5]
