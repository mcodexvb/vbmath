NBHM · 2017 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Let $G$ be a group. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $H$ and $K$ be subgroups of $G$ of orders $3$ and $5$ respectively. Then $H \cap K = \{e\}$, where $e$ is the identity element of $G$. \item If $G$ is an abelian group of odd order, then $\varphi(x) = x^2$ is an automorphism of $G$. \item If $G$ has exactly one element of order $2$, then this element belongs to the centre of $G$. \end{enumerate}
  2. 1.2 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 2$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Any finite group $G$ of order $n$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$. \item The group $\mathbb{Z}_n$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$. \item The group $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $S_7$. \end{enumerate}
  3. 1.3 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The matrices $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ are conjugate in $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$. \item The matrices $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ are conjugate in $SL_2(\mathbb{R})$. \item The matrices $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ are conjugate in $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$. \end{enumerate}
  4. 1.4 Let $p$ be an odd prime. Find the number of non-zero squares in $\mathbb{F}_p$.
  5. 1.5 Find a generator of $\mathbb{F}_7^\times$, the multiplicative group of non-zero elements of $\mathbb{F}_7$.
  6. 1.6 The characteristic polynomial of a matrix $A \in \mathbb{M}_5(\mathbb{R})$ is given by $x^5 + \alpha x^4 + \beta x^3$, where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are non-zero real numbers. What are the possible values of the rank of $A$?
  7. 1.7 Let $A \in \mathbb{M}_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a symmetric matrix whose eigenvalues are $1, 1$ and $3$. Express $A^{-1}$ in the form $\alpha I + \beta A$, where $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$.
  8. 1.8 Let $A \in \mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{R})$, $n \ge 2$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ If $A^{2n} = 0$, then $A^n = 0$. \qquad b.\ If $A^2 = I$, then $A = \pm I$. \qquad c.\ If $A^{2n} = I$, then $A^n = \pm I$.
  9. 1.9 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item There does not exist a non-diagonal matrix $A \in \mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$ such that $A^3 = I$. \item There exists a non-diagonal matrix $A \in \mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$ which is diagonalizable over $\mathbb{R}$ and which is such that $A^3 = I$. \item There exists a non-diagonal matrix $A \in \mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$ such that $A^3 = I$ and such that $\operatorname{tr}(A) = -1$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 1.10 Let $n \ge 2$ and let $W$ be the subspace of $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{R})$ consisting of all matrices whose trace is zero. If $A = (a_{ij})$ and $B = (b_{ij})$, for $1 \le i, j \le n$, are elements in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{R})$, define their inner-product by $(A, B) = \sum_{i,j=1}^{n} a_{ij} b_{ij}$. Identify the subspace $W^\perp$ of elements orthogonal to the subspace $W$.
  11. 2.1 Let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of real numbers. Let $\alpha = \liminf_{n \to \infty} x_n$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item For every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a subsequence $\{x_{n_k}\}$ such that $x_{n_k} \le \alpha + \varepsilon$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$. \item For every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a subsequence $\{x_{n_k}\}$ such that $x_{n_k} \le \alpha - \varepsilon$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$. \item There exists a subsequence $\{x_{n_k}\}$ such that $x_{n_k} \to \alpha$ as $k \to \infty$. \end{enumerate}
  12. 2.2 Let $\{a_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ is divergent. Which of the following series are convergent?\\ a.\ $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_n}{1 + na_n}$. \qquad b.\ $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_n}{1 + n^2 a_n}$. \qquad c.\ $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_n}{1 + a_n}$.
  13. 2.3 Let $\{a_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ is convergent. Which of the following series are convergent?\\ a.\ $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_n}{1 + a_n}$. \qquad b.\ $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{a_n^{\frac{1}{4}}}{n^{\frac{4}{5}}}$. \qquad c.\ $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} na_n \sin\frac{1}{n}$.
  14. 2.4 Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a given function. It is said to be \emph{lower semi-continuous} (respectively \emph{upper semi-continuous}) if the set $f^{-1}(]-\infty, \alpha])$ (respectively, the set $f^{-1}([\alpha, \infty[)$) is closed for every $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$. Let $f$ and $g$ be two real valued functions defined on $\mathbb{R}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $f$ and $g$ are continuous, then $\max\{f, g\}$ is continuous. \item If $f$ and $g$ are lower semi-continuous, then $\max\{f, g\}$ is lower semi-continuous. \item If $f$ and $g$ are upper semi-continuous, then $\max\{f, g\}$ is upper semi-continuous. \end{enumerate}
  15. 2.5 Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $f$ is continuously differentiable, then $f$ is uniformly continuous. \item If $f$ has compact support, then $f$ is uniformly continuous. \item If $\lim_{|x| \to \infty} |f(x)| = 0$, then $f$ is uniformly continuous. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Let $f : ]0, 2[ \to \mathbb{R}$ be defined by \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2, & \text{if } x \in ]0, 2[ \cap \mathbb{Q}, \\ 2x - 1, & \text{if } x \in ]0, 2[ \setminus \mathbb{Q}. \end{cases} \] Check for the points of differentiability of $f$ and evaluate the derivative at those points.
  17. 2.7 Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of continuous real valued functions defined on $\mathbb{R}$ which converges pointwise to a continuous real valued function $f$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $0 \le f_n \le f$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_n(t)\,dt = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\,dt$. \item If $|f_n(t)| \le |\sin t|$ for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$ and for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_n(t)\,dt = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\,dt$. \item If $|f_n(t)| \le e^t$ for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$ and for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then for all $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$, $a < b$, $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_a^b f_n(t)\,dt = \int_a^b f(t)\,dt$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 2.8 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The following series is uniformly convergent over $[-1, 1]$: $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^2}{(1+x^2)^n}$. \item $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} \frac{\sin nx}{nx^5}\,dx = \pi$. \item Define, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sin nx^2}{1 + n^3}$. Then $f$ is a continuously differentiable function. \end{enumerate}
  19. 2.9 Write down the Laurent series expansion of the function $f(z) = \dfrac{-1}{(z - 1)(z - 2)}$ in the annulus $\{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid 1 < |z| < 2\}$.
  20. 2.10 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item There exists a non-constant entire function which is bounded on the real and imaginary axes of $\mathbb{C}$. \item The ring of analytic functions on the open unit disc of $\mathbb{C}$ (with respect to the operations of pointwise addition and pointwise multiplication) is an integral domain. \item There exists an entire function $f$ such that $f(0) = 1$ and such that $|f(z)| \le \frac{1}{|z|}$ for all $|z| \ge 5$. \end{enumerate}
  21. 3.1 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space and let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ and $\{y_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be arbitrary Cauchy sequences in $X$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The sequence $\{d(x_n, y_n)\}$ converges as $n \to \infty$. \item The sequence $\{d(x_n, y_n)\}$ converges as $n \to \infty$ only if $X$ is complete. \item No conclusion can be drawn about the convergence of $\{d(x_n, y_n)\}$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $X$ be a set equipped with two topologies $\tau_1$ and $\tau_2$. Assume that any given sequence in $X$ converges with respect to the topology $\tau_1$ if, and only if, it also converges with respect to the topology $\tau_2$. Then $\tau_1 = \tau_2$. \item Let $(X, \tau_1)$ and $(Y, \tau_2)$ be two topological spaces and let $f : X \to Y$ be a given map. Then $f$ is continuous if, and only if, given any sequence $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ such that $x_n \to x$ in $X$, we have $f(x_n) \to f(x)$ in $Y$. \item Let $(X, \tau)$ be a compact topological space and let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence in $X$. Then, it has a convergent subsequence. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $n \ge 2$. The subset of nilpotent matrices in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ is closed in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$. \item Let $n \ge 2$. The set of all matrices in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$ which represent orthogonal projections is closed in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{C})$. \item The set of all matrices in $\mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$ such that both of their eigenvalues are purely imaginary, is closed in $\mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 3.4 Which of the following sets are dense? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The set of all numbers of the form $\frac{m}{2^n}$ where $0 \le m \le 2^n$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, in the space $[0, 1]$. \item The set of all polynomial functions in the space $L^1(]0, 1[)$. \item The linear span of the family $\{\sin nt\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ in the space $L^2(]-\pi, \pi[)$. \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Let $n \ge 2$. Which of the following subsets are nowhere dense in $\mathbb{M}_n(\mathbb{R})$?\\ a.\ The set $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$. \qquad b.\ The set of all matrices whose trace is zero. \qquad c.\ The set of all singular matrices.
  26. 3.6 Which of the following topological spaces are separable? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Any real Banach space which admits a Schauder basis $\{u_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$. \item The space $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$. \item The space $L^p(]0, 1[)$, where $1 \le p \le \infty$. \end{enumerate}
  27. 3.7 Which of the following sets are connected? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The set of all points in the plane with at least one coordinate irrational. \item An infinite set $X$ with the topology $\tau$ given by $\tau = \{X, \emptyset\} \cup \{A \subset X \mid X \setminus A \text{ is a finite set}\}$. \item The set $K = \left\{f \in \mathcal{C}[0, 1] \;\middle|\; \int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} f(t)\,dt - \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1} f(t)\,dt = 1\right\}$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item There exists a continuous bijection $f : [0, 1] \to [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$. \item There exists a continuous map $f : S^1 \to \mathbb{R}$ which is injective, where $S^1$ stands for the unit circle in the plane. \item There exists a continuous map $f : [0, 1] \to SL_2(\mathbb{R})$ which is surjective. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $g \in \mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ be fixed. Then the set $A = \left\{f \in \mathcal{C}[0, 1] \;\middle|\; \int_0^1 f(t)g(t)\,dt = 0\right\}$ is closed in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$. \item Let $g \in \mathcal{C}_c(\mathbb{R})$ be fixed. Then the set $A = \left\{f \in \mathcal{C}_c(\mathbb{R}) \;\middle|\; \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)g(t)\,dt = 0\right\}$ is closed in $\mathcal{C}_c(\mathbb{R})$. \item Let $g \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ be fixed. Then the set $A = \left\{f \in L^2(\mathbb{R}) \;\middle|\; \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)g(t)\,dt = 0\right\}$ is closed in $L^2(\mathbb{R})$. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $X$ be a compact topological space and let $\mathcal{F}$ be a family of real valued functions defined on $X$ with the following properties: (i) If $f, g \in \mathcal{F}$, then $fg \in \mathcal{F}$, where $(fg)(x) = f(x)g(x)$ for all $x \in X$. (ii) For every $x \in X$, there exists an open neighbourhood $U(x)$ of $x$ and a function $f \in \mathcal{F}$ such that the restriction of $f$ to $U(x)$ is identically zero. Then the function which is identically zero on all of $X$ belongs to $\mathcal{F}$. \item Let $X = \{f : [0, 1] \to [0, 1] \mid |f(t) - f(s)| \le |t - s| \text{ for all } s, t \in [0, 1]\}$. Define $d(f, g) = \max_{t \in [0, 1]} |f(t) - g(t)|$ for $f, g \in X$. Then $(X, d)$ is a compact metric space. \item Let $\{f_i\}_{i \in I}$ be a collection of functions in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ such that given any finite subfamily of functions, its members vanish at some common point (which depends on that subfamily). Then there exists $x_0 \in [0, 1]$ such that $f_i(x_0) = 0$ for all $i \in I$. \end{enumerate}
  31. 4.1 Let $x > 1$. Define $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_{x^2}^{x^3} \tan(xy^2)\,dy$. Differentiate $F$ with respect to $x$.
  32. 4.2 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-2x^2}\,dx$.
  33. 4.3 Let $\mathbf{n}(x, y, z)$ denote the unit outer normal vector on the surface $S$ of the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 \le 4$, $0 \le z \le 3$. Compute $\int_S \mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{n}\,dS$ where $\mathbf{v}(x, y, z) = xz\mathbf{i} + 2yz\mathbf{j} + 3xy\mathbf{k}$.
  34. 4.4 Evaluate the line integral $\int_C P\,dx + Q\,dy$, where $C$ is the circle centered at the origin and of radius $a > 0$ (described in the counter-clockwise sense) in the plane and $P(x, y) = \frac{-y}{x^2+y^2}$, $Q(x, y) = \frac{x}{x^2+y^2}$.
  35. 4.5 Let $\Omega$ be a bounded open subset of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and let $\partial\Omega$ denote its boundary. Given sufficiently smooth real valued functions $u$ and $v$ on $\overline{\Omega}$, let $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ and $\frac{\partial v}{\partial n}$ denote the outer normal derivatives of $u$ and $v$ respectively on $\partial\Omega$. Fill in the blank in the following identity: \[ \int_{\partial\Omega} \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}v - \frac{\partial v}{\partial n}u\right)\,dS = \int_{\Omega} (\dots\dots\dots)\,dx\,dy\,dz. \]
  36. 4.6 Find the maximum value of $x^2 + xy$ subject to the condition $x^2 + y^2 \le 1$.
  37. 4.7 Interchange the order of integration: $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{2}\int_{-x}^{2-x^2} f(x, y)\,dy\,dx$.
  38. 4.8 Find all the non-trivial solutions $(\lambda, u)$ (\emph{i.e.}\ $u \not\equiv 0$), of the boundary value problem: $-u''(x) = \lambda u(x)$, $0 < x < 1$, and $u(0) = u'(1) = 0$.
  39. 4.9 Consider the initial value problem: $u'(t) = Au(t)$, $t > 0$, and $u(0) = u_0$, where $u_0$ is a given vector in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 1 & a \end{bmatrix}$. Find the range of values of $a$ such that $|u(t)| \to 0$ as $t \to \infty$.
  40. 4.10 Let $u(x, t)$ be the solution of the wave equation: $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$, $x \in \mathbb{R},\ t > 0$; $u(x, 0) = u_0(x)$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$; $u_t(x, 0) = 0$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Let $u_0(x)$ be the function defined by $u_0(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } |x| < 2, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ Compute $u(x, 1)$ at all points $x \in \mathbb{R}$ where it is continuous.
  41. 5.1 Let $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k}\sin\left(x + \frac{k\pi}{2}\right)$.
  42. 5.2 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 2$. Let $x_1, \dots, x_n \in ]0, \pi[$. Set $x = (x_1 + \dots + x_n)/n$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ $\prod_{k=1}^{n} \sin x_k \ge \sin^n x$. \qquad b.\ $\prod_{k=1}^{n} \sin x_k \le \sin^n x$. \qquad c.\ Neither (a) nor (b) is necessarily true.
  43. 5.3 Which of the following sets are convex?\\ a.\ $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid xy \ge 1,\ x \ge 0,\ y \ge 0\}$. \qquad b.\ $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid |x|^{\frac{1}{3}} + |y|^{\frac{1}{3}} \le 1\}$. \qquad c.\ $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid y \ge x^2\}$.
  44. 5.4 Find the area of the circle got by intersecting the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ with the plane $x + y + z = 1$.
  45. 5.5 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 3$. Find the area of the polygon with one vertex at $z = 1$ and whose other vertices are situated at the roots of the polynomial $1 + z + z^2 + \dots + z^{n-1}$ in the complex plane.
  46. 5.6 Find the maximum value of $3x + 2y$ subject to the conditions: $2x + 3y \ge 6$, $y - x \le 2$, $0 \le x \le 3$, $y \ge 0$.
  47. 5.7 A committee of six members is formed from a group of $7$ men and $4$ women. What is the probability that the committee contains\\ a.\ exactly two women? \qquad b.\ at least two women?
  48. 5.8 Find the sum of the infinite series: $\dfrac{1}{2\cdot 3\cdot 4} + \dfrac{1}{4\cdot 5\cdot 6} + \dfrac{1}{6\cdot 7\cdot 8} + \cdots$
  49. 5.9 Find the remainder when $8^{130}$ is divided by $13$.
  50. 5.10 Let $a_i \in \mathbb{R}$, $1 \le i \le 4$. Evaluate: \[ \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ a_1 & a_2 & a_3 & a_4 \\ a_1^2 & a_2^2 & a_3^2 & a_4^2 \\ a_1^3 & a_2^3 & a_3^3 & a_4^3 \end{vmatrix}. \]
