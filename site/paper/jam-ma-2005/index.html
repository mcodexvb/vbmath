IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2005

  1. Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\},\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ be sequences of real numbers such that $b_{n}=a_{2 n}$ and $c_{n}=a_{2 n+1}$. Then $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is convergent \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item implies $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is convergent but $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ need not be convergent \item implies $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ is convergent but $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ neet not be convergent \item implies both $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ are convergent \item if both $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ are convergent \end{enumerate}
  2. An integrating factor of $x \frac{d y}{d x}+(3 x+1) y=x e^{-2 x}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $x e^{3 x}$ \item $3 x e^{x}$ \item $x e^{x}$ \item $x^{3} e^{x}$ \end{enumerate}
  3. The general solution of $x^{2} \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}-5 x \frac{d y}{d x}+9 y=0$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\left(c_{1}+c_{2} x\right) e^{3 x}$ \item $\left(c_{1}+c_{2} \ln x\right) x^{3}$ \item $\left(c_{1}+c_{2} x\right) x^{3}$ \item $\left(c_{1}+c_{2} \ln x\right) e^{x^{3}}$ \end{enumerate} (Here $c_{1}$ and $c_{2}$ are arbitrary constants.)
  4. Let $\vec{r}=x \hat{i}+y \hat{j}+z \hat{k}$. If $\phi(x, y, z)$ is a solution of the Laplace equation then the vector field $(\vec{\nabla} \phi+\vec{r})$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item neither solenoidal nor irrotational \item solenoidal but not irrotational \item both solenoidal and irrotational \item irrotational but not solenoidal \end{enumerate}
  5. Let $\vec{F}=x \hat{i}+2 y \hat{j}+3 z \hat{\mathrm{k}}, S$ be the surface of the sphere $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=1$ and $\hat{\mathrm{n}}$ be the inward unit normal vector to $S$. Then $\iint_{S} \vec{F} \cdot \hat{n} d S$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $4 \pi$ \item $-4 \pi$ \item $8 \pi$ \item $-8 \pi$ \end{enumerate}
  6. Let $A$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix with eigenvalues $1,-1$ and 3 . Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $A^{2}+A$ is non-singular \item $A^{2}-A$ is non-singular \item $A^{2}+3 A$ is non-singular \item $A^{2}-3 A$ is non-singular \end{enumerate}
  7. Let $T: \boldsymbol{R}^{3} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}^{3}$ be a linear transformation and $I$ be the identity transformation of $\boldsymbol{R}^{3}$. If there is a scalar $c$ and a non-zero vector $x \in \boldsymbol{R}^{3}$ such that $T(x)=c x$, then $\operatorname{rank}(T-c I)$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item cannot be 0 \item cannot be 1 \item cannot be 2 \item cannot be 3 \end{enumerate}
  8. In the group $\{1,2, \ldots, 16\}$ under the operation of multiplication modulo 17, the order of the element 3 is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 4 \item 8 \item 12 \item 16 \end{enumerate}
  9. A ring $R$ has maximal ideals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item if $R$ is infinite \item if $R$ is finite \item if $R$ is finite with at least 2 elements \item only if $R$ is finite \end{enumerate}
  10. The integral $\int_{0}^{1}\left[\int_{0}^{1-z}\left(\int_{0}^{2} d x\right) d y\right] d z$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\quad \int_{0}^{1}\left[\int_{0}^{1-y}\left(\int_{0}^{2} d x\right) d z\right] d y$ \item $\quad \int_{0}^{1}\left[\int_{0}^{1-y}\left(\int_{0}^{1} d x\right) d z\right] d y$ \item $\int_{0}^{2}\left[\int_{0}^{2}\left(\int_{0}^{1-z} d x\right) d z\right] d y$ \item $\int_{0}^{2}\left[\int_{0}^{2}\left(\int_{0}^{1-y} d x\right) d z\right] d y$ \end{enumerate}
  11. Let $f: \boldsymbol{R} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be continuous and $g, h: \boldsymbol{R}^{2} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be differentiable. Let $F(u, v)=\int_{v}^{u} f(t) d t$, where $u=g(x, y)$ and $v=h(x, y)$. Then $\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}=$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(g(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial g}{\partial y}\right]-f(h(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial h}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial h}{\partial y}\right]$ \item $f(h(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial g}{\partial y}\right]-f(g(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial h}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial h}{\partial y}\right]$ \item $f(h(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial g}{\partial y}\right]+f(g(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial h}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial h}{\partial y}\right]$ \item $f(g(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial g}{\partial y}\right]+f(g(x, y))\left[\frac{\partial h}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial h}{\partial y}\right]$ \end{enumerate}
  12. Let $y=f(x)$ be a smooth curve such that $0<f(x)<K$ for all $x \in[a, b]$. Let $L=$ length of the curve between $x=a$ and $x=b$ $A=$ area bounded by the curve, $x$-axis, and the lines $x=a$ and $x=b$ $S=$ area of the surface generated by revolving the curve about $x$-axis between $x=a$ and $x=b$ Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $2 \pi K L<S<2 \pi A$ \item $S \leq 2 \pi A<2 \pi K L$ \item $2 \pi A \leq S<2 \pi K L$ \item $2 \pi A<2 \pi K L<S$ \end{enumerate}
  13. Let $f: \boldsymbol{R} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be defined by $f(t)=t^{2}$ and let $U$ be any non-empty open subset of $\boldsymbol{R}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(U)$ is open \item $f^{-1}(U)$ is open \item $f(U)$ is closed \item $f^{-1}(U)$ is closed \end{enumerate}
  14. Let $f:(-1,1) \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be such that $f^{(n)}(x)$ exists and $\left|f^{(n)}(x)\right| \leq 1$ for every $n \geq 1$ and for every $x \in(-1,1)$. Then $f$ has a convergent power series expansion in a neighbourhood of \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item every $x \in(-1,1)$ \item every $x \in\left(\frac{-1}{2}, 0\right)$ only \item no $x \in(-1,1)$ \item every $x \in\left(0, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ only \end{enumerate}
  15. Let $a>1$ and $f, g, h:[-a, a] \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be twice differentiable functions such that for some $c$ with $0<c<1<a$, $$ \begin{aligned} &f(x)=0 \text { only for } x=-a, 0, a \\ &f^{\prime}(x)=0=g(x) \text { only } x=-1,0,1 \\ &g^{\prime}(x)=0=h(x) \text { only for } x=-c, c . \end{aligned} $$ The possible relations between $f, g, h$ are \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f=g^{\prime}$ and $h=f^{\prime}$ \item $f^{\prime}=g$ and $g^{\prime}=h$ \item $f=-g^{\prime}$ and $h^{\prime}=g$ \item $f=-g^{\prime}$ and $h^{\prime}=f$ \end{enumerate} \begin{center} \end{center}
  16. \begin{enumerate} \item Solve the initial value problem $$ \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}-y=x\left(\sin x+e^{x}\right), \quad y(0)=y^{\prime}(0)=1 $$ \item Solve the differential equation $$ \left(2 y \sin x+3 y^{4} \sin x \cos x\right) d x-\left(4 y^{3} \cos ^{2} x+\cos x\right) d y=0 $$ \end{enumerate}
  17. Let $G$ be a finite abelian group of order $n$ with identity $e$. If for all $a \in G, a^{3}=e$, then, by induction on $n$, show that $n=3^{k}$ for some non-negative integer $k$.
  18. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $f:[a, b] \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be a differentiable function. Show that there exist points $c_{1}, c_{2} \in(a, b)$ such that $$ 2 f\left(c_{1}\right) f^{\prime}\left(c_{1}\right)=f^{\prime}\left(c_{2}\right)[] $$ \item Let $$ f(x, y)= \begin{cases}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)\left[\ln \left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)+1\right] & \text { for }(x, y) \neq(0,0) \\ \alpha & \text { for }(x, y)=(0,0)\end{cases} $$ Find a suitable value for $\alpha$ such that $f$ is continuous. For this value of $\alpha$, is $f$ differentiable at $(0,0)$ ? Justify your claim. \end{enumerate}
  19. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $S$ be the surface $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=1, z \geq 0$. Use Stokes' theorem to evaluate $$ \int_{C}[(2 x-y) d x-y d y-z d z] $$ where $C$ is the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}=1, z=0$, oriented anticlockwise. \item Show that the vector field $\vec{F}=\left(2 x y-y^{4}+3\right) \hat{\mathrm{i}}+\left(x^{2}-4 x y^{3}\right) \hat{\mathrm{j}}$ is conservative. Find its potential and also the work done in moving a particle from $(1,0)$ to (2,1) along some curve. \end{enumerate}
  20. Let $T: \boldsymbol{R}^{3} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}^{3}$ be defined by $T(x, y, z)=(y+z, z, 0)$. Show that $T$ is a linear transformation. If $v \in \boldsymbol{R}^{3}$ is such that $T^{2}(v) \neq 0$, then show that $B=\left\{v, T(v), T^{2}(v)\right\}$ forms a basis of $\boldsymbol{R}^{3}$. Compute the matrix of $T$ with respect to $B$. Also find a $v \in \boldsymbol{R}^{3}$ such that $T^{2}(v) \neq 0$
  21. \begin{enumerate} \item For each $n \in N$, define $f_{n}:[-1,1] \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ by $$ f_{n}(x)= \begin{cases}4 n^{2} x & \text { for } x \in\left[0, \frac{1}{2 n}\right) \\ -4 n^{2}\left(x-\frac{1}{n}\right) & \text { for } x \in\left[\frac{1}{2 n}, \frac{1}{n}\right) \\ 0 & \text { for } x \in\left[\frac{1}{n}, 1\right]\end{cases} $$ Compute $\int_{0}^{1} f_{n}(x) d x$ for each $n$. Analyse pointwise and uniform convergence of the sequence of functions $\left\{f_{n}\right\}$. \item Let $f: \boldsymbol{R} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be a continuous function with $|f(x)-f(y)| \geq|x-y|$ for every $x, y \in \boldsymbol{R}$. Is $f$ one-one? Show that there cannot exist three points $a, b, c \in \boldsymbol{R}$ with $a<b<c$ such that $f(a)<f(c)<f(b)$ \end{enumerate}
  22. Find the volume of the cylinder with base as the disk of unit radius in the $x y$-plane centred at $(1,1,0)$ and the top being the surface $z=\left[(x-1)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}\right]^{3 / 2}$. \begin{center} \end{center}
  23. \begin{enumerate} \item Bag A contains 3 white and 4 red balls, and bag B contains 6 white and 3 red balls. A biased coin, twice as likely to come up heads as tails, is tossed once. If it shows head, a ball is drawn from bag A, otherwise, from bag B. Given that a white ball was drawn, what is the probability that the coin came up tail? \item Let the random variables $X$ and $Y$ have the joint probability density function $f(x, y)$ given by $$ f(x, y)= \begin{cases}y^{2} e^{-y(x+1)} & x \geq 0, y \geq 0 \\ 0 & \text { otherwise }\end{cases} $$ Are the random variables $X$ and $Y$ independent? Justify your answer. \end{enumerate}
  24. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}$ be independently identically distributed random variables (rv's) with common probability density function (pdf) $f_{X}(x, \theta)=\frac{1}{\theta} e^{-x / \theta} ; x>0, \theta>0$. Obtain the moment generating function (mgf) of $\bar{X}=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_{i}$. Also find the mgf of the rv $Y=2 n \bar{X} / \theta$ \item Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{9}$ be an independent random sample from $N(2,4)$ and $Y_{1}, Y_{2}, Y_{3}, Y_{4}$ be an independent random sample from $N(1,1)$. Find $P(\bar{X}>\bar{Y})$, where $\bar{X}$ and $\bar{Y}$ are sample means. [ Given $P(Z>1.2)=0.1151$, where $Z \sim N(0,1)]$ \end{enumerate} \begin{center} \end{center}
  25. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}$ be a random sample from a distribution having pdf $$ f\left(x ; x_{0}, \alpha\right)= \begin{cases}\frac{\alpha x_{0}^{\alpha}}{x^{\alpha+1}} & \text { for } x>x_{0} \\ 0 & \text { otherwise }\end{cases} $$ where $x_{0}>0, \alpha>0$. Find the maximum likelihood estimator of $\alpha$ if $x_{0}$ is known. \item Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{5}$ be a random sample from the standard normal population. Determine the constant $c$ such that the random variable $$ Y=\frac{c\left(X_{1}+X_{2}\right)}{\sqrt{X_{3}^{2}+X_{4}^{2}+X_{5}^{2}}} $$ will have a $t$-distribution. \end{enumerate}
  26. \begin{enumerate} \item A random sample of size $n=1$ is drawn from pdf $f_{X}(x, \theta)=\frac{1}{\theta} e^{-x / \theta} ; x>0, \theta>0$. It is decided to test $H_{0}: \theta=5$ against $H_{1}: \theta=7$ based on the criterion: reject $H_{0}$ if the observed value is greater than $10$. Obtain the probabilities of type I and type II errors. \item Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}$ be a random sample from a normal population $N\left(\mu, \sigma^{2}\right)$. Find the best test for testing $H_{0}: \mu=0, \sigma^{2}=1$ against $H_{1}: \mu=1, \sigma^{2}=4$. \end{enumerate} \begin{center} \end{center}
  27. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $f, g: \boldsymbol{R} \longrightarrow \boldsymbol{R}$ be such that for $x, y \in \boldsymbol{R}$, $$ \phi(x+i y)=e^{x}[f(y)+i g(y)] $$ is an analytic function. Find a differential equation of order 2 satisfied by $f$. \item Compute $\int_{|z+1|=2}(2 z+1) e^{(\sqrt{2}+1 / z)} d z$. \end{enumerate}
  28. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $f(z)$ be analytic in the whole complex plane such that for all $r>0$, $$ \int_{0}^{2 \pi}\left|f\left(r e^{i \theta}\right)\right| d \theta \leq \sqrt{r} $$ Find $\frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n !}$ for all $n \geq 0$ \item Find all values of $\alpha \in C$ such that $f(z)=(z+\bar{z})^{2}+2 \alpha|z|^{2}+\alpha(\bar{z})^{2}$ is analytic at some point $z$ having non-zero real part. \end{enumerate} \begin{center} \end{center}
  29. A hemispherical bowl of radius $12 \mathrm{~cm}$ is fixed such that its rim is horizontal. A light rod of length $20 \mathrm{~cm}$ with weights $w$ and $W$ attached to its two ends is placed inside the bowl. In equilibrium, the weight $w$ is just touching the rim of the bowl. Find the ratio $w: W$.
  30. A uniform ladder of length $2 a$ and mass $m$ lies in a vertical plane with one end against a smooth wall, the other end being supported on a horizontal floor. The ladder is released from rest when inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. Find the inclination of the ladder to the horizontal when it ceases to touch the wall.
  31. \begin{enumerate} \item Estimate the error in evaluating the integral $\int_{0}^{8}\left(1+x^{2}\right) e^{-x} d x$ by Simpson's $\frac{1}{3}$ rd rule with spacing $h=0.25$. \item Using Newton-Raphson method, compute the point of intersection of the curves $y=x^{3}$ and $y=8 x+4$ near the point $x=3$, correct up to 2 decimal places. [Round-off the first iteration up to 2 decimal places for further computation] \end{enumerate}
  32. The polynomial $p_{3}(x)=x^{3}+x^{2}-2$ interpolates the function $f(x)$ at the points $x=-1,0,1$ and 2 . If the data $f(3)=-14$ is added, find the new interpolating polynomial by using Newton's forward difference formula. Also find $f(2.5)$ by using Newton's backward difference formula with pivot value 3. Justify whether the value obtained will be the same if pivot value 2 is taken.
