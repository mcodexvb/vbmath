MJMA-7

Algebra-III
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2026
  1. 1 Answer \emph{any ten} questions. Provide a clear and complete justification for each answer whenever necessary. Each question carries 2 marks. [$(10\times2=20)$]
  2. 1(a) Give an example of a vector space over a finite field.
  3. 1(b) Determine whether the vectors $(1,2,3)$, $(2,4,6)$, $(1,1,0)$ are linearly dependent or independent in $\mathbb R^3$.
  4. 1(c) Is the set $W=\{(a,b,c)\in\mathbb R^3:a^2+b^2+c^2=0\}$ a subspace of $\mathbb R^3$?
  5. 1(d) Find the dimension of the vector space $M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)$.
  6. 1(e) Determine whether the set $\{1+x,1-x,x^2\}$ forms a basis for $\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R)$.
  7. 1(f) Find the row-rank of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\2&4&6\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix}$.
  8. 1(g) Let $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ be defined by $T(a,b)=(a+b,a-b)$. Check whether $T$ is linear.
  9. 1(h) Give an example of a linear transformation whose kernel is nontrivial.
  10. 1(i) Find the nullity of the linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ defined by $T(x,y,z)=(x+y,y+z)$.
  11. 1(j) Find $T(a,b)$, where $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ represents the projection onto the $y$-axis along the $x$-axis.
  12. 1(k) Let $\beta=\{(1,0),(0,1)\}$ be the standard basis of $\mathbb R^2$. Find the coordinate vector of $(3,-2)$ relative to $\beta$.
  13. 1(l) Can two vector spaces of different dimensions be isomorphic?
  14. 1(m) Can the row vectors of a nonsingular matrix $A$ form a basis for the row space of $A$?
  15. 1(n) Define the dual basis in a vector space.
  16. 1(o) Can a vector space over an infinite field have only a finite number of bases?
  17. 2(a) Let $V=M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)$ and let $W=\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in V:a+d=0\}$. Show that $W$ is a subspace of $V$. Further, describe the quotient space $V/W$. [[3+3]]
  18. 2(b) Show that a subset $W$ of a vector space $V$ is a subspace of $V$ if and only if $L(W)=W$. [[4]]
  19. 2(c) In $M_{m\times n}(F)$ define $W_1=\{A\in M_{m\times n}(F):A_{ij}=0\text{ whenever }i>j\}$ and $W_2=\{A\in M_{m\times n}(F):A_{ij}=0\text{ whenever }i\le j\}$. Show that $M_{m\times n}(F)=W_1\oplus W_2$. [[5]]
  20. 3(a) Determine whether the following sets are linearly dependent or linearly independent: \par i. $\{x^3+2x^2,-x^2+3x+1,x^3-x^2+2x-1\}$ in $P_3(\mathbb R)$. \par ii. $\{(1,1,0),(1,0,1),(0,1,1)\}$ in $\mathbb Z_2^3$. [[3]]
  21. 3(b) Find conditions on $a,b,c$ such that $w=(a,b,c)$ is a linear combination of $u=(1,2,3)$ and $v=(2,3,1)$ in $\mathbb R^3$. [[3]]
  22. 3(c) Let $V$ be a vector space with dimension $n$. Prove that any linearly independent subset of $V$ containing exactly $n$ vectors is a basis for $V$. [[6]]
  23. 4(a) Find the row space and the row rank of the matrix $M=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&3&1&4\\1&3&1&2&1\\3&9&4&5&2\\4&12&8&8&7\end{bmatrix}$. [[1+4]]
  24. 4(b) Consider the subspaces $U=\{(a,b,c,d)\in\mathbb R^4:b-2c+d=0\}$ and $W=\{(a,b,c,d)\in\mathbb R^4:a=d,\ b=2c\}$ of $\mathbb R^4$. Find a basis and the dimension of $U+W$. [[6]]
  25. 4(c) Show that the vector space of all real numbers is a infinite dimensional vector space over the field of rational number. [[4]]
  26. 5(a) Consider the linear transformation $T:P(\mathbb R)\to P(\mathbb R)$ defined by $T(f(x))=\int_0^x f(t)dt$. Prove that $T$ is linear and one-to-one, but not onto and determine whether or not the linear map $F:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ defined by $F(x,y,z)=(x+y+z,2x+3y+5z,x+3y+7z)$ is nonsingular. [[2+2+4]]
  27. 5(b) Suppose $F$ and $G$ are linear operators on $V$ and that $F$ is nonsingular. Assume that $V$ has finite dimension. Show that $\operatorname{rank}(FG)=\operatorname{rank}(GF)=\operatorname{rank}(G)$. [[3+3]]
  28. 5(c) Find the dimension of $\operatorname{Hom}(M_{2,4},P_2(t))$. [[1]]
  29. 6(a) Let $T$ be the linear operator on $P_1(\mathbb R)$ defined by $T(p(x))=p'(x)$, the derivative of $p(x)$. Let $\beta=\{1,x\}$ and $\beta'=\{1+x,1-x\}$ be two ordered bases of $P_1(\mathbb R)$. Find the change of basis matrix from $\beta$ to $\beta'$. [[4]]
  30. 6(b) Let $D=\frac{d}{dt}$ be the differential operator on the vector space $V=\{f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R\}$ of real functions. Find the matrix representation of $D$ relative to the basis $S=\{e^{3t},te^{3t},t^2e^{3t}\}$. [[4]]
  31. 6(c) Let $T:V\to V$ be a linear operator, and let $S$ be a finite basis of $V$. Prove that for any vector $v\in V$, $[T]_S[v]_S=[T(v)]_S$. [[7]]
  32. 7(a) Suppose $\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_n\}$ is a basis of $V$ over a field $K$. Let $\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_n\in V^*$ be defined by $\phi_i(v_j)=0$ for $i\ne j$, and $\phi_i(v_i)=1$. Show that $\{\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_n\}$ is a basis of $V^*$. [[7]]
  33. 7(b) Find the basis $\{v_1,v_2\}$ of the vector space $V=\{a+bt:a,b\in\mathbb R\}$ that is dual to the basis $\{\phi_1,\phi_2\}$ of $V^*$ defined by $\phi_1(f(t))=\int_0^1 f(t)dt$ and $\phi_2(f(t))=\int_0^2 f(t)dt$. [[4]]
  34. 7(c) Let $v$ be a nonzero vector in a finite dimensional vector space $V$ over a field $\mathbb F$. Show that there exists a $\phi\in V^*$ such that $\phi(v)\ne0$. [[4]]
