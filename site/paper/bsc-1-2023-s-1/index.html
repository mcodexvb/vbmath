MJMA-1

Algebra I and Analysis I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2023
  1. 1(a) Let $G$ be a set with $n$ elements. How many binary operations can you define on $G$?
  2. 1(b) Let $G=\{-1,1,i,-i\}$ where $i=\sqrt{-1}$. Prove that $G$ is a cyclic group under multiplication.
  3. 1(c) Is the group $GL_2(\mathbb R)$ of invertible $2\times2$ real matrices cyclic? Justify your answer. [[2+5+3]]
  4. 2(a) Prove that the order of every element of a finite group is finite.
  5. 2(b) Let $x,y$ and $z$ be elements of a group $G$ such that $xyz=e$. Does it follow that $yzx=e$?
  6. 2(c) Let $G$ be a group such that $g^2=e$ for all $g\in G$. Prove that $G$ is abelian. [[3+3+4]]
  7. 3(a) Prove that $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ is not a group under multiplication for any integer $n\ge2$.
  8. 3(b) Let $G$ be an infinite group and let $H_1,H_2,\dots$ be a collection of groups such that $H_n\subseteq H_{n+1}$ for all $n\ge1$. Prove that $\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty H_n$ is a subgroup of $G$.
  9. 3(c) Let $G$ be a finite group of order $n>2$. Prove that $G$ does not have any subgroup of order $n-1$. [[3+3+4]]
  10. 4(a) Define the center $Z(G)$ of a group $G$. Determine, with proof, $Z(Q_8)$ and $Z(S_n)$ for $n\ge3$.
  11. 4(b) What is the maximum possible order of an element of $S_7$? Justify your answer.
  12. 4(c) For $n\ge4$, prove that the number of elements in $S_n$ which are the product of two disjoint 2-cycles is $\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)}8$. [[5+2+3]]
  13. 5(a) Write the elements of the group $(\mathbb Z/18\mathbb Z)^*$ and justify whether it is a cyclic group or not.
  14. 5(b) Let $G$ be a group of order 28. Prove that $G$ has a non-trivial proper subgroup.
  15. 5(c) Prove that every non-abelian group has a non-trivial cyclic subgroup. [[4+3+3]]
  16. 6(a) Prove that every finitely generated subgroup of $(\mathbb Q,+)$ is cyclic.
  17. 6(b) Determine, with proof, the smallest subgroup of $(\mathbb Z,+)$ containing 6, 8 and 10.
  18. 6(c) Let $H$ be a subgroup of $(\mathbb Q,+)$ with the property that $\frac1x\in H$ for every non-zero element $x\in H$. Prove that either $H=\{0\}$ or $H=\mathbb Q$. [[4+3+3]]
  19. 1(a) Prove the following results:\\(i) If $a\ne0$ and $b\in\mathbb R$ such that $a.b=1$ then $b=\frac1a$.\\(ii) If $a.b=0$ then either $a=0$ or $b=0$. [[5]]
  20. 1(b) For any three elements $a,b,c\in\mathbb R$ prove the following results :\\(i) If $a>b$ and $b>c$ then $a>c$.\\(ii) If $a>b$ and $c>0$ then $ca>cb$. [[5]]
  21. 2(a) Let $x,y\in\mathbb R$ and $x<y$. Then prove that\\(i) there is a rational number $r$ such that $x<r<y$.\\(ii) there is an irrational number $s$ such that $x<s<y$. [[5][5]]
  22. 2(b) Show that $\mathbb Q$ ( set of all rational numbers ) is countable.
  23. 3(a) Define Cauchy sequence and give an example for the same. Prove that every Cauchy sequence is bounded. Does the converse hold? Justify. [[5]]
  24. 3(b) Examine if the sequence $x_n=1-\frac12+\frac13+\dots+\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}n$ is a Cauchy sequence. [[5]]
  25. 4(a) Prove that every monotone decreasing sequence which is bounded below is convergent and converges to its greatest lower bound. [[5]]
  26. 4(b) Evaluate $\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\{(a+1)(a+2)\cdots(a+n)\}^{\frac1n}$ if $a>0$. [[5]]
  27. 5(a) Define subsequence of a sequence. Prove that if a sequence is convergent then every subsequence of it is convergent. Does the converse hold? Justify. [[4]]
  28. 5(b) State and prove nested intervals theorem. [[6]]
  29. 6(a) Give $(\epsilon-\delta)$ definition of the limit of a function and hence evaluate $\lim_{x\to3}f(x)$ where $f(x)=x^3+3x^2+3x+5$, $x\in[2,5]$. [[5]]
  30. 6(b) If $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=l$ and $\lim_{x\to a}g(x)=m$ then prove that $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)g(x)=lm$. \fbox{\parbox{3.4cm}{\small Use separate answer script for each unit}} [[5]]
