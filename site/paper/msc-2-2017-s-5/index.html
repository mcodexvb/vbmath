MMC-25

Solid Mechanics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2017
  1. 1(a) Prove that the extremum values of normal strains at a point of a continuum are principal strains. [5]
  2. 1(b) The displacement components in an elastic solid are given as follows: \begin{align*} u_1&=\epsilon(X_1+2X_2+3X_3)\\ u_2&=\epsilon(-2X_1+X_2)\\ u_3&=\epsilon(X_1+4X_2+2X_3) \end{align*} where $\epsilon$ is a small quantity. Calculate dilatation, rotation and principal strains. [5]
  3. 2(a) Write down the expressions for Lagrangian and Eulerian finite strain tensor. Show that, in case of infinitesimal deformation, the distinction between them disappears. [1+4]
  4. 2(b) For the deformation $u_i=A_{ij}X_j$, where $A_{ij}$ are constants $(i,j=1,2,3)$, determine an expression for change of volume per unit original volume. If $A_{ij}$ are very small, show that it reduces to cubical dilatation. [5]
  5. 3(a) Prove that the stress vector at a point on any arbitrary plane surface is a linear function of three stress vectors acting on any three mutually perpendicular planes through that point. [5]
  6. 3(b) The stress tensor at a point is given by \[ (\tau_{ij})=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&2\\1&b&1\\2&1&0\end{pmatrix} \quad\text{where }b\text{ is a constant.} \] Determine $b$ so that stress vector on some plane at the point will be zero. Determine the direction cosines of the normal to that plane. [5]
  7. 4(a) State the principles of conservation of mass in both material and spatial methods. Write down their equations. Show that these two forms are equivalent. [2+1+3]
  8. 4(b) Given the following stress distribution \[ (\tau_{ij})=\begin{pmatrix}x_2&-x_3&0\\-x_3&0&-x_2\\0&-x_2&T\end{pmatrix}, \] find $T$ such that the stress distribution is in equilibrium with body force $\vec F=-g\hat e_3$. [4]
  9. 5(a) Define the first BVP of elastostatics and obtain the compatibility equation for stresses. [1+4]
  10. 5(b) In case of equilibrium of elastic body under zero body forces, prove that the first strain invariant $\Delta$ and rotation tensor $\omega_{ij}$ are harmonic functions. \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [5]
  11. 6(a) Derive the displacement components in plane elasticity from Airy's stress function $\chi(x,y)$. [5]
  12. 6(b) When a body is subjected to uniform pressure such that \[ \tau_{11}=\tau_{22}=\tau_{33}=-p,\quad \tau_{23}=\tau_{31}=\tau_{12}=0. \] Prove that $\dfrac{u_1}{x_1}=\dfrac{u_2}{x_2}=\dfrac{u_3}{x_3}=\dfrac{-p}{3K}$, assuming that the displacement and rotation at $x_1=0$, $x_2=0$, $x_3=0$ are zero. \endgroup \begingroup\renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of value as indicated in the margin.\par } [5]
