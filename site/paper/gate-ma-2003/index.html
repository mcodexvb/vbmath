GATE Mathematics · Mathematics (MA), 2003

  1. 1 Let $T$ be an arbitrary linear transformation from $\mathbb{R}^n$ to $\mathbb{R}^n$ which is not one-one. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\operatorname{Rank} T > 0$ \item $\operatorname{Rank} T = n$ \item $\operatorname{Rank} T < n$ \item $\operatorname{Rank} T = n - 1$ \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $T$ be a linear transformation from $\mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ defined by $T(x,y,z) = (x+y,\, y-z)$. Then the matrix of $T$ with respect to the ordered bases $\{(1,1,1),\, (1,-1,0),\, (0,1,0)\}$ and $\{(1,1),\, (1,0)\}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\begin{bmatrix} -2 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$ \item $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$ \item $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ \item $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$ \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let the characteristic equation of a matrix $M$ be $\lambda^2 - \lambda - 1 = 0$, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $M^{-1}$ does not exist \item $M^{-1}$ exists but cannot be determined from the data \item $M^{-1} = M + I$ \item $M^{-1} = M - I$ \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Consider a function $f(z) = u + iv$ defined on $|z - i| < 1$, where $u, v$ are real-valued functions of $x, y$. Then $f(z)$ is analytic for $u$ equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $x^2 + y^2$ \item $\ln(x^2 + y^2)$ \item $e^{xy}$ \item $e^{x^2 - y^2}$ \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 At $z = 0$, the function $f(z) = z^2 \bar{z}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item does not satisfy Cauchy--Riemann equations \item satisfies Cauchy--Riemann equations but is not differentiable \item is differentiable \item is analytic \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 The bilinear transformation $w$ which maps the points $0, 1, \infty$ in the $z$-plane onto the points $-i, \infty, 1$ in the $w$-plane is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\dfrac{z - 1}{z + i}$ \item $\dfrac{z - i}{z + 1}$ \item $\dfrac{z + i}{z - 1}$ \item $\dfrac{z + 1}{z - i}$ \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 The continuous function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by $f(x) = (x^2 + 1)^{2003}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item onto but not one-one \item one-one but not onto \item both one-one and onto \item neither one-one nor onto \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Diameter of a set $S$ in a metric space with metric $d$ is defined by $\operatorname{diam}(S) = \operatorname{l.u.b.}\{d(x,y) : x, y \in S\}$. Thus, the diameter of the cylinder $C = \{(x,y,z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid x^2 + y^2 = 1,\ -1 < z < 1\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with standard metric is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $2$ \item $2\sqrt{2}$ \item $\sqrt{2}$ \item $\pi + 2$ \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 Let $X = (0,1) \cup (2,3)$ be an open set in $\mathbb{R}$. Let $f$ be a continuous function on $X$ such that the derivative $f'(x) = 0$ for all $x$. Then the range of $f$ has \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item uncountable number of points \item countably infinite number of points \item at most $2$ points \item at most $1$ point \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 The orthogonal trajectory to the family of circles $x^2 + y^2 = 2cx$ ($c$ arbitrary) is described by the differential equation \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(x^2 + y^2)y' = 2xy$ \item $(x^2 - y^2)y' = 2xy$ \item $(y^2 - x^2)y' = xy$ \item $(y^2 - x^2)y' = 2xy$ \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 Let $y_1(x)$ and $y_2(x)$ be solutions of $y'' + x^2 y' + (\sin x)y = 0$, which satisfy the boundary conditions $y_1(0) = 0$, $y_1'(1) = 1$ and $y_2(0) = 1$, $y_2'(1) = 0$ respectively. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $y_1$ and $y_2$ do not have common zeroes \item $y_1$ and $y_2$ have common zeroes \item either $y_1$ or $y_2$ has a zero of order $2$ \item both $y_1$ and $y_2$ have zeroes of order $2$ \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 For the Sturm--Liouville problem $(1 + x^2)y'' + 2xy' + \lambda x^2 y = 0$ with $y'(1) = 0$ and $y'(10) = 0$, the eigenvalues $\lambda$ satisfy \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\lambda \ge 0$ \item $\lambda < 0$ \item $\lambda \ne 0$ \item $\lambda \le 0$ \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 The number of groups of order $n$ (up to isomorphism) is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item finite for all values of $n$ \item finite only for finitely many values of $n$ \item finite for infinitely many values of $n$ \item infinite for some values of $n$ \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 The set of all real $2 \times 2$ invertible matrices acts on $\mathbb{R}^2$ by matrix multiplication. The number of orbits for this action is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $1$ \item $2$ \item $4$ \item infinite \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 Let $l_2$ be the set of real sequences $\{x_n\}$ such that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_n|^2 < \infty$. For $x \in l_2$ define $\|x\|^2 = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_n|^2$. Consider the set $S = \{x \in l_2 : \|x\| < 1\}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item interior of $S$ is compact \item $S$ is compact \item closure of $S$ is compact \item closure of $S$ is not compact \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 On $X = C[0,1]$, define $T : X \to X$ by $T(f)(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^x f(t)\,dt$ for all $f \in X$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $T$ is one-one and onto \item $T$ is one-one but not onto \item $T$ is not one-one but onto \item $T$ is neither one-one nor onto \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $M$ be the length of the initial interval $[a_0, b_0]$ containing a solution of $f(x) = 0$.   18. 18 On evaluating $\displaystyle\int_1^2 \int_1^2 \frac{1}{x + y}\,dx\,dy$ numerically by the Trapezoidal rule, one would get the value \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\dfrac{17}{48}$ \item $\dfrac{11}{48}$ \item $\dfrac{21}{48}$ \item $\dfrac{17}{52}$ \end{enumerate}

  20. 20 Pick the region in which the following differential equation is hyperbolic: \[ y u_{xx} + 2xy u_{xy} + x u_{yy} = u_x + u_y. \] \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $xy \ne 1$ \item $xy \ne 0$ \item $xy > 1$ \item $xy > 0$ \end{enumerate}
  21. 21 If the total kinetic energy of a system of particles about the origin is equal to its kinetic energy about the center of mass, then the center of mass is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item at rest \item moving along a circle \item moving on a straight line \item moving along an ellipse \end{enumerate}
