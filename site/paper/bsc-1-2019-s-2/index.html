CC-2

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2019
  1. 1(a) Find the general solution of the system of equations whose augmented matrix is given by $\begin{pmatrix}1&-7&0&6&5\\0&0&1&-2&-3\\-1&7&-4&2&7\end{pmatrix}$. \par Explain the consistency of a system of linear equations whose coefficient matrix has a pivot position in every rows. [2]
  2. 1(b) Give an example to show that row echelon form of a matrix may not be unique. [2]
  3. 1(c) Let $m,n,p$ be three positive integers such that $m>n$. Suppose that $A$ and $B$ be two matrices of order $m\times n$ and $n\times p$ respectively. If row rank of $B$ is $p$, find row rank of $AB$. [2]
  4. 2(a) Suppose $A\in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb R)$ be such that $C_1,C_2,\ldots,C_n$ are $n$ columns of $A$. If the system of equations $AX=b$ has a solution show that $b$ is a linear combination of $C_1,C_2,\ldots,C_n$. [3]
  5. 2(b) Give a geometric description of span $(\{(8,2,-6),(12,13,-9)\})$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Given $A$ be a $3\times4$ matrix, let $y_1$ and $y_2$ be two vectors in $\mathbb R^3$, and let $w=y_1+y_2$. Suppose $y_1=Ax_1$ and $y=Ax_2$ for some vectors $x_1,x_2$ in $\mathbb R^4$. Find the condition such that the system $Ax=w$ is consistent? [2]
  7. 2(d) Suppose that the solution set of a certain system of linear equations can be described as $x_1=5+4x_3,\ x_2=-2-7x_3$, with $x_3$ free. Use these vectors to describe that this set represents a line in $\mathbb R^3$. [2]
  8. 3(a) Show that two vectors in $\mathbb R^2$ is linearly dependent if and only if they lie on a line passing through the origin. [3]
  9. 3(b) How many pivot columns must be in a $5\times7$ matrix, if its columns span $\mathbb R^5$? Justify your answer. [3]
  10. 3(c) Determine if the columns of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&4&-3&0\\-2&-7&5&1\\-4&-5&7&5\end{pmatrix}$ forms a linearly independent set in $\mathbb R^3$. [4]
  11. 4(a) Let $H$ be the set of points inside and on the unit circle in the $xy$-plane. Show that $H$ is not a subspace of $\mathbb R^3$. [2]
  12. 4(b) Find a basis and dimension of the null space of $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&-3&2&-4\\-3&9&-1&5\\2&-6&4&-3\\-4&12&2&7\end{pmatrix}$ in $\mathbb R^4$. Hence find the dimension of column space of $A$, by using Rank-Nullity Theorem. [4+2]
  13. 4(c) How the linear independenceness of the row vectors of a square matrix depend on its nonsingularity? [2]
  14. 5(a) Let $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ be defined by, $T(x,y,z)=(x-y+z,\ 2x+y-3z)$. Show that $T$ is a linear transformation. Interpret Ker $T$ geometrically. [3+3]
  15. 5(b) Let $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ be a linear transformation, and let $\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$ be a linearly dependent set in $\mathbb R^n$. Explain whether the set $\{Tv_1,Tv_2,Tv_3\}$ is linearly independent or not. [2]
  16. 5(c) Let $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ be a linear transformation that maps $u=\begin{pmatrix}5\\2\end{pmatrix}$ into $\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}$ and maps $v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\end{pmatrix}$ into $\begin{pmatrix}-1\\3\end{pmatrix}$. Find the image of $3u+2v$. [2]
  17. 6(a) If $v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_n$ are eigen vectors that correspond to distinct eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\text{---},\lambda_n$ of an $n\times n$ matrix $A$, then show that the set $\{v_1,v_2,\text{---},v_n\}$ is linearly independent. [3]
  18. 6(b) Consider an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ with the property that the row sums are all equal and equals to $s$. Show that $s$ is an eigen value of $A$. [3]
  19. 6(c) Find a basis for the eigenspace corresponding to the eigenvalue $\lambda=4$ of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}3&0&2&0\\1&3&1&0\\0&1&1&0\\0&0&0&4\end{pmatrix}$. Hence find the dimension of column space of $A-4I$, where $I$ is the $4\times4$ identity matrix. [2+2]
  20. 7(a) If $\alpha=\cos\frac{2\pi}n+i\sin\frac{2\pi}n$ and $p$ is prime to n, then prove that $1+\alpha^p+\alpha^{2p}+\ldots+\alpha^{(n-1)p}=0$. [3]
  21. 7(b) If $n$ be a positive integer and $(1+z)^n=p_0+p_1z+p_2z^2+\ldots+p_nz^n$, prove that $p_0+p_4+p_8+\ldots=2^{n-2}+2^{(n-2)/2}\cos\frac{n\pi}4.$. [4]
  22. 7(c) Show that the sum of the 99th powers of the roots of the equation $z^7-1=0$ is zero. [3]
  23. 8(a) State Fundamental Theorem of Classical Algebra. Show that an mth degree algebraic equation has exactly $m$ number of roots. [1+4]
  24. 8(b) Show that the equation $(x-a)^3+(x-b)^3+(x-c)^3+(x-d)^3=0$, where $a,b,c,d$ are all positive and not all equal, has only one real root. [3]
  25. 8(c) If $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be the roots of the equation $x^3+px^2+qx+r=0$, find the value of $(\beta+\gamma-\alpha)^3+(\gamma+\alpha-\beta)^3+(\alpha+\beta-\gamma)^3$. [2]
  26. 9(a) If $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be the roots of the equation $x^3-2x^2+3x-1=0$, find the value of $\frac{\beta\gamma-\alpha^2}{\beta+\gamma-2\alpha},\ \frac{\gamma\alpha-\beta^2}{\gamma+\alpha-2\beta},\ \frac{\alpha\beta-\gamma^2}{\alpha+\beta-2\gamma}$. [4]
  27. 9(b) Solve by Ferrari's method $x^4+12x-5=0$. [3]
  28. 9(c) If $a,b,c$ are sides of a triangle and $a+b+c=2s$, then show that\par i) $s^3\ge27(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$\par ii) $\frac{s^2}{3\sqrt3}\ge A$, where $A=$ area of the triangle. [3]
