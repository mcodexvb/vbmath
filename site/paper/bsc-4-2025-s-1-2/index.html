MJMA-8

Analysis IV
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2025
  1. 1(a) Define pointwise and uniform convergence of a sequence of functions $\{f_n\}$. [2]
  2. 1(b) If $f_n(x)=n^2x^2e^{-nx}$, determine whether the sequence $\{f_n\}$ is uniformly convergent on $[0,\infty)$. [4]
  3. 1(c) Show that the sequence $\{\tan^{-1}nx\}$ is pointwise convergent on $\mathbb R$. [4]
  4. 2(a) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a pointwise convergent sequence of bounded functions on $D\subseteq\mathbb R$. Show that its limit function may not be bounded on $D$. [4]
  5. 2(b) If a sequence $\{f_n\}$ of Riemann integrable functions converges uniformly to a function $f$ on a closed and bounded interval $[a,b]$, show that $f$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$ and the sequence $\{\int_a^b f_n\}$ converges to $\int_a^b f$. [6]
  6. 3(a) State and prove Cauchy's principle for uniform convergence of series of functions. [4]
  7. 3(b) Show that the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n-1}\cos nx}{k^2-n^2}$ (where $k\in\mathbb R\setminus\mathbb Z$) is uniformly convergent on any closed and bounded interval. [3]
  8. 3(c) Check if the series $1+x+x^2+x^3+\cdots$ is uniformly convergent on $[0,1)$. [3]
  9. 4(a) Let a series $\sum f_n$ of continuous functions be uniformly convergent on $D\subseteq\mathbb R$. Prove that its sum function is continuous on $D$. [4]
  10. 4(b) Show that the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n(x^n-x^{n+1})$ converges uniformly on $[0,1]$ but $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty(x^n-x^{n+1})$ does not converge uniformly on $[0,1]$. [3+3]
  11. 5(a) If a power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ diverges for $x=c$, show that it diverges for all $x\in\mathbb R$ with $|x|>|c|$. [4]
  12. 5(b) State Cauchy-Hadamard Theorem for power series. If $\sum a_n$ is an absolutely convergent series of real numbers, then show that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ converges absolutely on $[-1,1]$. [2+4]
  13. 6(a) Find the radius and interval of convergence of the power series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{n^5+1}{(n^7+1)3^n}x^n$. [4]
  14. 6(b) What do you mean by a real analytic function? Explain with example. [3]
  15. 6(c) Prove or disprove: All functions which are infinitely many times differentiable on an interval are real analytic functions. [3]
  16. 7(a) Prove that if $\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}f(x,y)$ exists finitely then $f(x,y)$ is necessarily bounded in some deleted neighbourhood of the point $(a,b)$. [3]
  17. 7(b) Using $\epsilon-\delta$ definition, show that $\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{x^2y^4}{x^2+y^2}=0$. [3]
  18. 7(c) Prove that if the double limit $\lim_{(x,y)\to(a,b)}f(x,y)=l$ and one repeated limit $\lim_{x\to a}\lim_{y\to b}f(x,y)=l'$ exists then $l=l'$. [4]
  19. 8(a) Show that the function $f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\frac{xy}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}&\text{for }(x,y)\ne(0,0)\\0&\text{for }(x,y)=(0,0)\end{cases}$ is continuous at $(0,0)$. [3]
  20. 8(b) Prove that if $f(x,y)$ is continuous at a point $(a,b)$ and $f(a,b)>0$, then $f(x,y)>0$ in some neighbourhood of $(a,b)$. [2]
  21. 8(c) When a function $f(x,y)$ is said to be differentiable at a point $(a,b)$? If $f(x,y)$ is differentiable at a point $(a,b)$, then show that the partial derivatives $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$ and $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}$ both exist finitely at $(a,b)$. [1+4]
  22. 9(a) State and prove a sufficient condition for differentiability of a function of two variables at a point $(a,b)$. [4]
  23. 9(b) State and prove Young's theorem for equality of two mixed second order partial derivatives of a function $f(x,y)$ at a point $(a,b)$. [6]
  24. 10(a) Let $f(x_1,x_2)$ be differentiable in a region $R$ and $x_1\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}+x_2\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}=nf(x_1,x_2)$ for $(x_1,x_2)$ in $R$. Then show that $f(x_1,x_2)$ is a homogeneous function of degree $n$ in $R$. [4]
  25. 10(b) State and prove Mean Value Theorem for a function of two variables. [4]
  26. 10(c) Expand $f(x,y)=xy^2+3x-2$ in powers of $(x+1)$ and $(y-2)$. [2]
  27. 11(a) State and prove Implicit function theorem for a function of two variables. [5]
  28. 11(b) Prove that a necessary condition for a function $f(x,y)$ to have an extreme value at $(a,b)$ is that $\frac{\partial f(a,b)}{\partial x}=0$, $\frac{\partial f(a,b)}{\partial y}=0$, provided these partial derivatives exist. [3]
  29. 11(c) Show that $f(x,y)=x^4+x^2y+y^2$ has a minimum at $(0,0)$. [2]
  30. 12(a) Use Lagrange method to find the shortest distance from the point $(2,0)$ to the parabola $y^2=8x$. [4]
  31. 12(b) Evaluate $\iint_R xy^2(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy$ over $R:\{(x,y):0\le x\le a;0\le y\le b\}$. [3]
  32. 12(c) Evaluate $\iint_E x^2y^2\,dx\,dy$ over the region $E$ bounded by $x\ge0,y\ge0$ and $x^2+y^2\le1$. [3]
