CC-9

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1 What do you mean by a scalar, vector and tensor fields. Define covariant, contravariant and mixed tensors, their order and rank. A field $A(p,q,r,s)(\tilde x)$ depending on the coordinates $\tilde x(=(x^1,x^2,x^3,\ldots,x^n))$ is such that their relations in two coordinate systems $\tilde x$ and $\tilde{\bar x}(=(\bar x^1,\bar x^2,\ldots,\bar x^n))$ are \[ \bar A(p,q,r,s)(\tilde{\bar x})= \frac{\partial\bar x^s}{\partial x^l} \frac{\partial x^k}{\partial\bar x^r} \frac{\partial x^i}{\partial\bar x^p} \frac{\partial x^j}{\partial\bar x^q} A(i,j,k,l)(\tilde x), \quad p,q,r,s=1,2,\ldots,n. \] Determine whether the field is tensor. Determine its order and rank, if year. If $\vec r$ be the position vector of a point, determine whether (a) $\frac{\partial\vec r}{\partial x^p}(\equiv\vec\alpha(p))$, (b) $dx^p(\equiv\vec\beta(p))$ are tensors. State their order and rank, if yes.
  2. 2 Find the covariant and contravariant components of a tensor in (a) spherical polar if its contravariant components in rectangular Cartesian coordinates $(x,y,z)$ are $2xy$, $x+y$, $yz$.\par Define symmetric and skew-symmetric tensors. Given $A^{ijk}$ and $B_{lmn}$ are two skew-symmetric tensors. Verify the nature of $C^{ij}_{lm}=A^{ijk}B_{lmk}$.
  3. 3 What do you mean by line element of a curve in space? Show that square of the line element can be expressed as a quadratic form. What is the name of the coefficients involved in the quadratic form? Do they behave like a tensor? Give the order and rank, if yes. Provide geometric interpretation of the coefficients. Find the coefficients in the square of the line element described in the cylindrical polar coordinates.
  4. 4 Give the parametric representation of a surface and a curve on a surface. Derive the second derivative of position vector of a curve on a surface with respect to its parameter. Is the second derivative of position vector at a point on the surface lie in the tangent plane at that point on the surface? Derive the relation between Christoffel symbol of first, second kind and the metric tensor $g_{\mu\nu}$ of the curved space.
  5. 5 Define parallel displacement of a vector on a curved space. If $K_\nu$ is the parallel displacement of $A_\nu$ at $P(\tilde x)$ to a neighbouring point $Q(\tilde x+d\tilde x)$. Prove that $dA_\nu=K_\nu-A_\nu=A^\mu y^n_\mu y_{n,\nu,\sigma}dx^\sigma=A^\mu\Gamma_{\mu\nu\sigma}dx^\sigma$. Where $\Gamma_{\mu\nu\sigma}$ is Christoffel symbol of first kind.
  6. 6 Define curvature tensor. Establish the relation among curvature tensor and the Christoffel symbol of the second kind. Hence or otherwise find the curvature tensor at any point on a spherical surface of constant radius $a$.
