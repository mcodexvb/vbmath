MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_C\bar z\,dz$, where $C$ is the curve $z(t)=t^2+it$ joining the points $z=0$ and $z=4+2i$. [2]
  2. 1(b) State Cauchy's Fundamental theorem.\par Show by an example that the conditions assumed in Cauchy's Fundamental theorem are sufficient but not necessary. [1+2]
  3. 1(c) State and prove Cauchy's integral formula. [3]
  4. 1(d) Evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_C\frac{z^2dz}{(2z-1)(z+2)}$, where $C$ is the circle $|z-1|=1$. [2]
  5. 2(a) The function $f(z)$ is regular in $|z|<R_1$. If $|a|<R<R_1$, then prove that\par \[f(a)=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{\left(R^2-a\bar a\right)}{(z-a)\left(R^2-z\bar a\right)}f(z)\,dz,\text{ where $C$ is the circle }|z|=R.\] [2]
  6. 2(b) State and prove Liouville's theorem. [1+2]
  7. 2(c) State and prove fundamental theorem of algebra. [1+2]
  8. 2(d) Show that $f(z)=z^9+z^4+1$ vanishes at least for one point in $|z|<2$. [2]
  9. 3(a) State Talylor's theorem.\par Expand $f(z)=\dfrac1{z-4}$ in its power series about $z=3$. [1+2]
  10. 3(b) State and prove Laurent's theorem. [1+4]
  11. 3(c) Prove that $e^{z+\frac1z}=\displaystyle\sum_{-\infty}^{\infty}a_nz^n$ where $\pi a_n=\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}e^{2\cos\theta}\cos n\theta\,d\theta$. [2]
  12. 4(a) Prove that a function $f$ has a pole at $\alpha$ of order $m$ if and only if $f$ can be expressed in the form $f(z)=\dfrac{\psi(z)}{(z-\alpha)^m}$, where $\psi(z)$ is analytic at $\alpha$ and $\psi(\alpha)\ne0$. [3]
  13. 4(b) Show that the sum of the residues of $\dfrac{e^{-z}}{z^2-a^2}$ at its pole is $-\dfrac1a\sinh a$. [2]
  14. 4(c) If $f(z)$ has a pole at $\alpha$ of order $m$, then show that $|f(z)|\to\infty$ as $z\to\alpha$ in any manner. [3]
  15. 4(d) Prove that the zeros of a non-constant analytic function are isolated points. \par\hfill P.T.O. [2]
  16. 5(a) If $f(z)=\dfrac{P(z)}{Q(z)}$, where $P(z)$ and $Q(z)$ are analytic at $\alpha$ and $P(\alpha)\ne0$, while $\alpha$ is a simple zero of $Q(z)$, then show that $\alpha$ is a simple pole of $f(z)$ with residue $\dfrac{P(\alpha)}{Q'(\alpha)}$. [2]
  17. 5(b) Find the singularities of the function $f(z)=\tan\dfrac1z$ and determine the nature of it. [3]
  18. 5(c) State and prove Cauchy's residue theorem. [1+2]
  19. 5(d) Show that all the roots of the equation $z^5+az+1=0$ lie within the circle $|z|=r$ if $|a|<r^4-\dfrac1r$. [2]
  20. 6(a) Define residue of a function at the point at the infinity. Find $\mathrm{Res}[f(z),\infty]$, where,\par \[f(z)=\frac z{z^2-1}.\] [1+2]
  21. 6(b) A function which is analytic everywhere including the point at infinity is a constant -- prove it. [2]
  22. 6(c) Evaluate \textit{any one} of the following by the method of contour integration: \par \hspace*{1em}i) $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{a^2+x^2},\ a<0;$\par \hspace*{1em}ii) $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x\sin x}{a^2x^2+b^2}\,dx,\ a,b>0.$ [5]
