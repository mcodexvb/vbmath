BME-51

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2017
  1. 1(a) What do you mean by `significant digit'? If the first significant figure of an approximate number is `$k$', and the number is correct to $n$ significant figures, then show that the relative error is less than $10^{1-n}/k$. [1+3]
  2. 1(b) Using general formula for estimation of errors, show that the relative error of the product of $n$ approximate numbers is less than or equal to the sum of the relative errors of all the approximate numbers. [2]
  3. 1(c) Use the method of separation of symbols to prove $u_0+\frac{u_1x}{1!}+\frac{u_2x^2}{2!}+\frac{u_3x^3}{3!}+\dots=e^x\left[u_0+x\Delta u_0+\frac{x^2}{2!}\Delta^2u_0+\dots\right]$ [4]
  4. 2(a) Derive Newton's forward and backward interpolation formulae without error terms. [3+3]
  5. 2(b) Compute the first and second derivatives of the function tabulated below at the points $x=0.96$ and $1.04$. \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{c|ccccc}$x$&0.96&0.98&1.00&1.02&1.04\\\hline$f(x)$&0.7825&0.7739&0.7651&0.7563&0.7473\end{tabular}\end{center} [2+2]
  6. 3(a) Derive composite Simpson's one-third rule of numerical integration. Also find the error committed in this method. [3+3]
  7. 3(b) Compute $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sqrt{\cos x}dx$ by Weddle's rule of numerical integration, taking 12 equal intervals correct to 4-significant figures. [4]
  8. 4(a) Describe Graeffe's root squaring method for finding root of fourth degree polynomial equation whose all roots are real and two numerically equal (smallest root is repeated). Discuss about the detection of the final stage of root squaring for the above case. [4+2]
  9. 4(b) Compute the roots of the equation $x^3-3x^2-x+3=0$ by Graeffe's root squaring method. [4]
  10. 5(a) Describe the regula falsi method for computing a simple root of an equation $f(x)=0$. Also find the condition of convergence of this method, if there be any. [3+3]
  11. 5(b) Find one positive root of the equation $x^3-3x+1.06=0$, by the method of bisection, correct to three decimal places. [4]
  12. 6(a) Explain the Gauss-Seided iterative method of solving a system of $n$ linear equations having $n$ unknowns. Find sufficient condition for convergence of the process. [2+3]
  13. 6(b) Use Gauss elimination method to solve the following system of linear equations: $6.1x_1+2.2x_2+1.2x_3=16.55$, $2.2x_1+5.5x_2-1.5x_3=10.55$, $1.2x_1-1.5x_2+7.2x_3=16.80$ correct to five significant figures. [5]
