IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2015

  1. Suppose $N$ is a normal subgroup of a group $G$. Which one of the following is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $G$ is an infinite group then $G / \mathrm{N}$ is an infinite group \item If $G$ is a nonabelian group then $G / N$ is a nonabelian group \item If $G$ is a cyclic group then $G / N$ is an abelian group \item If $G$ is an abelian group then $G / N$ is a cyclic group \end{enumerate}
  2. Let $y(x)=u(x) \sin x+v(x) \cos x$ be a solution of the differential equation $y^{\prime \prime}+y=\sec x$. Then $u(x)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\ln |\cos x|+C$ \item $x+C$ \item $-x+C$ \item $\ln |\sec x|+C$ \end{enumerate}
  3. Let $a, b, c, d$ be distinct non-zero real numbers with $a+b=c+d$. Then an eigenvalue of the matrix $\left[\begin{array}{ccc}a & b & 1 \\ c & d & 1 \\ 1 & -1 & 0\end{array}\right]$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $a+c$ \item $a+b$ \item $a-b$ \item $b-d$ \end{enumerate}
  4. Let $S$ be a nonempty subset of $\mathbb{R}$. If $S$ is a finite union of disjoint bounded intervals, then which one of the following is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $S$ is not compact, then sup $S \notin S$ and inf $S \notin S$ \item Even if $\sup S \in S$ and inf $S \in S, S$ need not be compact \item If $\sup S \in S$ and inf $S \in S$, then $S$ is compact \item Even if $S$ is compact, it is not necessary that sup $S \in S$ and inf $S \in S$ \end{enumerate}
  5. Let $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ be a convergent sequence of real numbers. If $x_{1}>\pi+\sqrt{2}$ and $x_{n+1}=\pi+\sqrt{x_{n}-\pi}$ for $n \geq 1$, then which one of the following is the limit of this sequence? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\pi+1$ \item $\pi+\sqrt{2}$ \item $\pi$ \item $\pi+\sqrt{\pi}$ \end{enumerate}
  6. The volume of the portion of the solid cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2} \leq 2$ bounded above by the surface $z=x^{2}+y^{2}$ and bounded below by the $x y$-plane is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\pi$ \item $2 \pi$ \item $3 \pi$ \item $4 \pi$ \end{enumerate}
  7. Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function with $f(0)=0$. If for all $x \in \mathbb{R}, 1<f^{\prime}(x)<2$, then which one of the following statements is true on $(0, \infty)$ ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ is unbounded \item $f$ has at least one zero \item $f$ is increasing and bounded \item $f$ is periodic \end{enumerate}
  8. If an integral curve of the differential equation $(y-x) \frac{d y}{d x}=1$ passes through $(0,0)$ and $(\alpha, 1)$, then $\alpha$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $2-e^{-1}$ \item $1-e^{-1}$ \item $e^{-1}$ \item $1+e$ \end{enumerate}
  9. An integrating factor of the differential equation $$ \frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{2 x y^{2}+y}{x-2 y^{3}} $$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{y}$ \item $\frac{1}{y^{2}}$ \item $y$ \item $y^{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  10. Let $A$ be a nonempty subset of $\mathbb{R}$. Let $I(A)$ denote the set of interior points of $A$. Then $I(A)$ can be \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item empty \item singleton \item a finite set containing more than one element \item countable but not finite \end{enumerate}
  11. Let $S_{3}$ be the group of permutations of three distinct symbols. The direct sum $S_{3} \oplus S_{3}$ has an \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 4 \item 6 \item 9 \item 18 \end{enumerate}
  12. The orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves $y=C_{1} x^{3}$ are \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $2 x^{2}+3 y^{2}=C_{2}$ \item $3 x^{2}+2 y^{2}=C_{2}$ \item $3 x^{2}+y^{2}=C_{2}$ \item $x^{2}+3 y^{2}=c_{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  13. Let $G$ be a nonabelian group. Let $\alpha \in G$ have order 4 and let $\beta \in G$ have order 3 . Then the order of the element $\alpha \beta$ in $G$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item is 6 \item is of the form $12 k$ for $k \geq 2$ \item is 12 \item need not be finite \end{enumerate}
  14. Let $S$ be the bounded surface of the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$ cut by the planes $z=0$ and $z=1+x$. Then the value of the surface integral $\iint_{S} 3 z^{2} d \sigma$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\int_{0}^{2 \pi}(1+\cos \theta)^{3} d \theta$ \item $\int_{0}^{2 \pi} \sin \theta \cos \theta(1+\cos \theta)^{2} d \theta$ \item $\int_{0}^{2 \pi}(1+2 \cos \theta)^{3} d \theta$ \item $\int_{0}^{2 \pi} \sin \theta \cos \theta(1+2 \cos \theta)^{2} d \theta$ \end{enumerate}
  15. Suppose that the dependent variables $z$ and $w$ are functions of the independent variables $x$ and $y$, defined by the equations $f(x, y, z, w)=0$ and $g(x, y, z, w)=0$, where $f_{z} g_{w}-f_{w} g_{z}=1$. Which one of the following is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $z_{x}=f_{w} g_{x}-f_{x} g_{w}$ \item $z_{x}=f_{x} g_{w}-f_{w} g_{x}$ \item $z_{x}=f_{z} g_{x}-f_{x} g_{z}$ \item $z_{x}=f_{z} g_{w}-f_{z} g_{x}$ \end{enumerate}
  16. Let $A=\left[\begin{array}{cc}0 & 1-i \\ -1-i & i\end{array}\right]$ and $B=A^{T} \bar{A}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item an eigenvalue of $B$ is purely imaginary \item an eigenvalue of $A$ is zero \item all eigenvalues of $B$ are real \item $A$ has a non-zero real eigenvalue \end{enumerate}
  17. The limit is equal to $$ \lim _{x \rightarrow 0+} \frac{1}{\sin ^{2} x} \int_{\frac{x}{2}}^{x} \sin ^{-1} t d t $$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item $\frac{1}{8}$ \item $\frac{1}{4}$ \item $\frac{3}{8}$ \end{enumerate}
  18. Let $P_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ be the vector space of polynomials in $x$ of degree at most 2 with real coefficients. Let $M_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ be the vector space of $2 \times 2$ real matrices. If a linear transformation $T: P_{2}(\mathbb{R}) \rightarrow M_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ is defined as then $$ T(f)=\left[\begin{array}{cc} f(0)-f(2) & 0 \\ 0 & f(1) \end{array}\right] $$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $T$ is one-one but not onto \item $T$ is onto but not one-one \item Range $(T)=\operatorname{span}\left\{\left[\begin{array}{ll}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{array}\right],\left[\begin{array}{cc}-2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{array}\right]\right\}$ \item Null $(T)=\operatorname{span}\left\{x^{2}-2 x, 1-x\right\}$ \end{enumerate}
  19. Let $B_{1}=\{(1,2),(2,-1)\}$ and $B_{2}=\{(1,0),(0,1)\}$ be ordered bases of $\mathbb{R}^{2}$. If $T: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2}$ is a linear transformation such that $[T]_{B_{1}, B_{2}}$, the matrix of $T$ with respect to $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, is $\left[\begin{array}{cc}4 & 3 \\ 2 & -4\end{array}\right]$, then $T(5,5)$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(-9,8)$ \item $(9,8)$ \item $(-15,-2)$ \item $(15,2)$ \end{enumerate}
  20. Let $S=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\left[0, \frac{1}{2 n+1}\right] \cup\left[\frac{1}{2 n}, 1\right]\right)$. Which one of the following statements is FALSE? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exist sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ in $[0,1]$ such that $S=[0,1] \backslash \cup_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(a_{n}, b_{n}\right)$ \item $[0,1] \backslash S$ is an open set \item If $A$ is an infinite subset of $S$, then $A$ has a limit point \item There exists an infinite subset of $S$ having no limit points \end{enumerate}
  21. Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a strictly increasing continuous function. If $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is a sequence in $[0,1]$, then the sequence $\left\{f\left(a_{n}\right)\right\}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item increasing \item bounded \item convergent \item not necessarily bounded \end{enumerate}
  22. Which one of the following statements is true for the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n} \frac{(2 n) !}{n^{2 n}}$ ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The series converges conditionally but not absolutely \item The series converges absolutely \item The sequence of partial sums of the series is bounded but not convergent \item The sequence of partial sums of the series is unbounded \end{enumerate}
  23. The sequence $\left\{\cos \left(\frac{1}{2} \tan ^{-1}\left(-\frac{n}{2}\right)^{n}\right)\right\}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item monotone and convergent \item monotone but not convergent \item convergent but not monotone \item neither monotone nor convergent \end{enumerate}
  24. If $y(t)$ is a solution of the differential equation $y^{\prime \prime}+4 y=2 e^{t}$, then $$ \lim _{t \rightarrow \infty} e^{-t} y(t) $$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{2}{3}$ \item $\frac{2}{5}$ \item $\frac{2}{7}$ \item $\frac{2}{9}$ \end{enumerate}
  25. For what real values of $x$ and $y$, does the integral $\int_{x}^{y}\left(6-t-t^{2}\right) d t$ attain its maximum? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $x=-3, y=2$ \item $x=-2, y=2$ \item $x=2, y=3$ \item $x=-3, y=4$ \end{enumerate}
  26. The area of the planar region bounded by the curves $x=6 y^{2}-2$ and $x=2 y^{2}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ \item $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}$ \item $\frac{4 \sqrt{2}}{3}$ \item $\sqrt{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  27. For $n \geq 2$, let $f_{n}: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be given by $f_{n}(x)=x^{n} \sin x$. Then at $x=0, f_{n}$ has a \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item local maximum if $n$ is even \item local maximum if $n$ is odd \item local minimum if $n$ is even \item local minimum if $n$ is odd \end{enumerate}
  28. For $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, define $f_{m, n}(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{c}x^{m} \sin \left(\frac{1}{x^{n}}\right. \\ 0\end{array} \quad x=0\right.$ Then at $x=0, f_{m, n}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item differentiable for each pair $m, n$ with $m>n$ \item differentiable for each pair $m, n$ with $m<n$ \item not differentiable for each pair $m, n$ with $m>n$ \item not differentiable for each pair $m, n$ with $m<n$ \end{enumerate}
  29. Let $G$ and $H$ be nonempty subsets of $\mathbb{R}$, where $G$ is connected and $G \cup H$ is not connected. Which one of the following statements is true for all such $G$ and $H$ ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $G \cap H=\emptyset$, then $H$ is connected \item If $G \cap H=\emptyset$, then $H$ is not connected \item If $G \cap H \neq \emptyset$, then $H$ is connected \item If $G \cap H \neq \emptyset$, then $H$ is not connected \end{enumerate}
  30.   32. Let $\vec{F}$ be a vector field given by $\vec{F}(x, y, z)=-y \hat{\imath}+2 x y \hat{\jmath}+z^{3} \hat{k}$, for $(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3}$.   33. Let $V$ be the set of $2 \times 2$ matrices $\left[\begin{array}{ll}a_{11} & a_{12} \\ a_{21} & a_{22}\end{array}\right]$ with complex entries such that $a_{11}+a_{22}=0$.
