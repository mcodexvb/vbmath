CC-3

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2020
  1. 1 Answer any five questions
  2. 1(a) Find closure of the sets \(\mathbb N,\mathbb R,\mathbb Q,\mathbb Z\).
  3. 1(b) Find the limit points of the sets \((0,1)\cup(1,2)\cup(2,3)\cdots\).
  4. 1(c) Define \(\limsup\) and \(\liminf\) of the sequence \(\{x_n\}\).
  5. 1(d) Give example with justification of two sets which are both open and closed.
  6. 1(e) Show that arbitrary intersection of open sets may not be an open set.
  7. 1(f) Find asymptote of the curve \(x^3+y^3-3axy=0\).
  8. 1(g) Find area and total length of a circle of radius \(a\).
  9. 2 State and prove Taylor's theorem with Cauchy's form of remainder. [5]
  10. 3 Find total length of perimeter of the cardioid \(r=a(1+\cos\theta)\). [5]
  11. 4 Expand \((1+x)^{-1}\) in power series with proper justification [5]
  12. 5 State and prove Heine Borel theorem. [5]
  13. 6 Show that closure of the set \(A\), \(\overline A=A\cup A'\) where \(A'\) is derived set of \(A\). [5]
  14. 7 A circle moves with its centre on the parabola \(y^2=4ax\) and always passes through the vertex of the parabola. Show that the envelope of the circle is the curve \(x^3+y^2(x+2a)=0\) [5]
  15. 8(a) If \(a\) is an odd integer, show that \(24\mid a^2(a^2-1)\). [3]
  16. 8(b) Find integers \(u\) and \(v\) satisfying \(52u-91v=78\). [4]
  17. 8(c) Find the primes of the form \(n^2-4\), \(n\) being an integer. [3]
  18. 9(a) Find the remainder when \(1!+2!+\cdots+50!\) is divided by 15. [3]
  19. 9(b) Find the least positive integer that leaves remainders 2, 3 and 2 when divided by 3, 4 and 5 respectively. [5]
  20. 9(c) Find the units digit in \(7^{99}\). [2]
  21. 10(a) Show that the set \(\{5,15,25,35\}\) is a group under multiplication modulo 40. Can you see any relationship between this group and \(U_8\)?
  22. 10(b) For any elements \(a\) and \(b\) from a group and any integer \(n\), prove that \((a^{-1}ba)^n=a^{-1}b^na\). [4]
  23. 11(a) In a finite group \(G\) of even order show that there exists an odd number of elements of order 2. [3]
  24. 11(b) Give an example of a group \((G,\cdot)\) in which \(O(a)O(b)\ne O(a\cdot b)\). [2]
  25. 11(c) Let \(H\) and \(K\) be two subgroups of a group \(G\). Then prove that \(HK\) is a subgroup of \(G\) if and only if \(HK=KH\). [5]
  26. 12(a) If an Abelian group \(G\) of order 10 contains an element of order 5, prove that \(G\) must be cyclic. [3]
  27. 12(b) Let \(G\) be a cyclic group of order 30 generated by \(a\). Find a subgroup \(H\) of order 6 and generators of \(H\).
  28. 12(c) Determine all finite subgroups of \(R^*\). [2]
