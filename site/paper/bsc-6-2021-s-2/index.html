CCMA-14

Algebra IV
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2021
  1. 1(a) Let $f:G\longrightarrow G'$ be a homomorphism. Show that for every $a\in G$, $f^{-1}(f(a))=a\mathit{Ker}\,f$. [2]
  2. 1(b) If $|\mathit{Ker}\,f|=n$, then show that $f$ is an $n$-to-1 map. [2]
  3. 1(c) Consider the group homomorphism $f:\mathbb R\longrightarrow S^1$ defined by $f(x)=e^{i\pi x}$. Find the kernel of $f$. [1]
  4. 2(a) Consider the subgroup $G=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}1&n\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\mid n\in\mathbb Z\right\}$ of the group $GL_2(\mathbb R)$. Show that $G\simeq\mathbb Z$. [2]
  5. 2(b) Show that every finite cyclic group of order $n$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z_n$. [3]
  6. 3(a) State and prove the first isomorphism theorem for groups. [3]
  7. 3(b) Define a suitable homomorphism $f:\mathbb R\longrightarrow\{-1,1\}$ so that $\mathbb R/\mathit{Ker}\,f\simeq\{-1,1\}$. [2]
  8. 4(a) Find all homomorphisms from $\mathbb Z_8$ into $\mathbb Z_6$. [2]
  9. 4(b) Show that there are only two groups of order 4 up to isomorphism. [3]
  10. 5(a) Show that $Inn(G)$ is a normal subgroup of $Aut(G)$. Also show that $G/Z(G)\simeq Inn(G)$. [2+2]
  11. 5(b) Show that $|Aut(\mathbb Z)|=2$. [1]
  12. 6(a) Find $Aut(S_3)$. [2]
  13. 6(b) Prove that $Aut(\mathbb Z_n)\simeq U_n$. [3]
  14. 1(a) State and prove the second isomorphism theorem for rings. [3]
  15. 1(b) Let $R$ be a ring with 1. If the characteristic of $R$ is 0, then show that $R$ has a subring isomorphic to $\mathbb Z$. [2]
  16. 2(a) Find all ring homomorphisms $f:\mathbb Z_n\longrightarrow\mathbb Z_n$. [3]
  17. 2(b) Show that the quotient field of $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]$ is $\mathbb Q[\sqrt2]$. [2]
  18. 3(a) If $D$ is an integral domain and $f(x)$ is a nonzero polynomial in $D[x]$ of degree $n$, then show that $f(x)$ has at most $n$ roots in $D$ counted according to multiplicity. Show that the result fails if $D$ is not an integral domain. [3]
  19. 3(b) Find the number of elements in the quotient ring $\mathbb Z_5[x]/{<x^3+x+1>}$. [2]
  20. 4(a) If $F$ is a field, then show that every ideal in $F[x]$ is a principal ideal. [3]
  21. 4(b) Find all ideals of the quotient ring $\mathbb R[x]/{<(x-1)(x-2)>}$. [2]
  22. 5(a) Show that gcd of any two nonzero polynomials over a field exists. [3]
  23. 5(b) Suppose that over a field $F$, $f(x)\mid g(x)h(x)$ and $\gcd(f(x),g(x))=1$. Does $f(x)\mid h(x)$? Justify. [2]
  24. 6(a) Define irreducible polynomial over field. State and prove a necessary and sufficient condition for a polynomial over a field to be irreducible. [3]
  25. 6(b) Find the total number of monic irreducible cubic polynomials over the field $\mathbb Z_5$. [2]
  26. 1(a) A linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3$ is defined by $T(x,y,z)=(x-y+z,x+2y-z,3x+z)$. Find the rank and nullity of $T$. [2]
  27. 1(b) Is there any linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3$ such that $\ker T=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid x=y=z\}$ and $Im\,T=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid x+y+z=0\}$? If so, find one such linear transformation. [3]
  28. 2(a) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a linear transformation. Then show that $ST=TS$ for every $S\in\mathcal L(V)$ if and only if $T=cI_V$ for some scalar $c$. [3]
  29. 2(b) A linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^2\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2$ is given by $T(x,y)=(x,x+y)$. Find the image of the line $x+y=1$ under $T$. [2]
  30. 3(a) Let $T:U\longrightarrow V$ be a linear transformation. For every subspace $W$ of $V$, show that $T(T^{-1}(W))=Im\,T\cap W$. [3]
  31. 3(b) A linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^2\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2$ is given by $T(x,y)=(ax+by,cx+dy)$. If $T$ is an isomorphism then show that $ad-bc\ne0$. [2]
  32. 4(a) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ is a linear transformation and $\beta$ an $\gamma$ be two bases of $V$. Show that $[T]_\beta$ and $[T]_\gamma$ are similar. [2]
  33. 4(b) Let $U,V$ and $W$ be three finite dimensional vector spaces and $T:U\longrightarrow V$ and $S:V\longrightarrow W$ be two linear transformations. Then show that $\mathit{rank}(ST)=\mathit{rank}(T)-\dim(Im\,T\cap\ker S)$. [3]
  34. 5(a) Let $f,g\in V^*$ be two linear functionals such that $f(v)=0$ implies that $g(v)=0$ for every $v\in V$. Prove that $g=cf$ for some $c\in F$. [2]
  35. 5(b) Define transpose $T^t$ of a linear transformation $T:U\longrightarrow V$. Show that $\ker T^t=(Im\,T)^o$. [3]
  36. 6(a) Consider the linear transformation $T(a+bt+ct^2)=-(a+2b+c)+(2a+3b)t-2(a+b)t^2$. Show that 1 is an eigen value of $T$. Find $E_1$ and a basis of $E_1$. Is $T$ diagonalizable? [3]
  37. 6(b) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a linear operator, $\beta$ be a basis of $V$ and $\lambda$ be an eigen value of $T$. Then show that $v\in V$ is an eigen vector of $T$ corresponding to $\lambda$ if and only if $[v]_\beta$ is an eigen vector of $[T]_\beta$ corresponding to $\lambda$. [2]
