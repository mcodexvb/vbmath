NBHM · Research Scholarships Screening Test 2008

  1. 1.1 Let $S_7$ denote the group of permutations of $7$ symbols. Find the order of the permutation: \[ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\ 6 & 4 & 5 & 7 & 3 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}. \]
  2. 1.2 Write down the number of mutually nonisomorphic abelian groups of order $19^5$.
  3. 1.3 For two ideals $\mathcal{I}$ and $\mathcal{J}$ in a commutative ring $\mathcal{R}$, define $\mathcal{I} : \mathcal{J} = \{a \in \mathcal{R} : a\mathcal{J} \subset \mathcal{I}\}$. In the ring $\Z$ of all integers, if $\mathcal{I} = 12\Z$ and $\mathcal{J} = 8\Z$, find $\mathcal{I} : \mathcal{J}$.
  4. 1.4 Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a prime ideal in a commutative ring $\mathcal{R}$ and let $S = \mathcal{R} \setminus \mathcal{P}$, \emph{i.e.}\ the complement of $\mathcal{P}$ in $\mathcal{R}$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $S$ is closed under addition. \item $S$ is closed under multiplication. \item $S$ is closed under addition and multiplication. \end{enumerate}
  5. 1.5 Let $p$ be a prime and consider the field $\Z_p$. List the primes for which the following system of linear equations DOES NOT have a solution in $\Z_p$: \begin{align*} 5x + 3y &= 4 \\ 3x + 6y &= 1. \end{align*}
  6. 1.6 Let $A$ be a $227 \times 227$ matrix with entries in $\Z_{227}$, such that all its eigenvalues are distinct. Write down its trace.
  7. 1.7 Let $B$ be a nilpotent $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries. Set $A = B - I$. Write down the determinant of $A$.
  8. 1.8 Let $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ be two non-zero $n \times 1$ vectors. If $\mathbf{y}^T$ denotes the transpose of $\mathbf{y}$, what are the eigenvalues of the $n \times n$ matrix $\mathbf{x}\mathbf{y}^T$?
  9. 1.9 Let $A$ be a real symmetric $n \times n$ matrix whose only eigenvalues are $0$ and $1$. Let the dimension of the null space of $A - I$ be $m$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The characteristic polynomial of $A$ is $(\lambda - 1)^m \lambda^{m-n}$. \item $A^k = A^{k+1}$ for all positive integers $k$. \item The rank of $A$ is $m$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 1.10 What is the dimension of the space of all $n \times n$ matrices with real entries which are such that the sum of the entries in the first row and the sum of the diagonal entries are both zero?
  11. 2.1 Let $f(x) = \frac{1}{1+x^2}$. Consider its Taylor expansion about a point $a \in \R$, given by $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n(x - a)^n$. What is the radius of convergence of this series?
  12. 2.2 Consider the series \[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^2 + n}{n^2}. \] Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The series converges for all real values of $x$. \item The series converges uniformly on $\R$. \item The series does not converge absolutely for any real value of $x$. \end{enumerate}
  13. 2.3 Let $f : \R \to \R$ be a continuous function. Which of the following imply that it is uniformly continuous? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f$ is $2\pi$-periodic. \item $f$ is differentiable and its derivative is bounded on $\R$. \item $f$ is absolutely continuous. \end{enumerate}
  14. 2.4 Let $f : [-1, 1] \to \R$ be continuous. Assume that $\int_{-1}^{1} f(t)\,dt = 1$. Evaluate: \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \int_{-1}^{1} f(t)\cos^2 nt\,dt. \]
  15. 2.5 Let $f$ be a continuously differentiable $2\pi$-periodic real valued function on the real line. Let $a_n = \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(t)\cos nt\,dt$ where $n$ is a non-negative integer. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The derivative of $f$ is also a $2\pi$-periodic function. \item $|a_n| \le C\frac{1}{n}$ for all $n$, where $C > 0$ is a constant independent of $n$. \item $a_n \to 0$, as $n \to \infty$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Let $f_n$ and $f$ be continuous functions on an interval $[a, b]$ and assume that $f_n \to f$ uniformly on $[a, b]$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item If $f_n$ are all Riemann integrable, then $f$ is Riemann integrable. \item If $f_n$ are all continuously differentiable, then $f$ is continuously differentiable. \item If $x_n \to x$ in $[a, b]$, then $f_n(x_n) \to f(x)$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 2.7 Let $f : \R \to \R$ be a continuously differentiable function such that $f'(0) = 0$. Define, for $x$ and $y \in \R$, \[ g(x, y) = f\left(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}\right). \] Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $g$ is a differentiable function on $\R^2$. \item $g$ is a differentiable function on $\R^2$ if, and only if, $f(0) = 0$. \item $g$ is differentiable only on $\R^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 2.8 Find the square roots of the complex number $1 + 2i$.
  19. 2.9 Evaluate: \[ \int_{|z|=1} \frac{4 + z}{(2 - z)z}\,dz \] the circle $\{|z| = 1\}$ being described in the anticlockwise direction.
  20. 2.10 Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item There exists an analytic function $f$ on $\C$ such that $f(2i) = 0$, $f(0) = 2i$ and $|f(z)| \le 2$ for all $z \in \C$. \item There exists an analytic function $f$ in the open unit disc $\{z \in \C : |z| < 1\}$ such that $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 1$ and $f\left(\frac{1}{2^n}\right) = 0$ for all integers $n \ge 2$. \item There exists an analytic function $f$ on $\C$ whose real part is given by $u(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$, where $z = x + iy$. \end{enumerate}
  21. 3.1 Which of the following define a metric on $\R$? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $d_1(x, y) = \dfrac{\big|\,|x| - |y|\,\big|}{1 + |x|\cdot|y|}$. \item $d_2(x, y) = \sqrt{|x - y|}$. \item $d_3(x, y) = |f(x) - f(y)|$, where $f : \R \to \R$ is a strictly monotonically increasing function. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $f : X \to Y$ be a continuous bijection. Under which of the following conditions will $f$ be a homeomorphism? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $X$ and $Y$ are complete metric spaces. \item $X$ and $Y$ are Banach spaces and $f$ is linear. \item $X$ is a compact topological space and $Y$ is Hausdorff. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Let $(X, d)$ be a compact metric space and let $\{f_\alpha : \alpha \in A\}$ be a uniformly bounded and equicontinuous family of functions on $X$. Define \[ f(x) = \sup_{\alpha \in A} f_\alpha(x). \] Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item For any $t \in \R$, the set $\{x \in X : f(x) < t\}$ is an open set in $X$. \item The function $f$ is continuous. \item Every sequence $\{f_{\alpha_n}\}$ contained in the above family admits a uniformly convergent subsequence. \end{enumerate}
  24. 3.4 Let $D = \{x \in \R^2 : |x| \le 1\}$ where $|x|$ is the usual euclidean norm of the vector $x$. Let $f : D \to X$ be a continuous function into a topological space $X$. Pick out the cases below when $f$ will NEVER be onto. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $X = [-1, 1]$. \item $X = [-1, 1] \setminus \{0\}$. \item $X = ]-1, 1[$. \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Let $\M_n(\R)$ denote the set of all $n \times n$ matrices with real entries, considered as the space $\R^{n^2}$. Which of the following subsets are compact? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The set of all invertible matrices. \item The set of all orthogonal matrices. \item The set of all matrices whose trace is zero. \end{enumerate}
  26. 3.6 With the notations as in the preceding question, which of the following sets are connected? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The set of all invertible matrices. \item The set of all orthogonal matrices. \item The set of all matrices whose trace is zero. \end{enumerate}
  27. 3.7 Let $f : \R \to \R$ be a continuous function. Define \[ G = \{(x, f(x)) : x \in \R\} \subset \R^2. \] Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $G$ is closed in $\R^2$. \item $G$ is open in $\R^2$. \item $G$ is connected in $\R^2$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Let $X$ be a topological space and let $A \subset X$. Let $\partial A$ denote the boundary of $A$, \emph{i.e.}\ the set of points in the closure of $A$ which are not in the interior of $A$. A closed set is \emph{nowhere dense} if its interior is the empty set. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item If $A$ is open, then $\partial A$ is nowhere dense. \item If $A$ is closed, then $\partial A$ is nowhere dense. \item If $A$ is any subset, then $\partial A$ is always nowhere dense. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 Let $V$ be a complete normed linear space and let $B$ be a basis for $V$ as a vector space. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $B$ can be a finite set. \item $B$ can be a countably infinite set. \item If $B$ is infinite, then it must be an uncountable set. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Let $V_1 = \mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ with the metric $d_1(f, g) = \max_{t \in [0, 1]} |f(t) - g(t)|$. Let $V_2 = \mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ with the metric $d_2(f, g) = \int_0^1 |f(t) - g(t)|\,dt$. Let $id$ denote the identity map on $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $id : V_1 \to V_2$ is continuous. \item $id : V_2 \to V_1$ is continuous. \item $id : V_1 \to V_2$ is a homeomorphism. \end{enumerate}
  31. 4.1 Write down the Laplace transform of the function $f(x) = \cos 2x$.
  32. 4.2 Let $B$ denote the unit ball in $\R^N$, $N \ge 2$. Let $\alpha_N$ be its ($N$-dimensional) volume and let $\beta_N$ be its ($(N-1)$-dimensional) surface measure. Apply Gauss' divergence theorem to the vector field $\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}$ and derive the relation connecting $\alpha_N$ and $\beta_N$.
  33. 4.3 Assume that the rate at which a body cools is proportional to the difference in temperature between the body and its surroundings. A body is heated to $110^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and is placed in air at $10^\circ\mathrm{C}$. After one hour, its temperature is $60^\circ\mathrm{C}$. At what time will its temperature reach $30^\circ\mathrm{C}$?
  34. 4.4 A body of mass $m$ falls under gravity and is retarded by a force proportional to its velocity. Write down the differential equation satisfied by the velocity $v(t)$ at time $t$.
  35. 4.5 Solve the above equation given that the velocity is zero at time $t = 0$.
  36. 4.6 Write down the critical points of the nonlinear system of differential equations: \begin{align*} \frac{dx}{dt} &= y(x^2 + 1) \\ \frac{dy}{dt} &= 2xy^2. \end{align*}
  37. 4.7 Classify the following differential operator as elliptic, hyperbolic or parabolic: \[ \mathcal{L}(u) = 2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} - 2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial y} + 2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}. \]
  38. 4.8 Let $B$ be the unit ball in the plane and let $u$ be a solution of the boundary value problem: \begin{align*} \Delta u &= C \quad \text{in } B \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial n} &= 1 \quad \text{on } \partial B \end{align*} where $\Delta$ denotes the Laplace operator, $\partial B$ denotes the boundary of $B$ and $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ denotes the outer normal derivative on the boundary. Evaluate $C$, given that it is a constant.
  39. 4.9 Write down the dual of the linear programming problem: \[ \text{Max.}: 2x + 3y \] such that \begin{align*} x + 2y &= 3 \\ 2x + y &\ge 4 \\ x + y &\le 5 \\ x \ge 0,&\quad y \ge 0. \end{align*}
  40. 4.10 Write down the Newton-Raphson iteration scheme to find the square root of $a > 0$, by solving the equation $x^2 = a$.
  41. 5.1 Differentiate with respect to $t$: \[ f(t) = \int_{-t^2}^{a} e^{-x^2}\,dx,\quad (a > 0). \]
  42. 5.2 Sum the series: \[ 1 + \frac{1 + 3}{2!} + \frac{1 + 3 + 3^2}{3!} + \cdots \]
  43. 5.3 Sum the series: \[ 1 + \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{4^2} + \frac{1}{7}\cdot\frac{1}{4^3} + \cdots \]
  44. 5.4 Let $A$ be the point $(0, 1)$ and $B$ the point $(2, 2)$ in the plane. Consider all paths made up of the two line segments $AC$ and $CB$ as the point $C$ varies on the $x$-axis. Find the coordinates of $C$ for which the corresponding path has the shortest length.
  45. 5.5 Find the area of the pentagon formed in the plane with the fifth roots of unity as its vertices.
  46. 5.6 Let $A, B, C, D$ and $E$ be five points marked, in clockwise order, on the unit circle in the plane (with centre at origin). Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be real numbers and set $f(P) = \alpha x + \beta y$ where $P$ is a point whose coordinates are $(x, y)$. Assume that $f(A) = 10$, $f(B) = 5$, $f(C) = 4$ and $f(D) = 10$. Which of the following are impossible?\\ (a) $f(E) = 2$ \qquad (b) $f(E) = 4$ \qquad (c) $f(E) = 5$
  47. 5.7 Let $r$ identical red balls and $b$ identical black balls be arranged in a row. Write down the number of arrangements for which the last ball is black.
  48. 5.8 It is known that a family has two children. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item If it is known that one of the children is a girl, what is the probability that the other child is also a girl? \item If it is known that the elder child is a girl, what is the probability that the younger child is also a girl? \end{enumerate}
  49. 5.9 A real number is called \emph{algebraic} if it occurs as the root of a polynomial with integer coefficients. Otherwise it is said to be a \emph{transcendental} number. Consider the interval $[0, 1]$ considered as a probability space when it is provided with the Lebesgue measure. What is the probability that a number chosen at random in $[0, 1]$ is transcendental?
  50. 5.10 List all primes $p \le 13$ such that $p$ divides $n^{13} - n$ for every integer $n$.
