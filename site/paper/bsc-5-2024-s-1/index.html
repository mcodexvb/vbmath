CCMA-11

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2024
  1. 1(a) Show that $\int_0^\infty e^{-x}x^{n-1}\,dx$ converges for $n>0$. [3]
  2. 1(b) Test for convergence of the functions \[\text{(i) }\int_1^2\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\log x}\,dx\qquad \text{(ii) }\int_0^1\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^3-x^6}}\,dx.\] [2+2]
  3. 1(c) Evaluate $\int_0^\infty\frac{\log(1+4x^2)}{x^2}\,dx$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of continuous functions converges to $f$ uniformly on $[a,b]$. Show that $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$. Also show that the condition of uniform convergence can not be omitted from above statement. [3+1]
  5. 2(b) Let $\{r_i\}$ be the listing of rational numbers in $[0,1]$. Consider the sequence $\{f_n\}$, \[f_n(x)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }x=r_1,r_2,\ldots,r_n,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}\] Show that each $f_n$ is Riemann-integrable but the limit function is not. Is the convergence of $\{f_n\}$ uniform? [3]
  6. 2(c) Show that the sequence of functions $\{nxe^{-nx^2}\}$ converges uniformly on $[1/2,1]$ but not on $[0,1]$. [3]
  7. 3(a) State and prove Weierstass M-test for uniform convergence of a series of function. [4]
  8. 3(b) Test for uniform convergence of the series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{x}{n(1+nx^2)}$ in $\mathbb R$. [3]
  9. 3(c) State Abel test and Dirichlet test for uniform convergence of a series of functions. [3]
  10. 4(a) Define radius of convergence of a power series. State and prove Cauchy-Hadamard Theorem for power series of real variables. [2+4]
  11. 4(b) Find the interval of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n\frac{(x-2)^n}{n10^n}$. [2]
  12. 4(c) If a power series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n$ has radius of convergence $\rho$, $0<\rho<\infty$ and it converges at $\rho$ then show that \[\lim_{x\to\rho^-}\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n\rho^n.\] [2]
  13. 5(a) When is the trigonometric series $\frac{a_0}2+\sum_{n=1}^\infty(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx)$ referred to as a Fourier series? \par Write the Bessel's inequality of Fourier series and prove it. [1+4]
  14. 5(b) If $f$ is Riemann-integrable in $[a,b]$ then show that $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^b f(x)\cos nx\,dx=0$. [4]
  15. 5(c) Find the Fourier coefficients of a constant function in $[-\pi,\pi]$. [1]
  16. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  17. 1(a) Form a partial differential equation (PDE) from $\phi\left(\frac{u}{x^3},\frac{y}{x}\right)=0$ by eliminating the arbitrary function $\phi$.
  18. 1(b) Find the integral surface of the Cauchy value problem $\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+2\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=3$, $u(x,0)=x^2$.
  19. 1(c) Construct a PDE of the family of orthogonal surfaces, each member of which cuts each member of the family $u^2=kxy$, $k\in\mathbb R$ is a parameter.
  20. 1(d) Find a singular solution of the following non-linear PDE, if any: \[u=x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac23\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}.\]
  21. 1(e) What are the basic limitations of Charpit's method for solving a first order PDE \[f(x,y,u,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y})?\]
  22. 1(f) Comment on the initial strip of the following PDE in connection with the Cauchy's method of characteristics: \[\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2+y^2\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2=u^2,\quad u(x,1)=x^2.\]
  23. 1(g) Find the particular integral (PI) of the PDE $\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-3\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}-2u\right)^2=2e^{2x}\tan(3x+y)$.
  24. 1(h) Solve: \[x^3y^2\frac{\partial^3 u}{\partial x^3}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} -x^2y^3\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}\frac{\partial^3 u}{\partial y^3}=0.\] [$5\times2=10$]
  25. 2(a) Find the integral surface of the following PDE: \[(xy^3-2x^4)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+(2y^4-x^3y)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} =9u(x^3-y^3),\quad u(x,x)=x^3.\]
  26. 2(b) Find the complete solution of the following PDE using Charpit's method: \[2ux+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2 =x\left(x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right).\] [5+5]
  27. 3(a) Prove that the PDE \[\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right) \left(x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)=1 \] is compatible with the PDE $\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=2024\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$, and hence find the one-parameter family of solutions.
  28. 3(b) Solve: \[\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}-\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y} +\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}-u=\cos(x+2y)+e^y.\] [5+5]
  29. 4(a) Define Cauchy-Euler PDE with one dependent and two independent variables. Hence, solve the following PDE: \[x^2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}-y^2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} +x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\log_e(xy).\]
  30. 4(b) Find the integral surface satisfying the PDE \[\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}-4\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}+4\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0\] and passing through the space curves $\alpha u=y^2$, $x=0$ and $\beta u=x^2$, $y=0$.
