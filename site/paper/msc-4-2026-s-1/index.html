MMC-41

Differential Geometry and Manifold Theory
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2026
  1. 1(a) Define germs of functions at a point $p\in\mathbb R^n$. What do you mean by a point-derivation of $C^\infty_p(\mathbb R^n)$? Give an example. If $D$ is a point-derivation of $C^\infty_p(\mathbb R^n)$, show that $D(c)=0$ for any constant function $c$. [[2+2+3]]
  2. 1(b) Explain a smooth manifold with an example. [[3]]
  3. 2(a) Define a vector field on a manifold and show that a $C^\infty$ vector field on an open set of a manifold gives rise to an $\mathbb R$-linear map on the class of all such vector fields. [[1+4]]
  4. 2(b) Examine further whether the above $\mathbb R$-linear map becomes a derivation of the same class. [[5]]
  5. 3(a) How is the wedge product of a differential $k$-form and an $l$-form defined? Explain. Suppose the standard coordinates on $\mathbb R^3$ are $\rho$, $\phi$ and $\theta$. If $x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta$, $y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta$ and \unreadable{symbol before $=$ missing in print} $=\rho\cos\phi$, calculate $dx,dy,dz$ and $dx\wedge dy\wedge dz$ in terms of $d\rho,d\phi,d\theta$. [[1+4]]
  6. 3(b) If $A_k(V)$ denotes the set of all alternating $k$-tensors on a vector space $V$ of dimension $n$, show that the subset $\{\alpha^I:I=(i_1,i_2,\ldots,i_k)\}$ is a basis for $A_k(V)$. Also find the dimension of $A_k(V)$. [[5]]
  7. 4(a) Let $w$ be a $C^\infty$ differential $k$-form on an open set $U$ of a manifold $M$ of dimension $n$. Define the exterior derivative of $w$. Show that an anti-derivation of degree 1 satisfying some characterzing properties (to be stated by you) becomes the exterior differentiation. [[1+4]]
  8. 4(b) Prove that the operators gradient (on scalar-valued functions) and curl, divergence (on vector-valued functions) are precisely the exterior derivatives of certain differential forms on $\mathbb R^3$. [[5]]
  9. 5(a) Prove or disprove: Any atlas $\mathcal U=\{(U_\alpha,\phi_\alpha)\}$ on a locally Euclidean space is contained in a unique maximal atlas. [[3]]
  10. 5(b) Determine whether the general linear group $GL(n,\mathbb R)$ is a manifold. [[4]]
  11. 5(c) If $(U,\phi)$ is a chart on a manifold $M$ of dimension $n$, then prove that the coordinate map $\phi:U\longrightarrow\phi(U)\subset\mathbb R^n$ is a diffeomorphism. [[3]]
  12. 6(a) Describe how one can define a regular submanifold of a manifold. Show that the solution set $S$ of $x^3+y^3+z^3=1$ in $\mathbb R^3$ is a manifold of dimension 2. [[2+2]]
  13. 6(b) State Regular Level Set Theorem. Use it to show that the special linear group $SL(n,\mathbb R)$ is a regular submanifold of $GL(n,\mathbb R)$ of dimension $n^2-1$. [[2+4]]
