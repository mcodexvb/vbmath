CC-11

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2023
  1. 1(a) If $\phi$ is bounded and monotonic in $[a,\infty]$ and $\int_a^\infty f\,dx$ is convergent then show that $\int_a^\infty f\phi\,dx$ is convergent. [4]
  2. 1(b) Show that $\int_0^\infty \frac{\sin x}{x}$ is convergent but not absolutely. [5]
  3. 1(c) Prove that $\int_1^\infty\frac{\sin x}{x^p}$ is absolutely convergent for $p>1$. [1]
  4. 2(a) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of R-integrable functions converges to $f$ uniformly on $[a,b]$. Show that $f$ is R-integrable on $[a,b]$ and $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^b f_n=\int_a^b f$. Also show that the condition of uniform convergence can not be omitted from above statement. [4+2]
  5. 2(b) Consider the sequence $\{f_n\}$, \[f_n(x)=\begin{cases} 1-nx&\text{for }0\le x\le\frac1n,\\ 0&\text{for }\frac1n<x\le1. \end{cases}\] Find its limit function $f$ and show that $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f_n=\int_0^1 f$. Is the convergence of $\{f_n\}$ uniform? [3]
  6. 2(c) Show that the convergence of $\{x^n\}$ on $[0,1]$ is not uniform. [1]
  7. 3(a) State and prove Dirichlet's test for the uniform convergence of the series $\sum f_ng_n$. [4]
  8. 3(b) Consider the series $\sum\frac{\sin nx}{n^p}$ with $0\le p\le1$ in the interval $[a,b]$. \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Show that the series converges uniformly in $[a,b]$ if $2k\pi<a<b<2(k+1)\pi$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. \item Show that the convergence is not uniform in any interval which includes the points $2k\pi$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. \end{enumerate} [3+3]
  9. 4(a) State and prove Weierstrass's Approximation Theorem. [5]
  10. 4(b) Find the interval of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{x^n}{n^2 2^n}$. [2]
  11. 4(c) If $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_nx^n=\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_nx^n$ for some non zero distinct points $c_1,c_2,\ldots$ with $c_k\to 0$ then show that the two power series are identical. [3]
  12. 5(a) When is the trigonometric series $\frac{a_0}2+\sum_{n=1}^\infty(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx)$ referred to as a Fourier series? \par Show that if $\frac{a_0}2+\sum_{n=1}^\infty(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx)$ converges uniformly in $[-\pi,\pi]$ to a function $f$ then it is the Fourier series of $f$. [1+3]
  13. 5(b) If $a_n$ and $b_n$ are Fourier coefficient of $f$ then show that $a_n,b_n\to0$ as $n\to\infty$. [4]
  14. 5(c) Show that the Fourier series of $x^2$ in $[-\pi,\pi]$ is $\frac{\pi^2}3+4\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n\left[\frac{\cos nx}{n^2}\right]$. [2]
  15. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  16. 1(a) Find the partial differential equation (PDE) for the family of planes if the intercepts made by the arbitrary member of the family on the co-ordinate axes are $a$, $b$ and $1$ ($a\ne0$, $b\ne0$).
  17. 1(b) Find the general integral of the PDE: $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=3x+4y+5z$.
  18. 1(c) Prove that the following PDEs are compatible. $z^2=xy\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$, $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$.
  19. 1(d) Find the family of surfaces orthogonal to the family of surfaces whose PDE is given by $(y+z)\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+(z+x)\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=x+y$.
  20. 1(e) Let $z=z(x,y)$ be the complete integral of the PDE $pq=x^2y^3$ passing through the points $(0,0,4)$ and $(3,2,16)$ in the $xyz$-space. Find the value of $z(x,y)$ at $(-3,4)$.
  21. 1(f) Show that there is no singular integral for a PDE of the following type: $f\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x},\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)=0$.
  22. 1(g) Find the initial strip of the following PDE passing through the curve $\Gamma:x^2=z,\ y=0$ in the context of Cauchy's method of characteristic: \[\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2-3\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2=z.\]
  23. 1(h) Find the complementary function (CF) of the following PDE: \[(D^3D'+D^2D'^2-6DD'^3)z=y\cos(3y-2x);\quad D^n\equiv\frac{\partial^n}{\partial x^n},\quad D'^n\equiv\frac{\partial^n}{\partial y^n}.\] [$5\times2=10$]
  24. 2(a) Find the general integral and integral surface passing through the curve $3x=2y=z$ of the following PDE: \[x(y^2+z^2)\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+y(x^2+z^2)\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=(x^2-y^2)z.\]
  25. 2(b) Let $z(x,y)$ be the solution of the following Cauchy problem: $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}-x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=1-z$; $-\infty<x<\infty$, $y\ge0$, $z(x,0)=\cos(x)$. Find the value of $z(0,1)$ and hence comment on the nature of the solution. [5+5]
  26. 3(a) Find the complete integral of the following first-order nonlinear PDE by the Charpit's method. \[\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\left(y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}+z\right)^2.\] Hence find the singular integral, if any.
  27. 3(b) Solve: \[\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}-\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}+x-y.\]
  28. 4(a) Classify the compatible systems of first-order PDEs in two independent variables. Find a one-parameter family of common solutions of the following PDEs: $x\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2+y\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2=z$, $x\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)^2=y\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right)^2$.
  29. 4(b) Find a surface passing through the parabolas, given by $z=0$, $y^2=4ax$ and $z=1$, $y^2=-4ax$ and satisfying the PDE: \[x\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}+2\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=0.\]
