MJMA-1

Algebra-I and Analysis I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2025
  1. 1(a) Consider the relation $R=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb Z^2:y=|x|\}$. Check whether $R$ is transitive and symmetric or not? [1+2]
  2. 1(b) Show that the set $U(n)=\{[a]\in\mathbb Z_n-\{0\}$ such that $gcd(a,n)=1\}$ is a commutative group with respect to multiplication modulo $n$. [4]
  3. 1(c) In a group $G$, show that equations $ax=b$ and $ya=b$ have unique solutions in $G$ for all $a,b\in G$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Let $a$ belong to a group and $O(a)=m$. If $n$ is relatively prime to $m$, show that $a$ can be written as the $n$th power of some element in the group. [3]
  5. 2(b) For the group $G=\langle a,b|\ O(a)=3,\ O(b)=2$ and $ba=a^2b\rangle$, determine $|G|$ and $aba^2$. [1+3]
  6. 2(c) If $A$ belongs to $GL(2,R)$ and $O(A)$ is finite, what can you say about $\det A$? [3]
  7. 3(a) Describe the subgroups of the set of integers $\mathbb Z$. Suppose that $H$ is a proper subgroup of $\mathbb Z$ under addition and that $H$ contains $12, 30$, and $54$. What are the possibilities for $H$? [4+3]
  8. 3(b) List all subgroups of order $2$ in $S_4$. [3]
  9. 4(a) Show that there is unique subgroup of order $m$ in a finite cyclic group $G$ for each divisor $m$ of $|G|$. How many subgroups does $\mathbb Z_{20}$ have? [5+2]
  10. 4(b) Let $a$ be a generator of a cyclic group $G$. If $|G|=n$ and $\langle a^{10}\rangle=\langle a^5\rangle$ prove that $n$ is odd. [3]
  11. 5(a) Show that the set of all even permutations in $S_n$ forms a subgroup of $S_n$. [5]
  12. 5(b) What is the order of the product of a pair of disjoint cycles of lengths $4$ and $6$? What about the product of three disjoint cycles of lengths $6$, $8$, and $10$?. [1+2]
  13. 5(c) What cycle is $(a_1a_2\ldots a_n)^{-1}$? [2]
  14. 6(a) Show that $Z(S_3)=\{e\}$. [4]
  15. 6(b) Find a permutation in $S_6$ that commutes with $(1\ 2\ 3)(4\ 5)$. [3]
  16. 6(c) Let $H=\{x^2|x\in A_4\}$. Prove $H$ is not a subgroup of $A_4$. [3]
  17. 7(a) Prove the following results: \par i. If $a\ne0$ and $b\in\mathbb R$ such that $a.b=1$ then $b=\frac1a$.\par ii. If $a.b=0$ then either $a=0$ or $b=0$. [5]
  18. 7(b) For any three elements $a,b,c\in\mathbb R$ prove the following results : \par i. If $a>b$ and $b>c$ then $a>c$.\par ii. If $a>b$ and $c>0$ then $ca>cb$. [5]
  19. 8(a) Let $x,y\in\mathbb R$ and $x<y$. Then prove that \par i. there is a rational number $r$ such that $x<r<y$.\par ii. there is an irrational number $s$ such that $x<s<y$. [5]
  20. 8(b) Prove that the set of all irrational numbers in $(0,1)$ is uncountable. [5]
  21. 9(a) Show that if A and B are countable sets then $A\times B$ is countable. [4]
  22. 9(b) Let S be a nonempty bounded subset of $\mathbb R$ with Sup S $=$M and Inf S $=$m. Find SupT and Inf T where $T=\{x-y:x,y\in S\}$ [4]
  23. 9(c) Find Sup A and Inf A where $A=\{\frac1m+\frac1n:m,n\in\mathbb N\}$. [2]
  24. 10(a) Find $y_n$ where \par (i) $y=\cos^3x$\par (ii) $y=e^{ax}sin(bx+c)$. [4]
  25. 10(b) If $y=tan^{-1}x$ then prove that $(1+x^2)y_1=1$, and $(1+x^2)y_{n+1}+2nxy_n+n(n-1)y_{n-1}=0$. Find also the value of $(y_n)_0$. [6]
  26. 11(a) Evaluate $\lim_{x\to0}(\cos x)^{\cot^2x}$. [4]
  27. 11(b) If $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{a\sin x-\sin2x}{\tan^3x}$ is finite, find the value of $a$ and the limit. [6]
  28. 12(a) State Rolle's Theorem. Is Rolle's Theorem applicable to $f(x)$ where $f(x)=tanx$, $x\in[0,\pi]$? [2]
  29. 12(b) State and prove Lagrange's Mean Value Theorem. [6]
  30. 12(c) If $f'(x)=0$ for all $x\in[a,b]$, then prove that $f(x)$ is constant in $[a,b]$. [2]
