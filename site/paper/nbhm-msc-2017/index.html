NBHM · 2017 · M.Sc. scholarship

  1. 1.1 Let $\sigma = (1\ 4\ 3\ 8\ 7)$ and $\tau = (5\ 4\ 8)$ be cycles in $S_8$. Express $\sigma\tau$ and $\tau\sigma$ (see Notations) as the product of disjoint cycles.
  2. 1.2 Let $G$ and $G'$ be groups and let $\phi : G \to G'$ be a group homomorphism which is surjective. Which of the following statements are true?\\ (a) If $G$ is abelian, then $G'$ is abelian. \quad (b) If $G$ is cyclic, then $G'$ is cyclic. \quad (c) If $N$ is a normal subgroup of $G$, then $\phi(N)$ is a normal subgroup of $G'$.
  3. 1.3 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 2$. Which of the following subgroups are normal in $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$?\\ (a) $H = \{A \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C}) \mid A \text{ is upper triangular}\}$. \quad (b) $H = \{A \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C}) \mid A \text{ is diagonal}\}$. \quad (c) $H = \{A \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C}) \mid \det(A) = 1\}$.
  4. 1.4 Find the multiplicative inverse of $x + 1$ in the field $\mathbb{F}_5[x]/(x^2 + x + 1)$.
  5. 1.5 Let $N \ge 2$. Let $x \in \mathbb{R}^N$, $x \neq 0$, be a column vector. Find the rank of the matrix $A = xx^T$.
  6. 1.6 Let $N \ge 2$. Let $x \in \mathbb{R}^N$, $x \neq 0$, be a column vector. Find the condition on $x$ such that the matrix $I - 2xx^T$ is orthogonal.
  7. 1.7 Let $W = \{A \in M_3(\mathbb{R}) \mid A = A^T \text{ and } \operatorname{tr}(A) = 0\}$. Write down a basis for $W$.
  8. 1.8 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exists $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ which is orthogonal and has $2$ as an eigenvalue. \item There exists $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ which is orthogonal and has $i$ as an eigenvalue. \item If $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ is orthogonal, then $\|Ax\| = \|x\|$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^2$, where $\|\cdot\|$ denotes the usual euclidean norm on $\mathbb{R}^2$. \end{enumerate}
  9. 1.9 Let $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be such that its eigenvalues are $1$ and $-1$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ (a) $A^{-1} = A$. \qquad (b) $A^{-1} = -A$. \qquad (c) No conclusion can be drawn about $A^{-1}$.
  10. 1.10 Solve the equation: $x^4 - 10x^3 + 26x^2 - 10x + 1 = 0$.
  11. 2.1 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} (\cos x)^{\frac{1}{\sin^2 x}}$.
  12. 2.2 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n}\left(\prod_{k=1}^{n} (n + k)\right)^{\frac{1}{n}}$.
  13. 2.3 Let $a > 0$ be a real number and let $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Evaluate: \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\left(a + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n \left(a + \frac{2}{n}\right)^n \cdots \left(a + \frac{k}{n}\right)^n}{a^{nk}}. \]
  14. 2.4 Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{1}{h}\int_{a-h}^{a+h} f(t)\,dt$.
  15. 2.5 Let $f_n(x) = n^2 x(1 - x)^n$. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x)$ for $0 < x < 1$.
  16. 2.6 Which of the following series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ are convergent?\\ (a) $a_n = (n^3 + 1)^{\frac{1}{3}} - n$. \qquad (b) $a_n = \dfrac{n^3 + 1}{2^n + 1}$. \qquad (c) $a_n = \dfrac{(n + 1)(n + 2)\cdots(n + n)}{n^n}$.
  17. 2.7 Determine if each of the following series is absolutely convergent, conditionally convergent or divergent.\\ (a) $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{1}{n + 8}$. \qquad (b) $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{1}{n(n + 8)}$. \qquad (c) $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{n}{n + 8}$.
  18. 2.8 Let $f(x) = e^{\frac{1}{x}}$. In which of the following domains is it uniformly continuous?\\ (a) $]0, 1[$. \qquad (b) $]1, \infty[$. \qquad (c) $]1, 2[$.
  19. 2.9 Let $f(z) = \dfrac{1}{7 - z}$ for $z \in \mathbb{C}$, $z \neq 7$. Write down the Taylor expansion of $f$ in a neighbourhood of $z = 5$.
  20. 2.10 Let $C$ be the contour in the complex plane consisting of two straight line segments, one from $z = 0$ to $z = i$ and the other from $z = i$ to $z = 1 + i$. Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int_C f(z)\,dz$, where $f(z) = y - x - 3x^2 i$, $z = x + iy$, $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$.
  21. 3.1 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n \ge 2$, be a fixed positive integer. Let $C_k = \binom{n}{k}$ for $0 \le k \le n$. Evaluate: \[ C_1 - 2C_2 + 3C_3 - 4C_4 + \cdots + (-1)^{n-1} n C_n. \]
  22. 3.2 An algebraic number is a complex number which is the root of a polynomial with integer coefficients. Which of the following numbers are algebraic?\\ (a) $7 + 2^{\frac{1}{3}}$. \qquad (b) $\sqrt{3} + i\sqrt{5}$. \qquad (c) $\cos\dfrac{2\pi}{7}$.
  23. 3.3 From a set of three women and four men, three are chosen at random to stand in a row for a photograph. What is the probability that the photograph shows a woman in the middle flanked by two men, one on either side of her?
  24. 3.4 Find the angle between the planes $2x - y + z = 6$ and $x + y + 2z = 3$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
  25. 3.5 A line moves in the plane so that it passes through the point $(1, 1)$ and such that it intersects the two coordinate axes. Find the locus of the centre of the circle which passes through these two points of intersection of the line with the coordinate axes, and through the origin.
  26. 3.6 Sum the infinite series: \[ \frac{5}{3 \cdot 6}\frac{1}{4^2} + \frac{5 \cdot 8}{3 \cdot 6 \cdot 9}\frac{1}{4^3} + \frac{5 \cdot 8 \cdot 11}{3 \cdot 6 \cdot 9 \cdot 12}\frac{1}{4^4} + \cdots \]
  27. 3.7 A magic square of size $N$, $N \ge 2$, is an $N \times N$ matrix with integer entries such that the sums of the entries of each row, each column and the two diagonals are all equal. If the entries of the magic square are made up of integers in arithmetic progression with first term $a$ and common difference $d$, what is the value of this common sum?
  28. 3.8 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Define $\Lambda(n) = \begin{cases} \log p, & \text{if } n = p^r,\ p \text{ a prime and } r \in \mathbb{N}, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ Given $N \in \mathbb{N}$, compute $\displaystyle\sum_{d \mid N} \Lambda(d)$, where the sum ranges over all divisors of $N$.
  29. 3.9 For $a \in \mathbb{R}$, define $a^+ = \max\{a, 0\}$. Let $u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be an arbitrary function. Let $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Define $v(x) = (u(x) - t)^+ + t$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid v(x) = t\} = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid u(x) = t\}$. \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid v(x) > t\} = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid u(x) > t\}$. \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid v(x) > \tau\} = \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid u(x) > \tau\}$ for all $\tau \ge t$. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Let $V$ be a real vector space of real-valued functions on a fixed set $X$ such that (i) all constant functions are in $V$, and (ii) if $f \in V$, then $f^2$ (i.e.\ the function defined by $x \mapsto (f(x))^2$ for all $x \in X$) and $|f|$ (i.e.\ the function defined by $x \mapsto |f(x)|$ for all $x \in X$) are also in $V$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $f$ and $g$ belong to $V$, then $fg$ (i.e.\ the function defined by $x \mapsto f(x)g(x)$ for all $x \in X$) also belongs to $V$. \item If $f$ and $g$ belong to $V$, then $\max\{f, g\}$ (i.e.\ the function defined by $x \mapsto \max\{f(x), g(x)\}$ for all $x \in X$) also belongs to $V$. \item If $f \in V$ and if $p$ is a polynomial in one variable with real coefficients, then $p(f)$ (i.e.\ the function defined by $x \mapsto p(f(x))$ for all $x \in X$) also belongs to $V$. \end{enumerate}
