CC-7

Differential Equations-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2021
  1. 1 Answer any \textit{ten} questions from the following: [\(10\times2=20\)]
  2. 1(a) Solve the differential equation \((1+e^{x/y})\,dx+e^{x/y}(1-\frac{x}{y})\,dy=0\). [2]
  3. 1(b) Solve: \((D^2+9)^2(D^2+3D+2)^2(D+2)^3y=0\). [2]
  4. 1(c) Solve: \(\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{7xy+y^2}{7x^2}\). [2]
  5. 1(d) Find a second order linear homogeneous differential equation whose linearly independent solutions are \(x\), and \(xe^x\). [2]
  6. 1(e) Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of parabolas \(cy^2=x^3\). [2]
  7. 1(f) Solve the nonlinear differential equation \(\sin(x\frac{dy}{dx})\cos y=\cos(x\frac{dy}{dx})\sin y+\frac{dy}{dx}\). [2]
  8. 1(g) Find four different solutions of the differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=y^{2/3},\ y(0)=0\). [2]
  9. 1(h) Solve: \(y\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=(\frac{dy}{dx})^2\). [2]
  10. 1(i) Let \(y(x)\) be the solution of the differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=(y-11)(y-19)(y-25)\) satisfying the initial condition \(y(0)=15\). Find the value of \(y(x)\) when \(x\to\infty\). [2]
  11. 1(j) Consider the differential equation \(x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}-4y=0\). If \(W(1)=1\), then find the value of \(W(7)-W(3)\). [2]
  12. 1(k) Find an integrating factor of the differential equation \((y^4+2y)\,dx+(xy^3+2y^4-4x)\,dy=0\). [2]
  13. 1(l) Explain why it is always possible to solve any homogeneous differential equation \[M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0\] by the substitution \(y=vx\). [2]
  14. 1(m) Explain why there exists no constant solution of the differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=y^2+16\). [2]
  15. 1(n) Find the solution of \(x\frac{dy}{dx}=y^2-y\) that passes through the point \((2,1/4)\). [2]
  16. 2(a) Solve the second order linear differential equation \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-y=x\cos 2x+x^2+e^{3x}\). [4]
  17. 2(b) Prove that the transformation \(v=y^{1-n}(n\ne0\text{ or }1)\) reduces the Bernoulli equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=P(x)y+Q(x)y^n\) to a linear equation in \(v\). Hence solve the equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}-y=e^xy^2\). [3]
  18. 2(c) Find the differential equation corresponding to the family of curves \(y=k(x-k)^2\), where \(k\) is an arbitrary constant. [2]
  19. 2(d) Solve: \(\frac{dy}{dx}=\tan^2(x+y)\). [1]
  20. 3(a) Solve the differential equation \(y=(x-b)p+\frac ap\). [3]
  21. 3(b) Using the method of undetermined coefficients, solve the equation \(D^2(D-1)y=3e^x+\sin x\). [4]
  22. 3(c) If \(u\) and \(v\) are any two solutions of the equation \(\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0\) on an interval \([a,b]\), then prove that their Wronskian \(W(u,v)\) is either identically zero or never zero on \([a,b]\). [3]
  23. 4(a) Verify that the equation \((2x^2+3x)y''+(6x+3)y'+2y=(x+1)e^x\) is exact and then solve it. [4]
  24. 4(b) Solve the differential equation \((x-3y+3)\,dx+(3x+y+9)\,dy=0\). [3]
  25. 4(c) One solution of the Legendre differential equation \((1-x^2)y''-2xy'+2y=0\) is \(y=x\). Find a second solution. [2]
  26. 4(d) Convert the differential equation \(y=xf(p)+\phi(p)\) to a linear equation in \(x\). [1]
  27. 5(a) Using the method of variation of parameters, solve the equation \((D^2+1)y=f(x)\), where \(f(x)\) is a known integrable function. [3]
  28. 5(b) Find the singular solution and extraneous loci of the differential equation \(4xp^2=(3x-5)^2\). [4]
  29. 5(c) Solve the equation \(x^2y''+xy'-y=x^4e^{3x}\). [3]
  30. 6(a) Solve the differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}+y=f(x)\) with initial condition \(y(0)=1\), where \(f(x)=1\), when \(0\le x<1\) and \(f(x)=-1\), when \(x\ge1\). Hence find the value of \(y(5)\). [4]
  31. 6(b) Reduce the equation \((x^2+y^2)(1+p)^2-2(x+y)(1+p)(x+py)+(x+py)^2=0\) to Clairaut's form by the substitution \(x^2+y^2=v\) and \(x+y=u\). Hence solve the equation. [3]
  32. 6(c) Solve: \(\frac{d^4y}{dx^4}-\cot x\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}=0\). [2]
  33. 6(d) Use the method of isoclines to sketch some of the solution curves of the equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=x+y+1\). [1]
  34. 7(a) If \(e^{\int\phi(\frac{x^2}{y})\,d(\frac{x^2}{y})}\) is an integrating factor of the differential equation \[M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0\] , then find the expression of \(\phi(\frac{x^2}{y})\). [3]
  35. 7(b) Show that if \(f\) is any solution of the differential equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=A(x)y^2+B(x)y+C(x)\), then the transformation \(y=f+\frac1v\) reduces the above differential equation to a linear in \(v\). Hence solve the equation \(\frac{dy}{dx}=-y^2+xy+1\); given solution \(f(x)=x\). [4]
  36. 7(c) Find the equation of the family of curves which cut the members of the family of parabolas \(y^2=4ax\) at angle of \(45^\circ\). \def\paperfull{25} [3]
