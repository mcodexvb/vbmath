MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2017
  1. 1(a) Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\frac{dz}{z-3}$ over the upper half of the circle $|z-3|=3$. [2]
  2. 1(b) If $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$ are two simple closed contours, $\Gamma_2$ lying wholly within $\Gamma_1$ and if $f(z)$ is analytic in the closed annular region determined by $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$, then show that $\displaystyle\oint_{\Gamma_1}f(z)dz=\oint_{\Gamma_2}f(z)dz$, the integrals being taken in the same sense. [2]
  3. 1(c) State Cauchy's integral formula. Show that \[ \left(\frac{a^n}{n!}\right)^2=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_\Gamma\frac{a^ne^{az}}{n!z^{n+1}}dz, \] where $\Gamma$ is the circle $|z|=3$ and deduce that \[ \sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\frac{a^n}{n!}\right)^2 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} e^{2a\cos\theta}d\theta. \] [1+3]
  4. 1(d) Evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_\Gamma\frac{9z^2-iz+4}{z(z^2+1)}dz$, where $\Gamma$ is the circle $|z|=2$. [2]
  5. 2(a) Define an entire function. Show that if $f(z)$ is an entire function and $|f(z)|\geq\frac13$ for all values of $z$, then $f(z)$ is a constant. [1+2]
  6. 2(b) State and prove Morera's theorem. [4]
  7. 2(c) State Maximum Modulus theorem. If $f(z)$ be a non-constant analytic function regular in a region $D$ including the boundary $C$ and $f(z)\ne0$ for any point in $D$, then $|f(z)|$ can not attain its minimum in $D$. [1+2]
  8. 3(a) State Laurent's theorem. Expand the function $f(z)=\dfrac1{(z+1)(z+3)}$ in $1<|z|<3$. [3]
  9. 3(b) State and prove Schwarz's Lemma. [1+3]
  10. 3(c) If $f(z)$ has a pole of multiplicity $m$ at $z=\alpha$, then show that \[ \operatorname{Res}(f;\alpha)=\frac1{(m-1)!}\lim_{z\to\alpha}\frac{d^{m-1}}{dz^{m-1}}\left[(z-\alpha)^mf(z)\right]. \] \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [3]
  11. 4(a) If a function $f$ is bounded and analytic throughout a domain $0<|z-\alpha|<\delta$, then prove that either $f$ is analytic at $\alpha$ or else $\alpha$ is a removable singularity of $f$. [3]
  12. 4(b) If $z=\alpha$ is a zero of $f(z)$ of order $m$, then show that $\dfrac1{f(z)}$ has a pole of order $m$ at $z=\alpha$. [2]
  13. 4(c) Determine the zeros and singularities of the function $\dfrac{z-2}{z^2}\sin\bigl(\dfrac1{z-1}\bigr)$. Also find the residue at its pole. [3]
  14. 4(d) Prove that in every neighbourhood of an essential singular point of $f$, the function $f$ takes values arbitrarily close to every complex number. [2]
  15. 5(a) State and prove Rouche's theorem (Principle of Argument may be assumed). [4]
  16. 5(b) Let $f(z)$ be analytic in $|z|\leq1$ and let $|f(z)|<1$ on $|z|=1$. Show that there exists just one point $\alpha$ within the circle $|z|=1$ such that $f(\alpha)=\alpha$. [2]
  17. 5(c) If a function $f(z)$ is regular in a domain $D$ and if $z_1,z_2,\ldots,z_n,\ldots$ is a sequence of zeros of $f(z)$ having limit point as an interior point $\alpha$ of $D$, then show that $f(z)\equiv0$. [3]
  18. 5(d) Evaluate the integral \[ \oint_C \frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}dz,\quad \text{where }f(z)=\frac{(z^2+1)^2}{(z^2+3z+2)^3} \] and $C$ is the circle $|z|=3$. [1]
  19. 6(a) Find $\operatorname{Res}[f(z),\infty]$ where $f(z)=\dfrac1{z^2-1}$. [2]
  20. 6(b) If the point at infinity is a pole of order $m$ for an entire function $f$, then $f(z)$ is a polynomial of degree $m$ and conversely -- prove it. [3]
  21. 6(c) Evaluate \textbf{\textit{any one}} of the following by the method of contour integration: \begin{romanparts} \item $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{(a+b\cos\theta)^2},\quad a>b>0;$ \item $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\sin x^2dx.$ \end{romanparts} [5]
