BMC-13

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2018
  1. 1 Find the angle through which the axes are to be rotated so that the equation $7x^2+8xy-5y^2=1$ may be reduced to the form $Ax^2+By^2=1,\ A>0$; find also $A$ and $B$. [4]
  2. 2 Verify whether the equation of the pair of straight lines through the origin perpendicular to the pair of straight lines $ax^2+2hxy+by^2=0$ is $bx^2-2hxy+ay^2=0$. [4]
  3. 3 Derive the equations of the tangents to the conic $x^2+4xy+3y^2-5x-6y+3=0$ which are parallel to the straight line $x+4y=-2$, if exists. [4]
  4. 4 Find the pole of the straight line $2x-5y=4$ with respect to the parabola $y^2=8x$. [4]
  5. 5 Reduce the equation $6x^2-5xy-4y^2+7x+8y+4=0$ to its canonical form and determine the nature of the conic. [4]
  6. 6 Show that the straight line $r\cos(\theta-\beta)=p$ in polar coordinate touches the conic $\frac lr=1+e\cos\theta$, if $(l\cos\beta-ep)^2+l^2\sin^2\beta=p^2$. \begin{center}\textbf{Gr.-B} (Full Marks=24)\\(Three Dimensional Geometry)\\Answer \textit{any three} questions.\end{center} [4]
  7. 7 Find the angle between two straight lines whose directions cosines are given by $l+m+n=0$, $l^2+m^2-n^2=0$, if they are not parallel. [4]
  8. 8 Verify whether the equation $2x^2-y^2+3z^2-xy+7xz+2yz=0$ represents a pair of planes. Find the angle between them, if yes and are not parallel. [4]
  9. 9 A plane cuts the coordinate axes in $A,B,C$ such that the centroid of the triangle $ABC$ is the point $(a,b,c)$. Show that the equation of the plane is $\frac xa+\frac yb+\frac zc=k$, where $k$ is a number to be determined by you. [4]
  10. 10 Prove that the plane passing through the point $(a,b,c)$ containing the straight line $x=py+q=rz+s$ can be put into the form $\begin{vmatrix}x&py+q&rz+s\\a&pb+q&rc+s\\1&1&1\end{vmatrix}=0$. [4]
  11. 11 Show that by a special choice of coordinate axes the equation of two skew lines can be put in the form $y=\pm mx,\ z=c$. [4]
  12. 12 Obtain the equation of the sphere through the point $(1,1,2)$ and $(2,-2,3)$ and having its center on the line $2x+3y=0=5x+y-z$. [4]
  13. 13 Prove that a general equation of second degree $ax^2+by^2+cz^2+2fyz+2gzx+2hxy+2ux+2vy+2wz+d=0$ may represent a cone provided the condition $\begin{vmatrix}a&h&g&u\\h&b&f&v\\g&f&c&w\\u&v&w&d\end{vmatrix}=0$ on coefficients $a,b,c,f,g,h,u,v,w,d$ holds. [4]
  14. 14 Derive the equation of a cylinder whose guiding curve is $ax^2+by^2+2hxy+2gx+2fy+c=0,\ z=0$ and whose generators are parallel to the line $\frac xl=\frac ym=\frac zn$. [4]
