NBHM · 2013 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Find the number of elements of order two in the symmetric group $S_4$ of all permutations of the four symbols $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
  2. 1.2 Let $G$ be the group of all invertible $2 \times 2$ upper triangular matrices (under matrix multiplication). Pick out the normal subgroups of $G$ from the following: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $H = \{A \in G : a_{12} = 0\}$; \item $H = \{A \in G : a_{11} = 1\}$; \item $H = \{A \in G : a_{11} = a_{22}\}$, where $A = \begin{bmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} \\ 0 & a_{22} \end{bmatrix}$. \end{enumerate}
  3. 1.3 Let $G = GL_n(\R)$ and let $H$ be the (normal) subgroup of all matrices with positive determinant. Identify the quotient group $G/H$.
  4. 1.4 Which of the following rings are integral domains? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\R[x]$, the ring of all polynomials in one variable with real coefficients. \item $\M_n(\R)$. \item The ring of complex analytic functions defined on the unit disc of the complex plane (with pointwise addition and multiplication as the ring operations). \end{enumerate}
  5. 1.5 Find the condition on the real numbers $a, b$ and $c$ such that the following system of equations has a solution: \begin{align*} 2x + y + 3z &= a \\ x + z &= b \\ y + z &= c. \end{align*}
  6. 1.6 Let $\mathcal{P}_n$ denote the vector space of all polynomials in one variable with real coefficients and of degree less than, or equal to, $n$, equipped with the standard basis $\{1, x, x^2, \dots, x^n\}$. Define $T : \mathcal{P}_2 \to \mathcal{P}_3$ by \[ T(p)(x) = \int_0^x p(t)\,dt + p'(x) + p(2). \] Write down the matrix of this transformation with respect to the standard bases of $\mathcal{P}_2$ and $\mathcal{P}_3$.
  7. 1.7 Determine the dimension of the kernel of the linear transformation $T$ defined in Question 1.6 above.
  8. 1.8 A symmetric matrix in $\M_n(\R)$ is said to be \emph{non-negative definite} if $x^T A x \ge 0$ for all (column) vectors $x \in \R^n$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If a real symmetric $n \times n$ matrix is non-negative definite, then all of its eigenvalues are non-negative. \item If a real symmetric $n \times n$ matrix has all its eigenvalues non-negative, then it is non-negative definite. \item If $A \in \M_n(\R)$, then $AA^T$ is non-negative definite. \end{enumerate}
  9. 1.9 Only one of the following matrices is non-negative definite. Find it.\\ a.\ $\begin{bmatrix} 5 & -3 \\ -3 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$ \qquad b.\ $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ -3 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$ \qquad c.\ $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$.
  10. 1.10 Let $B$ be the real symmetric non-negative definite $2 \times 2$ matrix such that $B^2 = A$ where $A$ is the non-negative definite matrix in Question 1.9 above. Write down the characteristic polynomial of $B$.
  11. 2.1 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \sin\left(\left(2n\pi + \frac{1}{2n\pi}\right)\sin\left(2n\pi + \frac{1}{2n\pi}\right)\right)$.
  12. 2.2 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n}\left[(n+1)(n+2)\cdots(n+n)\right]^{\frac{1}{n}}$.
  13. 2.3 Which of the following series are convergent? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\frac{1}{2} + (-1)^n}{n}$. \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n(\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n})$. \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sin(n^{\frac{3}{2}})}{n^{\frac{3}{2}}}$. \end{enumerate}
  14. 2.4 Which of the following functions are uniformly continuous? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f(x) = x\sin\frac{1}{x}$ on $]0, 1[$. \item $f(x) = \sin^2 x$ on $]0, \infty[$. \item $f(x) = \sin(x\sin x)$ on $]0, \infty[$. \end{enumerate}
  15. 2.5 Find the points where the following function is differentiable: \[ f(x) = \begin{cases} \tan^{-1} x, & \text{if } |x| \le 1, \\ \frac{\pi x}{4|x|} + \frac{|x|-1}{2}, & \text{if } |x| > 1. \end{cases} \]
  16. 2.6 Which of the following sequences/series of functions are uniformly convergent on $[0, 1]$? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f_n(x) = (\cos(\pi n! x))^{2n}$. \item $\displaystyle\sum_{m=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(m^6 x)}{m^3}$. \item $f_n(x) = n^2 x(1 - x^2)^n$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 2.7 Let $f \in \mathcal{C}^1[0, 1]$. For a partition $(\mathcal{P}) : 0 = x_0 < x_1 < x_2 < \dots < x_n = 1$, define $S(\mathcal{P}) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |f(x_i) - f(x_{i-1})|$. Compute the supremum of $S(\mathcal{P})$ taken over all possible partitions $\mathcal{P}$.
  18. 2.8 Write down the Taylor series expansion about the origin in the region $\{|x| < 1\}$ for the function \[ f(x) = x\tan^{-1}(x) - \frac{1}{2}\log(1 + x^2). \]
  19. 2.9 Write down all possible values of $i^{-2i}$.
  20. 2.10 What is the image of the set $\{z \in \C : z = x + iy,\ x \ge 0,\ y \ge 0\}$ under the mapping $z \mapsto z^2$?
  21. 3.1 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space. For subsets $A$ and $B$ of $X$, define $d(A, B) = \inf\{d(a, b) : a \in A,\ b \in B\}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $\overline{A} \cap \overline{B} = \emptyset$, then $d(A, B) > 0$. \item If $d(A, B) > 0$, then there exist open sets $U$ and $V$ such that $A \subset U$, $B \subset V$, $U \cap V = \emptyset$. \item $d(A, B) = 0$ if, and only if, there exists a sequence of points $\{x_n\}$ in $A$ converging to a point in $B$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Let $X$ be a set and let $(Y, \tau)$ be a topological space. Let $g : X \to Y$ be a given map. Define $\tau' = \{U \subset X : U = g^{-1}(V) \text{ for some } V \in \tau\}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\tau'$ defines a topology on $X$. \item $\tau'$ defines a topology on $X$ only if $g$ is onto. \item Let $g$ be onto. Define the equivalence relation $x \sim y$ if, and only if, $g(x) = g(y)$. Then the quotient space of $X$ with respect to this relation, with the topology inherited from $\tau'$, is homeomorphic to $(Y, \tau)$. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Find pairs of homeomorphic sets from the following:\\ $A = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : xy = 0\}$; \quad $B = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x + y \ge 0,\ xy = 0\}$;\\ $C = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : xy = 1\}$; \quad $D = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x + y \ge 0,\ xy = 1\}$.
  24. 3.4 Let $(X, \tau)$ be a topological space. A map $f : X \to \R$ is said to be \emph{lower semi-continuous} if for every $\alpha \in \R$, the set $f^{-1}(]-\infty, \alpha])$ is closed in $X$. It is said to be \emph{upper semi-continuous} if, for every $\alpha \in \R$, the set $f^{-1}([\alpha, \infty[)$ is closed in $X$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $\{f_n\}$ is a sequence of lower semi-continuous real valued functions on $X$, then $f = \sup_n f_n$ is also lower semi-continuous. \item Every continuous real valued function on $X$ is lower semi-continuous. \item If a real valued function is both upper and lower semi-continuous, then it is continuous. \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Let $S = \{A \in \M_n(\R) : \tr(A) = 0\}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $S$ is nowhere dense in $\M_n(\R)$. \item $S$ is connected in $\M_n(\R)$. \item $S$ is compact in $\M_n(\R)$. \end{enumerate}
  26. 3.6 Let $S$ be the set of all symmetric non-negative definite matrices (see Question 1.8) in $\M_n(\R)$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $S$ is closed in $\M_n(\R)$. \item $S$ is connected in $\M_n(\R)$. \item $S$ is compact in $\M_n(\R)$. \end{enumerate}
  27. 3.7 Which of the following sets are compact in $\M_n(\R)$? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The set of all upper triangular matrices all of whose eigenvalues satisfy $|\lambda| \le 2$. \item The set of all real symmetric matrices all of whose eigenvalues satisfy $|\lambda| \le 2$. \item The set of all diagonalizable matrices all of whose eigenvalues satisfy $|\lambda| \le 2$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Let $X$ be the set of all real sequences. Consider the subset \[ S = \left\{x = (x_n) \in X : \begin{array}{l}x_n \in \Q \text{ for all } n,\\ x_n = 0, \text{ except for a finite number of } n\end{array}\right\}. \] Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $S$ is dense in $\ell_1$, the space of absolutely summable sequences, provided with the metric $d_1(x, y) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_n - y_n|$. \item $S$ is dense in $\ell_2$, the space of square summable sequences, provided with the metric $d_2(x, y) = \left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_n - y_n|^2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$. \item $S$ is dense in $\ell_\infty$, the space of bounded sequences, provided with the metric $d_\infty(x, y) = \sup_n \{|x_n - y_n|\}$. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item There exists a continuous function $f : \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : 2x^2 + 3y^2 = 1\} \to \R$ which is one-one. \item There exists a continuous function $f : ]-1, 1[ \to ]-1, 1]$ which is one-one and onto. \item There exists a continuous function $f : \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : y^2 = 4x\} \to \R$ which is one-one. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Let $f : ]0, \infty[ \to ]0, \infty[$ be such that $|f(x) - f(y)| \le \frac{1}{2}|x - y|$ for all $x$ and $y$. Then $f$ has a fixed point. \item Let $f : [-1, 1] \to [-1, 1]$ be continuous. Then $f$ has a fixed point. \item Let $f : \R \to \R$ be continuous and periodic with period $T > 0$. Then there exists a point $x_0 \in \R$ such that $f(x_0) = f\left(x_0 + \frac{T}{2}\right)$. \end{enumerate}
  31. 4.1 Find all the solutions $(\lambda, u)$, $u \not\equiv 0$, of the problem: \[ u'' + \lambda u = 0 \text{ in } ]0, 1[,\qquad u(0) = 0 = u'(1). \]
  32. 4.2 Find the constant $c$ such that the following problem has a solution: \[ -u'' = c \text{ in } ]a, b[,\qquad u'(a) = -1,\quad u'(b) = 1. \]
  33. 4.3 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-(3x^2 + 2\sqrt{2}xy + 3y^2)}\,dxdy$.
  34. 4.4 Find the stationary function $y = y(x)$ of the integral $\int_0^4 [xy' - (y')^2]\,dx$ satisfying the conditions $y(0) = 0$ and $y(4) = 3$.
  35. 4.5 Let $L(y)$ denote the Laplace transform of a function $y = y(x)$. If $y$ and $y'$ are bounded, express $L(y'')$ in terms of $L(y)$, $y$ and $y'$.
  36. 4.6 Find the singular points of the differential equation $x^3(x - 1)y'' - 2(x - 1)y' + 3xy = 0$ and state whether they are regular singular points or irregular singular points.
  37. 4.7 Let $(\lambda_1, y_1)$ and $(\lambda_2, y_2)$ be two solutions of the problem \[ (p(x)y'(x))' + \lambda q(x)y(x) = 0 \text{ in } ]a, b[,\qquad y(a) = 0 = y(b) \] where $p$ and $q$ are positive and continuous functions on $]a, b[$. If $\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2$, evaluate $\int_a^b q(x)y_1(x)y_2(x)\,dx$.
  38. 4.8 Solve: $xy'' - y' = 3x^2$.
  39. 4.9 Let $f \in \mathcal{C}[a, b]$. Write down Simpson's rule to approximate $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ using the points $x = a$, $x = (a + b)/2$ and $x = b$.
  40. 4.10 What is the highest value of $n$ such that Simpson's rule (see Question 4.9 above) gives the exact value of the integral of $f$ on $[a, b]$ when $f$ is a polynomial of degree less than, or equal to, $n$?
  41. 5.1 Let $m > n$. In how many ways can we seat $m$ men and $n$ women in a row for a photograph if no two women are to be seated adjacent to each other?
  42. 5.2 Let $n \in \N$ be fixed. For $r \le n$, let $C_r$ denote the usual binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{r}$ which gives the number of ways of choosing $r$ objects from a given set of $n$ objects. Evaluate: \[ C_0 + 4C_1 + 7C_2 + \dots + (3n + 1)C_n. \]
  43. 5.3 Let $A =$ the set of all sequences of real numbers, $B =$ the set of all sequences of positive real numbers, $C = \mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ and $D = \R$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item All the four sets have the same cardinality. \item $A$ and $B$ have the same cardinality. \item $A, B$ and $D$ have the same cardinality, which is different from that of $C$. \end{enumerate}
  44. 5.4 For a positive integer $n$, define \[ \Lambda(n) = \begin{cases} \log p, & \text{if } n = p^r,\ p \text{ a prime and } r \in \N, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases} \] Given a positive integer $N$, evaluate: $\displaystyle\sum_{d \mid N} \Lambda(d)$, where the sum ranges over all divisors $d$ of $N$.
  45. 5.5 Let $a, b$ and $c$ be real numbers. Evaluate: \[ \begin{vmatrix} b^2 c^2 & bc & b + c \\ c^2 a^2 & ca & c + a \\ a^2 b^2 & ab & a + b \end{vmatrix}. \]
  46. 5.6 Write down the equation (with leading coefficient equal to unity) whose roots are the squares of the roots of the equation $x^3 - 6x^2 + 10x - 3 = 0$.
  47. 5.7 Let $A = (0, 1)$ and $B = (1, 1)$ in the plane $\R^2$. Determine the length of the shortest path from $A$ to $B$ consisting of the line segments $AP$, $PQ$ and $QB$, where $P$ varies on the $x$-axis between the points $(0, 0)$ and $(1, 0)$ and $Q$ varies on the line $\{y = 3\}$ between the points $(0, 3)$ and $(1, 3)$.
  48. 5.8 Let $x_0 = a$, $x_1 = b$. If $x_{n+2} = \frac{1}{3}(x_n + 2x_{n+1})$, $n \ge 0$, find $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n$.
  49. 5.9 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item If $a, b$ and $c$ are the sides of a triangle, then $\dfrac{ab + bc + ca}{a^2 + b^2 + c^2} \ge \dfrac{1}{2}$. \item If $a, b$ and $c$ are the sides of a triangle, then $\dfrac{ab + bc + ca}{a^2 + b^2 + c^2} \le 1$. \item Both statements above are true for all triples $(a, b, c)$ of strictly positive real numbers. \end{enumerate}
  50. 5.10 Let $f \in \mathcal{C}[a, b]$. Assume that $\min_{x \in [a, b]} f(x) = m > 0$ and let $M = \max_{x \in [a, b]} f(x)$. Which of the following inequalities are true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\dfrac{1}{M}\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx + m\int_a^b \frac{1}{f(x)}\,dx \ge 2\sqrt{\frac{m}{M}}(b - a)$. \item $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx \int_a^b \frac{1}{f(x)}\,dx \ge (b - a)^2$. \item $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,dx \int_a^b \frac{1}{f(x)}\,dx \le (b - a)^2$. \end{enumerate}
