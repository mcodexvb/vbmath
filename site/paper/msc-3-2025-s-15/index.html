MMO-31(A3)

Computational Fluid Dynamics - I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1(a) Find the second order accurate finite difference analog of second order partial derivative using the values of the dependent variable at the $(i,j),(i-1,j),(i-2,j)$ and $(i-3,j)$ points. [5]
  2. 1(b) Find the truncation error and stabilty of the Schmidt method for the numerical solution of the one dimensional heat conduction equation $u_t=u_{xx}$. [2+3]
  3. 2 Solve the one dimensional heat conduction equation $u_t=u_{xx}$ subject to the initial and boundary conditions\par \[u(x,0)=\sin(\pi x),0\le x\le1\] \[u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0\] using (i) the Schmidt method, (ii) the Laasonen method, (iii) the Crank-Nicolson method and (iv) the Dufort-Frankel method for $h=1/3$ and $k=1/36$. Integrate upto two time levels. [2+2+4+2]
  4. 3(a) Find the stability condition of the Peaceman-Rachford Alternating Direction Implicit (ADI) method for the numerical solution of two dimensional heat conduction equation $u_t=u_{xx}+u_{yy}$. [5]
  5. 3(b) Discuss the stability of a method to integrate the heat equation\par \hspace*{2em}$u_t=u_{xx}\qquad0\le x\le1,\ t\ge0$\par \hspace*{2em}$u(0,t)=u(1,t)\qquad t\ge0$\par \hspace*{2em}$u(x,0)=$ given $\ \textit{\unreadable{handwritten: any continuously differentiable fu[nction]}}\ 0\le x\le1$\par based on a central difference approximation of $u_{xx}$ and the trapezoidal rule for the time integration. [5]
  6. 4 Find the solution of the initial value problem\par \[\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=0\] together with an initial condition\par \hspace*{2em}$u=0,\qquad x<0$\par \hspace*{2em}$\ =x/2,\qquad0\le x\le2$\par \hspace*{2em}$\ =2-x/2,\qquad2\le x\le4$\par \hspace*{2em}$\ =0,\qquad x>4$\par using\par(i) Lax-Wendroff method and\par(ii) leap-frog scheme,\par with $h=0.5,\ r=0.5$ where $r=k/h$. Integrate upto two time steps. [5+5]
  7. 5 Derive second order finite difference scheme which is valid for internal node whose four neighbouring nodes are inside the domain for the numerical solution of the elliptic partial diffrential equation $A\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+C\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}+D\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+E\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+Fu=G(x,y)$. Also derive the modified finite difference scheme when the mesh lengths on all four sides of the nodal point are different. [4+6]
  8. 6 Solve the partial differential equation\par \[\nabla^2u=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=x^2-1,\ |x|\le1,|y|\le1\] $u=0$, on the boundary of the square.\par Write the five point and the nine point difference formulation with mesh spacing $h$ in both the directions. Solve the difference equations with for $h=1/2$. [10]
