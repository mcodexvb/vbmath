CC-13

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2020
  1. 1(a) If $\lim_{z\to\alpha}f(z)=l$, then show that $\lim_{z\to\alpha}\Re\{f(z)\}=\Re\{l\}$. [3]
  2. 1(b) Prove that the limit of a function of complex variable when exists is unique. [4]
  3. 1(c) If $\lim_{z\to\alpha}f(z)=l\ne0$, then show that there exists a deleted neighbourhood of $\alpha$ in which $f(z)\ne0$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Show that if the functions $f(z)$ and $g(z)$ are continuous at $z=\alpha$, then $f(z).g(z)$ is also continuous there. [4]
  5. 2(b) If $f(z)$ is continuous in a bounded closed region $D$, then show that it is bounded in $D$. [6]
  6. 3(a) If $f(z)$ is differentiable at $\alpha$, then show that it is continuous at $\alpha$. Show by an example converse is not always true. [3]
  7. 3(b) Show that for the following function C-R equations are satisfied at the origin but $f'(0)$ does not exist: \[\begin{aligned} f(z)&=\frac{xy^2(x+iy)}{x^2+y^4},\quad\text{for }z\ne0\\ &=0,\quad\text{for }z=0. \end{aligned}\] [4]
  8. 3(c) If the functions $f(z)=u+iv$ and $\overline{f(z)}=u-iv$ are both analytic in a region $R$, then show that $f(z)$ is constant in $R$. [3]
  9. 4(a) Define a harmonic function. Find a harmonic conjugate $v(x,y)$ when \[u(x,y)=\sinh x\sin y.\] [4]
  10. 4(b) Show that if $\lim_{n\to\infty}z_n=z$ then $\lim_{n\to\infty}|z_n|=|z|$. [3]
  11. 4(c) Prove that any absolutely convergent series is convergent. [3]
  12. 5(a) Prove that cross ratio remains invariant under any Bilinear Transformation. [2]
  13. 5(b) Show that the equation $\left|\frac{z-\alpha}{z-\beta}\right|=k$ ($k\ne1$) represents a circle with respect to which $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are inverse points. [4]
  14. 5(c) Find the stereographic projection of a circle in the complex plane whose equation is given as $x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0$. [4]
  15. 1(a) Define a metric space. Give an example of it. [4]
  16. 1(b) For any four points $x,y,z,w$ in a metric space $(X,\rho)$, prove that \[|\rho(x,y)-\rho(z,w)|\le\rho(x,z)+\rho(y,w).\] [3]
  17. 1(c) If $\rho(x,y)$ is a metric for a set $X$, then show that $3\rho(x,y)$ is also a metric for $X$. [3]
  18. 2(a) Let $(X,\rho)$ be a metric space. Then show that each open sphere in $X$ is an open set. [4]
  19. 2(b) Show that arbitrary union of open sets is open in a metric space. [3]
  20. 2(c) Is the arbitrary intersection of open sets open? Give reason. [3]
  21. 3(a) Define a Cauchy sequence in a metric space. [2]
  22. 3(b) Show that if a sequence $\{x_n\}$ converges, then it is a Cauchy sequence. [4]
  23. 3(c) Give an example of a Cauchy sequence in a metric space. [4]
  24. 4(a) Define a complete metric space. [2]
  25. 4(b) Prove that $C[a,b]$ is a complete metric space. [6]
  26. 4(c) Give an example of an incomplete metric space. [2]
  27. 5(a) Let $X$ be a complete metric space and let $Y$ be a subset of $X$. Then show that $Y$, considered as a metric space is complete if and only if it is closed. [6]
  28. 5(b) Let $X=(0,\frac14)$ be a metric space with the metric of $R$. Let $T:X\longrightarrow X$ be given by $Tx=x^3$. Show that $T$ is a contraction mapping. [4]
