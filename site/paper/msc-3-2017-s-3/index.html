MMC-33

Fluid Mechanics
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1(a) Derive the equation of continuity by both the Eulerian and Lagrangian methods. Write the Eulerian form of the equation of continuity in cylindrical and spherical polar co-ordinate systems. [2+2+1]
  2. 1(b) Define stream line, path line, vortex line, streak line, vortex filament. [5]
  3. 2(a) Derive Euler's equation of motion and hence deduce Bernoulli's equation for steady flow of an incompressible fluid in the absence of body forces. [3+2]
  4. 2(b) Show that $\varphi=xf(r)$ is a possible form for the velocity potential of an incompressible liquid motion. Given the liquid speed $q\to0$ as $r\to\infty$, deduce that the surfaces of constant speed are $\left(r^2+3x^2\right)r^{-8}=$constant. [5]
  5. 3(a) Stating the physical significance of the energy equation, derive the energy equation. [2+4]
  6. 3(b) If the velocity field $u(x,y)=\dfrac{B(x^2-y^2)}{(x^2+y^2)^2},\ v(x,y)=\dfrac{2Bxy}{(x^2+y^2)^2},\ w(x,y)=0$ satisfies the equations of motion for inviscid incompressible flow, then determine the pressure associated with this velocity field, B being a constant. [4]
  7. 4(a) State and prove the theorem of Blasius. [1+4]
  8. 4(b) In the region bounded by a fixed quadrantal arc and its radii, deduce the motion due to a source and an equal sink situated at the ends of one of the bounding radii. Show that the stream line leaving either end at an angle $\alpha$ with the radius is $r^2\sin(\alpha+\theta)=a^2\sin(\alpha-\theta)$. [5]
  9. 5(a) State and prove Kelvin's circulation theorem. Hence deduce the permanence of irrotational motion. [1+2+1]
  10. 5(b) If $\Sigma$ be the boundary of a large spherical surface of radius R, containing fluid in motion and also enclosing one or more closed surfaces, then the mean value of $\varphi$ on $\Sigma$ is of the form $\bar\varphi=\dfrac MR+c$ where $M,c$ are constants, provided that the fluid extends to infinity and is at rest there. [3]
  11. 5(c) The circle $(x-a)^2+y^2=a^2$ is placed in an oncoming wind of velocity $U$ and there is a circulation $2\pi k$. Find the complex potential and show that the moment about the origin is $2\pi kpaU$. \par\hfill P.T.O. [3]
  12. 6(a) Derive the Navier-Stokes equation of motion for an incompressible fluid. [5]
  13. 6(b) Determine fluid velocity of an incompressible fluid flowing steadily, without body forces, through an infinite circular pipe of radius $a$. Show that the coefficient of friction can be given by $16/\mathrm{Re}$ where $\mathrm{Re}=2ap\,u_a/\mu$ is the Reynolds number with $\rho$, $u_a$ and $\mu$ denoting the density of the fluid, average velocity and dynamical co-efficient of viscosity, respectively. [3+2]
