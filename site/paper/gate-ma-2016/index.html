  1. 1 An apple costs Rs.~10. An onion costs Rs.~8. Select the most suitable sentence with respect to grammar and usage. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*)] \item The price of an apple is greater than an onion. \item The price of an apple is more than onion. \item The price of an apple is greater than that of an onion. \item Apples are more costlier than onions. \end{enumerate}
