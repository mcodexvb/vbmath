MMO-41(P1)

Advanced Functional Analysis-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) Define an orthonormal set in a Hilbert space $H$. If $\{e_k\}$ be an orthonormal sequence in $H$ then prove that for every $x\in H,\ \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}|\langle x,e_k\rangle|^2\le\|x\|^2.$ [4]
  2. 1(b) Prove that every Hilbert space is reflexive. [3]
  3. 1(c) When is an orthonormal set of vectors in a Hilbert space said to be complete? If $\{e_i\}$ be an orthonormal sequence in a Hilbert space $H$ then prove that for each $x\in H,\ x=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\langle x,e_i\rangle>e_i.$ [3]
  4. 2(a) Define sesquilinear form in a Hilbert space. Show that inner product in a Hilbert space is a bounded sesquilinear form. [3]
  5. 2(b) State and prove Riesz representation theorem for a sesquilinear form in a Hilbert space. [4]
  6. 2(c) If $T$ is a normal operator defined on a Hilbert space $H$, Show that $Tx=\lambda x$ iff $T^*x=\bar\lambda x\ \ \forall x\in H$ and $\forall\lambda.$ [3]
  7. 3(a) Defined unitary operator on a Hilbert space $H$. Prove that a bounded linear operator $T$ on $H$ is unitary if $T$ is surjective and isometric. [3]
  8. 3(b) Prove that a continuous linear operator $T$ on a Hilbert space is unitary iff $\{T(e_i)\}$ is a complete orthonormal set whenever $\{e_i\}$ is a complete orthonormal set. [4]
  9. 3(c) Prove that a continuous linear operator $T:H\to H$ where $H$ is a Hilbert space is normal iff $\|T^*x\|=\|Tx\|\ \ \forall x\in H.$ [2]
  10. 3(d) Let $T$ be a bounded linear operator defined on a Hilbert space. Show that $T$ is normal iff its red and imagenary parts commute. [1]
  11. 4(a) Define Projection operator on a Hilbert space $H$ and show that a bounded linear operator $T:H\to H$ is a projection operator iff $T$ is self-adjoint and idempotent. [4]
  12. 4(b) Let $T:H\to H$ be a bounded self-adjoint linear operator on a complex Hilbert space $H$. Show that a number $\lambda$ belongs to the resolvent set $\rho(T)$ of $T$ iff $\exists c>0$ such that for every $x\in H,\ \|T_\lambda x\|\ge c\|x\|.$ [4]
  13. 4(c) Let $X$ be a normed linear space and $T\in B(X,X)$ be invertible. Show that $\sigma\left(T^{-1}\right)=\left\{k:\dfrac1k\in\sigma(T)\right\}$, symbols bear their usual meaning. \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}( 2 )\end{center} [2]
  14. 5(a) Prove that spectrum of a bounded linear operator on a complex Banach space is non-empty. [4]
  15. 5(b) Define compact linear operator. Let $\{Tn\}$ be a sequence of compact linear operators defined on a normed linear space to a Banach space $Y$. If $\|Tn-T\|\to0$ then prove that $T$ is a compact linear operator where $\|\ \|$ is the norm on $B(X,Y)$. [4]
  16. 5(c) Let $T:H\to H$ be a self-adjoint operator where $H$ is a complex Hilbert space. Show that eigen vectors corresponding to different eigen values of $T$ are orthogonal. [2]
  17. 6(a) Define Banach algebra and give an example for the same. [3]
  18. 6(b) Let $A$ be a complex Banach algebra with identity $e$. If $x\in A$ satisfies $\|x\|<1$ then prove that $e-x$ is invertible and $(e-x)^{-1}=e+\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x^i.$ [3]
  19. 6(c) Let $W$ be the set of all self-adjoint operators defined on a Hilbert space $H$. For $T_1,T_2\in W$, define $T_1\le T_2$ if $\langle T_1x,x\rangle\le\langle T_2x,x\rangle\ \ \forall x\in H$. Prove that $\le$ is a partial order relation in $W$. [2]
  20. 6(c) Give an example to show that the spectrum $\sigma(T)$ of a bounded linear operator on a real Banach space is empty. [2]
