MMO-41(A9)

Lie Group of Transformations and Partial Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1 Derive infinitesimal generators for Lie group of point transformations admitted by the Burger equation in one space dimension $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\nu\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=0$, $\nu$ being a parameter. Show that some of admitted symmetry groups generated by infinitesimal generators derived here are translations and scaling. Derive rule of transformation corresponding to rest infinitesimal generators. Find similarity variables and reduced equation corresponding to infinitesimal generators of translational symmetry groups together. \begin{center}\textbf{OR}\end{center} Show that heat equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}-\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=0$ with the initial condition $u(x,0)=\delta(x)$ (Dirac delta function) and boundary conditions $u(\pm\infty,t)=0\ (t>0)$ admits three parameter Lie group of point transformations. Find infinitesimal generators. Hence obtain the fundamental solution to the initial-boundary value problem mentioned above. [7+6]
  2. 2 Derive expressions for $\phi_{u_m}^{(1)x_i},\ i,m=1,2;\ \phi_{u_m}^{(2)x_ix_j},\ i,j,m=1,2$ in the prolongation of the infinitesimal generator $\hat X=\xi_i(\bar x,u)\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x_i}+\phi_{u_m}(\bar x,u_m)\dfrac{\partial}{\partial u_m}.$ [9]
  3. 3 A partial differential equation (PDE) $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x_{k_1}\partial x_{k_2}}(\bar x)=f\left(\bar x,u,\bar\partial u,\bar\partial^2u\right),(f(.)$ does not contain $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x_{k_1}\partial x_{k_2}}\Big)$ is linear, admits a Lie group of point transformations with infinitesimal generator $\hat X=\xi_i(\bar x,u)\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x_i}+\phi(\bar x,u)\dfrac{\partial}{\partial u}.$ Then show that $\xi_i(\bar x,u)$ are free from $u$ and $\phi(\bar x,u)$ is linear in $u$. [9]
  4. 4 Derive determining equations for functions $\tau,\xi,\phi$ involved in the infinitesimal generator for the Lie group of point transformations admitted by the nonlinear heat condition equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\left(K(u)\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)_x$. Find $\tau,\xi,\phi$ for $K(u)=\lambda e^{\gamma u}.$ \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}( 2 )\end{center} [3+6]
  5. 5 Verify whether the nonlinear wave equation $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=A^2(u+B)^4\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$ admits a Lie group of point transformations generated by $\hat X=x^2\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}+x(u+B)\dfrac{\partial}{\partial u}.$ If yes, find similarity variables and reduced equation in terms of new variables. [9]
  6. 6 Show that the KdV equation in one space dimension remains invariant under translations in space-time variables. Hence find corresponding group invariant solution which is localized in space (soliton). [3+6]
