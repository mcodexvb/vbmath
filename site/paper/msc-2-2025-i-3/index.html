MMC-41

Nonlinear Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1 Find the period of the point $\frac18(5+\sqrt5)$ for the map $f(x)=4x(1-x),\ x\in[0,1]$. Also determine its stability. [5]
  2. 2(a) Consider the system $\dot x=p(y)+x^n,\ \dot y=q(x)$, where $p,q\in C^1$, and $n\in\mathbb N$. Derive the sufficient condition for $n$ so that the system has no periodic solution. [2.5]
  3. 2(b) Let $\dot x=-y+(x^2+y^2-1)x,\ \dot y=x+(x^2+y^2-1)y$ has no closed orbits inside the circle with center at $(0,0)$ and radius $\frac1{\sqrt2}$. [2.5]
  4. 3 Using Poincar\'e-Bendixson theorem show that the system $\dot x=x-y-(x^2+2y^2)x\ ,\ \dot y=x+y-(x^2+2y^2)y$ has a limit cycle. [5]
