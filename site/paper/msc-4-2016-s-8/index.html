MMO-41

Advanced Functional Analysis-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2016
  1. 1(a) Prove that for any nonempty subset M of a Hilbert space H, the span of M is dense in H iff $M^\perp=\{\theta\}$ where $M^\perp$ denotes the orthogonal complement of M. [3]
  2. 1(b) Show that every orthonormal subset of a separable Hilbert space is countable. [3]
  3. 1(c) Show that every Hilbert space is reflexive. [4]
  4. 2(a) When is an orthonormal set of vectors in a Hilbert space said to be complete? If $\{e_i\}$ be an orthonormal sequence in a Hilbert space H then prove that for each $x\in H$,\par \hspace*{1em}(i) $x=\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\langle x,e_j\rangle e_j$\qquad(ii) $\|x\|^2=\left|\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\langle x,e_j\rangle\right|^2$ [5]
  5. 2(b) Let $T_1$ and $T_2$ be two bounded linear operators defined on a Hilbert space $H_1$ to another Hilbert space $H_2$. Then prove the following:\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace $(T_1+T_2)^*=T_1^*+T_2^*$\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace $\|T_1^*T_1\|=\|T_1T_1^*\|=\|T_1\|^2$\par \hspace*{1em}(iii)\enspace $(T_1^*)^*=T_1$\par where $T_1^*$ and $T_2^*$ denote the adjoint of $T_1$ and $T_2$ respectively. [5]
  6. 3(a) Define a Projection operator over a Hilbert space H and show that a bounded linear operator $T:H\to H$ is a projection operator iff T is self-adjoint and idempotent. [5]
  7. 3(b) If $P:H\to H$ be a projection operator then prove that\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace $\langle Px,x\rangle=\|Px\|^2\quad\forall x\in H.$\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace $\|P\|\le1$ and $\|P\|=1$ if $P(H)\ne\{\theta\}$. [3]
  8. 3(c) Let f be a bounded sesquilinear form defined on a normed linear space then prove that \[\|f\|=\sup_{\substack{\|x\|=1\\\|y\|=1}}|f(x,y)|.\] [2]
  9. 4(a) Define normal operator on a Hilbert space. Prove that the set of all normal operators defined on a Hilbert space H is a closed subset of $B(H,H)$ where $B(H,H)$ is the set of all bounded linear operators defined on H. [4]
  10. 4(b) Let T be a bounded linear operator defined on a Hilbert space. Show that T is normal iff its real and imaginary parts commute, \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}[ 2 ]\end{center} [2]
  11. 4(c) Prove that a continuous linear operator T on a Hilbert space is unitary iff $\{T(e_i)\}$ is a complete orthonormal set whenever $\{e_i\}$ is a complete orthonormal set. [4]
  12. 5(a) Let $T:H\to H$ be a bounded self-adjoint linear operator on a complex Hilbert space H. Show that a number $\lambda$ belongs to the resolvent set $\rho(T)$ of T iff $\exists C>0$ such that for every $x\in H,\ \|T_\lambda x\|\ge C\|x\|.$ [4]
  13. 5(b) Let $T:H\to H$ be a self-adjoint operator where H is a Hilbert space. Show that spectrum $\sigma(T)$ lies in the closed interval $[m,M]$ where\par $m=\inf\limits_{\|x\|=1}\langle Tx,x\rangle$ and $M=\sup\limits_{\|x\|=1}\langle Tx,x\rangle.$ [4]
  14. 5(c) Let X be a normed linear space and $T\in B(X,X)$ be invertible. Show that $\sigma\left(T^{-1}\right)=\left\{k:\dfrac1k\in\sigma(T)\right\}.$ [2]
  15. 6(a) Define Banach algebra and give an example for the same. [3]
  16. 6(b) Let A be a complex Banach algebra with identity e. If $x\in A$ satisfies $\|x\|<1$ then prove that $e-x$ is invertible and $(e-x)^{-1}=e+\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x^i.$ [4]
  17. 6(c) Define unitary operator over a Hilbert space H. Prove that a bounded linear operator T over H is unitary if T is isometric and subjective. [3]
