MMO-31(A1)

Biomathematics-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1 Develop a logistic model for a single species non-age structured population. Show that the population growth curve approaches saturation limit as time advances largely and it has a point of inflexion when half the final population size is attained. Find also the time at which the point of inflexion occurs.
  2. 2 Investigate a two-species ecosystem in which both the species compete for the same limited source of nutrients and show that the extinction of one species would lead to survival of the other. Write down your observation from this study by mentioning the statement of Gauss's principle.
  3. 3 Develop a stochastic model by considering a linear birth-death-immigration-emigration process in a single species non-age structured population. If one disregards both immigration and emigration, find the probability of both dying and survival for distinct birth and death rates.
  4. 4 Consider a stochastic epidemic model consisting of a $n$ number of susceptibles and one infective in the absence of any removed individuals. Find the probability of having $m$ susceptibles in the system at time $t$ and hence obtain the mean of the distribution.
  5. 5 Formulate a deterministic general epidemic model. Carry out the stability analysis of the system in the environment of infected and removed individuals in the case of inclusion of loss of immunity and of immigration or emigration separately.
  6. 6 Develop an SIR endemic disease model. Discuss the situations (i) in the absence of disease related deaths and equal natural birth and death rates, (ii) when natural birth rate is greater than the death rate. Hence comment on the stability of equilibrium positions for both the situations.
