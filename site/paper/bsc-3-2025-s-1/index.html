MJMA-5

Analysis-III
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1(a) Discuss the convergence of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1n$. [[5]]
  2. 1(b) Examine the convergence of the series $\frac13+\frac12+(\frac13)^2+(\frac12)^2+(\frac13)^3+(\frac12)^3+\cdots$. [[5]]
  3. 2(a) Prove or disprove: There exists a convergent series $\sum a_n^2$ such that $\sum a_n$ is divergent. [[4]]
  4. 2(b) What is Cauchy's root test for positive series? Use it to test the convergence of the series $\sum n^3e^{-n}$. [[2+4]]
  5. 3(a) Discuss the convergence and absolute convergence of the series $1-\frac1{2^2}+\frac1{3^2}+\frac1{4^2}-\frac1{5^2}-\frac1{6^2}$. [[4]]
  6. 3(b) State Leibnitz's test and Dirichlet's test for infinite series. Hence show that the series $1-\frac12+\frac{1\cdot3}{2\cdot4}-\frac{1\cdot3\cdot5}{2\cdot4\cdot6}+\cdots$ is convergent. [[2+2+2]]
  7. 4(a) Define the continuity of a function at a point using $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations. Show that a function $f:[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ is continuous at $c\in[a,b]\subset\mathbb R$ if for every sequence $\{x_n\}$ in $[a,b]$ converging to $c$, the sequence $\{f(x_n)\}$ converges to $f(c)$. [[2+4]]
  8. 4(b) Show that the function $f:\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}\cos\frac1x&\text{if }x\ne0\\0&\text{if }x=0\end{cases}$ is not continuous at $0$. [[4]]
  9. 5(a) Show that the function $f$ defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\text{ is rational}\\1-x&\text{if }x\text{ is irrational}\end{cases}$ is continuous only at $x=\frac12$. [[5]]
  10. 5(b) State and prove intermediate value theorem. [[1+4]]
  11. 6(a) Let $f:[0,1]\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ be continuous on $[0,1]$ and let $f$ assume only rational values. If $f(\frac13)=3$, find the value of $f(\frac1\pi)$. [[4]]
  12. 6(b) If $f$ is uniformly continuous on an interval $I$, show that $f$ is continuous on $I$. Is the converse true? Justify your answer. [[4+2]]
  13. 7(a) Prove or disprove: A bounded function $f:[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ is necessarily a function of bounded variation on $[a,b]$. [[5]]
  14. 7(b) Determine whether the function $f:[0,2]\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ defined by $f(x)=x\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}$ is a function of bounded variation on $[0,2]$. [[5]]
  15. 8(a) Let $f:[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ be a bounded function on $[a,b]$ and $P$ be a partition of $[a,b]$. Prove that $L(P,f)\le U(P,f)$. [[4]]
  16. 8(b) When is a function $f$ called Riemann integrable over $[a,b]$? If $f(x)=[x]$, $x\in[1,26]$, examine the Riemann integrability of $f$. [[2+4]]
  17. 9(a) What is fundamental theorem of integral calculus? Use this to show that the function $f$ defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}3x^2\cos\frac{\pi}{x^2}+2\pi\sin\frac{\pi}{x^2}&\text{if }x\ne0\\0&\text{if }x=0\end{cases}$ is Riemann integrable on $[-2,2]$ and find the value of $\int_{-2}^2 f$. [[2+2+2]]
  18. 9(b) Find the lower and upper Riemann integrals of bounded function $f(x)=x$ on a closed and bounded interval $[a,b]$, $a>0$ and determine if $f$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$. [[4]]
  19. 10(a) State the first mean value theorem and use it to show that \[\frac{\pi}{6}\le\int_0^{1/2}\frac1{\sqrt{(1-x^2)(1-k^2x^2)}}\le\frac{\pi}{6}\cdot\frac1{\sqrt{1-\frac{k^2}{4}}},\quad\text{where }k^2<1.\] [[2+4]]
  20. 10(b) Evaluate the limit $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}[\frac1{n+1}+\frac1{n+2}+\cdots+\frac1{2n}]$ as an integral with proper justification. [[4]]
  21. 11(a) If $a$ is the only point of infinite discontinuity of $f$ in $[a,b]$, then discuss the convergence of the improper integral $\int_a^b f(x)dx$. [[3]]
  22. 11(b) Examine the convergence of the improper integrals $\int_0^1\frac1{\sqrt{x}}$ and $\int_0^1\frac{x^{p-1}}{1-x}$. [[3+4]]
  23. 12(a) Examine the convergence of the improper integral $\int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx$. [[4]]
  24. 12(b) Show that the improper integral $\int_0^1 x^{m-1}(1-x)^{n-1}dx$ is convergent if and only if $m>0$ \& $n>0$. \fbox{\shortstack[l]{Use separate answer\\script for each unit}} [[6]]
