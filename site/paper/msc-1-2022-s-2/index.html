MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2022
  1. 1(a) Let $f(z)$ be a continuous function in a simply connected domain $D$ and $z_0$ be any arbitrary but fixed point in $D$. If $F(z)=\int_{z_0}^{z}f(w)dw$, where the integral is independent of the path so long as the path lies in $D$, then show that $F'(z)=f(z)$ for every $z$ in $D$. [3]
  2. 1(b) State and prove the maximum modulus theorem. [5]
  3. 1(c) Using maximum modulus theorem show that $f(z)=z^{10}+z^5-7$ has at least one zero in $|z|<2$. [2]
  4. 2(a) Using Taylor's theorem expand $f(z)=\frac1{z-1}$ in powers of $(z+1)$. [2]
  5. 2(b) State and prove Laurent's theorem. [5]
  6. 2(c) Expand $f(z)=\frac1{(z+2)(z-3)}$ in Laurent series in the region $2<|z|<3$. [3]
  7. 3(a) Show that if $f(z)=(z-\beta)^m\phi(z)$, where $\phi(z)$ is analytic at $\beta$ and $\phi(\beta)\ne0$ then $\beta$ is a zero of $f(z)$ of order $m$ and conversely. [4]
  8. 3(b) State and prove Riemann's theorem on removable singularity. [3]
  9. 3(c) If $f(z)$ has a pole at $\alpha$ of order $m$ then show that $|f(z)|\to\infty$ as $z\to\alpha$ in any manner. [3]
  10. 4(a) Determine the nature of the singularities of $\sec\frac1z$. [3]
  11. 4(b) Prove that the zeros of a non-constant analytic function are isolated points. [2]
  12. 4(c) State and prove Cauchy's residue theorem. Using this theorem evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{e^z}{2z-1}dz$. [3+2]
  13. 5(a) State and prove Rouche's theorem(Principle of Argument may be assumed). [3]
  14. 5(b) Show that the equation $e^z=3z^n$, where $n$ is a positive integer has $n$ roots inside the circle $|z|=1$. [3]
  15. 5(c) State and prove open mapping theorem. [4]
  16. 6(a) Prove that a function which is analytic every where including the point at infinity is a constant. [3]
  17. 6(b) Find $\mathit{Res}[f(z),\infty]$, where $f(z)=\frac1{z^2-1}$. [2]
  18. 6(c) Evaluate any one of the following by the method of contour integration: \begin{romanparts} \item $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos x\,dx}{x^2+a^2}$, $a<0$; \item $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{(a+b\cos\theta)^2}$, $a>b>0$. \end{romanparts} \begingroup\renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of values as indicated in the margin.} [5]
