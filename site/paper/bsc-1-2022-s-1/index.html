CCMA-2

Algebra I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2022
  1. 1(a) If $a,b,c$ are positive real numbers and $a+b+c=1$, show that $8abc\le(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)\le\frac8{27}$.
  2. 1(b) For any positive integer $n$, prove that $\frac{1.3.5\dots(2n+1)}{2.4.6\dots2n}<2\sqrt{n+1}$.
  3. 1(c) Find the maximum value of $a^4b^5$, where $a,b$ are positive real numbers satisfying $5a+4b=18$. [[4+3+3]]
  4. 2(a) Find the modulus and amplitude of $z=1+i\cot\theta$, where $0<\theta<\pi$ and express $z$ in polar form.
  5. 2(b) State the De Moivre's theorem. Use it to prove that $2i\sin73\alpha=u^{73}-\frac1{u^{73}}$, provided that $2i\sin\alpha=u-\frac1u$.
  6. 2(c) Find all the values of $(-i)^{\frac34}$. [[4+4+2]]
  7. 3(a) Express $x^4-3x^3+10x^2$ as a polynomial in $x-3$. Also find $f(x+3)$.
  8. 3(b) Construct a biquadratic equation with real coefficients, two of whose roots are $3i\pm\sqrt2$.
  9. 3(c) Solve the equation $x^4-5x^3-30x^2+40x+64=0$, provided that the roots are in geometric progression. [[3+3+4]]
  10. 4(a) What do you mean by a reciprocal equation? Solve the equation $6x^4+35x^3+32x^2+35x+6=0$.
  11. 4(b) Show that the special roots of the equation $x^{14}-1=0$ are the non-real roots of $x^7+1=0$.
  12. 4(c) Solve the equation $x^3+3x^2-3=0$ by Cardan's method. [[4+2+4]]
  13. 5(a) Find the solution(s), if there is any, of the system of linear equations $x+3y-5z=4$, $x+4y-8z=7$, $3x+7y-9z=6$ and check if the solution is unique.
  14. 5(b) When is an indexed set of vectors $\{v_1,v_2,\dots,v_k\}$ of $\mathbb R^n$ $(k<n)$ said to be linearly independent? Examine if the columns of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&4&-3&0\\-2&-7&5&1\\-4&-5&7&5\end{pmatrix}$ form a linearly independent set.
  15. 6(a) Find a respective basis for the null space and column space of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}3&-1&7&3&9\\-2&2&-2&7&5\\-5&9&3&3&4\\-2&6&6&3&7\end{pmatrix}$. Also find the rank of the matrix $A$.
  16. 6(b) Construct a $3\times3$ matrix $A$ and two non-zero vectors $b,c\in\mathbb R^3$ such that $b\notin\mathrm{Col}(A)$ but $c\in\mathrm{Nul}(A)$. [[6+4]]
  17. 7(a) Define eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a square matrix $A$. What do you mean by eigenspace of $A$ corresponding to an eigenvalue.
  18. 7(b) Prove that the eigenvectors $v_1,v_2,\dots,v_k$ corresponding to distinct eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\dots,\lambda_k$ of an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ are linearly independent. Does the result hold if $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\dots,\lambda_k$ are not all distinct? Justify.
  19. 8(a) Suppose that the matrix of a linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ has orthonormal columns. Show that $T$ preserves the lengths and orthogonality of vectors.
  20. 8(b) Use Gram-Schmidt process to obtain an orthonormal basis for the column space of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}-10&13&7&-11\\2&1&-5&3\\-6&3&13&-3\\16&-16&-2&5\\2&1&-5&-7\end{pmatrix}$. [[4+6]]
  21. 9(a) Let $\lambda$ be an eigenvalue of an $n\times n$ orthogonal matrix $A$. Show that $\frac1\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $A^T$. [[4+6]]
  22. 9(b) Diagonalize the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&13&8&4\\4&9&8&4\\8&6&12&8\\0&5&0&-4\end{pmatrix}$, if possible. [[4+6]]
