MMC-14

Ordinary Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2023
  1. 1(a) For the linear autonomous system \[ \frac{dx}{dt}=ax+by,\qquad\frac{dy}{dt}=cx+dy, \] where $a,b,c,$ and $d$ are real constants. \begin{romanparts} \item Show that if $a=d$, and $b$ and $c$ are of opposite sign, then the critical point $(0,0)$ of the given system is a spiral point. \item Show that if $a=d$, and either $b$ or $c=0$, then the critical point $(0,0)$ of the given system is a node. \item Show that if $a=-d$ and $ad>bc$, then the critical point $(0,0)$ of the given system is a center. \item Show that if $a=-d$ and $b$ and $c$ are of the same sign, then the critical point $(0,0)$ of the given system is a saddle point. \end{romanparts} [4]
  2. 1(b) Find the maximal interval of existence for the unique solution of the following systems. $x'(t)=x^2+\cos^2(t)$, $t>0$, $x(0)=0$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Apply Picard Iteration method to find first three approximation of the following IVP. $y'=x+y^2$, $y(0)=0$. Using Induction show that the solution tends to a unique solution. [3]
  4. 2(a) State and Prove Sturm's Seperation Theorem. [5]
  5. 2(b) Solve the following BVP \[ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=f(x),\quad y(0)=y(l)=0;\qquad 0<x<l, \] by constructing the Green's function. [5]
  6. 3(a) Show that the coefficient matrix $A$ can be expressed in terms of the Fundamental Matrix $\Phi(t)$ of the system $\dot{x}=Ax$. [5]
  7. 3(b) Consider a second-order homogeneous linear differential equation and write its adjoint equation. When is a differential equation called self-adjoint? Find the necessary and sufficient condition that a second-order linear differential equation $a_0(t)\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}+a_1(t)\frac{dx}{dt}+a_2(t)x=0$ be self-adjoint. [1+1+3]
  8. 4(a) If $N$ be a nilpotent matrix of order $k$. Then show that $e^{Nt}$ is a series containing finite terms only. Under which condition we can write $e^{A+B}=e^Ae^B$? [1+3+1]
  9. 4(b) Prove that for a Strum-Liouville problem the distinct eigen functions are orthogonal corresponding to distinct eigen values. [5]
  10. 5(a) Define a Sturm-Liouville Problem. Under which condition a Sturm-Liouville problem is called Regular and Singular? Find the eigen-values and the eigen-functions of the Sturm-Liouville problem $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\lambda y=0$, with $y(0)+y'(0)=0$, $y(1)+y'(1)=0$. [1+1+4]
  11. 5(b) With a suitable example that Lipchitz criteria is a weaker concept than Picard's condition for uniqueness of solutions of a first order IVP. [2]
  12. 5(c) Find the Natural Fundamental set of solutions of the differential equation $y''+4y=0$, with the initial time 0. [2]
  13. 6(a) Find the solution of the non-homogeneous system \[ \dot{x}=x+y+t,\qquad\dot{y}=-y+1 \] with the initial conditions $x(0)=1$, $y(0)=0$, using Fundamental Matrix. [5]
  14. 6(b) State Peano's Theorem for existence of first order IVP. Is the condition necessary? Justify your answer with a suitable example. [3]
  15. 6(c) Find the 'interval of definition' for the solution of the following IVP. \[ \frac{d^4y}{dt^4}+\frac1{t-4}\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{e^t}{2+t},\qquad y(0)=y'(0)=y''(0)=y'''(0)=0. \] \begingroup\renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of values as indicated in the margin.\par Notation and symbol have their usual meanings.} [2]
