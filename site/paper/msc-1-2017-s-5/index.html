MMC-13

Algebra-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2017
  1. 1(a) Prove that $\mathbb C^*\simeq\mathbb R^+\times S^1$.
  2. 1(b) Define internal direct product of groups. If a group $G$ is the internal direct product of its subgroups $H$ and $K$, then show that $G/H\simeq K$.
  3. 1(c) Let $G$ be a group and $a\in G$. Then show that $|C_l(a)|=[G:C(a)]$. Find the number of conjugates of $(1234)$ in $S_4$.
  4. 1(d) If a group $G$ is such that $[G:Z(G)]=n$, then prove that the conjugacy classes in $G$ have at most $n$ elements. [[2+3+3+2]]
  5. 2(a) State and prove the converse of the Lagrange's Theorem for finite abelian groups.
  6. 2(b) Let $p$ be a prime. Prove that every group of order $p^2$ is either cyclic or a direct product of two cyclic groups.
  7. 2(c) If $H$ is a $p$-subgroup of a finite group $G$, then show that $[N_G(H):H]\equiv[G:H](\bmod p)$. Hence or otherwise show that every subgroup or order $p^{n-1}$ of a group of order $p^n$ is normal. [[3+3+4]]
  8. 3(a) State and prove the Sylow's second theorem.
  9. 3(b) Show that every group of order 96 has a normal subgroup of order 32 or 16.
  10. 3(c) Let $N$ be a normal subgroup of a group $G$. If both $N$ and $G/N$ are solvable, then prove that $G$ is solvable. Also show that product of two solvable normal subgroups of a group is solvable. [[3+3+4]]
  11. 4(a) Let $S,T:U\to V$ be two nonzero linear transformations. If $\mathit{Im}\,S\cap\mathit{Im}\,T=\{\theta\}$, then prove that $\{S,T\}$ is linearly independent in $\mathcal L(U,V)$.
  12. 4(b) Define change of basis matrix. Let $V$ be a vector space. If $P$ is the change of basis matrix from the basis $\beta$ to the basis $\beta'$ of $V$, then show that $P[v]_\beta=[v]_{\beta'}$ for every $v\in V$.
  13. 4(c) Let $M=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$ and $T:M_2(\mathbb R)\to M_2(\mathbb R)$ be defined by $T(A)=MA$. Find the trace and the determinant of $T$. Is $T$ onto? \hfill\textbf{P.T.O.} [[3+3+4]]
  14. 5(a) Consider the basis $B=\{1,t,t^2\}$ of $P_2(\mathbb R)$. Find the dual basis $B^*$ of $B$. Express $\phi$ as a linear combination of $B^*$ where $\phi:P_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R$ is defined by $\phi(p(t))=\displaystyle\int_0^1p(t)dt$.
  15. 5(b) Let $U$ be the subspace of $\mathbb R^3$ generated by $\{(1,2,1),(2,3,1)\}$. Find $U^0$.
  16. 5(c) Let $T:U\to V$ be a linear transformation, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ be two bases of $U$ and $V$ respectively. Show that $[T]_\beta^\gamma=[T^*]_{\gamma^*}^{\beta^*}$, where $\beta^*$ and $\gamma^*$ are dual bases of $\beta$ and $\gamma$ respectively. [[3+3+4]]
  17. 6(a) Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space. Show that $\{\theta\}$ and $V$ are the only subspaces of $V$ which remain invariant under every linear transformation $T:V\to V$.
  18. 6(b) Give an example of a linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^4\to\mathbb R^4$ which has no (real) eigenvalues.
  19. 6(c) Let $T:V\to V$. be a linear transformation. If $\mathit{rank}\,T=k$, then show that $T$ has at most $k+1$ distinct eigenvalues.
  20. 6(d) Show that nonzero eigen vectors belonging to distinct eigenvalues of a linear transformation are linearly independent. [[2+2+3+3]]
