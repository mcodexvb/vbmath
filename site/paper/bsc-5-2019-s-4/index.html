Paper

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2019
  1. 1(a) Three different types of lorries $A$, $B$ and $C$ have been used to transport 60 tons solid and 35 tons liquid substance. $A$ type lorry can carry 7 tons solid and 3 tons liquid, $B$ type lorry can carry 6 tons solid and 2 tons liquid, $C$ type lorry can carry 3 tons solid and 4 tons liquid. The costs of transport are Rs. 500/-, Rs. 400/- and Rs. 450/- per lorry of $A$, $B$ and $C$ respectively. To find the minimum cost, formulate the linear programming problem mathematically. [4]
  2. 1(b) Solve the following LPP by graphical method: Maximize $z=6x_1+4x_2$ Subject to $7x_1+5x_2\le35$, $5x_1+7x_2\le35$, $4x_1+3x_2\ge12$, $3x_1+x_2\ge3$, And $x_1,x_2\ge0$. Clearly specify the corner points of the feasible region. [6]
  3. 2(a) In the following LPP Maximize $z=x_1+2x_2+3x_3-x_4$ Subject to $x_1+2x_2+3x_3=15$, $2x_1+x_2+5x_3=20$, $x_1+2x_2+x_3+x_4=10$ and $x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\ge0$, determine (i) the maximum number of possible basic solutions, (ii) the basic feasible solutions, (iii) the optimal basic feasible solution. [1+2+2]
  4. 2(b) Show that $x_1=5$, $x=0$, $x_3=-1$ is a basic solution of the system of equations $x_1+2x_2+x_3=4$, $2x_1+x_2+5x_3=5$. Find other basic solutions, if there be any. [5]
  5. 3 Solve by Charnes Big M-method, the following LPP, Minimize $z=4x_1+2x_2$ Subject to $3x_1+x_2\ge27$, $x_1+x_2\ge21$, $x_1+2x_2\ge30$, $x_1,x_2\ge0$. [10]
  6. 4 Solve the following transportation problem using North-West corner method and Vogel's Approximation Method (VAM) to determine the initial basic feasible solution. Determine the unique optimal solution and optimal cost for the initial basic feasible solution obtained by VAM: \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{cc|cccc|c}&&\multicolumn{4}{c|}{Destination}&$a_i$\\&&A&B&C&D&\\\hline\multirow{3}{*}{Source}&P&19&30&50&10&7\\&Q&70&30&40&60&9\\&R&40&8&70&20&18\\\hline&$b_j$&5&8&7&14&\end{tabular}\end{center} [10]
  7. 5(a) The Head of the department has five jobs $A,B,C,D,E$ and five sub-ordinates $V,W,X,Y,Z$. The number of hours each man would take to perform each job is as follows: \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{c|ccccc}&\multicolumn{5}{c}{Sub-ordinates}\\&V&M&X&Y&Z\\\hline A&3&5&10&15&8\\B&4&7&15&18&8\\C&8&12&20&20&12\\D&5&5&8&10&6\\E&10&10&15&25&10\end{tabular}\end{center} Determine how to allocate the jobs to minimize the total time. [5]
  8. 5(b) Reduce the following game into $2\times2$ game using dominance principle and then solve the game: \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{cc|ccccc}&&\multicolumn{5}{c}{Player B}\\&&$B_1$&$B_2$&$B_3$&$B_4$&$B_5$\\\hline\multirow{3}{*}{Player A}&$A_1$&2&2&1&$-2$&$-3$\\&$A_2$&4&3&4&$-2$&0\\&$A_3$&5&1&2&5&6\end{tabular}\end{center} [5]
  9. 6(a) Solve the game problem with the following pay off matrix by using graphical method: \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{cc|cccc}&&\multicolumn{4}{c}{Player B}\\&&$B_1$&$B_2$&$B_3$&$B_4$\\\hline\multirow{2}{*}{Player A}&$A_1$&$-1$&3&2&2\\&$A_2$&6&2&5&3\end{tabular}\end{center} [5]
  10. 6(b) Use algebraic method to solve the game problem with the following payoff matrix: \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{cc|ccc}&&\multicolumn{3}{c}{B}\\&&I&II&III\\\hline\multirow{3}{*}{A}&I&3&$-2$&4\\&II&$-1$&4&2\\&III&2&2&6\end{tabular}\end{center} [5]
  11. 7(a) In connection with statistics, explain the following: i) Population of a random variable, ii) Sample of size $n$ drawn from the population of a random variable, iii) Sampling distribution.
  12. 7(b) What is meant by an estimate of a population characteristic? Show that if $\mu_k$ exists, $m_k$ is a consistent estimator of $\mu_k$.
  13. 8(a) Show that $\frac{ns^2}{\sigma^2}$ has a $\chi^2$-distribution with $n-1$ degrees of freedom.
  14. 8(b) Show that $\sigma^2(s^2)=\frac1n\left\{\mu_4-\frac{n-3}{n-1}\sigma^4\right\}$.
  15. 9 Explain the method of maximum likelihood. Let $X_1,X_2,\dots,X_n$ be a random sample from the distribution with density function $f(x;\theta)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{\frac2\pi}e^{-\frac12(x-\theta)^2}&\text{if }0\le\theta\le x\\0&;\text{ elsewhere.}\end{cases}$ What is the Maximum Likelihood Estimator of $\theta$?
  16. 10 What is meant by a statistical hypothesis? Explain the test of significance. What are possible errors in decision? Explain the power of a test. State Neyman-Pearson theorem in connection with finding a best critical region. [5]
  17. 11 For a random variable $X$ about which the hypothesis $H_0:X\sim\text{Uniform}(0,1)$ against an alternative hypothesis $H_1:X\sim\text{Normal}(0,1)$ is to be tested. What is the most powerful test with significance level $\alpha=0.05$ based on one single observation of $X$? [5]
  18. 12 Let $X_1,X_2,\dots,X_n$ be a random sample from a $N(\mu,\sigma)$ population. What is the likelihood ratio test of significance of size $\alpha$ for testing the null hypothesis $H_0:\sigma^2=\sigma_0^2$ against an alternative hypothesis $H_1:\sigma^2\ne\sigma_0^2$? [5]
