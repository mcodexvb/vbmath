MMC-34

Calculus of Variations and Special Functions
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1(a) Show that the necessary condition for existence of extremals of the functional\par \[I[u(x,y)]=\iint_DF(x,y,u,u_x,u_y)dxdy\] is $F_u-\dfrac\partial{\partial x}(F_{u_x})-\dfrac\partial{\partial y}(F_{u_y})=0$, where $u(x,y)$ is continuous and has continuous second order partial derivatives in $D$ and is prescribed on the boundary $\partial D$. [5]
  2. 1(b) Find the extremals of the functional $V[y(x)]=\displaystyle\int_0^{\ln2}\left(e^{-x}y^{\prime2}-e^xy^2\right)dx$. [5]
  3. 2(a) Show that the geodesics on a sphere of radius $a$ are $\cot\theta=A\cos\phi+B\sin\phi$, where $A,B$ are arbitrary constants. [5]
  4. 2(b) Find the shortest distance between the circle $x^2+y^2=\unreadable{overprinted, reads 4}$ and the straight line $x+y=\unreadable{overprinted, reads 4}$. [5]
  5. 3(a) Find the closed plane curve of given length $L$ that encloses the largest area. [5]
  6. 3(b) Using Legendre condition, test for an extremum of the functional\par \[J[y(x)]=\int_0^{\frac\pi4}\left(y^2-y^{\prime2}+6y\sin2x\right)dx,\ y(0)=0,\ y\!\left(\frac\pi4\right)=1.\] [5]
  7. 1(a) Establish Rodrigue's formula\par \[P_n(x)=\frac1{2^nn!}\frac{d^n}{dx^n}(x^2-1)^n\] for Legendre polynomial. [4]
  8. 1(b) Prove the following recurrence relations for Legendre polynomial $(P_n(x))$\par (i) $nP_n=(2n-1)xP_{n-1}-(n-1)P_{n-2},n\ge2$,\par (ii) $nP_n=xP_n^\prime-P_{n-1}^\prime$,\par (iii) $(2n+1)P_n=P_{n+1}^\prime-P_{n-1}^\prime$. [2+2+2]
  9. 2(a) Solving Bessel's equation of order $n$ in series, find Bessel's function of the first kind of order $n$. [4]
  10. 2(b) Prove that $J_{-1/2}(x)=\sqrt{2/\pi x}\ \cos x$. [2]
  11. 2(c) Prove that $J_nJ_{-n}^\prime-J_n^\prime J_{-n}=-2\dfrac{\sin n\pi}{x\pi}$. [4]
  12. 3(a) Prove the following recurrence relation for Bessel's function $(J_n(x))$\par \[\frac d{dx}\left(x^nJ_n(x)\right)=x^nJ_{n-1}(x).\] [3]
  13. 3(b) Show that $\left(1-2xz+z^2\right)^{-1/2}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^nP_n(x),|x|\le1,|z|<1$. [3]
  14. 3(c) If $x>1$, show that $P_n(x)<P_{n+1}(x)$. [3]
  15. 3(d) Prove that $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\frac{J_n(z)}{z^n}=\frac1{2^n\Gamma(n+1)}$ where $n>-1$. [1]
