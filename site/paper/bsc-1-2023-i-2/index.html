MJMA-2

Analytical Geometry and Vector Calcus
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2023
  1. (Q1) If by the rotation of axes, the expression $ax^2+2hxy+by^2+2gx+2fy+c$ be changed to $a'x^2+2h'xy+b'y^2+2g'x+2f'y+c'$ then show that\\\centerline{(i) $a'+b'=a+b$ \qquad (ii) $a'b'-h'^2=ab-h^2$.}
  2. (Q2) What does the equation $x^2-3xy+3y^2+7x-18y+32=0$ become when the origin is moved to the point $(4,5)$ and axes are turned through an angle $45^\circ$.
  3. (Q3) The plane $x+2y+3z=6$ meets the co-ordinate axes in $A,B$ and $C$. Find the equation of the sphere $OABC$, $O$ being the origin and find out centre and radius of the sphere.
  4. (Q4) A sphere of constant radius $r$ passes through the origin $O$ and cuts the co-ordinate axes at $A$, $B$ and $C$. Prove that the locus of the foot of perpendicular from $O$ to the plane $ABC$ is $(x^2+y^2+z^2)^2\left(\frac1{x^2}+\frac1{y^2}+\frac1{z^2}\right)=4r^2$. \begin{center}Internal Exam of Vector Calculus\end{center} \begin{itemize}\item Answer all the questions; total marks is 9. All questions carry equal marks.\end{itemize}
  5. (1) Prove, using vector method, that the quadrilateral formed by joining the midpoints of the consecutive sides of a quadrilateral is a parallelogram.
  6. (2) State and prove the cosine law for triangles, using vector method.
  7. (3) Find the unit tangent $\vec T$, principal normal $\vec N$ and binormal $\vec B$ of the differentiable curve given by $\vec R(t)=3\cos t\,\hat i+3\sin t\,\hat j+4t\,\hat k$.
