DSEMA-4

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2025
  1. 1(a) Define Radioactive decay. A sample contains 1 gram of a radioactive isotope with a half-life of 10 days. How much of the isotope remains after 25 days and what is its activity if the decay constant is $\lambda=0.0693\,\text{days}^{-1}$?
  2. 1(b) The half-life of a radioactive isotope is 12 hours. If you start with a sample of 100 grams, how much remains after 36 hours?
  3. 2 Obtain the simple harmonic motion (SHM) for modelling a spring mass system. Convert the equation into a system of ordinary differential equations of the form $\dot X=AX$. Find the eigenvalues of the Matrix $A$ and state the nature of the fixed point. [1+2+2+1]
  4. 3 What is continuously compound interest? After a working life of careful saving and investing, your pension fund now totals \$400000. Your plan is to take out an investment that pays 2.0\% interest annually, continuously compounded and to withdraw \$25000 per year at a constant rate through the year. Assuming the interest rate does not change, how long do you expect the money to last? [1+5]
  5. 4(a) What restrictions are used to model the heat equation?
  6. 4(b) What is a wave equation? Explain the different boundary conditions may arise with the equation. [3+3]
  7. 5(a) Define Kirchhoff's voltage law, Ohm's law and Faraday's law.
  8. 5(b) A series LRC circuit has $L=0.1\,H$, $R=20\,\Omega$, $C=0.0001\,F$, initial charge $q(0)=0.01\,C$, initial current $\frac{dq}{dt}(0)=0\,A$ with no external voltage ($V(t)=0$). Find $q(t)$ and relate it to SHM. [3+3]
  9. 6 Write a short note on the following:
  10. 6(a) Discrete and continuous models
  11. 6(b) Deductive, inductive and floating models
  12. 6(c) Explicit and implicit models. [2+2+2]
  13. 7 A 1000 L tank of water initially contain 10 kg of dissolved salt. A pipe brings a salt solution (concentration 0.005 kg/L) into the tank at a rate 2 L/s and a second pipe carries away the excess solution at the same rate.
  14. 7(a) Calculate $c(t)$, the concentration of the salt in the tank, assuming the tank is well mixed.
  15. 7(b) After how many minutes has the concentration of salt decreased 25\% of its initial value?
  16. 7(c) What happens as \textquoteleft$t$\textquoteright{} tends to infinity? [2+2+2]
  17. 8 Convert the following system into polar coordinates and show that the system undergoes a limit cycle: \begin{align*} \frac{dx}{dt}&=y+\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\{1-(x^2+y^2)\},\\ \frac{dy}{dt}&=-x+\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\{1-(x^2+y^2)\}. \end{align*} [3+3]
  18. 1 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions.
  19. 1(a) How do you interpret stability-instability dynamics for a continuous one-dimensional autonomous system at an equilibrium point? Suppose a single infected individual migrates into a community containing $N$ individuals susceptible to a disease. The infected individual spreads the disease to all susceptible. Let $S(t)$ be the number of susceptibles at time $t$ and satisfy the following equations: \[ \frac{dS}{dt}=-rS(N+1-S),\qquad S(t=0)=N, \] where $r$ is a positive constant which measures the infection. Obtain the solution of the model and comment biologically for a long span of time.
  20. 1(b) Explain phase plane analysis for the following autonomous system: \[ \frac{du}{dt}=f(u,v),\qquad \frac{dv}{dt}=g(u,v). \] Sketch the phase diagram and classify the equilibrium point for the linear autonomous system \[ \frac{du}{dt}=-u+3v,\qquad \frac{dv}{dt}=-6u+5v. \]
  21. 1(c) Explain symbiosis and mutualism mechanism in ecological environment. Develop a two-species Lotka-Volterra model of mutualism mechanism and hence show that the system cannot have limit cycles. [$2\times5=10$]
  22. 2(a) \begin{romanparts} \item How do you explain the fixed point of a map $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ in the context of flow in a discrete system? \item Explain linear stability analysis around the fixed point $x^*$ of the map $x_{n+1}=f(x_n)$ where $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ and $x_n$ denotes the $n$-th iteration. \item Consider the map $f(x)=1-\mu x^2$, where $x\in[-1,1]$ and $\mu\in[0,2]$. Locate every fixed point on the map. Find out their stability characters as well. \end{romanparts}
  23. 2(b) A population follows a generalized logistic growth given by \[ \frac{du}{dt}=\frac{ru}{\alpha}\left[1-\left(\frac{u}{K}\right)^\alpha\right], \qquad u(t=0)=u_0\geq0. \] \begin{romanparts} \item Find the exact solution of the differential equation for $r,\alpha>0$ and show that the limiting population is still $K$. \item What happens to the model if $\alpha\to0$ and $\alpha\to-1$? \end{romanparts}
  24. 3 Construct \[ \mathcal L=\left[u-u^*-u^*\log_e\left(\frac{u}{u^*}\right)\right] +\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\left[v-v^*-v^*\log_e\left(\frac{v}{v^*}\right)\right] \] \textit{[A handwritten edit strikes the printed coefficient and adds $\beta/\gamma$ after the bracket.]} about the coexistence equilibrium point $\bigl(\frac{\delta}{\gamma},\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\bigr)$ for the Lotka-Volterra predator-prey model \begin{align*} \frac{du}{dt}&=\alpha u-\beta uv,\\ \frac{dv}{dt}&=\gamma uv-\delta v,\\ u(t=0)&\geq0,\quad v(t=0)\geq0, \end{align*} where $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ and $\delta$ are all positive. Is the system conservative? Justify your answer. [5+3+2]
  25. 4 Explain a reaction-diffusion system with an example. Consider the following reaction-diffusion system: \[ \frac{\partial u(x,t)}{\partial t}=R(u)+D\frac{\partial^2u(x,t)}{\partial x^2}. \]
  26. 4(a) Explain the reaction-diffusion model mathematically.
  27. 4(b) Calculate the values of $D$ for which the diffusion-driven instability about the non-trivial equilibrium point can take place. [2+3+5]
