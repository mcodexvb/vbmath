BMC-32

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1 Answer \textbf{\textit{any three}} questions: \hfill \(3\times5=15\)
  2. 1(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Let \(G\) be a group, \(a,b\in G\). Show that \((aba^{-1})^n=aba^{-1}\) if and only if \(b=b^n\) for \(n\in\mathbb N\). \item Give an example of a binary operation on a finite set which is commutative but not associative. \hfill \(3+2\) \end{enumerate}
  3. 1(b) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Let \(H\) and \(K\) be subgroups of a group \(G\). Show that if \(HK=KH\), then \(HK\) is subgroup of \(G\). \item Find an element \(a\) from some group such that \(|a|=6\) and \(c(a)\ne c(a^3)\). \hfill \(3+2\) \end{enumerate}
  4. 1(c) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Let \(G\) be a group such that \(G=\bigcup_{n=1}^{\alpha}G_n,\ G_n\subset G_{n+1}\). Then show that \(G\) cannot be cyclic. \hfill 2 \item Show that there is a cyclic subgroup in every noncommutative group. \hfill 3 \end{enumerate}
  5. 1(d) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Show that \((Q,+)\) is not finitely generated. \item In \(SL(2,\mathbb R)\), find \(\left\langle\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\right\rangle\). \hfill \(3+2\) \end{enumerate}
  6. 1(e) Let \(G\) be a group such that \(|G|>1\). Show that \(G\) has only trivial subgroup if and only if \(|G|\) is prime. \hfill 5
  7. 2 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions: \hfill \(2\times5=10\)
  8. 2(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Find all units of \(M_2(\mathbb Z)\). \item Give an example of an infinite noncommutative ring without unity. \hfill \(3+2\) \end{enumerate}
  9. 2(b) Let \(R\) be a finite commutative ring without zero divisors. Then show that \(R\) is a field. Give an example of a finite commutative ring which is not a field. \hfill \(4+1\)
  10. 2(c) Show that the characteristic of a finite ring \(R\) divides \(|R|\). Give an example of an infinite ring having finite characteristic. Justify your answer. \hfill \(3+2\)
  11. 3 Answer \textbf{\textit{any three}} questions: \hfill \(3\times5=15\)
  12. 3(a) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Is the set of positive real numbers \(\mathbb R^+\) a vector space over \(\mathbb R\) with respect to usual addition in \(\mathbb R^+\) and scaler multiplication defined by \(cx=x^c\) for \(x\in\mathbb R^+,\ c\in\mathbb R\)? Justify your answer. \item Give an example of a vector space \(V\) and its two subspaces \(W_1\) and \(W_2\) such that \(V=W_1+W_2\) but \(V\ne W_1\oplus W_2\). Justify your answer. \end{enumerate}
  13. 3(b) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Let \(S=\{(1,2),(2,1),(1,0),(5,3)\}\subset\mathbb R^2\). Find a smallest subset \(T\) of \(S\) such that \(L(S)=L(T)\). \hfill \(3+2\) \item Give an example to show that a set of vectors may be linearly independent over one field but may be linearly dependent over another field. \hfill 2 \end{enumerate}
  14. 3(c) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\roman{enumi})} \item Let \(V\) be a vector space and \(B\) be a basis of \(V\) with \(n\) elements. Then show that any linearly independent set of \(V\) can have at most \(n\) vectors. \hfill 3 \item Extend \(S=\{(1,0,0,0),(0,2,-1,1)\}\) to a basis of \(\mathbb R^4\). \hfill 2 \end{enumerate}
  15. 3(d) Show that every vector space has a basis. \hfill 5
  16. 3(e) Let \[ W_1=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&a\end{pmatrix}\in M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R):a,b,c\in\mathbb R\right\} \quad\text{and}\quad W_2=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}0&a\\-a&b\end{pmatrix}\in M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R):a,b\in\mathbb R\right\} \] be two subspaces of the vector space \(M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)\). Then find the dimensions of \(W_1,W_2,W_1+W_2\) and \(W_1\cap W_2\). \hfill \(2+2+0.5+0.5\)
