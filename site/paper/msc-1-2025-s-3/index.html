MMC-13

Linear Algebra
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2025
  1. 1(a) Find eigen values of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}5&-6&-6\\-1&4&2\\3&-6&-4\end{pmatrix}$. Also find the eigen space corresponding to the eigen value 2. [3]
  2. 1(b) Let $N$ be a $2\times2$ complex matrix. If $N$ is nilpotent, show that either $N=0$ or $N$ is similar to $A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Let $T:\longrightarrow V$ be a linear operator. Prove that eigen vectors corresponding to distinct eigen values of $T$ are linearly independent. [4]
  4. 2(a) Prove that a matrix $A\in M_n(F)$ is diagonalizable if and only if $F^n$ has a basis of eigen vectors of $A$. [4]
  5. 2(b) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a diagonalizable linear operator with eigen values $c_1,c_2,\cdots,c_k$. Prove that there is a resolution of identity $I_v=E_1+E_2+\cdots+E_k$ such that $T=c_1E_1+c_2E_2+\cdots+c_kE_k$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Find the minimal polynomial of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0&0\\-1&-1&0&0\\-2&-2&2&1\\1&1&-1&0\end{pmatrix}$. Is $A$ diagonalizable over $\mathbb C$? [3]
  7. 3(a) Define independent subspaces. Prove that $k$-subspaces $W_1,W_2,\cdots,W_k$ of a vector space $V$ are independent if and only if for every $1\le i\le k$, $W_i\cap(W_1+\cdots+W_{i-1}+W_{i+1}+\cdots+W_k)=\{\theta\}$. [4]
  8. 3(b) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a linear operator. If every subspace of $V$ is invariant under $T$, then show that $T=cI$ for some scalar $c$. [3]
  9. 3(c) Let $T$ be the indefinite integral operator $(Tf)(x)=\int_0^xf(t)dt$ on the real vector space $V=C[0,1]$, and $W=\{f\in C[0,1]\,|\,f(\frac12)=0\}$. Is $W$ invariant under $T$? [3]
  10. 4(a) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a linear operator, and $f(x)=g(x)h(x)\in F[x]$. If $\gcd(g(x),h(x))=1$, then prove that $\ker f(T)=\ker g(T)\oplus\ker h(T)$. [3]
  11. 4(b) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a nilpotent linear operator. Show that\par \hspace*{1em}(i) $I_{nul}(T)\le\dim V$,\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) if $I_{nul}(T)=p$ and $T^{p-1}(v)\ne\theta$ for some $v\in V$, then $\{v,T(v),\cdots,T^{p-1}(v)\}$ is linearly independent. [2+2]
  12. 4(c) Let $T$ be a linear operator and $E$ be a projection on $V$. Prove that both the null space and range space of $E$ are invariant under $T$ if and only if $ET=TE$. [3]
  13. 5(a) Let $T$ be a linear operator on an inner product space $V$. Prove that $T$ preserves inner product if and only if $T$ maps every orthonormal basis into an orthonormal basis of $V$. [4]
  14. 5(b) Let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$ which commutes with every projection operator on $V$. What can you say about $T$? [3]
  15. 5(c) Let $T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3$ be defined by $T(x,y,z)=(2x-y+5z,-x+2y-4z,7x+2y-z)$. Find the adjoint $T^*$ of $T$. [3]
  16. 6(a) State and prove the Riesz representation theorem for linear functional on an inner product space. [3]
  17. 6(b) Let $T$ be a linear operator on an inner product space $V$. Show that $\ker T^*=(Im\ T)^\perp$. [3]
  18. 6(c) Let $V=M_n(\mathbb C)$ with the inner product $<A,B>=tr\ (AB^*)$. For a fixed $M\in V$, define $T_M:V\longrightarrow V$ by $T_M(A)=MA$. Show that $T_M$ is unitary if and only if $M$ is a unitary matrix. [4]
