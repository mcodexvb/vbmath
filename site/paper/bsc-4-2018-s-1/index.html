BMC-41

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1 Answer \emph{any ten} questions: [$10\times1=10$]
  2. 1(a) Evaluate $\int_0^{\pi/4}\tan^6x\,dx$.
  3. 1(b) Find the area in the closed segment cut off from $y^2=4x$ by the line $y=2x$.
  4. 1(c) Test the convergence of the improper integral $\int_5^\infty\frac{dx}{x^\mu}$.
  5. 1(d) Find the value of the integration $\int_0^1 x^3\bigl(\log(\frac1x)\bigr)^5dx$.
  6. 1(e) Evaluate $\iint_R|x+y|\,dx\,dy$, where $R:[-1,1;-1,1]$.
  7. 1(f) Find the value of the integration $y(\pi)$, where $y(x)=\frac{d}{dx}\int_{\sin^2x}^{2\sin x}e^{t^2}dt$.
  8. 1(g) Find the differential equation corresponding to the integral equation \[Y(x)=\int_0^x(x-t)Y(t)dt-x\int_0^1(1-t)Y(t)dt.\]
  9. 1(h) Using the properties of Riemann integral, show that if $f$ is a non-negative continuous function on $[0,1]$ and $\int_0^1 f(x)dx=0$, then $f(x)=0$ $\forall x\in[0,1]$.
  10. 1(i) Suppose $f(x)$ is a continuous real valued function. Then show that $\int_0^1 f(x)dx^2=\frac13 f(\xi)$ for some $\xi\in[0,1]$.
  11. 1(j) Show that there does not exist a primitive function on $\mathbb R$ to the function $f$ defined by \[f(x)=\begin{cases}1&x\in\mathbb R^+\\0&x\in0\\-1&x\in\mathbb R^-\end{cases}\]
  12. 1(k) Give an example to show that the upper and lower integral (Riemann) may not be equal for a bounded function.
  13. 1(l) Is the function $f=\begin{cases}x\cos\frac{\pi}{2x},&x\ne0,\\0,&x=0,\end{cases}$ a function of bounded variation on $[0,1]$? Justify your answer.
  14. 1(m) Use the Second Mean Value Theorem (Bonnet's form) to show that $\big|\int_a^b\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx\big|\le\frac2a$.
  15. 1(n) Evaluate $\int_0^3x[x]dx$
  16. 2(a) Let a function $f$ be Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$. Then show that $|f|$ is Riemann integrable. Give an example to show that converse of the result does not hold in general. [4+2]
  17. 2(b) State and prove a condition for the Riemann integrability of $\frac1f$ for a Riemann integrable function $f$ on $[a,b]$. [1+3]
  18. 3(a) State and prove the First Mean Value Theorem of integral calculus. [1+4]
  19. 3(b) Let $f$ monotonic on $[a,b]$. If $\alpha$ is monotonic increasing and continuous on $[a,b]$, then show that $\zeta$ is Riemann-Stieltjes integrale on $[a,b]$. [3]
  20. 3(c) Find $\int_{-1}^1(x^2+e^x)d(\operatorname{sgn}x)$ [2]
  21. 4(a) Let $f$ be continuous on $[a,b]$, $f'$ exist and be bounded on $(a,b)$. Show that $f$ is a function of bounded variation on $[a,b]$. [3]
  22. 4(b) Calculate total variation for the function $f(x)=x-[x]$, on $[1,3]$. [2]
  23. 4(c) Find the value of $F(2,3,4)$, where \[F(a,b,c)=\iiint_E\sqrt{a^2b^2c^2-b^2c^2x^2-c^2a^2y^2-a^2b^2z^2}\,dx\,dy\,dz,\] $E$ being the region bounded by the ellipsoid $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1$. [3]
  24. 4(d) Prove that $\int_0^1 dx\int_0^1\frac{x-y}{(x+y)^3}dy\ne\int_0^1dy\int_0^1\frac{x-y}{(x+y)^3}dx$. Does the double integral $\int_0^1\int_0^1\frac{x-y}{(x+y)^3}dxdy$ exist? Justify your answer. [2]
  25. 5(a) Using the transformations $x=\frac12v(1-u)$, $y=\frac12v(1+u)$, evaluate the integral $\iint_E e^{\frac{y-x}{y+x}}dxdy$, where $E$ is the triangle with vertices $(0,0),(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. Also verify the result by using the transformation $y-x=2u$ and $y+x=2v$. [5]
  26. 5(b) Evaluate $\int_0^1dy\int_y^1 e^{x^2}dx$ by changing the order of the integration. [2]
  27. 5(c) Evaluate $\iint_R[x+y]dxdy$, where $R$ is the rectangle $[-1,1;0,2]$ and $[x+y]$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x+y$. [3]
  28. 6(a) Express $\int_0^1x^m(1-x^n)^p$ in terms of Beta function. Hence evaluate $\int_0^1x^5(1-x^3)^{10}dx$. [2.5]
  29. 6(b) Applying Dirichlet's test, show that $\int_0^\infty\sin(x^2)dx$ is convergent. [2]
  30. 6(c) Evaluate $\int_0^\infty 5^{-x^2}dx$. [1.5]
  31. 6(d) Find the value of $F(24,8)$, where $F(a,b)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\log(a^2\cos^2\theta+b^2\sin^2\theta)d\theta$, $a,b>0$ [4]
