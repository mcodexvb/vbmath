IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2016

  1. 1 The sequence $\left\{s_{n}\right\}$ of real numbers given by $$ s_{n}=\frac{\sin \frac{\pi}{2}}{1 \cdot 2}+\frac{\sin \frac{\pi}{2^{2}}}{2 \cdot 3}+\cdots+\frac{\sin \frac{\pi}{2^{n}}}{n \cdot(n+1)} $$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item a divergent sequence \item an oscillatory sequence \item not a Cauchy sequence \item a Cauchy sequence \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $P$ be the vector space (over $\mathbb{R}$ ) of all polynomials of degree $\leq 3$ with real coefficients. Consider the linear transformation $T: P \rightarrow P$ defined by $$ T\left(a_{0}+a_{1} x+a_{2} x^{2}+a_{3} x^{3}\right)=a_{3}+a_{2} x+a_{1} x^{2}+a_{0} x^{3} . $$ Then the matrix representation $M$ of $T$ with respect to the ordered basis $\left\{1, x, x^{2}, x^{3}\right\}$ satisfies \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $M^{2}+I_{4}=0$ \item $M-I_{4}=0$ \item $ M^{2}-I_{4}=0$ \item $M+I_{4}=0$ \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $f:[-1,1] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Then the integral $$ \int_{0}^{\pi} x f(\sin x) d x $$ is equivalent to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $$ \frac{\pi}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) d x $$ \item $$ \frac{\pi}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi} f(\cos x) d x $$ \item $$ \pi \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\cos x) d x $$ \item $$ \pi \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x) d x $$ \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Let $\sigma$ be an element of the permutation group $S_{5}$. Then the maximum possible order of $\sigma$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 5 \item 6 \item 10 \item 15 \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $f$ be a strictly monotonic contimuous real valued function defined on $[a, b]$ such that $f(a)<a$ and $f(b)>b$. Then which one of the following is TRUE? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There exists exactly one $c \in(a, b)$ such that $f(c)=c$ \item There exist exactly two points $c_{1}, c_{2} \in(a, b)$ such that $f\left(a_{i}\right)=c_{i}, i=1,2$ \item There exists no $c \in(a, b)$ such that $f(c)=c$ \item There exist infinitely many points $c \in(a, b)$ such that $f(c)=c$ \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 The value of $\lim _{(x, y) \rightarrow(2,-2)} \frac{\sqrt{(x-y)}-2}{x-y-4}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item $\frac{1}{4}$ \item $\frac{1}{3}$ \item $\frac{1}{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 Let $\vec{r}=(x \hat{\imath}+y \hat{\jmath}+z \hat{k})$ and $r=|\vec{r}|$. If $f(r)=\ln r$ and $g(r)=\frac{1}{r}, r \neq 0$, satisfy $2 \nabla f+h(r) \nabla g=\overrightarrow{0}$, then $h(r)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $r$ \item $\frac{1}{r}$ \item $2 r$ \item $\frac{2}{r}$ \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 The nonzero value of $n$ for which the differential equation $$ \left(3 x y^{2}+n^{2} x^{2} y\right) d x+\left(n x^{3}+3 x^{2} y\right) d y=0, \quad x \neq 0, $$ becomes exact is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-3$ \item $-2$ \item $2$ \item $3$ \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 One of the points which lies on the solution curve of the differential equation $$ (y-x) d x+(x+y) d y=0 $$ with the given condition $y(0)=1$, is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(1,-2)$ \item $(2,-1)$ \item $(2,1)$ \item $(-1,2)$ \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 Let $S$ be a closed subset of $\mathbb{R}, T$ a compact subset of $\mathbb{R}$ such that $S \cap T \neq \phi$. Then $S \cap T$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item closed but not compact \item not closed \item conmact \item neither closed nor compact \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 Let $S$ be the series $$ \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2 k-1) 2^{(2 k-1)}} $$ and $T$ be the series $$ \sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\left(\frac{3 k-4}{3 k+2}\right)^{\frac{(k+1)}{3}} $$ of real numbers. Then which one of the following is TRUE? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Both the series $S$ and $T$ are convergent \item $S$ is convergent and $T$ is divergent \item $S$ is divergent and $T$ is convergent \item Both the series $S$ and $T$ are divergent \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers satisfying $$ \frac{4}{a_{n+1}}=\frac{3}{a_{n}}+\frac{a_{n}^{3}}{81}, \quad n \geq 1, a_{1}=1 . $$ Then all the terms of the sequence lie in \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\left[\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2}\right]$ \item $[0,1]$ \item $[1,2]$ \item $[1,3]$ \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 The largest eigenvalue of the matix $\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1 & 4 & 16 \\ 4 & 16 & 1 \\ 16 & 1 & 4\end{array}\right]$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 16 \item 21 \item 48 \item 64 \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 The value of the integral $$ \frac{(2 n) !}{2^{2 n}(n !)} \int_{-1}^{1}\left(1-x^{2}\right)^{n} d x, \quad n \in \mathbb{N} $$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\quad \frac{2}{(2 n+1) !}$ \item $\frac{2(n)}{2 n+1}$ \item $\quad \frac{2 \pi}{(2 \pi+1) !}$ \item $\frac{(n+1) n}{2 n+1}$ \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 If the triple integral over the region bounded by the planes $$ 2 x+y+z=4, \quad x=0, \quad y=0, \quad z=0 $$ is given by $$ \int_{0}^{2} \int_{0}^{\lambda(x)} \int_{0}^{\mu(x, y)} d z d y d x, $$ then the function $\lambda(x)-\mu(x, y)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $x+y$ \item $x-y$ \item $x$ \item $y$ \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 The suface area of the portion of the plane $y+2 z=2$ within the cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2}=3$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{3 \sqrt{5}}{2} \pi$ \item $\frac{5 \sqrt{5}}{2} \pi$ \item $\frac{7 \sqrt{5}}{2} \pi$ \item $\frac{9 \sqrt{5}}{2} \pi$ \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be defined by $$ f(x, y)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll} \frac{x y^{2}}{x+y} & \text { if } x+y \neq 0 \\ 0 & \text { if } x+y=0 \end{array}\right. \text {. } $$ Then the value of $\left(\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial x \partial y}+\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial y \partial x}\right)$ at the point $(0,0)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item 1 \item 2 \item 4 \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 The function $f(x, y)=3 x^{2} y+4 y^{3}-3 x^{2}-12 y^{2}+1$ has a saddle point at \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(0,0)$ \item $(0,2)$ \item $(1,1)$ \item $(-2,1)$ \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 Consider the vector field $\vec{F}=x^{\beta}(y \hat{\imath}-x \hat{\jmath})$, where $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $\vec{r}=x \hat{\imath}+y \hat{\jmath}$ and $r=|\vec{r}|$.   21. 21 Let $y(x)$ be the solution of the differential equation $$ \frac{d}{d x}\left(x \frac{d y}{d x}\right)=x ; \quad y(1)=0,\left.\frac{d y}{d x}\right|_{x=1}=0 . $$ Then $y(2)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{2} \ln 2$ \item $\frac{3}{4}-\frac{1}{2} \ln 2$ \item $\frac{3}{4}+\ln 2$ \item $\frac{3}{4}-\ln 2$ \end{enumerate}
  22. 22 The general solution of the differential equation with constant coefficients $$ \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}+b \frac{d y}{d x}+c y=0 $$ approaches zero as $x \rightarrow \infty$, if \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $b$ is negative and $c$ is positive \item $b$ is positive and $c$ is negative \item both $b$ and $c$ are positive \item both $b$ and $c$ are negative \end{enumerate}
  23. 23 Let $S \subset \mathbb{R}$ and $\partial S$ denote the set of points $x$ in $\mathbb{R}$ such that every neighbourhood of $x$ contains some points of $S$ as well as some points of complement of S. Further, let $\bar{S}$ denote the closure of $S$. Then which one of the following is FALSE? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\partial \mathbb{Q}=\mathbb{R}$ \item $\partial(\mathbb{R} \backslash T)=\partial T, \quad T \subset \mathbb{R}$ \item $\partial(T \cup V)=\partial T \cup \partial V, \quad T, V \subset \mathbb{R}, T \cap V \neq \phi$ \item $\partial T=\bar{T} \cap(\overline{\mathbb{R} \backslash T}), \quad T \subset \mathbb{R}$ \end{enumerate}
  24. 24 The sum of the series $$ \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n}}{n^{2}+n-2} $$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{3} \ln 2-\frac{5}{18}$ \item $\frac{1}{3} \ln 2-\frac{5}{6}$ \item $\frac{2}{3} \ln 2-\frac{5}{18}$ \item $\frac{2}{3} \ln 2-\frac{5}{6}$ \end{enumerate}
