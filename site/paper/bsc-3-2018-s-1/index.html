CC-5

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Let \(A,D\subset\mathbb R\) and \(f:A\to\mathbb R,\ g:D\to\mathbb R\) where \(f(A)\subset D\). If \(c\) is a limit point of \(A\) and \(\lim_{x\to c}f(x)=\ell\) then prove the following: \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})} \item if \(\ell\in D\) and \(g\) is continuous at \(\ell\) then \(\operatorname*{Lt}_{x\to c}(g\circ f)(x)=g(\ell)\) \item if \(\ell\notin D\ (\ell\in D')\) and \(\operatorname*{Lt}_{y\to\ell}g(y)=m\) then \(\operatorname*{Lt}_{x\to c}(g\circ f)(x)=m\). \hfill 5 \end{enumerate}
  2. 1(b) If a function \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) is monotone, then prove that the set of points of discontinuities of \(f\) in \([a,b]\) is countable. \hfill 5
  3. 2(a) When is a function \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) said to be bounded variation? If \(f\) and \(g\) are two functions of bounded variation over \([a,b]\) then prove that \(f+g\) and \(fg\) are of bounded variation over \([a,b]\). \hfill 6
  4. 2(b) Examine if the function \(f\) defined by \(f(x)=\begin{cases}x\sin\frac{\pi}{x},&x\ne0\\0,&x=0\end{cases}\) is of bounded variation over \([0,1]\). \hfill 4
  5. 3(a) Define Dedekind's section of real numbers. If \((L,U)\) denotes the section of a real number \(\alpha\), then find the section of \(-\alpha\) with proper justifications. \hfill \(2+3\)
  6. 3(b) Using Dedekind's section of real numbers prove that every non-empty subset of the set of real numbers which has an upper bound has the least upper bound. \hfill 5
  7. 4(a) Prove that countable union of countable sets is countable. \hfill 4
  8. 4(b) Show that the set of all irrational numbers in \((0,1)\) is uncountable. \hfill 3
  9. 4(c) Which of the following sets are countable? Support our answer. \[ \text{(i) }\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}(n,n+2) \quad\text{(ii) }(0,1)-Q \quad\text{(iii) }Q-(0,1). \] \hfill 3
  10. 5(a) Let \(f\) be continuous over \([a,b]\) and \(g\) be a non-decreasing function over \([a,b]\). Prove that \((RS)\int_a^b f\,dg\) exists and \[ (RS)\int_a^b f\,dg=(RS)\int_{\underline a}^{b} f\,dg+(RS)\int_a^{\overline b} f\,dg. \] \hfill 4
  11. 5(b) If \(g\) is continuous and \(f\) is non-decreasing over \([a,b]\) then show that \(\exists\xi\in[a,b]\) such that \[ (RS)\int_a^b f\,dg=f(a)(RS)\int_a^\xi dg+f(b)(RS)\int_\xi^b dg. \] \hfill 3
  12. 5(c) Evaluate \((RS)\int_0^2 x\,d(\cos x)\). \hfill 3
  13. 6(a) Test the convergence of the following (any two): \hfill \(3+3\) \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})} \item \(\displaystyle\frac{1+2}{2^3}+\frac{1+2+3}{3^3}+\frac{1+2+3+4}{4^3}+\cdots\) \item \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty u_n\) where \(u_n=\sqrt{n^2+1}-\sqrt{n^2-1}\) \item \(\displaystyle\frac12+\frac13+\frac{1}{2^2}+\frac{1}{3^2}+\frac{1}{2^3}+\frac{1}{3^3}+\cdots\). \end{enumerate}
  14. 6(b) Let \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty u_n\) be a series of positive terms of real numbers and \(\overline{\lim}_{n\to\infty}u_n^{1/n}=r\). Show that \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty u_n\) is convergent if \(r<1\) and is divergent if \(r>1\). \hfill 4
  15. 7(a) Discuss the convergence of \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^\infty\frac1{n(\log n)^p},\ p>0\). \hfill 3
  16. 7(b) If \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty u_n\) is a convergent series, where \(u_n\ge0\) and \(\{u_n\}\) is a monotone decreasing sequence then show that \(\lim_{x\to\infty}nu_n=0\). \hfill 4
  17. 7(c) If \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty u_n\) be a series of positive terms of real numbers and \(v_n=\dfrac{u_1+u_2+\cdots+u_n}{n}\), prove that \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty v_n\) is divergent. \hfill 3
  18. 8(a) Define a function \(f:[0,2]\to\mathbb R\) by \(f(x)=\begin{cases}x+x^2,&\text{when }x\text{ is rational}\\x^2+x^3,&\text{when }x\text{ is irrational}\end{cases}\). Examine the \(R\)-integrability of \(f\). \hfill 3
  19. 8(b) Let \(f\) be a real valued function bounded on \([a,b]\). Show that \(f\) is \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\) iff \(\lim_{\lVert P\rVert\to0}\omega(P,f)=0\). \hfill 4
  20. 8(c) Show that the following function \(f\) defined by \[ f(x)=\begin{cases} \frac1{2^n}&\text{when }\frac1{2^{n+1}}<x\le\frac1{2^n}\ (n=0,1,2,\ldots)\\ 0&\text{when }x=0 \end{cases} \] is \(R\)-integrable on \([0,1]\). Find \(\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx\). \hfill 3
  21. 9(a) If \(f\) and \(g\) are two real valued functions, both are bounded and \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\), then show that \(fg\) is also \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\). Is the converse true? Justify your answer. \hfill \(3+1\)
  22. 9(b) Show by an example that the relation \(\int_a^b f'(x)\,dx=f(b)-f(a)\) is not always true, for a real valued function \(f\) over \([a,b]\). \hfill 3
  23. 9(c) Show that \(\displaystyle\frac1a\le\int_0^1\frac{\sin\pi x}{1+x^2}\,dx\le\frac2\pi\). \hfill 3
