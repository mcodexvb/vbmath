MJMA-3

Algebra III
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2026
  1. 1(a) Define reduced row echelon matrix. Give examples of (i) a matrix in row echelon form but not in reduced row echelon form, (ii) a matrix in reduced row echelon form. [4]
  2. 1(b) Find the reduced row echelon form, rank and pivot positions of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&4&4\\0&6&5&1\\3&2&9&0\end{pmatrix}$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Consider the system \begin{align*}x_1+2x_2+3x_3&=1\\4x_2-x_3&=5\\3x_1+x_2+x_3&=4.\end{align*} Find the rank of the coefficient matrix. Also find the free and basic variables. [3]
  4. 2(a) Find the value of $k$ so that the system \begin{align*}x_1+x_2+x_3&=0\\x_1+2x_2+4x_3&=k\\x_1+4x_2+10x_3&=k^2\end{align*} has infinitely many solutions. [4]
  5. 2(b) Find all solutions of the system \begin{align*}x_1-2x_2+x_3&=1\\2x_1+3x_2+4x_3&=2\\4x_1-x_2+6x_3&=4.\end{align*} [3]
  6. 2(c) Show that the system \begin{align*}x_1+2x_2+x_3&=2\\2x_1-4x_2+5x_3&=0\\4x_1-2x_2+6x_3&=4\end{align*} has no solutions. [3]
  7. 3(a) Define subspace of $\mathbb R^n$. Show that intersection of two subspaces of $\mathbb R^n$ is a subspace. [4]
  8. 3(b) Show that $V=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid 2x-3y+z=0\}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb R^3$. [3]
  9. 3(c) Find the value of $h$ such that $(1,-2,h)$ is in $L(\{(3,5,4),(2,-4,3)\})$. [3]
  10. 4(a) Let $AX=b$ be a system of linear equations. Show that this system has a solution if and only if the constant vector $b$ is a linear combination of the column vectors of $A$. [4]
  11. 4(b) Show that $(1,2,3)$ can not be expressed as a linear combination of $(2,1,-1)$ and $(1,1,1)$. [3]
  12. 4(c) Express $(2,5,7)$ as a linear combination of the three vectors $(1,2,3),(1,0,1)$ and $(1,3,4)$. Is this expression unique? [3]
  13. 5(a) Define linear independence of vectors. Find the value of $h$ so that the vectors $(1,-2,-1,3),(-3,0,5,-4)$ and $(-2,-2,4,h)$ are linearly independent. [4]
  14. 5(b) Show that the columns of a $3\times4$ matrix can not be linearly independent. [3]
  15. 5(c) Show that the nonzero rows of a reduced row echelon matrix are linearly independent. [3]
  16. 6(a) Draw the row picture and column picture for the system $2x+3y=12,\ x+2y=7$. [4]
  17. 6(b) Let $A\in M_3(\mathbb R)$ and $b\in\mathbb R^3$. If the system $AX=b$ has a unique solution, then show that $r(A)=3$. [3]
  18. 6(c) Show that three vectors in a plane in $\mathbb R^3$ must be linearly dependent. [3]
  19. 1(a) What do you mean by the limit of a sequence $\{a_n\}$? Explain with examples. Show that such a limit, if exists, is unique. [2+4]
  20. 1(b) Prove, using $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations, that the sequence $\left\{\frac{n^2+1}{n^2}\right\}$ converges to 1. [4]
  21. 2(a) State Sandwich Theorem and use it to prove that the sequence $\{(2^n+3^n)^{\frac1n}\}$ converges to 3. [1+3]
  22. 2(b) Define the limit inferior and limit superior of sequence $\{a_n\}$. Find them for the sequence $\left\{\frac{(n!)^{\frac1n}}{n}\right\}$ and decide whether it is convergent. [2+3+1]
  23. 3(a) Examine whether the limit $\lim_{x\to0}\sin\frac1x$ exists. [5]
  24. 3(b) If $f(x)=\begin{cases}\frac{x^2-4}{x-2}&\text{if }x\ne2\\5&\text{if }x=2\end{cases}$, using $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations, show that $\lim_{x\to2}f(x)=4$ [5]
  25. 4(a) Describe the sequential criterion for the continuity of a function $f:\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R$ at a point $c\in\mathbb R$. Hence show that the function $f(x)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }x\in\mathbb Q\\0&\text{if }x\notin\mathbb Q\end{cases}$ is continuous at no point of $\mathbb R$. [1+4]
  26. 4(b) Prove, using $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations, that the function $f(x)=\sin3x$ is continuous at every point of $\mathbb R$. [5]
  27. 5(a) Describe, in detail, how a point $z=x+iy$ of the complex plane is stereographically projected onto a point $(\xi,\eta,\zeta)$ on the sphere $\xi^2+\eta^2+\zeta^2=1$. Also derive the concerned formulae for the correspondence $z\longleftrightarrow(\xi,\eta,\zeta)$. [3+3]
  28. 5(b) Using $\epsilon$-$\delta$ notations, prove that the function $f(z)=\frac1z$ is continuous at $z=i$. [4]
  29. 6(a) Discuss the basic differences of limit and limit points of a sequence $\{z_n\}$ in $\mathbb C$. Find the limit points of the sequence $\{i^n+n^{(-1)^n}\}$. [2+4]
  30. 6(b) Find the stereographic projection of the curve $x^2+4x-6y+7=0$ of the complex plane $\mathbb C$ on the Riemann sphere $\xi^2+\eta^2+\zeta^2=1$. [4]
