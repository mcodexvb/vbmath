TIFR Graduate School · GS 2015 · Mathematics

  1. 1 Let $A$ be an invertible $10 \times 10$ matrix with real entries such that the sum of each row is $1$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item The sum of the entries of each row of the inverse of $A$ is $1$. \item The sum of the entries of each column of the inverse of $A$ is $1$. \item The trace of the inverse of $A$ is non-zero. \item None of the above. \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Which one of the following sets cannot be the image of $(0, 1]$ under $f$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\{0\}$ \item $(0, 1)$ \item $[0, 1)$ \item $[0, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $A$ be a $10 \times 10$ matrix with complex entries such that all its eigenvalues are non-negative real numbers, and at least one eigenvalue is positive. Which of the following statements is always false? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item There exists a matrix $B$ such that $AB - BA = B$. \item There exists a matrix $B$ such that $AB - BA = A$. \item There exists a matrix $B$ such that $AB + BA = A$. \item There exists a matrix $B$ such that $AB + BA = B$. \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Let $S$ be the collection of (isomorphism classes of) groups $G$ which have the property that every element of $G$ commutes only with the identity element and itself. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $|S| = 1$ \item $|S| = 2$ \item $|S| \ge 3$ and is finite \item $|S| = \infty$. \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ denote the function defined by $f(x) = (1 - x^2)^{3/2}$ if $|x| < 1$, and $f(x) = 0$ if $|x| \ge 1$. Which of the following statements is correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f$ is not continuous. \item $f$ is continuous but not differentiable. \item $f$ is differentiable but $f'$ is not continuous. \item $f$ is differentiable and $f'$ is continuous. \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Let $A$ be the $2 \times 2$ matrix $\begin{pmatrix} \sin\frac{\pi}{18} & -\sin\frac{4\pi}{9} \\ \sin\frac{4\pi}{9} & \sin\frac{\pi}{18} \end{pmatrix}$. Then the smallest number $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $A^n = I$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item {[obscured in source]} \item {[obscured in source]} \item {[obscured in source]} \item $27$. \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 Let $f$ and $g$ be two functions from $[0, 1]$ to $[0, 1]$ with $f$ strictly increasing. Which of the following statements is always correct? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item If $g$ is continuous, then $f \circ g$ is continuous. \item If $f$ is continuous, then $f \circ g$ is continuous. \item If $f$ and $f \circ g$ are continuous, then $g$ is continuous. \item If $g$ and $f \circ g$ are continuous, then $f$ is continuous. \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Let $f(x) = \frac{e^{-1/x}}{x}$, where $x \in (0, 1)$. Then, on $(0, 1)$ \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f$ is uniformly continuous. \item $f$ is continuous but not uniformly continuous. \item $f$ is unbounded. \item $f$ is not continuous. \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 Let $\{a_n\}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $|a_{n+1} - a_n| \le \frac{n^2}{2^n}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item The sequence $\{a_n\}$ may be unbounded. \item The sequence $\{a_n\}$ is bounded but may not converge. \item The sequence $\{a_n\}$ has exactly two limit points. \item The sequence $\{a_n\}$ is convergent. \end{enumerate}
  10. 10 For a group $G$, let $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ denote the group of automorphisms of $G$. Which of the following statements is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z})$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_2$. \item If $G$ is cyclic, then $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is cyclic. \item If $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is trivial, then $G$ is trivial. \item $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z})$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 11 Let $\{a_n\}$ be a sequence of real numbers. Which of the following is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item If $\sum a_n$ converges, then so does $\sum a_n^4$. \item If $\sum |a_n|$ converges, then so does $\sum a_n^2$. \item If $\sum a_n$ diverges, then so does $\sum a_n^3$. \item If $\sum |a_n|$ diverges, then so does $\sum a_n^2$. \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be an infinitely differentiable function that vanishes at $10$ distinct points in $\mathbb{R}$. Suppose $f^{(n)}$ denotes the $n$-th derivative of $f$, for $n \ge 1$. Which of the following statements is always true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $f^{(n)}$ has at least $10$ zeros, for $1 \le n \le 8$. \item $f^{(n)}$ has at least one zero, for $1 \le n \le 9$. \item $f^{(n)}$ has at least $10$ zeros, for $n \ge 10$. \item $f^{(n)}$ has at least one zero, for $n \ge 9$. \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 For a real number $t > 0$, let $\sqrt{t}$ denote the positive square root of $t$. For a real number $x > 0$, let $F(x) = \int_{x^2}^{4x^2} \sin\sqrt{t}\,dt$. If $F'$ is the derivative of $F$, then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $F'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 0$ \item $F'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \pi$ \item $F'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = -\pi$ \item $F'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 2\pi$. \end{enumerate}
  14. 14 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ be a six digit number whose base $10$ expansion is of the form $abcabc$, where $a, b, c$ are digits between $0$ and $9$ and $a$ is non-zero. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $n$ is divisible by $5$. \item $n$ is divisible by $8$. \item $n$ is divisible by $13$. \item $n$ is divisible by $17$. \end{enumerate}
  15. 15 The series $\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{\cos(3^n x)}{2^n}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item diverges, for all rational $x \in \mathbb{R}$. \item diverges, for some irrational $x \in \mathbb{R}$. \item converges, for some but not all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. \item converges, for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 16 Let $X$ be a proper closed subset of $[0, 1]$. Which of the following statements is always true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item The set $X$ is countable. \item There exists $x \in X$ such that $X \setminus \{x\}$ is closed. \item The set $X$ contains an open interval. \item None of the above. \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 In how many ways can the group $\mathbb{Z}_5$ act on the set $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $5$ \item $24$ \item $25$ \item $120$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 18 Let $f$ be a function from $\{1, 2, \dots, 10\}$ to $\mathbb{R}$ such that \[ \left(\sum_{i=1}^{10} \frac{|f(i)|}{2^i}\right)^2 = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{10} |f(i)|^2\right) \left(\sum_{i=1}^{10} \frac{1}{4^i}\right). \] Mark the correct statement. \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item There are uncountably many $f$ with this property. \item There are only countably infinitely many $f$ with this property. \item There is exactly one such $f$. \item There is no such $f$. \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 Let $U_1 \supset U_2 \supset \cdots$ be a decreasing sequence of open sets in Euclidean $3$-space $\mathbb{R}^3$. What can we say about the set $\bigcap U_i$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item It is infinite. \item It is open. \item It is non-empty. \item None of the above. \end{enumerate}
  20. 20 Let $n \ge 1$ and let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with real entries such that $A^k = 0$ for some $k \ge 1$. Let $I$ be the identity $n \times n$ matrix. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $I + A$ need not be invertible. \item $\det(I + A)$ can be any non-zero real number. \item $\det(I + A) = 1$. \item $A^n$ is a non-zero matrix. \end{enumerate}
  21. 21 Let $f: [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a fixed continuous function such that $f$ is differentiable on $(0, 1)$ and $f(0) = f(1) = 0$. Then the equation $f(x) = f'(x)$ admits \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item no solution $x \in (0, 1)$. \item more than one solution $x \in (0, 1)$. \item exactly one solution $x \in (0, 1)$. \item at least one solution $x \in (0, 1)$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 22 A complex number $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$ is called algebraic if there is a non-zero polynomial $P(x) \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ with rational coefficients such that $P(\alpha) = 0$. Which of the following statements is true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item There are only finitely many algebraic numbers. \item All complex numbers are algebraic. \item $\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ is algebraic. \item None of the above. \end{enumerate}
  23. 23 For a group $G$, let $F(G)$ denote the collection of all subgroups of $G$. Which one of the following situations can occur? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $G$ is finite but $F(G)$ is infinite. \item $G$ is infinite but $F(G)$ is finite. \item $G$ is countable but $F(G)$ is uncountable. \item $G$ is uncountable but $F(G)$ is countable. \end{enumerate}
  24. 24 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function and $A \subset \mathbb{R}$ be defined by \[ A = \{y \in \mathbb{R} : y = \lim_{n \to \infty} f(x_n) \text{ for some sequence } x_n \to +\infty\}. \] Then the set $A$ is necessarily \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item a connected set. \item a compact set. \item a singleton set. \item none of the above. \end{enumerate}
  25. 25 How many finite sequences $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_m$ are there such that each $x_i = 1$ or $2$, and $\sum_{i=1}^m x_i = 10$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $89$ \item $91$ \item $92$ \item $120$. \end{enumerate}
  26. 26 Let $(X, d)$ be a path connected metric space with at least two elements, and let $S = \{d(x, y) : x, y \in X\}$. Which of the following statements is not necessarily true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $S$ is infinite. \item $S$ contains a non-zero rational number. \item $S$ is connected. \item $S$ is a closed subset of $\mathbb{R}$. \end{enumerate}
  27. 27 Let $X \subset \mathbb{R}$ and let $f, g: X \to X$ be continuous functions such that $f(X) \cap g(X) = \emptyset$ and $f(X) \cup g(X) = X$. Which one of the following sets cannot be equal to $X$? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $[0, 1]$ \item $(0, 1)$ \item $[0, 1)$ \item $\mathbb{R}$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 28 Let $X = \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : 2x^2 + 3y^2 = 1\}$. Endow $\mathbb{R}^2$ with the discrete topology, and $X$ with the subspace topology. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $X$ is a compact subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$ in this topology. \item $X$ is a connected subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$ in this topology. \item $X$ is an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$ in this topology. \item None of the above. \end{enumerate}
  29. 29 Let $G$ be a group. Suppose $|G| = p^2 q$, where $p$ and $q$ are distinct prime numbers satisfying $q \not\equiv 1 \pmod p$. Which of the following is always true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $G$ has more than one $p$-Sylow subgroup. \item $G$ has a normal $p$-Sylow subgroup. \item The number of $q$-Sylow subgroups of $G$ is divisible by $p$. \item $G$ has a unique $q$-Sylow subgroup. \end{enumerate}
  30. 30 Let $d(x, y)$ be the usual Euclidean metric on $\mathbb{R}^2$. Which of the following metric spaces is complete? \begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.] \item $\mathbb{Q}^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with the metric $d(x, y)$. \item $[0, 1] \times [0, \infty) \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with the metric $d'(x, y) = \frac{d(x, y)}{1 + d(x, y)}$. \item $(0, \infty) \times [0, \infty) \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with the metric $d(x, y)$. \item $[0, 1] \times [0, 1) \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with the metric $d''(x, y) = \min\{1, d(x, y)\}$. \end{enumerate}
