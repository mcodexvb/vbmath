MMC-26

Numerical Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2026
  1. 1(a) Calculate the $n$th divided difference of $1/x$, based on the points $x_0,x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$. [3]
  2. 1(b) Derive Hermite interpolating polynomial of degree $2n+1$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Given the data\par \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline $x$&0&1&2&3\\\hline $f(x)$&1&2&33&244\\\hline\end{tabular}\end{center} fit quadratic splines with $M(0)=f^{\prime\prime}(0)=0$. Hence find an estimate of $f(2.5)$. [4]
  4. 2(a) Derive the normal equations for the least square straight line fit for the discrete data $(x_i,f_i),i=0,1,\ldots,N$. [1]
  5. 2(b) Obtain the least square straight line fit to the following data.\par \begin{center}\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline $x$&0.2&0.4&0.6&0.8&1\\\hline $f(x)$&0.447&0.632&0.775&0.894&1\\\hline\end{tabular}\end{center} [3]
  6. 2(c) Obtain the least square polynomial approximation of degree one and two for $f(x)=x^{1/2}$ on $[0,1]$. [2+3]
  7. 2(d) Obtain the rational approximation of the form $\frac{a_0+a_1x}{1+b_1x}$; to $e^x$. [1]
  8. 3(a) Derive Gauss Legendre three-point integration formula. [5]
  9. 3(b) Determine $a,b$ and $c$ such that the formula $\int_0^hf(x)dx=h(af(0)+bf(h/3)+cf(h))$ is exact for polynomials of as higher order as possible. [2]
  10. 3(c) Evaluate the integral, $I=\int_0^1\frac{dx}{(x+1)}$; using Gauss-Legendre three point formula. [3]
  11. 4(a) Describe power method to determine the largest eigenvalue in magnitude of a real matrix having distinct eigen values. [5]
  12. 4(b) Consider the equations\par \hspace*{2em}$x_1+x_2+x_3=1$\par \hspace*{2em}$4x_1+3x_2-x_3=6$\par \hspace*{2em}$3x_1+5x_2+3x_3=4.$\par Use the $LU$- decomposition method to solve the system. [5]
  13. 5(a) Write down the forward time central space formulation of the heat conduction $u_t=u_{xx}$.\par \hspace*{1em}i. Determine the truncation error. \par \hspace*{1em}ii. Investigate the stability using Von Neumann method. [2]
  14. 5(b) Derive Lax-Wendroff scheme for the solution of first order hyperbolic partial differential equation. Also finds its condition of stability. [1+2]
  15. 5(c) For the boundary value problem\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=4,\ |x|\le1,|y|\le1\] $u=0$ on the boundary\par write a five point difference formulation with mesh spacing $h$ in both directions. Solve the difference equations for $h=1/2$. [4]
  16. 6(a) Derive second order Runge-Kutta method for the solution of first order ordinary non-linear initial value problem. [5]
  17. 6(b) Solve the initial value problem $u^\prime=-2tu^2,u(0)=u_0=1$ with $h=0.2$ on $[0,0.4]$; using the fourth order classical Runge-Kutta method and compare with the exact solution. [5]
