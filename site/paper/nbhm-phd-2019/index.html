NBHM · 2019 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $G$ is a finite group, then there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $G$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$. \item There exists an infinite group $G$ such that every element, other than the identity element, is of order $2$. \item The group $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$ contains a cyclic subgroup of order $5$. \end{enumerate}
  2. 1.2 Let $p$ be a prime number. Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $n > 1$. What is the order of a $p$-Sylow subgroup of $GL_n(\mathbb{F}_p)$?
  3. 1.3 Give an example of a $5$-Sylow subgroup of $GL_3(\mathbb{F}_5)$.
  4. 1.4 What is the number of elements of order $2$ in $S_4$?
  5. 1.5 Let $G$ be a group of order $10$. Which of the following could be the class equation of $G$?\\ a.\ $10 = 1 + \dots + 1$ ($10$ times). \qquad b.\ $10 = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4$. \qquad c.\ $10 = 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 5$.
  6. 1.6 Find the number of irreducible monic polynomials of degree $2$ in $\mathbb{F}_p$, where $p$ is a prime number.
  7. 1.7 Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix with real entries. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ $r(A^T A) \le r(A)$. \qquad b.\ $r(A^T A) = r(A)$. \qquad c.\ $r(A^T A) > r(A)$.
  8. 1.8 Let $V = \{(x, y, z, t) \in \mathbb{R}^4 \mid x + y - z = 0 \text{ and } x + y + t = 0\}$. Write down a basis for $V^\perp$.
  9. 1.9 Let $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^3$ be the column vector such that $x_0^T = (1, 1, 1)$. Let $V = \{A \in \mathbb{M}_3(\mathbb{R}) \mid Ax_0 = 0\}$. What is the dimension of $V$?
  10. 1.10 Let $A = \begin{bmatrix} 19 & 2019 \\ 2019 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The matrix $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb{R}$. \item There exists a basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$ consisting of eigenvectors $\{w_1, w_2\}$ of the matrix $A$ such that $w_1^T w_2 = 0$. \item There exists a matrix $B \in GL_2(\mathbb{R})$ such that $B^3 = A$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 Let $f$ be a function that is known to be analytic in a neighbourhood of the origin in the complex plane. Furthermore it is known that for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, \[ f^{(n)}(0) = (n - 1)!(n + 1)\left(\frac{n + 1}{n}\right)^{(n+1)(n-1)}. \] Find the radius of the largest circle with centre at the origin inside which the Taylor series of $f$ defines an analytic function.
  12. 2.2 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The function $f(x) = \sin^2 x$ is uniformly continuous on $]0, \infty[$. \item If $f : ]0, \infty[ \to \mathbb{R}$ is uniformly continuous, then $\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)$ exists. \item If $f : ]0, \infty[ \to \mathbb{R}$ is uniformly continuous, then $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x)$ exists. \end{enumerate}
  13. 2.3 Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ $\log x \le \frac{x}{e}$ for all $x > 0$. \qquad b.\ $\log x \ge \frac{x}{e}$ for all $x > 0$. \qquad c.\ $e^\pi > \pi^e$.
  14. 2.4 Let $\{r_1, \dots, r_n, \dots\}$ be an enumeration of the rationals in the interval $[0, 1]$. Define, for $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and for each $x \in [0, 1]$, $f_n(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } x = r_1, \dots, r_n, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The function $f_n$ is Riemann integrable over the interval $[0, 1]$ for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$. \item The sequence $\{f_n\}$ is pointwise convergent and the limit function is Riemann integrable over the interval $[0, 1]$. \item The sequence $\{f_n\}$ is pointwise convergent but the limit function is not Riemann integrable over the interval $[0, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  15. 2.5 Let $[a, b] \subset \mathbb{R}$ be a finite interval. A function $f : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ is said to be of \emph{bounded variation} if there exists a real number $L > 0$ such that for every partition $\mathcal{P} = \{a = x_0 < x_1 < \dots < x_n = b\}$, where $n \in \mathbb{N}$, we have $\sum_{i=1}^{n} |f(x_i) - f(x_{i-1})| \le L$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $f : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ is monotonic, then it is of bounded variation. \item If $f \in \mathcal{C}^1[a, b]$, then it is of bounded variation. \item If $f : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ and $g : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ are of bounded variation, then $f + g$ is also of bounded variation. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Let $[a, b] \subset \mathbb{R}$ be a finite interval. Let $f : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a bounded and Riemann integrable function. Define, for $x \in [a, b]$, $F(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\,dt$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ The function $F$ is uniformly continuous. \qquad b.\ The function $F$ is of bounded variation. \qquad c.\ The function $F$ is differentiable on $]a, b[$.
  17. 2.7 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The sequence of functions $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$, defined by $f_n(x) = x^n(1 - x)$, is uniformly convergent on the interval $[0, 1]$. \item The sequence of functions $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$, defined by $f_n(x) = n\log\left(1 + \frac{x^2}{n}\right)$, is uniformly convergent on $\mathbb{R}$. \item The series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 2^n \sin\frac{1}{3^n x}$ is uniformly convergent on the interval $[1, \infty[$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 2.8 Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of functions in $\mathcal{C}^1[0, 1]$ such that $f_n(0) = 0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If the sequence $\{f_n\}$ converges uniformly on the interval $[0, 1]$, then the limit function is in $\mathcal{C}^1[0, 1]$. \item If the sequence $\{f_n'\}$ is uniformly convergent over the interval $[0, 1]$, then the sequence $\{f_n\}$ is also uniformly convergent over the same interval. \item If the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n'$ converges uniformly over the interval $[0, 1]$ to a function $g$, then $g$ is Riemann integrable and $\int_0^1 g(t)\,dt = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n(1)$. \end{enumerate}
  19. 2.9 a.\ Write down the Laurent series expansion of the function $f(z) = \dfrac{-1}{(z - 1)(z - 2)}$ in the region $\{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid 1 < |z| < 2\}$.\\ b.\ Let the circle $\Gamma = \{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid |z| = \frac{3}{2}\}$ be described in the counter-clockwise sense. Evaluate: $\int_\Gamma f(z)\,dz$.
  20. 2.10 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item There exists an analytic function $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ such that its real part is the function $e^x$, where $z = x + iy$. \item There exists an analytic function $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ such that $f(z) = z$ for all $z$ such that $|z| = 1$ and $f(z) = z^2$ for all $z$ such that $|z| = 2$. \item There exists an analytic function $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ such that $f(0) = 1$, $f(4i) = i$ and for all $z_j$ such that $1 < |z_j| < 3$, $j = 1, 2$, we have $|f(z_1) - f(z_2)| \le |z_1 - z_2|^{\frac{\pi}{3}}$. \end{enumerate}
  21. 3.1 Let $X$ be a topological space. If $A \subset X$, we denote by $A^\circ$, the interior of $A$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $A$ and $B$ are subsets of $X$, then $(A \cup B)^\circ = A^\circ \cup B^\circ$. \item If $A$ and $B$ are subsets of $X$, then $(A \cap B)^\circ = A^\circ \cap B^\circ$. \item If $A \subset X$, then $A^\circ = (\overline{A^c})^c$, where for $B \subset X$, we denote by $B^c$, its complement, \emph{i.e.}\ $B^c = X \setminus B$, and by $\overline{B}$, the closure of $B$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $X$ be a topological space such that the set $\Delta = \{(x, x) \mid x \in X\}$ is closed in $X \times X$ (with the product topology). Then $X$ is Hausdorff. \item Let $X$ be a set and let $Y$ be a Hausdorff space. Let $f : X \to Y$ be a given mapping. Define $U \subset X$ to be open in $X$ if, and only if, $U = f^{-1}(V)$ for some set $V$ open in $Y$. This defines a Hausdorff topology on $X$. \item The weakest topology on $\mathbb{R}$ such that all polynomials (in a single variable) are continuous, is Hausdorff. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item The map $f : [0, 2\pi[ \to S^1$, defined by $f(t) = e^{it}$, is a homeomorphism. \item The spaces $S^1$ and $S^2$, with their topologies inherited from $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $\mathbb{R}^3$, respectively, are homeomorphic. \item Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $f : X \to Y$ be a continuous map. Define $G(f) = \{(x, f(x)) \mid x \in X\} \subset X \times Y$. If $G(f)$ inherits the product topology from $X \times Y$, then $X$ is homeomorphic to $G(f)$ via the map $x \mapsto (x, f(x))$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 3.4 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space. Let $J$ be an indexing set. Consider a set of the form $S = \{x_j \in X \mid j \in J\}$ with the property that $d(x_j, x_k) = 1$ for all $j \neq k$, $j, k \in J$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If such a set exists in $X$, then there exist open sets $\{U_j\}_{j \in J}$ in $X$ such that $U_j \cap U_k = \emptyset$ for all $j \neq k$, $j, k \in J$. \item There exists such a set $S$ in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ with $J$ being uncountable. \item If such a set exists in $X$, and if $X$ is compact, then $J$ must be finite. \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Let $E = \left\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \;\middle|\; \frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1\right\}$, with the topology inherited from $\mathbb{R}^2$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item There exists $f : E \to \mathbb{R}$ that is continuous and onto. \item If $[\alpha, \beta]$ is any finite interval in $\mathbb{R}$, there exists $f : E \to [\alpha, \beta]$ that is continuous and onto. \item If $[\alpha, \beta]$ is any finite interval in $\mathbb{R}$, there exists $f : E \to [\alpha, \beta]$ that is continuous, one-one and onto. \end{enumerate}
  26. 3.6 Let $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous $2\pi$-periodic function, \emph{i.e.}\ for every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, we have $f(t) = f(t + 2\pi)$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ There exists $t_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(t_0) = f(-t_0)$.\\ b.\ There exists $t_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(t_0) = f(t_0 + \frac{\pi}{2})$.\\ c.\ There exists $t_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(t_0) = f(t_0 + \frac{\pi}{4})$.
  27. 3.7 Which of the following spaces are connected? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$, with the topology inherited from $\mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$. \item $GL_2(\mathbb{C})$, with the topology inherited from $\mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{C})$. \item The space $X$ of all symmetric matrices in $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$ with both the eigenvalues belonging to the interval $]0, 2[$, with the topology inherited from $\mathbb{M}_2(\mathbb{R})$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Let $X$ be a topological space. A mapping $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ is said to be \emph{lower semi-continuous} if the set $\{x \in X \mid f(x) \le \alpha\}$ is closed for every $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$. Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item If $f$ is continuous, then it is lower semi-continuous. \item If the set $\{x \in X \mid f(x) > \alpha\}$ is open for every $\alpha \in \mathbb{Q}$, then $f$ is lower semi-continuous. \item If $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{m}$ is a finite collection of lower semi-continuous functions defined on $X$, then $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by $f(x) = \min_{1 \le n \le m} f_n(x)$ is lower semi-continuous. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 Which of the following statements are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.] \item Let $\mathcal{F}$ be an infinite family of continuous real-valued functions on the interval $[0, 1]$ with the property that given any finite subfamily of functions $\mathcal{F}' \subset \mathcal{F}$, there exists at least one point $t \in [0, 1]$ (depending on the subfamily) such that $f(t) = 0$ for all $f \in \mathcal{F}'$. Then, there exists at least one point $t_0 \in [0, 1]$ such that $f(t_0) = 0$ for all $f \in \mathcal{F}$. \item Let $\mathcal{F} = \{f \in \mathcal{C}^1[0, 1] \mid |f(t)| \le 1 \text{ and } |f'(t)| \le 1 \text{ for all } t \in [0, 1]\}$. Given any sequence in $\mathcal{F}$, there exists a subsequence which converges uniformly on $[0, 1]$. \item If $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ is lower semi-continuous, then there exists $t_0 \in [0, 1]$ such that $f(t_0) = \min_{t \in [0, 1]} f(t)$. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Let $D$ be the closed unit disc in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Let $d(\cdot, \cdot)$ denote the Euclidean distance in $D$. Let $T : D \to D$ be a mapping such that $d(T(x), T(y)) = d(x, y)$ for all points $x$ and $y$ in $D$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ There exists $x_0 \in D$ such that $T(x_0) = x_0$. \qquad b.\ The image of $T$ is closed. \qquad c.\ The mapping $T$ is surjective.
  31. 4.1 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \tan^3 x\,dx$.
  32. 4.2 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\iint_{\mathbb{R}^2} e^{-(19x^2 + 2xy + 19y^2)}\,dx\,dy$.
  33. 4.3 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\iint_S (x^4 + y^4 + z^4)\,dS$, where $S = \{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = a^2\},\ a > 0$.
  34. 4.4 Evaluate $f'(3)$, where $f(x) = \displaystyle\int_{-x}^{x} \frac{1 - e^{-xy}}{y}\,dy,\ x > 0$.
  35. 4.5 Find the maximum value of $3x^2 + 2xy + y^2$ over the set $\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 \mid x^2 + y^2 = 1\}$.
  36. 4.6 Solve: $(x + y)\,dx = (x - y)\,dy$.
  37. 4.7 Find the general solution of the system of differential equations: $\frac{dx}{dt} = x + 2y$, $\frac{dy}{dt} = 3x + 2y$.
  38. 4.8 Find a function whose Laplace transform is given by $\dfrac{1}{s^4 + s^2}$.
  39. 4.9 Find all solutions $(u, \lambda)$ where $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ and $u \not\equiv 0$ of the boundary value problem: $-u''(x) = \lambda u(x)$ for $0 < x < 1$, $u'(0) = u(1) = 0$.
  40. 4.10 Solve the boundary value problem: $-\Delta u = 1$ in $B(0; R) \subset \mathbb{R}^2$, $u = 0$ on $\partial B(0; R)$, where $\Delta$ is the usual Laplace operator in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $B(0; R)$ is the open ball of radius $R (> 0)$ with centre at the origin.
  41. 5.1 Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ be fixed. Let $C_r = \binom{n}{r}$ for $0 \le r \le n$. Evaluate: $C_0^2 + 3C_1^2 + \dots + (2n + 1)C_n^2$.
  42. 5.2 Find all positive integers which leave remainders $5, 4, 3$ and $2$ when divided by $6, 5, 4$ and $3$, respectively.
  43. 5.3 Evaluate: $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{5n + 1}{(2n + 1)!}$.
  44. 5.4 Find the value(s) of $c$ such that the straight line $y = 2x + c$ is tangent to the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$.
  45. 5.5 Find the equation of the plane passing through the point $(-1, 3, 2)$ and which is perpendicular to the planes $x + 2y + 2z = 5$ and $3x + 3y + 2z = 8$.
  46. 5.6 Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $N = (k + 1)^2$. Evaluate the following sum as a closed form expression in terms of $k$: $\sum_{n=1}^{N} [\sqrt{n}]$, where $[x]$ denotes the largest integer less than, or equal to, $x$.
  47. 5.7 A triangle has perimeter of length $16$ units and a fixed base of length $6$ units. What is the maximum value of the area of the triangle?
  48. 5.8 Let $B$ be a square of side $3$ units in length. Ten points are marked in it at random. What is the probability that there exist at least two points amongst them which are of distance less than or equal to $\sqrt{2}$ units apart?
  49. 5.9 Let $X$ be a set and let $V$ be a (real) vector space of real-valued functions defined on $X$, with pointwise addition and scalar multiplication as the operations. Assume in addition that if $f \in V$, then $f^2 \in V$ and $|f| \in V$. Which of the following statements are true?\\ a.\ If $f$ and $g$ are in $V$, then $fg \in V$. \qquad b.\ If $f$ and $g$ are in $V$, then $\max\{f, g\} \in V$. \qquad c.\ If $f \in V$, then $f^3 \in V$.
  50. 5.10 A portion of a wooden cube is sawed off at each vertex so that a small equilateral triangle is formed at each corner with vertices on the edges of the cube. The $24$ vertices of the new object are all connected to each other by straight lines. How many of these lines (with the exception, of course, of their end-points) lie entirely in the interior of the original cube?
