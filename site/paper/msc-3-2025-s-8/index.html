MMO-31

P-02
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1(a) If $K$ and $C$ are compact and closed susets respectively in a topological vector space (tvs) $X$ such that $K\cap C=\phi$, show that there is a neighbourhood (nbd) $V$ of $\theta$ in $X$ such that $(K+V)\cap(C+V)=\phi$. Hence show that every tvs is Hausdorff. [5+2]
  2. 1(b) If $X$ is a locally convex tvs then prove that there exists a nbd base of $\theta$ consisting of convex and balanced nbd. [3]
  3. 2(a) Let $p$ be a seminorm on a vector space $X$. Then for $x,y\in X$, prove the following:\par \hspace*{1em}(i) $p(\theta)=0$;\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) $|p(x)-p(y)|\le p(x-y)$;\par \hspace*{1em}(iii) $p(x)\ge0$;\par \hspace*{1em}(iv) $\{x\in X:p(x)=0\}$ is a subspace of $X$;\par \hspace*{1em}(v) if $B=\{x\in X:p(x)<1\}$, then $B$ is convex, balanced, absorbing and $\mu_B=p$ where $\mu_B$ denotes the Minkowski functional for $B$. [6]
  4. 2(b) (i) Verify that every compact set in a tvs is bounded as well as closed.\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) If $A$ and $B$ are two bounded sets in a tvs $X$ then prove that $A+B$ is bounded in $X$. [4]
  5. 3(a) Let $T$ be a non-zero linear functional define on a tvs $X$. Show that the following statements are equivalent:\par \hspace*{1em}(i) $T$ is continuous\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) Null space $N(T)$ is closed\par \hspace*{1em}(iii) $N(T)$ is non-dense in $X$\par \hspace*{1em}(iv) $T$ is bounded in some nbd of $\theta$. [8]
  6. 3(b) Let $T:(X,\tau)\to(Y,\tau^\prime)$ be a linear mapping where $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\tau^\prime)$ be two tvs. If $T$ is continuous at $\theta$ then show that $T$ is continuous on $X$. [2]
  7. 4(a) Stae and prove Closed Graph Theorem. [6]
  8. 4(b) Let $X$ be a tvs and $Y$ be a Hausdorff space. If $f:X\to Y$ is continuous then prove that the graph $G$ of $f$ is closed. [2]
  9. 4(c) When a tvs is said to be locally convex ? Give an example for the same. [2]
  10. 5(a) Let $A$ and $B$ be disjoint,nonempty and convex sets in a tvs $X$. If $A$ is open then show that there exists $T\in X^*$( dual space of $X$ ) and $\gamma\in\mathbb R$ such that $\mathrm{Re}\,T(x)<\gamma\le\mathrm{Re}\,T(y)$ for every $x\in A$ and $y\in B$. [6]
  11. 5(b) When a tvs $X$ is said to be normable? Prove that $X$ is normable iff its origin $\theta$ has a bounded convex nbd. [4]
  12. 6(a) Let $T_1,T_2,\ldots T_n$ and $T$ be linear functionals on a vector space $X$ and $N=\{x\in X:T_1(x)=T_2(x)=\ldots=T_n(x)=0\}$. Then show that following statements are equivalent.\par \hspace*{1em}(i) There are scalars $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\ldots\alpha_n$ such that $T=\alpha_1T_1+\alpha_2T_2+\ldots+\alpha_nT_n$\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) There exists $\gamma<\infty$ such that $|T(x)|\le\gamma\ max_j|T_j(x)|,\ x\in X,1\le j\le n$.\par \hspace*{1em}(iii) $T(x)=0\ \forall x\in X$. [5]
  13. 6(b) If $Y$ is a subspace of a tvs $X$ and $Y$ is an F-space ( with respect to the topology inherited from $X$ ), then prove that $Y$ is a closed subspace of $X$. [5]
