MMO-31(A8)

Computational Fluid Dynamics-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1(a) Classify the partial differential equation\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}+\left(5+2x^2\right)\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial t}+\left(1+x^2\right)\left(4+x^2\right)\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=0\] and find the characteristics. Reduce the equation to its standard form. [5]
  2. 1(b) Find the second order accurate discretized form of second order partial derivative with respect to one independent variable using the values of the dependent variable at the $(i,j),(i-1,j),(i-2,j)$ and $(i-3,j)$ points. [5]
  3. 2(a) If the heat conduction equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$ is approximated by the schemes of Schmidt and Laasonen then determine the truncation errors. Also investigate the stability of both the methods using Von Neuman method. [6]
  4. 2(b) Show that the DuFort and Frankel method of solving the heat conduction equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$ becomes the hyperbolic equation $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}+C^2\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}-\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=0$ under finite difference approximations and certain condition of grid spacings. [4]
  5. 3(a) If a difference method for the numerical solution of $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$, $t>0$ under suitable initial and boundary conditions be written as $u_m^{n+1}=\dfrac43u_m^n-\dfrac13u_m^{n-1}+\dfrac{2\lambda}3\delta_x^2u_m^{n+1}$ where $\lambda=k/h^2$, find the truncation error and examine the stability of the method. If the initial condition is\par \hspace*{2em}$u(x,0)=x\qquad0\le x\le\frac12$\par \hspace*{4.4em}$=1-x\quad\frac12\le x\le1$\par and the boundary conditions are $u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0$, find the finite difference solution where $h=\frac14$ and $\lambda=\frac12$. Perform upto two time steps. \par\hfill P.T.O. [10]
  6. 4(a) Show that the partial differential equation $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}$ with $u=f(x)$ and $\partial u/\partial t=g(x)$ at $t=0$, may be replaced by the difference scheme\par \[u_m^{n+1}=-u_m^{n-1}+2\left(1-r^2\right)u_m^n+r^2\left(u_{m+1}^n+u_{m-1}^n\right)\] \[u_m^0=f_m\] \[u_m^1=kg_m+\left(1-r^2\right)f_m+\tfrac12r^2\left(f_{m+1}+f_{m-1}\right)\] where $r=k/h$. Find an expression for the local truncation error of each of the finite difference expressions and find the order of the method.\par Using\par \hspace*{2em}$f(x)=x,\ 0\le x\le\frac12$\par \hspace*{2.9em}$=1-x,\ \frac12\le x\le1$\par \hspace*{2em}$g(x)=0,\ 0\le x\le1$\par and $u(0,t)=0=u(1,t)$, find the solution upto two time steps with $h=0.2,\ r=\frac12$. [10]
  7. 5 Consider the initial-boundary value problem $\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=\dfrac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-2C\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t},\ 0<x\le1,\ t>0$\par \hspace*{2em}$u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0,\ u(x,0)=f(x),u_t(x,0)=g(x)$.\par \hspace*{1em}i) If the finite difference approximation $\delta_t^2u_m^n=r^2\delta_x^2u_m^n-crh\,\delta_{2t}u_m^n$ is used, where $r=k/h$, show that the difference scheme is stable if $0<r\le1$.\par \hspace*{1em}ii) If the implicit difference approximation $\delta_t^2u_m^n=r^2\left(1+\tau\delta_t^2\right)\delta_x^2u_m^n-crh\,\delta_{2t}u_m^n$ is used, where $r=k/h$ and $\tau$ is arbitrary, then show that the order of accuracy of the method is $k^2+h^2$ for arbitrary $\tau$. What stability criterion is applicable? [10]
  8. 6 Find the solution of the mixed boundary value problem\par \hspace*{2em}$\nabla^2u=0,\ x>0,y>0,\ x^2+y^2<1$\par \hspace*{2em}$u(x,y)=x^2-y^2$ on $x=0$ or $y=0$\par \hspace*{2em}$\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial n}=2\left(x^2-y^2\right)$, on $x^2+y^2=1$\par with step length $h=\frac12$ and a five point difference scheme. [10]
