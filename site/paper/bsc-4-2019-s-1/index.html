BMC-41

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1(a) Evaluate the integral $\int_0^1 dx\int_x^{1/x}\frac{y\,dy}{(1+xy)^2(1+y^2)}$ by changing the order of the integration. [5]
  2. 1(b) Evaluate $\iiint_V\frac{dx\,dy\,dz}{(1+x+y+z)^3}$, where $V$ is the tetrahedron bounded by the planes $x=0$, $y=0$, $z=0$ and $x+y+z=1$. [5]
  3. 2(a) Evaluate $\iint_E\frac{dx\,dy}{\sqrt{(x+y+1)^2-4xy}}$, by using the transformation $x=u(1+v)$, $y=v(1+u)$, where $E$ is the triangle with vertices $(0,0),(2,0)$ and $(2,2)$. [5]
  4. 2(b) Test the convergence of the integral $\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos x\,dx}{1+x^2}$. [2]
  5. 2(c) Discuss the convergence of the Beta function $\beta(m,n)=\int_0^1 x^{m-1}(1-x)^{n-1}dx$. [3]
  6. 3(a) Evaluate: $\int_0^1\sqrt{1-x^4}\,dx$. [2.5]
  7. 3(b) Evaluate: $\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{\sin x}}dx\int_0^{\pi/2}\sqrt{\sin x}\,dx$. [2.5]
  8. 3(c) Using differentiation under integral sign, find the value of $F(2,3)$, where $F(a,b)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{dx}{(a^2\cos^2x+b^2\sin^2x)^2}$. [5]
  9. 4(a) Show that the function $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}x^2\sin\frac1x;&\text{if }x\ne0\\0;&\text{if }x=0\end{cases}$, is a function of bounded variation on $[0,1]$. [5]
  10. 4(b) Let $f$ is a monotonic function on $[a,b]$, and $\alpha$ is a monotonic increasing function on $[a,b]$. If $\alpha$ is continuous then show that $f$ is Riemann-Stieltjes integrable on $[a,b]$. [5]
  11. 5(a) Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be a bounded function and $P$ be a partition of $[a,b]$ with $\|P\|=\delta$. If $P_k$ be a refinement of $P$ with $k$ additional points, then show that \[\begin{gathered} U(P,f)\ge U(P_k,f),\ L(P,f)\le L(P_k,f);\ U(P_k,f)-U(P,f)\ge(m-M)kS,\\ L(P_k,f)-L(P,f)\le(M-m)k\delta, \end{gathered}\] where $m=\inf_{x\in[a,b]}f(x)$ and $M=\sup_{x\in[a,b]}f(x)$. [2+3]
  12. 5(b) Find the upper and lower sum of $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ defined by $f(x)=\begin{cases}x;&\text{if }x\text{ is rational}\\x^2;&\text{if }x\text{ is irrational}\end{cases}$ [5]
  13. 6(a) Show that if $f(x)$ be monotone on $[a,b]$ then $\int_a^b f(x)$ lies in between $f(a)(b-a)$ and $f(b)(b-a)$. [3]
  14. 6(b) Let $f(x)$ be Riemann integrable and non negative on $[a,b]$. If $f(x)$ be continuous at $c$ on $[a,b]$ and $f(c)>0$, then prove that $\int_a^b f(x)dx>0$. [3]
  15. 6(c) Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\int_0^x\sin t\,dt}{x^2}$, if it exists. [2]
  16. 6(d) Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}x;&\text{if }x\ne\frac1n\\1;&\text{if }x=\frac1n;\ n=1,2,\ldots\end{cases}$ Is $f$ Riemann integrable on $[0,1]$? \fbox{\shortstack[l]{For Non-CBCS\\Students only}} [2]
