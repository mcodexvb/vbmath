CCMA-4

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2022
  1. 1 Answer any five questions: [\(5\times2=10\)]
  2. 1(a) Determine the eccentricity of the conic \(xy=c^2\), where the rectangular axes are rotated through the angle \(\frac{\pi}{4}\).
  3. 1(b) Find the condition that the lines joining the origin to the point of intersection of the line \(lx+my+n=0\) and the curve \(y^2=4a(x+a)\) to be at right angle.
  4. 1(c) Find the length of the tangent from \((2,2)\) to the circle \(x^2+y^2-2x-2y+1=0\).
  5. 1(d) Find the value of \(a\) for which the equation \(ax^2+xy-12y^2+2x-31y-20=0\) represents a pair of straight lines.
  6. 1(e) Find the pole of the straight line \(x+y+3=0\) with respect to the circle \(x^2+y^2-2x+5=0\).
  7. 1(f) Examine whether the numbers \(\frac12,\frac12,\frac12\) are the direction cosines of any directed line or not. Find the direction cosines of the line joining the points \((1,2,3)\) and \((2,3,4)\).
  8. 1(g) Determine the values of \(a\) for which the plane \(x+y+z=a\sqrt3\) touches the sphere \(x^2+y^2+z^2-2x-2y-2z-6=0\).
  9. 2 If, by a rotation of rectangular axes about the origin, the expression \((ax^2+2hxy+by^2)\) changes to \((a'x'^2+2h'x'y'+b'y'^2)\), then prove that (i) \(a+b=a'+b'\) and (ii) \(ab-h^2=a'b'-h'^2\). [5]
  10. 3 Find the equation of the line joining the feet of the perpendiculars from the point \((r,0)\) on the lines \(ax^2+2hxy+by^2=0\). [5]
  11. 4 Tangents are drawn to the parabola \(y^2=4ax\), at the points whose abscissae, are in the ratio \(p:1\). Show that the locus of their point of intersection is a parabola. [5]
  12. 5 The normal to the rectangular hyperbola \(xy=c^2\) at a point \(P\) on it meets the curve again at \(Q\) and touches the conjugate hyperbola. Show that \((PQ)^4=512c^4\). [5]
  13. 6 A sphere of constant radius \(k\) passes through the origin \(O\) and cuts the axes in \(A,B,C\) respectively. Prove that the locus of the foot of the perpendicular from \(O\) to the plane \(ABC\) is \((x^2+y^2+z^2)^2(x^{-2}+y^{-2}+z^{-2})=4k^2\). [5]
  14. 7 The section of a cone whose guiding curve is \(\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1,\ z=0\) by the plane \(x=0\) is a rectangular hyperbola. Show that the locus of the vertex is \(\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2+z^2}{4}=1\). [5]
  15. 1 Answer any five questions: [\(5\times2=10\)]
  16. 1(a) Find the area of the parallelogram having diagonals \(3\vec i+\vec j-2\vec k\) and \(\vec i-3\vec j+4\vec k\).
  17. 1(b) If \(\vec a\) and \(\vec c\) be any two given vectors and \(k\), a given scalar. Determine all vectors \(\vec r\) such that \(\vec a\times\vec r=\vec c\) and \(\vec a\cdot\vec r=k\).
  18. 1(c) Define scalar triple product of three vectors \(\vec a,\vec b,\vec c\) and give its geometric interpretation.
  19. 1(d) Find the work done by the forces \(2\vec i-3\vec j+2\vec k\) and \(\vec i+2\vec k\) which together work on a particle and displace it from the point \((1,1,2)\) to the point \((0,1,-1)\).
  20. 1(e) Find the maximum value of the directional derivative at \((1,1,-1)\) of \(\phi=x^2-2y^2+4z^2\).
  21. 1(f) For what value of \(\lambda\), the vector \(\vec v=(x+3y)\vec i+(y-2z)\vec j+(x+\lambda z)\vec k\) is solenoidal.
  22. 1(g) Evaluate \(\oint_C\{(3x+4y)\,dx+(2x-3y)\,dy\}\) by Green's theorem where \(C\) is the circle in the \(xy\)-plane with centre at the origin and radius 2 units.
  23. 2(a) Use vector method to show that \(\cos(A-B)=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B\). [3]
  24. 2(b) Prove the identity \(\vec\alpha\times[\vec\alpha\times\{\vec\alpha\times(\vec\alpha\times\vec\beta)\}]=|\vec\alpha|^4\vec\beta\), where \(\vec\alpha\cdot\vec\beta=0\). [2]
  25. 3(a) State Serret-Frenet formulae. [2]
  26. 3(b) Show that for the curve \(\vec r=\vec r(s)\). \(\dfrac{d\vec r}{ds}\cdot\dfrac{d^2\vec r}{ds^2}\times\dfrac{d^3\vec r}{ds^3}=\kappa^2\tau\), where \(\kappa\) is the curvature, \(\tau\) the torsion and \(s\) the arc length of the curve. [3]
  27. 4 State and prove a necessary and sufficient condition for a vector function \(\vec r=\vec F(t)\) to have a constant direction. [5]
  28. 5 Show that the vector \(\vec f=(2x-yz)\vec i+(2y-zx)\vec j+(2z-xy)\vec k\) is irrotational. For \(\vec f\), find a scalar function \(\phi\) such that \(\vec f=\vec\nabla\phi\).
  29. 6 Prove the identity \(\vec\nabla\times(\vec\nabla\times\vec F)=-\nabla^2\vec F+\vec\nabla(\vec\nabla\cdot\vec F)\). [5]
  30. 7(a) If the vectors \(\vec F\) and \(\vec G\) be irrotational, then show that the vector \(\vec F\times\vec G\) is solenoidal. [3]
  31. 7(b) If \(\vec R\) be a unit vector in the direction of \(\vec r\), then prove that \(\vec R\times\dfrac{d\vec R}{dt}=\dfrac1{r^2}\vec r\times\dfrac{d\vec r}{dt}\), where \(r=|\vec r|\). [2]
