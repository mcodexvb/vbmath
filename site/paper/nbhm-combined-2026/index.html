NBHM · 2026 · Master’s and doctoral scholarship

  1. 1 A number is selected at random from the set $\{1, 2, \dots, 63\}$. What is the probability that it is coprime to $63$?
  2. 2 A professor graded $5$ exam papers and returned them randomly to $5$ students. Assume that these are the same students whose exam papers were graded. What is the number of ways in which no student receives their own exam paper?
  3. 3 Let $\mathbb{F}_7$ denote the field with $7$ elements. The cardinality of the ring \[ \frac{\mathbb{F}_7[x]}{\langle x^3 + 2x^2 + 6x + 5,\ x^3 + 3x^2 + 6x + 4 \rangle} \] is equal to what?
  4. 4 Describe all those positive integers $n$ such that the decimal expansion of the rational number $m/n$ --- where $m$ is a positive integer such that $m$ and $n$ are coprime --- is eventually constant (like $123.425555\dots$). (\textbf{Note.} To distinguish between the set of \textbf{positive} integers and the set of natural numbers, use $\mathbb{Z}_+$ to denote the former and $\mathbb{N}$ to denote the latter, \emph{if needed}.)
  5. 5 You are given that the equation $3^x = x^9$ has two real solutions. Determine whether it has any integer solutions and, if it does, then give all integer solutions. If there are no integer solutions, then write `no integer solutions'.
  6. 6 Usually we play carrom on a square board. However, there are games (like billiards) played on a rectangular board. Assume you are playing carrom on a rectangular board (with holes at its four corners) of length $l$ and breadth $b$ (so, $l > b$). Further, let $l$ and $b$ be two coprime positive integers. If we shoot the striker from one of the corners at a $45$ degree angle, how many times will the striker bounce off an edge of the board before it falls into a hole? (The figure below shows the striker's trajectory for a board with $l = 5$ and $b = 3$. In this example, the striker bounces $6$ times.) \begin{center} \relax {\emph{[Figure: $5 \times 3$ rectangular grid showing striker trajectory from $(0,0)$ to $(5,3)$ with $6$ bounces.]}} \end{center}
  7. 7 Let $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be twice differentiable and $f'(0) = f'(1) = 0$. Let $A = |f(1) - f(0)|$ and $B = \sup\{|f''(b) - f''(a)| : a, b \in (0, 1)\}$. Which of the following is true for any $f$ with the stated properties? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $B \le 4A$. \item $B \ge 8A$. \item There does not exist any relation between $A$ and $B$. \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Let $j \ge k$ be positive integers and let $R_k^j$ denote the number of distinguishable ways in which $j$ distinct balls can be put into $k$ different but indistinguishable urns in such a way that each urn receives at least one ball. Now, let $n > m$ be two positive integers. If we know that \[ R_{m+1}^n = A\left[R_m^{n-1} + \frac{(n - 1)!}{2!(n - 2)!}R_m^{n-2} + \cdots + \frac{(n - 1)!}{m!(n - m)!}R_m^m\right], \] then find the value of $A$.
  9. 9 Let $n$ be an integer $\ge 2$, let $P, Q \notin \{0, I_n\}$ be two linear projection maps from $\mathbb{R}^n$ to $\mathbb{R}^n$, where $I_n$ is the identity on $\mathbb{R}^n$, and suppose $Q - P$ is also a projection. Find the value of $\operatorname{trace}(QP + PQ)$ in terms of $P$.
  10. 10 Identify explicitly the set $\{\operatorname{trace}(A) : A \in M_3(\mathbb{R}),\ A^T A = I_3,\ \det(A) = 1,\ A \neq I_3\}$, where $M_3(\mathbb{R})$ is the set of all $3 \times 3$ matrices with real entries and $I_3$ is the $3 \times 3$ identity matrix.
  11. 11 Let $A$ be a $4 \times 4$ complex matrix whose minimal polynomial is $x^2 - 1$. What is the value of $\operatorname{rank}(A + I_4) + \operatorname{rank}(A - I_4)$, where $I_4$ is the $4 \times 4$ identity matrix?
  12. 12 Let $\mathbb{D}$ denote the open disc $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}$ in the complex plane. Let $f$ be any function that is holomorphic on a neighbourhood of $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ such that $|f(z)| > 2$ for each $z \in \partial\mathbb{D}$ and $f(0) = 1$. Which of the following is/are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ is always non-vanishing in $\mathbb{D}$. \item $f$ is never non-vanishing in $\mathbb{D}$. \item $f$ always has a simple zero in $\mathbb{D}$. \item It is possible for $f$ to have a zero of multiplicity at least $2$ in $\mathbb{D}$. \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 Two series of the form $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n z^n$ and $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} b_n z^n$, where $a_n, b_n \in \mathbb{C}$ for all $n$, are said to be \emph{distinct} if $a_n \neq b_n$ for at least one $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. You are asked to expand the function \[ f(z) = \frac{1}{(z + 1)^2} + \frac{1}{(z - 2)^3} \] in a series of the above form that is absolutely convergent at each point $z$ in a specified domain, where this domain is preserved by any rotation about $0 \in \mathbb{C}$. In how many such distinct series can $f$ be expanded?
  14. 14 Let $P = \{a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n\}$ be a permutation of $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$. For $n \ge 3$, we say that $P$ is a zig-zag if $a_1 > a_2 < a_3 > a_4 < \cdots$. Let $Z_n$ denote the number of zig-zags with $n$ elements. For instance, $Z_3 = 2$, given by the zig-zags $2 > 1 < 3$ and $3 > 1 < 2$. Given that $Z_3 = 2$, $Z_4 = 5$ and $Z_5 = 16$, what is the value of $Z_6$?
  15. 15 Let $A$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix with minimal polynomial $x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 2$. Let $f$ be a function on $3 \times 3$ matrices given by $f(M) = M^4 - M^2 + I$. What is the determinant of $f(A)$?
  16. 16 Let $A_6$ denote the group of all even permutations of $6$ symbols. How many elements of order $5$ are there in $A_6$?
  17. 17 Let $(a_n)_{n \ge 1}$ be the sequence of real numbers satisfying $a_1 = 1$ and $a_{n+1} = a_n + (1/a_n)$ for all $n \ge 1$. Determine the value of $\lfloor a_{100} \rfloor$, the greatest integer $\le a_{100}$.
  18. 18 For which of the following functions $f : (-1, 1) \to \mathbb{R}$ does there exist a function $F : (-1, 1) \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $F$ is differentiable on $(-1, 1)$ and $F'(x) = f(x)$ for all $x \in (-1, 1)$? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f(x) = |x|$. \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} -1, & \text{if } x < 0, \\ 1, & \text{if } x \ge 0. \end{cases}$ \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} \sin(1/x), & \text{if } x \neq 0, \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} \cos(1/x), & \text{if } x \neq 0, \\ 1, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$ \end{enumerate}
  19. 19 For which real numbers $x$ do the vectors $(1, x, x, x)$, $(x, 1, x, x)$, $(x, x, 1, x)$, $(x, x, x, 1)$ \textbf{not} form a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$?
  20. 20 Given $x \in \mathbb{R}$, let $\sqrt[3]{x}$ denote the unique \textbf{real} number $y$ such that $y^3 = x$ (i.e., the unique real cube root of $x$). Let $f : [-1, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be an even function that satisfies \[ f(x) = f(\sqrt[3]{x}) \qquad \forall x \in [-1, 1]. \] Assume that $f$ is continuous at $x = 1$. What can you say about $f$? Give the most specific answer possible from the information given.
  21. 21 Consider the ODE $\ddot{x} + (\dot{x})^2 = 0$ in $(0, \infty)$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item It has two linearly independent solutions in $(0, \infty)$. \item It has at most two linearly independent solutions in $(0, \infty)$. \end{enumerate}
  22. 22 Given a sequence $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ of functions $f_n : [0, 1] \to [-1, 1]$, define the function $h$ on $[0, 1]$ by \[ h(x) = \sup\{f_1(x), f_2(x), f_3(x), \dots\}. \] \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If every $f_n$ is continuous, then so is $h$. \item If every $f_n$ is non-negative and differentiable, then so is $h$. \end{enumerate}
  23. 23 Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a field and fix an integer $n \ge 2$. Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices with entries in $\mathbb{F}$. Let $I_n$ denote the $n \times n$ identity matrix. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Whenever $(I_n - AB)$ is invertible, $(I_n - BA)$ is necessarily invertible. \item Whenever $(I_n - AB)$ and $(I_n - BA)$ are both invertible, $(I_n - BA)^{-1}$ can be expressed as an $\mathbb{F}$-linear combination of monomials involving $I_n, A, B$, and $(I_n - AB)^{-1}$. \end{enumerate}
  24. 24 Consider the real line $\mathbb{R}$ equipped with the standard Euclidean metric $d$ and let $\mathbb{Z}_+$ denote the set of all positive integers. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Let $S$ be the subset of $\mathbb{R}$ defined by $S = \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{Z}_+} I_n$ where $I_n = [n, n + n^{-2}]$. The function $f : S \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by $f(x) = x^2$ is uniformly continuous. \item There exists a metric $D$ on $\mathbb{R}$ such that the Cauchy sequences of $(\mathbb{R}, d)$ and $(\mathbb{R}, D)$ are identical and the identity map from $(\mathbb{R}, d)$ to $(\mathbb{R}, D)$ is continuous but not uniformly continuous. \end{enumerate}
  25. 25 Let $d$ denote the Euclidean distance on $\mathbb{R}^3$. Assume $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ is any isometry of the metric space $(\mathbb{R}^3, d)$ that is not the identity map. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item If $f$ is an orientation preserving isometry that fixes the origin, then the set $\{x \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid f(x) = x\}$ is a one dimensional subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$. \item A composition of reflections with respect to at most $4$ distinct affine planes in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is sufficient to express $f$. \end{enumerate}
  26. 26 Let $K/\mathbb{Q}$ be a Galois extension with Galois group $D_4$, the dihedral group with $8$ elements. The number of intermediate fields $\mathbb{Q} \subsetneq L \subsetneq K$ such that $[L : \mathbb{Q}] = 4$ is equal to what?
  27. 27 State the value of \[ \sum_{k=0}^{2026} (-1)^k \binom{2026}{k} (2026 - k)^{2026}. \]
  28. 28 Let $\mathbb{F}_p$ denote the field with $p$ elements. Which of the following rings are fields? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\mathbb{F}_7[x, y]/\langle x^2 + 1,\ y + x \rangle$ \item $\mathbb{F}_7[x]/\langle x^2 + 1 \rangle$ \item $\mathbb{F}_{11}[x]/\langle x^3 + 2x + 5 \rangle$ \item $\mathbb{F}_{11}[x, y]/\langle x^2 + 1,\ y^{11} - y - 2x \rangle$ \end{enumerate}
  29. 29 What is the largest positive integer $k$ such that $5^N$ and $5^{N+k}$ have the same last $k$ decimal digits for all $N$ sufficiently large?
  30. 30 Let $\mathbb{D}$ denote the open disc $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}$ in the complex plane. You are given a sequence $(x_n)_{n \ge 1}$ in $(0, 1)$ such that $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = 0$ and a sequence of non-constant functions $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ such that each $f_n$ is continuous on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$, holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$, and maps $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ into $\{x + iy \in \overline{\mathbb{D}} : x \ge 0\}$. For any positive integer $p$, let $(\cdot)^{1/p}$ denote the \textbf{principal} holomorphic branch of the $p$-th root. Suppose $(p_n)_{n \ge 1}$ is an increasing sequence of positive integers such that \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} (f_n(0))^{1/p_n} \text{ exists and is not equal to } 1. \] Which of the following is/are true? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item There always exists a sequence of indices $(n_k)_{k \ge 1}$ such that $\lim_{k \to \infty} (f_{n_k}(x_{n_k}))^{1/p_{n_k}} = 1$. \item There always exists a sequence of indices $(n_k)_{k \ge 1}$ such that $\lim_{k \to \infty} f_{n_k}(x_{n_k})$ exists. \item The sequence $(f_n(x_n))_{n \ge 1}$ is never convergent. \item For any $(x_n)_{n \ge 1}$ and $(f_n)_{n \ge 1}$ as above, there exist a sequence $(p_n)_{n \ge 1}$ (with the above-mentioned properties) and a sequence of indices $(n_k)_{k \ge 1}$ such that $\lim_{k \to \infty} (f_{n_k}(x_{n_k}))^{1/p_{n_k}} = 1$. \end{enumerate}
  31. 31 Let $n \ge 2$. Let the minimal polynomial of $A \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$, an $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries, be $m_A(x) = (x - \lambda)^n$ for some $\lambda \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$. Describe, in terms of $A$, all $B \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ that commute with $A$.
  32. 32 Let $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function such that $f(0) = f(1)$. Let $A$ be the set consisting of all those $n \in \mathbb{Z}_+$ (i.e., all those positive integers $n$) such that there exist $a_n, b_n \in [0, 1]$ satisfying $b_n - a_n = 1/n$ and $f(b_n) = f(a_n)$. Describe the set $A$.
  33. 33 Write down the set of all real numbers $a$ for which the integral \[ \int_0^{\infty} \frac{|\cos(2x) - \cos(x)|}{x^a}\,dx \] exists (i.e., the integral is finite).
  34. 34 Suppose that an urn contains $n_1$ red balls and $n_2$ black balls, where $n_1, n_2 > 0$. A fair $6$-faced die is thrown and the number seen on its upturned face, say $N$, is noted. Next, $N$ balls are drawn from the urn \textbf{with replacement}. What is the probability that an equal number of red and black balls are drawn? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{n_1 n_2}{(n_1 + n_2)^4} + \dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{n_1^2 n_2^2}{(n_1 + n_2)^4} + \dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{n_1^3 n_2^3}{(n_1 + n_2)^4}$ \item $\dfrac{1}{3}\dfrac{n_1 n_2}{(n_1 + n_2)^2} + \dfrac{n_1^2 n_2^2}{(n_1 + n_2)^4} + \dfrac{10}{3}\dfrac{n_1^3 n_2^3}{(n_1 + n_2)^6}$ \item $\dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{n_1 n_2}{(n_1 + n_2)^2} + \dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{n_1^2 n_2^2}{(n_1 + n_2)^4} + \dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{n_1^3 n_2^3}{(n_1 + n_2)^6}$ \item $\dfrac{n_1 n_2}{(n_1 + n_2)^2} + \dfrac{n_1^2 n_2^2}{(n_1 + n_2)^4} + \dfrac{10}{3}\dfrac{n_1^3 n_2^3}{(n_1 + n_2)^6}$ \end{enumerate}
  35. 35 Let $\mathbb{F}_3$ denote the field with $3$ elements, let $M_3(\mathbb{F}_3)$ denote the set of all $3 \times 3$ matrices with entries in $\mathbb{F}_3$, and let \[ J = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}. \] The number of matrices $B \in M_3(\mathbb{F}_3)$ that are similar to $J$ is equal to what?
  36. 36 A Banach space $(X, \|\cdot\|)$ is said to be strictly convex if $\|x\| = 1 = \|y\|$, $x \neq y$, implies $\|(x + y)/2\| < 1$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(\mathbb{R}^n, \|\cdot\|_1)$, $n \ge 2$, where $\|x\|_1 = |x_1| + \cdots + |x_n|$, is not strictly convex. \item For every nonzero, bounded linear functional $f$ on a strictly convex Banach space $(X, \|\cdot\|)$, there is at most one $x \in X$ satisfying $\|x\| = 1$ and $f(x) = \|f\|_{\mathrm{op}}$. Here $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ denotes the operator norm. \end{enumerate}
  37. 37 For a ring $R$, denote as $R[x]$ the polynomial ring over $R$ in the variable $x$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The polynomial $x^5 + 5x^2 + 10$ is irreducible in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$. \item $x^5 + 2x + 1$ is irreducible in $\mathbb{F}_3[x]$. \end{enumerate}
  38. 38 Consider linear Diophantine equations of the form $ax + by = c$, where $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Z}$ and the solutions $(x, y)$ are restricted to positive integers. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Given any pair of positive integers $m$ and $n$, there exists a linear equation $ax + by = c$ such that the only solution in positive integers is $(x, y) = (m, n)$. \item For any positive integer $k$, there exists a linear equation $ax + by = c$ such that there are exactly $k$ solutions in positive integers. \end{enumerate}
  39. 39 Consider the following subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$: \[ X = [0, 1] \times \{0\} \cup \left(\bigcup_{r \in \mathbb{Q} \cap [0, 1]} \{r\} \times [0, 1 - r]\right). \] \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $X$ deformation retracts to the point $(0, 1)$. \item $X$ deformation retracts to the point $(1, 0)$. \end{enumerate}
  40. 40 Let $f : [0, \infty) \to [0, \infty)$ be a non-negative continuous function, and suppose $f \not\equiv 0$. Define $S_f := \{t \in (0, \infty) : \int_0^{\infty} (f(x))^t\,dx < \infty\}$. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The set $S_f$ is connected for any $f$ for which $S_f \neq \emptyset$. \item There exists a function $f$ with the above-mentioned properties such that $S_f$ is a non-empty finite set. \end{enumerate}
