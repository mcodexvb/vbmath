BMC-21

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2017
  1. 1 Answer \textit{any five} questions: \par
  2. 1(a) Find volume and surface area of a sphere of radius $a$.
  3. 1(b) Find asymptote of the curve $x^3+y^3=3axy$.
  4. 1(c) Suppose $A(a,a')$ and $B(b,b')$ are two points on a curve $y=f(x)$. Show that entire length of the arc $AB=\int_a^b\sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2}\,dx$.
  5. 1(d) Find the area between the curves $y=\sin x$ and $y=\cos x$ in $[0,\pi]$.
  6. 1(e) Show that $\limsup(a_n+b_n)\le\limsup a_n+\limsup b_n$.
  7. 1(f) Show that the sequence $\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n\frac1i\right\}$ is an unbounded sequence.
  8. 1(g) State Leibintz test for alternating series. Show that $\sum(-1)^n\frac1{\sqrt n}$ is conditionally convergent.
  9. 2(a) State and prove the necessary conditions for a power series representation of a function. [4]
  10. 2(b) Expand $\sin x$ in power series. [3]
  11. 2(c) State Taylor's theorem with Cauchy's form of remainder. Show that it is a generalisation of Lagrange's mean value theorem. [3]
  12. 3(a) Define envelope of a family of curves. Show that the envelope touches every member of the family at characteristic points. [1+4]
  13. 3(b) Find the envelope of the family of circles whose centres lie on the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ and which passes through its centre. [5]
  14. 4(a) If $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$ be the radii of curvatures at the ends $P$ and $D$ of conjugate diameters $CP$ and $CD$ respectively on the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=I$, where $C$ being the centre of the ellipse, then show that $\rho_1^{2/3}+\rho_2^{2/3}=\frac{a^2+b^2}{(ab)^{2/3}}$. [5]
  15. 4(b) Give a rough sketch of the curve $x=a(\theta+\sin\theta),\ y=a(1-\cos\theta)$, and find the length of its one arc. [5]
  16. 5(a) Show that a Cauchy sequence is convergent. [4]
  17. 5(b) Define upper and lower limits of a sequence. Show that they are equal if and only if the sequence is convergent. [2+4]
  18. 6(a) Find the total area bounded by the cardioid $r=a(1-\cos\theta)$. [4]
  19. 6(b) State comparison test in limit form for a series of positive terms and prove it. [3]
  20. 6(c) What is a $p$-series? Show that a $p$-series diverges for $p\ge I$. [3]
  21. 7(a) State and prove Kummer's test for the series of positive terms. Show that Raabe's test can be derived from Kummer's test. [4+2]
  22. 7(b) Show that the series $\frac{1^2}{2^2}+\frac{1^2\cdot3^2}{2^2\cdot4^2}+\frac{1^2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2}{2^2\cdot4^2\cdot6^2}+\ldots\ldots\ldots\ldots$ is divergent. [4]
