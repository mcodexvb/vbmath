MMC-42

Operations Research
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) What do you know about the relationship between regular simplex method and revised simplex method in connection with the finding of an optional solution to an LPP? [2]
  2. 1(b) Given the following LPP:\par Maximize $f(x_1,x_2)=3x_1+5x_2$\par subject to \quad $x_1\le4$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_2\le6$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$3x_1+2x_2\le18$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$.\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace Find an optimum solution to this programme by applying revised simplex method.\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace Is the solution degenerate? Give reason for your answer.
  3. 2(a) State the economical use of the sensitivity analysis. [2]
  4. 2(b) From the optimal simplex table of the following LPP\par Maximize $f(x_1,x_2,x_3)=2x_1-x_2+3x_3$\par subject to \quad $3x_1+x_2-2x_3\le6$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$2x_1+5x_2+x_3\le14$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1+4x_2+2x_3\le8$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2,x_3\ge0$,\par find the ranges of $C_2$ and $C_3$ such that the optimal basis remains unchanged. [8]
  5. 3(a) Define pure and mixed integer linear programming problem (ILPP). [2]
  6. 3(b) Solve the following all-integer programming problem using the `Gomory's cutting plane method'/`branch and bound method' :\par Minimize $f(x_1,x_2)=3x_1+\frac52x_2$\par subject to \quad $x_1+2x_2\ge20$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$3x_1+2x_2\ge50$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$ and integers. \par\hfill P.T.O. \begin{center}[ 2 ]\end{center} [8]
  7. 4(a) Distinguish between a linear programming problem (LPP) and a non-linear programming problem (NLPP). [2]
  8. 4(b) Using the Kuhn-Tucker's necessary and sufficient conditions solve the following NLPP:\par Maximize $f(x_1,x_2)=7x_1^2+6x_1+5x_2$\par subject to \quad $x_1+2x_2\le10$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1-3x_2\le9$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$. [4]
  9. 4(c) Show the following NLPP graphically:\par Minimize $f(x_1,x_2)=(x_1-1)^2+(x_2-2)^2$\par subject to \quad $x_1\le2$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_2\le1$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$. [4]
  10. 5(a) Find expressions for the optimal lot size and corresponding annual cost in an EOQ purchase model in which the demand is uniform, several production runs over unequal lengths and shortages are not allowed. [6]
  11. 5(b) The demand for an item is 6000 units per year. Its production rate is 1000 units per month. The carrying cost is Rs. 50 per unit per year and the set-up cost is Rs. 2000 per set-up. The shortage cost is Rs. 1000 per unit per year. Find various parameters of the inventory system. [4]
  12. 6(a) For $M/M/1:\infty/FCFS$ queue model, obtain steady state solution of the differential equations satisfied by probability $P_n$ of $n$ customers in the system.\par Also, obtain $P_n$ for $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ [6]
  13. 6(b) If for period of 2 hours in a day (8 AM-10 AM) trains arrive at a yard every 20 minutes but the service time continues to remain 36 minutes, then, for this period, calculate the following: \par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace the probability that the yard is empty,\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace average queue length on the assumption that the line capacity of the yard is limited to 4 trains only.
