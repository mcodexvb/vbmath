MMC-35

Galois Theory-I and Multivariable Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2019
  1. 1(a) Show that any field is either an extension of $\mathbb Z_p$ (where $p$ is prime) or $\mathbb Q$. [4]
  2. 1(b) If $[F:\mathbb Q]=2$, then show that there exists a square free integer $m$ different from 1 so that $F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt m)$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Let $F/K$ be a field extension of a field $K$ and $R$ be a ring such that $K\subseteq R\subseteq F$. Prove that if every element of $R$ is algebraic over $K$, then $R$ is a field. [3]
  4. 2(a) Find a splitting fields of the polynomial $x^2+x+1$ over $\mathbb Z_5$. Find $[S:\mathbb Z_5]$. [4]
  5. 2(b) Let $K$ be a field and $g(x)=x^n-x\in K[x]$. Show that if char $K=0$ or if char $K=p$ and $p\nmid n-1$, then $g(x)$ has no multiple root in any extension of $K$. [3]
  6. 2(c) Suppose $F/K$ be a field extension of a field $K$. Show that following statements are equivalent:\par \hspace*{1em}i) Every irreducible polynomial in $K[x]$ with one root in $F$ has all roots in $F$.\par \hspace*{1em}ii) Every $K$-isomorphism of $F$ is a $K$-automorphism. [3]
  7. 3(a) Show that any two fields of same order are isomorphic. [3]
  8. 3(b) How many subfields are there in a field of order $p^{121}$. Justify your answer. [2]
  9. 3(c) Construct a field with 27 elements. [2]
  10. 3(d) Given any positive integer $n$, does there exist an irreducible polynomial $p(x)$ over a finite field $F$ of degree $n$? Justify your answer. \par\hfill P.T.O. [3]
  11. 1(a) Show that $f=(f_1,f_2,\ldots,f_m):\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is differentiable at $x_0\in\mathbb R^n$ if and only if $f_1,f_2,\ldots,f_m$ are differentiable at $x_0$. [4]
  12. 1(b) If $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is linear then show that $f$ is differentiable everywhere. [2]
  13. 1(c) Let $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is differentiable at $x_0\in\mathbb R^n$. Show that there exist positive real numbers $M$ and $\delta$ such that $\|f(x)-f(x_0)\|\le M\|x-x_0\|$ for every $x\in A$, satisfying $\|x-x_0\|<\delta$. [4]
  14. 2(a) Consider the functions $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ and $g:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^4$ are defined by\par \hspace*{2em}$f(x,y,z)=\left(x^2y,\ z\sin y\right)$ and $g(u,v)=\left(uv,\ u^2v^2,\ u+v,\ u-v^2\right)$.\par Find the total derivative $Dh$ where $h=g\circ f$. [3]
  15. 2(b) Let $F:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ is differentiable at $a\in\mathbb R^n$, then show that the directional derivative $D_vF(a)$ exists for all $v\in\mathbb R^n$ and $D_vF(a)=DF(a).v$. [2]
  16. 2(c) Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$, $U\subset\mathbb R^n$. If the first and second partial derivatives of $f$ exist and are continuous on $U$ then show that $D_iD_jf=D_jD_if$ on $U$, $i,j=1,2,\ldots,n$. [5]
  17. 3(a) Suppose $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ is $C^1$ and $\det(f'(a))\ne0$ for all $a$ in the domain of $f$. Then show that $f$ is an open map. [5]
  18. 3(b) State and prove Inverse Function Theorem. [5]
