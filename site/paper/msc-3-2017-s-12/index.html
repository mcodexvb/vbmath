MMO-31(P1)

Advanced Functional Analysis-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1(a) Define a Topological vector space ($tvs$). For a fixed $a\in x$ and $\lambda(\ne0)\in F$, define $T_a,M_\lambda:X\to X,\ T_a(x)=x+a,\ x\in x,\ M_\lambda(x)=\lambda x(\lambda\ne0),x\in X$. Show that $T_a$ and $M_\lambda$ are homeomorphisms. [6]
  2. 1(b) Let $X$ be a $tvs$. Then prove the following:\par \hspace*{1em}i) if $C$ is a convex subset of $X$, then $C^0$ and $\bar C$ are also convex subsets of $X$.\par \hspace*{1em}ii) if $B$ is a balanced subset of $X$ then $\bar B$ is also so. [4]
  3. 2(a) When is a $tvs$ $X$ said to be locally compact? If $X$ is a $tvs$ and $Y$ is a locally compact subspace of $X$ then show that $Y$ is closed. [5]
  4. 2(b) Let $X$ be a $tvs$ and $E$ be a subset of $X$. Then prove that following statement are equivalent:\par \hspace*{1em}i) $E$ is bounded.\par \hspace*{1em}ii) if $\{x_n\}$ is any sequence in $E$ and $\{\alpha_n\}$ is any sequence of scalars such that $\lim_{x\to\infty}\alpha_n=0$ then $\alpha_nx_n=\theta$. [5]
  5. 3(a) Let $A$ and $B$ be two nonempty disjoint convex sets in a $tvs$ $X$. If $A$ is open then show that $\exists\Lambda\in X^*$ (dual space of $X$) and $\gamma\in\mathbb R$ such that $Rl\Lambda(x)<\gamma\le Rl\Lambda(y),\ \forall x\in A$ and $\forall y\in B$. [4]
  6. 3(b) When is a $tvs$ $X$ called normable? Prove that $X$ is normable iff $\theta$ in $X$ has a bounded convex neighbourhood. [4]
  7. 3(c) Let $B$ be a convex balanced local base in a $tvs$ $X$. For every $V\in B$, let $\mu_V$ be its Minkowski functional. Prove that $\{\mu_V:V\in B\}$ is a separating family of continuous seminorms on $X$. [2]
  8. 4(a) Let $X$ and $Y$ be two topological vector spaces and $\{\Lambda_n\}$ be a sequence of continuous linear mappings of $X$ into $Y$. Let $C=\{x\in X:\{\Lambda_n(x)\}$ is a Cauchy sequence in $Y\}$, $L=\{x\in X:\{\Lambda_n(x)\}$ is convergent in $Y\}$.\par Prove that\par \hspace*{1em}i) If $C$ is of $2^{\text{nd}}$ category in $X$ then $C=X$. \par \hspace*{1em}ii) If $L$ is of $2^{\text{nd}}$ category in $X$ and $Y$ is an $F$-space then $L=X$. \par\hfill P.T.O. [2]
  9. 4(b) Let $(X,\tau)$ be a $tvs$, $N$ be a closed subspace of $X$ and $\tau_N$ be the quotient topology on $X/N$ induced by the mapping $\Pi:X\to X/N$, where $\Pi(x)\to x+N,x\in x$. Then show that $\{X/N,\tau_N\}$ is a $tvs$. Further if $X$ is locally convex then $X/N$ is also so. [4]
  10. 5(a) When is a topological vector space a $X$ is said to be (i) locally convex\par \hspace*{1em}(ii) an F-space. [2]
  11. 5(b) Let $X$ be a $tvs$ and $Y$ be a subspace of $X$. If $\dim Y=n$ then prove that any isomorphism from $\mathbb C^n$ onto $Y$ is a homeomorphism. [6]
  12. 5(c) Let $X$ be a $tvs$ and $Y$ be a Hausdorff space. If $f:X\to Y$ is continuous, then prove that the graph $G$ of $f$ is closed. [2]
  13. 6(a) Define extreme point and extreme set in a vector space. [2]
  14. 6(b) State and prove Krein-Milman theorem. [8]
