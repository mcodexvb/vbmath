MMC-25

Solid Mechanics: Marks 10
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2026
  1. 1(a) Write down the expressions for Lagrangian and Eulerian infinitesimal strain tensor. Prove that, in case of infinitesimal deformation, the distinction between them disappears. [2+3]
  2. 1(b) A body undergoes deformation\par \hspace*{2em}$x_1=\sqrt2X_1+\frac34\sqrt2X_2$\par \hspace*{2em}$x_2=-X_1+\frac34X_2+\frac{\sqrt2}4X_3$\par \hspace*{2em}$x_3=X_1-\frac34X_2+\frac14\sqrt2X_3$\par Determine Lagrangian infinitesimal strain components. Find the direction after deformation of a line element with initial d.rs. 1:1:1. [3+2]
  3. 2(a) Prove that the extremum values of normal stress at a point of a continuum are principal stresses. [5]
  4. 2(b) If the principal stresses at a point are $T_1=1,T_2=-1,T_3=3$. If stress at a point is given by\par \[(T_{ij})=\begin{bmatrix}T_{11}&0&0\\0&1&2\\0&2&T_{33}\end{bmatrix}.\] Find the value of $T_{11}$ and $T_{33}$. [5]
  5. 3(a) State the principle of balance of linear momentum. Hence obtain the Cauchy's first equation of motion. [5]
  6. 3(b) Given the following stress distribution\par \[(T_{ij})=\begin{bmatrix}x_1+x_2&T_{12}&0\\T_{12}&x_1-2x_2&0\\0&0&x_2\end{bmatrix},\] find $T_{12}(x_1,x_2)$ in order that stress distribution is in equilibrium with zero body force and that the stress vector on $x_1=1$ is given by $\vec T^{(n)}=(1+x_2)\vec e_1+(2-x_2)\vec e_2$. [5]
  7. 1(a) Use Lyapunov direct method to show that the trivial equilibrium of the following Lorenz system is asymptotically stable:\par \[\dot x=\sigma(y-x),\ \dot y=rx-y-xz,\ \dot z=xy-bz,\] where $\sigma>0,\ b>0,\ 0<r<1$. [5]
  8. 1(b) State Hartman-Grobman theorem. Can you apply this theorem to the system\par \[\dot x=-2x+3y+xy,\ \dot y=-x+y-2xy^2\ ?\] Justify your answer. [1+4]
  9. 2(a) State and proof the Bendixson's negative criteria. [1+3]
  10. 2(b) Show that the following system of differential equations has at least one periodic orbit:\par \[\dot x=y,\ \dot y=-x+y(1-x^2-2y^2).\] [6]
  11. 3(a) Consider the difference equation\par \[x_{t+1}=\frac{rx_t}{x_t+A},\ (r>0,\ A>0).\] Use cobwebbing method, sketch its solutions for different choices of the initial values. [4]
  12. 3(b) Suppose a predator-prey system satisfies the following equations:\par \[x_{n+1}=x_n(a-x_n-y_n),\ y_{n+1}=y_n(b+x_n),\] where $x$ and $y$ represents the prey and predator populations at the $n$-th generation, respectively, and $a>0,0<b<1$. Find all fixed points of this system. Use stability conditions to show that the interior fixed point is locally asymptotically stable if $2<a+b<3$. [6]
