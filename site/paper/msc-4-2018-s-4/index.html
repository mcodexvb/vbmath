MMO-41(A2)

Dynamical Oceanography
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2018
  1. 1(a) Obtain the Gibb's-Duhem relation for a multicomponent thermodynamic system. Deduce the corresponding relation for sea-water, supposing it to be a mixture of salt and pure water. [3+2]
  2. 1(b) Show that in $(T,\ p,\ s)$ variables:\par \hspace*{1em}i) $C_v=C_p+T\cdot\left(\dfrac{\partial\tau}{\partial T}\right)^2\Big/\dfrac{\partial\tau}{\partial p}$\par \hspace*{1em}ii) $k_\eta=\dfrac{C_v}{C_p}k_T.$ [3+2]
  3. 2 Obtain the equation of motion of sea-water, relative to the rotating earth, in spherical coordinates $(\lambda,\phi,z)$ assuming sea-water to be non-viscous and incompressible. [10]
  4. 3(a) Assuming sea-water to be vertically stratified two-component mixture in equilibrium under gravity, establish the conditions for stability in absence of convection and obtain the expression for V\"ais\"al\"a frequency. [4]
  5. 3(b) Derive the following equation for transfer of entropy of the form \[T\rho\frac{D\eta}{Dt}=-\nabla\cdot\bar Q+\left\{\mu-\left(\frac{\partial I}{\partial s}\right)_{T,p}\right\}\nabla\cdot\vec I_s-\vec I_s\cdot\nabla\left(\frac{\partial I}{\partial s}\right)_{T,p}+\Phi\] [6]
  6. 4(a) Starting from the equations of small amplitude gravity wave motion in the ocean, derive an equation analogous to the conservation of energy equation. [6]
  7. 4(b) Obtain the boundary conditions at the free ocean surface, taking into account the fact that mass exchange process across the surface amounts to a flux `b' of pure water in unit time per unit area. [4]
  8. 5 Explain $\beta$-plane approximation in connection with the motion of sea water. Obtain the approximate form of the equations of continuity, momentum and energy of sea water, considering the sea water to be non-viscous compressible fluid, under the $\beta$-plane approximation. Also, provide justification for validity of the approximation in mid or low latitude. [8+2]
  9. 6(a) Write down the equations of problem $V$ and problem $H$ in which the equations of small amplitude gravity waves in the ocean may be separated. [3]
  10. 6(b) Determine the eigen curves of problem $H$. Assuming the eigen curves of problem $V$, show that there exists four types of waves when the V\"ais\"al\"a frequency $N_0$ is approximately constant. [7]
