MJMA-6

Differential Equations II and Dynamics of a Particle
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1 Answer \emph{any five} questions.
  2. 1(a) What do you mean by exactness for a higher-order ordinary differential equation (ODE)? Write down the condition of exactness for a linear ODE of order $n$.
  3. 1(b) Let $y_1(x)=e^x$ and $y_2(x)=x^2 e^x$ be two independent solutions of $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+P(x)\frac{dy}{dx}+Q(x)y=0$, then find the value of $P(x)$ at $x=1$.
  4. 1(c) If $y(x)=x$ is a solution of the ODE \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\left(\frac2{x^2}+\frac1x\right)\left(x\frac{dy}{dx}-y\right)=0,\quad x\in(0,\infty),\] then find its general solution.
  5. 1(d) Construct two first-order differential equations from the following ODE: \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+2026\,y=0\] with $y=1$ and $\frac{dy}{dx}=\sqrt{2026}$ at $x=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2026}}$.
  6. 1(e) Factorize the operator on the left hand side of $[xD^2+(x-1)D-1]y=x^2e^{2x}$, $D^n\equiv\frac{d^n}{dx^n}$.
  7. 1(f) Distinguish the conditions for integrability and exactness of the total differential equation \[P(x,y,z)\,dx+Q(x,y,z)\,dy+R(x,y,z)\,dz=0.\]
  8. 1(g) Find the roots of the indicial equation of \[2x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-x\frac{dy}{dx}+(1-x^2)y=0\] about the point $x=0$.
  9. 1(h) Identify the number of independent arbitrary constants within the general solution of the following linear simultaneous equations: \[\begin{aligned} \phi_1(D)x+\phi_2(D)y&=\phi(t),\\ \psi_1(D)x+\psi_2(D)y&=\psi(t),\quad D\equiv\frac{d}{dt}. \end{aligned}\] [[$5\times2=10$]]
  10. 2(a) Solve the ODE \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\sec^2y\tan y\] under the conditions, when $x=0$, then $y=0$ and $\frac{dy}{dx}=1$.
  11. 2(b) Solve the following ODE by reducing into normal form, that is, the form where the first derivative is absent: \[\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}+(x^2+2)y=e^{\frac12(x^2+2x)}.\]
  12. 2(c) Let $y_1(x)$ and $y_2(x)$ be two independent solutions of the ODE \[(1-x^2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}+(2\cos^2x-1)y=0,\quad -1<x<1\] with Wronskian $W(x)$. If $\left.y_1\right|_{x=0}=1$, $\left.\frac{dy_1}{dx}\right|_{x=0}=3$, $\left.y_2\right|_{x=0}=2$, $\left.\frac{dy_2}{dx}\right|_{x=0}=4$, find the value of $W(\frac12)$. [[4+6+5]]
  13. 3(a) Solve the ODE $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+(9-x)y=0$ in series near the ordinary point $x=0$. Hence find the particular solution for $\left.y\right|_{x=0}=\frac92$ and $\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=0}=\frac32$.
  14. 3(b) Find the particular solution of the following linear system using eigenvalue-eigenvector method: \[\begin{aligned} \frac{dx}{dt}&=4x+2y,\\ \frac{dy}{dt}&=3x-y,\\ x(t=0)&=77,\quad y(t=0)=49. \end{aligned}\] [[9+6]]
  15. 4(a) Solve the following simultaneous differential equations of type I: \[\begin{aligned} 2\frac{dx}{dt}-\frac{dy}{dt}+2x+y&=11t,\\ 2\frac{dx}{dt}+3\frac{dy}{dt}+5x-3y&=2. \end{aligned}\]
  16. 4(b) Verify that the following total differential equation is integrable and hence solve the equation: \[(2x^2+2xy+2xz^2+1)\,dx+dy+2z\,dz=0.\]
  17. 4(c) Solve: \[\frac{dx}{x}=\frac{dy}{y}=\frac{dz}{z-2025\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}}.\] [[5+5+5]]
  18. 1(a) Write down the equaion of motion of a particle moving along a straight line under the influence of a force,\par (i) depending on the distance of the particle from a fixed point on the line,\par (ii) depending on time only, and\par (iii) depending on the distance and velocity of the particle. [[3]]
  19. 1(b) A particle is let fall from rest from a point outside the earth at a distance $b$ from the centre. Prove that the square of the velocity of the particle on reaching the centre is $ga(3-\frac{2a}{b})$, where $a$ is the radius of the earth and $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity at its surface. [[7]]
  20. 2 Define simple harmonic motion (SHM). Obtain the expressions for displacement, velocity, and acceleration of a particle executing SHM. Hence, derive expressions for its kinetic and potential energy. [[2+5+3]]
  21. 3(a) Derive the equation of motion for a particle moving on a plane under the action of a force proportional to the distance of the particle from a fixed point on the plane. [[5]]
  22. 3(b) A particle moves freely in a parabolic path given by $y^2=4ax$, under a force always perpendicular to its axis. Find the law of force. [[5]]
  23. 4(a) Define central force and areal velocity. [[5]]
  24. 4(b) Derive the equation of motion of central orbit in polar coordinate system. [[5]]
  25. 5(a) State Kepler's laws of planetary motion. Show that the orbit is a conic section. [[2+3]]
  26. 5(b) Find the law of force in the case in which a particle describes an orbid $r^2=2ap$ under a force to the pole. [[5]]
  27. 6 Derive the equation of motion of a particle moving along a curve on a verticle plane under the action of gravity and a force of resistance that varies as the $n^{\mathrm{th}}$ power of the velocity. A heavy particle is moving vertically upwards under the influence of a force of resistance proportional to the square of velocity. If the particle starts with an inital velocity $V$, find the maximum height it attains. [[2+8]]
