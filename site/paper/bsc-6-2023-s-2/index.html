CCMA-14

Algebra IV
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester VI2023
  1. 1(a) Let $G=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}1&r\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\mid r\in\mathbb R\right\}$. Show that the group $G$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb R$. Are the groups $\mathbb R^*$ and $\mathbb R^+$ isomorphic? [2+1]
  2. 1(b) Consider the group homomorphism $f:\mathbb C^*\longrightarrow\mathbb C^*$ defined by $f(z)=z^{100}$. Find the kernel and image of $f$. [2]
  3. 2(a) Let $f:G\longrightarrow G'$ be a homomorphism and $H$ be a subgroup of $G$. Show that $f^{-1}(f(H))=H\ker f$. [2]
  4. 2(b) Prove that the group $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\simeq G$ where $G=\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb N}C_n$ is the group of all roots of unity. [3]
  5. 3(a) State and prove the second isomorphism theorem for groups. [3]
  6. 3(b) Define a suitable homomorphism $f:GL_2(\mathbb R)\longrightarrow\mathbb R^*$ to show that the order of the group $GL_2(\mathbb R)/N$ is two where $N=\{A\in GL_2(\mathbb R)\mid\det A>0\}$. [2]
  7. 4(a) Find all homomorphisms from $\mathbb Z$ onto $\mathbb Z_9$. [2]
  8. 4(b) Is $\mathbb Z$ a homomorphic image of $\mathbb R$? Justify. [3]
  9. 5(a) Show that $GL_2(\mathbb R)$ is not isomorphic to $\mathbb R^*$. [2]
  10. 5(b) Show that an infinite group $G$ is cyclic if and only if it is isomorphic to each of its nontrivial subgroups. [3]
  11. 6(a) Find $Aut(K_4)$. [2]
  12. 6(b) Prove that $Aut(\mathbb Z_n)\simeq U_n$. [3]
  13. 1(a) State and prove the correspondence theorem for rings. [3]
  14. 1(b) Let $R$ be a ring with 1. If the characteristic of $R$ is a prime $p$, then show that $R$ has a subring isomorphic to $\mathbb Z_p$. [2]
  15. 2(a) Find all ring homomorphisms $f:\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\mathbb Z$. [2]
  16. 2(b) Let $X$ be a nonempty set and $R=Map(X,\mathbb Z_2)$ be the ring of all mappings from $X$ into $\mathbb Z_2$. For every $a\in X$ define $C_a:X\longrightarrow\mathbb Z_2$ by \[C_a(x)=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }x=a\\0&\text{if }x\ne a.\end{cases}\] Show that as rings $\mathcal P(X)\simeq R$. [3]
  17. 3(a) Show that no two subrings of $\mathbb Z$ are isomorphic. [2]
  18. 3(b) Let $R$ be a ring homomorphic image of $\mathbb Z$. Show that $R$ is a commutative ring with unity and every ideal of $R$ is principal. [3]
  19. 4(a) Use correspondence theorem to find the number of ideals of $\mathbb Z_{100}$. [1]
  20. 4(b) Let $R$ be a commutative ring with 1, and $I$ and $J$ be two ideals of $R$ such that $R=I+J$. Show that $IJ=I\cap J$ and $R/IJ\simeq R/I\times R/J$. [4]
  21. 5(a) Find all maximal ideals of $\mathbb Z_{100}$. [2]
  22. 5(b) Let $R$ be a commutative ring with 1 and $M$ be an ideal of $R$. Show that $M$ is maximal if and only if $R/M$ is a field. [3]
  23. 6(a) Show that the ideal $<x>$ in $\mathbb Z[x]$ is a prime ideal. Is $<x>$ a maximal ideal? [3]
  24. 6(b) Show that every prime ideal in a Boolean ring with 1 is maximal. [2]
  25. 1(a) A linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3$ is defined by $T(x,y,z)=(x-y+2z,x+2y-z,2x+y+z)$. Find the rank and nullity of $T$. [2]
  26. 1(b) Is there any linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3$ such that $\ker T=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid 2x=y=3z\}$ and $Im\,T=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid x+2y+3z=0\}$? [3]
  27. 2(a) Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ real matrix. Define $T:\mathbb R^n\longrightarrow\mathbb R^m$ by $T(X)=AX$. Show that $T$ is one-to-one if and only if $r(A)=n$. Then show that $ST=TS$ for every $S\in\mathcal L(V)$ if and only if $T=cI_V$ for some scalar $c$. [3]
  28. 2(b) A linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^2\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2$ is given by $T(x,y)=(2x-3y,x+4y)$. Find the image of the line $x+2y=1$ under $T$. [2]
  29. 3(a) Let $V$ and $W$ be two finite dimensional vector spaces and $U$ is a subspace of $V$. Prove that there is a linear transformation $T:V\longrightarrow W$ with $\ker T=U$ if and only if $\dim U\ge\dim V-\dim W$. [3]
  30. 3(b) Let $T:V\longrightarrow W$ be a linear transformation. Show that $V$ has a subspace $U$ such that $V=\ker T\oplus U$ and $Im\,T=T(U)$. [2]
  31. 4(a) State and prove the rank-nullity theorem. [3]
  32. 4(b) Let $D,I:P(R)\longrightarrow P(R)$ be the differential and integral operators. Show that $\{D,I\}$ are linearly independent. [2]
  33. 5(a) Let $T:V\longrightarrow V$ be a linear operator such that $T^2=cT$ for some $c\in F$. Prove that $V=Im\,T\oplus\ker T$. [2]
  34. 5(b) Let $S,T:U\longrightarrow V$ be two linear transformations. Then show that $|\operatorname{rank}(S)-\operatorname{rank}(T)|\le\operatorname{rank}(S+T)\le\operatorname{rank}(S)+\operatorname{rank}(T)$. [3]
  35. 6(a) Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\1&0&-1\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix}$ be the matrix of a linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow P_2(\mathbb R)$ in the basis $\beta=\{(1,1,0),(1,0,1),(0,1,1)\}$ and $\gamma=\{1+t,t+t^2,1+t^2\}$. Find $T$. [3]
  36. 6(b) Show that the rank of a linear transformation is same as the rank of each of its matrix representations. [2]
