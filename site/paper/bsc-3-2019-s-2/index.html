CC-6

Algebra-II
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2019
  1. 1 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions: \hfill \(2\times10=20\)
  2. 1(a) \begin{romanparts} \item Let \(H\) be a subgroup of a group \(G\). Then show that \(\displaystyle\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}\) is a normal subgroup of \(G\). Characterize this subgroup for \(H=A_3\) and \(G=S_3\). \hfill \(2+1\) \item Suppose \(G\) be a group of order 35 and \(A,B\) be are subgroups of \(G\) of order 5 and 7, respectively. Show that \(G=AB\). \hfill 2 \item Write down the Cayley table for the quotient group \(\mathbb Z_{18}/\langle[6]\rangle\). Hence find the order of \([5]+\langle[6]\rangle\) in \(\mathbb Z_{18}/\langle[6]\rangle\). \hfill \(4+1\) \end{romanparts}
  3. 1(b) \begin{romanparts} \item Find \([Q^*:Q^+]\). \hfill 2 \item Let \(G\) be a non commutative group of order \(2n\), where \(n\) is an odd integer \((>1)\). Show that \(G\) has an element of order \(n\). \hfill 3 \item Find all the conjugate classes of the quaternion group \(\{\pm1,\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}\) and write down the class equation of it. \hfill \(3+2\) \end{romanparts}
  4. 1(c) \begin{romanparts} \item Show that \([S_n:A_n]=2\), for \(n\ge2\) and \(A_n\) is the only group of index 2 in \(S_n\). \hfill \(1+3\) \item Prove that the order of \(r\)-cycle in \(S_n\) is \(r\). Give an example of an element of order 2 in \(S_4\) which is not a 2-cycle. \hfill \(2+1\) \item Find the order of the subgroup \(\langle(123),(24)\rangle\) in \(S_4\). \hfill 3 \end{romanparts}
  5. 2 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions: \hfill \(2\times10=20\)
  6. 2(a) \begin{romanparts} \item For any positive integer \(n\), show that the ring \(\mathbb Z_n\) of all integers modulo \(n\) is an integral domain if an only if \(n\) is a prime integer. Hence or otherwise show that there does not exist any integral domain with six elements. \hfill \(4+1\) \item Find the smallest subring of \(\missingglyph\) containing \(\dfrac12\). \hfill 3 \item Suppose \(R\) be a finite ring with unity 1. If for \(a,b\in R,\ ab=1\) show that \(ba=1\). \hfill 2 \end{romanparts}
  7. 2(b) \begin{romanparts} \item Let \(F\) be a finite field with characteristic \(p\), where \(p\) is prime. Show that \(|F|=p^n\), for some integer \(n\). \hfill 4 \item Give an example to show that a ring and its subring may have different unities. Justify your answer. \hfill 3 \item Let \(S\) be any set and \(P(S)\) the power set of \(S\). Describe the units and zero divisors of the ring \((P(S),\Delta,\cap)\). \hfill 3 \end{romanparts}
  8. 2(c) \begin{romanparts} \item Find all idempotents of the quotient ring \(\mathbb Z/\langle p^r\rangle\), for a prime \(p\) and a positive integer \(r\). \hfill 3 \item Let \(R\) be a ring with unity. If \(A\) is a left ideal and \(B\) is a right ideal of \(R\), then prove that \(AB\) is an ideal of \(R\). \hfill 3 \item Find all maximal ideals in the ring \(\mathbb Z\). \hfill 4 \end{romanparts}
  9. 3 Answer \textbf{\textit{any two}} questions: \hfill \(2\times10=20\)
  10. 3(a) \begin{romanparts} \item Let \(X\) be a nonempty set. Show that forms \(P(X)\) be a vector space over \(\mathbb Z_2\) with respect to operations defined as follows: For \(A,B\in P(X)\) and \(C\in\mathbb Z_2\), \[ A+B=A\lhd B\quad\text{and}\quad C\cdot A=\begin{cases} A & \text{if } C=1\\ \varphi & \text{if } C=0. \end{cases} \] \hfill 4 \item Describe the subspaces of \(\missingglyph^3\). \hfill 4 \item Does the exist a proper subspace \(W\) of the vector space \(\missingglyph\) over \(\missingglyph\)? Justify your answer. \hfill 2 \end{romanparts}
  11. 3(b) \begin{romanparts} \item Let \[ S=\{1+x+3x^3,\ 1+x^2+2x^3,\ -2-2x-6x^3,\ x,\ -x^2+x^3\} \] be a subset of \(P_3(\missingglyph)\). Find the smallest subset \(T\) of \(S\) such that \(L(S)=L(T)\). \hfill 3 \item Extend \(S=\{(-2,1,1,0),(-1,2,0,1)\}\) to a basis of \(\missingglyph^4\). \hfill 3 \item Find the dimension of the subspace \(W=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b&c\\d&e&0\end{pmatrix}:a+c=d+e\right\}\). \hfill 4 \end{romanparts}
  12. 3(c) \begin{romanparts} \item Let \(M_{n\times n}(\missingglyph)\) be the vector space over the field \(\missingglyph\). Define \(\langle\, ,\,\rangle\) on \(M_{n\times n}(\missingglyph)\) as follows: For \(A,B\in M_{n\times n}(\missingglyph)\), \(\langle A,B\rangle=\operatorname{trace}(B*A)\). Show that \(\langle\, ,\,\rangle\) is an inner product on \(M_{n\times n}(\missingglyph)\). \hfill 3 \item Let \(V=P_2(R)\) with the inner product defined by \(\displaystyle\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-1}^{1}f(t)g(t)\,dt\), where \(f,g\in V\) with standered basis \(\beta\) of \(V\). Use Gram-Schmidt process to replace \(\beta\) by an orthogonal basis \(\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}\) for \(V\). \hfill 4 \item Show that every orthogonal set is linearly independent. \hfill 3 \end{romanparts} \def\paperfull{25}
