MMC-12

Complex Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2020
  1. 1(a) Let $f(z)$ be a continuous function in a simply connected domain $D$ and $\alpha$ be any arbitrary but fixed point in $D$. If the integral $g(z)=\int_\alpha^z f(w)dw$ is independent of the path so long as the path lies in $D$, then show that $g'(z)=f(z)$ for every $z$ in $D$. [4]
  2. 1(b) If $f(z)$ be a non-constant analytic function regular in a domain $D$ including the boundary $C$ and $f(z)\ne0$ for any point in $D$, then show that $|f(z)|$ can not attain its minimum in $D$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Show that $f(z)=z^{10}-z^4+1$ vanishes at least for one point in $|z|<2$. [3]
  4. 2(a) State Laurent's theorem. [2]
  5. 2(b) If $f(z)=e^{\frac12 k(z-\frac1z)}$, where $k$ is a constant, then show that $f(z)$ can be expanded in the form \[ f(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}a_nz^n,\quad \text{where }a_n=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\cos(n\theta-k\sin\theta)d\theta. \] [3]
  6. 2(c) Define a 'removable singularity'. If a function $f$ is bounded and analytic throughout a domain $0<|z-\alpha|<\delta$, then prove that either $f$ is analytic at $\alpha$ or else $\alpha$ is a removable singularity of $f$. [1+4]
  7. 3(a) Find the singularities of the function $f(z)=\sin\frac1{z-2}+\sin\frac1{z+2}$ and classify the singularities. [3]
  8. 3(b) If $f(z)$ be a non-constant function regular in a region $R$, then prove that $f(z)$ have at most a finite number of zeros in every closed sub region of $R$. [3]
  9. 3(c) Define a 'zero' of an analytic function. Prove that the zeros of a non-constant analytic function are isolated points. [1+3]
  10. 4(a) If $f(z)$ is analytic in $|z|<1$, $f(0)=0$ and $|f(z)|\leq1$, then show that $|f(z)|\leq|z|$. [2]
  11. 4(b) State and prove Cauchy's residue theorem. Using this theorem evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{e^{3z}}{(4z-\pi)^2}dz$. [5]
  12. 4(c) If $\alpha$ be a pole of $f(z)$ of order $p$, then show that the residue of $f(z)$ at $\alpha$ is given by \[ \frac1{(p-1)!}\lim_{z\to\alpha}\frac{d^{p-1}}{dz^{p-1}}[(z-\alpha)^pf(z)]. \] [3]
  13. 5(a) State and prove Rouche's theorem(Principle of Argument may be assumed). [4]
  14. 5(b) Show that the roots of the equation $16z^5-z+8=0$ all lie in the annulus between $|z|=\frac12$ and $|z|=1$. [4]
  15. 5(c) Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle\oint_{|z|=\frac52}\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}dz$, where $f(z)=\frac{(z^2+1)^2}{(z^2-3z+2)^3}$. [2]
  16. 6(a) Prove that a function which is analytic every where including the point at infinity is a constant. [3]
  17. 6(b) Find $\mathit{Res}[f(z),\infty]$, where $f(z)=\frac{z^2}{z^2-1}$. [2]
  18. 6(c) Evaluate any one of the following by the method of contour integration: (i) $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{x\sin x\,dx}{a^2x^2+b^2}$, $a<0<b$; (ii) $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{1+a^2-2a\cos\theta}$, $|a|<1$. \renewcommand{\exam}[5]{\begin{center}\textbf{#1}\\#2\\\textbf{#3}\\#4\end{center}\textbf{Time: #5}\hfill\textbf{Full Marks: 40}\par Questions are of values as indicated in the margin.} [5]
