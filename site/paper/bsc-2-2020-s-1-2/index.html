GEC-2

Theory
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2020
  1. 1(a) Let \(z_1,z_2\) be two non-zero complex numbers. If \(\theta_1\) be an argument of \(z_1\) and \(\theta_2\) be an argument of \(z_2\) then prove that \(\theta_1-\theta_2\) is an argument \(z_1/z_2\).
  2. 1(b) Prove that \(\sqrt{i}+\sqrt{-i}=\sqrt{2}\).
  3. 1(c) Find the sum of 99th powers of the roots of the equation \(x^7-1=0\).
  4. 1(d) Expand \(\cos^7\theta\) in a series of cosines of multiples of \(\theta\).
  5. 2(a) Prove that \(x^2+x+1\) is a factor of \(x^{10}+x^5+1\).
  6. 2(b) Find the remainder when \(x^4+2x^3+1\) is divided by \((x-1)(x-2)\).
  7. 2(c) Solve the equation \(3x^3-26x^2+52x-24=0\), if it's roots are in geometric progression(GP).
  8. 2(d) Find the equation whose roots are the roots of the equation \(x^3+5x^2-7x+2=0\) each increased by 1.
  9. 3(a) Solve the equation \(x^4-4x^3-4x^2-4x-5=0\) given that two roots \(\alpha,\beta\) are connected by the relation \(2\alpha+\beta=3\).
  10. 3(b) If \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta\) be the roots of the equation \(x^4+px^3+qx^2+rx+s=0\), then find the values of \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})} \item \(\sum\alpha\beta/\gamma\), \item \(\sum\alpha^2/\beta\). \end{enumerate}
  11. 4(a) Express the following matrix as the sum of a symmetric matrix and a skew-symmetric matrix, \[ \begin{pmatrix}0&5&2&3\\0&7&2&2\\8&7&2&6\\1&4&5&0\end{pmatrix}. \]
  12. 4(b) Define row-reduced echelon matrix. Apply elementary row operations to reduce the following matrix to a row reduced echelon matrix \[ \begin{pmatrix}0&0&2&2&0\\1&3&2&4&1\\2&6&2&6&2\\3&9&1&10&6\end{pmatrix}. \]
  13. 4(c) Suppose \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) real skew-symmetric matrix and \(P\) be an \(n\times1\) real matrix. Prove that \(P^tAP=0\).
  14. 5(a) Define rank of a matrix. Find all real value(s) of \(\lambda\) for which rank of the matrix \(\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1&1\\1&2&-1&\lambda\\5&7&1&\lambda^2\end{pmatrix}\) is (i)2 and (ii)3.
  15. 5(b) Consider the following system of linear equations, \[ \begin{aligned} x-2y+3z&=1,\\ 2x-5y+10z&=0,\\ 4x-7y+8z&=6. \end{aligned} \] Write down the matrix representation of the above system of linear equations. Solve the above system of linear equations by Gaussian elimination method. [3]
  16. 5(c) Write down the vector \(v=(1,-2,5)\) as a linear combination of the vectors \(u_1=(1,1,1)\), \(u_2=(1,2,3)\) and \(u_3=(2,-1,1)\) [4]
  17. 6(a) Define eigenvalue and eigenvector of a square matrix. Find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the matrix \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&6\\5&2\end{pmatrix}\).
  18. 6(b) Let \(A\) be a non-singular matrix and \(\lambda\) be a non-zero eigen value of \(A\). Then show that \(\lambda^{-1}\) is a eigen value of \(A^{-1}\). [3]
  19. 6(c) Prove that a square matrix \(A\) is singular if and only if 0 is an eigen value of \(A\). [2]
  20. 7(a) Let \(v_1=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\0\\6\end{pmatrix}\), \(v_2=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\2\\3\end{pmatrix}\), \(v_3=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-6\\3\end{pmatrix}\), \(w=\begin{pmatrix}1\\14\\-9\end{pmatrix}\). Determine if \(w\) is in \(\operatorname{col}(A)\), where \(A=\begin{pmatrix}v_1&v_2&v_3\end{pmatrix}\). [4]
  21. 7(b) Determine whether \(\{u_1,u_2,u_3\}\) forms a basis of \(R^3\). where \(u_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\), \(u_2=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\3\end{pmatrix}\), \(u_3=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix}\). [4]
  22. 7(c) Show that the set \(H\) of all points in \(R^2\) of the form \((3s;2+5s)\) is not a vector space, by showing that it is not closed under scalar multiplication. (Find a specific vector \(u\) in \(H\) and a scalar \(c\) such that \(cu\) is not in \(H\).) [2]
  23. 8(a) Verify the parallelogram law for vectors \(u\) and \(v\) in \(R^n\): \(\lVert u+v\rVert^2+\lVert u-v\rVert^2=2\lVert u\rVert^2+2\lVert v\rVert^2\). [3]
  24. 8(b) Consider vectors \(u=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\4\end{pmatrix}\), \(v=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\\5\end{pmatrix}\), \(w=\begin{pmatrix}4\\2\\-3\end{pmatrix}\) in \(R^3\). Find \begin{enumerate} \item \(u\cdot v\) \item \(v\cdot w\) \item \(\lVert u\rVert\) \end{enumerate} \hfill
  25. 8(c) Show that \(v_1,v_2,v_3\) is an orthonormal basis of \(R^3\), where \(v_1=\begin{pmatrix}3/\sqrt{11}\\1/\sqrt{11}\\1/\sqrt{11}\end{pmatrix}\), \(v_2=\begin{pmatrix}-1/\sqrt6\\2/\sqrt6\\1/\sqrt6\end{pmatrix}\), \(w=\begin{pmatrix}-1/\sqrt{66}\\-4/\sqrt{66}\\7/\sqrt{66}\end{pmatrix}\). \par
  26. 9(a) Verify the set \(S=\{u_1,u_2,u_3\}\) is a orthogonal basis of \(R^3\). Then express \(x\) as a linear combination of the vectors in \(S\). Where \(u_1=\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\), \(u_2=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\\1\end{pmatrix}\), \(u_3=\begin{pmatrix}-1/2\\-2\\7/2\end{pmatrix}\), \(x=\begin{pmatrix}6\\1\\-8\end{pmatrix}\).
  27. 9(b) Apply the Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process to find an orthogonal basis and then an orthonormal basis for the subspace \(U\) of \(R^4\) spanned by \(v_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\), \(v_2=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\), \(v_3=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\).
