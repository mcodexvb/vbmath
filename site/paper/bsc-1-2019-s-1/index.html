CC-1

Analysis-I
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2019
  1. 1(a) Let $S$ be a non-empty bounded subset of $\mathbb R$ with $\sup S=M$ and $Inf\,S=m$. Find $\sup T$ and $Inf\,T$ where $T=\{x-y:x,\ y\in S\}$. [4]
  2. 1(b) Let $x,y\in\mathbb R$ and $x<y$. Then prove that\par i) there is a rational number $r$ such that $x<r<y$.\par ii) there is an irrational number $s$ such that $x<s<y$. [3+1]
  3. 1(c) If $a\in\mathbb R$ and $0\le a<\frac1n$ for every natural number $n$, then prove that $a=0$. [2]
  4. 2(a) Show that a finite set has no limit points. [2]
  5. 2(b) Give an example of an infinite set $S\subset\mathbb R$ such that $S$ has no limit points. [2]
  6. 2(c) Show that for any two subsets, $A,B$ of $\mathbb R$, $(A\cup B)'=A'\cup B'$. [3]
  7. 2(d) Find the derived set of the set $S=\{\frac1n:n\in\mathbb N\}$. [3]
  8. 3(a) State and prove Rollel's theorem. Give its geometrical interpretations. [1+3+1]
  9. 3(b) Prove that $\left(1+\frac1x\right)^x>\left(1+\frac1y\right)^y$ if $x,y\in\mathbb R$ and $x>y>0$. [3]
  10. 3(c) Prove that between any two real roots of the equation $e^x\sin x+1=0$, there is at least one real root of the equation $\tan x+1=0$. [2]
  11. 4(a) Prove that $\frac x{1+x}<\log(1+x)<x\quad\forall x>0.$. [3]
  12. 4(b) Let $f$ be a real valued function defined on $\mathbb R$ satisfying $|f(x)-f(y)|\le(x-y)^2\quad\forall x,y\in\mathbb R$. Prove that $f$ is constant on $\mathbb R$. [3]
  13. 4(c) State Leibnitz theorem for higher order derivatives. If $y=e^{a\sin^{-1}x}$, prove that $(1-x^2)y_{n+2}-(2n+1)xy_{n+1}-(n^2+a^2)y_n=0$. [1+3]
  14. 5(a) If $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable on $[a,b]$, then prove that $f'([a,b])$ is an interval. [3]
  15. 5(b) Let a function $f$ be twice differentiable on $[a,b]$ and $f(a)=f(b)=0$ and $f(a)<0$ for some $c\in(a,b)$. Prove that there is at least one point $\xi\in(a,b)$ for which $f''(\xi)>0$. [3]
  16. 5(c) Sketch the curves (i) $r=\cos\theta$ (ii) $y=x^2+2x+3$. [2+2]
  17. 6(a) State sequential criterion for limit and use it to show that $\lim_{x\to0}\tan\frac1x$ does not exist. [4]
  18. 6(b) Prove that every monotone decreasing sequence which is bounded below is convergent and converges to its greatest lower bound. [6]
  19. 7(a) Let $\{x_n\}$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that $\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac{x_{n+1}}{x_n}\right)=\ell$. Then prove that $\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n^{1/n}=\ell$. [6]
  20. 7(b) Evaluate $\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\{(a+1)(a+2)\ldots(a+n)\}^{1/n}$ if $a>0$. [4]
  21. 8(a) Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous such that for $c\in(a,b),\ f(c)\ne0$. Show that $\exists\delta>0$ such that $f(x)$ has the same sign as that of $f(c)\ \forall x\in(c-d,c+d)\cap[a,b]$. [4]
  22. 8(b) A function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is continuous on $\mathbb R$ and $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)\ \forall x,y\in\mathbb R$. If $f(1)=k$, prove that $f(x)=kx\ \forall x\in\mathbb R$. [4]
  23. 8(c) Find the points of discontinuity of the function $f$ where $f(x)=[x]+[-x],\ x\in\mathbb R$. [2]
  24. 9(a) If $n$ is an odd positive integer then show that $\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^nx\,dx=\frac{n-1}n\cdot\frac{n-3}{n-2}\cdot\frac{n-5}{n-4}\ldots\frac45\cdot\frac28\cdot1$. [5]
  25. 9(b) State L. Hospital's theorem and hence evaluate $\lim_{x\to0}\left(\frac{\tan x}x\right)^{1/x}$. [5]
