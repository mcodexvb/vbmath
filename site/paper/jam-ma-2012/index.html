IIT JAM · Mathematics (MA), 2012

  1. Let $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ be the sequence $+\sqrt{1},-\sqrt{1},+\sqrt{2},-\sqrt{2},+\sqrt{3},-\sqrt{3},+\sqrt{4},-\sqrt{4}, \ldots$. If $$ y_{n}=\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}}{n} \text { for all } n \in \mathbb{N}, $$ then the sequence $\left\{y_{n}\right\}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item monotonic \item NOT bounded \item bounded but NOT convergent \item convergent \end{enumerate}
  2. The number of distinct real roots of the equation $x^{9}+x^{7}+x^{5}+x^{3}+x+1=0$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 1 \item 3 \item 5 \item 9 \end{enumerate}
  3. If $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is defined by $$ f(x, y)=\left\{\begin{array}{cc} \frac{x^{3}}{x^{2}+y^{4}} & \text { if }(x, y) \neq(0,0), \\ 0 & \text { if }(x, y)=(0,0), \end{array}\right. $$ then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f_{x}(0,0)=0$ and $f_{y}(0,0)=0$ \item $f_{x}(0,0)=1$ and $f_{y}(0,0)=0$ \item $f_{x}(0,0)=0$ and $f_{y}(0,0)=1$ \item $f_{x}(0,0)=1$ and $f_{y}(0,0)=1$ \end{enumerate}
  4. The value of $\int_{z=0}^{1} \int_{y=0}^{z} \int_{x=0}^{y} x y^{2} z^{3} d x d y d z$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{90}$ \item $\frac{1}{50}$ \item $\frac{1}{45}$ \item $\frac{1}{10}$ \end{enumerate}
  5. The differential equation $\left(1+x^{2} y^{3}+\alpha x^{2} y^{2}\right) d x+\left(2+x^{3} y^{2}+x^{3} y\right) d y=0$ is exact if $\alpha$ equals \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{2}$ \item $\frac{3}{2}$ \item 2 \item 3 \end{enumerate}
  6. An integrating factor for the differential equation $\left(2 x y+3 x^{2} y+6 y^{3}\right) d x+\left(x^{2}+6 y^{2}\right) d y=0$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $x^{3}$ \item $y^{3}$ \item $e^{3 x}$ \item $e^{3 y}$ \end{enumerate}
  7. For $c>0$, if $a \hat{i}+b \hat{j}+c \hat{k}$ is the unit normal vector at $(1,1, \sqrt{2})$ to the cone $z=\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}$, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\quad a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}=0$ \item $-a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=0$ \item $\quad a^{2}-b^{2}+c^{2}=0$ \item $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=0$ \end{enumerate}
  8. Consider the quotient group $\mathbb{Q} / \mathbb{Z}$ of the additive group of rational numbers. The order of the element $\frac{2}{3}+\mathbb{Z}$ in $\mathbb{Q} / \mathbb{Z}$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 2 \item 3 \item 5 \item 6 \end{enumerate}
  9. Which one of the following is TRUE ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item The characteristic of the ring $6 \mathbb{Z}$ is 6 \item The ring $6 \mathbb{Z}$ has a zero divisor \item The characteristic of the ring $(\mathbb{Z} / 6 \mathbb{Z}) \times 6 \mathbb{Z}$ is zero \item The ring $6 \mathbb{Z} \times 6 \mathbb{Z}$ is an integral domain \end{enumerate}
  10. Let $W$ be a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ and let $T: \mathbb{R}^{6} \rightarrow W$ be a linear transformation such that $S=\left\{T e_{2}, T e_{4}, T e_{6}\right\}$ spans $W$. Which one of the following must be TRUE ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $S$ is a basis of $W$ \item $T\left(\mathbb{R}^{6}\right) \neq W$ \item $\left\{T e_{1}, T e_{3}, T e_{5}\right\}$ spans $W$ \item $\operatorname{ker}(T)$ contains more than one element \end{enumerate}
  11. Consider the following subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ : $$ W=\left\{(x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^{3} \mid 2 x+2 y+z=0,3 x+3 y-2 z=0, x+y-3 z=0\right\} . $$ The dimension of $W$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 0 \item 1 \item 2 \item 3 \end{enumerate}
  12. Let $P$ be a $4 \times 4$ matrix whose determinant is 10 . The determinant of the matrix $-3 P$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-810$ \item $-30$ \item 30 \item 810 \end{enumerate}
  13. If the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_{n} x^{n}$ converges for $x=3$, then the series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_{n} x^{n}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item converges absolutely for $x=-2$ \item converges but not absolutely for $x=-1$ \item converges but not absolutely for $x=1$ \item diverges for $x=-2$ \end{enumerate}
  14. If $Y=\left\{\frac{x}{1+|x|} \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\right\}$, then the set of all limit points of $Y$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(-1,1)$ \item $\quad(-1,1]$ \item $[0,1]$ \item $[-1,1]$ \end{enumerate}
  15. If $C$ is a smooth curve in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ from $(0,0,0)$ to $(2,1,-1)$, then the value of $$ \int_{C}(2 x y+z) d x+\left(z+x^{2}\right) d y+(x+y) d z $$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-1$ \item 0 \item 1 \item 2 \end{enumerate}
  16. \begin{enumerate} \item Examine whether the following series is convergent: $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n !}{1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots(2 n-1)} \text {. } $$ \item For each $x \in \mathbb{R}$, let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Further, for a fixed $\beta \in(0,1)$, define $a_{n}=\frac{1}{n}[n \beta]+n^{2} \beta^{n}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Show that the sequence $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ converges to $\beta$. \end{enumerate}
  17. \begin{enumerate} \item Evaluate $\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\int_{0}^{x^{2}} \sqrt{4+t^{3}} d t}{x^{2}}$. \item For $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ with $a<b$, let $f:[a, b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be continuous on $[a, b]$ and twice differentiable on $(a, b)$. Further, assume that the graph of $f$ intersects the straight line segment joining the points $(a, f(a))$ and $(b, f(b))$ at a point $(c, f(c))$ for $a<c<b$. Show that there exists a real number $\xi \in(a, b)$ such that $f^{\prime \prime}(\xi)=0$. \end{enumerate}
  18. \begin{enumerate} \item Show that the point $(0,0)$ is neither a point of local minimum nor a point of local maximum for the function $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ given by $f(x, y)=3 x^{4}-4 x^{2} y+y^{2}$ for $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$ \item Find all the critical points of the function $f: \mathbb{R}^{2} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ given by $f(x, y)=x^{3}+y^{3}-3 x-12 y+40$ for $(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}$. Also, examine whether the function $f$ attains a local maximum or a local minimum at each of these critical points. \end{enumerate}
  19. \begin{enumerate} \item Evaluate $\int_{x=0}^{4} \int_{y=\sqrt{4-x}}^{2} e^{y^{3}} d y d x$. \item Using multiple integral, find the volume of the solid region in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ bounded above by the hemisphere $z=1+\sqrt{1-x^{2}-y^{2}}$ and bounded below by the cone $z=\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}$. \end{enumerate}
  20. Find the area of the portion of the surface $z=x^{2}-y^{2}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ which lies inside the solid cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2} \leq 1$.
  21. Let $y(x)$ be the solution of the differential equation $\frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}-y=0$ such that $y(0)=2$ and $y^{\prime}(0)=2 \alpha$. Find all values of $\alpha \in[0,1)$ such that the infimum of the set $\{y(x) \mid x \in \mathbb{R}\}$ is greater than or equal to 1.
  22. \begin{enumerate} \item Assume that $y_{1}(x)=x$ and $y_{2}(x)=x^{3}$ are two linearly independent solutions of the homogeneous differential equation $x^{2} \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}-3 x \frac{d y}{d x}+3 y=0$. Using the method of variation of parameters, find a particular solution of the differential equation $x^{2} \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}-3 x \frac{d y}{d x}+3 y=x^{5}$. \item Solve the differential equation $\frac{d y}{d x}+\frac{5 y}{6 x}=\frac{5 x^{4}}{y^{5}}$ subject to the condition $y(1)=1$. \end{enumerate}
  23. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $\vec{r}=x \hat{i}+y \hat{j}+z \hat{k}$ be the position vector field in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ and let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function. Show that $\vec{\nabla} \times\{f(|\vec{r}|) \vec{r}\}=\overrightarrow{0}$ for $\vec{r} \neq \overrightarrow{0}$. \item Let $W$ be the region inside the solid cylinder $x^{2}+y^{2} \leq 4$ between the plane $z=0$ and the paraboloid $z=x^{2}+y^{2}$. Let $S$ be the boundary of $W$. Using Gauss's divergence theorem, evaluate $\iint_{S} \vec{F} \cdot \hat{n} d S$, where $$ \vec{F}=\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-4\right) \hat{i}+(3 x y) \hat{j}+\left(2 x z+z^{2}\right) \hat{k} $$ and $\hat{n}$ is the outward unit normal vector to $S$. \end{enumerate}
  24. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $G$ be a finite group whose order is not divisible by 3 . Show that for every $g \in G$, there exists an $h \in G$ such that $g=h^{3}$. \item Let $A$ be the group of all rational numbers under addition, $B$ be the group of all non-zero rational numbers under multiplication and $C$ the group of all positive rational numbers under multiplication. Show that no two of the groups $A, B$ and $C$ are isomorphic. \end{enumerate}
  25. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $I$ be an ideal of a commutative ring $R$. Define Show that $A$ is an ideal of $R$. $$ A=\left\{r \in R \mid r^{n} \in I \text { for some } n \in \mathbb{N}\right\} . $$ \item Let $F$ be a field. For each $p(x) \in F[x]$ (the polynomial ring in $x$ over $F$ ) define $\varphi: F[x] \rightarrow F \times F$ by $\varphi(p(x))=(p(0), p(1))$. \begin{enumerate} \item Prove that $\varphi$ is a ring homomorphism. \item Prove that the quotient ring $F[x] /\left(x^{2}-x\right)$ is isomorphic to the ring $F \times F$. \end{enumerate} \end{enumerate}
  26. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $P, D$ and $A$ be real square matrices of the same order such that $P$ is invertible, $D$ is diagonal and $D=P A P^{-1}$. If $A^{n}=0$ for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then show that $A=0$. \item Let $T: V \rightarrow W$ be a linear transformation of vector spaces. Prove the following: \begin{enumerate} \item If $\left\{v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{k}\right\}$ spans $V$, and $T$ is onto, then $\left\{T v_{1}, T v_{2}, \ldots, T v_{k}\right\}$ spans $W$. \item If $\left\{v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{k}\right\}$ is linearly independent in $V$, and $T$ is one-one, then $\left\{T v_{1}, T v_{2}, \ldots, T v_{k}\right\}$ is linearly independent in $W$. \item If $\left\{v_{1}, v_{2}, \ldots, v_{k}\right\}$ is a basis of $V$, and $T$ is bijective, then $\left\{T v_{1}, T v_{2}, \ldots, T v_{k}\right\}$ is a basis of $W$. \end{enumerate} \end{enumerate}
  27. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $\left\{v_{1}, v_{2}, v_{3}\right\}$ be a basis of a vector space $V$ over $\mathbb{R}$. Let $T: V \rightarrow V$ be the linear transformation determined by $$ T v_{1}=v_{1}, T v_{2}=v_{2}-v_{3} \text { and } T v_{3}=v_{2}+2 v_{3} . $$ Find the matrix of the transformation $T$ with $\left\{v_{1}+v_{2}, v_{1}-v_{2}, v_{3}\right\}$ as a basis of both the domain and the co-domain of $T$. \item Let $W$ be a three dimensional vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ and let $S: W \rightarrow W$ be a linear transformation. Further, assume that every non-zero vector of $W$ is an eigenvector of $S$. Prove that there exists an $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $S=\alpha I$, where $I: W \rightarrow W$ is the identity transformation. \end{enumerate}
  28. \begin{enumerate} \item Show that the function $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, defined by $f(x)=x^{2}$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, is not uniformly continuous. \item For each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $f_{n}: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a uniformly continuous function. If the sequence $\left\{f_{n}\right\}$ converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$ to a function $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, then show that $f$ is uniformly continuous. \end{enumerate}
  29. \begin{enumerate} \item Let $A$ be a nonempty bounded subset of $\mathbb{R}$. Show that $\{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid x \geq a$ for all $a \in A\}$ is a closed subset of $\mathbb{R}$. \item Let $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ be a sequence in $\mathbb{R}$ such that $\left|x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right|<\frac{1}{n^{2}}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Show that the sequence $\left\{x_{n}\right\}$ is convergent. \end{enumerate}
