CC-5

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2020
  1. 1(a) If \(f\) is continuous on \([a,b]\) and \(f(a),f(b)\) are of opposite signs then show that there is a \(\eta\in(a,b)\) such that \(f(\eta)=0\). [5]
  2. 1(b) Let \(A,D\subset\mathbb R\) and \(f:A\to\mathbb R,\ g:D\to\mathbb R\) where \(f(A)\subset D\). If \(c\) is a limit point of \(A\) and \(\lim_{x\to c}f(x)=l\) then prove the following: \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})} \item if \(l\in D\) and \(g\) is continuous at \(l\) then \(\lim_{x\to c}(g\circ f)(x)=g(l)\) \item if \(l\notin D\ (l\in D')\) and \(\lim_{y\to l}g(y)=m\) then \(\lim_{x\to c}(g\circ f)(x)=m\). \end{enumerate} [5]
  3. 2(a) When a function \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) is said to be of bounded variation? If \(f,g\) be two functions of bounded variation over \([a,b]\) then prove that \(f-g\) and \(fg\) are of bounded variation on \([a,b]\). [6]
  4. 2(b) Verify whether the following functions are of bounded variation or not. \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})} \item \(f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R\) defined by \[ \begin{aligned} f(x)&=x^2\sin\frac{\pi}{x},\quad x\ne0\\ &=0,\quad x=0 \end{aligned} \] \item \(f:[1,2]\to\mathbb R\) defined by \(f(x)=5x^3-7x^2+12\). \end{enumerate}
  5. 3(a) Let \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) be differentiable on \([a,b)\) and \(f'(a)\ne f'(b)\). If \(k\) be a real number such that \(f'(a)<k<f'(b)\) than prove that \(\exists c\in(a,b)\) such that \(f'(c)=k\). [4]
  6. 3(b) If \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) is continuous on \([a,b]\), then show that \(f\) is uniformly continuous on \([a,b]\). [4]
  7. 3(c) Prove that \(f(x)=\sqrt{x}\) is uniformly continuous on \([1,\infty)\). [2]
  8. 4(a) If \(f\) and \(g\) be two real valued functions, both are bounded and \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\), then prove that \(fg\) is also \(R\)-integrable. Is the converse true? Justify your answer. [5]
  9. 4(b) Let \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) be bounded on \([a,b]\) and \(f\) be continuous on \([a,b]\) except on a subset \(S\subset[a,b]\) such that the number of limit points of \(S\) is finite. Prove that \(f\) is \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\). [5]
  10. 5(a) Suppose \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) is \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\). Prove that there exist a number \(\eta\) where \(\inf\{f(x):a\le x\le b\}\le\eta\le\sup\{f(x):a\le x\le b\}\) such that \((R)\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=\eta(b-a)\). [5]
  11. 5(b) Show that \(\displaystyle\frac14\le\int_0^{1/4}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^4}}\le\frac1{15}\). [5]
  12. 6(a) Prove that if any one of \((RS)\int_a^b f\,dg\) and \((RS)\int_a^b g\,df\) exists then the other will also exist and \((RS)\int_a^b f\,dg+(RS)\int_a^b g\,df=f(b)g(b)-f(a)g(a)\). [4]
  13. 6(b) Let \(f\) be continuous and \(g\) be such that \(g'\) exists, bounded and \(R\)-integrable on \([a,b]\). Then prove that \((RS)\int_a^b f\,dg\) and \((R)\int_a^b fg'\,dx\) exist and are equal. [3]
  14. 6(c) Evaluate \((RS)\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}(\pi-|x|)\,d(|x|+\sin x)\). [3]
  15. 7(a) State ratio test for a series of positive terms and give a proof for the case when limit is less than 1.
  16. 7(b) Give example for the following cases of an infinite series: \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})} \item ratio test fails but root test works. \item ratio test fails but Rabbe's test works. \end{enumerate}
  17. 8(a) State the conditions for convergence of a \(p\)-series and give the corresponding proof.
  18. 8(b) How many terms of the following series is required to make the sum correct upto two decimal places: \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n\log(1+\tfrac1n)\). [3]
  19. 9(a) A real function \(f\) is continuous on \([0,2]\) and \(f(0)=f(2)\). Prove that there exists at least a point \(c\in[0,1]\) such that \(f(c)=f(c+1)\). [2]
  20. 9(b) Show that \(\displaystyle\lvert\int_a^b\frac{\cos x}{(1+x)}\,dx<\frac{4}{1+a}\) if \(b>a>0\). \par\textit{[Closing vertical bar absent in the source.]} [4]
  21. 9(c) Verify whether the function \(f(x)=\frac{2}{2+x^2}\) is uniformly continuous or not on \(\mathbb R\). \def\paperplural{s} [4]
