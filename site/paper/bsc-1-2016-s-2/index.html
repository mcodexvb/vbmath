BMC-12

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2016
  1. 1(a) For any two sets $A$ and $B$, show that\\i) $A-B$ and $A\cap B$ are disjoint sets.\\ii) $(A-B)\cup B=A$ if and only if $B\subset A$. [2+2]
  2. 1(b) Define a partial order relation.\\If $\rho$ be a relation on $\mathbb Z$ (set of integers) defined by $a\rho b\Leftrightarrow a-b$ is divisible by 5, then show that $\rho$ is an equivalence relation. [1+2]
  3. 1(c) Give the definition of a mapping.\\If $X=\{2,3,4,6\}$ and $Y=\{2,4,6\}$. Suppose $f:X\to Y$ be such that $f(2)=4$ and $f(3)=6$. Is $f$ a mapping? Justify your answer. [1+2]
  4. 2(a) If a, b and c be the three sides of a triangle and s be the semiperimeter of the triangle, then show that $\begin{vmatrix}a^2&(s-a)^2&(s-a)^2\\(s-b)^2&b^2&(s-b)^2\\(s-c)^2&(s-c)^2&c^2\end{vmatrix}=2s^3(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$. [3]
  5. 2(b) If $A$ and $B$ be two invertible matrices of the same order, then show that $AB$ is invertible and $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$. [4]
  6. 2(c) If $A$ be a real orthogonal matrix and $(I+A)$ be non singular, where I is the identity matrix then prove that the matrix $(I+A)^{-1}(I-A)$ is skew symmetric. [3]
  7. 3(a) Show that an $n\times n$ matrix A is non-singular if and only if A is row equivalent to the identity matrix $I_n$. [3]
  8. 3(b) Define a row reduced echelon matrix. Find the row reduced echelon matrix of\\$A=\begin{bmatrix}2&-1&3\\3&2&1\\1&-4&5\end{bmatrix}$ and hence find the rank of A. [1+2+1]
  9. 3(c) Show that the equations $x+2y-z=3$, $3x-y+2z=1$, $2x-2y+3z=2$ and $x-y+z=-1$ are consistent and hence solve the equations. [2+1]
  10. 4(a) If $(x_1+iy_1)(x_2+iy_2)\ldots(x_n+iy_n)=x+iy$, then show that\\i) $\tan^{-1}\frac{y_1}{x_1}+\tan^{-1}\frac{y_2}{x_2}+\ldots+\tan^{-1}\frac{y_n}{x_n}=\tan^{-1}\frac yx$ and\\ii) $(x_1^2+y_1^2)(x_2^2+y_2^2)\ldots(x_n^2+y_n^2)=x^2+y^2$. [5]
  11. 4(b) If $x_r=\cos\frac\pi{2^r}+i\sin\frac\pi{2^r}$, $r=1,2,\ldots$. then show that $x_1x_2x_3\ldots$to $\infty=-1$. [2]
  12. 4(c) Show that the product of all the values of $(1+i\sqrt3)^{3/4}$ is 8. [3]
  13. 5(a) Show that the equation $(x-a)^3+(x-b)^3+(x-c)^3+(x-d)^3=0$, where a, b, c, and d are positive real numbers and not all equal, has only one real root. [4]
  14. 5(b) If $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ be the roots of the equation $x^3+px^2+qx+r=0$, find the value of $(\beta+\gamma-\alpha)^3+(\gamma+\alpha-\beta)^3+(\alpha+\beta-\gamma)^3$. [3]
  15. 5(c) If $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ be the roots of the equation $x^3-2x^2+3x-1=0$, find the equation whose roots are $\frac{\beta\gamma-\alpha^2}{\beta+\gamma-2\alpha},\frac{\gamma\alpha-\beta^2}{\gamma+\alpha-2\beta},\frac{\alpha\beta-\gamma^2}{\alpha+\beta-2\gamma}$. [3]
  16. 6(a) If x, y and z be positive but unequal real numbers and $x+y+z=1$, then prove that $8xyz<(1-x)(1-y)(1-z)<\frac8{27}$. [4]
  17. 6(b) Solve by Cardon's method, $x^3-18x-35=0$ [3]
  18. 6(c) Slove the biquadratic equation $x^4+12x-5=0$ by Ferrari's method. \fbox{\parbox{4.2cm}{\small Use separate answer scripts for each Unit}} [3]
