MMC-14

Ordinary Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2024
  1. 1(a) With a suitable example show that Lipchitz Criteria is a weaker concept than Picard's condition for uniqueness of solutions of a first order IVP. [2]
  2. 1(b) What is meant by Interval of Definition for the solution of an IVP. Find the Interval of Definition for the solution of the following IVP. \[ \frac{d^3x}{dt^3}+\frac1{t^2-4}x=\cos(t),\qquad x(1)=3,\ x'(1)=0,\ x''(1)=0. \] [1+2]
  3. 1(c) Show that the initial value problem $\frac{dy}{dx}=(y-1)/x$, $y(0)=1$ has infinite solutions. [2]
  4. 1(d) Find the third approximation of the solution of the equation $\frac{dy}{dx}=z$, $\frac{dz}{dx}=x^3(y+z)$ by the Picard's Method where $y=1$, $z=\frac12$ where $x=0$. [3]
  5. 2(a) What is a Wronskians? State and prove Abel's Theorem for a \textit{[first digit covered]}nd order ODE. \textit{[Handwritten: 2nd.]} Hence show that the Wronskians is either zero everywhere or zero nowhere. [1+3+1]
  6. 2(b) When a set of solutions is said to be Fundamental? What do you mean by a Natural Fundamental Sets of Solutions? Find the Natural Fundamental set of solutions of the following Differential Equations $y''-y'-2y=0$, with the initial time 0. [1+1+3]
  7. 3(a) Find the nature and stability of the fixed points of $\dot{x}=-ax+y$, $\dot{y}=-x-ay$ For the different values of the parameter $a$. [3]
  8. 3(b) Draw the phase diagrams for the linear harmonic undamped oscillators represented by $\ddot x+\omega^2x=0$. [2]
  9. 3(c) Find the solution of the non-homogeneous differential equation $\dot{\underline{x}}=A\underline{x}+F(t)$, where $A=\begin{bmatrix}6&-3\\2&1\end{bmatrix}$ and $F(t)=\begin{bmatrix}e^{5t}\\4\end{bmatrix}$. [5]
  10. 4(a) State and prove Sturm Separation Theorem. [4]
  11. 4(b) Let $f$ and $g$ be any two solutions of $\dfrac{d}{dt}[P(t)\dfrac{dx}{dt}]+Q(t)x=0$ on the interval $a\leq t\leq b$. Then show that for all $t$ on $a\leq t\leq b$, $P(t)[f(t)g'(t)-f'(t)g(t)]=k$, where $k$ is a constant. [3]
  12. 4(c) If $A=\begin{bmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{bmatrix}$, then prove that $e^{At}=e^{aIt}[I\cos(bt)+J\sin(bt)]$, where $I=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$ and $J=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}$. [3]
  13. 5(a) Solve the boundary-value problem $x^2y''-3xy'+3y=24x^5$, $y(1)=0$, $y(2)=0$. by using Green's function. [6]
  14. 5(b) Solve the initial-value problem $y''+4y=x$, $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=0$. by using Green's function. [4]
  15. 6(a) State and prove Gronwall's inequality. [1+4]
  16. 6(b) Find the eigenvalues and eigen-functions of the following Strum-Liouville's system $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}+\lambda y=0$, $y(0)=y'(0)$, $y(\pi)=y'(\pi)$. [5]
