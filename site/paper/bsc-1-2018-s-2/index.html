CC-2

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2018
  1. 1(a) Draw the column picture for the solution of the system of equations $x+y=5,\ x-y=-1$. [2]
  2. 1(b) Show that the following system of equations is inconsistent.\par $y-4z=8$,\quad $2x-3y+2z=1$,\quad $4x-8y+12z=1$ [2]
  3. 1(c) Find an equation involving $g,h,k$ that makes the following augmented matrix corresponds to a consistent system $\begin{pmatrix}1&-4&7&g\\0&3&-5&h\\-2&5&-9&k\end{pmatrix}$. [2]
  4. 1(d) Find the reduced row echelon form of $\begin{pmatrix}0&3&-6&6&4&-5\\3&-7&8&-5&8&9\\3&-9&12&-9&6&15\end{pmatrix}$. Hence or otherwise list the free and basic variables of the corresponding system of equations. Also find its solution. [4]
  5. 2(a) Suppose the coefficient matrix of a system of linear equations has a pivot position in every row. Explain why the system is consistent. [3]
  6. 2(b) Find the equation to describe geometrically, span $(\{(1,2,1),(2,-3,0)\})$. [2]
  7. 2(c) Express $b=(7,4,-3)$ as a linear combination of $a_1=(1,-2,-5)$ and $a_2=(2,5,6)$. [2]
  8. 2(d) Suppose $A$ is a $3\times3$ matrix and $b$ is a vector in $\mathbb R^3$ with the property that $Ax=b$ has a unique solutions. Explain why the columns of $A$ must span $\mathbb R^3$. [3]
  9. 3(a) Examine if the columns of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&-8&5\\3&-7&4\\-1&5&-4\\1&-3&2\end{pmatrix}$ are linearly independent. [3]
  10. 3(b) Prove that a set of vectors $S=\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_p\}$ in $\mathbb R^n$ is linearly dependent if and only if at least one of the vectors is $S$ is a linear combination of the others. [4]
  11. 3(c) Suppose that an $m\times n$ matrix $A$ has $n$ pivot columns. Explain why for each $b$ in $\mathbb R^m$ the system $Ax=b$ has at most one solution. [3]
  12. 4(a) Find the linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ which represents the following two consecutive reflections. [2]
  13. 4(b) Let $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ be a linear transformation given by $T(e_1)=(2,-3,1)$, $T(e_2)=(0,-2,5)$ and $T(e_3)=(-1,3,4)$. Find $T(x,y,z)$, where $e_1,e_2$ and $e_3$ are the standard basis vectors of $\mathbb R^3$. Hence find the standard matrix of $T$. [2+2]
  14. 4(c) Let $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ be a linear transformation and $A$ be the standard matrix of $T$. Prove that $T$ maps $\mathbb R^n$ onto $\mathbb R^m$ if and only if the columns of $A$ spans $\mathbb R^m$. [4]
  15. 5(a) Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix. If $A$ is invertible, then show that the linear transformation $x\to Ax$ is one-to-one. [3]
  16. 5(b) Find a basis for the null space of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}-3&6&1&1&-7\\1&-2&2&3&-1\\2&-4&5&8&-4\end{pmatrix}$. [3]
  17. 5(c) Let $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ be a linear transformation. Show that $\ker T$ is a subspace of $\mathbb R^n$. [2]
  18. 5(d) What can you say about null $B$, when $B$ is a $5\times4$ matrix with linearly independent columns. [2]
  19. 6(a) Determine the rank of the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&5&-3&-4&8\\4&7&-4&-3&9\\6&9&-5&2&4\\0&-9&6&5&-6\end{pmatrix}$ [2]
  20. 6(b) Find a basis and the dimension of $W$ where $W=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3\mid x+2y+3z=0\}$ [3]
  21. 6(c) Find the eigen values of $A=\begin{pmatrix}4&-1&6\\2&1&6\\2&-1&8\end{pmatrix}$. Also find the eigen space corresponding to the repeated eigen value, if any. [3]
  22. 6(d) Construct an example of a $2\times2$ matrix which has only one eigen value. [2]
  23. 7(a) Let $z_1$ and $z_2$ be two non zero complex numbers. If $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ be arguments of $z_1$ and $z_2$ respectively, then show that $\theta_1+\theta_2$ is an argument of $z_1z_2$.\par Does the result hold it one considers the principal arguments of complex numbers? Justify your answer. [2+2]
  24. 7(b) If $n$ is a positive integer and $(1+z)^n=a_0+a_1z+\ldots+a_nz^n$, then prove that\par (i) $a_0-a_2+a_4-\ldots=2^{n/2}\cos\frac{n\pi}4$\par (ii) $a_1-a_3+a_5-\ldots=2^{n/2}\sin\frac{n\pi}4$ [3]
  25. 7(c) Show that the product of all values of $(\sqrt3+i)^{3/5}$ is $8i$. [3]
  26. 8(a) Let $a,b,c,d$ be four positive real numbers, not all equal. Then show that the equation $(x-a)^3+(x-b)^3+(x-c)^3+(x-d)^3=0$ has only one real root. [4]
  27. 8(b) If $\alpha,\beta$ and $\gamma$ be the roots of the equation $2x^3+x^2+x+1=0$, find the equation whose roots are $\frac1{\alpha^2}+\frac1{\beta^2}-\frac1{\gamma^2},\ \frac1{\alpha^2}-\frac1{\beta^2}+\frac1{\gamma^2},\ -\frac1{\alpha^2}+\frac1{\beta^2}+\frac1{\gamma^2}$. Hence find the value of $\frac1{\alpha^2}+\frac1{\beta^2}+\frac1{\gamma^2}$. [3+1]
  28. 8(c) Expand $f(x)=x^4-x^3+2x^2+6x-2$ as a polynomial in $x-2$ and then find $f(x+2)$ [2]
  29. 9(a) If $x,y,z$ are positive real numbers and $x+y+z=1$, prove that $8xyz\le(1-x)(1-y)(1-z)\le\frac8{27}$. [3]
  30. 9(b) Solve $x^3+6x^2-12x+32=0$. [4]
  31. 9(c) Express $f(x)=x^4-2x^3-5x^2+10x-3$ in the form $(x^2+px+q)^2-(ax+b)^2$ and hence, solve the equation $f(x)=0$. [3]
