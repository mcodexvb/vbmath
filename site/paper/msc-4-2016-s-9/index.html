MMO-41

Advanced Topology-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2016
  1. 1(a) State and prove Urysohn's metrization theorem. [1+6]
  2. 1(b) (i) Check whether the collection $\mathcal D=\left\{\left(\dfrac1{n+1},\dfrac1n\right):n\in IN\right\}$ is locally finite or not in the set of reals with usual topology.\par (ii) If $\mathbf a$ is a locally finite collection in a topological space then show that $\{\bar A;A\in\mathbf a\}$ is locally finite and $\overline{\bigcup_{A\in\mathbf a}A}=\bigcup_{A\in\mathbf a}\bar A.$ [2+1+2]
  3. 2(a) Let $(X,\mathcal U)$ be a uniform space and $\tau_u$ be the uniform topology induced from $\mathcal U$. Then show that for any $A\subset X,\ \overline A^{\tau_u}=\bigcap_{U\in\mathcal U}U[A]$. Hence or otherwise show that every uniform space is regular. [3+2]
  4. 2(b) Give an example of a uniform space whose uniform topology is not Hausdorff. Prove that a uniform space $(X,\mathcal U)$ is Hausdorff iff $\bigcap_{U\in\mathcal U}U=\Delta(X).$ [2+3]
  5. 2(c) Prove that every uniformly continuous function is continuous. [2]
  6. 3(a) Prove that a uniform space having a countable base possesses a countable symmetric normalized base. [4]
  7. 3(b) Prove that a uniform space is pseudometrizable iff it has a countable base. [8]
  8. 4(a) Show that the additive group of reals with usual Topology is a topological group. [2]
  9. 4(b) Prove that in a topological group, translation mappings and the inversion mappings are homeomorphisms. [2+1]
  10. 4(c) Prove that a uniformity can be induced in a topological group compatible with topology. [4]
  11. 4(d) Prove that the centre of a Hausdorff topological group is a closed normal subgroup. [3]
  12. 5(a) Prove that in a Hausdorff connected topological group every normal discrete subgroup is a closed normal subgroup of the centre. [4]
  13. 5(b) Let $(G,\tau)$ be a topological group, H be an algebraic subgroup of G and $G/H$ be the class of all left cosets of H in G. Let $\tau'$ be the quotient topology on $G/H$ induced by the connonical mapping $\phi:G\to G/H$. Then show that\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace $(G/H,\tau')$ is $T_1$ iff H is closed in $(G,\tau)$.\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace $(G/H,\tau')$ is a discrete space iff H is open\par \hspace*{1em}(iii)\enspace $(G/H,\tau')$ is homogeneous. [2+2+4]
  14. 6 Answer \underline{any two}:\par \hspace*{1em}(i)\enspace Prove that a $T_0$ topological group is $T_2$.\par \hspace*{1em}(ii)\enspace Prove that every component of a topological group is a normal subgroup.\par \hspace*{1em}(iii)\enspace Check the metrizability of the cofinite topology on $\mathbb R$.
