  1. 1 The set of all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the vectors $(1, x, 0)$, $(0, x^2, 1)$, and $(0, 1, x)$ are linearly independent in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : x = 0\}$ \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : x \ne 0\}$ \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : x \ne 1\}$ \item $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : x \ne -1\}$ \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Consider the vector space $\mathbb{R}^3$ and the maps $f, g : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ defined by $f(x,y,z) = (x, |y|, z)$ and $g(x,y,z) = (x+1, y-1, z)$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item both $f$ and $g$ are linear \item neither $f$ nor $g$ is linear \item $g$ is linear but not $f$ \item $f$ is linear but not $g$ \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $M = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 9 \end{pmatrix}$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $M$ is diagonalizable but not $M^2$ \item $M^2$ is diagonalizable but not $M$ \item both $M$ and $M^2$ are diagonalizable \item neither $M$ nor $M^2$ is diagonalizable \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Let $M$ be a skew-symmetric, orthogonal real matrix. The only possible eigenvalues are \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-1, 1$ \item $-i, i$ \item $0$ \item $1, i$ \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 The principal value of $\log(i^{1/4})$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $i\pi$ \item $\dfrac{i\pi}{2}$ \item $\dfrac{i\pi}{4}$ \item $\dfrac{i\pi}{8}$ \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 Consider the functions $f(z) = x^2 + iy^2$ and $g(z) = x^2 + y^2 + ixy$. At $z = 0$, \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $f$ is analytic but not $g$ \item $g$ is analytic but not $f$ \item both $f$ and $g$ are analytic \item neither $f$ nor $g$ is analytic \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 The coefficient of $\dfrac{1}{z}$ in the expansion of $\operatorname{Log}\!\left(\dfrac{z}{z - 1}\right)$ valid in $|z| > 1$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-1$ \item $1$ \item $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ \item $\dfrac{1}{2}$ \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 Under the usual topology in $\mathbb{R}^3$, if $O = \{(x,y,z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x^2 + y^2 < 1\}$ and $F = \{(x,y,z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : z = 0\}$, then $O \cap F$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item both open and closed \item neither open nor closed \item open but not closed \item closed but not open \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 Suppose $E$ is a nonmeasurable subset of $[0, 1]$.   10. 10 The value of $\displaystyle\int_0^\pi \int_x^\pi \int_0^2 \frac{\sin y}{y}\,dz\,dy\,dx$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $-2$ \item $2$ \item $-4$ \item $4$ \end{enumerate}

  18. 18 Consider the Hilbert space $l^2 = \left\{(x_1, x_2, \dots) : x_i \in \mathbb{C} \text{ for all } i \text{ and } \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} |x_i|^2 < \infty\right\}$ with the inner product $\langle (x_1, x_2, \dots), (y_1, y_2, \dots) \rangle = \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} x_i \bar{y}_i$.   21. 21 The integral surface of the partial differential equation $x\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x} + y\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y} = 0$ satisfying the condition $u(1, y) = y$ is given by \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $u(x,y) = \dfrac{y}{x}$ \item $u(x,y) = \dfrac{2y}{x + 1}$ \item $u(x,y) = \dfrac{y}{2 - x}$ \item $u(x,y) = y + x - 1$ \end{enumerate}

  23. 23 A bead slides on a smooth rod which is rotating about one end in a vertical plane with uniform angular velocity $\omega$. If $g$ denotes the acceleration due to gravity, then the Lagrange equation of motion is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\ddot{r} = r\omega^2 - g\sin\omega t$ \item $\ddot{r} = r\omega^2 - g\cos\omega t$ \item $\ddot{r} = -g\sin\omega t$ \item $\ddot{r} = -g\cos\omega t$ \end{enumerate}

If $p_1, p_2$ denote the generalized momenta, then the Hamiltonian $H$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item depends on $p_1, p_2$ but not on $q_1, q_2$ \item depends on $q_1, q_2$ but not on $p_1, p_2$ \item depends on $p_1, q_1$ but not on $p_2, q_2$ \item is independent of $p_1, p_2, q_1, q_2$ \end{enumerate}
