NBHM · 2005 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Find the value of $a \in \Z$ such that $2 + \sqrt{3}$ is a root of the polynomial \[ x^3 - 5x^2 + ax - 1. \]
  2. 1.2 Let $V = \R^5$ and $W = \R^7$. Let $T : V \to W$ be a linear map. If $\mathcal{N}(T)$ denotes the null space of $T$ and $\mathcal{R}(T)$ denotes its range, then \[ \dim \mathcal{N}(T) + \dim \mathcal{R}(T) = ? \]
  3. 1.3 Let $A$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix whose eigenvalues are $-1, 1, 2$. Find $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ such that \[ A^{-1} = \alpha A^2 + \beta A + \gamma I. \]
  4. 1.4 What is the number of groups of order $6$ (upto isomorphism)?
  5. 1.5 Let $G$ be a cyclic group of order $10$. For $a \in G$, let $\langle a \rangle$ denote the subgroup generated by $a$. How many elements are there in the set \[ \{a \in G \mid \langle a \rangle = G\}? \]
  6. 1.6 Let $\alpha = 2^{\frac{1}{3}}$ and $\beta = 5^{\frac{1}{4}}$. Let $L$ be the field obtained by adjoining $\alpha$ and $\beta$ to $\Q$. What is the degree of the extension $[L : \Q]$?
  7. 1.7 Pick out the matrices which are diagonalizable over $\C$: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item Any $n \times n$ unitary matrix with complex entries. \item Any $n \times n$ hermitian matrix with complex entries. \item Any $n \times n$ strictly upper triangular matrix with complex entries. \item Any $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries whose eigenvalues are real. \end{enumerate}
  8. 1.8 Pick out the units in $\Z[\sqrt{3}]$.\\ (a) $-7 + 4\sqrt{3}$ \qquad (b) $5 + 3\sqrt{3}$ \qquad (c) $2 - \sqrt{3}$ \qquad (d) $-3 - 2\sqrt{3}$.
  9. 1.9 Pick out the integral domains from the following list of rings: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $\{a + b\sqrt{5} \mid a, b \in \Q\}$. \item The ring of continuous functions from $[0, 1]$ into $\R$. \item The ring of complex analytic functions on the disc $\{z \in \C \mid |z| < 1\}$. \item The polynomial ring $\Z[x]$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 1.10 Pick out the abelian groups from the following list: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item Any group of order $4$. \item Any group of order $36$. \item Any group of order $47$. \item Any group of order $49$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 Let $f : [0, 1] \to \R$ be a continuous function. Then \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=0}^{n} f\left(\frac{k}{n}\right) = ? \]
  12. 2.2 What is the radius of convergence of the following series? \[ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{z^n}{n!}. \]
  13. 2.3 Let $k \in [0, \infty[$ be a real number. Define \[ f_k(t) = \begin{cases} t^k \sin\frac{1}{t}, & t \neq 0, \\ 0, & t = 0. \end{cases} \] Let $A = \{k \in [0, \infty[ \mid f_k \text{ is differentiable}\}$. Then $A = ?$
  14. 2.4 What is the least value of $K > 0$ such that \[ |\sin^2 x - \sin^2 y| \le K |x - y| \] for all real numbers $x$ and $y$?
  15. 2.5 Let $\Gamma$ be the circle in the complex plane with centre at $z = 1$ and of radius unity. Evaluate: \[ \int_{\Gamma} \frac{z\,dz}{(z - 1)^4}. \]
  16. 2.6 If the plane $\R^2$ is provided with the Lebesgue measure, what is the measure of the set \[ A = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 \mid x^2 + y^2 = 1\}? \]
  17. 2.7 Pick out the sequences which are uniformly convergent: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f_n(x) = \sin^n x$ on $[0, \pi/2[$. \item $f_n(x) = \frac{x^n}{n} + 1$ on $[0, 1[$. \item $f_n(x) = \frac{1}{1 + (x - n)^2}$ on $]-\infty, 0[$. \item $f_n(x) = \frac{1}{1 + (x - n)^2}$ on $]0, +\infty[$. \end{enumerate}
  18. 2.8 Pick out the functions which are Riemann integrable on the interval $[0, 1]$: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } x \text{ is rational} \\ 0, & \text{if } x \text{ is irrational.} \end{cases}$ \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } x \in \{\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \ldots, \alpha_n\} \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$\\ where $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n$ are fixed, but arbitrarily chosen numbers in $[0, 1]$. \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} 0, & \text{if } x \text{ is irrational or if } x = 0 \\ \sin q\pi, & \text{if } x = p/q,\ p \text{ and } q \text{ positive and coprime integers.} \end{cases}$ \end{enumerate}
  19. 2.9 Pick out the functions from the following list which are analytic in $\C$: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f(z) = |z|^2$ \item $f(z) = \overline{z}$ \item $f(z) = \RePart(z)$ \end{enumerate}
  20. 2.10 Pick out the statements which are true: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $|\sin z| \le 1$ for all $z \in \C$. \item $\sin^2 z + \cos^2 z = 1$ for all $z \in \C$. \item $\sin z = (e^{iz} - e^{-iz})/2$ for all $z \in \C$. \end{enumerate}
  21. 3.1 Let $f : ]0, 1[ \to \R$ be continuous. It can be extended to a continuous function $\widetilde{f} : [0, 1] \to \R$ if, and only if, it is \underline{\hspace{3cm}}.
  22. 3.2 Consider the disjoint closed sets in $\R^2$ given by \[ A = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 \mid y = 0\} \quad \text{and} \quad B = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 \mid xy = 1\}. \] What is the distance $d(A, B)$ between them? \noindent\emph{In Questions 3.3 and 3.4 below, write `0' if the set $A$ is empty, the exact number of elements in it if the set is finite, and `infinite' if the set is infinite.}
  23. 3.3 Let $f : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ be such that $|f(x) - f(y)| \le \frac{1}{2}|x - y|$ for all $x, y \in [0, 1]$. Let $A = \{x \in [0, 1] \mid f(x) = x\}$. The number of elements in $A$ is \underline{\hspace{2cm}}.
  24. 3.4 Let $f : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ be continuous and such that $f(0) = f(1)$. Let \[ A = \{(t, s) \in [0, 1] \times [0, 1] \mid t \neq s \text{ and } f(t) = f(s)\}. \] The number of elements in $A$ is \underline{\hspace{2cm}}. \noindent\emph{In Questions 3.5 to 3.10 below, mark a \textbf{tick} over the topological properties true for the set $A$ and \textbf{strike out} the properties that do not hold. Your answer will be treated as correct only if \textbf{all} the choices are correctly made.}
  25. 3.5 Identify the space of all $n \times n$ matrices (with real entries) with $\R^{n^2}$. Let $A$ be the set of all invertible matrices.\\ Choices: open, closed, connected, dense.
  26. 3.6 $A = f(B) \subset X$ where $B = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 \mid 1 \le x^2 + y^2 \le 2\}$, $X$ is an arbitrary topological space and $f : \R^2 \to X$ is an arbitrary continuous map.\\ Choices: open, closed, compact, connected.
  27. 3.7 $A = X \setminus \{x_o\}$ where $X$ is an arbitrary Hausdorff topological space and $x_o \in X$.\\ Choices: open, closed, connected, dense.
  28. 3.8 $A = f(B) \subset \R$ where $B$ is a closed interval contained in $]0, \infty[$ and $f(t) = \log t$.\\ Choices: open, closed, connected, compact.
  29. 3.9 $A = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 \mid y = mx\} \setminus \{(0, 0)\} \subset \R^2$.\\ Choices: open, closed, connected, nowhere dense.
  30. 3.10 $A$ is the closure in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ of the set $B$ where \[ B = \{f \in \mathcal{C}^1[0, 1] \mid |f(x)| \le 1 \text{ and } |f'(x)| \le 1 \text{ for all } x \in [0, 1]\}. \] Choices: closed, compact, connected, dense.
  31. 4.1 Let $S = \{(x, y, z) \in \R^3 \mid x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\}$. Let $\mathbf{v} = (v_1, v_2, v_3)$ be a solenoidal vector field on $\R^3$. Evaluate: \[ \int_S [x(x + v_1(x, y, z)) + y(y + v_2(x, y, z)) + z(z + v_3(x, y, z))]\,dS. \]
  32. 4.2 Let $\mathbf{v} = (v_1, v_2, v_3)$ be a vector field on $\R^3$ where $v_1 = \sqrt{1 + x^2 + y^2}$, $v_2 = \sqrt{1 + z^2}$ and $v_3 = \sqrt{1 + x^2 y^2 z^2}$. Evaluate $\divg(\mathbf{curl}\,\mathbf{v})$.
  33. 4.3 What is the smallest value of $\lambda \in \R$ such that the boundary value problem: \[ u''(x) + \lambda u(x) = 0 \quad \text{in } ]0, 1[ \quad \text{and} \quad u(0) = u(1) = 0 \] has a non-trivial solution (\emph{i.e.}\ $u \not\equiv 0$)?
  34. 4.4 Let $\mathbf{u}(t) = (u_1(t), u_2(t))$ be the unique solution of the problem: \begin{align*} \frac{d\mathbf{u}}{dt}(t) &= A\mathbf{u}(t), \quad t > 0 \\ \mathbf{u}(0) &= \mathbf{u}_o \end{align*} where $\mathbf{u}_o = (1, 1)$ and $A$ is a symmetric $2 \times 2$ matrix such that $\tr(A) < 0$ and $\det(A) > 0$. Evaluate: \[ \lim_{t \to \infty} u_1(t). \]
  35. 4.5 Simpson's rule gives the exact value of $\int_0^1 p(t)\,dt$ for every polynomial of degree less than or equal to \underline{\hspace{2cm}}.
  36. 4.6 Consider the linear programming problem: Maximize $z = 5x + 7y$ such that \begin{align*} x - y &\le 1 \\ 2x + y &\ge 2 \\ x + 2y &\le 4 \\ x \ge 0,&\quad y \ge 0. \end{align*} What is the optimal value of $z$?
  37. 4.7 According to the classification of second order linear partial differential operators, the operator \[ \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} - 4\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x \partial y} + 5\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} \] is of \underline{\hspace{2cm}} type.
  38. 4.8 Evaluate: \[ \iint_{\R^2} e^{-(x+2y)^2 - (x+y)^2}\,dxdy. \]
  39. 4.9 A necessary and sufficient condition that the boundary value problem: \begin{align*} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} &= f(x, y) \quad \text{in } \Omega \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial n} &= 0 \quad \text{on } \partial\Omega \end{align*} (where $\Omega \subset \R^2$ is a bounded domain with boundary $\partial\Omega$ and $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ denotes the outer normal derivative of the function $u$) has a solution is \underline{\hspace{2cm}}.
  40. 4.10 The radius $r$ and height $h$ of a right circular cylinder of fixed volume $V$ and least total surface area are connected by the relation \underline{\hspace{2cm}}.
  41. 5.1 What is the maximum number of pieces that a pizza can be cut into by $7$ knife strokes?
  42. 5.2 Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer. Let $C_r$ denote the number of ways of choosing $r$ objects from a collection of $n$ objects. Evaluate: \[ C_1 + 2\cdot C_2 + \dots + n\cdot C_n. \]
  43. 5.3 Inside a square of side $2$ units, five points are marked at random. What is the probability that there are at least two points such that the distance between them is at most $\sqrt{2}$ units?
  44. 5.4 What is the area of the triangle in the complex plane formed by the points representing $1, \omega$ and $\omega^2$, where $\omega$ is a complex cube root of unity?
  45. 5.5 What is the number of points of intersection, in $\R^2$, of the two plane curves $(1 + x^2 + y^2)(x^2 + y^2 - 4) = 0$ and $y = 7x$?
  46. 5.6 What geometric figure is formed by the locus of a point which moves so that the sum of four times its distance from the $x$-axis and nine times its distance from the $y$-axis is equal to $10$?
  47. 5.7 A real number is \emph{algebraic} if it is the root of a polynomial with integer coefficients. Define $A : [0, 1] \to \R$ by \[ A(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } x \text{ is algebraic} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases} \] Evaluate: $\int_0^1 A(x)\,dx$.
  48. 5.8 In the rectangle $[0, \pi/2] \times [0, 1] \subset \R^2$, a point $(x, y)$ is chosen at random. What is the probability that $y \le \sin x$?
  49. 5.9 If $p$ is a prime greater than, or equal to, $11$, then, either $p^3 - 1$ or $p^3 + 1$ is divisible by $14$. True or False?
  50. 5.10 Evaluate: \[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n^2 - n + 1}{n!}. \]
