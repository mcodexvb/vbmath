MMO-41(P5)

Rings and Modules-II
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2017
  1. 1(a) Prove that a right $R$-module $M$ is Noetherian iff every submodule of $M$ is finitely generated. [4]
  2. 1(b) Let $O\longrightarrow M_1\xrightarrow{\ f\ }M\xrightarrow{\ g\ }M_2\longrightarrow O$ be a short exact sequence of right $R$-modules and $R$-morphisms. Prove that $M$ is Artinian iff both $M_1$ and $M_2$ are Artinian. [3]
  3. 1(c) Let $R$ be a right Artinian ring and $a,b\in R$ be such that $ab=1$. Then show that $ba=1$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Define injective right $R$-modules. Prove that a right $R$-module $M$ is injective iff for every right $R$-module $N$, and submodule $N'$ of $N$, every $R$-morphism $f:N'\to M$ can be extended to an $R$-morphism $g:N\to M$. [4]
  5. 2(b) Show that external direct sum $\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Delta}M_\alpha$ of $R$-divisible modules is $R$-divisible. [3]
  6. 2(c) Prove that every $R$-module can be embedded into an injective right $R$-module. [3]
  7. 3(a) Prove that every free module is projective. Is the converse true? Justify. [3]
  8. 3(b) Prove that a right $R$-module $M$ is projective iff $M$ is isomorphic to a direct summand of a free $R$-module. [4]
  9. 3(c) Let $M$ be a projective right $R$-module. Prove that the functor $Hom(M,-):Mod_R\to Ab$ is an exact functor. [3]
  10. 4(a) Prove that $Rad\left(\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Delta}M_\alpha\right)=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Delta}Rad\,M_\alpha.$ [4]
  11. 4(b) Show that $J(R)=\{a\in R\mid 1+\mathrm{ras}\text{ is a unit for every }r,s\in R\}.$ [3]
  12. 4(c) Show that $M_n(D)$ is $J$-semisimple, where $D$ is a division ring. [3]
  13. 5(a) Prove that a ring $R$ is semiprime iff it is a subdirect product of prime rings. [4]
  14. 5(b) Prove that every ring is a subdirect product of subdirectly irreducible rings. [3]
  15. 5(c) Show that every Boolean ring is a subdirect product of copies of the field $\mathbb Z_2$. [3]
  16. 6(a) Let $R$ be a right semisimple ring. Prove that $R$ is a direct sum of finitely many minimal right ideals. Hence or otherwise show that every right semisimple ring is both right Artinian and right Noetherian. [4]
  17. 6(b) Prove that a ring $R$ is right semisimple iff every right $R$-module is semisimple. [3]
  18. 6(c) Let $M=\displaystyle\bigoplus_{i=1}^{n}M_i$ where each $M_i$ is a simple right $R$-module and $M_i\simeq L$ for some simple right $R$-module $L$. Prove that $End_R(M)\simeq M_n\left(End_R(L)\right)$. [3]
