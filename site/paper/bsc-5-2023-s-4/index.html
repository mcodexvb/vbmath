Paper

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2023
  1. 1 A firm can produce three types of woolen clothes: A, B, and C. Three kinds of wool, say R, G, and Y, are required. One unit length of type A cloth needs 3 yards of R-type wool and 2 yards of Y-type wool; one unit of B-type cloth needs 2 yards of R-type wool and 2 yards of G-type wool, while one unit length of type C cloth needs 6 yards of R-type wool and 4 yards of Y-type wool. The firm has only 18 yards of R-type, 15 yards of G-type, and 21 yards of Y-type wool. It is assumed that the profit obtained from one unit length of type A cloth is Rs.3, of type B cloth is Rs.5, and that of type C cloth is Rs.2. Formulate a LPP to determine how the firm should utilize available materials mentioned above to maximize the profit from the finished cloth. Solve it. [3+3]
  2. 2 Discuss the difference between linear and convex combinations of two vectors in $\mathbb R^n$ ($n\ge2$) with illustrations. Define convex set. Give one example of an unbounded convex set. Examine whether the set $X=\{(x_1,x_2)\mid x_1x_2\le4\}$ is a convex set. [2+1+1+2]
  3. 3 Define convex hull, separating and supporting hyperplanes of a set. Prove that the optimal solution of a LPP lies on a supporting hyperplane of the convex set of feasible solutions to the LPP. Verify this statement for the following LPP \[\begin{aligned} \text{Maximize}\quad &Z=2x_1+x_2\\ \text{subject to}\quad &2x_1+x_2\le3,\\ &3x_1+x_2\le1,\\ &x_1,x_2\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [1+3+2]
  4. 4 Rewrite the following LPP \[\begin{aligned} \text{Maximize}\quad &Z=2x_1+x_2\\ \text{subject to}\quad &2x_1+x_2\le2,\\ &4x_1+3x_2\ge6,\\ &x_1,x_2\ge0 \end{aligned}\] in standard form by incorporating additional variables. Indicate the types of the variables precisely. Solve this LPP by using (Charne's) Big-M method. [2+4]
  5. 5 Give a schematic diagram for the interchange between primal and dual problems. Prove that dual of the dual is the primal. Use the principle of duality to solve the following LPP \[\begin{aligned} \text{Maximize}\quad &Z=5x_1+12x_2+4x_3\\ \text{subject to}\quad &x_1+2x_2+x_3\le5,\\ &2x_1-x_2\ge6,\\ &x_1,x_2,x_3\ge0. \end{aligned}\] by the graphical method, if admissible. [1+3+2]
  6. 6 Use the MODI method to find the minimum cost for the transportation problem: \[\begin{array}{c|ccc|c} &D_1&D_2&D_3&a_i\\\cline{2-4} O_1&50&30&220&1\\ O_2&90&45&170&3\\ O_3&250&200&50&4\\\cline{2-4} b_j&4&2&2& \end{array}\] [6]
  7. 7 Express an assignment problem in the form of an LPP. Find the optimal assignment to find the minimum cost for one of the following two assignment problems \[ \text{(a)}\quad \begin{array}{c|ccc|} &J_1&J_2&J_3\\\cline{2-4} P_1&120&100&80\\P_2&80&90&110\\P_3&110&140&120\\\cline{2-4} \end{array} \qquad \text{(b)}\quad \begin{array}{c|cccc|} &I&II&III&IV\\\cline{2-5} A&5&3&1&8\\B&7&9&2&6\\C&6&4&5&7\\D&5&7&7&6\\\cline{2-5} \end{array} \] [3+3]
  8. 8 Explain games with pure and mixed strategies. Solve a game with the following payoff matrix \[\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c|} &A&B&C&D&E\\\cline{2-6} I&3&6&4&9&6\\\cline{2-6} II&5&6&1&7&8\\\cline{2-6} III&8&7&9&8&7\\\cline{2-6} IV&4&2&8&5&1\\\cline{2-6} \end{array}\] [3+3]
  9. 1(a) Define a statistic and sampling distribution of a statistic corresponding to the population of a random variable $X$. Distinguish between the sampling distribution of a statistic and distribution of a sample. [1+1+2]
  10. 1(b) Find the sampling distribution of the mean for the Poisson population with parameter $\mu$. [2]
  11. 1(c) Show that the sample mean $\overline X$ and the Sample variance $S^2$ are uncorrelated if the third-order central moment ($\mu_3$) vanishes. [4]
  12. 2(a) Define an estimate of a population parameter. When is an estimate said to be consistent and unbiased? Find a consistent and unbiased estimate of the parameter $\sigma^2$ of a normal $(m,\sigma)$ population. [1+2+2]
  13. 2(b) A population is defined by the density function: \[f(x;\alpha)=\frac{x^{l-1}e^{-x/\alpha}}{\Gamma(l)\alpha^l},\quad 0<x<\infty,\] where $l$ is a known constant. Estimate the parameter $\alpha$ by the method of maximum likelihood. Examine whether the estimate is consistent and unbiased. [3+2]
  14. 3(a) The marks obtained by 18 candidates in an examination have a mean 56 and variance 65. Find 95\% confidence interval for the mean of the population of marks, assuming it to be normal. Given that for 17 degrees of freedom, $P(|t|>2.11)=0.05$. [5]
  15. 3(b) Show that approximate confidence limits for the parameter $p$ of a binomial $(n,p)$ population having confidence coefficient $1-\epsilon$ are \[\frac yn\pm u_\epsilon\sqrt{\frac{y(n-y)}{n^3}},\] where $y$ is an observed value of the population random variable $X$ and $\pm u_\epsilon$ are determined by \[\int_{u_\epsilon}^\infty\phi(x)\,dx=\frac12\epsilon\] with $\phi(x)$ denoting the standard normal density function of $X$. [5]
  16. 4(a) What is meant by the critical region of testing a statistical hypothesis? Define two types of errors. Also, define the level of significance and power of the test. [2+2+2]
  17. 4(b) Let $p$ be the probability that a coin will fall head in a single toss in order to test the null hypothesis $H_0:p=1/2$ against the alternative $H_1:p=3/4$. The coin is tossed 5 times and $H_0$ is rejected if more than 3 heads are obtained. Find the probabilities of two types of error. [4]
  18. 5(a) State the Neyman-Pearson theorem for finding the best critical region in testing a statistical hypothesis. Examine whether a best critical region exists for testing the null hypothesis $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$ against the alternative $H_1:\theta=\theta_1>\theta_0$ for the parameter $\theta$ of the following distribution: \[f(x,\theta)=\frac{1+\theta}{(x+\theta)^2},\quad 1\le x<\infty.\] [4]
  19. 5(b) For a normal $(0,\sigma)$ population construct a test of the null hypothesis $H_0:\sigma=\sigma_0$ against the alternative $H_1:\sigma=\sigma_1<\sigma_0$ on the basis of a sample of size $n$. Assume that the population mean is known. [6]
