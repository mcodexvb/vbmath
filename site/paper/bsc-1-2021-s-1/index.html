GEC-1

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2021
  1. 1 State and prove Cauchy's mean value theorem. [[1+4]]
  2. 2(a) State Taylor's theorem with Lagrange's form of remainder. [[2]]
  3. 2(b) Expand with justification the function $\sin x$ in power series about $x=0$. [[3]]
  4. 3(a) State L'Hospital's rule. Using it find the value of $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-e^{\sin x}}{x-\sin x}$. [[1+2]]
  5. 3(b) Trace the curve $a^2x^2=y^3(2a-y)$, $a>0$. [[2]]
  6. 4 Find all the asymptotes of the curve $x^3-x^2y-xy^2+y^3+2x^2-4y^2+2xy+x+y+1=0$. [[5]]
  7. 5(a) Find the envelope of the family of straight lines $\frac xa+\frac yb=1$, where $a$ and $b$ are parameters connected by the relation $a^2b^3=3125$. [[3]]
  8. 5(b) Check whether $\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{x^2y}{x^4+y^2}$ exists or not. [[2]]
  9. 6(a) State and prove Euler's theorem for the functions of two variables. [[1+2]]
  10. 6(b) If $V=\cos^{-1}\frac{x+y}{\sqrt x+\sqrt y}$, then show that $x\frac{\partial V}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial V}{\partial y}+\frac12\cot V=0$. [[2]]
  11. 1(a) If $I_{m,n}=\int\cos^mx\cos nx\,dx$, show that $$I_{m,n}=\frac{\cos^mx\sin nx}{m+n}+\frac m{m+n}I_{m-1,n-1}=\frac{n\sin nx\cos x-m\cos nx\sin x}{n^2-m^2}\cos^{m-1}x-\frac{m(m-1)}{n^2-m^2}I_{m-2,n}.$$ Hence deduce that $\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^nx\cos nx\,dx=\frac\pi{2^{n+1}}$. [[3+2+1]]
  12. 1(b) Find the areas of the surface generated by the arc of $r=2a\cos\theta$ in the first quadrant revolving about the vertical and horizontal axes. [[2+2]]
  13. 2(a) Examine whether the improper integral $\int_0^\pi\frac{dx}{\sin x}$ exists. If so, find its value. [[2]]
  14. 2(b) Test the convergence of the integral $\int_0^\infty\frac{x^{2s-1}-x^{2t-1}}{1-x}dx$ and specify the ranges of values of $s$ and $t$. [[3]]
  15. 2(c) Find the length of the loop of $x=t^2$, $y=t-t^3$, where $t$ is a parameter. [[3]]
  16. 2(d) Define Beta function and show that it is symmetric. [[2]]
  17. 3(a) Prove that the Cauchy principal value of the integral $\int_{-1}^1\frac{dx}x$ is zero, however, the integral $\int_{-1}^1\frac{dx}x$ does not exist, in general. [[2]]
  18. 3(b) Prove that the entire area of the loop of the curve, given by, $(ax)^{2/3}+(by)^{2/3}=(a^2-b^2)$ is $\frac{3\pi}8\frac{(a^2-b^2)^2}{ab}$. [[4]]
  19. 3(c) Find the volume generated by revolving the area bounded by the parabola $\sqrt x+\sqrt y=\sqrt a$, $x=0$ and $y=0$ about the $x$ axis. [[4]]
  20. 1(a) State Stokes' theorem. Verify Stokes' theorem for $\mathbf A=(2x-y)\mathbf i-yz^2\mathbf j-y^2z\mathbf k$, where S is the upper half surface of the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2=1$ and C is its boundary. [[1+3]]
  21. 1(b) Prove that for any vector $\mathbf A$, $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)=0$. [[3]]
  22. 1(c) Evaluate the integral $\iint_S\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS$, where $\mathbf F=4xz\mathbf i-y^2\mathbf j+yz\mathbf k$ and S is the surface of the cube bounded by $x=0,x=1$; $y=0,y=1$; $z=0,z=1$. [[3]]
  23. 2(a) Find the total work done in moving a particle in the force field given by $\mathbf F=z(\mathbf i+\mathbf j)+x\mathbf k$ along the helix $C$ given by $x=\cos t$, $y=\sin t$, $z=t$ from $t=0$ to $t=\pi/2$. [[3]]
  24. 2(b) Suppose $\mathbf A=(3x^2+6y)\mathbf i-14yz\mathbf j+20xz^2\mathbf k$. Evaluate $\int_C\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf r$ along the straight line joining the two points $(0,0,0)$ and $(1,1,1)$. [[3]]
  25. 2(c) Suppose $\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r$ is independent of the path $C$ joining any two points. Show that there exists a function $\phi$ such that $\mathbf F=\nabla\phi$. [[4]]
  26. 3(a) Find the value of $\nabla\phi$ where $\phi=\frac1{|\mathbf r|}$ and $\mathbf r=x\mathbf i+y\mathbf j+z\mathbf k$. [[3]]
  27. 3(b) Prove that $\nabla\cdot(\phi\mathbf A)=(\nabla\phi)\cdot\mathbf A+\phi(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A)$. [[3]]
  28. 3(c) Verify Green's theorem in the plane for $\oint_C(xy+y^2)dx+x^2dy$, where $C$ is the closed curve of the region bounded by $y=x$ and $y=x^2$. [[4]]
