MMO-31(A08)

New Syllabus
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2025
  1. 1(a) Explain the terms ``Collective behaviors'' and ``Quasineutrality'' related to plasmas. [3]
  2. 1(b) Define the Debye length and Debye sphere for a conducting medium. Show that in a plasma medium with inertialess thermal electrons and stationary positive ions, the electrostatic potential drops exponentially with the distance (relative to the Debye length) from the center of the Debye sphere. [3+4]
  3. 2(a) Discuss the motion of a charged particle under constant electric ($\mathbf E$) and magnetic ($\mathbf B$) fields. Hence define the $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$-drift velocity of the charged particle. [4+2]
  4. 2(b) Show that the total energy of a charged particle under uniform electromagnetic fields remains invariant. Check whether it is true for time-dependent electromagnetic fields. [3+1]
  5. 3(a) What are meant by `phase velocity' and `group velocity' of a plane progressive wave in a conducting medium? [3]
  6. 3(b) Obtain the dispersion relation for ion-acoustic waves in an unmagnetized plasma with inertialess Boltzmann distributed electrons and inertial warm ions. Hence discuss the characteristics of these waves. [4+3]
  7. 4 Show that the dispersion relation for electrostatic ion-cyclotron waves propagating perpendicular to the constant magnetic field in a cold electron-ion quasineutral plasma can be obtained in the form: $\omega^2=\omega_{ci}^2+c_s^2k^2$, where $\omega\ (k)$ is the wave frequency (wave number) and $\omega_{ci}$ is the ion-cyclotron frequency. Also, discuss the propagation characteristics of these waves. [8+2]
  8. 5 Write short notes on (i) Lower-hybrid frequency (ii) Upper-hybrid frequency of electrostatic waves propagating in a magnetoplasma. [5+5]
  9. 6(a) Explain the concept of the ordinary mode (O-mode) in a plasma under uniform magnetic field. [5]
  10. 6(b) Find the traveling wave solution of the KdV equation: $\partial_tf+f\partial_xf+(1/2)\partial_x^3f=0$ by using the appropriate boundary conditions. [5]
