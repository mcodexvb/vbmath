NBHM · 2009 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Pick out the cases where the given subgroup $H$ is a normal subgroup of the group $G$. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $G$ is the group of all $2 \times 2$ invertible upper triangular matrices with real entries, under matrix multiplication, and $H$ is the subgroup of all such matrices $(a_{ij})$ such that $a_{11} = 1$. \item $G$ is the group of all $2 \times 2$ invertible upper triangular matrices with real entries, under matrix multiplication, and $H$ is the subgroup of all such matrices $(a_{ij})$ such that $a_{11} = a_{22}$. \item $G$ is the group of all $n \times n$ invertible matrices with real entries, under matrix multiplication, and $H$ is the subgroup of such matrices with positive determinant. \end{enumerate}
  2. 1.2 Let $GL(n, \R)$ denote the group of all invertible $n \times n$ matrices with real entries, under matrix multiplication, and let $SL(n, \R)$ denote the subgroup of such matrices whose determinant is equal to unity. Identify the quotient group $GL(n, \R)/SL(n, \R)$.
  3. 1.3 Let $S_n$ denote the symmetric group of permutations of $n$ symbols. Does $S_7$ contain an element of order $10$? If `yes', write down an example of such an element.
  4. 1.4 What is the largest possible order of an element in $S_7$?
  5. 1.5 Write down all the units in the ring $\Z_8$ of all integers modulo $8$.
  6. 1.6 Pick out the cases where the given ideal is a maximal ideal. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The ideal $15\Z$ in $\Z$. \item The ideal $\mathcal{I} = \{f : f(0) = 0\}$ in the ring $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ of all continuous real valued functions on the interval $[0, 1]$. \item The ideal generated by $x^3 + x + 1$ in the ring of polynomials $\F_3[x]$, where $\F_3$ is the field of three elements. \end{enumerate}
  7. 1.7 Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix with complex entries which is non-zero and non-diagonal. Pick out the cases when $A$ is diagonalizable. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $A^2 = I$. \item $A^2 = 0$. \item All eigenvalues of $A$ are equal to $2$. \end{enumerate}
  8. 1.8 Let $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y} \in \R^n$ be two non-zero (column) vectors. Let $\mathbf{y}^T$ denote the transpose of $\mathbf{y}$. Let $A = \mathbf{x}\mathbf{y}^T$, \emph{i.e.}\ $A = (a_{ij})$ where $a_{ij} = x_i y_j$. What is the rank of $A$?
  9. 1.9 Let $\mathbf{x}$ be a non-zero (column) vector in $\R^n$. What is the necessary and sufficient condition for the matrix $A = I - 2\mathbf{x}\mathbf{x}^T$ to be orthogonal?
  10. 1.10 Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $A$ is always similar to an upper-triangular matrix. \item $A$ is always similar to a diagonal matrix. \item $A$ is similar to a block diagonal matrix, with each diagonal block of size strictly less than $n$, provided $A$ has at least $2$ distinct eigenvalues. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 Evaluate: \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} n\sin(2\pi e n!). \]
  12. 2.2 Evaluate: \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\sqrt{4n^2 - k^2}}. \]
  13. 2.3 Pick out the convergent series: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(n+1)^n}{n^{n + \frac{3}{2}}}$. \item $\displaystyle 1 + \frac{1}{2^2} + \frac{2^2}{3^3} + \frac{3^3}{4^4} + \cdots$ \item $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sqrt{\frac{1 + 4^n}{1 + 5^n}}$. \end{enumerate}
  14. 2.4 Which of the following functions are continuous? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $\displaystyle f(x) = x^2 + \frac{x^2}{1+x^2} + \frac{x^2}{(1+x^2)^2} + \cdots,\quad x \in \R$. \item $\displaystyle f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{\cos nx}{n^{\frac{3}{2}}},\quad x \in [-\pi, \pi]$. \item $\displaystyle f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^2 x^n,\quad x \in \left[-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right]$. \end{enumerate}
  15. 2.5 Which of the following functions are uniformly continuous? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ in $(0, 1)$. \item $f(x) = x^2$ in $\R$. \item $f(x) = \sin^2 x$ in $\R$. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Pick out the sequences $\{f_n\}$ which are uniformly convergent. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f_n(x) = nxe^{-nx}$ on $(0, \infty)$. \item $f_n(x) = x^n$ on $[0, 1]$. \item $f_n(x) = \dfrac{\sin nx}{\sqrt{n}}$ on $\R$. \end{enumerate}
  17. 2.7 Which of the following functions are Riemann integrable on the interval $[0, 1]$? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f(x) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \cos^{2n}(24\pi x)$. \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} 0, & \text{if } x \text{ is rational} \\ 1, & \text{if } x \text{ is irrational.} \end{cases}$ \item $f(x) = \begin{cases} \cos x, & \text{if } 0 \le x \le \frac{1}{2} \\ \sin x, & \text{if } \frac{1}{2} < x \le 1. \end{cases}$ \end{enumerate}
  18. 2.8 Let $z = x + iy$ be a complex number, where $x$ and $y \in \R$, and let $f(z) = u(x, y) + iv(x, y)$, where $u$ and $v$ are real valued, be an analytic function on $\C$. Express $f'(z)$ in terms of the partial derivatives of $u$ and $v$.
  19. 2.9 Let $z \in \C$ be as in the previous question. Find the image of the set $S = \{z : x > 0,\ 0 < y < 2\}$ under the transformation $f(z) = iz + 1$.
  20. 2.10 Find the residue at $z = 0$ for the function \[ f(z) = \frac{1 + 2z}{z^2 + z^3}. \]
  21. 3.1 Let $X$ be a metric space and let $f : X \to \R$ be a continuous function. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f$ always maps Cauchy sequences into Cauchy sequences. \item If $X$ is compact, then $f$ always maps Cauchy sequences into Cauchy sequences. \item If $X = \R^n$, then $f$ always maps Cauchy sequences into Cauchy sequences. \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Let $B$ be the closed ball in $\R^2$ with centre at the origin and radius unity. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item There exists a continuous function $f : B \to \R$ which is one-one. \item There exists a continuous function $f : B \to \R$ which is onto. \item There exists a continuous function $f : B \to \R$ which is one-one and onto. \end{enumerate}
  23. 3.3 Let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of $\R$. Define $C = \{a + b : a \in A,\ b \in B\}$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $C$ is closed if $A$ and $B$ are closed. \item $C$ is closed if $A$ is closed and $B$ is compact. \item $C$ is compact if $A$ is closed and $B$ is compact. \end{enumerate}
  24. 3.4 Which of the following subsets of $\R^2$ are compact? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $\{(x, y) : xy = 1\}$ \item $\{(x, y) : x^{\frac{2}{3}} + y^{\frac{2}{3}} = 1\}$ \item $\{(x, y) : x^2 + y^2 < 1\}$ \end{enumerate}
  25. 3.5 Which of the following sets in $\R^2$ are connected? \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $\{(x, y) : x^2 y^2 = 1\}$ \item $\{(x, y) : x^2 + y^2 = 1\}$ \item $\{(x, y) : 1 < x^2 + y^2 < 2\}$ \end{enumerate}
  26. 3.6 Let $\mathcal{P}$ denote the set of all polynomials in the real variable $x$ which varies over the interval $[0, 1]$. What is the closure of $\mathcal{P}$ in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$ (with its usual sup-norm topology)?
  27. 3.7 Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of functions which are continuous over $[0, 1]$ and continuously differentiable in $]0, 1[$. Assume that $|f_n(x)| \le 1$ and that $|f_n'(x)| \le 1$ for all $x \in ]0, 1[$ and for each positive integer $n$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item $f_n$ is uniformly continuous for each $n$. \item $\{f_n\}$ is a convergent sequence in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$. \item $\{f_n\}$ contains a subsequence which converges in $\mathcal{C}[0, 1]$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item Let $f : [0, 2] \to [0, 1]$ be a continuous function. Then, there always exists $x \in [0, 1]$ such that $f(x) = x$. \item Let $f : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ be a continuous function which is continuously differentiable in $]0, 1[$ and such that $|f'(x)| \le \frac{1}{2}$ for all $x \in ]0, 1[$. Then, there exists a unique $x \in [0, 1]$ such that $f(x) = x$. \item Let $S = \{\mathbf{p} = (x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 = 1\}$. Let $f : S \to S$ be a continuous function. Then, there always exists $\mathbf{p} \in S$ such that $f(\mathbf{p}) = \mathbf{p}$. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space. Let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of $X$. Define $d(A, B) = \inf\{d(a, b) : a \in A,\ b \in B\}$. For $x \in X$, define $d(x, A) = \inf\{d(x, a) : a \in A\}$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The function $x \mapsto d(x, A)$ is uniformly continuous. \item $d(x, A) = 0$ if, and only if, $x \in A$. \item $d(A, B) = 0$ implies that $A \cap B \neq \emptyset$. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 Let $B = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 \le 1\}$ and $D = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 < 1\}$. Pick out the true statements. \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item Given a continuous function $g : B \to \R$, there always exists a continuous function $f : \R^2 \to \R$ such that $f = g$ on $B$. \item Given a continuous function $g : D \to \R$, there always exists a continuous function $f : \R^2 \to \R$ such that $f = g$ on $D$. \item There exists a continuous function $f : \R^2 \to \R$ such that $f \equiv 1$ on the set $\{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 = \frac{3}{2}\}$ and $f \equiv 0$ on the set $B \cup \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 \ge 2\}$. \end{enumerate}
  31. 4.1 A spherical ball of volatile material evaporates (\emph{i.e.}\ its volume decreases) at a rate proportional to its surface area. If the initial radius is $r_0$ and at time $t = 1$, the radius is $r_0/2$, find the time at which the ball disappears completely.
  32. 4.2 A body of mass $m$ falling from rest under gravity experiences air resistance proportional to the square of its velocity. Write down the initial value problem for the vertical displacement $x$ of the body.
  33. 4.3 A body falling from rest under gravity travels a distance $y$ and has a velocity $v$ at time $t$. Write down the relationship between $v$ and $y$.
  34. 4.4 Let $\mathbf{x} = (x_1, \dots, x_n) \in \R^n$ and let $\mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}$. Apply Gauss' divergence theorem to $\mathbf{v}$ over the unit ball \[ B = \left\{\mathbf{x} \in \R^n : \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 \le 1\right\} \] and deduce the relationship between $\omega_n$, the ($n$-dimensional) volume of $B$, and $\sigma_n$, the ($(n-1)$-dimensional) surface measure of $B$.
  35. 4.5 Write down the general solution of the linear system: \begin{align*} \frac{dx}{dt} &= x + y \\ \frac{dy}{dt} &= 4x - 2y. \end{align*}
  36. 4.6 What is the smallest positive value of $\lambda$ such that the problem: \begin{align*} u'' + \lambda u &= 0 \quad \text{in } ]0, 1[ \\ u(0) = u(1) &\quad \text{and} \quad u'(0) = u'(1) \end{align*} has a solution such that $u \not\equiv 0$?
  37. 4.7 Let $f : \R \to \R$ be a continuous function. Write down the solution of the problem: \begin{align*} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} - \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} &= 0, \quad t > 0, \\ u(x, 0) &= f(x), \quad x \in \R, \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x, 0) &= 0, \quad x \in \R. \end{align*}
  38. 4.8 Use duality to find the optimal value of the cost function in the following linear programming problem: \[ \max\ x + y + z \quad \text{such that } 3x + 2y + 2z = 1,\quad x \ge 0,\ y \ge 0,\ z \ge 0. \]
  39. 4.9 The value of $\sqrt{10}$ is computed by solving the equation $x^2 = 10$ using the Newton-Raphson method. Starting from some value $x_0 > 0$, write down the iteration scheme.
  40. 4.10 Write down the Laplace transform $F(s)$ of the function $f(x) = x^3$.
  41. 5.1 Let $P = (0, 1)$ and $Q = (4, 1)$ be points on the plane. Let $A$ be a point which moves on the $x$-axis between the points $(0, 0)$ and $(4, 0)$. Let $B$ be a point which moves on the line $y = 2$ between the points $(0, 2)$ and $(4, 2)$. Consider all possible paths consisting of the line segments $PA$, $AB$ and $BQ$. What is the shortest possible length of such a path?
  42. 5.2 A convex polygon has its interior angles in arithmetic progression, the least angle being $120^\circ$ and the common difference being $5^\circ$. Find the number of sides of the polygon.
  43. 5.3 Let $a, b$ and $c$ be the lengths of the sides of an arbitrary triangle. Define \[ x = \frac{ab + bc + ca}{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}. \] Pick out the true statements.\\ (a) $\frac{1}{2} \le x \le 2$. \qquad (b) $\frac{1}{2} \le x \le 1$. \qquad (c) $\frac{1}{2} < x \le 1$.
  44. 5.4 What is the maximum number of pieces that can be obtained from a pizza by making $7$ cuts with a knife?
  45. 5.5 In arithmetic base $3$, a number is expressed as $210100$. Find its square root and express it in base $3$.
  46. 5.6 Evaluate: \[ \left(\frac{-1 + i\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2} + i\sqrt{2}}\right)^{20}. \]
  47. 5.7 Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer and let $C_r$ stand for the usual binomial coefficients \emph{i.e.}, the number of ways of choosing $r$ objects from $n$ objects. Evaluate: \[ C_1 + 2C_2 + \dots + nC_n. \]
  48. 5.8 Let $x, y$ and $z$ be real numbers such that $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$. Find the maximum and minimum values of $2x + 3y + z$.
  49. 5.9 Let $x_0 = 0$. For $n \ge 0$, define $x_{n+1} = x_n^2 + \frac{1}{4}$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[(a)] \item The sequence $\{x_n\}$ is bounded. \item The sequence $\{x_n\}$ is monotonic. \item The sequence $\{x_n\}$ is convergent. \end{enumerate}
  50. 5.10 Seven tickets are numbered consecutively from $1$ to $7$. Two of them are selected in order without replacement. Let $A$ denote the event that the numbers on the two tickets add up to $9$. Let $B$ be the event that the numbers on the two tickets differ by $3$. If each draw has equal probability $\frac{1}{42}$ (the draw $(1, 7)$ being considered as distinct from the draw $(7, 1)$, for example) find the probability $P(B \mid A)$.
