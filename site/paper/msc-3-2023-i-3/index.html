Internal test

Special Functions
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2023
  1. 1 Prove that $1+\frac12P_1(\cos\theta)+\frac13P_2(\cos\theta)+\cdots=\log[(1+\sin\frac\theta2)/\sin\frac\theta2]$. [[2]]
  2. 2 Prove that (i) $\frac{d}{dx}\{x^nJ_n(x)\}=x^nJ_{n-1}(x)$, (ii) $\frac{d}{dx}\{x^{-n}J_n(x)\}=-x^{-n}J_{n+1}(x)$. [[2]]
  3. 3 Prove that $\int_{-1}^1[P_n(x)]^2\,dx=\frac{2}{2n+1}$ \textit{[trailing print obscured by strike-through]}. [[2]]
  4. 4 Prove that (i) $J_{-1/2}(x)=\sqrt{\left(\frac{2}{\pi x}\right)}\cos(x)$, (ii) $J_{1/2}(x)=\sqrt{\left(\frac{2}{\pi x}\right)}\sin(x)$, (iii) $[J_{1/2}(x)]^2+[J_{-1/2}(x)]^2=\frac{2}{\pi x}$. [[2]]
  5. 5 Show that $nP_n=(2n-1)xP_{n-1}-(n-1)P_{n-2}$, $n\ge2$. [[2]]
  6. 1 What do you mean by continuous linear functional? Explain with a suitable example. [[3]]
  7. 2 Determine the order of proximity of the curves $y(x)=\frac{1}{n^2}\sin nx$, $y_1(x)=0$ on $[0,\pi]$. [[3]]
  8. 3 Show that the extremal of the functional $J[y(x)]=\int_1^2\frac{\sqrt{1+y'^2}}{x}\,dx$, satisfying $y(1)=0$ and $y(2)=1$ is a circle having its centre at $(0,2)$ and radius $\sqrt5$. [[6]]
  9. 4 Show that the Euler-Ostrogradsky equation for the functional \[S[z(x,y)]=\iint_\Omega\left\{\sqrt{1+z_x^2+z_y^2}\right\}dx\,dy,\] where the values of $z$ are prescribed on the boundary $\partial\Omega$ of the domain $\Omega$, is given by \[z_{xx}(1+z_y^2)-2z_xz_yz_{xy}+z_{yy}(1+z_x^2)=0.\] [[6]]
  10. 1(a) Find the extremal of the functional $I[y]=\int_1^e(xy'^2+yy')\,dx$ satisfying $y(1)=0$, $y(e)=1$. [[3]]
  11. 1(b) Find the stationary function of the functional $I[y]=\int_a^b(y'^2+yy'')\,dx$, subject to the conditions $y(a)=A$, $y'(a)=A_1$, $y(b)=B$, $y'(b)=B_1$. [[3]]
  12. 2 Find the solid of maximum volume formed by the revolution of a given surface. [[6]]
  13. 3 Find the extremal of the functional $J[y(x),z(x)]=\int_{x_1}^{x_2}(y'z'+2y^2+2z^2)\,dx$ with $y(0)=z(0)=0$ and the point $(x_2,y_2,z_2)$ moves over the fixed plane $x=x_2$. [[6]]
  14. 4 Define central field with a suitable example. Find the central field of extremals for the functional \[I[y]=\int_0^1(y^2-y'^2)\,dx.\] [[2+4]]
