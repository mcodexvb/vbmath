BMC-13

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2016
  1. 1 Derive at least two invariants corresponding to the transformation $ax+by\to a'x'+b'y'$ under the rotation of axes in the xy-plane.\\Find the transformed equation of the curve represented by $(4x+3y+7)(3x-4y+2)=10$ when the coordinate axes are transformed into $4x+3y+4=0$ and $3x-4y+3=0$ respectively.
  2. 2 Reduce the general equation of second degree $9x^2-6xy+y^2-14x-2y+12=0$ to its canonical form and determine its nature. Give the coordinates of its focus, if it is a conic. [4]
  3. 3 Derive the condition that the straight line $lx+my+n=0$ may be a tangent to the conic represented by a general equation of second degree. [4]
  4. 4 Establish condition to be satisfied by the coefficients involved in the general equation of second degree so that it represents a pair of parallel straight lines. Derive the expression for distance between them in terms of coefficients involved in the equation. [3+1]
  5. 5 Find the area of the triangle formed by the straight lines $ax^2+2hxy+by^2=0$ and $lx+my+n=0$. [4]
  6. 6 Find the polar equation of the straight line passing through the points $(r_1,\theta_1)$ and $(r_2,\theta_2)$. Hence derive the condition of collinearity of the points $(r_1,\theta_1),(r_2,\theta_2),(r_3,\theta_3)$.
  7. 7(i) Find the projection of the curve $x^2+y^2+z^2=4$, $(x-2)^2+y^2+(z-2)^2=4$ on yz-plan.\\
  8. 7(ii) Obtain an angle between the lines whose direction cosines satisfy relations $l+m+n=0$, $l^2+m^2-n^2=0$.
  9. 8(i) Show that the plane $3x-4y+z+5=0$ cuts the line segment joining $(2,-3,1)$ and $(-3,1,2)$ but does not cut the line segment joining $(3,-4,5)$ and $(3,1,0)$.\\
  10. 8(ii) Verify whether the planes $4x+3y+z+2=0$, $2x-y+3z-5=0$ and $3x+y+2z-1=0$ intersect pairwise in three distinct parallel lines or not. Find those lines, if yes.
  11. 9(i) Find the image of the straight line $5x-3y+4=0$, $2x-3y+10=0$ on the plane $2x-y+z+3=0$.\\
  12. 9(ii) Show that by a suitable choice of axes, the equation of two skewlines can be put in the form $y=\pm mx$, $z=\pm c$,, where $2c$ is the shortest distance between the lines.
  13. 10(i) A sphere of constant radius $r$ passes through the origin and cuts the three axes in A, B, C respectively. Prove that the locus of the foot of the perpendicular from O to the plane ABC is given by $(x^2+y^2+z^2)^2\left(\frac1{x^2}+\frac1{y^2}+\frac1{z^2}\right)=4r^2$.\\
  14. 10(ii) Find the equation of the cone whose vertex is $(1,2,3)$ and the guiding curve is the ellipse $\frac{x^2}9+\frac{y^2}4=1$, $z=0$.
  15. 11(i) Obtain values of $k$ for which the plane $5x+2z+k=0$ may be a tangent plane to the hyperboloid of two sheets $\frac{x^2}3+\frac{y^2}4-\frac{z^2}{25}=-1$.\\
  16. 11(ii) Reduce the equation $11x^2+10y^2+6z^2-8yz+4zx-12xy+72x-72y+36z+150=0$ to its canonical form and determine the type of the quadric represented by it. Find the center and the equation of axes, if it is a conicoid.
