MMO-31(P10)

Rings and Modules-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Let $R$ be a commutative ring with 1. If $R$ is simple, then show that $R$ is a field. Also prove that $M_n(R)$ is simple for every simple ring $R$. [4]
  2. 1(b) Let $I$ be an ideal of a ring $R$. Show that $\dfrac{M_n(R)}{M_n(I)}\simeq M_n\!\left(\dfrac RI\right)$. [3]
  3. 1(c) If $N$ is a submodule of an $R$-module $M$ and $A$ is a right ideal of $R$, then show that $\left(\dfrac MN\right)A=\dfrac{(N+MA)}N$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Define direct product of a family of $R$-modules. Prove that any two direct products of a family $\{M_\alpha\}_{\triangleleft}$ of $R$-modules are isomorphic. [3]
  5. 2(b) Let $\{M_\alpha\}_{\triangleleft}$ be a family of $R$-modules and $N$ be an $R$-module. Show that\par \[\mathrm{Hom}_R\!\left(\oplus^e_{\triangleleft}M_\alpha,N\right)=\prod_{\triangleleft}\mathrm{Hom}_R(M_\alpha,N)\text{ as abelian groups.}\] [4]
  6. 2(c) Give an example with justifications of an $R$-module $M$ such that $M=M\oplus M$. [3]
  7. 3(a) Give an example of an $R$-module $M$ which has bases of different cardinalities. [4]
  8. 3(b) Prove that every $R$-module $M$ is isomorphic to a quotient of the free $R$-module $F_X$ for some $X$. [2]
  9. 3(c) Prove that every commutative ring has the IBN property. [4]
  10. 4(a) Let $M$ be a right $R$-module. Show that $M\otimes_RR\simeq M$. [3]
  11. 4(b) Let $f_1:M_1\to M_2$, $f_2:M_2\to M_3$, $g_1:N_1\to N_2$ and $g_2:N_2\to N_3$ be four $R$-linear maps. Prove that $(f_2\otimes g_2)\circ(f_1\otimes g_1)=(f_2\circ f_1)\otimes(g_2\circ g_1)$ [2]
  12. 4(c) Show that $\mathbb Q\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q\simeq\mathbb Q$. [2]
  13. 4(d) Let $d$ be the gcd of $m$ and $n$. Show that $\mathbb Z_m\otimes\mathbb Z_n\simeq\mathbb Z_d$. [3]
  14. 5(a) Define subcategory and additive category. [2]
  15. 5(b) Show that in a category every section is injection and every injection is monic. Give an example of a monic morphism which is not an injection. [4]
  16. 5(c) Prove that every monic morphism is an injection in $Mod_R$ category. [2]
  17. 5(d) Let $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ be a covariant functor and $T:\mathcal D\to E$ be a contravariant functor. Determine whether $TF$ is covariant or contravariant. \par\hfill P.T.O. [2]
  18. 6(a) Let $O\to M_1\xrightarrow{f}M\xrightarrow{g}M_2\to0$ be a shirt exact sequence. If $M=M_1\oplus M_2$ then show that the short exact sequence splits. [3]
  19. 6(b) If $O\to M_1\xrightarrow{f}M\xrightarrow{g}M_2\to0$ is a split short exact sequence and $f'$ and $g'$ are splitting maps for $f$ and $g$, respective, show that $O\to M_2\xrightarrow{g'}M\xrightarrow{f'}M_1\to0$ is a split short exact sequence. [3]
  20. 6(c) Show that the functors $\mathrm{Hom}_R(X,-):Mod_R\to Ab$ is left exact. [4]
