MMC-15

Partial Differential Equations
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2025
  1. 1(a) Find the integral surface of the following Cauchy problem:\par \[(x^2+y^2+yu)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+(x^2+y^2-xu)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=(x+y)u,\] \[u(x,x)=\sqrt x.\] What would you say about the nature of the integral surface?
  2. 1(b) Distinguish between general and complete integrals of a first-order partial differential equation (PDE) of the form $f(x,y,u,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y})=0,\ u=u(x,y)$. Find the singular integral of the first-order non-linear PDE $u=x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}-2\sqrt{\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}}$, if any.
  3. 1(c) Define Monge cone of a first order nonlinear PDE $f(x,y,u,\frac{\partial u}{\partial x},\frac{\partial u}{\partial y})=0$ at any point $(x_0,y_0,u_0)$. Find the expression of the Monge cone of the following PDE at $(0,0,1)$:\par \[\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=1.\]
  4. 2(a) Determine the characteristics of the PDE\par \[u=\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2-\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2\] and find the integral surface which passes through the parabola $4u+x^2=0,\ y=0$.
  5. 2(b) Show that a linear PDE of the type $\sum_{r,s}C_{rs}x^ry^s\frac{\partial^{r+s}u}{\partial x^r\partial y^s}=f(x,y)$ may be reduced to one with constant coefficients by the substitutions $\xi=\log_ex,\ \eta=\log_ey$. Hence, find the complete integral of the PDE\par \[2x\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-y\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}+2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+6xy=0.\]
  6. 3(a) Reduce the PDE to canonical form\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=(1+y)^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}.\]
  7. 3(b) Solve the following PDE by Monge's method:\par \[\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}-2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}+\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)^2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)^2.\] [5+5]
  8. 4(a) Using Laplace integral transform method, solve the initial boundary value problem (IBVP) described as\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}+\sin t+\cos t,\ 0\le x<\infty,0\le t<\infty,\] BC's: $u(0,t)=0$, $u(x,t)$ is bounded as $x\to\infty$,\par IC's: $u(x,0)=0,\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\Big|_{(x,0)}=0$.
  9. 4(b) Using Fourier integral transform method, solve the following two-dimensional (2D) Laplace equation:\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=0,\ 0\le x\le\pi,\ 0\le y\le\pi,\] BC's: $u(x,0)=0,\ u(x,\pi)=u_0,\ 0<x<\pi$,\par $u(0,y)=0=u(\pi,y),\ 0<y<\pi$ and $u(x,y)$ is bounded. [5+5]
  10. 5(a) Evaluate $u(\frac14,\frac1{2\pi^2})$, where $u(x,t)$ satisfies the IBVP\par \[\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=9\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2},\ 0\le x\le1,\ t\ge0,\] BC's: $u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0,\ t>0$,\par IC: $u(x,0)=\sin^2(\pi x),\ 0<x<1$.
  11. 5(b) By using the method of separation of variables, find the value of $u(25,26)$ of the following PDE:\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x\partial y}+\left(\frac{5y}x\right)^2u=0,\] \[u(x,0)=e^{\frac{25}x}.\]
  12. 5(c) Let $u(x,t),\ 0\le x\le2025,\ t\ge0$, be the solution of the IBVP\par \[\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial t^2}=\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2},\] BC's: $u(0,t)=0,\ u(2025,t)=0,\ t>0$,\par IC's: $u(x,0)=x,\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,0)=1,\ 0<x<2025$.\par Determine the value of $u(1,2025)$. [3+4+3]
  13. 6(a) Define Neumann boundary condition for the Laplace equation in some function domain $\mathbb R$ and on some boundary of the domain $\partial\mathbb R$. Prove that, if the Neumann problem has a solution $u(x,y)$ which is continuous in a bounded region $\bar{\mathbb R}\ (=\mathbb R\cup\partial\mathbb R)$ and satisfies the Laplace equation $\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=0$ in the interior of $\mathbb R$, then it may or may not be unique.
  14. 6(b) Solve the following interior Dirichlet problem for a circle:\par PDE: $\nabla^2u=0,\ 0\le r<a,\ 0\le\theta<2\pi$\par BC: $u(a,\theta)=f(\theta),\ 0\le\theta<2\pi$, where $f(\theta)$ is a continuous function on $\partial\mathbb R$.\par Hence deduce Poisson's integral formula of the form\par \[u(r,\theta)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\frac{(a^2-r^2)f(\phi)}{a^2-2ar\cos(\phi-\theta)+r^2}d\phi.\]
