MMC-21

Functional Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2026
  1. 1(a) Prove that every compact subset of a metric space is totally bounded. [4]
  2. 1(b) Define Lebesgue number. Prove that in a sequentially compact metric space every open cover has a Lebesgue number. [4]
  3. 1(c) If a discrete metric space $(X,d)$ is compact then show that $X$ contains finitely many points. [2]
  4. 2(a) When two norms in a linear space are said to be equivalent ? In $\mathbb R^2$, give example (with justification) of two equivalent norms. [3]
  5. 2(b) Show that $l_2$ is a Banach space w.r.t. a norm to be defined by you. [4]
  6. 2(c) Prove that every linear operator defined on a normed linear space is continuous iff it is bounded. [3]
  7. 3(a) Prove that a subset $M$ in a finite dimensional normed linear space is compact iff $M$ is closed and bounded. [4]
  8. 3(b) If $T$ is a bounded linear operator defined over a normed linear space $X$ to another normed linear space $Y$, then show that $\|T\|=Sup\{\|Tx\|:x\in X,\|x\|\le1\}$. [3]
  9. 3(c) Consider the normed linear space $C[a,b]$ w.r.t. supnorm. Let $t_0\in[a,b]$ be a fixed element and define a function $f$ on $C[a,b]$ by $f(x)=x(t_0)\ \forall x\in C[a,b]$. Prove that $f$ is a bounded linear functional and find $\|f\|$. [3]
  10. 4(a) State and prove Uniform Boundedness Principle Theorem. [6]
  11. 4(b) Show that norm $(\|\ \|)$ is a continuous function. [2]
  12. 4(c) Prove that every open ball in a normed linear space is a convex set. [2]
  13. 5(a) State and prove Cauchy-Schwartz's Inequality and hence show that every inner product space is a normed linear space. [5]
  14. 5(b) Prove that $\mathbb C^n$ is a Hilbert space w.r.t. an inner product to be defined by you. [3]
  15. 5(c) Prove that in an inner product space $X$, any two vectors $x,y$ are orthogonal iff $\|x+\alpha y\|=\|x-\alpha y\|$ for any scalar $\alpha$ where $\|\ \|$ is the norm induced by the inner product. [2]
  16. 6(a) Prove that a Banach space $X$ is a Hilbert space iff Parallelogram law holds in $X$. [5]
  17. 6(b) Define weak convergence and strong convergence of a sequence of elements in a normed linear space. Prove that strong convergence implies weak convergence. Does the converse hold ? Support your answer. [5]
