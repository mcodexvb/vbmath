MMO-31(A5)

Dynamics of Ecological System-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Sketch the phase diagram and obtain the dynamic solutions of the following autonomous system: \par \[\frac{du}{dt}=4u-5v,\qquad\frac{dv}{dt}=5u-4v.\] [4]
  2. 1(b) In \textit{2D} autonomous system, it is well known that there can be no limit cycles in models of competitive or co-operative populations -- Validate the statement via mathematical analysis.
  3. 2(a) The generalized Verhulst population model, with crowding effect of the form $\left(-\beta N^{\alpha}\right)$ is given by\par \[\frac{dN}{dt}=rN-\beta N^{\alpha}\ (\alpha>1).\] Obtain the steady state solution and check for stability.\par Also, solve the model for $N$ and predict the behaviour of the population for a long period of time.\par Find the point of inflexion, if any.
  4. 2(b) Consider the following predator-prey system with Holling-Tanner type II functional response\par \[\frac{du}{dt}=ru\left(1-\frac uK\right)-\frac{muv}{a+u}\unreadable{small stray mark after the denominator},\] \[\frac{dv}{dt}=\left(\frac{emu}{a+u}-d\right)v,\] \hspace*{2em}$u(0)>0,\ v(0)>0,\ 0<e<1.$\par Find the parametric restrictions for which the co-existence equilibrium point $(u^*,v^*)$ is globally asymptotically stable with the use of the following Lyapunov function\par \[L(u,v)=\int_{v^*}^{v}s^{\theta-1}(s-v^*)\,ds+cv^{\theta}\int_{u^*}^{u}\frac{\xi-u^*}{\xi}\,d\xi,\ \text{ for some }c>0,\ \theta>0.\] \par\hfill P.T.O. [5]
  5. 3 Consider the following ratio-dependent Bazykin's interacting species model:\par \[\frac{du}{dt}=au-\frac{buv}{v+Au}-eu^2,\] \[\frac{dv}{dt}=-cv+\frac{duv}{v+Au}-hv^2,\] \hspace*{2em}$u(0)=u_0>0,\ v(0)=v_0>0.$\par Write down your observations around the co-existence equilibrium point on the subsequent issues.\par (i) Bonndedness, (ii) Global stability through Dulac's criteria,\par (iii) Hopf-bifurcation, (iv) Saddle-node bifurcation.
  6. 4(a) Note down the influence of the refuge parameter `m' and harvesting efforts $E_1$ and $E_2$ about the co-existence fixed point of the following interacting species system:\par \[\frac{du}{dt}=\alpha u\left(1-\frac uK\right)-\frac{\beta(u-m)v}{1+a(u-m)}-q_1E_1u,\] \[\frac{dv}{dt}=-dv+\frac{c\beta(u-m)v}{1+a(u-m)}-q_2E_2v,\] \hspace*{2em}$u(0)>0,\ v(0)>0,\ 0<c<1.$
  7. 4(b) Comment on `optimal harvesting policy' via `Pontryagin's maximal principle' around unique positive equilibrium point of the following host-parasite model system:\par \[\frac{dH}{dt}=(r_1-b_1H)H-a_1HP-c_1H,\] \[\frac{dP}{dt}=\left(r_2-\frac{a_2P}H\right)P-c_2P,\] \hspace*{2em}$H(0)>0,\ P(0)>0,\ 0\le c_i<r_i,\ i=1,2.$ [5]
  8. 5(a) Develop and analyze the SIR model for general epidemic and consequently comment on Kermack and McKendrick threshold theorem. [6]
  9. 5(b) Consider the two compartment SIS model and assume that all infected individuals recover and immediately re-enter the susceptible class. Find the equilibria of the model and establish their stability in the context of $R_0$. [4]
  10. 6 An eco-epidemiological interacting species model where predators distinguish between susceptible and infected prey is given by\par \[\frac{dS}{dt}=rS\left(1-\frac{S+I}K\right)-\lambda SI-\frac{pYS}{mY+S},\] \[\frac{dI}{dt}=\lambda SI-\frac{cYI}{mY+I}-\gamma I,\] \[\frac{dY}{dt}=\delta Y\left(1-\frac{hY}{I+S}\right),\] \hspace*{2em}$S(0)\ge0,\ I(0)\ge0,\ Y(0)\ge0.$\par
  11. 6(a) Determine and classify all the ecologically meaningful equilibria.\par
  12. 6(b) Can you interpret the impact of disease on prey in the absence of predators and disease effects on predators in the absence of healthy prey?
