MMO-31(P2)

Advanced Topology-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2017
  1. 1(a) Prove that a topological space $X$ is Hausdorff iff limit of a convergent net in $X$ is unique. [4]
  2. 1(b) Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and $f:X\to Y$ be a function. Prove that $f$ is continuous at $x_0$ iff whenever a net $S$ converges to $x_0$, $f\circ S$ converges to $f(x_0)$. [4]
  3. 1(c) Prove that a filter $\Im$ in $X$ is an ultrafilter iff for any $A,B\subset X,\ A\cup B\in\Im\Rightarrow A\in\Im$ or $B\in\Im$. [4]
  4. 2(a) Prove that in a locally compact Hausdorff space compact neighbourhoods form a local base. [3]
  5. 2(b) Prove that quotient space of a locally connected space is locally connected. [4]
  6. 2(c) Prove that in a space $(X,\tau)$ every sequence has a cluster point iff every infinite set has an $w$-limit point. Hence show that every sequentially compact space is $(B-W)$ compact. [4+1]
  7. 3(a) Prove that product of a family of topological spaces is compact iff each component space is compact. [1+4]
  8. 3(b) State and prove Embedding Lemma. [1+6]
  9. 4(a) Let $f:X\to[0,1]$ be a continuous function and for $t\in R$, $F_t=\{x\in X;f(x)<t\}$. Prove that $\{F_t;t\in\mathbb R\}$ is a family of open sets in $X$ such that $\bar F_s\subset F_t$ if $s<t$ and $f(x)=\inf\{t\in Q;x\in F_t\}$. [4]
  10. 4(b) Prove that a topological space $X$ is normal iff for every closed set $A$ in $X$, every continuous function $f:A\to[-1,1]$ has a continuous extension $F:X\to[-1,1]$. [8]
  11. 5(a) Describe one-point compactification of a topological space. [5]
  12. 5(b) Prove that one-point compactification of a space is Hausdorff iff the space is locally compact and Hausdorff. Prove that every locally compact Hausdorff space is a Tychonoff space. [5+2]
  13. 6 Answer \textit{any two} \hfill\par \hspace*{1em}a) Give an example of a $T_1$ space $X$ and a net in $X$ converging to two distinct points. \par \hspace*{1em}b) Prove that a connected $T_4$ space with at least two points must be uncountable. \par \hspace*{1em}c) In a discrete space show that the neighbourhood filters are ultrafilters. [2]
