CC-2

B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2017
  1. 1(a) Prove by the principle of mathematical induction that for every positive integer $n$, $(\cos\theta_1+i\sin\theta_1)(\cos\theta_2+i\sin\theta_2)\ldots(\cos\theta_n+i\sin\theta_n)=\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2+\ldots+\theta_n)+i\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2+\ldots+\theta_n)$ Hence deduce De Moivre's theorem for positive integers. [4]
  2. 1(b) If $(1+x)^n=a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2+\ldots$, then show that $a_0-a_2+a_4-\ldots=2^{n/2}\cos\frac{n\pi}4$. [3]
  3. 1(c) Show that the mapping $f:\mathbb R\to(-1,1)$ defined by $f(x)=\frac x{1+|x|}$ is a bijective mapping. Also find $f^{-1}$. [3]
  4. 2(a) Prove that for any positive integer $n$, $3n^2-1$ is never a perfect square. [3]
  5. 2(b) Show that the product of four consecutive integers is $1$ less than a perfect square. [2]
  6. 2(c) For positive integers $n$ and positive integers $a,b$ show that the relation $a^n\mid b^n$ implies that $a\mid b$. [3]
  7. 2(d) Find $gcd\,(2a+1,9a+4)$, where $a\in\mathbb Z$. [2]
  8. 3(a) If $p\ge q\ge5$ and $p$ and $q$ are both primes, then show that $24\mid p^2-q^2$. [3]
  9. 3(b) Show that there are infinite number of primes. Give an example to show that there are arbitrarily large gaps in the sequence of primes. [3+1]
  10. 3(c) Find the remainder when the sum $1^5+2^5+\text{---}+100^5$ is divided by $4$. Also by using Format's theorem find the unit digit of $3^{100}$. [3]
  11. 4(a) Obtain three consecutive integers, each having factor $2^2$, $3^2$ and $5^2$, respectively. [4]
  12. 4(b) Let $m,n$ be two positive integers. Show that $\varphi(m,n)=\frac{\varphi(m)\varphi(n)gcd(m,n)}{\varphi(\gcd(m,n))}$ [3]
  13. 4(c) If $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers, Also show that $m^{\varphi(n)}+n^{\varphi(m)}\equiv1(\mathrm{mod}\ mn)$ [3]
  14. 5(a) Determine if the following system of linear equations $x-2y+z=0$, $y-4z=4$ and $x-z=2$ is consistent. If so, find a solution. [3]
  15. 5(b) Row reduce the matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-3&-6&4&9\\-1&-2&-1&3&1\\-2&-3&0&3&-1\\1&4&5&-9&-7\end{pmatrix}$ to echelon form and find the pivot columns of $A$. [3]
  16. 5(c) Let $a_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\-5\end{pmatrix},\ a_2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\5\\6\end{pmatrix}$ and $b=\begin{pmatrix}7\\4\\-3\end{pmatrix}$. Determine whether $b$ is a linear combination of $a_1$ and $a_2$. Give geometric description of $L(\{a_1,a_2\})$. [4]
  17. 6(a) Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix. Show that the following conditions are equivalent:\par i) for each $b\in\mathbb R^m$, the equation $AX=b$ has a solution.\par ii) each $b\in\mathbb R^m$ is a linear combination of the columns of $A$.\par iii) the columns of $A$ spans $\mathbb R^m$. [4]
  18. 6(b) Describe all solutions of $Ax=b$, where $A=\begin{pmatrix}3&5&-4\\-3&-2&4\\6&1&-8\end{pmatrix}$ and $b=\begin{pmatrix}7\\1\\-4\end{pmatrix}$. [3]
  19. 6(c) Define a linear transformation $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$. If $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is a linear transformation, then prove that $T$ is one-to-one if and only if the equation $T(X)=0,\ X\in\mathbb R^n$ has only the trivial solution. [3]
  20. 7(a) Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix. Prove that the following conditions are equivalent.\par i) $A$ is an invertible matrix\par ii) $A$ is row equivalent to the $n\times n$ identity matrix\par iii) $A$ has $n$ pivot position\par iv) the equation $Ax=0$ has only the trivial solution. [4]
  21. 7(b) Define subspace of $\mathbb R^3$. Show that any plane through the origin is a subspace in $\mathbb R^3$. [3]
  22. 7(c) Find the dimension of the null space of $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&4&-2&1\\-2&-5&7&3\\3&7&-8&6\end{pmatrix}$ [4]
  23. 8(a) Define eigen value and eigen vector of a matrix $A$. If $\lambda$ an eigen value of a non-singular matrix $A$, then show that $\lambda^{-1}$ is an eigen value of $A^{-1}$. [4]
  24. 8(b) Show that two nonzero eigen vectors of a square matrix $A$ over $\mathbb R$ corresponding to two distinct eigen values are linearly independent. [3]
  25. 8(c) The characteristic roots of a $3\times3$ real matrix $A$ are in A.P.. Determine the characteristic roots, where trace $A=15$ and $|A|=80$. [3]
  26. 9(a) If $\lambda$ is an eigen value of a nonsingular matrix $A$, then show that $\frac{|A|}\lambda$ is an eigen value of adj $A$. [3]
  27. 9(b) If $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\4&5\end{pmatrix}$. Find the eigen values and the eigen vectors of $A$. [4]
  28. 9(c) Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over $\mathbb R$. Show that to a non-zero eigen vector of $A$ there corresponds unique eigen value of $A$. [3]
