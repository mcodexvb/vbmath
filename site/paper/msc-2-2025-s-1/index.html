MMC-21

Functional Analysis
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1(a) Define compact metric space and prove that $\mathbb R$ is not a compact metric space. [3]
  2. 1(b) Prove that every compact subset in a metric space is closed and bounded. Does the converse hold ? Support your answer. [4]
  3. 1(c) Let $A$ be a compact subset in a metric space $(X,d)$. Show that for every subset $B$ of $X$ there exists a point $x_0\in A$ such that $d(x_0,B)=d(A,B)$. [3]
  4. 2(a) State Riesz Lemma and hence show that if a closed unit ball in a metric space $X$ is compact then $X$ is finite dimensional. [4]
  5. 2(b) Show that $C[a,b]$ is a Banach space with respect to a norm to be defined by you. [4]
  6. 2(c) Show that $\mathbb R^2$ is a normed linear space with respect to $\|\ \|$ defined by $\|x\|=max\{\frac{|x_1|}a,\frac{|x_2|}b\}$ where $a,b>0$ and $x=(x_1,x_2)\in\mathbb R^2$. [2]
  7. 3(a) Show that every finite dimensional normed linear space is complete. [3]
  8. 3(b) Prove that dual space $X^*$ of any normed linear space $X$ is a Banach space. [5]
  9. 3(c) If $T_1,T_2:X\to Y$ are bounded linear operators where $X$ and $Y$ are normed linear spaces then prove that\par (i) $\|T_1+T_2\|\le\|T_1\|+\|T_2\|$ and (ii) $\|T_1T_2\|\le\|T_1\|\|T_2\|$. [2]
  10. 4(a) State and prove Closed Graph Theorem. [7]
  11. 4(b) Let $T:C[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ defined by $T(x)=\int_0^1xdt,x\in C[0,1]$. Show that $T$ is a bounded linear functional. [3]
  12. 5(a) State and prove Bessel's Inequality in Hilbert space. [4]
  13. 5(b) Prove that $\mathbb C^n$ is a Hilbert space w.r.t. an inner product to be defined by you. [4]
  14. 5(c) Show that $l_p(p\ge1,p\ne2)$ is not a Hilbert space. [2]
  15. 6(a) State and prove Riesz representation theorem for a bounded linear functional defined on a Hilbert space. [5]
  16. 6(b) Show that a normed linear space $(X,\|\ \|)$ is a Banach space iff $\{x\in X:\|x\|=1\}$ is complete. [3]
  17. 6(c) Prove that closure of a convex set in a normed linear space is convex. [2]
