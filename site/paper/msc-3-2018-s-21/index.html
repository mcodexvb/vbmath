MMO-31

Advanced Functional Analysis-I
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2018
  1. 1(a) Give definition of a topological vector space (tvs) and prove that every tvs is regular. [1+2]
  2. 1(b) Let $X$ be a tvs. Then prove the following:\par \hspace*{1em}i) there is a neighbourhood (nbd.) base of $\theta$ consisting of balanced neighbourhoods (nbds.). \par \hspace*{1em}ii) if $X$ is locally convex then there is a nbd. base of $\theta$ consisting of convex and balanced nbds. [3]
  3. 1(c) Let $X$ be a tvs and $A\subset X$. Show that $\bar A=\bigcap\{A+V:V$ is any nbd. of $\theta\}$. [2]
  4. 2(a) When is a tvs said to be locally compact? Prove that every locally compact tvs is of finite dimension. [4]
  5. 2(b) Let $X$ be a tvs. If $X$ is metrizable by an invariant metric $d$ then prove the following:\par \hspace*{1em}i) $d(nx,\theta)\le nd(x,\theta),\ x\in X$ and $n\in\mathbb N$.\par \hspace*{1em}ii) if $\{x_n\}$ be a sequence in $X$ such that $x_n\to\theta$ (as $n\to\infty$) then there exists a sequence $\{\gamma_n\}$ of scalars such that $\gamma_n\to\infty$ and $\gamma_nx_n\to\theta$ as $n\to\infty$. [4]
  6. 2(c) If $X$ is a locally bounded tvs with Heine-Borel property, then prove that $X$ is finite dimensional. [2]
  7. 3(a) Let $X$ be a linear space and $A$ be a convex and absorbing set in $X$ containing $\theta$. Then show that\par \hspace*{1em}i) $\mu_A(x+y)\le\mu_A(x)+\mu_A(y)$, for all $x,y\in X$;\par \hspace*{1em}ii) $\mu_A(tx)=t\mu_A(x),\ \forall t\ge0$ and for all $x\in X$;\par \hspace*{1em}iii) $\mu_A$ is a seminorm, if $A$ is balanced set in $X$;\par \hspace*{1em}iv) if $B=\{x\in X;\mu_A(x)<1\}$ and $C=\{x\in X;\mu_A(x)\le1\}$ then\par \hspace*{3em}$B\subset A\subset C$ and $\mu_A=\mu_B=\mu_C$. [6]
  8. 3(b) If $Y$ is a subspace of a tvs $X$ and $Y$ is an F-space (in respect of the topology inherited from $X$), then prove that $Y$ is a closed subspace of $X$. [4]
  9. 4(a) State and prove Hahn-Banach theorem for a complex linear space (assuming that the corresponding result holds for a real linear space). [4]
  10. 4(b) If $f$ is a continuous linear functional on a subspace $M$ of a locally convex space $X$, then show that there exists a $T\in X^*$ such that $T=f$ on $M$. [3]
  11. 4(c) Let $T:(X,\tau)\to(Y,\tau')$ be a linear mapping where $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\tau')$ are two topological vector spaces. If $T$ is continuous at $\theta$ in $X$ then show that $T$ is continuous on $X$. \par\hfill P.T.O. [3]
  12. 5(a) Suppose $\Lambda_1,\Lambda_2,\ldots,\Lambda_n$ and $\Lambda$ are linear functionals on a linear space $X$ and\par \hspace*{2em}$N=\{x\in X:\Lambda_1x=\Lambda_2x=\ldots=\Lambda_nx=0\}.$\par Show that the following statements are equivalent:\par \hspace*{1em}i) There are scalars $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\ldots,\alpha_n$ such that\par \hspace*{3em}$\Lambda=\alpha_1\Lambda_1+\alpha_2\Lambda_2+\ldots+\alpha_n\Lambda_n.$\par \hspace*{1em}ii) There exists a scalar $0<\gamma<\infty$ such that $|\Lambda x|\le\gamma\max_{1\le i\le n}|\Lambda_i(x)|\ \forall x\in X$.\par \hspace*{1em}iii) $\Lambda x=0\ \forall x\in N$. [5]
  13. 5(b) Define a seminorm on a linea space. For any seminorm $p$ on a linear space $X$, prove that the set $\{x\in X:p(x)\le1\}$ is convex and balanced. [3]
  14. 5(c) If $X$ is a tvs and $A,B$ are bounded subsets of $X$ then prove that $A\cup B$ and $A+B$ are also bounded subsets of $X$. [2]
  15. 6(a) Define Weak$^*$ topology for a normed linear space. State and prove Banach-Alogulu theorem. [8]
  16. 6(b) If $\tau_1\subset\tau_2$ are topologies on a set $X$ and if $\tau_1$ is Hausdorff and $\tau_2$ is compact then show that $\tau_1=\tau_2$. [2]
