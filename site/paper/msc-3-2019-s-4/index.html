MMC-34

Calculus of Variations and Special Functions
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester III2019
  1. 1(a) Show that the validity of Euler's differential equation is a necessary condition for the existence of the extremum of the functional\par \[J(y)=\int_{x_0}^{x_1}F(x,y,y')\,dx.\] [3]
  2. 1(b) Find the stationary function and stationary value of\par \[I(y)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\left[y^2+(y')^2-2y\sin x\right]dx,\ y(0)=0,\ y(\pi/2)=\tfrac12.\] [3]
  3. 1(c) Find the plane curve of fixed perimeter which has maximum area above $x$-axis. [4]
  4. 2(a) Show that the curve which extremizes the functional\par \[I=\int_0^{\pi/4}\left(y''^2-y^2+x^2\right)dx,\] under the conditions $y(0)=0,\ y'(0)=1,\ y(\pi/4)=y'(\pi/4)=\frac1{\sqrt2}$ is $y=\sin x$. [4]
  5. 2(b) A particle of mass $m$ is falling vertically, under the action of gravity. If $y$ is the distance measured downward and no resistive forces are present then show that the Lagrangian function is $L=T-V=m\left(\frac12\dot y^2+gy\right)+$ constant, and verify that the Euler equation of the problem $\delta\displaystyle\int_{t_1}^{t_2}L\,dt=0$ is the proper equation of motion of the particle. [4]
  6. 2(c) Reduce the following boundary value problem into a variational problem:\par \[\frac d{dx}\left(x\frac{dy}{dx}\right)+y=x\ \text{ with }y(0)=0,\ y(1)=1.\] [2]
  7. 3(a) Find the shortest distance between the parabola $y=x^2$ and the straight line $x-y=5$. [5]
  8. 3(b) Solve the boundary value problem\par \hspace*{2em}$y''-y+x=0\ (0\le x\le1)$, with $y(0)=y(1)=0$ by Rayleigh Ritz method. [3]
  9. 3(c) Describe Galerkin method for solving a boundary value problem. \par\hfill P.T.O. [2]
  10. 4(a) Define the generating function for Legendre polynomials $P_n(x)$ of degree $n$. Hence prove the following recurrence relation\par \hspace*{2em}$(n+1)P_{n+1}(x)-(2n+1)xP_n(x)+nP_{n-1}(x)=0.$ [1+3]
  11. 4(b) Express $x^4+2x^3+2x^2-x-3$ in terms of Legendre's polynomials. [3]
  12. 4(c) Use Rodrigue's formula to show that $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}P_n(x)\,dx=0$ for $n\ge1$. [3]
  13. 5(a) If $J_n(x)$ denotes the Bessel's function of the first kind of order $n$ and $n$ an integer, then show that $J_n'(x)=\dfrac12\left[J_{n-1}(x)-J_{n+1}(x)\right]$. [3]
  14. 5(b) Show that the Laguerre's polynomials of order $n$, $L_n(x)$ are orthogonal over the interval $(0,\infty)$ with respect to the weighted function $e^{-x}$. [4]
  15. 5(c) Prove that $e^{2tx-t^2}=\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^n}{n!}H_n(x)$, where $H_n(x)$ denotes the Hermite polynomial of order $n$. [3]
  16. 6(a) When is a series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}C_n$ called hypergeometric? Define the hypergeometric function. [1+3]
  17. 6(b) Solve the differential equation\par \[x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+x\frac{dy}{dx}+\left(4x^4-\frac14\right)y=0\ \text{ in terms of Bessel's function.}\] [3]
  18. 6(c) Prove that if $m<n$, then\par \[\frac{d^m}{dx^m}\left(H_n(x)\right)=\frac{2^mn!}{(n-m)!}H_{n-m}(x),\ \text{where }H_n(x)\text{ denotes the Hermite polynomial of order }n.\] [3]
