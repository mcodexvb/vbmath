MMO-41(A4)

M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2019
  1. 1 What is Allee effect? Discuss about Strong and weak Allee effects. [4]
  2. 2 Solve the migration model $x_{n+1}-x_n=5x_n+2y_n-3x_n$, $y_{n+1}-y_n=7y_n-2y_n+3x_n$. [4]
  3. 3 What is Jury's stability criterion? Draw the stability region in trace-determinant plane for a two dimensional discrete system. [4]
  4. 4 Find the equilibrium points and discuss about their stability of the following system with fear effect: \[x_{n+1}=x_n\exp\left[\frac a{1+ky_n}-d-bx_n-cy_n\right],\qquad y_{n+1}=y_n\exp\left[\mu cx_n-e\right].\] [4]
  5. 5 Show that the logistic equation $x_{n+1}=\gamma\,x_n(1-x_n)$ undergoes a period doubling bifurcation at $\gamma=3$. [4]
  6. 6 A certain species of rat has a maximum life span of three years. The probability of a rate surviving its first year is 50\%, and the probability of surviving its second year is 60\%. On average, each rate in its first year produces 0.6 offspring, rats in their second year produce 2.4 offspring and rats in their third year produce 0.5 offspring each.\par The number of rats at initially are 55, 32, 25 for first, second and third year respectively. Write the Leslie matrix and determine the number of rats after the one year. [4]
  7. 7 What is Neimark-Sacker bifurcation? Prove that the system $x_{n+1}=\alpha x_n(1-x_n)-x_ny_n$, $y_{n+1}=\dfrac1\beta x_ny_n$ undergoes Neimark-Sacker bifurcation around the interior equilibrium point. [4]
  8. 8 Using center manifold theorem, find the nature of the equilibrium point of the system $u_{n+1}=-u_n+u_nw_n$, $v_{n+1}=-\dfrac{v_n}2+u_n^2$, $w_{n+1}=\dfrac{w_n}2-u_nv_n$. \par\textbf{P.T.O.} [4]
  9. 9 What is bistability? Show that the discrete model with Allee effect $x_{n+1}=r\left(1-\dfrac{x_n}k\right)\left(\dfrac{x_n}{k_0}-1\right)$ exhibits bistability behavior. [4]
  10. 10 Draw the bistability region for the two dimensional discrete system $x_{n+1}=x_n+\left[x_n(1-x_n)(x_n-\theta)-x_ny_n\right]$, $y_{n+1}=y_n+\left[cx_ny_n-dy_n\right]$ in $c$-$d$ parametric space. [4]
  11. 11 Find the indirect $z$-controller $u_{pred}(t)$ for the discrete system $x_{n+1}=x_n\exp\left(r\left(1-\dfrac{x_n}k\right)-\lambda y_n\right)$, $y_{n+1}=y_n\exp(c\lambda y_n-d)-u_{pred}(t)\cdot y_n$. [4]
  12. 12 Solve the delay difference equation $U_{k+1}=rU_k(1-U_{k-1})$. [4]
  13. 13 Find the conditions for the existence of the equilibrium points of the system \[x_{n+1}=x_n\exp\left[\gamma\left(1-\frac{x_n}k\right)\left(\frac{x_n}{x_n+\theta}\right)-\frac{my_n}{ax_n^2+bx_n+c}\right],\quad y_{n+1}=y_n\exp\left[\frac{\mu my_n}{ax_n^2+bx_n+c}-d\right].\] [4]
  14. 14 Write a MATLAB code to draw the bifurcation diagrams in a single figure for both the populations of the system \[x_{n+1}=x_n+ax_n\left(1-\frac{x_n}k\right)-\frac{px_ny_n}{x_n+y_n+1},\qquad y_{n+1}=y_n+\frac{cpx_ny_n}{x_n+y_n+1}-m\cdot y_n.\] [4]
