SECMA-2

Analysis-II and Tensor Calculus
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2023
  1. 1 Define covariant, contravariant, mixed tensor fields, their order, and rank. Transform the contravariant components of a tensor field $(2xy,x+y,y^2z)$ in Cartesian coordinates to components in cylindrical polar coordinates. [3+2]
  2. 2 Define symmetric and skew-symmetric tensors. Prove that every tensor of rank two can be expressed as the sum of a symmetric and a skew-symmetric tensor. Let $A^{ijk}$ and $B_{lmn}$ be two skew-symmetric tensors. Verify the nature of $C_{lm}^{ij}=A^{ijk}B_{lmk}$. [1+2+2]
  3. 3 Define inner multiplication and quotient law of tensor fields. If $A_i^{pqr}$ and $B_{st}^{lm}$ are two tensor fields, examine which one among their sum, difference $A_i^{pqr}\pm B_{st}^{lm}$, and the product $A_i^{pqr}B_{st}^{irm}$ is a tensor field. Justify your answer. Prove that contraction of $B_t^l$ is a scalar field. [1+1+1+2]
  4. 4 What do you mean by the line element of a curve in space? Show that square of the line element can be expressed as a quadratic form. What is the name of the coefficients involved in the quadratic form? Do they behave like tensors? Give the order and rank if yes. Find the coefficients in the square of the line element described in the spherical polar coordinates. [1+1+1+1+1]
  5. 5 Let a quantity $B(i,j,k)$ be such that $B(i,j,k)C_i^{jl}=A_k^l$, where $C_i^{jl}$ is an arbitrary tensor field. Prove that $B(i,j,k)$ is a tensor field. Determine its rank. [4+1]
  6. 6 Give the parametric representation of a surface $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ and a curve $\Gamma\subset S$ in $\mathbb R^3$. Derive the second derivative of the position vector of a curve on a surface $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ with respect to the parameter appearing in its parametric representation. Examine whether the second derivative of the position vector of $\Gamma$ with respect to the parameter at any point on it lies in the tangent plane at that point of the surface. [1+1+2+1]
  7. 7 Prove that the partial derivative of a scalar field is a tensor field while the partial derivative of a vector or a tensor field is not a tensor field. [5]
  8. 8 Write the formula for the second-order covariant derivatives of a vector field $B_i$ with respect to two variables $u^l,u^n$ (say). Is it commutative under the interchange of derivatives with respect to $u^l,u^n$? If no, find the difference $(B_{i,ln}-B_{i,nl})$, and examine whether this difference can be written as a sum of $B_l$. Hence or otherwise define Riemann-Christoffel tensor. \renewcommand{\paperfull}{60} \renewcommand{\meaningplural}{s} [5]
