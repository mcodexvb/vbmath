MMC-23

Abstract Algebra
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester II2025
  1. 1 Let $R$ be an integral domain. Which of the following statements is always true?\par(a) Every prime element in $R$ is irreducible.\par(b) Every irreducible element in $R$ is prime.\par(c) If $R$ is a PID, then every prime element is irreducible.\par(d) If $R$ is a UFD, then every irreducible element is prime.
  2. 2 Let $F$ be a field. Which of the following is an ED?\par(a) $F[x,y]$\par(b) $F[[x]]$ (ring of formal power series)\par(c) $F[x]$\par(d) $F[x]/<x^2>$
  3. 3 In the ring $\mathbb Z[i]$ (Gaussian integers), the GCD of $5+i$ and $3-i$ is:\par(a) 1\par(b) $1+i$\par(c) $2+i$\par(d) $i$
  4. 4 In the ring $\mathbb Z$, the element 6 is:\par(a) Prime\par(b) Irreducible\par(c) Neither prime nor irreducible\par(d) Both prime and irreducible
  5. 5 Let $R$ be an integral domain. A prime ideal $P$ in $R$ is a maximal ideal if:\par(a) $R$ is a UFD.\par(b) $R$ is a PID.\par(c) $R$ is an ED.\par(d) This statement is generally not true without further conditions on $R$.
  6. 6 Which of the following is a prime element in $\mathbb Z[i]$?\par(a) 2\par(b) 3\par(c) 5\par(d) $1+i$
  7. 7 Let $R$ be a UFD. If $p\in R$ is a prime element, then $p$ is:\par(a) Always a unit.\par(b) Always irreducible.\par(c) Sometimes irreducible, sometimes not.\par(d) \unreadable{blurred; reads "Always generates a maximal ideal."}
  8. 8 Let $D$ be an ED. Then every nonzero non-unit element in $D$ can be written as a product of:\par(a) Prime elements, uniquely up to associates and order.\par(b) Irreducible elements, uniquely up to associates and order.\par(c) Both (a) and (b) simultaneously.\par(d) Neither (a) nor (b) necessarily.
  9. 9 Consider the integral domain $R=\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-3}]=\{a+b\sqrt{-3}\mid a,b\in\mathbb Z\}$. Which of the following is true?\par(a) $R$ is a PID.\par(b) $R$ is an ED.\par(c) $R$ is a UFD.\par(d) None of the above.
