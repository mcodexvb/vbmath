  1. 1 Let $a_{1}=b_{1}=0$, and for each $n \geq 2$, let $a_{n}$ and $b_{n}$ be real numbers given by $$ a_{n}=\sum_{m=2}^{n} \frac{(-1)^{m} m}{(\log (m))^{m}} \text { and } b_{n}=\sum_{m=2}^{n} \frac{1}{(\log (m))^{m}} . $$ Then which one of the following is TRUE about the sequences $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ ? \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item Both $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ are divergent \item $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is convergent and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is divergent \item $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ is divergent and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ is convergent \item Both $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ are convergent \end{enumerate}
  2. 2 Let $T \in M_{m \times n}(\mathbb{R})$. Let $V$ be the subspace of $M_{n \times p}(\mathbb{R})$ defined by $$ V=\left\{X \in M_{n \times p}(\mathbb{R}): T X=0\right\} . $$ Then the dimension of $V$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $p n-\operatorname{rank}(T)$ \item $p(m-\operatorname{rank}(T))$ \item $m n-p \operatorname{rank}(T)$ \item $p(n-\operatorname{rank}(T))$ \end{enumerate}
  3. 3 Let $g: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a twice differentiable function. Define $f: \mathbb{R}^{3} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ by $$ f(x, y, z)=g\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right) $$ Then $\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial x^{2}}+\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial y^{2}}+\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial z^{2}}$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $4\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-4 z^{2}\right) g^{\prime \prime}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right)$ \item $4\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+4 z^{2}\right) g^{\prime \prime}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right)$ \item $4\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right) g^{\prime \prime}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right)$ \item $4\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+4 z^{2}\right) g^{\prime \prime}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right)+8 g^{\prime}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}-2 z^{2}\right)$ \end{enumerate}
  4. 4 Let $\left\{a_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{\infty}$ and $\left\{b_{n}\right\}_{n=0}^{\infty}$ be sequences of positive real numbers such that $n a_{n}<b_{n}<n^{2} a_{n}$ for all $n \geq 2$. If the radius of convergence of the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_{n} x^{n}$ is 4 , then the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} b_{n} x^{n}$ \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item converges for all $x$ with $|x|<2$ \item converges for all $x$ with $|x|>2$ \item does not converge for any $x$ with $|x|>2$ \item does not converge for any $x$ with $|x|<2$ \end{enumerate}
  5. 5 Let $S$ be the set of all limit points of the set $\left\{\frac{n}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{n}: n \in \mathbb{N}\right\}$. Let $\mathbb{Q}_{+}$be the set of all positive rational numbers. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\mathbb{Q}_{+} \subseteq S$ \item $S \cap\left(\mathbb{R} \backslash \mathbb{Q}_{+}\right) \neq \varnothing$ \item $S \subseteq \mathbb{Q}_{+}$ \item $S \cap \mathbb{Q}_{+} \neq \varnothing$ \end{enumerate}
  6. 6 If $x^{h} y^{k}$ is an integrating factor of the differential equation $$ y(1+x y) d x+x(1-x y) d y=0 $$ then the ordered pair $(h, k)$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $(-2,-2)$ \item $(-2,-1)$ \item $(-1,-2)$ \item $(-1,-1)$ \end{enumerate}
  7. 7 If $y(x)=\lambda e^{2 x}+e^{\beta x}, \beta \neq 2$, is a solution of the differential equation $$ \frac{d^{2} y}{d x^{2}}+\frac{d y}{d x}-6 y=0 $$ satisfying $\frac{d y}{d x}(0)=5$, then $y(0)$ is equal to \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 1 \item 4 \item 5 \item 9 \end{enumerate}
  8. 8 The equation of the tangent plane to the surface $x^{2} z+\sqrt{8-x^{2}-y^{4}}=6$ at the point $(2,0,1)$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $2 x+z=5$ \item $3 x+4 z=10$ \item $3 x-z=10$ \item $7 x-4 z=10$ \end{enumerate}
  9. 9 The value of the integral $$ \int_{y=0}^{1} \int_{x=0}^{1-y^{2}} y \sin \left(\pi(1-x)^{2}\right) d x d y $$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\frac{1}{2 \pi}$ \item $2 \pi$ \item $\frac{\pi}{2}$ \item $\frac{2}{\pi}$ \end{enumerate} \hfill [JAM 2019] % MAINUL

  11. 11 Let $H$ and $K$ be subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}_{144}$. If the order of $H$ is 24 and the order of $K$ is 36 , then the order of the subgroup $H \cap K$ is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item 3 \item 4 \item 6 \item 12 \end{enumerate}
  12. 12 Let $P$ be a $4 \times 4$ matrix with entries from the set of rational numbers. If $\sqrt{2}+i$, with $i=\sqrt{-1}$, is a root of the characteristic polynomial of $P$ and $I$ is the $4 \times 4$ identity matrix, then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $P^{4}=4 P^{2}+9 I$ \item $P^{4}=4 P^{2}-9 I$ \item $P^{4}=2 P^{2}-9 I$ \item $P^{4}=2 P^{2}+9 I$ \end{enumerate}
  13. 13 The set $\left\{\frac{x}{1+x}:-1<x<1\right\}$, as a subset of $\mathbb{R}$, is \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item connected and compact \item connected but not compact \item not connected but compact \item neither connected nor compact \end{enumerate} \hfill [JAM 2019] % MAINUL

  16. 16 Let $f(x)=(\ln x)^{2}, x>0$. Then \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)] \item $\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty} \frac{f(x)}{x}$ does not exist \item $\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty} f^{\prime}(x)=2$ \item $\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty}(f(x+1)-f(x))=0$ \item $\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty}(f(x+1)-f(x))$ does not exist \end{enumerate}
  17. 17 Let $f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable function such that $f^{\prime}(x)>f(x)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, and $f(0)=1$.
