MJMA-2

Analytical Geometry and Vector Calcus
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2023
  1. 1(a) Find the angle by which the axes should be rotated so that the equation $ax^2+2hxy+by^2=0$ becomes another equation in which the term $xy$ is absent. Hence find the angle through which the axes are to be rotated so that the equation $17x^2+18xy-7y^2=1$ may be reduced to the form $Ax^2+By^2=1$, $A>0$. [[5]]
  2. 1(b) Transform the equation $x^2-5xy+y^2+8x-20y+15=0$ to its canonical form and determine the nature of the conic. [[5]]
  3. 2(a) Prove that the locus of the mid-points of the family of focal chords of the conic $\frac lr=1+e\cos\theta$ is $r(1-e^2\cos^2\theta)+el\cos\theta=0$. [[5]]
  4. 2(b) Find the equation of the common tangent to the circle $x^2+y^2=4ax$ and the parabola $y^2=4ax$. [[5]]
  5. 3(a) A plane passes through a fixed point $(p,q,r)$ and cuts the co-ordinate axes at A,B and C. Show that the locus of the centre of the sphere OABC is $\frac px+\frac qy+\frac rz=2$. [[5]]
  6. 3(b) Find the centre and the radius of the circle $x^2+y^2+z^2-2y-4z-11=0$, $x+2y+2z=15$. [[5]]
  7. 4(a) Prove that a homogenous second degree equation in $x,y,z$ represents a cone whose vertex is at origin. [[5]]
  8. 4(b) Find the equation of the straight lines in which the plane $2x+y-z=0$ cuts the cone $4x^2-y^2+3z^2=0$. Find also the angle between them. [[5]]
  9. 5(a) Prove that the plane $ax+by+cz=0$ $(a,b,c\ne0)$ cuts the cone $yz+zx+xy=0$ in perpendicular lines if $\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac1c=0$. [[5]]
  10. 5(b) If $\frac x1=\frac y2=\frac z3$ represents one of a set of three mutually perpendicular generators of the cone $5yz-8zx-3xy=0$, find the equations of the other two. [[5]]
  11. 6(a) Find the equation of the tangent plane to the paraboloid $ax^2+by^2=2z$ which is parallel to the plane $lx+my+nz=0$. [[5]]
  12. 6(b) Find the equation of the generators of the hyperboloid $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}-\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1$ which pass through the point $(a\cos\alpha,b\sin\alpha,0)$. [[5]]
  13. 1(a) Using vector method, prove that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.
  14. 1(b) Prove, using vector method, that the medians of a triangle are concurrent.
  15. 1(c) Let $\vec A,\vec B$ and $\vec C$ be three vectors on $\mathbb R^2$. Prove that $|\vec A+\vec B+\vec C|\le|\vec A|+|\vec B|+|\vec C|$. [[4+4+2]]
  16. 2(a) Determine the equation of the plane perpendicular to the vector $2\hat i-3\hat j+6\hat k$ and psssing through the terminal point of the vector $\hat i+2\hat j+3\hat k$.
  17. 2(b) Determine the acute angle formed by the two main diagonals of a cube.
  18. 2(c) Let $\vec A,\vec B$ and $\vec C$ be three vectors in $\mathbb R^3$. Prove that $\vec A\times(\vec B\times\vec C)+\vec B\times(\vec C\times\vec A)+\vec C\times(\vec A\times\vec B)=\vec0$. [[3+3+4]]
  19. 3(a) Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be a twice differentiable function. Prove, using vector method, that the length of the curve between the points $(a,f(a))$ and $(b,f(b))$ is $\int_a^b\sqrt{1+(f'(x))^2}dx$.
  20. 3(b) For the differentiable curve $\vec R(t)=3\cos t\,\hat i+3\sin t\,\hat j+4t\,\hat k$, find the unit tangent vector $\vec T$, principal normal $\vec N$, the binormal vector $\vec B$, curvature $\kappa$, torsion $\tau$ and verify the Frenet-Serret formula. [[4+6]]
  21. 4(a) Prove that if $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R$ is defined by $f(x,y,z)=x^2\sin y+y^2\cos z$, then $\nabla\times(\nabla f)=\vec0$.
  22. 4(b) Can the vector field $F(x,y,z)=x(y^2+1)\hat i(ye^x-e^z)\hat j+x^2e^z\hat k$ be the curl of some vector field $G:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$? Justify your answer.
  23. 4(c) Let $\vec r(x,y,z)=x\hat i+y\hat j+z\hat k$ and $r(x,y,z)=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}$. For any integer $n\ge1$, prove that $\nabla\cdot(r^n\vec r)=(n+3)r^n$. [[3+3+4]]
  24. 5(a) Determine real numbers $a,b$ and $c$ such that the vector field $F(x,y,z)=(-4x-3y+az)\hat i+(bx+3y+5z)\hat j+(4x+cy+3z)\hat k$ is irrotational.
  25. 5(b) Determine the flow lines of the constant vector field $F(x,y,z)=2\hat i-3\hat j+\hat k$.
  26. 5(c) Let $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R$ be of class $C^2$. Prove that the curl of $\mathrm{grad}(f)$ is $\vec0$. [[3+3+4]]
  27. 6(a) Determine the point on the plane $2x-3y-z=4$ that is closest to the origin.
  28. 6(b) Determine three positive real numbers whose sum is 18 and whose product is as large as possible.
  29. 6(c) Determine the critical points of the function $f(x,y)=e^{-(x^2+y^2)}$ and find the nature of the critical points using Hessian criterion. [[4+3+3]]
