Paper

Linear Programming Problem, Game Theory and Mathematical Statistics
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester V2024
  1. 1 Answer any five questions ($5\times2=10$)
  2. 1(a) Verify if $S=\{(x,y):x^2+y^2=25\}$, a subset of $E^2$, is a convex set.
  3. 1(b) What is the nature of the feasible solution of an LPP having the set of constraints $-x+y\ge3$, $x-y\ge2$ where $x,y\ge0$.
  4. 1(c) What is the relation between the optimal values of primal problem and dual problem (assume that both exist)?
  5. 1(d) If the $k$-th constraint of a primal problem be an equation, then what can you can you say about the nature of $k$-th dual variable?
  6. 1(e) How can you establish geometrically that a set of vectors $a_{ij}$ associated with the variables $x_{ij}$ in a T.P are linearly independent?
  7. 1(f) In a T.P with 3 origins and 4 destinations, may the variables $x_{11},x_{22},x_{33},x_{34},x_{24},x_{23}$ be considered as basic variables? Give reasons.
  8. 1(g) Is the optimal solution of an assignment problem unique? Justify your answer.
  9. 1(h) How can you solve a restricted assignment problem?
  10. 2(a) What is the relation between the optimum value of maximization and minimization problem? What do you mean by an optimal solution of an LPP? [1+1]
  11. 2(b) A dietitian wants to create a diet plan for their clients by combining two types of food items: Food A and Food B. The diet plan must meet the following daily nutritional requirements: \begin{itemize} \item At least 30 grams of protein \item At least 20 grams of fat \item At least 40 grams of carbohydrates \end{itemize} The nutritional content and cost per unit of each food item are given in the following table:
  12. 3(a) Show that a hyperplane is a convex set.
  13. 3(b) State Fundamental Theorem of Linear Programming Problem.
  14. 3(c) How many solution are there of the following system of equations: \[\begin{aligned} 4x_1+2x_2+3x_3-8x_4&=6\\ 3x_1+5x_2+4x_3-6x_4&=8. \end{aligned}\] Does there exist a basic feasible solution with $x_2$ and $x_3$ simultaneously as nonbasic variables? [1+2]
  15. 4 Consider the LPP \[\begin{aligned} \text{Max }Z&=2x_1+5x_2\\ \text{Subject to:}\qquad&\\ 2x_1+5x_2&\le40\\ x_1+2x_2&\le11\\ x_2&\ge4,\\ x_1&\ge0,\quad x_2\ge0. \end{aligned}\] Solve it geometrically. What is the type of convex set of feasible solution of the LPP and what is impact of that on the optimality of the objective function? [3+1+1]
  16. 5 Solve the LPP (if possible) by Big M-method, \[\begin{aligned} \text{Max }Z&=3x_1-x_2\\ \text{Subject to:}\qquad&\\ -x_1+x_2&\ge2\\ 5x_1-2x_2&\ge2\\ x_1&\ge0,\quad x_2\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [5]
  17. 6 Solve the LPP (if possible) by two phase method, \[\begin{aligned} \text{Min }Z&=4x_1+x_2\\ \text{Subject to:}\qquad&\\ x_1+2x_2&\le3\\ 4x_1+3x_2&\ge6\\ 3x_1+x_2&=3\\ x_1&\ge0,\quad x_2\ge0. \end{aligned}\] [5]
  18. 7 Find the optimal solution and the corresponding cost of the following transportation problem: \[\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline \text{Origin/Destination}&D_1&D_2&D_3&D_4&\text{Supply}\\\hline O_1&2&5&4&7&4\\\hline O_2&6&1&2&5&6\\\hline O_3&4&5&2&4&8\\\hline \text{Demand}&3&7&6&2&18\\\hline \end{array}\] [5]
  19. 8 In a factory there are five operators A,B,C,D,E and the five machines I,II,III, IV, V the operating cost is given if $i$-th operator operates $j$-th machine [$i,j=1,2,\ldots 5$]. But there is a restriction that C can not be allowed to operate the third machine and similarly B can not be allowed to operate the fifth machine. The cost matrix is given below. Find the optimal assignment and the optimal assignment cost. \[\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline \text{Operators/Machines}&I&II&III&IV&V\\\hline A&24&29&18&32&19\\\hline B&17&26&34&22&21\\\hline C&27&16&28&17&25\\\hline D&22&18&28&30&24\\\hline E&28&16&31&24&27\\\hline \end{array}\] [5]
  20. 1(a) Define population of a random variable $X$ and a random sample of the population. Show that if the size of a random sample is large, the distribution of the sample is the statistical image of the distribution of the population. [2+3]
  21. 1(b) For a continuous population explain what are meant by \emph{class intervals}, \emph{class limits}, and \emph{grouping of data}. Describe how to obtain a graphical representation of \emph{grouped data}. [3+2]
  22. 2 Define an estimate of a population parameter. When is an estimate said to be consistent and unbiased? For a normal $(m,\sigma)$ population, show that the sample variance $S^2$ is a consistent but biased estimate of $\sigma^2$. Obtain an estimate of $\sigma^2$ that is both consistent and unbiased.
  23. 3(a) Define a \emph{statistic} and sampling distribution of a \emph{statistic}. Find the sampling distribution of the mean for the gamma $(l)$ population. [2+3]
  24. 3(b) Prove that the maximum likelihood estimate of the parameter $\alpha$ of a population having density function $f(x)=2(\alpha-x)/\alpha^2$ ($0<x<\alpha$) for a sample of unit size is $2x$, $x$ being the sample value, and examine whether the estimate is biased or unbiased. [4+1]
  25. 4(a) The population of scores of 10-year old children in a psychological performance test is known to have a standard deviation 5.2. If a random sample of size 20 shows a mean of 16.9, find 95\% confidence limits for the mean score of the population, assuming that the population is normal. Given $P(U>1.96)=0.025$, where $U$ is a standard normal variate.
  26. 4(b) Define (i) Simple hypothesis, (ii) Composite hypothesis, and (iii) Null hypothesis, and give examples of each of them. [2+2+2]
  27. 5(a) Define the critical region of testing a statistical hypothesis. If $x\ge1$ is the critical region for testing $H_0:\theta=2$ against the alternative $H_1:\theta=1$ on the basis of the single observation from the population with density $f(x,\theta)=\theta e^{-\theta x}$, $0\le x<\infty$, obtain the values of two types of errors. [2+4]
  28. 5(b) Using the Neyman-Pearson theorem construct a test of the null hypothesis $H_0:m=m_0$ against an alternative $H_1:m=m_1$ for a normal $(m,\sigma)$ population when $m_0$, $m_1$, and $\sigma$ are known and $m_0<m_1$. [4]
