MMC-42

Operations Research
M.Sc. Mathematics · Semester IV2016
  1. 1(a) When an LPP has\par \hspace*{2em}(i) no feasible solution and\par \hspace*{2em}(ii) unbounded solution?\par Give the answer in the context of revised simplex method. [2]
  2. 1(b) Use revised simplex method to solve the following LPP:\par Minimize \quad $f(x_1,x_2)=x_1+2x_2$\par subject to \quad $2x_1+5x_2\ge6$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1+x_2\ge2$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$.\par Is the solution unique? Justify your answer.
  3. 2(a) Write a short note on sensitivity analysis. [2]
  4. 2(b) Find the optimal solution of the LPP\par Maximize \quad $f(x_1,x_2)=4x_1+5x_2$\par subject to \quad $3x_1+4x_2\le14$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$4x_1+2x_2\le8$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$2x_1+x_2\le6$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$.\par Show that the optimality of the solution is not violated if the right hand side of the first constraint varies between 6 and 16.\par Show further that the range of $C_2$ is $\left(\dfrac52,\dfrac{20}3\right)$ in order that the optimal solution obtained remains optimal.
  5. 3(a) Define integer linear programming problem in matrix form. Also discuss why integer linear programming problem is a linear programming problem.
  6. 3(b) Solve the following LPP and obtain the optimal integer solution:\par Maximize \quad $f(x_1,x_2)=3x_1+4x_2$\par subject to \quad $3x_1+2x_2\le8$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1+4x_2\le10$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$ and are integers. \par\hfill P.T.O. [7]
  7. 4(a) State necessary and sufficient Kuhn-Tucker conditions for a non-linear programming problem (NLPP) with maximization type objective function and with all `less than or equal to' type constraints. Hence solve the following NLPP:\par Maximize \quad $f(x_1,x_2)=50x_1+15x_2-6x_1x_2-0.5x_1^2-10x_2^2$\par subject to \quad $4x_1+x_2\le50$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1+x_2\le40$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$.
  8. 4(b) Solve the following NLPP graphically:\par Maximize \quad $f(x_1,x_2)=100x_1-x_1^2+100x_2-x_2^2$\par subject to \quad $x_1+x_2\ge80$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1+2x_2\le100$,\par \hspace*{4.2em}$x_1,x_2\ge0$. [4]
  9. 5(a) Obtain mathematical expressions for the optimal lot size and corresponding annual cost in an EOQ manufacturing model with shortages. [6]
  10. 5(b) The demand of a bought out item in a store is 12000 units per year. The carrying cost is Rs. 2 per unit per year and the ordering cost is Rs. 600 per order. The shortage cost is Rs. 10 per unit per year. Find the EOQ and the corresponding number of orders per year, the maximum inventory and maximum shortage quantity. [4]
  11. 6(a) For the queueing model $M/M/1:\infty/FCFS$, having Poisson input, exponential service, single service channel, infinite capacity of the system, first come first service queue discipline; find the average number of customers in the system at any time. [6]
  12. 6(b) A barber shop has space to accommodate 10 customers. The barber can service only one person at a time. If a customer comes to his shop and finds it full, he goes to the next shop. Customers randomly arrive at an average rate of $\lambda=10$ per hour and the barber's service time is negative exponential with an average of $\dfrac1\mu=5$ minutes per customer.\par Find $P_0$ and $P_n$. [4]
