MJMA-2

Analytical Geometry and Vector Tensor Calculus
B.Sc. Mathematics · Semester I2025
  1. 1(a) If by rotation of rectangular axes about the origin, the expression $ax^2+2hxy+by^2$ changes to $a'x'^2+2h'x'y'+b'y'^2$, then prove that (i)$a+b=a'+b'$ (ii)$ab-h^2=a'b'-h'^2$. [6]
  2. 1(b) Prove that the linear part of the equation $4x^2-12xy+2y^2-4x-6y+3=0$ can not be made disappear by only change of parallel axes. [4]
  3. 2(a) Transform the equation $x^2-2xy+2y^2-4x-6y+3=0$ to its canonical form and determine the nature of the conic. [6]
  4. 2(b) Find the polar equation of the straight line. [4]
  5. 3(a) Find the polar equation of the straight line joining two points on the parabola $\frac{2a}r=1+\cos\theta$ with $(\alpha-\beta)$ and $(\alpha+\beta)$ are their vectorial angles. [5]
  6. 3(b) Show that the sum of the reciprocals of the two perpendicular focal chords of a conic is constant. [5]
  7. 4(a) Show that the equation of the spheres which pass through the circle $x^2+y^2+z^2=5,x+2y+3z=3$ and touch the plane $4x+3y=15$ are $x^2+y^2+z^2+2x+4y+6z=11$ and $5(x^2+y^2+z^2)-(x+2y+3z)=13$. [5]
  8. 4(b) A sphere of radius $k$ passes through origin and meets the co-ordinate axes in A,B and C. Find the locus of the centriod of the triangle ABC. [5]
  9. 5(a) Prove that the equation of a cone whose vertex is at origin is a homogenous second degree equation in $x,y$ and $z$. [5]
  10. 5(b) If $\frac x1=\frac y2=\frac z3$ represents one of a set of three mutually perpendicular generators of the cone $5yz-8zx-3xy=0$ find the equations of the other two. [5]
  11. 6(a) A variable plane through $x$-axis and a variable plane through $y$-axis are inclined at a constant angle $\alpha$. Prove that their line of intersection generates the cone $z^2(x^2+y^2+z^2)=x^2y^2\tan^2\alpha$. [5]
  12. 6(b) A variable plane passes through a fixed point $(p,q,r)$ and cuts the co-ordinate axes in A,B and C. Show that the locus of the centre of the sphere OABC is $\frac px+\frac qy+\frac rz=2$ where O is the origin. [5]
  13. 1(a) Using vector method, prove that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.
  14. 1(b) Prove, using vector method, that semi-circular angle is right-angle.
  15. 1(c) Using vector method, determine the angle between the two main diagonals of a cube. [2+4+4]
  16. 2(a) Let $\vec F(t)$ be a differentiable vector-valued function defined from $\mathbb R$ to $\mathbb R^3$. If $\vec F.\vec F\equiv0$ for all $t\in\mathbb R$, then prove that $\vec F$ is constant.
  17. 2(b) Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be constants and let $\vec R(t)=\alpha\cos t\hat i+\alpha\sin t\hat j+\alpha t\tan\beta\hat k$. Prove that the magnitude of $\frac{d\vec R(t)}{dt}\times\frac{d^2\vec R(t)}{dt^2}$ is constant.
  18. 2(c) If $\vec R(t)$ is a twice differentiable vector-valued function such that $\frac{d^2\vec R(t)}{dt^2}=\vec R(t)$, then prove that $\vec R(t)\times\frac{d\vec R(t)}{dt}$ is constant. [3+3+4]
  19. 3(a) Define the curvature of a parametrized differentiable curve. Prove that the curvature of a straight line is $0$ and that of a circle of radius $r$ is $\frac1r$.
  20. 3(b) Prove that the curvature is constant at all points for the curve $4\cos t\hat i+4\sin t\hat j+2t\hat k$.
  21. 3(c) State the Frenet-Serret formulae. [5+3+2]
  22. 4(a) Prove that the vector fields $\vec F_1(x,y,z)=3\hat i-2\hat j+\hat k$ and $\vec F_2(x,y,z)=2x\hat i+2y\hat j-3\hat k$ are both gradient fields.
  23. 4(b) Let $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R$ be a smooth function. Prove that curl(grad$(f))=\vec 0$.
  24. 4(c) Let $\vec F:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ be a smooth vector field. Prove that div(curl$(\vec F))=0$. [3+3+4]
  25. 5(a) Let $\vec F_1$ and $\vec F_2$ be two vector fields. Prove that div$(\vec F_1+\vec F_2)=$ div$(\vec F_1)+$div$(\vec F_2)$ and curl$(\vec F_1+\vec F_2)=$ curl$(\vec F_1)+$curl$(\vec F_2)$.
  26. 5(b) Let $\vec F:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ be a vector field such that div$(\vec F)=0$ and curl$(\vec F)=\vec 0$. Is it necessarily true that $\vec F\equiv\vec 0$?
  27. 5(c) Prove that if $(xyz)^b(x^a\hat i+y^a\hat j+z^a\hat k)$ is irrotational, then either $b=0$ or $a=-1$. [3+3+4]
  28. 6(a) Let $\vec F(x,y)=xy\hat i+y^2\hat j$ and let $D$ be the region in the first quadrant bounded by the straight line $y=x$ and the parabola $y=x^2$. Compute $\oint_{\partial D}\vec F\cdot d\vec s$ directly and using Green's theorem.
  29. 6(b) Let $C$ be a simple, closed, smooth curve in $\mathbb R^2$. Prove that $\oint_C-y^3dx+(x^3+2x+y)dy$ is always positive.
  30. 6(c) Let $\vec F$ be the vector field given by $\vec F(x,y,z)=x\hat i+y\hat j+z\hat k$. If $\vec R(t)$ is a curve lying on the surface of the sphere $\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:x^2+y^2+z^2=1\}$, then prove that $\int_{\vec R}\vec F\cdot d\vec s=0$. [3+3+4]
