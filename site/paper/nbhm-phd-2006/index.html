NBHM · 2006 · Doctoral scholarship

  1. 1.1 Let $f : (\Q, +) \to (\Q, +)$ be a non-zero homomorphism. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f$ is always one-one. \item $f$ is always onto. \item $f$ is always a bijection. \item $f$ need be neither one-one nor onto. \end{enumerate}
  2. 1.2 Consider the element \[ \alpha = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 \\ 2 & 1 & 4 & 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \] of the symmetric group $S_5$ on five elements. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The order of $\alpha$ is $5$. \item $\alpha$ is conjugate to $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 5 & 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 5 & 4 & 3 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$. \item $\alpha$ is the product of two cycles. \item $\alpha$ commutes with all elements of $S_5$. \end{enumerate}
  3. 1.3 Let $G$ be a group of order $60$. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $G$ is abelian. \item $G$ has a subgroup of order $30$. \item $G$ has subgroups of order $2, 3$ and $5$. \item $G$ has subgroups of order $6, 10$ and $15$. \end{enumerate}
  4. 1.4 Consider the polynomial ring $R[x]$ where $R = \Z/12\Z$ and write the elements of $R$ as $\{0, 1, \dots, 11\}$. Write down all the distinct roots of the polynomial $f(x) = x^2 + 7x$ of $R[x]$.
  5. 1.5 Let $R$ be the polynomial ring $\Z_2[x]$ and write the elements of $\Z_2$ as $\{0, 1\}$. Let $(f(x))$ denote the ideal generated by the element $f(x) \in R$. If $f(x) = x^2 + x + 1$, then the quotient ring $R/(f(x))$ is \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item a ring but not an integral domain. \item an integral domain but not a field. \item a finite field of order $4$. \item an infinite field. \end{enumerate}
  6. 1.6 Consider the set of all linear transformations $T : \R^3 \to \R^4$ over $\R$. What is the dimension of this set, considered as a vector space over $\R$ with point-wise operations?
  7. 1.7 Consider the matrix $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$. Write down a matrix $P$ such that $P^{-1}AP$ is a diagonal matrix.
  8. 1.8 Let $A$ be an orthogonal $3 \times 3$ matrix with real entries. Pick out the true statements: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item The determinant of $A$ is a rational number. \item $d(Ax, Ay) = d(x, y)$ for any two vectors $x$ and $y \in \R^3$, where $d(u, v)$ denotes the usual Euclidean distance between vectors $u$ and $v \in \R^3$. \item All the entries of $A$ are positive. \item All the eigenvalues of $A$ are real. \end{enumerate}
  9. 1.9 Pick out the correct statements from the following list: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item A homomorphic image of a UFD (unique factorization domain) is again a UFD. \item The element $2 \in \Z[\sqrt{-5}]$ is irreducible in $\Z[\sqrt{-5}]$. \item Units of the ring $\Z[\sqrt{-5}]$ are the units of $\Z$. \item The element $2$ is a prime element in $\Z[\sqrt{-5}]$. \end{enumerate}
  10. 1.10 Let $p$ and $q$ be two distinct primes. Pick the correct statements from the following: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\Q(\sqrt{p})$ is isomorphic to $\Q(\sqrt{q})$ as fields. \item $\Q(\sqrt{p})$ is isomorphic to $\Q(\sqrt{-q})$ as vector spaces over $\Q$. \item $[\Q(\sqrt{p}, \sqrt{q}) : \Q] = 4$. \item $\Q(\sqrt{p}, \sqrt{q}) = \Q(\sqrt{p} + \sqrt{q})$. \end{enumerate}
  11. 2.1 Let $f$ be a real valued function on $\R$. Consider the functions \[ w_j(x) = \sup\left\{|f(u) - f(v)| : u, v \in \left[x - \frac{1}{j}, x + \frac{1}{j}\right]\right\}, \] where $j$ is a positive integer and $x \in \R$. Define next, \[ A_{j,n} = \left\{x \in \R : w_j(x) < \frac{1}{n}\right\},\quad n = 1, 2, \dots \] and \[ A_n = \bigcup_{j=1}^{\infty} A_{j,n},\quad n = 1, 2, \dots \] Now let \[ C = \{x \in \R : f \text{ is continuous at } x\}. \] Express $C$ in terms of the sets $A_n$.
  12. 2.2 Let $f$ be a continuous real valued function on $\R$ and $n$, a positive integer. Find \[ \frac{d}{dx} \int_0^x (2x - t)^n f(t)\,dt. \]
  13. 2.3 For each $n \ge 1$, let $f_n$ be a monotonic increasing real valued function on $[0, 1]$ such that the sequence of functions $\{f_n\}$ converges pointwise to the function $f \equiv 0$. Pick out the true statements from the following: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $f_n$ converges to $f$ uniformly. \item If the functions $f_n$ are also non-negative, then $f_n$ must be continuous for sufficiently large $n$. \end{enumerate}
  14. 2.4 Let $\Q$ denote the set of all rational numbers in the open interval $]0, 1[$. Let $\lambda(U)$ denote the Lebesgue measure of a subset $U$ of $]0, 1[$. Pick out the correct statements from the following: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\lambda(U) = 1$ for every open set $U \subset ]0, 1[$ which contains $\Q$. \item Given any $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists an open set $U \subset ]0, 1[$ containing $\Q$ such that $\lambda(U) < \varepsilon$. \end{enumerate}
  15. 2.5 A real valued function on an interval $[a, b]$ is said to be a function of bounded variation if there exists $M > 0$, such that for any finite set of points $a = a_0 < a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_n = b$, we have $\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} |f(a_i) - f(a_{i+1})| < M$. Which of the following statements are necessarily true? \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item Any continuous function on $[0, 1]$ is of bounded variation. \item If $f : \R \to \R$ is continuously differentiable, then its restriction to the interval $[-n, n]$ is of bounded variation on that interval, for any positive integer $n$. \item Any monotone function on $[0, 1]$ is of bounded variation. \end{enumerate}
  16. 2.6 Let $f$ be a differentiable function of one variable and let $g$ be the function of two variables given by $g(x, y) = f(ax + by)$, where $a, b$ are fixed nonzero numbers. Write down a partial differential equation satisfied by the function $g$.
  17. 2.7 The curve $x^3 - y^3 = 1$ is asymptotic to the line $x = y$. Find the point on the curve farthest from the line $x = y$.
  18. 2.8 Let $k$ be a fixed positive integer. Find $R_k$, the radius of convergence of the power series $\sum \left(\frac{n+1}{n}\right)^{n^2} z^{kn}$.
  19. 2.9 Let $\gamma$ be a closed and continuously differentiable path in the upper half plane \[ \{z \in \C : z = x + iy,\ x, y \in \R,\ y > 0\} \] not passing through the point $i$. Describe the set of all possible values of the integral \[ \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_{\gamma} \frac{2i}{z^2 + 1}\,dz. \]
  20. 2.10 Let $f$ be a function of three (real) variables having continuous partial derivatives. For each direction vector $h = (h_1, h_2, h_3)$ such that $h_1^2 + h_2^2 + h_3^2 = 1$, let $D_h f(x, y, z)$ be the directional derivative of $f$ along $h$ at $(x, y, z)$. For a point $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ where the partial derivative $\frac{\partial}{\partial x} f(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ is not zero, maximize $D_h f(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ (as a function of $h$).
  21. 3.1 Let $f$ be the function on $\R$ defined by $f(t) = \frac{p+\sqrt{2}}{q+\sqrt{2}} - \frac{p}{q}$ if $t = \frac{p}{q}$ with $p, q \in \Z$ and $p$ and $q$ coprime to each other, and $f(t) = 0$ if $t$ is irrational. Answer the following: \begin{enumerate}[i)] \item At which irrational numbers $t$ is $f$ continuous? \item At which rational numbers $t$ is $f$ continuous? \end{enumerate}
  22. 3.2 Let $f$ and $g$ be two continuous functions on $\R$. For any $a \in \R$ we define $J_a(f, g)$ to be the function given by $J_a(f, g)(t) = f(t)$ for all $t \le a$ and $J_a(f, g)(t) = g(t)$ if $t > a$. For what values of $a$ is $J_a(f, g)$ a continuous function?
  23. 3.3 Let $A$ and $B$ be two finite subsets of $\R$. Describe a necessary and sufficient condition for the spaces $\R \setminus A$ and $\R \setminus B$ to be homeomorphic.
  24. 3.4 Let $f : \R^2 \to \R$ be a continuous function. Let $D$ be the closed unit disc in $\R^2$. Is $f(D)$ necessarily an interval in $\R$? If it is an interval, which of the forms $]a, b[$, $[a, b[$, $]a, b]$ and $[a, b]$, with $a, b \in \R$ can it have?
  25. 3.5 For $v \in \R^2$ and $r > 0$ let $D(v, r)$ denote the closed disc with centre at $v$ and radius $r$. Let $v = (5, 0) \in \R^2$. For $\alpha > 0$ let $X_\alpha$ be the subset \[ X_\alpha = D(-v, 3) \cup D(v, 3) \cup \{(x, \alpha x) : x \in \R\}. \] Determine the condition on $\alpha$ for $X_\alpha$ to be connected; when it is not connected how many connected components does $X_\alpha$ have?
  26. 3.6 Which two of the following spaces are homeomorphic to each other? \begin{enumerate}[i)] \item $X_1 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : xy = 0\}$; \item $X_2 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x + y \ge 0 \text{ and } xy = 0\}$; \item $X_3 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : xy = 1\}$; \item $X_4 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x + y \ge 0 \text{ and } xy = 1\}$. \end{enumerate}
  27. 3.7 Which of the following spaces are compact? \begin{enumerate}[i)] \item $X_1 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : |x| + |y| < 10^{-100}\}$; \item $X_2 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : |x| + |y| \le 10^{100}\}$; \item $X_3 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : 1 \le x^2 + y^2 \le 2\}$; \item $X_4 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 = 1 \text{ and } xy \neq 0\}$. \end{enumerate}
  28. 3.8 Which of the following spaces are locally compact? \begin{enumerate}[i)] \item $X_1 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x, y \text{ odd integers}\}$; \item $X_2 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + 103xy + 7y^2 > 5\}$; \item $X_3 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : 0 \le x < 1,\ 0 < y \le 1\}$; \item $X_4 = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x, y \text{ irrational}\}$. \end{enumerate}
  29. 3.9 Which of the following metric spaces $(X_i, d_i)$, $1 \le i \le 4$, are complete? \begin{enumerate}[i)] \item $X_1 = ]0, \pi/2[ \subset \R$, $d_1$ defined by $d_1(x, y) = |\tan x - \tan y|$ for all $x, y \in X_1$. \item $X_2 = [0, 1] \subset \R$, $d_2$ defined by $d_2(x, y) = \frac{|x-y|}{1+|x-y|}$ for all $x, y \in X_2$. \item $X_3 = \Q$, and $d_3$ defined by $d_3(x, y) = 1$ for all $x, y \in X_3$, $x \neq y$. \item $X_4 = \R$, $d_4$ defined by $d_4(x, y) = |e^x - e^y|$ for all $x, y \in X_4$. \end{enumerate}
  30. 3.10 On which of the following spaces is every continuous (real-valued) function bounded? \begin{enumerate}[i)] \item $X_1 = ]0, 1[$; \item $X_2 = [0, 1]$; \item $X_3 = [0, 1[$; \item $X_4 = \{t \in [0, 1] : t \text{ irrational}\}$. \end{enumerate}
  31. 4.1 Let $\Gamma(s)$ stand for the usual Gamma function. Given that $\Gamma(1/2) = \sqrt{\pi}$, evaluate $\Gamma(5/2)$.
  32. 4.2 Let $S = \{(x, y, z) \in \R^3 : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1,\ z > 0\}$. Let $C = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 = 1\}$. Let $\tau$ be the unit tangent vector to $C$ in the $xy$-plane pointing left as we move clockwise along $C$. Let $\varphi(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^3 + z^4$. Evaluate: \[ \int_C \nabla \varphi \cdot \tau\,ds. \]
  33. 4.3 Let $a > 0$ and let $S = \{(x, y, z) \in \R^3 : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = a^2\}$. Evaluate: \[ \iint_S (x^4 + y^4 + z^4)\,dS. \]
  34. 4.4 Let $f(x) = x^2 - 5$ for $x \in \R$. Let $x_0 = 1$. If $\{x_n\}$ denotes the sequence of iterates defined by the Newton-Raphson method to approximate a solution of $f(x) = 0$, find $x_1$.
  35. 4.5 Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix with real entries. Consider the linear system of ordinary differential equations given in vector notation as: \[ \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt}(t) = A\mathbf{x}(t), \qquad \text{where } \mathbf{x}(t) = \begin{pmatrix} u(t) \\ v(t) \end{pmatrix}. \] Pick out the cases from the following when we have $\lim_{t \to \infty} u(t) = 0$ and $\lim_{t \to \infty} v(t) = 0$: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$. \item $A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 \\ 0 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$. \item $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -6 \\ 1 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$. \item $A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -6 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$. \end{enumerate}
  36. 4.6 Let $\Delta = \partial^2/\partial x^2 + \partial^2/\partial y^2$ denote the Laplace operator. Let $\Omega = \{(x, y) \in \R^2 : x^2 + y^2 < 1\}$. Let $\partial\Omega$ denote the boundary of the domain $\Omega$. Consider the following boundary value problem: \begin{align*} \Delta u &= c \quad \text{in } \Omega \\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial \nu} &= 1 \quad \text{on } \partial\Omega \end{align*} where $c$ is a real constant and $\partial u/\partial \nu$ denotes the outward normal derivative of $u$ on $\partial\Omega$. For what values of $c$ does the above problem admit a solution?
  37. 4.7 Consider the Tricomi equation: \[ \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} - y \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = 0. \] Describe the region in the $xy$-plane where this equation is elliptic.
  38. 4.8 Evaluate: \[ \iint_{\R^2} e^{-(3x+2y)^2 - (4x+y)^2}\,dxdy. \]
  39. 4.9 Let $J_p$ denote the Bessel function of the first kind, of order $p$ and let $\{P_n\}$ denote the sequence of Legendre polynomials defined on the interval $[-1, 1]$. Pick out the true statements from the following: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\frac{d}{dx} J_0(x) = -J_1(x)$. \item Between any two positive zeroes of $J_0$, there exists a zero of $J_1$. \item $P_{n+1}(x)$ can be written as a linear combination of $P_n(x)$ and $P_{n-1}(x)$. \item $P_{n+1}(x)$ can be written as a linear combination of $xP_n(x)$ and $P_{n-1}(x)$. \end{enumerate}
  40. 4.10 Consider the linear programming problem: Maximize $z = 2x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3$ such that \begin{align*} 4x_1 + 3x_2 + x_3 &= 6 \\ x_1 + 2x_2 + 5x_3 &\ge 4 \\ x_1, x_2, x_3 &\ge 0. \end{align*} Write down the objective function of the dual problem.
  41. 5.1 A unimodular matrix is a matrix with integer entries and having determinant $1$ or $-1$. If $m$ and $n$ are positive integers, write down a necessary and sufficient condition such that there exists a unimodular matrix of order $2$ whose first row is the vector $(m, n)$.
  42. 5.2 For any integer $n$ define $k(n) = \frac{n^7}{7} + \frac{n^3}{3} + \frac{11n}{21} + 1$ and \[ f(n) = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } k(n) \text{ is an integer,} \\ \frac{1}{n^2} & \text{if } k(n) \text{ is not an integer.} \end{cases} \] Find $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} f(n)$.
  43. 5.3 Let $n \ge 2$. Evaluate: \[ \sum_{k=2}^{n} \frac{n!}{(n-k)!(k-2)!}. \]
  44. 5.4 A fair coin is tossed ten times. What is the probability that we can observe a string of eight heads, in succession, at some time?
  45. 5.5 Evaluate the product $\prod_{n=2}^{\infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{n^2} + \frac{1}{n^4} + \frac{1}{n^6} + \dots\right)$.
  46. 5.6 Find all solutions of the equation \[ (x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 1)^2 + (x + y + z - 3)^2 = 0. \]
  47. 5.7 For any real number $x$, let $f(x)$ denote the distance of $x$ from the nearest integer. Let $I(k) = [k\pi, k\pi + 1]$. Find $f(I(k))$ for all integers $k$.
  48. 5.8 Let $K$ be a finite field. Can you always find a non-constant polynomial over $K$ which has no root in $K$? If yes, give one such polynomial.
  49. 5.9 Evaluate: \[ \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{k^2}{k!}. \]
  50. 5.10 Pick out the countable sets from the following: \begin{enumerate}[a.] \item $\{\alpha \in \R : \alpha \text{ is a root of a polynomial with integer coefficients}\}$. \item The complement in $\R$ of the set described in statement (a) above. \item The set of all points in the plane whose coordinates are rational. \item Any subset of $\R$ whose Lebesgue measure is zero. \end{enumerate}
